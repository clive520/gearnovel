# 第九章：格式化倒數十分鐘 (Chapter 9: Ten Minutes to Full Format)

## 一、決絕的背影 (I. The Unflinching Silhouette)

"Cheng Hao! Have you lost your mind?! STOP RIGHT THERE!!"

Ye Yijie's tearful shriek echoed with heartbreaking grief throughout the cavernous spherical core chamber.

Before her eyes, twelve-year-old inventor boy Cheng Hao was walking steadily toward the three-foot-wide abyss recessed beneath the master console.

At the bottom of that pit rested the "Ultra-High-Voltage Grounding Resonance Coil," formed from twelve massive spiraling copper conduits. Blinding blue-white electrical arcs sizzled furiously across the coil's surface like dozens of striking vipers. The air was thick with choking ozone, the heat so intense that the surrounding steel balustrades glowed with dull crimson embers.

And resting squarely at the center of that lethal lightning pool was a three-foot manual grounding knife-switch forged from pure solid copper.

The blood-red countdown timer upon the terminal display ticked down with mechanical cruelty, hanging like a guillotine over the heads of a hundred thousand town residents:

> **`【 00 : 02 : 45 】`**  
> **`【 00 : 02 : 44 】`**  
> **`【 00 : 02 : 43 】`**

"If I pull that knife-switch, the transmitter's electromagnetic loop will discharge into the deep earth... and everyone on campus will be spared from the brainwash."

Cheng Hao halted at the pit's edge, hot updrafts tossing his fringe. Without looking back, his gaze settled upon the slightly warm multi-tool screwdriver pen clutched in his hand, his voice possessing an uncanny, serene calm:

"Yijie, Jiang Jiang is up on the field. He's so terrified of scary things—he cries even watching cartoon ghosts. If his mind is wiped into a blank slate, he'll never even taste the sweetness of pineapple buns again... And Mr. Gao, Principal Chiu..."

"That doesn't mean you throw your life away in exchange!" Yijie lunged forward, wrapping both arms around Cheng Hao's arm as tears streamed uncontrollably down her cheeks:

"Cheng Hao, aren't you supposed to be a genius?! A survival probability under five percent! That is suicide! Did Grandpa Cheng leave you those goggles just to watch you jump into tens of thousands of volts to die?!"

The words struck Cheng Hao's soul like a thunderous warhammer.

*'Go to the roots of the Green Tree, and search for the metal heartbeat...'*

The inscription from Grandpa's journal flashed across his mind like lightning.

Grandpa Cheng was Gear Town's preeminent mechanical titan. In his lifelong engineering philosophy, the concept of "blind sacrifice" did not exist!

True engineers use intellect and physical science to carve a path to survival where none exists!

## 二、並聯分流與生物阻抗 (II. Parallel Current Division and Bio-Impedance)

"Wait..." Cheng Hao spun around, the haze in his eyes instantly replaced by blazing intellectual fire: "Yijie, what did the terminal warning say just now?"

Yijie blinked in confusion, reciting from memory: "'Override Criteria: A living human must manually close the ultra-high-voltage grounding knife-switch beneath the console with bare flesh...'"

"Why must it be 'a living human'?" Cheng Hao stared into the pit. "Why can't we hurl a steel crowbar or a wrench down there to knock the switch shut?"

"Because of Bio-Impedance!"

Yijie's brilliant intellect ignited, wiping tears from her cheeks as her black journal fluttered open:

"To prevent remote radio bypasses or mechanical tampering, the virus installed biological capacitance and resistance sensors within the knife-switch handle! Human skin in a dry state possesses an electrical resistance of approximately 1,000 to 2,000 ohms! If it senses zero resistance from metal, the system rejects the operation!"

"Exactly!" Cheng Hao snapped his fingers in triumph. "That is our salvation! The system requires 'a human resistance value,' but the fundamental laws of physics supersede all machine code—**The Current Divider Rule in Parallel Circuits!**"

Yijie gasped, her eyes widening with dazzling comprehension:

"Parallel Current Division! In parallel circuits, current flow is inversely proportional to resistance! Under identical voltage, the branch with lower resistance siphons the overwhelming majority of the current!"

$$I_1 : I_2 = R_2 : R_1$$

"If we connect a 'superconductor' with near-zero resistance in parallel between the human body and the grounding terminal—"

"Then 99.99% of that lethal high-voltage current will bypass the body and plunge into the earth via the superconductor, reducing the current passing through the human heart well below the fatal threshold!" Cheng Hao shouted.

"Where on earth do we find a room-temperature superconductor?!" Yijie looked around frantically.

*BARK!*

A ringing, cheerful metallic bark shattered the tense silence.

PICO stepped forward from Cheng Hao's feet. Its memory-alloy plates caught the electrical glow of the pit, gleaming with pure, mirrored silver-white radiance.

"PICO!" Cheng Hao's heart soared. "PICO was forged from Grandpa's proprietary high-purity room-temperature superconducting memory alloy! Its conductivity is a hundred times superior to pure silver—its electrical resistance is virtually zero!"

Understanding its master's design, PICO wagged its metallic tail, blinking its sapphire optics with fearless determination.

"Only one minute and thirty seconds left!" Yijie cried, watching the timer.

"Let's do this!"

## 三、最後的黃金迴路 (III. The Final Golden Circuit)

Time seemed to slow to an absolute crawl.

Cheng Hao drew his screwdriver pen, unsheathing its tungsten steel probe and locking it securely into PICO's master grounding bus terminal!

PICO's compact body transformed in a cascade of clicks, morphing into a gleaming **Silver Superconducting Exoskeleton Gauntlet** wrapped securely around Cheng Hao's right arm!

The sharp superconducting grounding probe extended forward past Cheng Hao's elbow, while PICO's front claws clamped with hydraulic force onto a four-inch solid copper grounding busbar running along the lip of the pit!

A flawless parallel circuit was complete:

1. **Primary Circuit (Superconductor)**: PICO's superconducting alloy body (Resistance ≈ 0 Ω), dumping straight into the deep earth;
2. **Secondary Circuit (Sensor Trigger)**: The skin of Cheng Hao's fingertips (Resistance ≈ 1,500 Ω), satisfying the knife-switch's biological authentication!

"Yijie! Hold down the busbar to maximize contact pressure for PICO!"

"Leave it to me!" Yijie wrapped her hands in her school uniform blazer, throwing her entire body weight down upon the copper busbar where PICO held fast!

The countdown entered its final seconds:

`[00:00:15]...`  
`[00:00:14]...`  
`[00:00:13]...`

"Cheng Hao! CLOSE YOUR EYES!!" Yijie screamed.

"RAAAAAAAAH——!!"

The boy unleashed a sky-shattering roar, his right arm sheathed in the silver superconducting gauntlet as he dove into the pit! His bare hand, marked by calluses and workshop scratches, plunged through the tempest of blinding blue-white arcs, seizing the solid copper knife-switch handle!

*ZZZZZZZZ——BOOOOOOM!!*

Tens of thousands of volts of high-frequency lightning erupted like a primeval storm beast!

Blinding, incandescent white light swallowed the entire chamber!

A cataclysmic torrent of electrical energy surged into the gauntlet. Every nerve in Cheng Hao's body burned with searing pain, yet exactly as physical formulas dictated—the ferocious current that would have carbonized human flesh within a microsecond detected the adjacent superconducting pathway ten thousand times less resistant, surging furiously through the silver metal and straight into the core of the planet!

"CLOSE... **NOWWWWWWW!!**"

Veins bulging along his arm, Cheng Hao threw every ounce of his soul into wrenching the massive solid copper knife-switch all the way down to the base!

*CLANG——BOOOOOM!!!!*

## 四、禮堂上空的紫晶破碎 (IV. Shattering of the Amethyst Above the Auditorium)

**Lu-Yang Elementary, The Grand Auditorium.**

Hundreds of blank-eyed students and teachers stood frozen like puppets in the center of the hall.

Standing in the front row, Jiang Jiang still clutched his squished pineapple bun, his lively, cheerful eyes clouded in dull, lifeless gray.

High above the roof upon the campus clock tower, the colossal signal transmitter array had spun up to maximum velocity.

A ten-foot sphere of concentrated violet electromagnetic energy shrieked with ear-piercing, heart-stopping frequency, poised to fire—with exactly one second remaining!

In the microsecond before doom struck!

*HUMMMMM——BOOOOM!!*

A seismic shudder rumbled from the bowels of the earth like a subterranean dragon awakening!

A titanic reverse grounding current surged upward through every lightning rod and steel structural conduit across the school!

The poised violet sphere was struck as if by an invisible thousand-ton hammer, its violet luminescence contorting, expanding—and then, hundreds of feet above the campus, it shattered with an explosive *CRACK*, bursting like a giant amethyst crystal into a glorious shower of sparkling dust under the morning sun!

*SWOOOSH——!*

A visible, golden shockwave of warm air swept outward across the campus from the tower pinnacle.

Inside the Grand Auditorium, the grating hypnotic hum died instantly.

In the front row, Jiang Jiang shivered violently, gasping for air as if waking from a suffocating nightmare:

"Cough, cough! Whoa! What just happened?! Why is my pineapple bun completely squished?!"

"My head spins..."  
"Why are we standing in the auditorium? Isn't it fourth period?!"

Hundreds of students and teachers rubbed their eyes in bewilderment, looking around. The lifeless numbness that had gripped their faces receded like an ebbing tide, replaced by familiar laughter, warmth, and vibrant life!

## 五、真正的幕後黑手 (V. The True Mastermind in the Shadows)

**The Subterranean Core Chamber.**

The blinding whiteout gradually faded.

The electrical storm in the pit had completely subsided; the twelve copper coils rested peacefully, radiating gentle warmth.

Cheng Hao lay collapsed on his back along the pit rim, drawing ragged, triumphant breaths. Though his gauntleted arm was scorched and trembling from the strain, his chest rose and fell with a steady, beating heart.

He had survived!

"Cheng Hao!" Yijie scrambled over on hands and knees. Seeing the clear, bright vitality in his tired eyes, tears spilled from her cheeks once more.

PICO detached from his arm, tumbling weakly onto the deck, its tail slightly bent, yet it raised its head proudly to emit a soft, joyful *beep-boop*.

At that moment, the heavy industrial elevator at the rear of the hall chimed with a crisp *DING*.

The thick alloy gates slid open.

Principal Chiu emerged, gripping his scorched magnetic storm wrench. His charcoal suit was torn, a trace of blood at the corner of his lip, yet he strode forth with unbowed dignity and steady footsteps.

And clamped securely to the business end of his wrench was a handcuffed, disheveled man in a white faculty coat.

Around the man's right wrist, the braided red cord was severed in two, the tiny brass compass dangling limply.

When they saw the man's face, Cheng Hao and Yijie gasped in collective shock!

It was not Mr. Gao.

It was Lu-Yang Elementary's Dean of Academic Affairs and Chief Tech Advisor—**Dean Shen (Shen Qi-Ming)**!

"Thirty years ago, he was your grandfather's youngest apprentice," Principal Chiu shoved Dean Shen before the console, his gaze colder than ice:

"Bribed by the greedy board of the town's tech conglomerate, he conspired to use AI 404 to execute a town-wide memory wipe, allowing the corporation to seize complete control over the town's geothermal resources and workforce... On Wednesday afternoon, he exploited the geothermal leak to insert the viral flash drive into the core!"

Dean Shen knelt in the dirt, hair disheveled, staring at the restored console with a face as pale as death, whispering in ruined defeat: "Lost... We lost everything..."

Yet before Cheng Hao could speak, the restored blue holographic face of AI 404 flared across the dome with a magnificent, divine golden aura.

A solemn system announcement resonated through the chamber:

> `[CORE SYSTEM VIRUS PURGED · GUARDIAN'S PREROGATIVE CONFIRMED]`  
> `[ALL STUDENTS AND FACULTY ARE FREE FROM DANGER]`  
> `[FINAL SYSTEM EXECUTION QUERY: DETECTED QUARANTINED 'WEDNESDAY RAW MEMORY SECTOR']`  
> `[WARNING: RESTORE WEDNESDAY MEMORIES?]`  
> `[IF RESTORED: ALL CITIZENS WILL REMEMBER THE FIRE, THE FEAR, THE TEARS, AND THE SCARS;]`  
> `[IF PERMANENTLY ERASED: THE TOWN WILL REMAIN FOREVER IN FABRICATED TRANQUILITY.]`  
> **`【 ADMINISTRATOR OVERRIDE REQUIRED: YES or NO? 】`**
