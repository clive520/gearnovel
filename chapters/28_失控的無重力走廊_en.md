# 第二十八章：失控的無重力走廊 (Chapter 28: The Runaway Zero-G Corridor)

## 一、深入浮空城中樞 (I. Penetrating the Celestial Citadel Core)

Passing through the massive white-jade arcade, Cecilia guided Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, and Jiang Jiang into the subterranean primary maintenance artery leading to the core of the floating continent.

Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan remained aboard The Aoki Gear, maintaining long-wave radio relay with Principal Chiu in the town below and modulating boiler heat exchangers to supply remote energy conduits to the strike team.

Yet less than a hundred meters down the subterranean gallery, the spectacle ahead caused every breath to catch in their throats—

The once-immaculate white-jade masonry was rent asunder by gargantuan, serpent-thick spikes of black anti-matter crystals. Around the obsidian lattices, visible ripples of shimmering violet spatial distortion pulsed with a disorienting, low-frequency infrasonic thrum that made their eardrums ache.

"Everyone halt! The gravitational field ahead has collapsed completely!"

Cecilia held out her arm, halting the team, retrieving a heavy brass nut from her toolbelt and tossing it gently forward.

An astonishing phenomenon manifested before their eyes—

The moment the brass nut crossed the threshold of a shattered bronze blast door, far from falling, it decelerated as if plunging into an invisible fluid, floating, drifting, and rotating in midair six feet above the deck with serene, weightless grace!

Across the entire six-hundred-foot hollow gallery, the gravitational constant had been completely canceled out by the black crystals, plunging the sector into a chaotic **"Zero-G Abyssal Anomaly"**!

## 二、漫天漂浮的發條風暴與動量守恆 (II. Floating Clockwork Tempest and Conservation of Momentum)

Worse still, inside this completely weightless corridor, thousands of multi-hundred-pound bronze gears, heavy steel wrenches, and razor-sharp crystal shards originally secured along the walls were now tumbling, colliding, and ricocheting erratically amidst turbulent drafts!

"Zero gravity?!" Jiang Jiang's round eyes widened as he experimentally dipped one boot across the threshold.

In a flash, both feet left the deck. The chubby boy thrashed his limbs in midair like an upturned turtle, screaming in sheer panic: "WAHHH! HELP! Why is my belly floating toward the ceiling?!"

"Stop thrashing! In zero gravity, without an external anchor, blind flailing merely converts into angular momentum, sending you into uncontrollable flat spins!" Ye Yijie lunged forward, grabbing Jiang Jiang's ankle, snapping safety carabiners together to link the four of them into an aligned tandem array.

*BOOM! CRASH——!*

The reinforced blast door behind them shook violently under the concussion of high-explosive breaching charges!

Ten shock troopers clad in matte-black stealth exoskeletons, clutching Gauss electromagnetic pulse rifles and stomping forward upon magnetic-soles boots, burst through the smoke like mechanical specters!

"Kuroshio is through the door!" Cecilia's eyes sharpened with resolve: "The corridor spans one hundred and eighty meters; Kuroshio's Gauss snipers require twelve seconds to cycle a full capacitor charge! We must cross this gallery of tumbling bronze gears within twelve seconds!"

"Without solid footing, how do we propel ourselves?!" Cheng Hao pressed urgently.

"The Law of Conservation of Momentum! Reactionary rocket thrust!!"

A dazzling spark of mathematical inspiration ignited in Ye Yijie's eyes:


"**EXACT RECOIL VELOCITY: FIFTEEN METERS PER SECOND! CECILIA DEPLOYS WINGS FOR AVOIDANCE CONTROL!**"

Ye Yijie tightened the locking carabiners: "Jiang Jiang! Lock the skillet behind PICO's high-pressure nozzle! STAND BY FOR BLAST IN THREE SECONDS!!"

## 三、鍋爐噴氣與星際衝浪 (III. Boiler Jet and Interstellar Surfing)

"Understood! Skillet Rocket Thruster——manually aligned dead astern!!"

Gritting his teeth, Jiang Jiang gripped the handles of his cast-iron skillet with both hands, aiming the concave bottom squarely toward the pursuing Kuroshio soldiers.

PICO transformed into a conical superconducting nozzle gleaming with ice-blue photon lines, clamping seamlessly onto the skillet's central mounting bracket.

"Three... two... one... **VENT GAS————!!**"

*SHHHH——BOOOOOOOOOM!!*

Two thousand meters per second of high-pressure nitrogen gas reflected off the curved iron pan, erupting into a colossal, focused kinetic shockwave!

The immense reactionary recoil hammered into the four-person array in a millisecond!

*SWOOOSH————!*

The two-hundred-kilogram tethered unit launched forward like an armor-piercing kinetic dart, knifing through the zero-gravity corridor at a blinding fifteen meters per second!

Dead ahead, dozens of six-foot bronze gears and serrated black crystal boulders floated in slow, menacing orbits across their vector!

"Leave it to me! Celestial Control Surfaces——DEPLOY!!"

Cecilia barked, the titanium memory-alloy glider wings upon her back snapping outward with a sharp *SHING*!

Gripping her astrolabe with both hands, the girl made micro-adjustments to her wing angles with breathtaking aviator intuition!

Gliding against the thin air, the four-person tandem carved an impossible, three-dimensional double-helix slalom through the chasm!

*WHOOSH! WHOOSH! CLANG!*

Multi-ton clockwork wheels roared past Jiang Jiang's nose and Cheng Hao's goggles with inches to spare, not a single gear tooth grazing their armor!

## 四、反鎖閘門與引力大廳的曙光 (IV. Locking the Bulkhead and the Light of the Core)

"Ten seconds! Eleven seconds! WE'RE THERE!!"

The eight-inch-thick titanium emergency bulkhead at the far end of the corridor loomed before them!

Snapping his screwdriver pen into its holster, Cheng Hao caught the brass handrail along the doorframe in midair, swinging the entire tethered team with centrifugal momentum cleanly through the hatchway!

*CLACK! RUMBLE——BOOM!!*

Cheng Hao threw his entire weight onto the ten-ton hydraulic lock lever!

In the exact microsecond the heavy bulkhead sealed—

*BANG! BANG! BANG!*

Gauss kinetic penetrators fired by the Kuroshio squad slammed into the exterior face, raising monstrous craters across the armored steel, but failing to pierce the reinforced titanium door!

"Phew... HAH... We lost them!!"

Collapsing onto the deck as artificial gravity re-engaged, Jiang Jiang wept tears of relief, clutching his skillet: "My skillet... has officially been promoted to a rocket engine!"

Cecilia retracted her wings, looking toward Cheng Hao and Yijie with undisguised admiration and awe: "The keepers of thirty years ago... truly were miraculous men of science!"

Cheng Hao smiled, wiping sweat from his brow, turning to open the crystalline doors leading to the inner sanctum.

Unfolding before their eyes was the sacred, beating heart of the entire floating continent—

A bottomless abyss plunging a thousand meters into the void!

Suspended above the chasm, twelve colossal **"Planetary Floating Gear Stairways"** revolving in synchronization with the orbits of the solar system ascended toward the celestial peak, where the majestic, star-bathed **"Celestial Clock Tower"** stood!

Time remaining until surface collision: **Twelve hours and forty minutes**!
