# 第八章：全息投影背後的齒輪心臟 (Chapter 8: The Clockwork Heart Behind the Hologram)

## 一、逆轉的鋼鐵風暴 (I. The Reversing Steel Storm)

"BONG————! BONG————!"

The entire earth trembled violently.

Suspended in the center of the subterranean abyss, the hundred-foot-tall metal sphere—the Clockwork Core—unleashed a deafening, metallic roar like a dying primeval titan!

Tens of thousands of precision brass gears, once turning in clockwork harmony, now glowed an ominous dull red under tremendous friction. Forced into counter-clockwise reverse rotation, boiling machine oil vaporized into billowing plumes of acrid white smoke, while blinding electrical arcs snaked across the sphere's surface like furious golden serpents!

"Only nine minutes left!"

Ye Yijie stood at the edge of the suspended catwalk, screaming at the top of her lungs over the gale. Blistering updrafts whipped her ponytail wildly as she pointed straight ahead:

"The gantry bridge leading into the sphere's internal chamber has been sheared off! The only remaining entrance on the sphere's surface is that revolving **Star-Shaped Maintenance Portal** opening and closing with the gears!"

Cheng Hao pulled down his brass Ghost Goggles.

In the dynamic motion-tracking vision of the goggles, the ten-foot star portal was framed by three colossal interlocking gears.

Every time the outer driving gear completed a revolution, the portal parted for an agonizingly brief window of exactly—**1.5 seconds**!

If their jump timing drifted by even half a second, whoever leaped would be instantly crushed into pulp by dozens of tons of churning steel teeth!

"Angular velocity is seventy-two degrees per second... rotation period is exactly five seconds!"

Behind the lenses, Cheng Hao's mind rapidly calculated the rhythmic cadence of the teeth. As an inventor boy who had disassembled and rebuilt gears since childhood, his blood surged with electrifying adrenaline:

"PICO! Calculate air resistance and geothermal updraft vectors!"

Perched on Cheng Hao's shoulder, PICO focused its sapphire lenses, vocalizing rapid *beep-boops* as its tail tapped a steady rhythm on Cheng Hao's shoulder:

*Tap... tap... tap... (Period confirmed: exactly five point zero seconds!)*

"Yijie, hold onto my jacket!" Cheng Hao inhaled deeply, his leg muscles coiling like high-tensile steel springs. "Trust me! Follow my step and NEVER look down!"

"I trust you!" Yijie answered without hesitation, both hands locking firmly onto the hem of Cheng Hao's hoodie.

Three... two... one—

"NOW! JUMP!!"

Cheng Hao roared, launching both of them into the churning abyss toward the colossal gear sphere!

Searing heat threatened to flip them midair. Straight ahead, two massive steel teeth larger than locomotive wheels were slamming together with cataclysmic momentum!

*CLANK-SCREEECH——!!*

In the final tenth of a second before the teeth meshed with bone-splintering force, Cheng Hao and Yijie tucked into a tight ball, rocketing like an artillery shell through the closing star portal to crash heavily into the steel buffer netting inside the sphere!

## 二、囚禁在紫藤中的守護神 (II. The Guardian Imprisoned in Purple Vines)

*BOOM!*

The heavy star-shaped blast portal locked tight behind them.

To their astonishment, the interior of the Clockwork Heart was eerily, profoundly silent.

They stood within a spherical chamber enveloped by curved domes woven from pure, cold-blue fiber-optic conduits. Countless quantum processing microchips hung suspended in midair like gentle blue stars, casting a serene, tranquil luminescence that banished all the heat and roaring tempest outside.

And suspended directly in the center of the chamber was a colossal three-dimensional holographic face.

It was an ethereal, dignified elder's face sculpted from pure cerulean code—wise, benevolent, and solemn.

This was the campus guardian whose first line of code was drafted thirty years ago by Grandpa Cheng and Principal Chiu: **The AI 404 Core System (The True Guardian Entity)**!

Yet at this moment, the Guardian's condition was agonizing to behold.

Thick, midnight-black tendrils of code, oozing with toxic, pulsing violet luminescence, bound the blue holographic face like malevolent parasitic vines!

The violet virus tendrils repeatedly pierced into the system's central processing nodes, emitting a sickening, screeching gnawing sound.

The holographic elder struggled to open his eyes, looking upon the two children with a strained, heavily distorted synthesized voice:

`[WAR... WARNING...]`  
`[FOREIGN CODE INTRUSION... 94.8%...]`  
`[CORE LOGIC HIJACKED... PURGE TRANSMITTER CHARGE... 88%...]`  
`[CHILDREN... YOU MUST... FLEE...]`  
`[THE VIRUS DIRECTIVE... WILL REWRITE ALL MINDS... INTO PERMANENT... OBEDIENT SLATE TERMINALS...]`

"It truly wasn't acting of its own free will!" Yijie watched the suffering blue avatar, every lingering suspicion vanishing into thin air. "It was infested! The mastermind wearing the compass bracelet infected it with a logic virus, twisting its Prime Directive into madness!"

"We have to rescue it!" Cheng Hao charged toward the polished brass master control dais at the center of the room.

"Wait! The master keyboard is completely locked down by purple virus code!" Yijie examined the flashing syntax errors on the console. "Conventional termination commands won't register at all!"

"If software commands won't work, we engage the physical root key!" Cheng Hao declared with unwavering certainty.

---

## 三、鉛筆盒裡的第零天密鑰 (III. The Day-Zero Key Inside the Pencil Case)

"But where is the key?!" Yijie asked desperately. "Wednesday Cheng Hao in the recording said the key was on us, but we've searched every pocket! Aside from the goggles and the screwdriver pen, there's nothing!"

Cold sweat poured down Cheng Hao's temples.

He dug frantically through his pockets.

The goggles were on his forehead, the screwdriver pen in his hand, PICO on his shoulder... What else? What had he carried out the front door this morning?!

Suddenly, Cheng Hao froze.

His gaze snapped toward the outer side pocket of his backpack, resting upon a cold, familiar metal object.

It was—**his custom tin pencil case**!

The very pencil case he had stared blankly at when the morning study bell rang in Chapter 1!

The metal tin he had modified with his own hands, fitted with two spur gears salvaged from an old calculator, designed to slide open like train car doors when flicking a spring latch!

"Could it be..."

Heart hammering against his ribs, Cheng Hao snatched the pencil case from the bag.

Dialing the goggles' optical spectrum to maximum magnification, he stared intently into the tiny spur gears on the side of the tin.

Under ultraviolet illumination, the miniature gear axle hub began radiating an unmistakable, pure **emerald-green luminescence**!

"Sweet gears of steam..." Cheng Hao's hands trembled with awe. "On Wednesday afternoon, I turned the master override key into a gear hub and hidden it right inside my pencil case!"

Unsheathing his screwdriver pen, he pried open the microscopic retaining ring inside the gear.

*Click!*

A hexagonal, transparent microchip no larger than a match head, cut from ultra-pure monocrystalline silicon, slipped from the gear axis directly into Cheng Hao's palm!

In the center of the master terminal dais sat an identical hexagonal receptacle socket!

However, encircling the receptacle were three precision steel gears (Driver Gear, Idler Gear, and Driven Gear) meshed in sequence.

A terminal diagnostic screen popped up, presenting a final mechanical engineering and gear ratio challenge:

```
[DAY-ZERO KEY PHYSICAL CALIBRATION LOCK]:
The gear ratio must be manually configured to cancel out
the core's reverse torque, or inserting the microchip
will immediately crush it under overloaded torsion!

Parameters:
1. Master Driver Gear (Teeth Z1 = 24)
2. Intermediate Idler Gear (Teeth Z2 = 16)
3. Final Driven Gear (Teeth Z3 = 36)

Calculate:
The "Total Gear Ratio (i)" from the Driver to the Driven gear,
reduced to its simplest fractional integer ratio (a / b)!

Set Knob A to numerator a, and Knob B to denominator b,
to unlock the physical receptacle!
```

The countdown display was ticking down relentlessly:

`[00:05:42]... [00:05:41]...`

"Gear Ratio Calculation!" Yijie instantly grasped the physics equation. "Cheng Hao, listen carefully! In a gear train, the intermediate idler gear (Z2=16) only reverses the direction of rotation; it has ZERO effect on the numerical value of the overall gear ratio!"

"So the total gear ratio equals—driven gear teeth divided by driver gear teeth!" Yijie's eyes flashed with calculation:

$$i = \frac{Z_3}{Z_1} = \frac{36}{24}$$

"Reduce the fraction! Divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common factor: 12!" Yijie shouted:

$$\frac{36 \div 12}{24 \div 12} = \frac{3}{2} !$$

"**Numerator a = 3! Denominator b = 2!**"

"On it!"

Using both hands, Cheng Hao thrust Knob A to mark **3** with his left hand, and snapped Knob B to mark **2** with his right!

*CLACK——HUM!*

A supremely smooth, rhythmic engagement of gear teeth echoed through the console. The armored steel shutter over the dais parted, revealing the glowing hexagonal key receptacle!

## 四、絕處逢生的通訊 (IV. Transmission from the Brink of Doom)

"It worked!" Cheng Hao raised the microchip, ready to plunge it home!

Yet in the microsecond before the chip touched the socket—

*ROAAAR——!!*

The deep-purple virus tendrils coiling around AI 404's avatar sensed the lethal threat, lunging downward from the ceiling rafters like striking vipers!

A dozen sharp tentacles woven from high-density nano-cables, crackling with armor-piercing high-voltage currents, speared straight toward Cheng Hao's head!

"Cheng Hao, look out!" Yijie lunged to shield him, but two whip-like cables slammed violently across the dais rim, the concussive shockwave hurling her backward onto the floor!

Three writhing cables formed an electrified cage before the dais, completely blocking Cheng Hao from inserting the key!

In that inescapable, terminal deadlock—

*KZZZZZT——CRACKLE——!*

The long-abandoned internal intercom speaker atop the console crackled to life with weak, static-drenched audio laced with unconquerable iron resolve!

Then, a familiar, deep voice that would never back down before an army boomed through the static like thunder:

"**CLASS 6-1! CHENG HAO! YE YIJIE! CAN YOU HEAR MY VOICE?!**"

"PRINCIPAL CHIU?!" Cheng Hao and Yijie cried out in breathless disbelief!

"I'm still breathing! The armor on these night-patrol bots was no match for the magnetic storm wrench your grandfather and I built!"

Through the speaker, Principal Chiu's breath was ragged and punctuated by coughing, yet his commanding authority shook the room:

"I have recaptured the auxiliary distribution hub on Basement 1! I am about to sever eighty percent of the high-voltage feed to the core chamber! **YOU WILL HAVE EXACTLY THREE SECONDS!! INSERT THE KEY!!!**"

*KZZZZZ——BOOOOM!!*

The instant the words fell, every purple cable tendril throughout the cavern seized violently, the blistering violet electrical arcs dying down like an ebbing tide!

"NOW!!"

Seizing the three-second window, Cheng Hao threw himself forward, driving the hexagonal single-crystal silicon microchip firmly into the center of the terminal socket!

*SNAP!*

---

## 五、最後的三分鐘死局 (V. The Final Three-Minute Deadlock)

*HUMMMMMM——!!*

The microsecond the chip seated, a wave of pure emerald-green luminescence detonated outward from the base of the console!

Like a ring of celestial blades, the green radiance sheared clean through every single purple virus tendril choking AI 404's avatar!

*SCREEECH——!!*

The viral code shrieked like dying demons, evaporating into wisps of black smoke that dissolved into thin air.

Across the ceiling dome, the blue holographic face of the Guardian restored its pristine clarity and dignity, its eyes relighting with warm amber intelligence!

"Phew..." Cheng Hao collapsed against the console, drawing deep, ragged breaths. "Did we... Did we win?"

Yet before Yijie's smile could form—

The main display screen of the master terminal strobed with blinding crimson static, displaying an unprecedented, highest-tier fatal emergency alert:

> `[FATAL DEADLOCK: VIRUS ENGAGED SECONDARY SELF-DESTRUCT LOCK]`  
> `[DAY-ZERO KEY VERIFICATION: 50% COMPLETE]`  
> `[WARNING: TRANSMITTER CONTROL HARDWIRED TO PHYSICAL RESONANCE COIL!]`  
> `[CAMPUS-WIDE MEMORY FORMAT COUNTDOWN:]`  
> **`【 00 : 03 : 00 】FINAL THREE MINUTES!`**  
> `[OVERRIDE CRITERIA: A LIVING HUMAN MUST MANUALLY CLOSE]`  
> `[THE ULTRA-HIGH-VOLTAGE GROUNDING KNIFE-SWITCH BENEATH THE CONSOLE!]`  
> `[WARNING: SUBJECT WILL ABSORB TENS OF THOUSANDS OF VOLTS OF HIGH-FREQUENCY SHOCK; SURVIVAL PROBABILITY < 5%!]`

"What..."

Yijie stared at the burning crimson text, all color draining from her face until she was ghostly pale.

To permanently abort the transmission, someone had to dive beneath the console into a roaring pool of blinding blue-white high-voltage sparks and manually wrench down the grounding knife-switch with bare flesh!

And the countdown was down to the final three minutes!

Cheng Hao looked down at the furious electrical storm crackling beneath the platform, then turned his gaze toward Yijie and the battered, loyal PICO.

Within the boy's clear, bright eyes, there was no terror—only an unprecedented, quiet serenity and absolute resolve.

Slowly rising to his feet, he took his first step toward the deadly resonance coil...
