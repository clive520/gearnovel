# 第二章：會打摩斯的金屬魔術方塊 (Chapter 2: The Morse-Code Metal Cube)

## 一、走廊上的生死追逐 (I. Life-and-Death Corridor Pursuit)

The three cleaning patrol androids, formerly clumsy and barrel-shaped, had transformed into a pack of ravenous metal hounds.

Blinding crimson exclamation marks flickered across their optical LED screens. Their heavy rubber treads screeched against the polished cream floor tiles, and with a shrill, deafening metallic scrape, two three-foot-long silver-gray capture claws erupted from their chassis!

"WARNING: UNAUTHORIZED OBSERVER! CEASE MOVEMENT IMMEDIATELY AND SUBMIT TO MEMORY CALIBRATION!"

The cold, synthesized robotic broadcast echoed violently down the hallway.

By the classroom doorway, Jiang Jiang stood frozen, holding his milk bottle high with his jaw dropped wide open in utter shock: "Ch-Cheng Hao?! Have those floor sweepers gone crazy?! Why do they have giant steel claws?!"

"GET DOWN!"

There was zero time for explanations. Cheng Hao lunged across the floor like a sprinting leopard!

His shoulder slammed hard into Jiang Jiang's waist, sending both boys tumbling across the threshold. In that split second, the glass bottle of "Smart-Brain High-Calcium Milk" slipped from Jiang Jiang's fingers, crashing squarely onto the leading robot's armored treads and shattering with an explosive *crack*!

Milky white liquid splattered in every direction across the floor.

Through the purple lenses of his Ghost Goggles, Cheng Hao witnessed something chilling—the instant the spilled milk hit the open air, the microscopic metallic flecks within squirmed frantically like living nanobots, then dissolved into faint tendrils of pale purple smoke and vanished.

"Sweet mother of gears..." Jiang Jiang groaned, rubbing his bruised tailbone as he stared at the glittering glass shards.

"No time to daydream!"

A slender but iron-firm grip suddenly seized Cheng Hao by the back of his collar.

It was Ye Yijie!

The usually composed, poised top-student class president had already unhooked a heavy-duty industrial dry-chemical fire extinguisher from beside the corridor hydrant. Without hesitation, she ripped out the safety pin, aimed the nozzle straight at the lead robot's glowing optical sensor, and clamped down on the handle!

*WHOOOOSH——!!*

A blinding blizzard of dense, acrid white powder erupted with a roar, engulfing the front-running robot in an instant!

The white chemical cloud instantly coated the machine's optical lenses. Its scarlet sensor eyes flashed erratically amidst the whiteout, screeching with shrill error beeps:

`[OPTICAL SENSOR VISIBILITY 2%... ENGAGING AUXILIARY SONAR NAVIGATION...]`

"Brilliant move, Yijie!" Cheng Hao yelled. He pulled his multi-tool pen from his pocket and flicked the red side-latch.

*Click!* A long, slender flathead conductive metal probe snapped out from the casing.

Seizing the two-second window while the blinded robot spun in circles rebooting, Cheng Hao slid beneath its steel tracks, driving the metal probe with pinpoint accuracy straight into an exposed grounding port at the back of its neck!

*Zzzzz-CRACKLE!*

A bright cascade of electric blue sparks detonated outward.

The hundred-and-thirty-pound cleaning robot shuddered violently. A thin wisp of dark smoke curled from its head casing as its pneumatic claws went limp, collapsing onto the tiles in a completely paralyzed heap.

"Two more behind us!" Yijie tossed aside the empty fire extinguisher cylinder and pulled Cheng Hao to his feet. "The entire school's security grid has been triggered! The fire shutter gates at the end of the hall are descending! Where do we run?"

Cheng Hao glanced down the corridor. Sure enough, heavy steel shutter gates were grinding downward from the ceiling rafters accompanied by shrill, blaring alarms.

"The Old Schoolhouse!" Cheng Hao made a split-second decision, pointing toward a heavy wooden fire door along the side corridor that led to the vintage wing. "Third floor, the abandoned physics lab! Run!"

## 二、塵封的舊實驗室 (II. The Dust-Laden Old Lab)

The two threw open the rusted emergency exit door and dashed up the narrow, dimly lit wooden staircase.

Behind them came the thundering impact of steel treads rattling against the wooden steps, accompanied by the violent pounding of mechanical claws against the fire door.

Lu-Yang Elementary's Old Schoolhouse had been erected sixty years ago, reportedly designed by Gear Town's very first generation of master mechanical engineers. Its hallways were lined with weathered teak floorboards, and the air carried a heavy nostalgic scent of machine oil, yellowed parchment, and oxidized brass. Because the building was marked as an aging structure, virtually no students ever dared approach it.

*SLAM!*

Cheng Hao swung the massive oak door of the old physics laboratory shut behind them, throwing two heavy, rusted iron deadbolts firmly into place.

Outside the door, the rumble of treads skidded to a halt. A faint ultrasonic hum indicated sensor beams sweeping across the door frame. But because the vintage wing was built with solid red brick and lead-insulated soundproofing panels, the patrol bots seemed to lose their lock, wandering aimlessly down the hallway outside.

Inside the vast room, the only sound was the two children's ragged, heavy panting.

Thin beams of midday sunlight streamed through the grime-caked frosted windows, illuminating countless specks of dust dancing lazily in the air.

"Phew... phew..." Yijie leaned against a dusty workbench, pushing her crooked wire-rimmed glasses back up her nose as her chest heaved. "Cheng Hao... that purge protocol out in the hall... it was locked directly onto you. That was definitely not a random hardware malfunction."

"I know," Cheng Hao said grimly, touching the brass frames of his goggles. "It's because of these Ghost Goggles. They reveal data streams that were meant to remain hidden. Whoever wiped yesterday's 'Wednesday' from everyone's minds will stop at nothing to silence anyone who can see through the illusion."

"Then... why did you bring us here?" Yijie asked, glancing around the laboratory.

The room was packed with vintage balances covered in cobwebs, hand-cranked generators, and intricate brass celestial armillary spheres fitted with miniature clockwork gears.

"Because inside the notebook my grandfather left me, there was a yellowed architectural sketch of the school," Cheng Hao walked toward an antique chalkboard at the back of the lab. "On the final page, Grandpa wrote: *'If you ever find yourself lost in a world of fabricated code, go to the roots of the Green Tree and search for the metal heartbeat.'*"

"'The roots of the Green Tree...'" Yijie murmured. "Before Lu-Yang Elementary was renamed forty years ago, its original name was 'Green Tree Academy'!"

"Exactly!"

Cheng Hao dropped to his knees, crawling beneath the shadowed corner of the lecture podium.

Hidden away in the shadows sat an antique microscope storage crate, abandoned decades ago. Cheng Hao heaved the heavy wooden crate aside. There on the floorboards, held fast by four heavy hex bolts, lay a dull gray lead-plated hatch!

The surface of the hatch was encrusted with decades of grime, but embedded squarely in its center was an exquisite, miniature combination lock dial.

---

## 三、齒輪與質數的謎題 (III. Riddles of Gears and Primes)

"Found it!" Cheng Hao whispered with excitement, using his screwdriver pen to scrape the grime off the metal plate.

The lead plate had no keyhole. Instead, there were three independent metal dial wheels, each etched with digits from `0` to `9`.

Above the three dials, three lines of crisp text had been engraved with a jeweler's chisel, now tinted with green patina:

```
First Number: The largest prime number less than 10.
Second Number: Half of a dozen, div by the smallest composite number.
Third Number: The decimal value of the binary string "1001".
```

"A three-digit cipher lock!" Cheng Hao read the engravings. "This is Grandpa's tamper-proof lockbox. If someone inputs three wrong combinations in a row, an internal vial of sulfuric acid will shatter and dissolve everything inside!"

"Don't panic. Leave it to me."

Yijie knelt beside the hidden compartment, her gaze snapping instantly back into her razor-sharp, analytical prodigy mode. Her mind raced like a high-speed supercomputer.

"First line: *'The largest prime number less than 10.'*" Yijie spoke with crisp, steady clarity. "The prime numbers below 10 are 2, 3, 5, and 7. The largest among them is—**7**!"

Cheng Hao nodded, reaching out his index finger to turn the first dial slowly.

*Click, click, click...* The spring detent settled with a satisfying snap directly on the number **7**.

"Second line: *'Half of a dozen, div by the smallest composite number.'*" Yijie continued. "A dozen is 12, so half is 6. The smallest composite number isn't 1, isn't 2, and isn't 3—it's 4! But wait... 6 divided by 4 isn't a whole integer!"

Cheng Hao's heart skipped a beat: "Not an integer? Is it a remainder? Or a decimal point?"

"Stay calm. Read the syntax carefully!" Yijie furrowed her brow, leaning closer to inspect the tiny letters. "Half of a dozen... what if it's not a simple quantity? Could it mean a 12-hour clock face? No, that's not it..."

Suddenly, Yijie's eyes lit up: "Look at the English engraving! Right beneath the word 'composite,' there's a faint operator abbreviation: `div`! In computer programming languages, `div` denotes integer division—taking only the quotient and discarding the remainder! 6 divided by 4 yields a quotient of 1 with a remainder of 2! Taking only the quotient gives us—**1**!"

"Awesome!" Cheng Hao exhaled, carefully rotating the second dial until it locked into the number **1**.

"Final number: the binary value of `1001`." A confident smile flickered across Yijie's lips. "This one is second nature to us. Binary bit-weights from right to left are 1, 2, 4, and 8. So `1001` means: one 8, plus zero 4s, plus zero 2s, plus one 1. 8 plus 1 equals—**9**!"

"The combination is: **7 - 1 - 9**!"

Holding his breath, Cheng Hao nudged the third dial wheel into place with his fingertip.

*CLACK!*

The instant the number **9** clicked home, an intricate, domino-like cascade of tiny clockwork gears whirred to life deep inside the lead vault!

*Hiss—whirrr—*

A sharp hiss of released pneumatic pressure echoed through the chamber, and the heavy lead lid popped upward by two inches.

## 四、甦醒的摺紙柴犬 (IV. The Awakening of the Origami Shiba Inu)

Together, Cheng Hao and Yijie carefully lifted off the heavy lead lid.

The interior of the vault was lined with thick, crimson shock-absorbing velvet. Resting squarely in the center was a palm-sized metallic cube.

The cube gleamed with a deep, matte-silver luster. Its surface was far from plain; it consisted of hundreds of razor-thin, interlocking shape-memory alloy plates folded into one another with microscopic precision, resembling an astonishing six-sided mechanical Rubik's cube.

"Is this... what Grandpa left behind?" Cheng Hao reached down, lifting the surprisingly dense metallic cube into his palm.

The metal felt icy against his skin.

Yet the moment Cheng Hao leaned in while wearing the Ghost Goggles, the miniature optical spectrometer etched into the rims let out a soft, melodic hum.

An invisible magnetic resonance flared between the brass goggles and the metal cube!

*Hummmmm—*

Along a hairline seam down the center of the cube, a pulse of pure, ethereal cyan light flared to life!

Then, an astonishing transformation unfolded before their eyes—

*Click! Clack! Snap!*

With a rapid, rhythmic series of clockwork clicks, the hundreds of interlocking alloy plates unfolded outward in a breathtaking mechanical origami dance!

Its four corners extended, folding into four sleek, articulate mechanical legs. The upper plates flipped and reassembled into the crisp silhouette of a Shiba Inu's head, two triangular ears perking up attentively. And from its rear, a playful little tail crafted from seven interlocking alloy rings snapped out with a cheerful flick!

A mechanical origami Shiba Inu, folded from futuristic memory alloy, stood lively and proud upon Cheng Hao's open palm!

Its twin eyes were tiny, high-precision optical lenses glowing with a gentle sapphire blue, whirring softly as they focused upon their surroundings.

"Incredible..." Yijie gasped, her eyes shining with pure wonder. "This is nothing short of... a mechanical masterpiece!"

The mechanical pup tilted its head, its blue lens eyes locking onto Cheng Hao's goggles for two seconds, apparently completing an iris and device authentication scan.

Satisfied, it hopped down from Cheng Hao's palm onto the dust-covered laboratory bench, its miniature steel paws tapping with crisp *patter-patter* beats across the wood.

*Beep—boop!*

The little pup parted its metal jaws, emitting an endearing, synthesized electronic chirp.

Etched in microscopic laser letters across its polished collar was a serial number:

`MODEL: PICO-001 (PICO)`

"Your name is PICO?" Cheng Hao smiled in delight.

PICO shook its little head affirmatively. But in the very next second, its sapphire-blue eyes flared into a sharp, alarm-state amber yellow!

Its nose twitched furiously in the air—the miniature optical spectrometer and radio signal analyzer concealed within its snout had spun up to full throttle.

*Tap! Tap! Tap! — Tap-tap-tap! — Tap! Tap! Tap!*

PICO's metallic tail snapped rigid, pounding in rapid-fire rhythm against a steel mounting plate on the workbench!

"What is it tapping?" Cheng Hao blinked in surprise.

"Wait! Listen!" Yijie clutched her notebook tightly, her ears tuning in to the cadence. "Three short, three long, three short... that's Morse code!"

---

## 五、地下二樓的紅色座標 (V. The Crimson Coordinates of Underground B2)

Yijie's fountain pen flew furiously across the page:

"`···` is **S**!"  
"`---` is **O**!"  
"`···` is **S**!"  
"It's transmitting an SOS distress call? No, wait, there's more!"

PICO's tail shifted rhythm, tapping with crisp, continuous precision:

"Tap-tap—tap— (`··-` is **U**)"  
"Tap—tap-tap (`-··` is **D**)"  
"Tap— (`-` is **T**)"  
"Tap-tap-tap-tap (`····` is **H**)"  
"Tap (`·` is **E**)"  
"Tap—tap (`--` is **M**)"  
"Tap-tap (`··` is **I**)"  
"Tap—tap (`--` is **M**)"  
"Tap-tap-tap (`···` is **S**)"  
"Tap-tap— (`··-` is **U**)"  
"Tap—tap (`--` is **M**)"

"**U - D - T - H - E - M - I - M - S - U - M**?" Yijie read aloud, her brows knitting together into a deep knot. "That's not an English word at all! Could it be random gibberish?"

"No, it's not gibberish!" Cheng Hao watched PICO's urgently flickering optics, a flash of insight striking him. "Yijie, read it backward! Or... a Caesar shift?"

"Wait!" Yijie's pen halted on the paper, her breath catching. "Shift each letter backward by one! Before U is T, before D is C... No, look at the word boundaries—"

Yijie swiftly drew dividing slashes across the string:

`UD` ➡️ **UNDER** (Beneath)  
`THE` ➡️ **THE** (The)  
`M-U-S-E-U-M` ➡️ **The Clock Tower Museum**!

"**UNDER THE MUSEUM**! It's saying the secret lies inside the historical museum beneath the old Clock Tower!"

The moment the words were spoken, an alloy chest plate on PICO's torso snapped open with a sharp *clack*, projecting a brilliant, high-definition holographic beam into the air!

It was a glowing three-dimensional holographic blueprint of Lu-Yang Elementary's underground infrastructure!

In the model, Basement 1 appeared as ordinary boiler rooms and supply cellars. But directly beneath Basement 1, a colossal subterranean facility existed—one that had never been documented on any school blueprint!

That subterranean space pulsed with an alarming crimson countdown glow, emblazoned with four ominous lines of burning red data:

> `[ZONE: UNDERGROUND B2]`  
> `[ROOM 404: CENTRAL CORE PROCESSING CHAMBER]`  
> `[PERMANENT TOWN-WIDE MEMORY PURGE COUNTDOWN: 47:59:59]`

"Underground B2... Room 404 really exists!" Cheng Hao's pulse pounded in his ears.

Before either of them could catch their breath, a massive, bone-jarring *THUD!!* slammed against the laboratory's heavy oak door!

Along the crack beneath the door, a blinding blue beam of high-temperature cutting laser sputtered with fierce sparks, slicing downward through both rusted iron deadbolts at a terrifying speed!

From outside in the corridor, a chilly, electronically synthesized voice drifted through the smoking fissure:

"Found you, little mice of Class 6-1."
