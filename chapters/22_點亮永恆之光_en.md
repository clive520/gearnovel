# 第二十二章：點亮永恆之光（第二卷大結局） (Chapter 22: Kindling the Eternal Light - Volume 2 Finale)

## 一、破曉的八十公尺燈塔之巔 (I. Dawn Upon the Eighty-Meter Lighthouse Pinnacle)

*WHOOOOSH——BOOM!*

The pneumatic ascension pod traced a blinding streak of golden light up the vertical shaft. Accompanied by a deep, resonant hiss of pressure depressurization, it burst through three thousand meters of mantle bedrock, arriving smoothly back within the crystalline rotunda atop the eighty-meter Lost Lighthouse!

As the heavy blast doors hissed open, the first pure rays of morning sunlight broke through the dawn clouds, bathing the scarred, smoke-blackened floor of the lantern room in warm golden light.

The tempest had dissipated completely.

Gazing out through the shattered crystal dome, the once-boiling, violent Thousand-Island Gear Sea had returned to tranquil, shimmering glass. The swells mirrored the violet-gold radiance of the morning horizon; Windward Island in the distance gleamed like a lush emerald, its giant clockwork windmills turning serenely in the ocean breeze.

The Abyssal Rift had fallen still, the destructive seismic spikes reduced to scrap iron, the ancient volcanic magma returning to its eternal slumber as a warm life-giving artery for the deep sea.

In the center of the rotunda, the thirty-foot "Master Fresnel Lens" stood silent under the morning sun.

Yet at the heart of the lens mount, the sacred beacon that had guided the Thousand-Island Sea for centuries remained dormant, awaiting its destined masters to reignite its eternal flame.

"Thirty years..."

Supported by Buck and Ding-Ding, Old Morris stepped toward the pedestal, his withered hand trembling as he stroked the cool bronze casing with tears in his eyes:

"Yuan-Shan... The final step we couldn't complete thirty years ago... falls to the children today."

Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan smiled and patted Morris on the shoulder, then turned his gaze upon Cheng Hao with boundless warmth and quiet certainty:

"Hao-Hao, bring forth the three Celestial Relics."

## 二、光柵繞射與三神具合體 (II. Grating Diffraction and Fusion of the Three Relics)

"Yes, Grandpa!"

Taking a deep, steady breath, Cheng Hao unclasped his gear and walked to the marble altar under everyone's watchful eyes.

Three relics that had journeyed across thirty years of peril were laid out under the morning light:

1. The 【First-Generation Ghost Optical Goggles】 etched with micro-code (Optical Polarizing Filter);
2. The golden 【Dodecahedral Ancient Astrolabe】 representing the twelve cosmic coordinates (Spatiotemporal Geometry Core);
3. The pale-violet 【Lyra Optical Astrolabe】 (Acoustic and Spectral Harmonic Tuner).

The instant Cheng Hao brought the three relics together, a miracle occurred—

From within their housings echoed the rapid, harmonious chattering of thousands of microscopic gear teeth!

*CLACK... CLICK... SHING————!*

The prisms of the dodecahedral astrolabe folded into an outer ring pedestal, the amethyst axis of the Lyra Astrolabe transformed into a photonic condenser, and the polarizing lenses of the goggles clicked seamlessly into the apex, merging within a millisecond into a sixteen-inch masterpiece bathed in liquid golden light—**【The Prismatic Sun Core】**!

Yet as the core seated into the base socket, the micro-etched brass sundial upon the pedestal deployed a precision micrometer dial:

```
[FRESNEL ETERNAL CORE DIFFRACTION DISPERSION EQUATIONS]:
Grating Diffraction Condition:
d × sin(θ) = m × λ

[ANCIENT ETERNAL LIGHT SPECTRAL PARAMETERS]:
Base Transmission Grating Line Density: N = 500 lines/mm
Grating Slit Constant: d = 1 / N = 1 / 500 mm = 2.0 μm = 2000 nm!
Ancient Geothermal Crystal Photon Wavelength:
λ = 589 nm (Pure golden-yellow solar spectrum line / Sodium D-line median)
Diffraction Order (First-order principal maximum, energy concentration 98%): m = 1

[DISPERSION ANGLE CALIBRATION LAW]:
sin(θ) = (m × λ) / d
sin(θ) = (1 × 589) / 2000 = 0.2945!
Exact Deflection Angle: θ = arcsin(0.2945) ≈ 17.13° ≈ 17.1°!

Operational Directive:
Rotate the micrometer drum to lock the dispersion angle at exactly "17.1°"!
A deviation of even 0.1° will prevent photons from injecting into the Fresnel focus!
```

"**GRATING DIFFRACTION ANGLE: SEVENTEEN POINT ONE DEGREES (17.1°)!**"

Ye Yijie pushed her glasses up, eyes gleaming with awe: "Cheng Hao! The grating constant across the lens is two thousand nanometers; the crystal wavelength is five hundred and eighty-nine nanometers! Only when the first-order diffraction angle locks onto **seventeen point one degrees** will photons from the mantle achieve perfect constructive interference, igniting the eternal flame at the lens focus!"

"Seventeen point one degrees... Leave it to me!"

Holding his breath, Cheng Hao's fingers gripped the brass micrometer vernier dial.

This time, there was no hesitation. The young inventor's hands—tempered by trials of fire and sea—were as steady as bedrock.

Right then, a pair of warm, calloused hands gently rested over Cheng Hao's.

Cheng Hao looked up to see the boundless faith and gentleness in his grandfather's eyes.

"Hao-Hao, breathe easy. Follow your instincts, just like the heartbeat you felt when you assembled PICO for the very first time."

Grandfather and grandson turned the brass dial together, moving the hands that carried thirty years of dreams.

*Click... 17.0 degrees...*

*Click... **17.1 DEGREES! LOCKED!!***

## 三、點亮永恆之光！ (III. Kindling the Eternal Light!)

*HUMMMMMM——BOOOOOOOOOM!!!!*

The instant the vernier locked at seventeen point one degrees, the eighty-meter Lost Lighthouse resonated with a majestic, sacred chime that reverberated across the ocean!

From the ancient reactor three thousand meters deep within the mantle, an ocean of pure, golden photonic energy surged upward like an awakened celestial dragon, flooding through bronze flumes straight into the Prismatic Sun Core at the lantern center!

*BOOOOOM————!!*

The thirty-foot Fresnel crystal lens ignited in all its glory!

Thousands of concentric prisms burst with the brilliance of a hundred suns rising as one!

A three-meter-wide beam of dazzling, gentle, yet fog-cleaving **Eternal Golden Light** speared skyward from the lantern room, sweeping across the Thousand-Island Gear Sea like a spinning celestial blade!

Ten nautical miles! Thirty miles! Fifty miles! One hundred nautical miles!!

In that glorious heartbeat, the entire ocean was bathed in daylight:

Upon Windward Island, islanders emerged from shelters to behold the golden beacon sweeping across the clouds, erupting into deafening cheers of joy; Lan's mother knelt upon the beach in tears of thanksgiving;

At the Iron Anchor pirate cove, hundreds of clockwork vessels sounded festive whistles as young pirates tossed caps into the morning sky;

And hundreds of miles away at the edge of the continent—atop the clock tower of Lu-Yang Elementary in Gear Town—white-haired Principal Chiu held his twin-gear pocket watch, gazing into the horizon. When the golden beam crossed thirty years of storms and oaths to illuminate the clock hands, the old educator smiled through silent tears...

"Yuan-Shan... Morris... Children..."

"You truly did it."

## 四、英雄的慶典與溫暖的告別 (IV. Heroes' Celebration and Warm Farewells)

That afternoon upon the cantilever flight deck of the Lost Lighthouse, a gentle breeze carried a mouth-watering aroma across the waves.

Wearing his white chef's apron aboard The Aoki Gear, Jiang Jiang swung his legendary cast-iron skillet, stir-frying oversized garlic butter King Crabs with dazzling flair. Little Ding-Ding tugged at PICO's alloy ears, drooling: "Chef Jiang! Are the crab legs ready yet? PICO can't wait any longer!"

PICO gave a cheerful *BARK*, nodding its head as little drumstick icons danced across its optical displays.

"Patience, patience! Special Garlic Butter Crab plus freshly baked crispy pineapple buns—second helpings for everyone!" Jiang Jiang wiped sweat from his brow, grinning from ear to ear.

Along the lighthouse balustrade, Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, Lan, and Buck leaned shoulder-to-shoulder, savoring the ocean breeze.

All evidence of Kuroshio's crimes had been transmitted to the authorities; maritime patrol cruisers had taken the manacled Shen Tian-Cheng into custody, shattering the deep-sea syndicate's conspiracy forever.

"Hey, Cheng Hao," Buck said, a lollipop stick in his grin, hands resting behind his head: "The Coast Guard towed away the Leviathan, but all those autonomous skimmers Kuroshio left behind belong to the Iron Anchor Gang now! With us and Windward Island on patrol, not a soul will touch a bolt of this lighthouse!"

Lan gripped her alloy oar, beaming at Cheng Hao and Yijie:

"Cheng Hao, Yijie, Jiang Jiang! You are honorary heroes of Windward Island forever! Come summer vacation, visit Grandpa Morris and me—I'll take you surfing on the most gorgeous coral reefs in the sea!"

"We will, Lan!" Cheng Hao nodded, bumping fists with Lan and Buck.

Nearby, Old Morris, dressed in clean oilskins, handed two mugs of spiced rum tea to Grandpa Cheng.

"Yuan-Shan, you're really heading back to the mainland?" Morris asked, watching the horizon.

"Aye. Thirty years... it's time to see old Chiu and check on that school in town." Grandpa smiled, sipping warm tea, watching the youngsters laughing on deck:

"Besides, these children have grown faster than I could have ever dreamed. The future belongs to them now."

## 五、尾聲：來自雲端天穹的神秘呼叫 (V. Epilogue: Mysterious Call from the Cloud Realm)

Toward dusk, The Aoki Gear set course for home beneath a pastel sky.

At the stern, Grandpa Cheng held delicate tools, patiently guiding Cheng Hao on calibrating PICO's internal nano-clockwork. PICO rested comfortably across Cheng Hao's lap, purring with gentle mechanical vibrations.

Inside the cabin, Ye Yijie neatly closed the final page of Volume 2: *The Lost Lighthouse of the Thousand-Island Gear Sea*. Etched upon the back cover was a sketch of a golden lighthouse revolving under the sunset.

Curled over his empty cooler, Jiang Jiang snored sweetly against a sack of flour.

The sea was tranquil, the journey smooth.

Yet the instant The Aoki Gear approached the outer harbor of Gear Town—

PICO's ears suddenly snapped straight up!

The miniature antenna on its chest—jointly maintained by Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Grandpa—began pulsing with an erratic, powerful **Stratospheric Long-Wave High-Frequency Signal**!

*KZZZZZT... STATIC...*

Interspersed with the howling of high-altitude gales and the grinding of fracturing gears, an urgent distress transmission filtered through The Aoki Gear's radio speaker from miles above the clouds:

> *"Kzzzt... Calling from... thirty thousand feet... 【The Stratospheric Floating City · Wings of Aether】..."*  
> *"The Gravitational Reversal Clockwork Core... is corrupted by unknown black crystals... central hands are slipping..."*  
> *"The entire floating continent... is falling toward the Earth... at eighty meters per second..."*  
> *"Adventurers on the surface... can you hear us... please save... the City of the Heavens..."*

"The Stratospheric Floating City?! An airborne continent at thirty thousand feet?!"

Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, and Grandpa Cheng sprang to their feet in unison, gazing upward into the boundless midnight sky above Gear Town!

Deep within the glittering tapestry of stars, a colossal, mysterious star resembling a clockwork hand flickered with ominous brilliance...

A new gear had begun to turn, and a grander adventure among the clouds was about to unfold!
