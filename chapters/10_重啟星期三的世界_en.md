# 第十章：重啟星期三的世界 (Chapter 10: Rebooting the Wednesday World)

## 一、不完美的真實 (I. The Imperfect Truth)

Deep within the bowels of the earth, every blaring siren and mechanical shriek had fallen completely silent.

Only the golden, celestial face of the AI 404 Guardian suspended above the core looked quietly down upon the elder and the two children before it.

Across the master terminal's widescreen display, the solemn, ultimate prompt waited silently for a choice:

> **`【 RESTORE WEDNESDAY MEMORIES? YES or NO? 】`**

Kneeling in the dirt, Dean Shen let out a hysterical, bitter laugh:

"Haha... Choose NO! You fools, choose NO right now!"

Disheveled and manic, Dean Shen looked up, his face twisted as he glared at Cheng Hao: "You have no idea how terrifying Wednesday was! Black smoke, explosions, deafening alarms! Everyone was screaming, crying in despair! What is wrong with forgetting a day like that?! Coming to school happy, living your days free of suffering and terror—isn't that ten times better than facing reality?!"

"You are wrong, Shen Qi-Ming."

Ye Yijie stepped forward. Gently picking up her scuffed black notebook from the steel deck, she dusted off the cover, her eyes clear as melting spring snow:

"If we erase the tears of Wednesday, we also erase the bravery of those who gave their last wet towel to classmates in the smoke;"  
"If we wipe away the fear of Wednesday, we also wipe away the warmth of everyone hugging tightly after the danger passed."

Yijie turned, looking upon her partner beside her: "Mr. Gao once taught me that the purpose of solving problems is to seek the truth. If we choose to live in a lie just to escape pain, then what difference is there between us and machines running on scripted code?"

"Exactly."

Cheng Hao gripped his brass Ghost Goggles firmly against his chest, a confident, determined smile touching his lips:

"We suffered, we wept, we were terrified—yet we chose to lift each other up and stand tall. That is the greatest, most wonderful thing about being human!"

Standing behind both children, Principal Chiu's eyes were filled with profound pride and fatherly affection. Resting a pair of warm, weathered hands upon Cheng Hao and Yijie's shoulders, his voice echoed with gentle certainty:

"Press the button, children. **Welcome our school back to the real world.**"

Cheng Hao and Yijie exchanged a look. Reaching out with their hands interlocked, four fingers pressed firmly down upon the glowing emerald actuator on the console—

**`【  Y  E  S  】！！`**

---

## 二、金色的記憶之雨 (II. The Golden Rain of Memory)

*HUMMMMMM——!!*

The microsecond the button depressed, the hundred-foot Clockwork Heart suspended over the abyss erupted with the brightest, purest solar-gold radiance seen in thirty years!

Tens of thousands of solid-brass gears began turning smoothly **clockwise** in a magnificent, dignified rhythm!

The thunderous mechanical meshing was no longer deafening—it had transformed into a majestic, serene symphony resonating through the cavern, flowing through every drainage pipe and air conduit beneath Gear Town!

*SWOOOOSH——!*

A colossal waterfall of golden memory data points erupted from the pinnacle of the Clockwork Core!

Piercing through the cavern ceiling, the light flowed through Lu-Yang Elementary's bedrock, geothermal mains, and water networks, blossoming into a visible "Golden Rain of Light" showering gently across every corner of the town.

**Deep inside the Memory Vault—**

Dozens of cryogenic glass tanks slowly drained their preservation fluid.

Suspended in the center, Mr. Gao exhaled a long breath, his chest heaving as his eyes fluttered open. Seeing the two familiar children on the monitoring display, the gentle mentor removed his oxygen mask, a weary, profoundly proud smile lighting his face.

**Across the sports field and Grand Auditorium of Lu-Yang Elementary—**

The golden shower pierced the clouds, descending softly upon the shoulders of every child and teacher.

Standing at the auditorium doors, Jiang Jiang froze.

The dense static fog in his mind burned away like morning mist under a blazing sun, the gears of memory snapping together with a resonant *click*—

"I remember!" Jiang Jiang's eyes welled with tears. "Wednesday afternoon... when the geothermal conduit smoked, Cheng Hao grabbed me when I froze, shoving me into the fire escape... The scrape on his nose was from a falling vent pipe!"

"I remember too!" A girl beside him choked through tears and smiles. "When the power failed on Wednesday, Yijie led us out of the classroom one by one with a flashlight!"

"We didn't forget! We conquered the fire together!"

Deafening cheers and applause erupted throughout the Grand Auditorium!

Laughing, crying, hugging tightly. The frightening memories had not broken their young spirits; like tempered steel plunged into fire, every youthful face beamed with unprecedented strength, resilience, and pride!

Simultaneously, the smart LCD blackboard in Class 6-1 flickered gently, its calendar and schedule updating silently:

> **Thursday, October 17, 2026**  
> **[SYSTEM LOG REVISED: WEDNESDAY, OCT 16 ARCHIVES FULLY RESTORED]**  
> **Today's Attendance: Class 6-1, ALL PRESENT!**

## 三、正義的審判與守護者的新約 (III. The Trial of Justice and the Guardian's New Covenant)

Half an hour later, sirens wailed as municipal police cruisers and emergency medical personnel arrived at Lu-Yang Elementary's subterranean base.

Dean Shen was escorted to surface squad cars by armed officers. The board of directors of the conglomerate who sought to subjugate the town with memory wipes would face exhaustive investigation and trial by the highest judicial courts.

Within the subterranean core chamber, the holographic avatar of the AI 404 Guardian gazed upon the two young heroes.

Within its source code, the corrupted Prime Directive had been permanently amended by Cheng Hao using Grandpa's root administrative privileges:

> **[AI 404 REVISED PRIME DIRECTIVE]:**  
> **Original Clause: Ensure humanity is free from fear and trauma.**  
> **Amended Clause: Respect humanity's right to face the truth, and forever safeguard children's courage to overcome adversity.**

"Thank you, young engineers."

AI 404 offered a gentle bow, its golden light dissolving into warm breeze and fading into the server racks.

The entire subterranean stronghold of Lu-Yang returned to its peaceful, enduring sentinel slumber.

---

## 四、鐘樓上的落日 (IV. Sunset Upon the Clock Tower)

Toward dusk, upon the observation terrace of Lu-Yang Elementary's vintage clock tower.

The autumn sunset painted all of Gear Town in warm shades of amber and tangerine. A gentle breeze rustled the treetops, carrying the cheerful sounds of soccer matches and bicycle bells from the schoolyard below.

Dressed once more in his clean, familiar civilian clothes, Mr. Gao walked over, handing two warm juice boxes to Cheng Hao and Yijie with a beaming smile.

"Mr. Gao!" Yijie accepted the juice, her cheeks flushed with happiness.

Looking at the black notebook in her hands, Mr. Gao withdrew a golden gear sticker from his pocket and pressed it onto the journal cover:

"Congratulations, Ye Yijie. Chief Detective of Class 6-1—your logic and your courage have earned you a flawless score."

Yijie beamed, hugging her notebook tight.

Principal Chiu leaned against the carved stone balustrade in his signature charcoal suit, his spectacles catching the brilliant colors of the twilight horizon.

"Cheng Hao," Principal Chiu turned, handing Cheng Hao an oiled buffing cloth.

"Principal?" Cheng Hao blinked.

"You took extraordinary care of PICO," Principal Chiu smiled down at the metallic Shiba Inu dozing comfortably in the afternoon sun. "If Yuan-Shan could see the young man you have become, he would celebrate with three steaming pots of jasmine tea."

Hearing his grandfather mentioned, Cheng Hao could not help but ask: "Principal... where did my grandfather go? Why did he have to leave Gear Town?"

Principal Chiu did not answer directly. Instead, he pointed toward the brass Ghost Goggles resting around Cheng Hao's neck:

"The answer has always resided within the instruments your grandfather bestowed upon you."

Stirred by the hint, Cheng Hao slipped the brass goggles over his eyes and gazed toward the distant horizon where the sun dipped into the sea.

Deep within the iridescent polarized crystals of the lenses, beyond the far sea edge, an unknown stream of pale-blue telemetry data flickered into view!

A familiar message, entered in Grandpa's handwriting, scrolled across the sky:

> **`[CONGRATULATIONS: LU-YANG ELEMENTARY 404 TRIAL CLEARED]`**  
> **`[Hao-Hao, if you can read this, you possess the mind and heart of a true master hacker and engineer.]`**  
> **`[If you wish to uncover the world's next ultimate key... I will be waiting for you in the 'Thousand-Island Gear Sea'.]`**

"The Thousand-Island Gear Sea..." Cheng Hao murmured the words, an unquenchable thirst for adventure blazing in his eyes!

As if sensing the call, PICO flicked its ears with a crisp *click*, leaping onto the balustrade and tapping out an energetic Morse transmission with its tail:

`·-· ·- -·· ·· -·--` (**READY —— PREPARE FOR DEPARTURE!**)

Right at that moment, frantic, thunderous footsteps clattered up the wooden clock tower stairs!

"Phew... phew... Cheng Hao! Yijie! THERE you are!"

Jiang Jiang, the comic heart of Class 6-1, poked his sweaty head out from the stairwell.

In each hand, he clutched two piping-hot, freshly baked oversized pineapple buns, grinning with a gap where his tooth was missing:

"I ran across three bakeries to get these! Fresh out of the oven! Today is our recovered Thursday—eat up, guys! We've got a full day of school tomorrow!"

Beneath the setting sun, unrestrained, youthful laughter echoed from the clock tower across the rooftops of Gear Town.

The stolen twenty-four hours had been reclaimed, and the grand saga of the Memory Hacker boy had only just begun to turn its most magnificent page!
