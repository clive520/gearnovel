# 第四章：失控的清潔工機器人群 (Chapter 4: The Rogue Janitor Android Swarm)

## 一、深淵中的滑行 (I. Slide Into the Abyss)

"Surrender the goggles..."

The left cheek of the holographic projection cloaking Mr. Gao's face had completely disintegrated into shimmering green lines of digital code, exposing the cold steel gears and servo motors of the mechanical skull beneath. The hand that once held chalk now popped backward at the knuckles with a sharp *clack-clack*, extending five razor-sharp titanium pincer talons!

With a whistling rush of displaced air, the steel hand lunged straight for Cheng Hao's face!

"Cheng Hao! DUCK!" Yijie screamed.

Cheng Hao ducked on pure instinct. The razor tips of the metal talons scraped across the brass frame of his Ghost Goggles, sending an explosion of fiery sparks showering through the air!

"Fall back! Toward the stairs!" Cheng Hao yelled, grabbing a massive unabridged dictionary from the library reading table and hurling it with all his might into Mr. Gao's face!

*THWACK!* The heavy book was ripped into a blizzard of shredded paper by the alloy claws, dense white pages swirling between them like blinding snow. Unfazed, the android Mr. Gao took a heavy stride forward with a pneumatic roar of hydraulic fluid, his footstep cracking the marble flagstones beneath his shoes.

In that hopeless, life-or-death deadlock—

*HUMMMMMM——!!*

Deep within the forest of colossal inverted brass gears in the vaulted ceiling overhead, an unnatural, high-frequency resonance abruptly shrieked.

Along the southwest ceiling rafters, an aging high-pressure fire deluge pipe surged, as if remotely overloaded by an external terminal. A pressure relief valve hidden behind a three-foot spur gear snapped open with a violent *BANG*!

*BOOOOOM!*

A torrential, icy jet of high-pressure water carrying tons of kinetic force erupted downward like an enraged dragon, slamming squarely onto the crown of android Mr. Gao's head!

The deluge flooded the android's exposed facial circuitry in an instant.

*ZZZZZ-CRACKLE-POP!!*

Blinding webs of blue-violet electrical arcing danced frantically through the android's chassis. Its sensor eyes flared with blinding white discharge, its limbs flailing in uncontrolled, spastic seizures as garbled electronic noise sputtered from its throat:

`[WARNING: MAINBOARD WATER INGRESS... GROUNDING PROTECT... SHORT CIRCUIT... SAFETY PROTOCOL... FORCED REBOOT ENGAGED...]`

"Someone triggered the emergency valve from the ceiling?!" Yijie wiped spray from her glasses, her eyes wide with shock.

Cheng Hao gazed up at the concealed valve. The optical spectrum on his goggles showed clear as day that the remote purge command had been transmitted from a private encrypted channel originating in the Principal's Office!

It was Principal Chiu! He had intervened from the shadows!

"Don't stop to think—it'll reboot in seconds! JUMP!"

Cheng Hao scooped PICO into his hoodie, seized Yijie by the wrist, and leaped boldly into the pitch-black basalt void at the center of the library rotunda!

"WAAAAAAAH——!!"

Neither of them crashed onto hard stone flagstones.

Running down the center of the helical basalt staircase was a mirror-smooth parabolic metal slide coated in a specialized low-friction lubricant!

Frigid subterranean wind roared in their ears as the two children rocketed downward through the gravity chute at terrifying speed, dark utility indicator lights and fiber conduits blurring past like shooting stars.

*RUMBLE-CLANG——!*

A thunderous mechanical crash resonated from far overhead.

Driven by massive counterweights, the library's marble floor tiles slid firmly back into place, sealing shut and completely locking the malfunctioning android Mr. Gao and the dangers of the surface world above.

## 二、地下清潔工兵工廠 (II. The Subterranean Android Armory)

*WHUMP! SWISH!*

At the end of the slide, a massive elastic buffer net caught both children securely before depositing them gently onto a thick mound of acoustic dampening foam.

"Cough, cough..."

Cheng Hao shook his dazed head and scrambled to his feet: "Yijie, are you alright?"

"I'm okay... just scraped an elbow," Yijie pushed her crooked glasses straight and climbed out from the safety mesh.

PICO poked its little head from Cheng Hao's jacket, shaking off dust with cheerful *click-clacks* as its twin sapphire lenses whirred and focused, scanning their surroundings with vigilant curiosity.

When Cheng Hao pulled down his brass Ghost Goggles and took in the view, what he saw made him gasp in sheer astonishment.

This was no damp, cramped drainage tunnel. They stood in a vast, vaulted subterranean hall rising nearly fifty feet high, spanning an area as large as three full soccer fields!

The air hung heavy with the pungent tang of ozone, synthetic motor oil, and humming high-voltage currents. Suspended from the arched ceiling were hundreds of amber explosion-proof tungsten lanterns. Heavy steel cables and brass pneumatic transmission ducts, thick as ancient tree trunks, intertwined across the rafters in dizzying complexity.

Yet what truly chilled the blood was the scene across the floor—

Arranged in disciplined, uniform rows across the expansive steel-grated flooring were over a hundred magnetic charging berths.

And docked inside every single berth rested a dormant cylindrical metal android!

They were the ubiquitous "Campus Cleaning Patrol Androids" seen roaming the school—except here stood over a hundred of them! Some were retrofitted with high-speed scrubbing discs, others with heavy hydraulic steel clamps, and still others with eight-foot pneumatic capture nets!

"Sweet heavens..." Yijie's voice shook. "There are only about ten cleaning bots active on campus at any given time... Why on earth is an entire army hidden down here?!"

"This isn't a cleaning crew," Cheng Hao stared at the serialized military designations freshly stenciled across their armor. "This is a covert subterranean munitions factory!"

The moment the words left his lips, a colossal crimson rotating beacon mounted at the apex of the vaulted ceiling flared to life with a low, vibrating *HUM*!

Accompanied by sweeping beams of revolving scarlet light, emergency sirens wailed through every ventilation duct in the underground hangar:

> `[ZONE B2 INTRUSION DETECTED!]`  
> `[UNAUTHORIZED BIOLOGICAL ENTITIES IN CENTRAL STORAGE DEPOT]`  
> `[ENGAGING AUTOMATED DEFENSE PROTOCOL——AWAKEN ALL CLEANING UNITS!]`

## 三、鋼鐵蜂群的甦醒 (III. Awakening of the Steel Swarm)

*CLANK! CLANK! CLANK!*

Across the floor, the LED status screens of all one hundred cleaning androids flipped simultaneously from dormant gray to blazing, furious crimson exclamation marks!

Rubber tracks spun up at high velocity, grinding against the steel flooring with an ear-splitting metallic shriek.

All one hundred androids ejected their charging cables in unison. Heavy metal chassis thundered in deafening cadence as an iron tidal wave converged from every corridor, surging directly toward Cheng Hao and Yijie in the center of the depot!

Spinning steel wire scrubbers became lethal spinning saws; hedge-trimming landscaping bots raised razor-sharp dual-action alloy shears; and the vacuum intake tubes of heavy-duty suction units flared with high-voltage stun grids!

*BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!*

PICO leaped in front of Cheng Hao and Yijie, every shape-memory plate on its chassis flaring open as it unleashed a fierce electronic battle snarl. But facing over a hundred steel behemoths, the miniature pup looked like a paper boat before a raging typhoon.

"Cheng Hao! We're surrounded!" Yijie pressed her back against a massive steel pillar, clutching her notebook white-knuckled. "There are too many of them—fire extinguishers and screwdrivers won't stand a chance!"

Behind his goggles, Cheng Hao's eyes scanned the swarm with feverish speed.

In the polarized vision of the Ghost Goggles, none of the hundred androids possessed an independent central processor. Instead, faint blue microwave network vectors beamed downward from the ceiling, linking all of them into a singular lattice!

"They aren't acting independently!" Cheng Hao pointed toward a towering steel control gantry thirty feet away. "Look there! That's the depot's Master Node! These units operate on a distributed Swarm Network! Every movement command is broadcast synchronously from that master terminal!"

"You mean if we shut down that master terminal, every android will freeze simultaneously?!" Yijie's eyes flared.

"Yes! But the console is secured behind safety interlocks!" Cheng Hao yelled. "PICO, clear a path!"

PICO let out a ringing metallic bark. Digging its steel paws into the grating, it blurred into a silver missile, weaving between the dense ranks of androids at breathtaking speed! Darting nimbly beneath chassis and treads, it whipped its tail to short-circuit steering rudders, causing frontline bots to crash headlong into one another!

"Now! SPRINT!"

Seizing the chaotic gap PICO created, Cheng Hao dragged Yijie forward, scrambling hand over foot up the ten-foot steel maintenance gantry!

## 四、邏輯門與三色總閘 (IV. Logic Gates and the Tri-Color Levers)

*SLAM!*

The moment Cheng Hao crested the gantry, he pulled the heavy steel roll-gate shut, temporarily blocking the horde of androids battering the support struts below.

At the center of the platform stood an enormous military-green metal control cabinet.

Instead of a conventional keyboard or touchscreen, the cabinet featured three massive high-voltage insulated lever switches, painted **Red**, **Blue**, and **Yellow**.

Directly above the three knife-switches sat a glowing logic circuit diagram rendered in colored LEDs, accompanied by yellowed, handwritten engineering notes:

```
[SWARM CENTRAL SLEEP PROTOCOL ENGAGEMENT REQUIREMENTS]:
Tri-color inputs (A: Red, B: Blue, C: Yellow); Powered = "1", Disconnected = "0".

Output Logic Formula:
Y = (A AND (NOT B)) OR (B AND C)

WARNING: An electromagnetic sleep pulse will ONLY release
when the final output Y evaluates to "1"!
Incorrect inputs within 30 seconds will trigger high-voltage defense arcs,
incinerating the console!
```

At the top of the console, a crimson countdown timer was ticking down at breakneck speed:

`[00:29]... [00:28]... [00:27]...`

Below, dozens of cleaning bots tore at the gantry's steel ladder. The entire metal superstructure groaned in agony, shuddering violently!

"Logic Gates!" Yijie stepped before the panel, her eyes blazing as she instantly mapped out the truth table in her mind:

"Cheng Hao, listen to me! This is fundamental Boolean Logic—the bedrock of all computing!"  
"`AND` means both inputs must be 1 to yield 1!"  
"`OR` means if either input is 1, the output is 1!"  
"`NOT` inverts the state—0 becomes 1, and 1 becomes 0!"

"We need Y to equal 1!" Cheng Hao watched the timer flash `[00:19]`. "Which levers do we pull?!"

"Look at the first term: `(A AND (NOT B))`!" Yijie pointed at the formula, calculating aloud: "If we set **A = 1** (engage the Red lever), and **B = 0** (leave the Blue lever open)—then `NOT B` becomes 1! 1 AND 1 equals 1! That guarantees the entire first half of the equation evaluates to 1!"

"What about C—the Yellow lever?" Sweat drenched Cheng Hao's palms as he gripped the red handle.

"Under the `OR` condition, as long as the first term is 1, the final output will ALWAYS be 1 regardless of whether the second term is 0 or 1!" Yijie took a deep breath and shouted with absolute authority:

"**PULL RED (A=1)! PUSH BLUE AWAY (B=0)! LEAVE YELLOW AS IT IS (C=0)!**"

"Got it!"

Cheng Hao gritted his teeth, his arm muscles flexing as he hauled the heavy red insulated knife-switch all the way down to the base with a mighty heave! He then threw a punch into the blue switch, locking it open!

*CLANK——BOOOOOM!!*

The logic diagram in the center of the console flared with an emerald-green radiance!

`[LOGIC CHECK: Y = (1 AND 1) OR 0 = 1]`  
`[VERIFICATION PASSED——EXECUTING GLOBAL SWARM SLEEP PROTOCOL!]`

## 五、爺爺的銘牌與沉睡的容器 (V. Grandpa's Nameplate and the Sleeping Pods)

*HUMMMMM——BOOOOOM!!*

A massive toroidal discharge coil atop the gantry erupted with a visible ring of ethereal blue electromagnetic pulse radiation!

Like ripples expanding across a tranquil pond, the pulse swept across the entire underground hangar in a fraction of a second!

Every cleaning android tearing at the gantry froze instantly in place.

The scarlet exclamation marks in their sensor eyes flickered twice before dying completely. Spinning wire scrubbers ground to a halt; pneumatic claws dropped limply to the floor. The vast underground chamber plunged back into total, heavy silence, broken only by the hiss of steam valves venting pressure.

"We did it..." Cheng Hao sank against the console, panting in exhaustion.

PICO bounded up onto the gantry, nuzzling against Cheng Hao's ankle with contented electronic purrs.

Cheng Hao turned around, pulling out his screwdriver pen to inspect the internal circuitry. But as his flashlight beam illuminated the interior of the cooling vent behind the panel, his gaze suddenly froze.

Welded beneath the heavy copper busbars was a solid-brass identification plaque the size of a postcard.

Etched into the brass with a mechanical punch-press were two rows of traditional characters and a date:

> **[LU-YANG SUBTERRANEAN SAFETY GRID · AUXILIARY CONTROL NODE]**  
> **Chief Mechanical Engineer: Cheng Yuan-Shan**  
> **System Security Consultant: Chiu Shou-Jen**  
> **October 1996**

"Cheng Yuan-Shan..." Cheng Hao's hand trembled as he touched the cold brass plate. "That's my grandfather's name."

"And Chiu Shou-Jen..." Yijie gasped, covering her mouth. "That is Principal Chiu's full legal name! They built this place together thirty years ago!"

This underground labyrinth was never an evil stronghold erected by mysterious invaders—it was a subterranean fortress designed by Grandpa Cheng and Principal Chiu to protect Lu-Yang Elementary!

"Then... who seized control of the system?" Cheng Hao's eyes hardened with determination.

Walking across the sea of immobilized androids, the two followed the reinforced blast rails toward the far end of the hangar.

At the terminus of the hall stood a massive chamber sealed behind dual airtight lead vault doors, stenciled in heavy black enamel:

`[ZONE B2 CORE: MEMORY VAULT]`

Behind a two-foot-thick reinforced observation window, a faint, eerie blue glow pulsed within.

Cheng Hao and Yijie tiptoed to the window and peered inside—

What met their eyes made their hearts skip a beat!

Floating inside a massive circular cooling reservoir were dozens of giant cylindrical glass tanks filled with translucent pale-blue liquid. Suspended inside every single tank was a sleeping human being!

And floating inside the largest central tank, wearing his familiar navy-blue knit sweater and wire glasses, was a middle-aged man with his eyes closed, breathing through an oxygen mask.

It was—**the real Mr. Gao of Class 6-1**!

"Mr. Gao is in there... then who was chasing us in the library...?" Yijie's voice shook with utter disbelief.

Yet before Cheng Hao could speak, a solitary spotlight clicked on behind the darkened control console inside the chamber.

A tall, dignified silhouette slowly swiveled his leather armchair, turning to face the two children through the observation glass.

The man wore gold-rimmed reading glasses that caught the light, his fingers steepled upon his lap. Gazing through the bulletproof pane with calm, unfathomable eyes, he looked directly at Cheng Hao and Yijie.

It was—**Principal Chiu**!
