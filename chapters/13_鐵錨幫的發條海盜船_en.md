# 第十三章：鐵錨幫的發條海盜船 (Chapter 13: The Clockwork Pirate Ship of the Iron Anchor Gang)

## 一、海盜的榮譽與結盟的號角 (I. Pirate's Honor and the Horn of Alliance)

*SCREEECH——BANG!*

The blinding crimson distress flare etched a thick trail of sulfurous smoke against the twilight sky beyond Windward Island.

Along the old docks, the island's tranquil waterborne village was thrown into immediate commotion. Adults ushered children inside stilt cabins, heavy wooden shutters carved with seabird totems slamming shut one after another.

"That is Old Lighthouse Keeper Morris's private distress beacon!"

Lan stood upon the deck of The Aoki Gear, knuckles white around her folding alloy oar, veins bulging against her tanned skin as fury ignited within her spirited eyes:

"Three months ago, the eternal beam of the Lost Lighthouse died without warning, plunging the sea into fog. Grandpa Morris has been standing solitary vigil aboard his patrol skiff near the reefs to investigate... How dare those greedy Kuroshio Heavy Industries vultures strike right at our doorstep?!"

"Kuroshio Heavy Industries?" Ye Yijie adjusted her titanium glasses, quickly retrieving intelligence from her notebook: "The maritime tech conglomerate headquartered on the Western Continent? Shen Qi-Ming, who was arrested in Volume 1, received offshore financing from Kuroshio!"

"A nest of vipers coiled around the same stone!" Cheng Hao pulled down his brass Ghost Goggles, his expression cold and focused. "Morris was Grandpa's brother-in-arms thirty years ago. We are going to save him!"

"Count me in! My pineapple buns demand peaceful waters!" Jiang Jiang jammed his cast-iron skillet onto his tool belt, gripping the steam throttle valve beside the helm.

*HOOOOONK————BLARE————!!*

Just as The Aoki Gear prepared to cast off, a deafening, thunderous horn blasted through the harbor channel!

Spewing plumes of dense black coal smoke, the iron dreadnought "Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor" carved through the water, cutting across The Aoki Gear's flank with aggressive swagger!

Captain Buck stood atop the bow anchor catapult in his black naval greatcoat, his gear eyepatch glinting with metallic coldness. Slamming his steam harpoon gun against the deck, he grinned with haughty, untamed bravado:

"Hey! Landlubbers! Did you think the Thousand-Island Gear Sea was your backyard bathtub?!"

"Buck! Are you trying to rob us in the middle of a crisis?!" Lan snapped.

"Rot!" Buck spat into the foam, scowling: "I am a pirate, but I was born and bred an honest clockwork pirate of the Thousand-Island Sea! Those corporate dogs from Kuroshio treat our home waters like their private oil basin, and now they want to drag off Old Morris?! Have they asked the iron anchor cannon on my bow?!"

Standing behind Buck, ten-year-old brute-strength powerhouse Ding-Ding brandished an oversized rusted hammer, cheering with delight:

"Allies! Let's smash Kuroshio's tin cans together! Once we win, I get to borrow the silver puppy for a whole night of cuddles!"

Cheng Hao looked upon Buck's soot-streaked yet fiercely proud face. A smile touched his lips as he extended a closed fist toward the young pirate captain:

"The Aoki Gear takes high-speed tactical breakthrough; the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor provides heavy suppressive firepower. Care to wager who shatters the enemy flagship first?!"

Buck slammed his armored fist against Cheng Hao's, sparks dancing from the collision:

"You're on! Loser scrubs the fleet's boilers for a whole month! Iron Anchor Gang—HELM OVER! MAXIMUM REVS!!"

## 二、鋼鐵死神：高壓機械獵鯊 (II. The Steel Reapers: High-Pressure Mechanical Hunting Sharks)

*SPLAAASH——!*

Both warships sounded general quarters in unison, tearing across the emerald lagoon like two roaring dragons of black iron and polished bronze, charging at twenty knots toward the crimson flare three kilometers away!

The moment the vessels cleared the lagoon channel, the savage scene before them made every heart skip a beat.

Stranded on the jagged coral teeth of the isolated "Black Crag Reef" was a battered twenty-foot vintage patrol skiff, her hull pierced in dozens of places as seawater surged into her bilges.

And churning within a six-hundred-foot radius around the skiff, the ocean boiled like a cauldron!

A dozen streamlined behemoths thirteen feet long, sheathed in cold-rolled deep-sea alloy plating, patrolled the perimeter at terrifying speeds exceeding forty knots!

Carbon-fiber dorsal fins sharp as razor blades broke the surface, their snouts armed with dual rows of tungsten-steel saw teeth driven by high-pressure hydraulic rams that pulsed with malevolent crimson luminescence. Every lash of their tails carved cavitation wakes through the dark swells!

More formidable still: stationed several hundred yards behind the pack lay a sleek, windowless black autonomous surface mothership over a hundred and fifty feet long. Its revolving crimson radar dome broadcasted high-frequency command pulses, coordinating the hunting sharks for the final slaughter!

"Kuroshio Heavy Industries' **'Deep-Sea Purger Mk-III' Mechanical Hunting Sharks**!" Yijie held her hydrophone, face pale. "Their jaw clamping force exceeds five tons—they can bite clean through steel plate! The skiff's hull will collapse in less than three minutes!"

*BOOM!*

A mechanical shark leaped from the swell, its saw-toothed jaws clamping shut to shear off a massive section of the patrol skiff's rudder! Splintered timber and fiery electrical sparks exploded into the twilight air!

"FIRE CANNON!! BLOW THOSE TIN SQUIDS TO SCRAP!!"

Captain Buck roared, manually tripping the anchor cannon's firing actuator!

*THUUUUMP————!!*

With a concussive roar of thunder, a four-hundred-pound barbed solid clockwork anchor shot through billows of black smoke, slamming squarely onto the dorsal armor of a mechanical shark!

*CLANG!*

Yet what followed stunned everyone aboard—

The devastating kinetic strike, capable of caving in a cargo hold, merely scraped blinding sparks across the shark's ultra-hard tungsten-carbide shell, ricocheting harmlessly away! The shark did not even flinch, its cold electronic sensor eye pivoting to lock onto the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor!

"Useless!" Lan shouted from the skimmer on the flank. "Their hulls are deep-sea pressure-tempered! Solid shot won't penetrate! Their sole vulnerability is the acoustic sonar guidance array in their skulls!"

## 三、聲波共振與水翼衝浪極限衝鋒 (III. Acoustic Resonance and the Hydrofoil Surf Charge)

"If kinetic strikes fail, we shatter them with acoustic resonance!"

Yijie slipped off her headset, slapping a high-frequency acoustic spectrogram onto the chart table:

```
[KUROSHIO MECHANICAL HUNTING SHARK PACK TELEMETRY]:
Sonar Guidance Center Frequency: f = 40 kHz (40,000 Hz) Ultrasonic
Speed of Sound in Seawater: v ≈ 1500 m/s
Swarm Sync Pulse: Continuous sweeping at 120 pulses/second

Acoustic Decryption Core:
Wave Equation Wavelength: λ = v / f
λ = 1500 / 40000 = 0.0375 m = 3.75 cm!

Vulnerability Assessment:
Each shark's cranial vertex features an acoustic cavity
measuring exactly 3.75 cm in length!
Injecting an anti-phase (180° offset) acoustic wave at identical frequency
will trigger immediate catastrophic overload
in their internal piezoelectric ceramic transducers!
```

"**WAVELENGTH 3.75 CM! DESTRUCTIVE INTERFERENCE!**"

Intellectual brilliance blazed in Yijie's eyes: "Cheng Hao! The sharks communicate at 40 kHz! If we drive a reverse acoustic probe into the cranial resonance chamber of the pack alpha, it will trigger a domino chain-reaction short across the entire pack's sonar lattice!"

"Who can get close enough to a pack of steel monsters moving at forty knots?!" Buck roared over the wind.

"I can!"

Cheng Hao raised his head, patting the poised silver hound beside him: "PICO! Transform——**Hydrofoil Surf Mode**!"

*CLACK-CLACK-SHING——!*

With a dazzling ripple of memory alloy, PICO's chassis unfolded into a three-foot-wide dual-tier hydrofoil surfboard etched with flow-channel grooves! Its tail water-jet turbine emitted a pure, high-frequency whine as twin superconducting skimming runners locked into place!

Cheng Hao drew his multi-tool screwdriver pen, extending its conductive probe into a twelve-inch acoustic transmitting antenna, dialing the frequency selector firmly to the **40 kHz** anti-phase detent!

"Jiang Jiang! Maximum launch boost!" Cheng Hao planted one foot on the board, poised heroically at the bulwark.

"You got it! Behold my ultimate culinary propulsion—Special Reserve High-Pressure Steam Catapult!"

Jiang Jiang bellowed, slamming his cast-iron skillet down upon The Aoki Gear's emergency steam dump valve!

*BOOOOOM————!!*

A roaring jet of superheated steam erupted from the stern. Cheng Hao, riding the hydrofoil board, shot across the dark swells like a silver javelin, knifing through the night spray at terrifying speed!

## 四、波峰上的致命一刺 (IV. The Lethal Strike on the Crest)

"Insane! The kid is surfing over a pack of sharks!" Captain Buck's binoculars nearly slipped from his fingers.

Out on the open water, the sun had plunged beneath the horizon, leaving midnight-black swells rolling beneath the stars.

Crouched low, Cheng Hao maintained his center of gravity against the hurricane-force slipstream. PICO's vacuum suction pads locked firmly to Cheng Hao's soles, its water-jet turbine spewing a ten-foot white rooster tail!

Forty-five knots!

Through the high-sensitivity infrared mode of the Ghost Goggles, ambient ocean clutter dissolved, revealing a sixteen-foot Alpha Shark gleaming with golden alloy plating rising from the depths flanked by three wing-sharks, its gaping maw surging straight toward Cheng Hao's landing vector!

Fifteen feet! Ten feet! Three feet!

"Lan! NOW!" Cheng Hao roared into the gale.

"Leave it to me!"

Flanking the swell, Lan's steam skimmer "Seagull" executed an impossible high-speed power slide into the crest! Heaving the helm, Lan pitched the skimmer's port hull high into the air, creating a natural thirty-degree inclined launch ramp across the water!

"HIT THE RAMP!!"

Cheng Hao locked eyes on the ramp, steering PICO's runners straight up the upturned hull of the Seagull!

*WHOOOOSH————!*

The hydrofoil board rode the kinetic launch into the sky, carving a radiant twenty-foot silver arc against the stars!

The exact millisecond the Alpha Shark breached with snapping steel jaws, Cheng Hao executed an acrobatic lateral twist in midair. Gripping his acoustic tool pen blazing with blue high-frequency arcing light, he drove the antenna probe straight into the 3.75-centimeter cooling slit on the shark's skull!

*ZZZZZZZZ————BOOOOOM!!*

**A 40 kHz anti-phase destructive resonance pulse flooded the cranial cavity!**

The devastating physics of wave interference detonated within the enclosed chamber!

The ultrasonic pulse created a phase-cancellation feedback loop, the localized acoustic pressure pulverizing the piezoelectric ceramic sensor into microscopic dust in less than a millisecond!

*ZZZZ-POP-CRACKLE!*

The Alpha Shark's crimson sensor eyes died instantly, blinding electrical arcs thrashing along its armored spine like furious lightning serpents! Deadlier still, its scrambled anti-phase interference screamed outward through the seawater medium!

Every mechanical shark in the hunting pack suffered instant, catastrophic navigation failure! Their tail fins seized in violent spasms, tumbling blind through the swells to smash headlong into the basalt cliffs of the Black Crag Reef, shattering into scrap iron that sank into the deep!

"DIRECT HIT! ENEMY SWARM ANNIHILATED!!" Jiang Jiang cheered from the deck of The Aoki Gear, pumping his fists!

"Ding-Ding! Anchor cannon! Drive off the mothership!" Buck seized the tactical initiative, firing another heavy anchor.

The barbed iron anchor whistled through the night, smashing the unmanned mothership's radar mast to pieces. With its swarm destroyed and telemetry severed, the Kuroshio mothership deployed a cloud of black defensive smoke, submerging in hasty retreat into the outer ocean.

## 五、深海利維坦與爺爺的手寫信 (V. The Deep-Sea Leviathan and Grandpa's Letter)

Cheng Hao landed cleanly upon the pebble beach of the Black Crag Reef. PICO reassembled into its Shiba Inu form with fluid clicks, shaking spray from its coat and giving a proud bark toward the pirate ship:

*BARK!*

Across the water, Ding-Ding's eyes sparkled with awe, clapping her little hands until they were pink.

Buck stood with hands on his hips, watching Cheng Hao walk through the surf. The swagger in his posture finally softened into genuine, deep-seated respect, muttering under his breath: "Fine... Those landlubbers have real iron in their blood."

Lan, Yijie, and the others brought their ships alongside, rushing into the wreckage of the flooded patrol skiff.

Yet when Cheng Hao pried open the twisted cabin door with his screwdriver pen, the scene within made everyone's hearts sink.

Lying in the cabin was not Old Morris.

It was a twenty-year-old apprentice in a battered protective suit, covered in bruises and cuts. His chest was injured by fallen bulkheads, his breathing shallow, yet his arms were locked tightly around a heavy brass mechanical case etched with astrological constellations.

"Where is Grandpa Morris?!" Lan rushed to his side, lifting his head with tears in her eyes.

The young apprentice coughed weakly, blood trickling from the corner of his mouth as his trembling finger pointed toward the bottomless black trench offshore:

"Master Morris... half an hour ago... was forcibly dragged into the ten-thousand-meter rift... by Kuroshio's deep-sea flagship, the Leviathan..."

"The Chairman of Kuroshio came in person... They are forcing Master to unlock the final portal to the ancient seabed ruins..."

Summoning the last of his strength, the apprentice shoved the brass case into Cheng Hao's arms:

"Before he was taken... Master ordered me... if I encountered a boy wearing brass Ghost Goggles... I had to give this to him..."

His strength spent, the apprentice lapsed into unconsciousness. Jiang Jiang rushed forward with clean bandages and warm water from his hamper to tend his wounds.

Under everyone's solemn gaze, Cheng Hao unlocked the heavy brass case.

*Click.*

Within lay no gold or jewels, but a gleaming dodecahedral "Ancient Astrolabe Core" carved from gold and meteoric crystal.

And beneath the astrolabe's pedestal lay a yellowed sheet of machine-oil-stained parchment.

The handwriting upon it was bold, steady, and unmistakably familiar—

> **[Hao-Hao:]**  
> **[If you have opened this case, you have successfully crossed the first tide of the Gear Sea.]**  
> **[Do not fear for Morris, and do not fear for me. Kuroshio Heavy Industries seeks not merely the energy beneath the sea, but the 'Celestial Key' buried here for three hundred years.]**  
> **[The true trial lies at the 'Lost Lighthouse' on the floor of the Abyssal Rift. Guard this astrolabe well; only when its twelve prism facets align with Polaris atop the lighthouse will the abyssal seal dissolve.]**  
> **[I am waiting for you at the deepest heart of the lighthouse.]**  
>  
> —— **Your Grandfather: Cheng Yuan-Shan**

"Grandpa..." Cheng Hao clutched the parchment, his eyes misting over as the lingering haze in his soul dissolved into absolute clarity.

Grandpa was alive!

And he was waiting down in the depths of the legendary Lost Lighthouse!

*RUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

Suddenly, miles offshore in the midnight deep, the ocean surface bulged upward into a terrifying water mountain dozens of yards high!

Breaking through the dark waves from the bottomless trench was a colossal leviathan over six hundred feet long, resembling an ancient primeval steel whale!

Atop the conning tower, a colossal, blood-red deep-sea cyclops searchlight snapped on, casting an icy, piercing crimson glare across the black waters squarely onto the children upon the reef!

It was Kuroshio Heavy Industries' deep-sea juggernaut flagship—**"Leviathan-0"**!

The clarion call for the ultimate battle that would decide the fate of the Gear Sea had sounded!
