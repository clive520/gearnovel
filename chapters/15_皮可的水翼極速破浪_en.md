# 第十五章：皮可的水翼極速破浪 (Chapter 15: PICO's High-Velocity Hydrofoil Breaker)

## 一、深海懸崖上的最後倒數 (I. The Final Countdown on the Abyssal Cliff)

In the fathomless depths of the ten-thousand-meter Abyssal Rift, water temperatures hovered near freezing, yet dozens of geothermal vents encircling the Lost Lighthouse gurgled furiously, spewing clouds of blistering white bubbles.

The clash of icy deep-sea currents and superheated thermal plumes forged rippling, visible haloclines around the eighty-meter bronze sentinel.

Kuroshio Heavy Industries' two-hundred-foot "Leviathan Extractor"—resembling a primeval iron spider from the ocean floor—clamped its eight train-car-sized hydraulic steel claws firmly into the basalt cliff edge.

And dangling beneath the extractor's towering heavy-duty gantry, Old Lighthouse Keeper Morris was imprisoned within a six-foot transparent titanium diving cage, suspended directly over the bottomless void!

*Tick... tock... tick... tock...*

Shen Tian-Cheng held a gold-rimmed bone china teacup in one hand, while his white-gloved fingers rested with cultured poise upon the red quick-release winch trigger. Glancing down at Cheng Hao upon the deck, a cruel smirk touched his lips:

"Final ten seconds. Ten, nine, eight..."

"STOP! I'LL GIVE IT TO YOU!!"

Cheng Hao took a sudden stride forward, holding aloft the dazzling golden "Dodecahedral Ancient Astrolabe Core" with both hands!

"Cheng Hao?!" Lan gasped behind him, gripping the bulwark railing.

"Don't lose your head! That's the only key into the lighthouse!" Buck shouted, drenched in cold sweat.

Yet none of them noticed Cheng Hao's left hand, concealed behind his back, rhythmically tapping his wireless bone-conduction comms unit, transmitting a flurry of encrypted taps straight into Ye Yijie's earpiece:

`---. --- / -.-. .- - -.-. ....` (**GO CATCH —— PREPARE FOR THE SNATCH!**)

Yijie's pupils constricted, instantly deciphering Cheng Hao's tactical gamble!

"Chairman Shen, the astrolabe is here, but it weighs over twenty pounds—I can only rig a towline to hoist it to you!" Cheng Hao shouted, feigning panic as he shuffled toward the port railing of The Aoki Gear.

"A wise bird chooses the right branch. Drop it onto my manipulator claw!" Greed flashed in Shen Tian-Cheng's eyes as he leaned eagerly forward.

In that microsecond when Shen Tian-Cheng's attention was completely ensnared by the golden relic—

"BUCK! SMOKE SCREEN! DING-DING, SEVER THE CABLE!" Cheng Hao roared with earth-shattering authority!

## 二、水翼升力矩與地熱亂流的極限計算 (II. Hydrofoil Lift and Geothermal Turbulence Calculations)

"I'VE BEEN WAITING FOR THIS ALL DAY!!"

Aboard the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor, Captain Buck bellowed, stomping his boot down upon the deck smoke-pedal!

*BOOM——CRACK!*

A dozen pirate-engineered sulfur-cuttlefish smoke mortar rounds shrieked into the air, detonating directly before the observation bridge of the Leviathan Extractor, blinding Shen Tian-Cheng's viewing dome in an impenetrable curtain of inky black smog!

"Little wretches! You dare play me?! DIE, OLD MAN!!"

Robbed of vision, Shen Tian-Cheng mashed the quick-release actuator in a spasm of blind fury!

*CLACK!*

The hydraulic winch claw snapped open. The multi-ton titanium cage carrying Old Morris plunged like an untethered lead sinker toward the pitch-black, bottomless ten-thousand-meter abyss!

"Downward acceleration $g' \approx 8.2\,\text{m/s}^2$! Only a hundred feet to the abyssal suction zone!"

Yijie's eyes remained glued to the sonar telemetry, her lips moving at hyper-computational speed:

```
[HYDROFOIL LIFT CALCULATION EQUATION]:
L = 1/2 × C_L × ρ × v² × A

Parameter Baseline:
Total mass of cage + occupant + Cheng Hao: W ≈ 7,000 N
PICO dual-tier hydrofoil surface area: A = 1.2 m²
Deep-sea geothermal seawater density: ρ ≈ 980 kg/m³
Hydrofoil lift coefficient at 18° angle of attack: C_L ≈ 0.85

Calculating critical takeoff velocity v:
7000 = 0.5 × 0.85 × 980 × v² × 1.2
7000 = 499.8 × v²
v² ≈ 14.00 ➡️ v ≈ 3.74 m/s (Basic buoyancy glide speed)

CRITICAL TURBULENCE CORRECTION:
The abyssal rift precipice suffers violent geothermal downwelling,
pulling downward at -6.5 m/s!
To conquer the downwelling and arrest the fall within a 2.5s window:
PICO's submerged velocity must overdrive to——42 knots (approx. 21.6 m/s)!
```

"**FORTY-TWO KNOTS! EIGHTEEN-DEGREE ATTACK ANGLE!**"

Yijie's shriek pierced the comms: "Cheng Hao! You have exactly two and a half seconds! Miss it, and you plunge into the mantle rift forever!"

"PICO! SUPERCONDUCTING OVERDRIVE——**FULL BURST HYDROFOIL MODE**!!"

*VROOOOOOOM——BOOOOM!!*

Cheng Hao's soles locked with vacuum grip onto PICO's four-foot dual-tier alloy hydrofoil board. The micro-turbine at PICO's stern flared with blinding sapphire electrical arcs, and with a roaring cavitation howl like a surging dragon, the board rocketed across the dark swell at impossible speed!

## 三、波谷深處的極限救贖 (III. Extreme Salvation in the Trough)

*WHOOOOOSH——!*

Abyssal slipstreams slammed against Cheng Hao's brass Ghost Goggles like steel blades, tremendous ambient water pressure bearing down upon the boy's chest.

In the high-sensitivity infrared HUD, the transparent titanium cage was plummeting through forests of glowing blue coral branches, falling straight toward the gaping jaws of the midnight trench!

One and a half seconds before total abyss entry!

"Thirty feet! Fifteen feet!"

Crouched low against the torrent, Cheng Hao's knuckles turned white around the board's pressure rudder.

Guided by the scarlet beams from PICO's eyes, the submerged turbine spun past ten thousand RPM, compressing flow along the lower foil surface to create a massive pressure differential—

Tremendous **Dynamic Lift** solidified in a tenth of a second!

Banking along the trench wall at a suicidal forty-five-degree dive, Cheng Hao knifed straight beneath the falling cage!

"PICO! MAGNETIC GRAPPLING JAWS!!"

*CLANG!*

Memory-alloy plates along PICO's prow flowed forward, morphing into two muscular hydraulic grappling arms that clamped violently onto the cage's reinforced steel undercarriage!

"LOCKED ON!!" Cheng Hao roared!

Yet the crushing downward momentum and downwelling current instantly dragged Cheng Hao and the board downward by more than sixty feet! The freezing water hammered against his spine like a sledgehammer, the alloy board groaning in structural agony!

"LIFT... **TAKE OFFFFFF——!!**"

Veins bulging across his temples, Cheng Hao threw every ounce of his soul into hauling back the hydrofoil elevator, locking it onto the extreme—**eighteen-degree critical attack angle**!

*BOOOOOOM!!*

Guided by the eighteen-degree hydrofoils, the crushing downward inertia was miraculously inverted into skyward vertical lift!

PICO's turbine blasted a sixteen-foot column of cavitating white bubbles. Supporting the multi-ton titanium cage, the board carved an awe-inspiring, dolphin-like leap through the deep ocean, bursting through the downwelling current to slam heavily down onto the level stone terrace of the Lost Lighthouse!

*THUD!*

The cage touched down safely.

Inside, Old Morris drew ragged, breathless gasps. Gazing upon the kneeling boy before him—sweat-drenched, veins pulsing, chest heaving—the old keeper's eyes welled with tears:

"That grit... that fire in your eyes... Yuan-Shan... did you bring this boy to me..."

## 四、巨械傾覆與落荒而逃 (IV. The Behemoth Overturns and Flees in Ruin)

"Useless! A pack of useless fools!!"

Above the cliff, as the smoke cleared, Shen Tian-Cheng watched Morris being snatched away right under his nose. His face contorted with rage, his bespoke suit ruined with grease.

Shoving his co-pilot aside, he slammed the master salvo lever to the stops:

"LEVEL THE LIGHTHOUSE! NO ONE GETS THE CELESTIAL KEY!!"

All eight colossal hydraulic claws of the Leviathan Extractor reared upward. Six heavy armor-piercing drill heads spun up with ear-splitting shrieks, bearing down like a collapsing steel mountain to crush everyone upon the terrace!

"Trying to squash my old captain?! TASTE CHEF JIANG'S SCALDING OIL SURPRISE!!"

In that razor-thin fraction of a second, Jiang Jiang—wearing thick asbestos furnace mitts aboard The Aoki Gear—heaved an enormous stockpot filled with bubbling, incandescent scalding grease and steam, slinging it forward along the Seagull's towline!

*SPLAAAASH——!*

The superheated oil mist splashed squarely across the exposed rubber hydraulic seals on the extractor's primary knee joint!

The extreme deep-sea cold clashed instantly with the boiling grease, triggering violent thermal shock and material embrittlement!

"Buck! Target the starboard knee seal!" Lan shouted the cue.

"ORDERS RECEIVED!!"

The bow anchor cannon on the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor was already locked on. Buck drove his sledgehammer onto the primer!

*BOOOOOM————!!*

A four-hundred-pound armor-piercing clockwork anchor round screamed through the water, detonating squarely upon the brittle joint bearing!

*CRACK——SHATTER!!*

The three primary starboard support legs snapped like dry twigs! The multi-hundred-ton steel behemoth lost all equilibrium along the cliff edge. With a subterranean shriek of twisting iron, the entire industrial leviathan rolled sideways, tumbling into the bottomless abyss of the rift, throwing up towering plumes of deep-sea silt as it plunged into eternal darkness!

"Wretched brats... MARK MY WORDS! THE LEVIATHAN FLEET WILL GRIND YOU TO DUST!!"

At the final second, Shen Tian-Cheng ejected in a spherical high-pressure escape capsule, fleeing in frantic retreat toward the outer ocean.

## 五、阿基米德浮力重壓水門 (V. The Archimedean Buoyancy Blast Door)

Calm returned to the edge of the Abyssal Rift once more.

"Phew... We won... We actually flipped a mountain-sized steel monster!" Jiang Jiang slumped onto the terrace, smearing soot across his nose, giggling like a madman.

Ding-Ding bounced over, wrapping both arms around PICO in its Shiba Inu form, rubbing her cheek against its alloy ears as PICO rolled its eyes in comic, long-suffering electronic sighs.

Cheng Hao stepped forward, slicing through the cage's titanium locks with his screwdriver pen, gently helping the weathered old Morris out.

The veteran lighthouse keeper gripped Cheng Hao's calloused hands, his beard trembling with emotion:

"Fine boy... You are the spitting image of your grandfather! Thirty years ago, Yuan-Shan dragged me off the reef in a gale in just the same way!"

Morris pointed trembling fingers toward the towering sentinel behind them:

"Yuan-Shan is inside the deepest heart of the lighthouse... Thirty years ago, we discovered the ancient 'Eternal Light Reactor' here. To stop Kuroshio from turning it into a tectonic seismic weapon, Yuan-Shan used his own life-force neural matrix to lock the reactor's core!"

"Grandpa is inside?!" Cheng Hao's heart hammered against his ribs.

"Yes! But Kuroshio's drilling breached the outer hydraulic balance!" Old Morris's face darkened, pointing toward the colossal bronze double doors at the base of the tower:

"This is the **Archimedean Buoyancy Blast Door**! Behind it lie three interconnected mercury ballast tanks. Seawater is flooding the outer corridor; if we cannot balance the ballast buoyancy within **one hundred and eighty seconds**, the hydrostatic pressure will crush the Fresnel crystal lens, sinking the lighthouse forever!"

*HUMMMMMM——!!*

As if confirming Old Morris's warning, the eighty-meter Lost Beacon shuddered violently!

Across the center of the bronze portal, glowing azure glyphs of fluid dynamics flared to life, alongside a blood-red countdown timer:

> `[EMERGENCY: EXTERNAL PRESSURE OVERLOAD AT 100 ATM!]`  
> `[WATER RISING! FRESNEL LENS COLLAPSE IN: 179s, 178s...]`  
> `[IMMEDIATELY CONFIGURE BALLAST DISPLACEMENT RATIOS (V1 : V2 : V3)!]`

A desperate race against time and crushing water pressure began before the ancient gates!
