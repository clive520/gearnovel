# 第十四章：海事旗語與浮標信號迷陣 (Chapter 14: Maritime Flag Semaphore and the Beacon Labyrinth)

## 一、巨獸的陰影與避難迷道 (I. Shadow of the Behemoth and the Refuge Channel)

*BOOOOOM————!!*

The pitch-black sea exploded into torrential white fog as twin ultra-high-pressure water cannons roared from the prow of Kuroshio Heavy Industries' flagship, "Leviathan-0"!

The six-hundred-foot deep-sea nuclear-steam juggernaut was armored in three-foot-thick sound-dampening acoustic rubber and special tungsten-carbide blast plating. Atop her conning tower, a colossal cyclopean red searchlight flared like a bloody broadsword, pinning everyone upon the Black Crag Reef within its icy beam!

"Deep-sea sonar locked! Flooding high-pressure pulse torpedo tubes!"

Chilling, synthesized machine commands crackled through Leviathan-0's acoustic loudspeakers, rattling through the swells to pound against the children's eardrums:

> `[WARNING: UNREGISTERED ARMED VESSELS AND ILLEGAL RECONNAISSANCE ENTITIES!]`  
> `[SURRENDER ALL WEAPONS, THE DODECAHEDRAL ASTROLABE, AND ALL MECHANICAL SPECIMENS!]`  
> `[FAILURE TO COMPLY WILL ENGAGE AREA-WIDE SATURATION ANNIHILATION STRIKE IN 30 SECONDS! COUNTDOWN: 29, 28...]`

"Those corporate cowards—deploying a city-sized steel mountain to bully children! Have they no shame?!"

Captain Buck spat his matchstick into the surf, wiping sea spray with his sleeve, his eyes ablaze with fury. Yet he knew all too well that though his Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor was rugged, standing toe-to-toe against a modern six-hundred-foot industrial colossus armed with heavy anti-ship torpedoes was sheer suicide!

"We can't fight it in open water!"

Lan seized Cheng Hao and Buck by their coats, her hand pointing urgently toward the dense, milky fog bank churning behind them:

"Head northwest! Into the natural exclusion zone of the Gear Sea—**The Twin-Mist Strait & Beacon Signal Labyrinth**!"

"A labyrinth?!" Ye Yijie quickly trained her flashlight onto her soaked sea charts.

"Exactly!" Lan shouted over the wind. "The sea floor there bristles with a dense forest of ancient metal stalactites and clockwork defense spikes! If Leviathan-0's clumsy six-hundred-foot hull dares force its way in, subterranean stone pillars will eviscerate her bilges within sixty seconds!"

"But that maze is barricaded by submerged hydraulic chain-gates every few hundred yards!" Buck added grimly. "The locks open ONLY by deciphering maritime flag signals on the buoys in exact sequence! One wrong input, and barbed underwater capture nets will snag our hulls solid!"

"Leave the deciphering to Yijie and me!" Cheng Hao lashed the brass astrolabe case tightly across his chest, vaulting onto The Aoki Gear's deck. "Buck, your Iron Anchor holds the rear to suppress enemy drones! Lan, your Seagull leads the vanguard to recon the buoys!"

"Let's move!"

Both warships engaged maximum throttle. Just as Leviathan-0's first ranging incendiary shell detonated behind them, the two vessels tore like lightning into the dense, white milk-soup of the Twin-Mist Strait!

## 二、迷霧中的五色航標 (II. The Five-Color Beacons in the Mist)

*WHOOOOSH————*

The moment they plunged into the Twin-Mist Strait, the furious open-ocean swells died down miraculously.

In their place descended an eerie, suffocating hush. The air hung heavy with the reek of volcanic sulfur and ancient bronze corrosion. The sea was as flat as black glass, framed by towering basalt cliffs looming like the ribcages of primeval titans in the fog.

"Forward visibility is under sixty feet!"

Jiang Jiang dialed his heavy binoculars to maximum magnification, balancing precariously upon the mast yardarm, yelling down at the top of his lungs:

"Report from the crow's nest! Massive black iron buoy tower dead ahead! Five signal flags hoisted atop the spire! They're illuminated!"

Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Lan rushed to the bow.

Straddling the throat of the narrow channel stood a fifty-foot antique cast-iron beacon tower. Meshed with heavy hydraulic gears, two colossal cast-iron anchor chains stretched hundreds of feet to either shore, completely blockading the passage!

And fluttering from five halyards atop the tower were five distinct square and triangular maritime signal flags, glowing under electric lamps!

"The **International Code of Signals (ICS)**!"

Yijie's fingers flew across her black notebook, cross-referencing standard international naval flag charts at blinding speed:

```
[FIRST ANCIENT HYDRAULIC LOCK DECRYPTION MATRIX]:
Buoy Base Inscription:
"None but true pilots shall pass the threshold of the deep."
"Identify the five maritime flags in sequence, spell out the ancient Passcode,
and input the acoustic resonance frequency to drop the lock!"

Flag Characteristics Logged:
Flag 1: Deep blue field with a solid white square in the center!
Flag 2: Bright yellow field with a solid black circle in the center!
Flag 3: Yellow-and-black checkered flag in four quadrants (Black upper-left,
        Yellow upper-right, Yellow lower-left, Black lower-right)!
Flag 4: Square flag divided diagonally from upper-left to lower-right
        into equal Yellow and Red triangles!
Flag 5: Vertical tricolor of equal Red, White, and Blue stripes!
```

"Yijie! Enemy vanguard pursuit drones are breaking through the soup! Thirty seconds remaining!" Lan warned, watching a dozen crimson laser designators dancing through the mist astern.

"Don't panic! I am decoding!"

Yijie took a steadying breath, her mind calculating like a quantum processor as her eyes darted between the flags and her charts:

"Flag 1: Blue field with white central square ➡️ International Naval Code **'P' (Papa Flag)**! Meaning: 'All persons report on board; vessel is about to proceed to sea'!"

"Flag 2: Yellow field with black circle ➡️ International Code **'I' (India Flag)**! Meaning: 'I am altering my course to port'!"

"Flag 3: Yellow-and-black checkered quadrants ➡️ International Code **'L' (Lima Flag)**! Meaning: 'You should stop your vessel immediately'!"

"Flag 4: Diagonal yellow and red split ➡️ International Code **'O' (Oscar Flag)**! Meaning: 'Man overboard; take evasive action'!"

"Flag 5: Red, white, blue vertical tricolor ➡️ International Code **'T' (Tango Flag)**! Meaning: 'Engaged in pair trawling; keep clear of me'!"

"String the five letters together!" Cheng Hao shouted.

"**P - I - L - O - T**!" Yijie shouted at the top of her lungs. "**PILOT——The Helmsman!**"

"Five tuning-fork levers on the pedestal!" Lan vaulted onto her skimmer. Skimming past the buoy base, she swung her alloy oar, striking the five bronze tuning forks in the alphabetical sequence of P (16), I (9), L (12), O (15), and T (20)!

*CLANG——PING——CHIME——RING——DONG!*

Five crystalline notes formed a flawless harmonic chord in the mist!

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE——!*

The massive iron lock gate's pneumatic floats purged air with a deafening hiss. Driven by counterweights, the colossal iron chains sank into the deep silt of the channel bed!

The waterway was clear!

## 三、巴克的絕殺：發條鏈彈阻擊 (III. Buck's Masterstroke: The Clockwork Chain-Shot Intercept)

"FULL THROTTLE! PUNCH THROUGH!!"

The Aoki Gear and the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor skimmed over the sinking chain gate with inches to spare!

Yet before anyone could catch their breath, six streamlined "Kuroshio Dart-Drone Pursuit Craft" broke through the fog less than two hundred yards behind them, skimming the surface at fifty knots!

Flanking each drone were six-tube high-pressure underwater armor-piercing torpedo launchers, their crimson targeting lasers locked onto The Aoki Gear's rudder!

"Cheng Hao! Steer straight, don't look back!"

Aboard the trailing Iron Anchor, Captain Buck ripped off his naval greatcoat, revealing wiry, sun-darkened muscular arms. Hauling open the ammunition locker of his bow cannon, he revealed a rare, devastating antique ordnance—two solid cast-iron cannonballs bound together by three feet of forged steel chain!

"My old man's legacy—**Heavy Clockwork Chain-Shot**!"

Gritting his teeth, Buck spun the elevation handwheels, raising the cannon barrel high:

"Ding-Ding! Wind the mainspring to 300% overload! Let these corporate tech-hounds taste the romance of old-school piracy!"

"Clockwork overload 300%! Pressure primed!" Ding-Ding swung her heavy sledgehammer, smashing the percussion cap with a thunderous blow!

*BOOOOOM————!!*

A deafening roar split the heavens within the narrow canyon walls!

Bound by the high-tensile chain, the twin iron balls spun at supersonic velocity through the air, transforming into a screaming **Flying Scythe of Steel Death**, sweeping across the water with cataclysmic rotational kinetic energy!

*CRASH! KABOOOOM!!*

The whirling chain-shot sheared through the carbon-fiber masts and launcher rails of the two lead drones with surgical fury! Severed fuel lines and fractured props ignited in a blinding series of secondary explosions across the strait!

A towering wall of roaring flames engulfed the narrow gorge, sealing the remaining pursuit craft behind a curtain of fire and the sunken iron barrier!

"BEAUTIFULLY SHOT, BUCK!" Jiang Jiang waved his skillet from the crow's nest in wild celebration.

Buck gave a haughty sniff, wiping soot from his nose with a thumb: "Hmph. Told you this sea was my turf!"

## 四、深海大裂谷與囚籠中的老莫里斯 (IV. The Abyssal Rift and Old Morris in the Cage)

Clearing the first signal labyrinth, the two vessels rode the roaring subterranean current, plummeting down a steep hydraulic flume.

Water temperatures plummeted; natural sunlight vanished entirely.

When The Aoki Gear burst out from the narrow limestone tunnel, what unfurled before them stole the breath from every child aboard—

A vast, abyssal subterranean basin spanning miles across lay before their eyes in majestic twilight splendor.

Thousands of feet overhead rolled the black subterranean thermocline. Across the ocean floor, vast fields of bioluminescent azure and violet corals swayed beside towering geothermal chimneys, illuminating the abyss like an enchanted alien forest.

And poised directly at the precipice of a bottomless **Abyssal Rift** miles wide stood a monumental wonder—

An eighty-meter-tall marvel of architectural genius, sculpted entirely from bronze gears, mercury counterweights, and crystal prisms—**The Lost Beacon**!

Yet atop this sacred, ancient sentinel, the giant Fresnel lens lay dark and dead.

Dozens of hydraulic cables as thick as primeval serpents gripped the base of the lighthouse like vampiric vines.

And anchored several hundred yards before the lighthouse was a two-hundred-foot industrial nightmare—the **Leviathan Extractor**, powered by hundreds of hydraulic rams and nuclear-steam turbines, swinging eight colossal excavator talons to batter the ancient stone portal at the lighthouse foundation!

Suspended beneath the extractor's central gantry was a transparent deep-sea cage crafted from high-tensile titanium.

Chained within that cage was an elderly man in an oilskin sailor's coat, his snow-white beard trailing down his chest!

"GRANDPA MORRIS!!" Lan shrieked, tears springing to her eyes!

Though battered and bruised, the old keeper's weathered blue eyes burned with unconquered fire. Hearing the approach across the water, he raised his head weakly toward The Aoki Gear.

At that moment, a smooth, chilling, cultured male laugh echoed from the extractor's PA speakers:

"Heheheh... How delightful. A handful of elementary school fledglings actually deciphered the buoy flags and dismantled my prized hunters and drones."

With that voice, the pressurized canopy of the command bridge slowly hissed open.

Stepping onto the balcony in a tailored deep-sea bespoke suit, a solid-gold pocket watch glinting at his breast pocket, an elegant middle-aged gentleman sipped from a steaming cup of Earl Grey, gazing down at Cheng Hao:

"Allow me to introduce myself. I am the Executive Chairman of Kuroshio Heavy Industries—**Shen Tian-Cheng**."

"Shen Qi-Ming was my incompetent younger brother. No matter; thanks to you locking him away, I am free to claim the ultimate heritage of the Gear Sea entirely for myself."

Taking a delicate sip of tea, Shen Tian-Cheng's eyes locked onto the dodecahedral astrolabe upon Cheng Hao's chest, blazing with unvarnished corporate greed:

"The grandson of old Cheng Yuan-Shan, I presume? That dodecahedral astrolabe in your hands is the final master key to unlock the lighthouse core's eternal dynamo."

"Surrender it obediently. Otherwise... a single tap of this button, and my hydraulic talons will crush old Morris into deep-sea chum!"
