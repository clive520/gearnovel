# 第六章：代號 404 的幽靈走廊 (Chapter 6: The Ghost Corridor of Code 404)

## 一、校長的最後一道防線 (I. The Principal's Final Defense)

"CRACK——! BOOOM!"

With an ear-splitting detonation of shattering composite material, the spiderwebbed fracture across the reinforced observation glass finally surrendered! Crystalline shards rained inward like a deadly hail of razor ice!

Three electromagnetic restraint lances crackling with violet high-voltage arcs plunged through the shattered window frame, embedding themselves with vicious, sizzling sparks into the anti-static floor of the projection vault!

"Target locked: Rogue Administrator Chiu Shou-Jen, and two unregistered student entities."

Through the breached portal, seven or eight Night-Patrol Enforcers clad in deep-violet powered armor vaulted into the chamber, steel-capped combat boots slamming heavily onto the floor. Void of human facial features, three triangular-mounted optical lenses in their helmets burned with predatory, bloodthirsty purple light through the icy mist.

"Run! Leave this to me!"

Principal Chiu shoved Cheng Hao and Ye Yijie behind the bulk of the projector.

The usually austere, dignified old educator moved with an athletic ferocity that defied his seventy years. Flipping open a concealed compartment beneath the console, he hoisted out an enormous, heavy wrench gleaming with dark-gold industrial alloy—the fabled **Magnetic Storm Disruptor Wrench**, co-engineered thirty years ago with Grandpa Cheng!

"Principal! We can't abandon you!" Cheng Hao's eyes burned red with emotion, gripping his screwdriver pen to rush forward.

"Cheng Hao! Listen to me!"

Principal Chiu whirled, barking with stern authority. Yet beneath his reading glasses, his eyes were brimming with boundless trust and warmth:

"These enforcers seek the Root Administrative Keycard on my person. As long as I hold this chamber, not a single one will pursue you! Behind the projector lies a concealed bronze portal bearing the twin-gear crest. Unlatch it, and you will enter the **Ghost Corridor** leading directly to the mainframe!"

Speaking without breaking stride, Principal Chiu threw the induction coils on the wrench with a snap of his wrist.

*ZZZZ-BOOOOOM!!*

A blinding arc of golden magnetic storm electricity detonated along the jaws of the wrench. Holding the massive tool like an immovable bulwark before the shattered observation window, Principal Chiu braced himself against the lunging purple soldiers:

"Remember, Cheng Hao! Trust nothing your eyes perceive! Reach the Core Chamber and unlock your grandfather's final protocol! **A true principal never permits his students to take bullets in his stead! NOW RUN!!**"

"PRINCIPAL——!"

Fighting back her tears, Yijie seized Cheng Hao by the arm with all her strength, dragging him toward the hidden door behind the projector.

PICO blurred into a silver streak, slamming its steel paws onto the bronze latch!

*CLACK!*

The concealed door popped open with a narrow gap. Yijie pulled Cheng Hao, tumbling through the threshold together.

In the final split second before the heavy door slammed shut, Cheng Hao caught a glimpse through the closing seam—Principal Chiu's storm wrench carving a blazing golden crescent through the air, hurling two armored enforcers backward across the floor, before emergency blast shutters descended with a deafening rumble, sealing the battlefield behind them!

## 二、時空的殘影 (II. Phantoms of Time and Space)

"Phew... phew..."

Beyond the secret door lay an ethereal, mind-bending corridor far beyond their wildest dreams.

There was no concrete, no steel girders, and no exposed piping.

The entire corridor stretched for hundreds of yards, its walls, arched ceiling, and floorboards crafted entirely from seamless, transparent **Quantum Optical Glass**, flowing with billions of microscopic optical fiber threads beneath the surface!

Streams of azure and deep-violet data packets darted through the crystalline depths like swarms of glowing fireflies at dizzying speeds.

This was Vault 404's most enigmatic artery—**The Ghost Corridor of Code 404**!

"Cheng Hao, put on your goggles!" Yijie reminded him between ragged breaths.

Wiping cold sweat from his forehead, Cheng Hao pulled down his brass Ghost Goggles.

The instant the crystal lenses settled over his eyes, the silent glass hallway rippled like the surface of a disturbed lake, shimmering with violent optical distortion!

Dozens of translucent, ghostly silhouettes condensed out of thin air—**temporal residual echoes** captured by the optical fiber matrix over decades past!

Near the start of the corridor, Cheng Hao witnessed a young Grandpa Cheng from thirty years ago, clad in grease-stained coveralls and clutching a massive blueprint, walking shoulder-to-shoulder with a young, dashing Principal Chiu, both men laughing with bold, fearless confidence;

Advancing several dozen paces, the scene shifted to a late autumn three years ago. Grandpa Cheng, hair now silver, walked alone through the hall, his stooped silhouette heavy with solemn burden as he cradled the lead lockbox containing PICO;

Yet the most startling apparition materialized thirty yards ahead of them—

**A temporal echo from Wednesday afternoon at 3:30 PM!**

A tall, slender silhouette draped in a pristine white Lu-Yang Elementary faculty lab coat was moving swiftly and suspiciously through the corridor.

Clutched in his hand was an unapproved, custom black flash drive pulsing with an ominous blood-red glow, as he hurried toward the heavy blast doors of Room 404.

As the lab-coated figure passed their position, Cheng Hao leaned in, straining to decipher his face!

Yet the man's facial features were entirely masked by dense pixelated code static. But in the fraction of a second when he raised his right hand to dab sweat from his brow, Cheng Hao and Yijie saw it with absolute clarity—

Bound around the mysterious white-coated figure's right wrist was a braided crimson cord, at the center of which sat an exquisite, miniature **Brass Compass**!

"A red-cord compass bracelet?!" Yijie gasped. "Who on the faculty wears a bracelet like that?!"

"I've seen it somewhere before..." Cheng Hao's mind raced, but before the memory could surface, a shrill, piercing klaxon shrieked down the length of the Ghost Corridor!

## 三、激光迷陣與光學反射定律 (III. The Laser Labyrinth and Laws of Reflection)

*HUMMMMMM——!!*

The transparent glass floor beneath their feet began to tremble violently!

Along the next two hundred yards of the corridor, dozens of precision laser emitter turrets popped out from the side walls with rapid *clack-clack-clack* snaps!

Over a hundred crimson infrared laser beams crisscrossed the air like glowing scarlet harp strings, forming a deadly, impenetrable three-dimensional laser kill-grid! Every single beam radiated searing heat capable of cleaving solid steel, scorching the air with an acrid tang of burnt ozone.

"Perimeter Defense Laser Grid!" Yijie skidded to a halt, yanking Cheng Hao back. "One misstep on an induction tile or breaking a single beam will release thirty thousand volts of high-voltage lightning across the entire hall in less than a second!"

At Cheng Hao's feet, PICO scanned the web of red lasers with rapid *beep-boops*, its sapphire lenses whirring in alarm.

"Look down there!"

Cheng Hao pointed at the floor by the start of the corridor.

At their feet rested three heavy, cast-brass rotary pedestal mounts, each bearing a high-reflectivity silver plane mirror (Mirror A, Mirror B, and Mirror C).

And at the far end of the corridor, suspended above the massive blast portal, pulsed an emerald-green circular optical target receptor.

Etched into the starting pedestal was an antique engineering override manual:

```
[OPTICAL CORRIDOR BYPASS PROTOCOL]:
Guide the starting green calibration beam via reflections
to strike the terminal optical target bullseye with precision,
temporarily taking the crimson defense lasers offline for 60 seconds!

Physical Law Warnings:
Angle of Incidence equals Angle of Reflection (θi = θr).
Corridor width: 6 meters; Green laser source launch angle: 30 degrees.
Configure the deflection angles of Mirrors A and B in sequence,
and deploy a "Compensatory Reflector" at the critical deflection node!
```

A pencil-thin emerald-green laser clicked on from the pedestal, angling diagonally toward the first mirror mount.

"Pure geometric optical reflection!" Yijie pushed her glasses up, equations firing through her eyes:

"Cheng Hao, angle of incidence equals angle of reflection! The green beam strikes Mirror A at 30 degrees. To reflect across the six-meter-wide corridor perpendicularly toward Mirror B on the opposite wall—"

"Mirror A's normal deflection angle must be 60 degrees!" Yijie calculated at lightning speed. "That gives an incident angle of 30° and reflection angle of 30°, projecting the beam across at a composite angle of 60°!"

"Got it!" Cheng Hao lunged forward, gripping the brass mount with both hands and wrenching the dial precisely to **60°**!

*ZING!*

The green beam bounced cleanly off Mirror A, spearing straight through the web of crimson lasers to strike Mirror B on the opposite wall with pinpoint accuracy!

"Now for Mirror B!" Yijie shouted. "Once the beam hits B, it must travel straight down the center of the hall through the two-foot clearance corridor! Mirror B must tilt downward at **45 degrees**!"

Cheng Hao dashed to the second pedestal, flicking the vernier latch to lock Mirror B at **45°**!

The green beam deflected once more, shooting straight down the center of the hall, piercing through hundreds of lethal crimson lasers toward the portal!

Yet just as the beam neared its destination, disaster struck—

Less than ten feet from the bullseye, an aged pane of ceiling glass slipped, falling squarely into the path of the beam, deflecting the green light twelve inches off course into a dead zone!

"Oh no! The beam is off!" Cheng Hao yelled. "There is no fourth mirror!"

If the green beam failed to strike the target within ten seconds, the system would register a failed calibration, advancing the crimson laser wall to incinerate the corridor!

`[00:09]... [00:08]... [00:07]...`

In that split second of ultimate crisis—

*BARK!*

The origami Shiba Inu PICO sprang into action!

*Clack! SHING——*

PICO leaped with astonishing agility, soaring high into midair! Its memory-alloy plates flipped open in flight, and its polished, nanometer-smooth mirror-like chest plate thrust upward squarely into the path of the stray green beam!

*ZAP!*

The emerald laser hit PICO's mirrored alloy chest with pinpoint precision!

Tilting its head exactly 15 degrees in midair to serve as the third compensatory reflector, the mechanical pup deflected the wayward beam right back onto its intended vector—

*DING————!!*

The green laser spot struck dead-center into the circular optical target bullseye above the terminal portal!

## 四、終點的大門與未來的倒數 (IV. The Terminal Portal and the Final Countdown)

`[CALIBRATION SUCCESS: SAFETY PROTOCOL VERIFIED]`  
`[CRIMSON DEFENSE LASERS OFFLINE FOR 60 SECONDS! COUNTDOWN COMMENCING!]`

The sky full of lethal crimson lasers extinguished simultaneously!

PICO executed a graceful somersault, landing lightly on its paws and wagging its tail with proud satisfaction at Cheng Hao.

"Incredible, PICO!"

Cheng Hao and Yijie wasted not a single millisecond, sprinting across the cool glass floorboards with all their might, hurtling toward the sixteen-foot-tall black double blast gates at the end of the hall before the sixty-second timer expired.

Above the portal hung a massive diagnostic LCD display.

Across the screen, the three-dimensional blueprint of Lu-Yang Elementary flashed with a burning crimson countdown:

> `[ZONE: UNDERGROUND B2 - CENTRAL CORE CHAMBER]`  
> `[PERMANENT TOWN-WIDE MEMORY FORMAT COUNTDOWN: 46:15:22]`  
> `[PREPARING PHASE 4: CAMPUS-WIDE DEEP CORTICAL PURGE INITIATIVE]`

"Only forty-six hours left..." Yijie stared at the ticking digits, her face turning pale.

"We have to stop it," Cheng Hao clenched his fists.

Reaching out, he pushed open the massive, freezing black double doors.

## 五、橋上的熟人 (V. An Acquaintance on the Bridge)

The heavy gates slid smoothly open to both sides.

A sweltering thermal wave laced with volcanic sulfur, high-pressure steam, and crackling electromagnetic induction surged out to engulf them.

Unfurling before the two children was a subterranean cavern of monumental scale, resembling a futuristic sci-fi metropolis!

The cavern soared dozens of stories into the earth. Suspended directly above a bottomless geothermal lava shaft was a colossal sphere over a hundred feet in diameter, constructed from tens of thousands of massive brass gears encasing a glowing blue quantum core—the mechanical heart of Gear Town: **The Clockwork Core**!

The gear sphere turned with slow, solemn majesty, each massive tooth engaging with deep, seismic thuds like the heartbeat of an ancient titan: *thump, thump, thump*.

A narrow, suspended catwalk of industrial steel spanned the dizzying chasm, leading straight to the central control dais of the core sphere.

Yet standing directly in the center of that suspended catwalk were two figures.

One was the android duplicate of Mr. Gao—its facial circuitry repaired from the library deluge, now fitted with a heavy-duty hydraulic replacement arm!

Its mechanical eye sockets burned with an icy emerald flame.

And standing beside the android was a living, breathing human being, wearing a standard Lu-Yang Elementary faculty lab coat, hands casually resting in his pockets.

As Cheng Hao and Yijie's footsteps rang out across the steel catwalk, the man in the white coat turned slowly around.

Bathed in the blue luminescence of the core sphere, he offered a subtle, knowing smile.

Fastened securely around his right wrist was a bright braided red cord, and at its center, a miniature **Brass Compass** flashed brilliantly under the quantum light!

The instant she saw that face, Ye Yijie's pupils dilated in pure horror. Her black notebook slipped from her fingers, clattering onto the steel catwalk as she froze, pale as a ghost:

"H-How... How could it be YOU?!"
