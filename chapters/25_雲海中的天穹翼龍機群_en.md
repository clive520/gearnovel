# 第二十五章：雲海中的天穹翼龍機群 (Chapter 25: The Aether Pterosaurs in the Cloud Sea)

## 一、黑雲中的鋼鐵利爪 (I. Steel Talons in the Black Cloud)

Freezing rain laced with walnut-sized hailstones battered violently against the tungsten-steel armor plates of The Aoki Gear.

Altitude: six thousand two hundred meters. At the ceiling of the troposphere, the temperature had plunged to a bitter minus thirty-five degrees Celsius, frosting the tear-resistant envelopes with a shimmering lattice of crystalline rime.

Just as the skyship strained with all her power against horizontal wind shear howling at forty meters per second—

*SCREEECH————!!*

A savage, ear-splitting metallic shriek tore through the thunderous gale without warning!

In the black storm clouds ahead, dozens of pairs of cold, predatory, blood-red optical sensors blinked to life!

A heartbeat later, more than twenty colossal airborne mechanical predators with sixteen-foot wingspans armored in obsidian alloy feathers—**"The Aether Pterosaur Drones"**—dove from the upper cloud deck like gargoyles bursting from the abyss, screaming with sonic-boom shockwaves!

Their talons were eighteen-inch high-frequency rotary titanium chainsaws, while dangerous arcs of high-voltage lightning danced within their razor-sharp beaks!

"Hostiles incoming! It's the ancient autonomous sentinels of the celestial city!"

Cheng Hao threw open the bridge observation hatch, shouting into the biting polar gale.

"Blast it! Why are these iron birds treating us like enemies?!" Jiang Jiang clung desperately to a deck capstan, his running nose freezing into icicles.

"The gravitational core's black crystal corruption!" Ye Yijie's fingers flew across her holographic terminal, her pupils contracting: "The electromagnetic distortion generated by the crystals fried the pterosaurs' 'Optical IFF Transponders'! Their combat protocols have defaulted to an indiscriminate extermination subroutine!"

*ROAAAR-SCREECH——!*

The three lead mechanical pterosaurs banked in a lethal combat arc, their razor talons slashing directly toward The Aoki Gear's golden port lift envelope!

If those talons tore through the pressurized envelopes, the forty-five-ton warship would lose all aerostatic buoyancy within half a second, plunging into the abyss below to be crushed into scrap metal!

"Then we shall break their wings with fluid dynamics!"

"**CRITICAL STALL ANGLE: SIXTEEN POINT FIVE DEGREES! GENERATE A VERTICAL UPWASH OF THIRTY-FIVE METERS PER SECOND!!**"

Ye Yijie shouted toward Cheng Hao over the roar of the gale: "Cheng Hao! The pterosaurs' vulnerability lies upon the upper surface of their wings! The moment they close to fifty meters ahead of our bow, blast a vertical upward gale under their bellies to drive their angle of attack past thirty-one degrees! ## 三、皮可的超音速滑翔翼模態 (III. PICO's Supersonic Glider Mode)

"PICO! Transform——**SUPERSONIC WINGED GLIDER**!!"

Cheng Hao dialed the frequency ring on his screwdriver pen, transmitting the custom superconducting resonance code!

*HOWWWWWL————!!*

Mounted upon the starboard sponson, the silver origami Shiba Inu PICO chattered with the crystalline ring of thousands of memory-alloy plates!

Decoupling from the turbine mount, it leaped gracefully into the minus-thirty-five-degree tempest! Countless silver superconducting leaves folded, unfurled, and interlocked in midair, reassembling into a ten-foot-wingspan mechanical falcon shimmering with ice-blue photon lines!

Dual high-pressure supersonic micro-pulse jet thrusters deployed from PICO's chest, punching an azure sonic-boom vapor cone through the storm clouds like an arrow of light, rocketing straight toward the three diving lead pterosaurs!

Fifty meters! Thirty meters! Ten meters!

In the final millisecond before the three drones swung their titanium talons into striking range—

PICO executed an impossible, razor-sharp ninety-degree vertical climb!

*BOOOOOOOOM————!!*

The dual pulse thrusters on PICO's chest erupted with a continuous, supersonic overpressure blast! A fifteen-foot column of pure, high-velocity upward air (surpassing forty-five meters per second) hammered like an invisible sledgehammer straight into the bellies and underwings of the approaching pterosaurs!

## 四、集體失速與穿越雲海 (IV. Swarm Stall and Piercing the Cloud Sea)

*SCREEECH——CHIRP?!*

The three ferocious mechanical predators uttered bewildered, glitching chirps of alarm.

Hammered by the forty-five-meter-per-second upwash, the effective angle of attack across their wings spiked past thirty-five degrees!

More than double the critical stall angle!

In the next microsecond, the smooth laminar boundary layer hugging the upper titanium wing surfaces tore away violently into a chaotic storm of churning, turbulent vortices!

Aerodynamic lift collapsed to absolute zero!

The heavy obsidian hulls of the three pterosaurs yawed violently in midair, their rudders whipping in vain, unable to catch a single breath of supporting wind!

*CRASH... WHIRRRR——!*

Amidst a chorus of failing servomotors, the three colossal iron pterosaurs tumbled end over end like solid cannonballs, howling as they plummeted straight into the bottomless cloud banks below!

Caught in the wake of the catastrophic downwash, the trailing twenty drones collided in midair in a panic of warning sirens, scattering in complete disorder before vanishing into the endless sea of gray!

"SPLENDID!!" Jiang Jiang banged his skillet like a victory gong across the deck: "Pure physics just snapped their wings in two!"

Carving a graceful swallowtail arc through the freezing mist, PICO settled back onto Cheng Hao's shoulder, purring affectionately against his cheek as golden stars danced across its electronic eyes.

Yet before anyone could breathe a sigh of relief, the clouds ahead erupted with a blinding, incandescent blue-white glare—

The Aoki Gear had plunged straight into the heart of the storm: the **"Ten-Thousand-Volt Supercell Core"**!

Hundreds of thunderous lightning bolts as thick as ancient dragons exploded in all directions around the skyship!
