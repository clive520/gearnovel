# 第二十章：老守燈人的三十年約定 (Chapter 20: The Old Lighthouse Keeper's Thirty-Year Vow)

## 一、三千米地心的熔岩聖殿 (I. The Magma Sanctuary of the 3000-Meter Mantle)

*WHOOOOOSH————!*

The high-pressure titanium elevator plummeted down the vertical basalt shaft at terminal free-fall velocity.

Beyond the cabin walls, the ambient rock temperature surged violently as depth increased. From twenty degrees Celsius to fifty, then climbing to a suffocating eighty degrees! Condensation along the thermal armor exterior vaporized instantly into white clouds of scalding steam.

*HUMMM——CLATTER!*

At the three-thousand-meter mark, just as the depth needle kissed the bottom stop, the superconducting reverse magnetic braking array engaged with a concussive shock!

Crushing deceleration G-forces slammed everyone back into the shock-absorbing bucket seats, the elevator's twelve hydraulic suspension rams bottoming out with a thunderous crash onto the level granite foundations!

With a long pneumatic hiss of depressurization, the twelve-inch double blast doors slid smoothly open to both sides.

The instant everyone stepped through the portal, the breath of the four children and PICO froze in their throats—

Spreading across an expanse of over one square kilometer deep within the Earth's crust was an ancient subterranean magma cathedral!

Suspended overhead was a vaulted dome woven from thousands of interlocking colossal bronze clockwork gears; beneath their feet, rivers of roaring dark-red geothermal lava flowed under two-foot-thick transparent diamond thermal floor tiles, illuminating the magnificent underground realm in an ocean of incandescent scarlet glory.

And at the center of the sanctuary, upon a suspended granite control bridge one hundred feet high, a colossal **Tectonic Clockwork Anchor Array** consisting of seven massive obsidian steel spring columns shrieked in mechanical strain as the continental plates convulsed.

At this very moment, standing solitary before the main tension lever of that gargantuan array, was an aged yet towering silhouette standing straight as an ancient cedar.

He wore a navy-blue cable-knit sailor's sweater soaked with high-temperature engine oil, his weathered hands locked around a heavy manual tension capstan as thick as a tree trunk. Every muscle in his body trembled under thousands of tons of clockwork torque, sweat pouring down his deeply lined face, turning to steam the second it hit the burning tiles.

Resting against his chest hung an immaculately polished brass dual-gear pocket watch.

And perched over his brow was a battle-scarred optical goggle frame, its lenses etched with micro-code.

"G-Grandpa?!"

Cheng Hao's mind went entirely blank, scalding tears bursting uncontrollably down his cheeks!

---

## 二、三十年的重逢與守燈人誓約 (II. Thirty-Year Reunion and the Keeper's Vow)

The elder before the capstan stiffened.

Turning his head with agonizing effort, his bloodshot eyes looked through the shimmering heat haze, settling upon the boy in the orange floatation vest wearing brass Ghost Goggles.

Within those eyes—tested by thirty years of storms and solitary vigil—a flicker of disbelief gave way to boundless warmth, tenderness, and unconquerable pride.

"Hao-Hao..."

Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan's voice was hoarse as sandpaper, yet brimming with undiminished vigor: "You little rascal... you actually wore Grandpa's goggles... and found your way down here!"

"GRANDPA!!"

Unable to contain himself any longer, Cheng Hao sprinted wildly across the granite bridge, throwing himself headlong into the old man's embrace!

Wrapping warm, calloused, burn-scarred hands around his grandson's shoulders, the elder smiled through tears, patting Cheng Hao's back with hearty affection:

"You've grown taller... stronger! A true man who can shoulder the heavens!"

Perched at Cheng Hao's feet, PICO's sapphire optics curved into joyful crescents, nuzzling its alloy muzzle frantically against the old man's pant leg, emitting affectionate electronic whines.

"Good PICO..." The elder knelt down, stroking the silver mechanical pup's head: "Your shape-memory exoskeleton is impeccably maintained; Cheng Hao treated you well, didn't he?"

*Bark! Bark!* PICO snapped to attention and rendered a crisp salute.

Ye Yijie, Lan, and Jiang Jiang stepped onto the gantry.

Cheng Yuan-Shan raised his head, his gaze sweeping over the young heroes with shining admiration:

"You must be the young lady from the Ye family? That sharp, observant intellect behind your glasses is the spitting image of your father;"  
"And Lan... the fiery spirit of Windward Island's storm navigators blazes in your eyes; your grandfather's sea cleaver passed to you, I take it?"  
Finally, the elder's eyes settled on the soot-blackened cast-iron skillet strapped to Jiang Jiang's waist, erupting into a hearty belly laugh:  
"Haha! And this chubby fellow with the skillet! Thirty years ago, our expedition ship's chef dressed exactly like that, never going anywhere without his seasoning kit!"

Jiang Jiang rubbed his crew-cut head sheepishly, hastily pulling a tinfoil-wrapped pineapple bun from his coat, offering it with both hands:

"Grandpa Cheng! You've worked so hard! I baked this special reserve pineapple bun myself—it's still warm! Have some carbs, fast!"

Accepting the bun, the old engineer took an enormous bite, chewing with glistening eyes, nodding repeatedly: "Delicious! Truly delicious! That's the warmest, finest meal I've tasted in thirty years!"

Wiping his mouth, the elder's expression turned solemn as he looked back at the violently vibrating geothermal array, exhaling a long sigh:

"Children, you must be wondering what truly happened thirty years ago."

"Thirty years ago, Principal Chiu, Old Morris, and I were surveying the Thousand-Island Sea aboard The Aoki Gear when we stumbled upon this ancient civilization's core buried deep in the mantle. This geothermal array is, in truth, the 'Tectonic Shock-Absorbing Clockwork' for the entire Western Pacific plate."

"Back then, the previous chairman of Kuroshio Heavy Industries sought to blast the core to seize the crystals. To prevent our ocean from tearing apart, the three of us swore the **'Thirty-Year Lighthouse Keeper's Vow'**——"

"Principal Chiu returned to the surface, safeguarding Gear Town and the children of Lu-Yang Elementary, establishing a surface intelligence perimeter; Morris held the waves, using the Lost Lighthouse's beam to watch over the atolls; and I... descended three thousand meters into the Earth's core, fusing my life with this ancient clockwork array to anchor the mantle fault line."

The elder looked toward Cheng Hao, his face grave:

"Yet now, that monster Shen Tian-Cheng has driven **'Seven Geothermal Seismic Spikes'** into the mantle fractures! Superheated steam is flooding the fault at thousands of tons per second; the entire clockwork array... can hold out for **twenty minutes** at most!"

## 三、七星正多邊形與質數步長阻尼矩陣 (III. The Heptagonal Array and Prime Step-Damping Matrix)

"Twenty minutes?!" The skillet nearly slipped from Jiang Jiang's hands.

"Can't we just yank those seismic spikes out?!" Lan pressed urgently.

"Out of the question!" Grandpa Cheng shook his head firmly. "The spikes are embedded deep in the mantle core, saturated with supercritical geothermal fluid. Forcible extraction would trigger instantaneous 'Cavitation Blowout', spewing tens of thousands of tons of white-hot magma through the crust to unleash a hundred-meter megatsunami!"

"The sole solution is using the Lyra Optical Astrolabe's resonance frequency to inject an **'Anti-Phase Damping Matrix'** into the seven spikes in exact sequence, reducing their internal high-pressure resonance to zero!"

Ye Yijie rushed to the master holographic terminal, her fingers tracing the coordinates of the seven seismic spikes:

```
[TECTONIC SEISMIC SPIKE DAMPING DECAY MATRIX]:
Array Geometry: Regular Heptagonal Array
Numbering: Circular clockwise sequence 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7

Geothermal Pulsation Base Frequency: f₀ = 7.5 Hz
Harmonic Frequency per Spike: f_k = k × f₀ (Superposition of harmonics 1 to 7)

Annihilation Hazard:
Neutralizing spikes in adjacent order (1 ➡️ 2 ➡️ 3...)
creates an adjacent frequency delta (Δf = 7.5 Hz),
triggering violent localized "Low-Frequency Beating Phenomenon",
cracking the tectonic plate!

Mathematical Topology Annihilation Law (Eulerian Prime-Step Cycle):
Follow the diagonal star-polygon trace of the heptagon
with a prime step size coprime to 7: step s = 2!
Modular State Transition Equation:
k_next = (k_curr + 2) mod 7
(If the result evaluates to 0, map to Spike 7!)

Starting Node: Begin at lowest-energy Spike 1!
```

"**MODULAR ARITHMETIC WITH PRIME STEP SIZE! STAR-TOPOLOGY DE-RESONANCE!**"

Ye Yijie's eyes blazed with the sublime beauty of mathematics: "Look! The seven spikes form a regular heptagon! Sequential removal triggers fatal low-frequency beating! We must utilize modular arithmetic with a **step size of two**!"

Yijie jotted the equations across the console:

$$\text{Step 1: } k_1 = 1$$  
$$\text{Step 2: } k_2 = (1 + 2) \bmod 7 = 3$$  
$$\text{Step 3: } k_3 = (3 + 2) \bmod 7 = 5$$  
$$\text{Step 4: } k_4 = (5 + 2) \bmod 7 = 7 \quad (\text{since } 7 \bmod 7 = 0)$$  
$$\text{Step 5: } k_5 = (7 + 2) \bmod 7 = 9 \bmod 7 = 2$$  
$$\text{Step 6: } k_6 = (2 + 2) \bmod 7 = 4$$  
$$\text{Step 7: } k_7 = (4 + 2) \bmod 7 = 6$$

"The annihilation sequence is locked!" Yijie shouted with decisive authority:

"**SPIKE 1 ➡️ SPIKE 3 ➡️ SPIKE 5 ➡️ SPIKE 7 ➡️ SPIKE 2 ➡️ SPIKE 4 ➡️ SPIKE 6!**"

"Cheng Hao! Rotate the heptagram selector on the Lyra Astrolabe and fire the anti-phase laser damping pulses in that exact order!!"

"Understood!"

Cheng Hao took a deep breath, steadying the merged relic in his hands. The heptagram alignment needle at the core of the Lyra Astrolabe clicked with crisp, mechanical detents beneath his steady fingers!

"Spike 1——PULSE INJECTED!!"

*ZING!* A golden-blue laser speared from the astrolabe, extinguishing the crimson glow of the first distant geothermal pillar!

"Spike 3——INJECTED!!"

"Spike 5——EXTINGUISHED!!"

"Spike 7——EXTINGUISHED!!"

As each seismic spike was neutralized, the violent tremors rattling the cathedral subsided miraculously, the magma churning beneath the diamond tiles slowing before their very eyes!

## 四、浴血的莫里斯與電鋸惡魔 (IV. Bloodstained Morris and the Chainsaw Fiend)

"It's working! Only two spikes remaining!" Lan cheered, waving her oar.

Yet just as Cheng Hao aligned the astrolabe toward "Spike 4"—

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE————BOOOOOM!!*

A cataclysmic detonation tore through the vertical elevator shaft behind them!

Plummeting through severed cables and a storm of fiery sparks, the crumpled, burning wreckage of the ten-ton titanium elevator smashed like a meteorite into the perimeter terrace three thousand meters below, shattering into flaming scrap!

And striding out from the flames and smoke, towering ten feet tall in blood-red lacquered armor, was a **Heavy Powered Exoskeleton Titan**, its steel footfalls cracking the granite flagstones!

Mounted along the titan's arms, two high-speed **High-Frequency Thermal Plasma Chainsaws** spinning at two thousand degrees whined with blood-chilling fury!

The armored visor retracted, exposing Shen Tian-Cheng's gore-streaked, completely deranged face:

"Hahahaha... Cheng Yuan-Shan! Did you think hiding three thousand meters down would save you from me?!"

Dragged behind the titan's armored claws was a severely crushed deep-sea emergency escape pod.

Spiderweb fractures covered the viewing dome. Inside lay Old Morris, covered in blood, his oilskin coat shredded, his chest rising and falling with shallow, agonizing breaths after the brutal battle atop the lighthouse!

"GRANDPA MORRIS!!" Lan screamed, tears bursting from her eyes as she raised her oar to charge!

"Lan! STAY BACK! That's Kuroshio's deadlier frontline assault unit—the 'Single-Pilot Plasma Titan'!" Grandpa Cheng shielded Lan behind his body, his eyes cold as glacial ice.

Shen Tian-Cheng revved the sizzling plasma chainsaws, pointing them down toward the old master and Cheng Hao with a hideous, manic grin:

"Old dog, and you clever little runts."

"Seven seismic spikes—you pulled five of them, didn't you? Impressive!"

Shen Tian-Cheng jammed a red self-destruct toggle on the titan's breastplate, sneering:

"But as long as one remains, the micro cold-fusion core in my chassis can detonate it! Surrender the Celestial Key right now... **or every last one of you will burn to ash with this temple in the mantle magma!!**"

*VROOOOOOM——!!*

Both plasma chainsaws whined to maximum RPMs, screaming like an apocalyptic tempest of fire and sparks, descending toward Cheng Hao and Cheng Yuan-Shan!

Only **fifteen minutes** remained before the final self-destruct detonation!
