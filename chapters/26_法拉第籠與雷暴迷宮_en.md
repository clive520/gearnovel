# 第二十六章：法拉第籠與雷暴迷宮 (Chapter 26: The Faraday Cage and the Thundercloud Maze)

## 一、萬伏特雷暴的電弧牢籠 (I. The High-Voltage Thunderstorm Cage)

Altitude: seven thousand five hundred meters.

The world around them was completely engulfed in blinding blue-white lightning and suffocating pitch-black storm clouds.

The gale battered The Aoki Gear like an enraged primeval titan. The vertical electrostatic field strength in the atmosphere had surged to a terrifying three hundred kilovolts per meter ($300\,\text{kV/m}$)!

Along every brass stanchion, instrument bezel, and even at the tips of the crew's hair, dancing tufts of cerulean St. Elmo's fire hissed and crackled. The sharp, acrid stench of ionized ozone filled everyone's nostrils.

Drifting around the colossal dual envelopes were over a dozen floating golden spheres of "High-Voltage Ball Lightning" up to two feet in diameter, bobbing and dancing erratically upon the violent air currents like spectral will-o'-the-wisps!

"Everyone hold fast to safety bulkheads! Power down all non-essential high-frequency communications circuits!" Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan roared, muscles straining as he held the master helm steady.

Yet at that very heartbeat, the deadliest crisis struck without warning!

*CRACKLE——ZAP!*

At the anchor point of the primary envelope harness atop the cabin, a blinding shower of short-circuit sparks erupted into the storm!

One of the arm-thick "Master Braided Copper Ground Straps," battered by the violent wind shear, had sheared clean through its locking detent, whipping violently in the hurricane winds, leaving a lethal fourteen-inch air gap between the cable and the main tungsten keel!

## 三、黑鐵平底鍋的終極接地 (III. The Ultimate Grounding of the Cast-Iron Skillet)

"High melting point! High conductivity! Massive surface area?!"

Crouched in the corner guarding the supplies, Jiang Jiang's round eyes popped open, his hands instantly unbuckling the soot-blackened, legendary cast-iron skillet from his belt!

"USE MY SKILLET!!" Jiang Jiang bellowed at the top of his lungs.

"The skillet?!" Cheng Hao and Yijie stared in disbelief.

"Aye! My grandfather forged this beast with deep-sea dark iron blended with virgin red copper! Eight millimeters thick and sixteen inches across! Melts above fifteen hundred degrees Celsius—it's the exact width to jam that fourteen-inch gap solid!"

Donning two heavy heat-resistant asbestos gloves without a second thought, Jiang Jiang took a deep breath. Braving the storm of incandescent lightning that tore the sky asunder, he scrambled across the pitching deck straight toward the howling gap atop the cabin!

"Jiang Jiang! WATCH THE LIGHTNING!" Cheng Hao's eyes burned red as he lunged forward, locking a safety carabiner onto his best friend's belt.

"MY SKILLET... HAS NO RIVAL IN THIS WORLD!!"

With a primal roar, Jiang Jiang summoned every ounce of his strength, slamming the heavy iron skillet down into the break with an earth-shattering *CLANG*!

Both rims of the skillet locked securely against the severed copper tracks, providing thousands of square millimeters of solid conductive cross-section!

"PICO! TRANSFORM——HIGH-VOLTAGE SUPERCONDUCTING CLAMP!!"

*BARK!!*

PICO transformed into dual blue-glowing superconducting vises, locking down on the skillet's handles with lightning speed, crushing contact resistance down to 0.001 ohms!

The fully closed Faraday equipotential circuit——RESTORED!

In the exact millisecond the circuit closed—

*BOOOOOOOOOOOM————!!!!*

A blinding, three-foot-thick pillar of **"Million-Volt Downward Lightning"**—incandescent enough to sear the optic nerves of the world—descended like a god's war lance, striking dead-center upon the upturned bottom of the cast-iron skillet!

## 四、雷光洗禮與衝出對流層 (IV. Baptism of Lightning and Piercing the Troposphere)

An explosion of transcendent white brilliance washed over the deck!

Yet in that glorious microsecond, the triumphant marvel of physical law stood absolute—

That apocalyptic, hundred-thousand-ampere torrent capable of vaporizing steel girders was instantly captured and diverted by the superconducting ground circuit!

Channeling into dazzling sheets of golden-blue electric serpents, the charge cascaded harmlessly across the skillet, the tungsten armor, and the conductive lattice of the envelopes. Funneled through the trailing plasma discharge wicks at the stern, it vented harmlessly into the cloud deck like two glorious golden cometary tails!

Inside the Faraday Cage, the instrument needles didn't flinch. The boilers hummed in tranquil safety, not a single spark penetrating the sanctum!

"WE DID IT!! ELECTROSTATIC SHIELDING CONFIRMED!!" Ye Yijie shouted with triumphant joy.

Collapsing backward onto the deck, Jiang Jiang looked down at his soot-stained skillet—which, far from being destroyed, had been electrolytic-polished by the million-volt lightning strike into a mirror of shimmering platinum luster—wiping grime from his face with a dazed, blissful grin:

"Heheheh... When we get back to school, I won't even need oil to fry sunny-side eggs anymore..."

"CHANNEL THE REPULSION ENERGY——MAXIMUM CRUISE ASCENT!!" Grandpa Cheng roared, ramming the master throttle into the stops!

*VROOOOOM——SHING————!!*

Saturated with lightning energy, the superconducting turbofans blasted forward with unprecedented thrust, driving The Aoki Gear like a golden dragon breaking from the ocean, shattering the ceiling of the eight-thousand-meter storm cloud!

*WHOOOOSH————!*

Sunlight flooded through the bridge viewports in an instant.

The howling rain, freezing hail, and roaring thunder fell away into breathless, celestial silence.

Unfolding before their eyes was the pristine, deep, velvet-black sapphire sky of the stratosphere ten thousand meters above the Earth.

And rising dead ahead above a vast, blinding-white ocean of rolling clouds, a colossal five-kilometer airborne continent forged from white jade, brass clockwork, and floating levitation monoliths—**【The Stratospheric Floating City · Wings of Aether】**—gleamed majestically in the morning sunrise!

Countdown remaining until surface collision: **Sixteen hours and thirty minutes**!

The landing operation upon the City of the Heavens had officially begun!
