# 第十七章：海市蜃樓與全息折射航線 (Chapter 17: The Mirage and the Holographic Refraction Route)

## 一、破曉的二十七小時倒數 (I. Dawn's Twenty-Seven-Hour Countdown)

At six o'clock in the morning, when The Aoki Gear and the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor surfaced through the lighthouse hydraulic flume onto the open swells of the Gear Sea, an eerie violet-gold dawn was breaking along the eastern horizon.

There was not a breath of wind. The ocean lay as mirror-flat and motionless as an infinite expanse of liquid mercury.

Old Morris's voice crackled through the long-wave underwater radio in the wheelhouse with heavy static:

> *"Cheng Hao, do you read me?"*  
> *"The seismic wave sensors beneath the lighthouse recorded another 4.2-magnitude tremor... Shen Tian-Cheng's first seismic spike has begun injecting ultra-high-pressure superheated liquid steam into the mantle!"*  
> *"The pressure needles of the geothermal clockwork array have slipped into the yellow hazard zone... You have exactly——**twenty-seven hours** remaining!"*

"Copy that, Grandpa Morris!" Cheng Hao gripped the microphone, his eyes hard as tempered steel. "We have reached the northern quadrant of Windward Island. We will secure the Lyra Optical Astrolabe before nightfall!"

Signing off, Cheng Hao stepped out onto the forecastle.

Aboard the deck, every crew member was working feverishly against the ticking clock.

Jiang Jiang was slicing the remaining pineapple buns into small cubes, wrapping each tightly in tinfoil to distribute among the team, muttering: "Three pieces per person—this is our final calorie reserve! If anyone tries to solve puzzles on an empty stomach, they answer to me!"

Across the water, Buck and Ding-Ding were loading their final two crates of alloy clockwork munitions aboard the Iron Anchor.

"Hey, Cheng Hao!" Lan called from the bow of the Seagull, her shell anemometer spinning gently in her palm, brow furrowed: "Look ahead! We've entered the most treacherous sector north of the island—**The Sea of Phantoms**!"

Cheng Hao and Yijie followed Lan's outstretched finger toward the horizon.

Less than two miles dead ahead, morning fog and sunrise rays intertwined to manifest **three identical towering islands** floating upon the water!

Atop each island rose crystal cliffs three hundred feet high, refracting brilliant prismatic rainbows under the morning sun. Even the crashing white surf at their bases and the spinning clockwork windmills turned in eerie, synchronous harmony!

"Three Lyra Reef Islands?!" Jiang Jiang rubbed his eyes, nearly holding his binoculars backward. "Sweet heavens! Does this island know how to cast shadow clones?!"

"This is no supernatural sorcery; it is an atmospheric optical phenomenon known as a **'Superior Mirage'**, multiplied by an ancient holographic projection repeater network!" Ye Yijie donned her polarized navigator's shades, her expression solemn.

## 二、布魯斯特角與大氣逆溫層 (II. Brewster's Angle and the Atmospheric Inversion Layer)

"A superior mirage?" Buck leaned over suspiciously. "I've sailed these seas for three years and only seen inferior mirages—like puddles on hot pavement. What on earth is a superior mirage?!"

"Inferior mirages occur over scorching deserts or black asphalt, where hot air near the ground refracts light upward;"

Yijie opened her black journal, sketching two curved optical paths with her pen:

"Here in the Thousand-Island Gear Sea, freezing upwelling currents from the Abyssal Rift chill the sea-surface air to near freezing, making it dense and giving it a high refractive index ($n_1$); meanwhile, the air layer fifty yards above is warmed by geothermal plumes and sunrise rays, becoming thin with a lower refractive index ($n_2$)!"

"In meteorology, this is called an **'Extreme Temperature Inversion Layer'**!"

"When light travels diagonally upward from the dense cold air into the thin warm air, it continuously bends downward toward the cold, dense sea surface, 'lifting' objects located below the horizon into midair, creating a floating superior mirage!"

"Worse still—" Cheng Hao unclasped the dodecahedral astrolabe from his chest, pointing to the precision brass engravings along its base: "Grandpa left an encrypted inscription on the rim:"

```
[LYRA MIRAGE DECRYPTION PROTOCOL]:
"Three islands appear as one; two are false and one is true."
"One falsehood is a mirror mirage of submerged reefs;
 one is a synthetic laser holographic trap deployed by Kuroshio;"
"Entering a false island means destruction by underwater spikes
 or geothermal geysers."

Optical Decryption Directive:
"Natural reflections exhibit Brewster's Polarizing Properties,
 while synthetic lasers possess no such order."
"Rotate the goggle polarizing dial to Brewster's Angle (θ_B):"
tan(θ_B) = n_2 / n_1

Atmospheric Media Parameters:
Sea-surface cold air refractive index: n_1 = 1.00035
Inversion warm air refractive index: n_2 = 1.00020
Laser wavelength: λ = 532 nm

Operational Law:
Set the polarizing filter to θ_B to eliminate reflective glare;
the island whose refraction shows no step-distortion with altitude
and whose starlight polarization axis remains vertical is TRUE!
```

"**BREWSTER'S ANGLE! THE LAW OF TOTAL POLARIZATION!**"

Brilliance ignited in Ye Yijie's eyes: "When natural unpolarized light strikes the boundary between two media at a specific angle, the reflected beam becomes completely linearly polarized perpendicular to the plane of incidence! By rotating a polarizing filter to that precise angle, we can completely extinguish synthetic holographic lasers and sea-surface glare!"

"Calculate Brewster's Angle!"

Yijie's pen glided across the paper:

$$\tan\theta_B = \frac{n_2}{n_1} = \frac{1.00020}{1.00035} \approx 0.99985$$

$$\theta_B = \arctan(0.99985) \approx 44.995^{\circ} \approx 45.0^{\circ} !$$

"**FORTY-FIVE DEGREES POLARIZATION ANGLE!**"

Yijie yelled to Cheng Hao: "Cheng Hao! Dial the right polarizing thumbwheel on your Ghost Goggles to **forty-five degrees**! Scrutinize the baseline of all three islands!"

## 三、偏振鏡下的真偽撕裂 (III. Truth and Fiction Under the Polarizing Filter)

"Understood! Polarizer thumbwheel—locked at forty-five degrees!"

Cheng Hao took a deep breath, delicately rolling the knurled brass dial on the right rim of his goggles.

*Click, click...*

With a muted detent click of internal micro-lenses, the world transformed through the eyepieces—

Blinding morning glare vanished in a microsecond, the milky sea turning into a deep, crystal-clear emerald-black.

And under the forty-five-degree polarizing filter, the three towering crystal islands revealed their true natures:

To the left, **"Island A"**: through the polarizer, horizontal green laser scan lines flickered violently along the rock face! It was an artificial holographic interference projection deployed by Kuroshio upon marine buoys—a deadly lure designed to draw vessels onto floating sea mines!

In the center, **"Island B"**: the island's reflection was bizarrely inverted upside down, the cliffs rippling like water! It was an atmospheric superior mirage of an uninhabited atoll miles away, beneath which lay a lethal, boiling thousand-meter geothermal geyser!

And at thirty degrees to the starboard, **"Island C"**:

Under the forty-five-degree polarizing filter, its outline not only held firm, but became razor-sharp!

The basalt ramparts gleamed with natural deep brown hues, centered around an ancient bronze sundial observatory! Skyward, the faint rays of Vega in Lyra aligned directly with the bronze gnomon of the observatory!

"**STARBOARD! ISLAND C IS THE ONLY TRUE ISLAND!**"

Cheng Hao threw open the wheelhouse sash, shouting at thirty degrees to starboard: "Buck! Lan! Starboard thirty degrees! FULL THROTTLE! BREAK THROUGH THE MIRAGE!"

"Starboard thirty! CHARGE!!"

Both vessels sounded battle whistles, carving parallel white wakes across the calm sea, roaring straight toward Island C!

## 四、蜃樓中的致命伏擊 (IV. Deadly Ambush Within the Mirage)

Yet just as The Aoki Gear trimmed her heading, a deafening turbine howl erupted from within the holographic shroud of "Island A"!

*WHOOSH——WHOOSH——WHOOSH!*

Eight high-speed **"Manta-Ray Stealth Skimmers"** deployed by Kuroshio burst through the holographic veil like a flock of predatory black falcons, diving straight down from the sky!

Mounted beneath their hulls, six-tube micro surface-skimming rocket pods pulsed with lethal red laser targeters!

"Ambush!" Lan cried, the Seagull executing three high-speed evasive drifts across the swells, hurling dual smoke cannisters to raise a towering wall of white spray.

"Ding-Ding! BLOW THOSE IRON BATS OUT OF THE SKY!" Buck spun the anchor cannon handwheels with furious speed.

"Triple-burst clockwork salvo, primed!" Ding-Ding swung her heavy hammer, landing three titanic blows with her brute strength!

*THUD! THUD! THUD!*

Three timed fragmentation canisters exploded in midair, hurling a storm of white-phosphorus shrapnel that shredded the wings of the two lead manta skimmers, sending them spiraling into the sea in plumes of black smoke!

Yet the remaining six skimmers maintained formation, unleashing a barrage of armor-piercing rockets straight at The Aoki Gear's rudder!

"Cheng Hao! WATCH THE HELM!" Jiang Jiang screamed, hurling himself toward the stern, his cast-iron skillet ringing with *CLANG-CLANG-CLANG* deflections as it batted away rocket fragments!

"PICO! HYDROFOIL RIOT SHIELD MODE!!"

*HUMMMMM——SHING!*

Poised at the prow, PICO's alloy plates fanned outward in a tenth of a second like a peacock's tail, reassembling into a six-foot-wide, two-inch-thick **Heavy Alloy Ballistic Riot Shield** locking directly across the wheelhouse window!

*BANG-BANG-BANG-BANG!*

Rocket shrapnel blossomed across the shield in a blinding storm of white-hot sparks, incandescent slag sliding harmlessly along flow channels into the ocean. PICO's sapphire optics burned unwavering through the inferno, refusing to yield a single inch!

Shielded by PICO's golden defensive window, The Aoki Gear knifed cleanly through the natural limestone archway into the sea cave of Island C!

The thick, solid basalt walls of the sea cave acted like a natural bunker, sealing the pursuing gunships' bombardment outside!

## 五、天琴座光學星盤與深海鑽艇 (V. The Lyra Optical Astrolabe and the Deep-Sea Drill Sub)

Passing through the shadowy sea cave, the cavern expanded into a breathtaking sanctuary.

Hidden at the heart of the island lay a natural sea cenote. Luminous stalactites hung from the vaulted roof, morning light streaming down through ceiling fissures like celestial spotlights.

In the center of the tranquil pool rose an extraordinary **Ancient Lyra Observatory**, fashioned entirely from translucent white jade and solid brass gears.

And floating in the center of the dais was a twelve-inch astrolabe core ground from pure natural amethyst—**【The Lyra Optical Astrolabe】**!

Suspended upon four beams of acoustic levitation microwaves, twelve delicate clockwork hands turned in gentle synchrony with Vega in the sky above, radiating an enchanting, serene pale-violet starlight!

"We found it... The third Celestial Key!"

Ye Yijie held her racing heart, tears of wonder glinting in her eyes: "With this and the dodecahedral astrolabe, we can fully ignite the Fresnel lens of the Lost Lighthouse!"

Cheng Hao stepped onto the stone stairs of the observatory, reaching out with both hands to reverently receive the floating amethyst disc.

Yet in the microsecond before Cheng Hao's fingers touched the astrolabe—

*RUMBLE-CRASH————!!*

The tranquil pool at the base of the cenote erupted with an earth-shattering tremor that shook the entire island!

The stone floor shattered into rubble as a thirty-foot-wide black mechanical behemoth armed with six rotating diamond auger heads—**Kuroshio Heavy Industries' "Deep-Sea Heavy Shield-Tunneling Sub Mk-IV"**—burst from the bedrock in a geyser of mud and froth, violently smashing the lower pedestal of the observatory!

The upper hatch popped open. Holding a metal scepter crackling with high-voltage arcs, Shen Tian-Cheng appeared atop the conning tower like a phantom that refused to die!

Behind him, a holographic display flashed a terrifying blood-red countdown:

> `[TIME TO SEVEN SEISMIC SPIKES OVERLOAD DETONATION: 23 HOURS 59 MINUTES!]`

Shen Tian-Cheng straightened his necktie with aristocratic poise, a cold, triumphant sneer curling his lip:

"Heheheh... Truly a magnificent optical decryption, children."

"I must thank you for guiding me through the mirage and neutralizing every defensive obstacle."

Shen Tian-Cheng pointed his scepter forward, eight high-pressure harpoon launchers along the sub's hull locking onto Cheng Hao upon the altar:

"Now, that Lyra Astrolabe—and every single one of you meddlesome pests... **shall be buried forever in this watery grave!**"
