# 第二十四章：奔向平流層的熱氣球飛艦 (Chapter 24: The Stratospheric Airship Ascent)

## 一、船塢的不眠之夜與雙層氣囊 (I. Sleepless Night at the Docks and the Dual Envelopes)

At one in the morning, the subterranean heavy drydocks of Lu-Yang Port blazed with incandescent light as bright as high noon.

Through the cavernous hangar echoed the rhythmic thunder of forging hammers, the shrill scream of superheated steam jets, and the synchronized cadence of hundreds of master clockwork machinists shouting in unison.

Under the direct command of Principal Chiu and Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan, the town's most celebrated engineers and veteran shipwrights had assembled in full force.

In the center of the drydock, The Aoki Gear—suspended thirty meters above the stone slipway—hung securely in the grip of colossal gantry cranes.

Using tear-resistant clockwork fiber fabrics unique to the Thousand-Island Sea and high-polymer thermal silicone, craftsmen were mounting two forty-meter spindle-shaped **"Dual-Layer High-Altitude Lift Envelopes"** along both flanks of the hull!

The surface of each envelope was cross-hatched with high-tensile stress skeletons woven from shape-memory alloys, engineered to retain diamond-hard tensile resilience even in the bitter chill of minus fifty degrees Celsius.

And nestled just above the waterline along the ship's beam, PICO transformed into two five-foot-diameter **"Superconducting Ducted Jet Turbofans"**. Four miniature Tesla coils pulsed with cold cerulean arcs at the nacelle intakes, primed for the ignition of the high-energy crystals.

Smudged with engine soot, Cheng Hao climbed out from the boiler room, tightly clutching a custom brass torque wrench as he wiped sweat from his brow:

"Grandpa! The heat exchanger linkages between the ancient abyssal crystals and the primary boiler are fully welded! The pressure rating holds firm at one hundred and twenty atmospheres!"

Grandpa Cheng chuckled, affectionately tapping his grandson's brass goggles, handing him a clean towel: "Splendid work! Those heat exchanger blueprints were an unfinished dream between your father and me thirty years ago. I never imagined you would bring them to life today!"

## 二、理想氣體方程與臨界浮力平衡 (II. The Ideal Gas Law and Critical Buoyant Equilibrium)

"Attention, everyone! The final buoyancy and thermodynamic equilibrium parameters are locked!"

Standing before the navigation chart table, Ye Yijie adjusted her spectacles, switching the holographic projector to display the mass and lift distribution vectors of the entire skyship.

Across the glowing projection, The Aoki Gear's 3D wireframe was mapped with a dense network of red force arrows:

```
[STRATOSPHERIC SKYSHIP CRITICAL BUOYANCY & GAS STATE EQUATIONS]:
Ideal Gas Law:
P × V = n × R × T ➡️ Gas Density Equation: ρ = (P × M) / (R × T)

Skyship Aerostatic Equilibrium Condition:
Total Vertical Lift: F_buoyant = (ρ_air - ρ_gas) × V_envelope × g
Ascent Mandatory Criterion: F_buoyant ≥ m_total × g

[TOTAL SKYSHIP MASS & ATMOSPHERIC PARAMETERS]:
The Aoki Gear Gross All-Up Weight (Armor, Crew, Boiler, Fuel):
m_total = 45,000 kg (45 metric tons)
Sea-Level Ambient Air Density: ρ_air = 1.225 kg/m³

[DUAL ENVELOPE INFLATION PARAMETERS]:
Lifting Medium: High-Purity Helium and Superheated Steam Mixture
Envelope Internal Effective Density: ρ_gas = 0.325 kg/m³
Effective Density Differential: Δρ = ρ_air - ρ_gas = 1.225 - 0.325 = 0.900 kg/m³

[CRITICAL MINIMUM ENVELOPE VOLUME]:
V_min = m_total / Δρ = 45,000 / 0.900 = 50,000 cubic meters!
Dual Envelope Configuration: 25,000 m³ on port, 25,000 m³ on starboard!

[THERMODYNAMIC SUPERHEAT ACCELERATION THRUST]:
When burners heat envelope gas to T = 390 K (116.85°C):
Internal gas density drops further to: ρ_hot = 0.280 kg/m³!
Generated Net Acceleration Lift:
ΔF = (1.225 - 0.280) × 50,000 × 9.8 - 45,000 × 9.8
ΔF = (47,250 - 45,000) × 9.8 = 22,050 N (Newtons)!
Initial Vertical Climb Acceleration:
a = 22,050 / 45,000 ≈ 0.49 m/s²!
```

"**FIFTY THOUSAND CUBIC METERS DISPLACEMENT! HEATING TEMPERATURE: 116.85 DEGREES CELSIUS!**"

Ye Yijie's clear, authoritative voice resonated across the drydock: "Only when the dual envelopes are inflated to fifty thousand cubic meters and heated above one hundred and sixteen degrees will we generate twenty-two thousand Newtons of net vertical thrust, launching our forty-five-ton steel skyship into the clouds!"

"Understood! Open the high-pressure helium manifolds! Ignite the geothermal crystal burners!"

Grandpa Cheng hauled down the master red inflation lever on the control console!

## 三、掙脫重力！衝出海面 (III. Breaking Gravity! Surging from the Sea)

*ROOOOOOAAAR————!!*

Inside The Aoki Gear's main boiler, the ancient crystal brought back from the Thousand-Island Sea erupted in a blinding flash of golden luminescence!

Scalding, supercritical steam surged like a roaring golden dragon through insulated conduits, flooding furiously into the two colossal spindle envelopes!

Within five minutes, the deflated golden envelopes swelled with breathtaking speed, expanding into two seventy-foot-tall peaks of shimmering gold!

*CREEEAK... GROAN... SNAP!!*

Four arm-thick cast-iron mooring chains anchored to the cradle groaned under the forty-five-ton upward pull, shearing through their pins in a simultaneous, concussive crack!

In that glorious heartbeat, the keel of The Aoki Gear completely broke contact with the water!

"We're airborne! We're really flying!!" Jiang Jiang clutched his cooler, feet dangling off the deck planking, screaming with ecstatic joy.

"Retract the hangar skylights! FULL FLIGHT COMMENCE!!" Principal Chiu roared from the ground station.

The three-hundred-foot steel hangar roof parted smoothly to both sides, the pristine rays of dawn illuminating The Aoki Gear's gleaming golden envelopes like a celestial spotlight!

"PICO! SUPERCONDUCTING DUCTED TURBOFANS——MAXIMUM POWER IGNITION!!"

*HOWWWWWL————!!*

Mounted along the flanks, the superconducting turbofans blasted twin thirty-foot plumes of cerulean plasma exhaust, propelling the titanic Aoki Gear skyward like a great steel eagle breaking free from the shackles of Earth!

Below upon the quayside, thousands of citizens, teachers, and students waved their hats, their thunderous cheers echoing into the heavens!

Time remaining until the celestial city impacts the surface: **Eighteen hours and forty minutes**!

## 四、對流層邊界的音爆風暴 (IV. Sonic-Boom Gale at the Troposphere Boundary)

The Aoki Gear climbed at a breathtaking vertical rate of fifteen meters per second.

One thousand meters! Two thousand meters! Three thousand five hundred meters!

Lu-Yang Town, the verdant peaks of Mount Lu-Yang, and the boundless Thousand-Island Gear Sea shrank rapidly into a breathtaking living atlas below.

Yet just as the skyship was about to pierce the five-thousand-meter ceiling and traverse the mid-troposphere—

The sky turned ominously black without warning.

Looming dead ahead was a monstrous, horizon-swallowing **"Supercell Thunderstorm Cloud Wall"**, churning like an apocalyptic mountain range of black granite, armed with tens of thousands of incandescent lightning bolts that completely barricaded the corridor to the stratosphere!

Savage cross-shear winds howling at over forty meters per second slammed into the hull, violently warping the dual envelopes, tossing the skyship like a leaf at dangerous thirty-degree list angles!

"Warning! Severe horizontal wind shear detected! Port envelope stress nearing structural yield threshold!!" Ye Yijie gripped the bridge console, her face blanching.

Grapefruit-sized hailstones hammered against the armored crystal viewport like artillery scatter-shot, while deep within the pitch-black thundercloud maze, a chilling, otherworldly chorus of mechanical shrieks began piercing the rolling thunder, howling closer and closer...

The trial of life and death in the sky had officially begun!
