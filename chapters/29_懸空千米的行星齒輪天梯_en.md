# 第二十九章：懸空千米的行星齒輪天梯 (Chapter 29: The Suspended Planetary Gear Stairway)

## 一、千米深淵上的天體神殿 (I. The Celestial Temple Over the Thousand-Meter Abyss)

The rear hatch of the safety control chamber slid noiselessly aside, unveiling a vista that made the hearts of the four youths freeze in mid-beat.

Hidden deep within the base of the Celestial Citadel was a circular hollow rotunda spanning over eight hundred meters in diameter.

There was no floor beneath their boots—only a fathomless vertical chasm plunging a thousand meters straight into rolling white cloudbanks. Polar gales howling from the stratospheric depths surged upward, whipping their flight suits into violent ripples.

And suspended within the void of this thousand-meter abyss floated twelve colossal bronze clockwork dials ranging from thirty to over a hundred meters across!

The perimeter of each dial was geared with microscopic helical teeth and escapement mechanisms, their surfaces engraved with the astronomical orbital paths of the solar system. Driven by primordial anti-gravity mainsprings, the twelve gargantuan gear dials revolved and orbited gracefully through the void at differing angular velocities, mimicking the celestial dance of the planets!

This was the sole passage ascending to the zenith of the Celestial Clock Tower—**"The Suspended Planetary Gear Stairway"**!

Yet at this very moment, the celestial stairway had fallen into catastrophic disarray.

Distorted by the gravitational lensing of the dark crystals, the precision clockwork gear trains were plagued by massive phase misalignments. The traversable corridors connecting adjacent dials fractured every few seconds, multi-hundred-thousand-ton bronze wheels grinding together in midair, unleashing sheets of blinding golden sparks over fifty feet long!

"This stairway maps the orbital dynamics of our solar system," Cecilia pointed her celestial astrolabe into the center of the chasm, her expression solemn: "We must scale these rotating catwalks to reach the pinnacle hall of the clock tower!"

"B-But their orbital velocities are completely different!" Jiang Jiang peered into the dizzying drop, his knees knocking: "One missed step and we take a ten-thousand-meter swan dive straight back onto the school playground!"

"Two hundred meters in five seconds?! That's forty meters per second! No human on Earth can sprint at ninety miles per hour!!" Jiang Jiang shrieked in horror.

Cheng Hao and Cecilia exchanged a knowing glance, the wild fires of heroic adventure igniting in both their eyes!

## 三、生死十八秒極速攀登 (III. The Eighteen-Second Sprint for Life)

"Understood! Celestial Glider Wings——FULL SPAN DEPLOYMENT!!"

Cecilia commanded, the memory-alloy wings upon her back snapping wide into the freezing gale, expanding into a magnificent thirteen-foot golden hang-glider!

PICO transformed into a superconducting pulse-jet booster trailing white shock diamonds, locking rigidly behind the four-person array!

"Five seconds! Five... four... three..." Ye Yijie monitored the phase angle on her oscilloscope: "Two... one... **THEY ARE ALIGNED!! CHARGE!!**"

*HUMMMMMM——BOOOOM!!*

The instant the countdown reached zero, the gilded catwalks of Mercury, Earth, and Jupiter snapped into a straight, unbroken two-hundred-meter bridge suspended over the abyss!

"FULL REHEAT——LAUNCH!!"

The superconducting rocket booster on PICO's chest roared with deafening fury, hurling the four-person array skimming inches above the golden bridge at a blistering forty meters per second!

The howling wind tore at their ears, beneath their feet a sheer ten-thousand-meter plunge into the white abyss!

Second one! Fifty meters! Cleared the Mercury Dial!  
Second two! One hundred meters! Screamed across the Earth Dial!  
Second three! One hundred and sixty meters! Closing on the edge of Jupiter!

"TIME'S UP! THE GEARS ARE SHEARING!!" Jiang Jiang shrieked as the multi-thousand-ton bronze wheels began rotating out of phase on both sides.

Second four!

The Mercury Dial behind them sheared leftward with thunderous torque, two hundred-ton bronze gear teeth slamming together eighteen inches behind their boots in a blinding eruption of violent golden sparks!

"LAST FIFTY METERS! CECILIA——EJECT!!" Cheng Hao roared!

Flaring her wings with athletic fury, Cecilia caught the final thermal updraft, launching the entire team across the gap in a breathtaking parabolic arc!

## 四、抵達星穹鐘樓！ (IV. Reaching the Celestial Clock Tower!)

*THUD! SKID-CLATTER——!*

In the microsecond the fifth second ticked into the sixth, the team's forward momentum carried them through the archway, tumbling across the polished marble deck of the pinnacle rotunda, skidding dozens of feet before coming to a safe halt!

*CLANG-CRASH——RUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

Across the abyss behind them, the three planetary dials collided in off-phase engagement, unleashing a concussive shockwave that shook the airborne continent to its foundations as the golden bridge shattered into the void!

"We... We made it across..." Jiang Jiang lay flat against the cool marble flagstones, kissing the tiles with tears streaming down his face: "I lived again... I actually survived..."

Cheng Hao sprang to his feet, helping Ye Yijie and Cecilia up.

When everyone raised their eyes, the spectacle before them seized their souls in profound awe—

The vast marble hall of the Celestial Clock Tower soared three hundred feet into the heavens, supported by twelve colossal white-jade pillars carved with ancient astronomical charts.

Suspended upon the central dais in a circular array stood eleven thirty-foot **"Celestial Carillon Tuning Forks"** cast from ancient gold and bronze. Each fork turned in slow, hypnotic gyration, humming with the gentle, cosmic heartbeat of the universe.

Yet upon the sacred pedestal representing the "Twelfth Hour"—

A colossal tuning fork forged from pure solar gold lay choked and smothered within a room-sized, pulsating mass of **"Anti-Matter Black Resonance Crystals"** that throbbed like a corrupted heart!

Thick plumes of dark gravitational miasma billowed from the crystal, tearing the surrounding spacetime into violent, shimmering ripples!

Time remaining until surface collision: **Eight hours and fifteen minutes**!

The final battle to unlock the Celestial Clock Tower stood directly before them!
