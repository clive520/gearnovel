# 第三章：圖書館的倒懸齒輪 (Chapter 3: The Inverted Gears in the Library)

## 一、通風管道的大逃亡 (I. The Great Air-Duct Escape)

"SCREEECH—CRACKLE—!!"

With a deafening burst of electrical arcs, the final glowing segment of the sheared iron bolt snapped in two, clattering heavily onto the lab's concrete floor.

The reinforced oak door was forcefully shoved inward by an armored fist sheathed in matte-black carbon fiber!

Framed in the doorway was no clumsy, cylindrical floor-sweeping android. Standing over six feet tall was an imposing, heavy-duty Campus Security Enforcer. Its limbs were sleek and elongated, its armored breastplate emblazoned with the scarlet insignia of the "Campus Security Committee." At the tip of its right forearm, a plasma-cutting emitter flickered with pale blue light, humming with hazardous high-frequency vibrations.

"Target identified: S-Rank Non-Compliant Students... and unregistered antique automaton."

The three optical lenses in the enforcer's helmet rotated independently to lock coordinates. Its internal loudspeaker crackled like iron plates grating against each other: "Relinquish the Ghost Goggles and PICO-001 immediately. Submitting to memory reset will exempt subjects from physical reprimand."

"Exempt this, you overgrown toaster!"

Cheng Hao bellowed, snatching an expired glass bottle of copper sulfate solution from the nearest workbench and hurling it with all his might straight at the enforcer's faceplate!

*SMASH!*

The glass shattered against the alloy mask, splashing blue liquid across its sensor lenses. Enraged, the enforcer swept its massive hydraulic arm sideways, cleaving a solid oak lab table in half as if it were soft tofu! Splinters exploded through the air!

In that razor-thin fraction of a second, the origami Shiba Inu perched on Cheng Hao's shoulder sprang into action!

*Clack-clack! SHING——*

The shape-memory alloy plates across PICO's body fanned outward like a peacock's plumage. Instead of retreating, the pup blurred into a silver streak, lunging straight at the enforcer's helmet!

*BEEP! BEEP! BEEP!*

PICO's tiny steel paws wedged themselves directly into the optical sensor seams. Its tail hammered frantically against the enforcer's external radio antenna, triggering a wild spray of spitting blue short-circuit sparks!

"Sensory array compromised... initiating thermal purge override..." The enforcer flailed its massive arms in fury, trying to swat the metallic pup away.

"Cheng Hao! Look up!" Yijie yanked hard on his sleeve, pointing toward the upper corner of the high ceiling.

There, bolted behind four weathered wing nuts, was a wire-mesh ventilation grate. Decades of neglect had loosened two of the rusted nuts, exposing an opening just wide enough for a slender middle-schooler to squeeze through.

"PICO, disengage!" Cheng Hao shouted. Pulling his screwdriver pen from his pocket, he bounded onto an overturned filing cabinet and swiftly loosened the remaining two rusted wing nuts.

The wire mesh clattered to the floor!

At its master's call, PICO snapped its limbs inward with a crisp *click*, collapsing back into a sleek silver Rubik's cube. Sailing through the air in a graceful arc, it landed squarely into the front pocket of Cheng Hao's hoodie.

"Yijie, go!"

Cheng Hao cupped his hands under Yijie's sneakers and hoisted her upward with all his strength. With athletic agility, Yijie caught the lip of the duct, swung her legs up, and slipped inside.

Cheng Hao glanced back at the enforcer recalibrating its plasma blade. He leaped upward, gripped the duct rim, and wriggled into the dark shaft like an eel.

*BOOOM——!*

A blistering lance of azure laser heat grazed the soles of Cheng Hao's shoes, vaporizing the wooden cabinet below into a smoking heap of charred embers!

## 二、百年圖書館的秘密 (II. Secrets of the Century-Old Library)

The cramped ventilation duct was cloaked in darkness and coated in decades of dry dust.

The conduit sloped downward along the interior of the Old Schoolhouse's thick masonry walls. PICO poked its metallic head out of Cheng Hao's hoodie pocket, twin sapphire lenses shining like miniature spotlights to project a bright cone of illumination ahead.

"Judging by this incline... we've left the old classrooms behind and are passing beneath the central courtyard gardens," Yijie whispered, crawling forward on hands and knees while mentally calculating trajectory and distance. "I hear air currents ahead... and a strange, wonderful fragrance..."

"That's aged camphor wood and antique paper," Cheng Hao wiped grime from his brow. "We're here!"

At the end of the duct sat an ornate, cast-bronze louvered vent. Cheng Hao pressed against it with his elbow; the corroded brass latch gave way with a muted snap.

The two kids and one mechanical pup slid silently down from the six-foot drop, landing softly upon a thick, crimson floral-woven carpet.

When they stood up and looked around, the breathtaking spectacle before them stole the air from their lungs.

They had arrived inside Lu-Yang Elementary's oldest, most revered sanctuary—the **Century-Old Library**.

Far removed from the sterile plastic panels and cold digital LCD monitors of the modern school wings, this majestic library rose three full stories high. Massive, floor-to-ceiling walnut bookcases encircled the rotunda, holding tens of thousands of leather-bound volumes that radiated a serene, scholarly hush.

Yet what commanded absolute awe was the vaulted ceiling nearly fifty feet overhead.

Suspended across the entire expanse of the grand dome was a breathtaking, colossal array of solid-brass **Inverted Clockwork Gears**!

Hundreds of mechanical spur and bevel gears—ranging in diameter from a few inches to ten feet wide—hung suspended like a golden forest of clockwork stalactites. Heavy steel cables connected this mechanical labyrinth to the pendulum shaft of the campus clock tower above. Usually, the entire ceiling array remained motionless, turning half a revolution only when the massive noon bell chimed.

"Incredible..." Cheng Hao murmured, staring upward at the mechanical wonder. As an inventor, pure passion ignited in his eyes.

"Don't get lost in admiration, Cheng Hao," Yijie pushed her glasses up, scanning the shadowy book aisles. "PICO's Morse code said *'UNDER THE MUSEUM.'* The ground floor rotunda behind this library serves as the campus Historical Archives."

PICO leaped down to the carpet, pattering rapidly toward a massive black marble pillar in the very center of the hall.

At the foot of the pillar stood a three-foot-tall bronze armillary sphere depicting the twelve signs of the zodiac. Chiseled into its base was a worn inscription:

> **"Knowledge is the lever that unseals the subterranean gate; truth resides within the division of the third domain."**

## 三、索書號與圖書密碼 (III. Classification Numbers and the Book Cipher)

"Look down here!"

Cheng Hao pointed at the base of the armillary sphere.

Where a nameplate should have sat, someone had carefully scratched three cryptic library call numbers into the bronze with a hardened steel stylus:

```
[Target Book 1]: 823.91 / 04-A (Chapter 3, Fifth Word)
[Target Book 2]: 510.1 / 18-B (Page 1, Tenth Word)
[Target Book 3]: 621.8 / 09-C (Table of Contents, Second-to-Last Word)
```

Directly below these three lines of call numbers was a hexagonal recess carved into the stone pillar, bordered by a tiny brass spur gear.

"A classic Book Cipher!" A thrill of intellectual excitement sparked in Yijie's eyes. "Someone concealed the operational key inside specific volumes within this library!"

"There are tens of thousands of books here!" Cheng Hao ruffled his hair. "How do we locate three specific books in just a few minutes?!"

"We don't need to search tens of thousands—that is the beauty of classification numbers!" Yijie strode purposefully toward a massive wooden catalog directory mounted near the library rotunda entrance:

"Listen carefully, Cheng Hao! Under the Dewey Decimal and library classification systems:"  
"**The 800s** are *Literature*! 823 designates English Fiction—second floor, eastern walnut shelving!"  
"**The 500s** are *Natural Sciences and Mathematics*! 510 designates Pure Mathematics—ground floor, fourth aisle on the left!"  
"**The 600s** are *Applied Sciences and Technology*! 621 designates Mechanical Engineering and Instrumentation—ground floor, right aisle by the windows!"

"Divide and conquer!" Cheng Hao said.

"No need—PICO is ten times faster than either of us!"

Yijie knelt down and tapped PICO's metallic head gently: "PICO, head to the right aisle on the ground floor and fetch `621.8 / 09-C`. The spine should feature gear and clockwork emblems!"

PICO gave a cheerful *beep-boop*, its four little legs blurring into a silver cyclone as it vanished into the labyrinth of ground-floor shelving.

Meanwhile, Cheng Hao dashed toward the second-floor literature gallery, while Yijie sprinted to the mathematics section on the ground floor.

Seconds ticked by with agonizing weight.

In the vast, silent hall, the only sound was the rustle of fingertips skimming across aged book spines.

"Found the math volume!"

Yijie's hushed, triumphant voice echoed from below. In her hands was a yellowed, cloth-bound tome titled *Advanced Calculus and Principles of Prime Distributions*, bearing call number `510.1 / 18-B`!

Yijie turned to page one, running her finger along the dense typography: "One, two, three... nine, ten! The tenth word is—**CLOCKWISE**!"

At the exact same moment on the second-floor gallery, Cheng Hao pulled an enormous vintage English volume from beside the Sherlock Holmes collection, call number `823.91 / 04-A`.

Cheng Hao flipped to chapter three, counting across the opening line.

It was the very first sentence spoken by Holmes upon starting his investigation. The fifth word was unmistakably—**THREE**!

"My word is 'THREE'!" Cheng Hao called over the second-floor banister.

Right on cue, PICO came trotting proudly back across the carpet, carrying a heavy, leather-bound volume in its jaws: *Compendium of Nineteenth-Century Precision Horological Gearing* (`621.8 / 09-C`). It deposited the book neatly beside the armillary sphere.

Yijie opened the table of contents, zeroing in on the second-to-last word:

"**REVERSE**!"

Putting the three words together revealed the complete operational instructions:

> **`CLOCKWISE` ➡️ `THREE (rotations)` ➡️ `REVERSE`!**

## 四、穹頂的轟鳴 (IV. Thunder in the Vaulted Dome)

"It isn't a text password—it's a mechanical rotation sequence!" Cheng Hao vaulted down the spiral stairs two steps at a time.

He dropped beside the base of the armillary sphere, gripping the miniature brass gear encircling the hexagonal socket.

"Ready?" Cheng Hao looked at Yijie and PICO.

"Turn it!" Yijie clenched her fists.

Taking a deep breath, Cheng Hao began rotating the brass dial firmly in a **clockwise** direction.

*Click! Click! Click!*

Crisp spring-loaded detents snapped with mechanical precision in the quiet rotunda.

One full turn... two full turns... three full turns!

The instant the third rotation clicked into alignment, Cheng Hao threw all his weight into wrenching the gear in **reverse**!

*BOOM——RUUUUUUMBLE——!!*

In an instant, the entire century-old library shuddered violently!

Decades of dust rained down from the rafters like falling snow as the colossal array of **Inverted Gears** across the vaulted dome miraculously roared to life!

A ten-foot master driving wheel engaged first, its teeth meshing into dozens of secondary brass gears that spun in stunning mechanical harmony! The deep, rhythmic percussion of interlocking bronze teeth shook the air like the heartbeat of an underground titan!

"Look at the marble floor!" Yijie cried out.

In the center of the rotunda, the polished hexagonal marble flagstones began sliding backward beneath the floorboards in rhythmic succession, accompanied by the hiss of pneumatic pressure release!

A wide, spiral staircase carved from dark basalt unfolded steadily downward into the earth, descending into an abyss so deep that ambient light could not penetrate!

An icy, subterranean draft carrying the faint tang of aged electrical ozone surged upward from the depths.

"The entrance to Underground B2..." Cheng Hao pulled down his goggles.

Through the Ghost Goggles, cascading streams of cyan digital code flowed like waterfalls along the stone banisters, while deep within the stairwell, a glowing crimson number pulsed in the dark: **404**.

## 五、鏡像中的腳步聲 (V. Footsteps in the Reflection)

"We found it!" Cheng Hao's face broke into a broad grin.

Yet before they could take their first step downward, every memory-alloy plate on PICO's body snapped rigid!

Bristling like an agitated metallic porcupine, PICO unleashed an ear-piercing electronic alarm:

*BEEEEEEP————!!*

The pup's head whipped toward the main library entrance, its sapphire optics flaring instantly into an ominous **blood-red**!

*Step... step... step...*

A measured, rhythmic cadence of polished leather shoes echoing against parquet floorboards drifted from the shadowy entrance corridor.

It was not the rumble of robot tracks. It was the deliberate footsteps of a human being.

A tall, poised silhouette stepped out from the shadows and into the dim shaft of afternoon sunlight.

He wore a neat, navy-blue knit sweater, a Lu-Yang Elementary faculty pin affixed to his chest, his face bearing a warm, familiar smile.

"M-Mr. Gao?!" Yijie stumbled back half a step in disbelief.

It was their sixth-grade homeroom teacher—the ever-patient, gentle mentor who answered their every question.

"Cheng Hao, Yijie."

Mr. Gao came to a gentle halt, his voice smooth and caring, exactly like a teacher checking in on students after class: "Fourth period has already begun. Why are you two playing pranks down here and making such a mess of the library?"

Cheng Hao opened his mouth to reply. But the instant his eyes locked onto Mr. Gao through the brass Ghost Goggles—

Cheng Hao froze solid, an icy chill rocketing straight up his spine!

In the polarized spectrum of the goggles, the figure standing before them was no living human at all!

Behind Mr. Gao's lifelike synthetic "skin," dense matrices of green machine code and holographic scan-lines flickered continuously! More terrifying still: across his left cheek, a micro-glitch tore through the projection, revealing a terrifying skull-like armature of precision steel gears and glowing circuit boards beneath!

The warm smile remained plastered across "Mr. Gao's" face, but the second half of his sentence transformed into the chilling, synthesized drone of the heavy enforcer:

"Surrender the goggles... **or I cannot guarantee you will advance to middle school in one piece.**"

Before his words finished echoing, the massive bronze security gates at the library entrance slammed shut with a thunderous *CRANG*!

Their only remaining route was the bottomless black basalt abyss behind them!
