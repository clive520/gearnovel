# 第二十七章：登陸浮空城「奧秘之翼」！ (Chapter 27: Landing on the Floating City of Aether)

## 一、萬米雲海上的白玉之城 (I. The White Jade Citadel Above the Cloud Sea)

Tearing through the final wisps of cirrus clouds, The Aoki Gear glided smoothly into the stratospheric heavens at an altitude of nine thousand meters.

Unfolding before everyone's eyes was an airborne marvel worthy of mythology—

The entirety of 【The Stratospheric Floating City · Wings of Aether】 was a colossal, dodecagonal airborne continent spanning over five kilometers in diameter.

The perimeter of the city was encircled by glistening white-jade colonnades and soaring brass arcades, between which coursed suspended clockwork aqueducts flowing with shimmering golden lubricants. Dozens of levitation monoliths weighing tens of thousands of tons—glowing with soft internal fluorescence—orbited the perimeter like obedient moons, maintaining the city's delicate micro-gravitational attitude.

And rising directly from the geometric center of this floating domain, piercing the deep midnight-blue firmament, stood a majestic two-hundred-meter bronze citadel—**"The Celestial Clock Tower"**!

Yet at this very moment, the aerial sanctuary was shrouded in the shadow of impending annihilation.

The entire floating continent listed at a perilous fifteen-degree cant toward the southeast!

More terrifying still, creeping across the central Celestial Clock Tower and the subterranean gravitational pedestals was a parasitic, writhing lattice of pitch-black **"Extraterrestrial Anti-Matter Resonance Crystals"**! The crystalline tendrils choked thousands of massive, exposed brass gears like living vines, pulsing with dark gravitational ripples that warped the surrounding air!

"The entire continent is sinking at an initial free-fall velocity of eighty meters per second; the outer ring's rotational angular velocity is decaying catastrophically!"

Gazing through her binoculars, Ye Yijie spoke with grim gravity: "If we cannot execute a precision landing upon the outer ring's 'Third Rotating Docking Cradle', we will not even find an entryway into the Celestial Clock Tower!"

## 三、銀翼少女塞西莉亞的登場 (III. Arrival of Cecilia the Silver-Wing)

"Phew... HAH! I swear by my ancestors, I am never riding a washing-machine landing like that ever again!"

Jiang Jiang collapsed like a sack of flour onto the deck, panting for breath, his seasoned skillet nearly tumbling across the floor.

*Hiss...*

The pressurized gangway hatch slid smoothly open.

A crisp, thin breeze scented with high-altitude aerosols and ozone washed over them. Though the city's gravity was only sixty percent of Earth's—making each step feel light and springy—the sensation of solid stone beneath their boots brought immense relief.

Yet just as Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Grandpa Cheng set foot upon the boarding ramp—

*SWOOOSH————!*

From the vaulted white-jade colonnade overhead came the razor-sharp whistle of an aerodynamic glider!

A nimble, silver figure vaulted into the air from a hundred-foot archway!

It was a girl of about twelve. She wore a tailored silver-white thermal flight suit, her shoulder-length golden-blonde hair shimmering under the stratospheric sun. Spread across her back was a pair of gossamer-thin, six-foot **"Deployable Superconducting Glider Wings"** fashioned from memory-alloy foil!

Executing an elegant, sweeping one-hundred-and-eighty-degree banking turn in midair, she planted her boots lightly onto the white-jade tiles. With a metallic chime, the wings folded, compressed, and snapped flush against her back armor plate.

Clutched in her hand was a gleaming brass celestial astrolabe. Her deep-blue eyes—initially narrowed with fierce caution—widened in stunned disbelief the moment she beheld Cheng Hao's first-generation optical goggles and the silver origami Shiba Inu perched upon his shoulder!

"Those goggles... and that room-temperature superconducting memory-alloy mechanoid?!"

She sheathed her astrolabe, striding forward with hurried steps, her voice brimming with awe and breathless hope:

"You... You are the heirs to the Thirty-Year Lighthouse Keepers' Vow?! The ones who pierced the thunderstorm and received our distress signal... it was you?!"

## 四、十六小時的大陸墜毀警報 (IV. The Sixteen-Hour Continental Crash Alert)

"Yes! I am Cheng Hao, and this is Ye Yijie, Jiang Jiang, and my grandfather, Cheng Yuan-Shan!"

Cheng Hao unclasped his goggles, looking sincerely into the girl's eyes: "We answered your transmission and climbed eight thousand meters through the clouds! What happened here?!"

A glint of tears flashed through the girl's eyes. Taking a deep breath, she laid her hand across her breast in a formal pilot's salute:

"I am the Seventh-Generation Sky Machinist of this floating realm——**Cecilia, callsign 'Silver-Wing'**!"

Cecilia led the team swiftly through the tilted, echoing white-jade corridors into the outer ring's primary observation rotunda.

Across the master holographic display in the center of the hall, the continent's descent trajectory and structural stress curves were spiking in terrifying crimson:


Pointing toward the Celestial Clock Tower suffocated in black crystals, Cecilia spoke with a heavy heart:

"Three days ago, a fragment of an 'Anti-Matter Black Comet' from deep space pierced the atmosphere, striking dead-center upon the mainspring core of our Celestial Clock Tower."

"The black crystals possess a parasitic, self-replicating resonance. They did not merely jam the gargantuan clockwork powering gravitational inversion; worse still... **the crystals consumed the 'Twelfth Golden Tuning Fork' that harmonizes the gravitational equilibrium between Earth and sky!**"

"With the carillon out of tune, the gravitational constant broke down. The aerostatic lift field keeping this city aloft is evaporating by the minute!"

"Worse still—"

Cecilia pulled up the perimeter airspace radar, showing two hundred-meter stealth dreadnoughts coated in radar-absorbent matte black hulls closing in through the lower cloud decks!

Emblazoned upon their prows was the crest everyone knew all too well—**Kuroshio Heavy Industries**!

"Kuroshio's aerospace expedition fleet!" Jiang Jiang cried out in shock. "Shen Tian-Cheng's secret masters followed us into the sky?!"

Cecilia clenched her fists, her gaze turning cold as diamond:

"Kuroshio's shadowy backers have coveted our 'Gravitational Reversal Clockwork Core' for decades! They intend to forcibly dismantle the core before impact, plundering the anti-gravity crystals without a care for whether this five-kilometer continent obliterates the towns below into a smoking crater!"

"Sixteen hours..."

Cheng Hao tightened his grip on his multi-tool screwdriver pen, determination blazing in his eyes: "Before Kuroshio's fleet breaches the gates, we will cross the city, reclaim the Twelfth Note, and haul this sky continent back to thirty thousand feet!!"
