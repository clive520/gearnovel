# 第十八章：聲納共振與夜光機械群 (Chapter 18: Sonar Resonance and the Bioluminescent Mechanoids)

## 一、天坑深處的死局 (I. Deadlock Deep Within the Cenote)

Inside the enclosed sea cenote, the air was so dense with tension it felt on the verge of liquefying.

Morning light streamed through the natural skylight hundreds of feet overhead, illuminating the pristine, translucent white "Ancient Lyra Observatory" at the center of the grotto.

The pale-violet 【Lyra Optical Astrolabe】 floated serenely in the center of four transparent energy pillars woven from high-frequency ultrasonic waves. Its twelve internal clockwork hands turned with delicate micro-adjustments, resembling an amethyst heart sleeping inside a deep-sea sanctuary.

Yet this tranquility was shattered in an instant!

The thirty-foot black steel prow of the "Deep-Sea Heavy Shield-Tunneling Sub Mk-IV" loomed like a primeval iron monster across the pool. Six diamond-tipped rotary augers smoked with friction heat, dripping pungent black lubricating grease into the water.

Shen Tian-Cheng stood atop the bridge balcony, his high-voltage arc scepter crackling with blinding blue-white sparks.

"Little Cheng Hao, I strongly advise you to draw your hand back."

Shen Tian-Cheng smiled with feline grace, though his gaze was as predatory as a circling vulture:

"This ancient observatory utilizes the pinnacle of **'Acoustic Levitation'**. The astrolabe floats upon the zero-pressure nodes of standing waves, its base wired to hyper-sensitive self-destruct oscillators. If an uncalibrated biological entity touches it, the field collapses in a millisecond, pulverizing that priceless crystal astrolabe into dust!"

"Shen Tian-Cheng, you will never use it to destroy the tectonic fault!" Lan tightened her grip on her folding oar, barking with fiery indignation.

"Destroy the fault? No, no, my dear girl. We call it 'Energy Restructuring'." Shen Tian-Cheng glanced back at the flashing crimson timer behind him with a cold laugh:

> `[TIME TO SEVEN SEISMIC SPIKES OVERLOAD DETONATION: 23 HOURS 15 MINUTES!]`

"Once the volcanic geothermal energy detonates, every drop of ancient crystallized power across the Gear Sea will erupt to the surface! Kuroshio Heavy Industries will monopolize the energy lifeblood of the entire ocean! As for the savages of these atolls and that quaint little school... who could possibly care?"

Shen Tian-Cheng leveled his arc scepter squarely at the pool: "Toss the astrolabe over! Or I turn this pool into an electric furnace with a hundred thousand volts and snap your bones one by one!"

*ZZZZZZZZ——!*

Arcing lightning danced across the water, setting off terrifying blue sparks!

## 二、甦醒的深海螢光機械群 (II. Awakening of the Bioluminescent Mechanoids)

Yet Shen Tian-Cheng's reckless high-voltage discharge committed a fatal, unforgivable mistake!

*GLUG-GLUG-GLUG——!*

From the hundred-foot depths of the cenote pool, a subterranean resonance echoed like the heartbeat of a great whale.

The crystal-clear water erupted with rippling, breathtaking rings of **emerald and sky-blue cold light**!

Then, before everyone's astonished eyes, tens of thousands of **luminescent creatures** ranging from one to six feet in diameter rose like a field of floating stars from the underwater fissures!

"Are those... jellyfish? No, they're mechanical jellyfish!" Jiang Jiang gasped, his half-eaten pineapple bun nearly slipping into the drink.

They were no natural fauna; they were an autonomous deep-sea sentinel ecosystem engineered by the ancient Thousand-Island civilization—**"Ancient Photonic Mechanoid Jellyfish"**!

Their umbrella bells were sculpted from gossamer-thin photoelectric crystals, housing rapidly meshing miniature brass clockwork movements; dozens of ten-foot metallic tentacles swayed with graceful majesty through the water, tipped with glowing spheres of **pure cold plasma**!

Within seconds, the dim grotto was transformed by tens of thousands of mechanical jellyfish into a dazzling, ethereal sea of cerulean stars!

"Warning! High-density unidentified autonomous underwater vehicle swarm detected!" alarms wailed inside the drill sub.

"What is this scrap iron? Fire acoustic cannons to disperse them!" Shen Tian-Cheng waved his scepter in disgust.

Dual high-pressure sonic disruptor cannons along the sub's flanks fired simultaneously, unleashing a grating, discordant blast of chaotic high-frequency noise!

*SCREEECH——BOOM!*

That blast stirred a hornets' nest!

Stimulated by the discordant noise, the serene sky-blue glow of the mechanical jellyfish flipped in a single synchronized millisecond into **raging, bloodthirsty crimson**!

Plasma spheres across tens of thousands of tentacles overloaded violently, sheets of ionization sparks dancing across the pool as a catastrophic electromagnetic pulse built within the cavern!

"Do not attack them with discordant noise!" Old Morris's voice crackled frantically through the comms. "Those are the lighthouse's ancient purger sentinels! They obey ONLY pure harmonic resonance! If their fury is not pacified within sixty seconds, their collective plasma storm will melt the entire cenote into molten slag!"

## 四、天琴海鳴與等離子審判 (IV. The Lyra Sea-Chime and Plasma Judgment)

*VROOOOOM——TOOOOT————!!*

Inside the cenote, an unprecedented, magnificent symphony blending pirate warship steam, a kitchen skillet, a sea skimmer, and a mechanical Shiba Inu resonated in glorious unison!

Lan floored the Seagull's throttle, channeling superheated steam like an azure dragon into Pipe 1;  
Buck hauled back the fog horn valve, eighty knots of booming steam thundering into Pipe 2;  
Jiang Jiang roared as he pressed his cast-iron skillet onto the relief valve, the vibrating metal tuning the third frequency;  
PICO transformed into dual-turbine flight mode, screaming ten thousand RPMs of ultrasonic blast into Pipe 4!

*DONG——!!*  
*CHIME——!!*  
*AUM——!!*  
*RING————!!*

Four majestic, transcendent notes—like gods strumming celestial harp strings—boomed through the enclosed limestone cenote!

The marvel of acoustic physics manifested in that glorious heartbeat!

Under the harmonic resonance of "Do - Fa - Sol - High Do," the node phases of the four ultrasonic levitation pillars canceled each other out with mathematical perfection—

*Hum...*

Without triggering self-destruct, the pale-violet 【Lyra Optical Astrolabe】 drifted down like an autumn leaf, descending with pinpoint grace straight into Cheng Hao's open palm!

Cool and smooth to the touch, its twelve internal clockwork hands flared with radiant violet-gold starlight!

Simultaneously, a scene of awe-inspiring grandeur unfolded—

Upon hearing that sacred ancient chord, the blood-red frenzy consuming the tens of thousands of mechanical jellyfish dissolved instantly, shifting back into serene, pure **aurora cerulean**!

And guided by that cerulean dawn, the ancient ocean sentinels swiveled their tentacles in unison toward the noise-polluting intruder at the center of the pool—Shen Tian-Cheng's shield drill sub!

"N-No... IMPOSSIBLE! How could you possibly command them?!" Shen Tian-Cheng shrieked, face draining to chalk as the horizon of luminescent jellyfish converged upon him!

*ZZZZZZZZ————BOOOOOOOOOM!!*

The plasma spheres of tens of thousands of jellyfish detonated simultaneously!

A colossal, swirling **billion-volt deep-sea plasma vortex**—thick as a fortress tower—surged forth like an incandescent lightning dragon, engulfing the Kuroshio drill sub!

The heavy tungsten-steel hull burned white-hot under the plasma torrent, the six diamond drill bits melting into slag within seconds! Every instrument across the bridge exploded in sparks as the primary reactor suffered a catastrophic short-circuit shutdown!

"Brat... I will never forgive you... NEVER..."

Shen Tian-Cheng unleashed a desperate, curdling howl as the paralyzed drill sub, belching black smoke, sank into the bottomless depths of the cenote pool!

Jiang Jiang danced across the observatory, waving his scorched skillet: "We defeated a high-tech drill with music!!"

Ding-Ding tumbled across the platform with PICO in her arms, while PICO happily licked her cheek with its cold alloy tongue, its electronic optics blinking with affection.

Lan sheathed her oar and stepped to Cheng Hao's side, gazing at the amethyst disc in the boy's hand with heartfelt awe: "So this is the Lyra Astrolabe... The fabled treasure that surfaces in the Gear Sea once every three hundred years!"

Cheng Hao took a deep, steady breath.

Unclasping the golden "Dodecahedral Ancient Astrolabe" from his chest, he seated it firmly into the dodecagonal receptacle at the center of the "Lyra Optical Astrolabe."

*CLACK!*

With a resonant, crystal-clear mechanical chime, the two ancient relics merged as one after thirty long years!

The clockwork hands and crystal prisms spun in harmonious frenzy, projecting a pure, dazzling **golden celestial laser beam** straight through the ceiling skylight, spearing into the clouds like a javelin of destiny!

Through his Ghost Goggles, Cheng Hao saw the final navigational vector pointing toward the bottom of the Abyssal Rift—

The pinnacle of the Lost Lighthouse was the sole destination for this celestial light!

Yet before they could celebrate, Old Morris's panicked roar tore through the comms:

The final three hours!
