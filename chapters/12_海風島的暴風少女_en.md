# 第十二章：海風島的暴風少女 (Chapter 12: The Tempest Girl of Windward Island)

## 一、海盜的飛爪與鐵鍋之威 (I. The Pirate Grapple and the Power of the Skillet)

*BOOM!*

The massive, crude iron prow of the pirate flagship "Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor" sheared through the ocean swells like a mobile iron cliff, halting less than thirty feet from The Aoki Gear.

The air hung thick with pungent black smoke from combustion of low-grade coal and the acrid tang of charred kelp.

Captain Buck stood atop the towering forecastle, his sun-bleached naval greatcoat snapping violently in the gale. Nudging his brass single-gear eyepatch, he curled his lip into a grim, self-satisfied smirk:

"I'll count to three! Surrender your mechanical beast and all your salvage crates! One..."

"Surrender your face! TASTE THE SKILLET!!"

Before Buck could utter the word "two," a roar of righteous thunder detonated across the deck of The Aoki Gear!

Checkered apron fluttering, Jiang Jiang summoned an astonishing surge of brute strength, gripping his gleaming twelve-inch cast-iron skillet with both hands like a baseball bat, swinging it in a ferocious arc straight into the path of an incoming magnetic grappling claw!

*CLANG————!!*

A resonant metallic crash echoing like a temple bell boomed across the sea!

The multi-pound steel grapple was smacked in reverse by Jiang Jiang's seasoned iron skillet, rocketing backward with a sharp *CLATTER* onto the pirate deck, splintering a heavy teak plank!

The recoil jarred Jiang Jiang's palms numb, yet he planted his feet and held the skillet before his chest, eyes wide as saucers:

"What kind of petty pirate tries to touch my pineapple buns?! I'll flatten your rear with my seasoned iron cast!"

Aboard the pirate vessel, Captain Buck froze in utter disbelief. Looking from the ricocheted grapple to the chubby boy in the apron, his eye twitched uncontrollably:

"Where did this cook crawl out of?! Ding-Ding! Deploy the heavy-duty Size 100 clockwork capture net—bag every single one of them!"

"Waaah! That little metal puppy is so precious!"

Peeking over the pirate bulwarks, a ten-year-old girl with bouncing twin pigtails popped her head out. Slung across her back was a rusted industrial spanner twice her size, her eyes sparkling with adoration at PICO:

"Captain! I want that silver doggy! I'm going to capture it and use it as my pillow!"

Perched on the bow rail, PICO's sapphire lenses narrowed into disdainful slits, its tail tapping a furious Morse sequence against the deck:

`-.-. .-. .- --.. -.--` (**CRAZY —— LUNATICS!**)

## 二、暴風舵手的撐桿飛躍 (II. The Storm Helmsman's Vault)

"Buck! You scrap-metal scoundrel, embarrassing yourself on Windward Island's front porch again!"

A crisp, fiercely spirited cry ripped through the maritime air from the starboard flank!

*VROOOOOM——BOOOOM!!*

The ten-foot steam skimmer "Seagull" sliced across the water like a diving petrel, carving a brilliant white crescent wave as it darted between the two vessels at fifty knots!

Balanced on the skimmer's bow, the sun-kissed girl with sporty cropped hair—Lan—coiled her legs with explosive force the instant the skimmer crossed beneath the pirate ship's second tow cable!

Planting the tip of her eight-foot alloy folding oar into the swell, she rode the recoil into an astonishing thirty-foot aerial arc like an eagle in flight!

*SHING!*

In midair, Lan drew a spring-loaded alloy blade concealed within the oar grip, slicing down through the heavy hemp hawser with surgical precision!

*SNAP!*

The thick hawser severed instantly, whipped back across the waves with a whistling shriek.

A heartbeat later, the girl completed a graceful aerial somersault, landing lightly on the teak deck of The Aoki Gear, her seagull-feathered straw hat tilting with the breeze.

"Lan?!"

Captain Buck jumped like a cat having its tail stepped on, his cheeks flushing crimson. Hastily tucking his harpoon gun behind his back, he stammered defensively:

"W-Who said anything about robbing anyone?! This is standard maritime cargo inspection! Thousand-Island regulations require all foreign hulls to pay antique component tariffs—it's high-seas protocol!"

"Protocol, my foot!" Lan crossed her arms, raising an imperious eyebrow. "These three travelers deciphered the Razor Reef light signals, proving they are true, seasoned sailors! If you dare park your rusted barge across the channel again, see if I don't confiscate every slice of candied kelp crisps on Windward Island next time—not a single crumb for you!"

"Y-You wouldn't dare!" Captain Buck sputtered in fury, but behind him, pigtail-clad Ding-Ding tugged at his coat hem, whispering aloud: "Give it up, Captain. Last time she withheld the kelp crisps, you chewed on raw dried mackerel for three days until your face turned green..."

*Pfft...* Standing behind them, Ye Yijie couldn't help stifling a giggle.

Cheng Hao stepped forward, offering a warm smile to the spirited girl: "Thank you for intervening. I'm Cheng Hao, this is Ye Yijie, that's Jiang Jiang, and this is PICO."

"Just call me Lan!" She clapped Cheng Hao on the shoulder with enough force to make his knees buckle. "That evasive turn past the spiny gear reef was brilliant helmsmanship! However—"

Before Lan could finish, the calm ocean waters erupted with a subterranean rumble like boiling abyssal magma!

---

## 三、深淵巨齒：發條大漩渦 (III. Abyssal Fangs: The Clockwork Maelstrom)

*GLUG-GLUG-ROAAAR——!!*

The water beneath The Aoki Gear and the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor darkened instantly into ink!

Massive white froth surged from the seabed as the water began spinning in a furious, clockwise vortex!

A colossal whirlpool over three hundred feet across collapsed abruptly right between the two vessels!

"The Clockwork Tidal Reversal!" Lan's easygoing smile vanished, replaced by sheer alarm. "Disaster! Windward Island's clock tower spring was three minutes slow today! The ancient seabed tide pump reversed early!"

Through the polarized spectrum of his Ghost Goggles, Cheng Hao saw the nightmare with terrifying clarity—

A hundred feet below in the abyssal trench, two gargantuan antique bronze water-pumping gears over a hundred and fifty feet in diameter were grinding together in furious reverse! The vortex acted like the gaping maw of a deep-sea leviathan, generating a terrifying centripetal drag exceeding twenty knots!

"The helm won't answer!" Cheng Hao dashed into the wheelhouse, throwing his weight against the spokes.

Yet no matter how furiously The Aoki Gear's paddle wheels churned, the relentless centripetal current dragged the vessel spinning toward the black abyss at the center of the vortex at several yards per second!

Nearby, the hundred-ton iron pirate ship fared no better. Her antiquated steam locomotive boiler wailed in agony, several paddle blades snapping clean off in the violent turbulence!

"Help! My cast-iron skillet is getting sucked in!" Jiang Jiang wrapped both arms around the mainmast, his legs flapping horizontally in the gale.

"Cheng Hao! A single ship's thrust cannot overcome this centripetal force field!"

Yijie braced herself against the chart table, her notebook pages flapping violently:

"The whirlpool's centripetal current velocity is 22 knots! The Aoki Gear produces 14 knots of forward thrust, and the pirate steam engine produces 12 knots! If we try to flee separately, both ships will be pulled into the abyssal teeth within sixty seconds!"

"If we can't escape alone, we unite!" An engineering spark ignited in Cheng Hao's mind, roaring through the megaphone:

"Buck! If you don't want to become fish food, heave your main towlines across! Lash hulls together for a **Vector Traction Maneuver**!"

"WHAT?! Entrust our lives to you landlubbers?!" Buck screamed through the sea spray.

"Shut up and throw the lines if you want to live!" Lan shouted, leveling her alloy oar at his nose.

Buck gritted his teeth, stomping on the spring-loaded line-thrower: "Ding-Ding! LAUNCH THE DUAL HAWSERS!!"

*WHOOSH——THUD!*

Two thick steel cables shot through the air. PICO blurred into a silver flash, catching the hawsers in midair with steel claws, lashing them securely around The Aoki Gear's primary load-bearing bollards!

## 四、合力矩與伯努利夾角 (IV. Resultant Vectors and the Golden Angle)

Tethered by heavy steel cables, both ships pitched violently along the vortex rim!

"Yijie! Calculate the resultant vector angle!" Cheng Hao bellowed.

"Vector Resultant Force Equation!"

Yijie's eyes functioned like high-precision optical sensors, locking onto the towing angle and the tangent of the vortex:

$$\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{F_1^2 + F_2^2 + 2 F_1 F_2 \cos\theta}$$

"Listen carefully! We cannot charge forward side by side—that aligns thrust against the centripetal current!"  
"The pirate ship holds the port flank, providing tangential breakthrough force $F_1 = 12$ knots!"  
"We take the starboard flank, providing centrifugal acceleration force $F_2 = 14$ knots!"  
"The course angle $\theta$ between both hulls must be locked at exactly—**sixty degrees ($\cos 60^{\circ} = 0.5$)**!"

Yijie raised her voice above the thunder of the crashing waves:

$$\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{12^2 + 14^2 + (2 \times 12 \times 14 \times 0.5)}$$  
$$\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = \sqrt{144 + 196 + 168} = \sqrt{508} \approx 22.54 \text{ knots!}$$

"**22.54 KNOTS! GREATER THAN THE 22-KNOT CENTRIPETAL DRAG!**" Yijie screamed. "Hold sixty degrees, and centrifugal force will sling us outward along the tangent of the whirlpool!"

"Buck! Did you copy that?! Port rudder thirty degrees! FULL SPEED AHEAD!" Cheng Hao barked into the speaking tube.

"I despise mathematics, but I'm trusting you this once!" Buck slammed the throttle to the firewall. "Fire-Bones, MELT THE FURNACE!!"

"Wait! We're one bar short of boiler pressure!" Jiang Jiang shrieked from the deck.

Throwing open his oversized cooler, Jiang Jiang hauled out two massive tubs of pure pork lard meant for frying pineapple buns, hurling them straight into The Aoki Gear's firebox door!

"TASTE MY ULTRA-HIGH-CALORIE FAT SURGE!!"

*BOOOOOM——!!*

The moment the refined fat hit the roaring coals, a sixteen-foot pillar of golden flame roared from The Aoki Gear's funnel! The steam pressure gauge spiked past the redline in a heartbeat!

Both brass paddle wheels spun at blinding RPMs, churning two titanic rooster tails into the sky!

Guided by the sixty-degree golden angle, the twin lashed vessels traced a breathtaking spiral along the vortex wall, rocketing outward like an artillery shell past the grinding bronze teeth at the center, bursting free with a thunderous roar to slam safely onto the tranquil outer waters!

*SPLAAASH——!*

A torrential shower of sea foam cascaded down like tropical rain.

Behind them, the abyssal tidal gear closed with a subterranean thud, the maelstrom calming into glass.

## 五、翡翠潟湖與血紅信標 (V. The Emerald Lagoon and the Crimson Flare)

"Ha... Haha! WE BROKE OUT! WE'RE ALIVE!!"

Jiang Jiang wiped grease and sea salt from his face, dancing across the deck with his skillet held high.

Aboard the pirate ship, Captain Buck straightened his crooked eyepatch. Looking from his intact flagship to Cheng Hao and Yijie on the opposite deck, his expression was a battleground of emotion.

Finally, the stubborn youth crossed his arms and looked away with a gruff scoff:

"Hmph... You landlubbers have some grit after all. We'll call it even this time! Ding-Ding, cast off the lines!"

Reeling in the cables, little Ding-Ding leaned over the gunwale, waving tearfully at PICO:

"Silver puppy! Next time I see you, I'll trade an enormous sausage for you! Goodbye!"

The Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor gave a brief toot of her whistle, turning her prow toward the sheltered harbor on the far side of the archipelago.

Guided by Lan's skimmer "Seagull," The Aoki Gear navigated a narrow limestone sea channel, bursting into a breathtaking paradise.

Unfurled before them was the jewel of the sea—**Windward Island**.

The water here glowed a translucent, jewel-like emerald green. Hundreds of stilt houses supported by colossal brass pontoons floated upon the lagoon, topped with whimsical clockwork weathercocks. Suspended rope bridges linked lush verdant cliffs to cascading waterfalls, islanders paddling outrigger canoes through water alleys filled with the scent of grilled fish and sweet coconut milk.

"Welcome to Windward Island!" Lan spread her arms proudly from the prow.

Yet just as The Aoki Gear dropped anchor by the weathered pier, PICO, sunbathing on the forecastle, suddenly snapped its ears straight up!

*BEEEEEEP————!!*

The pup unleashed an unprecedented, shrill warning howl, its sapphire optics flaring instantly into **blood-red alert**!

Cheng Hao's heart leaped into his throat. Pulling down his goggles, he traced PICO's line of sight toward the outer reef—

Miles offshore above an isolated crag, a piercing **blood-red distress flare** shrieked into the twilight sky, carving through the sunset clouds!

Through the high-magnification spectrum of the goggles, Cheng Hao saw a dozen streamlined black steel shapes tearing through the water at forty knots beneath the red smoke, homing in on the distress point!

Rising from their armored spines were razor-sharp, inverted-triangle dorsal fins!

A pack of—**Automated High-Pressure Mechanical Hunting Sharks**!

And in the lower corner of the goggles, an intercepted emergency encrypted telegram decoded furiously across the lens:

> `[DISTRESS CALL: LOST LIGHTHOUSE PATROL SKIFF AMBUSHED!]`  
> `[ATTACKER: KUROSHIO HEAVY INDUSTRIES · 1ST ABYSSAL PURGE SQUADRON!]`  
> `[OBJECTIVE: SEIZE THE ANCIENT CELESTIAL SLATE FROM OLD MORRIS!]`
