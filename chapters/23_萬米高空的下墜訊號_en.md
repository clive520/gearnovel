# 第二十三章：萬米高空的下墜訊號 (Chapter 23: The Falling Signal from 30,000 Feet)

## 一、鹿陽港口的微弱電波 (I. Faint Radio Waves at Lu-Yang Port)

The myriad golden rays of the setting sun painted the waters of Lu-Yang Port in a warm, molten gold.

Her deep-blue hull tempered by tempests and blazing trials, The Aoki Gear glided smoothly alongside the stone quay to the steady, comforting thrum of her engines.

Having conquered the mountainous swells of the Thousand-Island Gear Sea, the submerged clockwork maze, and the life-and-death ordeal in the mantle three thousand meters below, the four young heroes and Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan had finally set foot upon the firm, welcoming soil of the mainland.

"We made it... We really survived and came home!"

Jiang Jiang collapsed onto a stone mooring bollard with a heavy thud, hugging his legendary cast-iron skillet tightly against his chest, grinning foolishly through tear-filled eyes.

From the head of the pier, hurried footsteps and familiar shouts echoed across the salt air—

White-haired Principal Chiu, flanked by homeroom teacher Mr. Gao and a cheering throng of teachers and students, pushed through the crowd with unsteady yet desperate haste. When the old principal's glistening eyes locked onto the tall, unyielding figure of his brother-in-arms Grandpa Cheng on the forecastle, the two veterans—united across thirty years of a sacred lighthouse keeper's vow—fell into a tight embrace beneath the evening glow, tears soaking their shoulders.

"Yuan-Shan... You old sea dog, you haven't changed a bit!"  
"Old Chiu... Thirty years, and you've raised these children magnificently. Lu-Yang Elementary... was held in the finest hands!"

Applause and thunderous cheers erupted across the entire harbor.

Yet in the exact microsecond when the joy of reunion reached its peak—

*CLATTER! HUMMMMMM——!*

Perched obediently upon Cheng Hao's shoulder, the silver origami Shiba Inu PICO suddenly trembled violently across every superconducting shape-memory plate of its chassis!

Its triangular alloy ears snapped bolt upright like high-gain radar arrays, the micro-antenna upon its chest—jointly calibrated by Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Grandpa—erupting into a ferocious storm of golden electrical sparks!

Simultaneously, through the speakers of The Aoki Gear's long-wave underwater radio, without any external antenna connected, a transmission crackling with raging gales, screeching metal fractures, and desperate distress cries broke through the air:

> *"Kzzzt... Static... Calling surface... Calling ground..."*  
> *"This is... thirty thousand feet in the stratosphere... the ancient sanctuary 【The Stratospheric Floating City · Wings of Aether】..."*  
> *"Unknown extraterrestrial black crystals... corrupting the Gravitational Reversal Clockwork Core... master celestial hands slipping..."*  
> *"The entire floating continent... is plummeting toward the surface... at an initial velocity of eighty meters per second..."*  
> *"WARNING! Altitude has dropped below nine thousand meters!... If anyone hears this... please save the City of the Heavens..."*

The broadcast collapsed into a shrill wall of static, dissolving into a flat, deafening carrier whine.

## 二、氣壓高度計與垂直遞減率 (II. The Barometric Formula and Vertical Pressure Gradient)

The entire harbor froze in breathless, deathly silence.

"The stratosphere... A floating city at thirty thousand feet?!"

Ye Yijie's pupils constricted sharply. Tearing off her satchel, she whipped out her black journal and cipher pen, instantly plugging into the radio's residual telemetry buffer.

"This is no practical joke!" Yijie's stylus flew across the holographic oscilloscope. "The signal carries authentic high-altitude atmospheric telemetry headers: ambient temperature minus fifty-three degrees Celsius, atmospheric pressure less than one-quarter of sea-level standard!"

Cheng Hao hurried forward, his brass Ghost Goggles pulsing with emerald diagnostic lines as a series of precise atmospheric equations manifested across the HUD:

```
[STRATOSPHERIC BAROMETRIC TELEMETRY DECRYPTION]:
Isothermal Barometric Formula:
P(h) = P₀ × exp( - (M × g × h) / (R × T) )

Sea-Level Standard Physical Constants:
Sea-Level Datum Pressure: P₀ = 1013.25 hPa
Mean Molecular Mass of Air: M = 0.02897 kg/mol
Standard Gravitational Acceleration: g = 9.80665 m/s²
Universal Gas Constant: R = 8.3145 J/(mol·K)
Lower Stratosphere Standard Temperature: T = 220 K (-53.15°C)

Ambient Pressure Transmitted in Distress Signal:
P_curr = 264.36 hPa

[ATMOSPHERIC SCALE HEIGHT (H) CALCULATION]:
H = (R × T) / (M × g)
H = (8.3145 × 220) / (0.02897 × 9.80665) ≈ 6,438 m!

[ALTITUDE INVERSION EQUATION]:
h = - H × ln( P_curr / P₀ )
h = - 6438 × ln( 264.36 / 1013.25 ) = - 6438 × ln( 0.2609 )
Logarithmic value: ln(0.2609) ≈ - 1.3436!
Calculated Altitude: h ≈ 6438 × 1.3436 ≈ 8,650 meters!

[DESCENT ORBITAL KINEMATICS EVALUATION]:
Initial Descent Altitude: 8,650 m
Signal footer indicates: City autonomous emergency damping envelopes have deployed,
limiting terminal descent velocity to 400 meters per hour!
Maximum Time Remaining Until Total Surface Impact:
t = 8,650 / 400 ≈ 21.625 hours = 21 hours 37 minutes!
```

"**EIGHT THOUSAND SIX HUNDRED AND FIFTY METERS!**"

Ye Yijie gasped, looking up with wide, shocked eyes: "The floating city originally orbited at twelve thousand meters in the upper stratosphere; it has already fallen to eight thousand six hundred and fifty meters! Without those emergency damping envelopes, it would have crashed already!"

"Yet even with those envelopes..."

Yijie's fingers trembled slightly as she penned the blood-red conclusion into her journal:

"**In twenty-one hours and thirty-seven minutes... an entire steel floating continent over three miles wide, carrying hundreds of millions of tons of kinetic energy, will smash like a doomsday asteroid directly onto Lu-Yang Town!**"

## 三、鐘樓的劇烈震顫 (III. Violent Tremors in the Clock Tower)

"Twenty-one hours?!" Jiang Jiang leaped to his feet, nearly dropping his bread into the ocean. "A steel continent three miles wide crashing down... Won't that blast our whole town into microscopic dust?!"

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

As if confirming Yijie's terrifying mathematics, the century-old brick clock tower standing deep within Lu-Yang Elementary suddenly groaned with a deafening metallic resonance!

Then, an impossible, surreal spectacle played out before everyone's eyes—

Pebbles, dust, and dry fallen leaves across the harbor quayside broke free from gravity without warning, drifting slowly and gently upward into midair!

Cheng Hao felt his boots lose traction as his body weight plummeted by nearly a third, feeling as if he were treading upon a bed of compressed air!

"Gravitational anomaly... This is no natural event!"

Principal Chiu retrieved his dual-gear pocket watch from his waistcoat. The escapement hands at the watch face were spinning counter-clockwise in a frantic blur at dozens of revolutions per second!

Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan's face hardened like frost. Looking up toward the twilight sky, he spoke with solemn gravity:

"Thirty years ago, when Chiu, Morris, and I surveyed the abyssal clockwork array, we saw the heraldic seal of that airborne continent upon the parchment backing of an ancient sea chart—its name is **'The Stratospheric Floating City · Wings of Aether'**."

"It is far more than a city in the sky; it is the celestial gyroscope maintaining the gravitational harmonic balance of our entire planet."

The old engineer pointed toward the night sky, where strange concentric rings of violet-blue luminescence were beginning to shimmer:

"Housed within its core is the crowning masterpiece of an ancient civilization—the 'Gravitational Reversal Clockwork Core' and the 'Celestial Clock Tower'. Every hour, its bells chime, maintaining the micro-gravitational harmonic between Earth's crust and atmosphere."

"If it smashes onto the surface, not only will Lu-Yang be vaporized, but the cataclysmic gravitational shockwave will rupture continental fault lines across the globe, triggering a worldwide apocalypse!"

## 四、改裝計畫：青木齒輪號的平流層飛升 (IV. The Refit Plan: Stratospheric Aoki Gear)

"We have to save it!"

Cheng Hao clenched his fists, his eyes burning behind his brass goggles with fierce resolve: "Ascend eight thousand meters within twenty-one hours and restore that Celestial Clock Tower!"

"B-But... How do we get up there?!" Mr. Gao gazed into the sky with frantic dread. "Commercial aircraft cannot enter a gravitational distortion zone, and our town doesn't have orbital rockets!"

"Who said we need a rocket?!"

Cheng Hao turned and pointed toward The Aoki Gear moored along the pier, the brilliant spark of inventive genius blazing in his eyes:

"We have The Aoki Gear! Her hull is built upon a tungsten-steel deep-sea pressure keel, and her boilers are powered by high-energy ancient crystals from the Thousand-Island Sea!"

"All we need is mounting **'Dual-Layer Thermal-Resistant Balloon Envelopes'** over the hull and strapping PICO's **'Superconducting Ducted Jet Turbines'** along both flanks! The Aoki Gear will transform into a dual-mode amphibious skyship capable of piercing the troposphere and rocketing into the stratosphere!"

"Splendid boy! That's the wild fire I had in my youth!" Grandpa Cheng let out a booming laugh, slapping Cheng Hao's shoulder with immense pride. "Chiu, unlock the underground heavy ordnance workshop immediately! Mobilize every master machinist in town!"

Light blazed in Principal Chiu's eyes as he swung his arm with authority: "Townwide mobilization! All shipyard slipways ablaze twenty-four hours! Forge the skyship for our children!"

"PICO! Deploy superconducting wind tunnel mapping module!" Cheng Hao yelled.

*BARK!!*

With a joyful, metallic howl, PICO transformed into a streak of silver light, racing into the shipyard to inaugurate the ultimate race toward the stratosphere!
