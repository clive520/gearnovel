# 第十九章：登上迷失燈塔！ (Chapter 19: Boarding the Lost Lighthouse!)

## 一、沸騰大洋的生死衝刺 (I. Life-or-Death Dash Across Boiling Seas)

Beyond the waters of Lyra Reef Atoll, the sea was no longer placid mercury—it had transformed into a boiling, crimson inferno!

Tectonic magma surging from miles beneath the seabed spewed columns of superheated sulfurous geysers dozens of meters into the air. The atmosphere reeked of combusting gases and acrid ozone, while a thick crust of scalding pumice ash floated across the swells.

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

Along the distant horizon, a colossal ring of sinking oceanic abyss—gaping like a subterranean maw upon the planet's crust—was swallowing millions of gallons of seawater into the mantle at terrifying speed!

"Tsunami harbinger! That's the **'Abyssal Oceanic Black Hole'** created by tectonic subsidence!"

Lan gripped the helm of The Aoki Gear with both hands, muscles tensed along her sun-tanned arms. With the Seagull lashed securely to the starboard flank, she shouted against the scalding gale:

"The tidal balance of the entire Gear Sea has completely collapsed! The megatsunami will strike in... exactly **two hours and forty-five minutes**!"

*HUMMMMMM——!*

At that moment, the combined relic of the "Dodecahedral Astrolabe" and the "Lyra Optical Astrolabe" clutched in Cheng Hao's hands erupted with a two-foot-thick beam of pure, iridescent golden light!

This beam—imbued with ancient celestial energy—pierced through the churning smoke and volcanic toxins like a golden chain of destiny, spearing straight toward the bronze silhouette of the Lost Lighthouse six miles away in the rift!

"Look! Grandpa's optical navigation vector!" Cheng Hao shouted, pointing to a serene channel of water cleaved open by the beam. "Ride the light straight through!"

*HOOOOONK————BLARE————!!*

Yet the second The Aoki Gear lined up her dive, the six-hundred-foot dreadnought "Leviathan-0" swung twelve supercavitating heavy torpedo tubes around, blockading the rift canyon with crushing industrial menace!

"Target locked! All tubes salvo fire—collapse the canyon entrance and bury them!" Shen Tian-Cheng's merciless voice barked through the open maritime frequency!

"Buck! WATCH STARBOARD TORPEDOES!" Jiang Jiang screamed through his binoculars.

"I SEE 'EM!!"

The scarred iron prow of the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor cut fearlessly across the twelve incoming torpedo tracks, interposing her entire hull directly before The Aoki Gear!

Captain Buck stood atop his forecastle, coat tails catching fire from stray sparks, yet his soot-blackened face broke into a wild, untamed grin:

"Cheng Hao! Don't you dare look down on the backbone of Thousand-Island pirates! Saving the world is for you righteous scholars, but holding this sea gate... is the sacred privilege of the Iron Anchor Gang!"

"Ding-Ding! DUMP EVERY CLOCKWORK MINE OVERBOARD! BUY THE AOKI GEAR HER SHOT!!"

"CLOCKWORK MINES AWAY!!" Ten-year-old Ding-Ding swung her heavy sledgehammer with tears streaming down her soot-streaked cheeks, smashing the salvo detonation rack with unbreakable grit!

*BOOM! BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!*

With breathtaking valor, the Wave-Breaker Iron Anchor detonated a thousand-foot wall of underwater fire right before Leviathan-0's torpedo salvo, triggering a cataclysmic sympathetic detonation mid-swell!

"PUNCH THROUGH, CHENG HAO! DON'T LOOK BACK AT ME! BRING OLD MORRIS AND GRANDPA CHENG BACK IN ONE PIECE!!"

Buck's hoarse roar, accompanied by the thunder of the Iron Anchor's return fire, was swallowed by the churning sea behind them.

With tears stinging his eyes and knuckles turning white, Cheng Hao barked into the speaking tube: "Lan! MAXIMUM REVS! DIVE INTO THE RIFT!!"

## 二、重返迷失燈塔 (II. Returning to the Lost Lighthouse)

*SPLAAASH——WHOOOOSH!!*

Guided by the celestial laser vector, The Aoki Gear plunged down into the Abyssal Rift at a suicidal forty-five-degree angle.

Ambient water temperature and hydrostatic pressure skyrocketed in an instant.

Bursting through the heavy hydraulic blast doors of the lighthouse perimeter, the vessel berthed heavily upon the suspended cantilever pad two hundred and sixty feet above the seabed, the entire bronze sentinel shaking from tremors beneath the crust.

Old Morris stood waiting at the gantry edge, his forehead wrapped in thick bandages, carrying four high-temperature thermal environmental suits and heavy diving masks:

"You made it! Did you secure the Lyra Astrolabe?!"

"Right here!" Cheng Hao presented the merged relics to Old Morris.

"Praise the stars... Follow me to the pinnacle console!" Panting heavily, Morris led the youth sprinting into the crystalline rotunda atop the tower:

"The energy shield Yuan-Shan anchored three thousand meters below requires photonic stimulation from this pinnacle **Fresnel Lens**! Kuroshio's artillery fire knocked the lens off its confocal optical axis! If we don't recalibrate it now, the astrolabe's beam cannot penetrate the mantle crust!"

## 三、菲涅耳共焦聚焦與幾何光學 (III. Fresnel Confocal Collimation and Geometric Optics)

The team stormed into the pinnacle chamber.

The colossal thirty-foot "Master Fresnel Lens," composed of thousands of concentric crystal prisms, stood at the center. Directly behind it, mounted upon heavy brass gear carriages, rested the "Auxiliary Collimating Concave Mirror."

Currently, the optical axes of the two units were severely misaligned, scattering what should have been a tight pencil beam into a blinding, chaotic glare across the vaulted rafters.

"Confocal Collimation Geometric Optics!"

Yijie slipped into her thermal suit, dialing parameters into the optical calculator upon the desk:

```
[FRESNEL LASER COLLIMATION GEOMETRIC OPTICS EQUATIONS]:
Thin Lens Imaging Formula:
1 / f = 1 / d_o + 1 / d_i
Infinite Parallel Laser Condition (Piercing 3000m of mantle without divergence):
Image distance d_i ➡️ ∞, thus 1 / d_i = 0!

Compound Optical System Parameters:
Master Fresnel Convex Crystal Lens Focal Length: f₁ = +6.0 m (converging)
Auxiliary Metal Concave Mirror Focal Length: f₂ = -2.0 m (diverging)

Confocal Infinity Collimation Separation Law:
When two lenses share an optical axis with coincident focal points,
the emitted beam becomes perfectly parallel!
Confocal Standard Distance d = f₁ + f₂ = 6.0 + (-2.0) = 4.0 m!

[GEAR TRACK ADJUSTMENT PARAMETERS]:
Current vernier track spacing: d_curr = 2.5 m
Required displacement: Δd = 4.0 - 2.5 = +1.5 m (extend backward)
Handwheel Gear Ratio: 1 full rotation moves carriage by 0.5 m!
Precise turns required: 1.5 / 0.5 = exactly 3 turns!
```

"**CONFOCAL SEPARATION: FOUR METERS! CRANK THE HANDWHEEL THREE FULL TURNS!**"

Yijie cried out: "Master convex focal length is plus six meters; auxiliary concave is minus two! Only when separated by exactly four meters will the beam be compressed into a four-inch **Super-Coherent Parallel Laser** intensified ten thousand times, spearing clean through three thousand meters of solid rock!"

"Four-meter rail! Three full revolutions!"

Cheng Hao, Lan, and Jiang Jiang threw their combined weight onto the heavy brass crank handles beneath the rail!

"One, two... HEAVE!!"

The gears groaned against dry lubricant with heavy *clatter-clack* revolutions.

"One turn... one meter!" Jiang Jiang strained, face crimson.  
"Two turns... one point five!" Lan's arms bulged with effort.  
"Final half-turn... THREE TURNS LOCKED!!" Cheng Hao bellowed, driving the locking detent pin home with a heavy *CLANG*!

*CLACK!*

The concave mirror locked onto the four-meter confocal mark with pinpoint precision!

## 四、神聖光束與三千米地心升降機 (IV. The Celestial Beam and the 3000-Meter Depth Elevator)

"Cheng Hao! INSERT THE ASTROLABE!!" Old Morris bellowed.

Holding the merged relic with both hands, Cheng Hao drove it firmly into the cerulean-glowing receptacle at the heart of the Fresnel lens!

*HUMMMMMM——BOOOOOM!!*

The two-hundred-and-sixty-foot Lost Lighthouse resonated with a majestic, crystalline bell chime that shook the abyss!

Thousands of crystal facets refracted blinding streams of golden fire. Constrained by the four-meter confocal axis, the scattered light was violently focused into a wrist-thick **Ultimate Golden Coherent Laser Beam** burning with the brilliance of a thousand suns!

*BOOM!*

With unstoppable penetrating velocity, the golden lance speared straight down from the lantern room, knifing through the vertical clockwork labyrinth like a god's javelin, plunging into the bottomless basalt shaft at the center of the lighthouse floor!

A heartbeat later, the floorboards of the rotunda began descending smoothly amidst a tremendous hiss of hydraulic pressure—

A sixteen-foot circular elevator platform armored in four-inch thermal titanium plates—the **Geothermal Depth Elevator**—rose from the floor with heavy industrial clatter!

"This elevator descends straight to the Geothermal Hall!" Old Morris hauled open the blast hatch, shoving the four children inside: "GET IN!"

## 五、莫里斯的抉擇：斷後死鬥 (V. Morris's Choice: Rearguard to the Death)

Yet in the split second before the hatch could seal—

*CRASH! SHATTER——!!*

The four-inch pressure crystal dome of the lantern room was blasted inward into a blizzard of razor shards by an armor-piercing high-explosive shaped charge!

A dozen black high-tension grappling cables anchored violently into the rafters!

Exoskeleton armor gleaming, arc scepter in hand, Shen Tian-Cheng descended through the shattered roof flanked by eight cybernetic shock troopers armed with plasma cutting blades!

Shen Tian-Cheng's cultured face was twisted with savage scars, his eyes burning with rabid madness:

"Hahaha... Little vermin! Thank you for firing up the lens and unlocking the elevator for me!"

"SHEN TIAN-CHENG! YOUR OPPONENT IS ME!!"

A roar of righteous fury shook the rotunda!

Snow-bearded Old Morris hoisted a seventy-pound heavy magnetic storm wrench, swinging it like an avalanche, hurling the two frontline cyborg troopers backward in a spray of oil and blood!

Then, slamming his shoulder into the external blast interlock, Old Morris locked the elevator doors solid from the outside!

"GRANDPA MORRIS!!" Lan threw herself against the observation glass, pounding against the pane in tears.

"Lan! Cheng Hao! Listen to me!"

Bracing his back against the elevator door, Old Morris wiped blood from his lip. Looking through the glass at the children, his face broke into the most proud, radiant smile of his thirty years:

"Thirty years ago, Yuan-Shan, Chiu, and I made a vow... This lighthouse is MY post to hold!"

"Your battlefield is below! Save Yuan-Shan! Tear out those seven damnable seismic spikes!!"

"GRANDPA MORRIS!! NOOOO!!" Cheng Hao hammered against the reinforced glass.

Without looking back, Old Morris severed the master telemetry cable atop the cabin, throwing his weight onto the red skull-engraved **Emergency Maximum Dive Plunge Lever**!

"MANTLE GATES——OPEN UP!!"

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

Propelled by gravitational acceleration and superconducting maglev tracks, the heavy titanium elevator dropped like a meteor through the bottomless void, wind howling deafeningly as it plunged toward the mantle fault line three thousand meters below!

Through the fading glass window, Old Morris brandished his giant wrench, clashing with the swarming Kuroshio soldiers as blinding golden arcs and plasma bursts consumed the hall...

Across the elevator dashboard, the velocity needle spiked into the redline:

> `[DESCENT VELOCITY: -55 m/s!]`  
> `[DEPTH: -500m... -1200m... -2400m...]`  
> `[DISTANCE TO GEOTHERMAL MAGMA HALL: 600 METERS!]`  
> `[TIME TO SEVENTH SEISMIC SPIKE TSUNAMI DETONATION: 1 HOUR 45 MINUTES!]`

The final battle at the Earth's core had begun!
