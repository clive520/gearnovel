# 第十六章：齒輪漩渦中的水下迷宮 (Chapter 16: The Submerged Labyrinth of the Clockwork Maelstrom)

## 一、阿基米德浮箱的生死配重 (I. Life-or-Death Counterweights of Archimedes)

*SWOOOSH——ROAAAR!!*

Frigid deep-sea torrents, like raging primeval serpents, surged through the fractures bored into the outer bronze corridor!

In less than thirty seconds, seawater flooded past everyone's knees, rising at a terrifying two inches per second. Churning swells hammered against the thirty-foot "Archimedean Buoyancy Blast Door" with deafening metallic groans.

Wiping brine from his beard, Old Morris pointed toward three thick, transparent mercury pressure metering columns flanking the lintel, shouting desperately:

"The portal's mechanism is driven by three interconnected mercury ballast tanks ($V_1, V_2, V_3$)! Each links to differential gear lever arms of varying lengths ($L_1 = 1\,\text{m}, L_2 = 2\,\text{m}, L_3 = 3\,\text{m}$)!"

"Kuroshio's drill breached the initial pressure equilibrium! If the counter-lifting torques generated by the three tanks are not balanced within **one hundred and eighty seconds**, 100 atmospheres of external hydrostatic pressure will shear the door hinges clean off! The Fresnel crystal lens will be obliterated!"

Across the bronze lintel, crimson countdown digits ticked down with icy cruelty:

> `[WARNING: TIME TO HINGE STRUCTURAL FATIGUE: 89s, 88s...]`  
> `[LEFT LEVER L1 = 1m ｜ CENTER LEVER L2 = 2m ｜ RIGHT LEVER L3 = 3m]`  
> `[TOTAL WATER BALLAST UNITS: 11 UNITS (MUST BE COMPLETELY DISTRIBUTED!)]`  
> `[IMMEDIATELY ADJUST VALVES TO CONFIGURE DISPLACEMENT RATIO V1 : V2 : V3!]`

"Eleven total ballast units! Torque Equilibrium!"

Planting her foot upon a raised bronze valve pedestal, Ye Yijie wiped sea mist from her glasses, her pen scratching furiously across her waterproof journal:

"Archimedes' Principle of Buoyancy: Upward buoyant force $F$ is directly proportional to displaced fluid volume $V$ ($F \propto V$)!"  
"Lever Torque Equilibrium Condition: Torque $\tau = F \times L$! To equalize the balancing torques exerted by three unequal lever arms upon the portal hinges ($\tau_1 = \tau_2 = \tau_3$), ballast volume $V$ and lever arm length $L$ must be **strictly inversely proportional**!"

$$\tau_1 = \tau_2 = \tau_3 \implies V_1 \times L_1 = V_2 \times L_2 = V_3 \times L_3$$

"Input the lever lengths: $L_1 = 1, L_2 = 2, L_3 = 3$!"

Intellectual brilliance burned in Yijie's eyes:

$$\text{Assume a common equilibrium torque of } 6 \text{ units:}$$  
$$V_1 = \frac{6}{L_1} = \frac{6}{1} = 6 \text{ units!}$$  
$$V_2 = \frac{6}{L_2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3 \text{ units!}$$  
$$V_3 = \frac{6}{L_3} = \frac{6}{3} = 2 \text{ units!}$$

"Verify total volume: $6 + 3 + 2 = 11$ units! Exactly exhausts all eleven units of ballast water!"

"**DISPLACEMENT RATIO: SIX TO THREE TO TWO (6 : 3 : 2)!**"

Yijie snapped her head up, screaming over the roar: "Spin the left wheel six turns! Center wheel three turns! Right wheel two turns! HURRY!!"

"Left wheel is mine!" Cheng Hao threw himself onto the massive left wheel.  
"Center is mine!" Buck rolled up his sleeves, gripping the center wheel spoke.  
"Watch the right!" Lan shouted, jamming her alloy oar into the wheel spokes to heave with all her might!

"One! Two! Three... HEAVE!!"

*CREEEAK——CLANK-BOOOM!*

In the final ten seconds as water crested their chests, all three bronze wheels engaged with heavy gear clatter!

Left wheel: exactly six revolutions! Center wheel: three revolutions! Right wheel: two revolutions!

The three columns of mercury and gravity floats rose simultaneously to the exact same datum line!

*CLANG————RUUUUUUMBLE!!*

Three multi-ton hydraulic levers locked into torque equilibrium in the same microsecond! The half-meter-thick Archimedean bronze blast door slammed down like an awakened primeval titan, sealing with an airtight, ocean-shaking boom!

Subterranean drainage grates snapped open, and in less than thirty seconds, high-pressure pumps purged every gallon of seawater from the vestibule.

Crisis averted!

## 二、垂直八十公尺的立體發條迴廊 (II. The Eighty-Meter Vertical Clockwork Gallery)

"Phew... hah... Mercy... my shoes smell like salted mackerel!"

Jiang Jiang collapsed onto the dry floor, dumping a torrent of seawater from his sneaker, wheezing in exhaustion.

"Stop whining, chubby. If that blast door hadn't sealed in time, you'd be a deep-sea kipper right now," Buck scoffed, wiping brine from his brow, though his gaze shone with boundless awe for the youngsters.

Ding-Ding knelt beside PICO with a little handkerchief, gently drying its alloy ears. PICO purred with half-closed lenses, its tail wagging like an electric fan.

Cheng Hao dried his goggles, gently opening the inner sanctum gates leading into the lighthouse interior.

The instant the portal parted, every breath in the room caught in throat at the staggering spectacle—

The interior of the lighthouse was a colossal hollow vertical shaft nearly one hundred feet in diameter, soaring two hundred and sixty feet straight up!

Completely sealed from the ocean, the chamber was warm and dry, scented with antique clockwork oils and crisp ozone.

More extraordinary still: there were no staircases. Suspended throughout the vertical chasm were hundreds of interlocking **Colossal Horizontal Bronze Gear Platforms** ranging from six to thirty feet in diameter!

*Tick-clack... tick-clack... tick-clack...*

Driven by a central clockwork spine, these bronze gear platforms rotated in alternating clockwise and counter-clockwise orbits at varying ratios and velocities! Etched with tread patterns, the gear teeth glinted with soft golden fluorescence, forming a three-dimensional mechanical labyrinth suspended in the abyss!

And suspended in the center of the shaft two hundred and sixty feet above hung a three-story crystal wonder—**The Fresnel Eternal Lens**!

Yet at this moment, the inner beam was as faint as a dying candle, flickering with feeble golden pulses high in the rafters.

"This is the 'Clockwork Revolving Labyrinth' your grandfather and we constructed thirty years ago," Old Morris gazed upward, eyes reflecting thirty years of memory. "To reach the pinnacle console, the only route is leaping from one intersecting gear platform to the next!"

"Their rotational velocities are entirely different!" Lan observed the platforms. "Miss the jump window by half a heartbeat, and you plummet hundreds of feet into interlocking steel teeth to be ground into dust!"

## 三、最小公倍數與相位共振跳躍 (III. Least Common Multiple and Phase Resonance Jumps)

"It's not random jumping—it is pure mathematical phase resonance!"

Ye Yijie stood at the precipice, nudging her glasses as her gaze locked onto three critical gear platforms ahead:

```
[CLOCKWORK LABYRINTH PHASE PARAMETERS]:
Lower Gear A (48 teeth): 1 revolution every 12 seconds (counter-clockwise)
Middle Gear B (32 teeth): 1 revolution every 8 seconds (clockwise)
Upper Gear C (24 teeth): 1 revolution every 6 seconds (counter-clockwise)

Safe Traverse Window:
Only when the non-slip tread plates of all three gears align vertically
does a seamless 3-second jump corridor manifest!

Mathematical Principle:
Calculate the "Least Common Multiple (LCM)" of the three periods!
LCM(12, 8, 6) = 24 seconds!

Phase Alignment Law:
Every 24 seconds, the golden detents align precisely once!
Countdown to next alignment: 7s, 6s, 5s...
```

"**THE LEAST COMMON MULTIPLE IS TWENTY-FOUR SECONDS!**"

Yijie's voice rang clear and unwavering: "Every twenty-four seconds, the golden tread plates align for exactly three seconds! Jump together on my mark!"

"Countdown: Three... two... one... **JUMP!!**"

*SWOOSH! SWOOSH! SWOOSH!*

Cheng Hao led the vanguard, vaulting from the rim of Gear A, soaring across the chasm to land squarely upon the arriving rim of Gear B!

Behind him, Lan darted with the grace of a swallow, her alloy oar planting softly midair; Buck gripped Ding-Ding's collar with one hand, landing like a mountain goat; Jiang Jiang clutched his cooler, scrambled over the platform under PICO's magnetic tow!

*CLANK!*

The millisecond everyone landed, the grinding teeth of the gears below engaged with thousands of tons of shearing force, unleashing a blinding shower of golden sparks!

"Too close... My backside was nearly sheared in two!" Jiang Jiang clutched his heart, white as a sheet.

"Maintain rhythm! Three gear sets remaining! Next resonance phase: eighteen seconds!"

Guided by Yijie's millisecond-precise mathematical cadence, the team moved like a disciplined elite unit, leaping across the rotating two-hundred-and-sixty-foot vertical clockwork gallery.

One hundred feet! One hundred and eighty feet! Two hundred and forty feet!

Every leap resonated with the solemn, majestic symphony of interlocking bronze; every landing was anchored in the purest beauty of mathematical and physical law!

## 四、爺爺的全息投影與三十小時大限 (IV. Grandpa's Hologram and the Thirty-Hour Deadline)

"Phew... HAH!"

With one final athletic pull, Cheng Hao caught the brass railing of the pinnacle circular bridge, vaulting onto the master observation deck two hundred and sixty feet above the ocean floor!

One by one, the others hauled themselves up.

Spreading before them was the ultimate heart of the Lost Lighthouse.

The thirty-foot Fresnel crystal lens stood right before their eyes, thousands of hand-ground concentric prisms casting rainbow halos under the faint golden core light.

Beneath the lens rested an antique teak navigator's desk. Draped over the chair was a faded, meticulously cared-for navy-blue woolen sailor's sweater.

And in the center of the desk rested an ancient holographic projector pulsing with soft cerulean luminescence.

The instant the "Dodecahedral Ancient Astrolabe" on Cheng Hao's chest neared the dais, the induction rings spun up with a flurry of whirs!

*HUMMMMMM——!*

A pillar of pure white holographic radiance erupted skyward, weaving together in midair—

The next instant, a silver-haired elder wearing a navy-blue sweater, his eyes warm and unconquerable, appeared before them with the very smile Cheng Hao knew so well!

"Grandpa..." Cheng Hao's eyes welled with tears, reaching out his hand, only for his fingers to pass through immaterial light.

In the recording, Grandpa Cheng Yuan-Shan held a steaming mug of coffee, his gaze seeming to pierce three decades of time to look straight into his grandson's eyes:

> *"Hao-Hao, if you stand before this Fresnel lens... it means you have crossed the Thousand-Island Sea and arrived at the final frontier of my life's work."*  
>  
> *"Do not fear. I am currently in the 'Ancient Geothermal Core Chamber' three thousand meters beneath this very lighthouse."*  
>  
> *"Shen Tian-Cheng of Kuroshio Heavy Industries has gone utterly mad. Operating Leviathan-0, he drove seven 'Geothermal Seismic Spikes' into the mantle, attempting to detonate the subterranean volcanos and plunder the ancient crystal energy..."*  
>  
> *"I am utilizing the final clockwork array here to brace the tectonic fault line... but these old bones **can hold out for thirty hours at most**."*  
>  
> *"Once thirty hours expire, not only will the Abyssal Rift collapse, but the resulting megatsunami will drown Windward Island and Lu-Yang Elementary entirely!"*  
>  
> *"To restart the lighthouse and disarm the seismic spikes, you must obtain the final third key—the 【Lyra Optical Astrolabe】 I concealed in the **'Mirage Optical Refraction Waters'** north of Windward Island!"*  
>  
> *"Time is short, children... The future of our world is entrusted to you!"*

*ZZZZZT——CRACKLE——*

The holographic transmission broke up into static, dissolving into golden motes of light.

Thirty hours!

A desperate final countdown deciding the fate of the island, their school, and Grandpa's very life!

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE————!!*

Suddenly, the entire two-hundred-and-sixty-foot Lost Lighthouse shuddered violently!

Peering through the pressure crystal viewing ports, everyone looked down in horror—

In the midnight chasm of the Abyssal Rift below, seven colossal steel pillars dozens of meters thick, blazing with volcanic magma, shrieked through the water like poisoned needles, plunging straight into the mantle fault lines!

The sea began to boil furiously!

The earth groaned in agony; the deep sea roared in fury!
