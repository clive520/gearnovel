# 第十一章：向著海平線啟航 (Chapter 11: Setting Sail Toward the Horizon)

## 一、齒輪港的晨風 (I. The Morning Breeze of Gear Port)

The late-autumn morning sea breeze carried a crisp tang of salt mingled with Gear Town's unmistakable perfume of lubricating machine oil, sweeping across the sparkling azure waters.

Three days following the peaceful resolution of Lu-Yang Elementary's "Memory Crisis 404," an extraordinary medium-sized wooden steam expedition vessel lay berthed at Pier 3 of the historic deep-water docks.

Spanning nearly sixty feet in length, her keel was hewn from century-old teak, shielded on the outside by heavy plates of corrosion-resistant polished brass armor. At the stern rose a glossy, soot-blackened steam funnel, flanked by dual paddle wheels powered by high-tension coiled clockwork springs and hydro-turbines.

Chiseled into the hardwood prow above the reinforced ram was her name—**"The Aoki Gear"**.

"Thirty years ago, your grandfather hammered the very first brass rivet into this ship's keel in the old drydocks."

Principal Chiu stood beside the mooring bitts, wearing a navy double-breasted sailor's pea coat. The morning sun glinted against his gold-rimmed spectacles, mirroring deep contemplation in his eyes:

"Thirty years ago, we navigated this vessel to survey the hydrology of the Thousand-Island Gear Sea. Later, when anomalous abyssal clockwork currents rendered the waters perilous, the Maritime Guild declared it an exclusion zone... Little did I dream that three decades later, her helm would pass into your hands, Cheng Hao."

"Thank you, Principal Chiu."

Cheng Hao wore a rugged orange floatation vest over his hoodie, his brass Ghost Goggles polished immaculately and resting around his neck. Under the morning light, the boy's playful gaze had forged into the quiet resolve of a true explorer.

Standing nearby, homeroom teacher Mr. Gao had fully recovered his healthy color. Smiling warmly, he presented an engraved brass nautical cylinder to Ye Yijie:

"Yijie, these are the original charts and current almanacs of the Thousand-Island Gear Sea from thirty years ago. The hydrodynamics there are extraordinary; influenced by colossal abyssal tidal gears, the ocean currents undergo unpredictable differential rotations every six hours. Out upon the open waves, your intellect will serve as the expedition's truest compass."

"Rest assured, Mr. Gao!" Yijie accepted the nautical cylinder, stowing it securely inside her waterproof rucksack.

Today her jet-black ponytail was tied high, framed by a sky-blue sweatband across her forehead, her delicate spectacles swapped for sea-spray-resistant titanium frames, radiating athletic poise.

"Huff... puff... WAIT FOR ME! WAIT FOR MEEEE!!"

Just then, heavy, thunderous footsteps clattered across the wooden planks of the pier!

---

## 二、海上大廚的保溫箱 (II. The Sea Chef's Thermal Hamper)

Cheng Hao and Yijie spun around in unison.

At the pier entrance, crew-cut Jiang Jiang was hauling a camouflaged, heavy-duty wheeled thermal cooler nearly half his height, sprinting toward the gangway drenched in sweat!

Around his neck hung a pair of oversized naval binoculars larger than his face, a red-and-white checkered apron was tied around his waist, and most bizarre of all—slung across his back like a battle shield was an enormous, gleaming cast-iron skillet!

"Huff... puff... I'm dead! Absolutely dead!" Jiang Jiang collapsed onto his cooler, wiping a waterfall of sweat from his forehead.

"Jiang Jiang?!" Cheng Hao's eyes bulged. "What on earth are you wearing? And why are you carrying a frying pan?!"

"You have the nerve to ask?!" Jiang Jiang planted his hands on his hips with righteous fury, flipping open the cooler lid.

*WHOOOSH!* A mouth-watering aroma of fresh bakery goods instantly enveloped the entire dock!

Stacked in neat, golden tiers were over a hundred freshly baked, crispy pineapple buns, dozens of vacuum-sealed garlic sausages, and five enormous bags of Gear Town roasted seaweed crisps!

"You two tried to sneak off on a high-seas expedition to the Thousand-Island Gear Sea and leave me behind to take math pop quizzes?! Not on my watch!"

Jiang Jiang banged on his chest frying pan with loud *CLANG-CLANG* strikes, declaring with supreme authority:

"What is the single most vital element of an ocean voyage? Nutrition! Calories! Soul-warming comfort food! Without me—your Chief Culinary Officer—you'd be chewing tasteless hardtack and succumb to scurvy in less than three days! Mr. Gao already approved my two-week culinary internship leave—you HAVE to take me!"

Mr. Gao gave a helpless chuckle, nudging his spectacles and nodding to Cheng Hao: "The boy showed up at my doorstep last night and pleaded for two straight hours in my living room... His culinary talent is truly undeniable. Take him along; having him will keep spirits high."

"HOORAY!!" Jiang Jiang leaped into the air, hoisting the heavy hamper with one hand and scurrying up the gangway of "The Aoki Gear" like a nimble penguin.

Perched on the prow railing, the origami Shiba Inu PICO eyed Jiang Jiang's pineapple buns. Its sapphire lenses blinked with delight as its tail tapped a crisp Morse sequence onto the teak decking:

`--. --- --- -..` (**GOOD —— EXCELLENT!**)

## 三、皮可的兩棲渦輪模組 (III. PICO's Amphibious Turbine Module)

*TOOOOT——!*

With a resonant, deep blast of her steam whistle, The Aoki Gear slipped her mooring lines, twin brass paddle wheels churning frothy white foam as she pulled steadily away from Gear Port.

On the pier, the figures of Principal Chiu and Mr. Gao dwindled in the golden morning light, eventually becoming two distant waving silhouettes against the horizon.

Down in the ship's mechanical workshop belowdecks, Cheng Hao knelt by the workbench, tightening the final stainless-steel hex bolt into PICO's tail interface.

*CLACK!*

A crisp, satisfying mechanical detent engaged.

"Done!" Cheng Hao wiped grease from his palms, gesturing to Yijie and Jiang Jiang. "Synthesizing Grandpa's blueprints over the past two days, I tailored this especially for PICO—**The Amphibious Marine Propulsion Exoskeleton**!"

"PICO, engage submerged drive mode!"

At Cheng Hao's command, PICO stood at attention.

The paper-thin memory-alloy plates across its chassis shifted backward in a fluid *shhh-shing* wave! Its coiled tail, normally composed of seven nested rings, reconfigured within three seconds into a four-inch shrouded **superconducting high-speed hydro-turbine**!

And beneath its four steel paws, four miniature high-vacuum suction cups deployed, allowing it to sprint across a slippery metal deck tilted at sixty degrees without slipping a single millimeter!

"Whoa! That is so cool!" Jiang Jiang, chewing on a sausage, stared wide-eyed. "Can it help me whisk egg whites for meringues?!"

"Jiang Jiang! PICO is a precision reconnaissance automaton, not an eggbeater!" Yijie rapped Jiang Jiang on the knuckles with her pen.

"It's far more than a propeller," Cheng Hao grinned, adjusting his goggles. "In an open-sea emergency, its four limbs can unfold into a three-foot-wide alloy hydrofoil board, skimming across the swells at forty knots!"

PICO raised its head proudly, chiming a cheerful electronic bark: *Beep-boop!*

## 四、迷霧與閃爍的信標浮標 (IV. Fog and the Flashing Beacon Buoy)

The Aoki Gear navigated open waters under tranquil skies for four full hours.

Just past midday, the pristine blue sky began undergoing an eerie transformation.

The azure water darkened rapidly into an impenetrable midnight-cyan. Stranger still, miniature golden bubbles resembling gear teeth surged from beneath the breaking swells!

Humidity soared in an instant as a wall of milk-white sea fog materialized silently from the horizon, swiftly swallowing the vessel whole.

"Visibility is down to less than a hundred feet!"

Jiang Jiang shouted down from the crow's nest atop the mast, binoculars pressed to his eyes: "White fog in every direction! Wait... dark shapes beneath the keel! Massive dark silhouettes!"

Cheng Hao leaped to the bridge, pulling down his brass Ghost Goggles.

In the polarized spectrum of the lenses, the underwater view made Cheng Hao gasp!

Less than two hundred feet dead ahead in the deep trenches lay hundreds of jagged, towering metal-basalt reef spires! Across every spire, colossal antique bronze gears measuring dozens of feet wide interlocked, turning at varying velocities driven by powerful subterranean currents!

"The Razor Reefs of the Gear Sea!" Yijie unfolded her waterproof charts, her face tightening. "The currents here are ferocious; if a wooden hull drifts off the channel by even ten feet, rotating bronze teeth will shred it into matchwood!"

Right at that moment, a rhythmic bell tolled from within the dense fog ahead:

*CLANG... CLANG... CLANG...*

Rising from the mist was a thirty-foot cast-iron navigational buoy.

Mounted atop the buoy, a high-intensity maritime searchlight was cutting through the soup, flashing a frantic sequence of white light pulses toward The Aoki Gear!

"An antique maritime light code!" Yijie uncapped her pen, logging flash durations in her black journal:

```
[RAZOR REEF NAVIGATION BEACON]:
Flash Pattern Characteristics:
[1 flash, sustained 3 seconds] ➡️ 1s eclipse
[3 quick flashes, 0.5s each] ➡️ 1s eclipse
[2 flashes, 1.0s each] ➡️ 2s eclipse

Base Inscription Formula:
Safe Channel True Course (TC) Calculation:
TC = (Phase 1 duration in seconds × 100)
   + (Phase 2 flash count × 10)
   + (Phase 3 total seconds × 5)
   - 50 degrees magnetic variation!

WARNING: A heading error greater than 2 degrees
will drive the ship into the port gear spire!
```

"Maritime Light Characteristics and True Course calculation!" Yijie's pen glided across the waterproof paper:

"Phase 1: 1 flash sustained 3 seconds ➡️ Duration is **3**!"  
"Phase 2: 3 quick flashes ➡️ Flash count is **3**!"  
"Phase 3: 2 flashes of 1s each ➡️ Total duration is 2 × 1 = **2**!"

"Input into the True Course equation!" Yijie shouted the arithmetic aloud:

$$\text{TC} = (3 \times 100) + (3 \times 10) + (2 \times 5) - 50$$  
$$\text{TC} = 300 + 30 + 10 - 50 = 290^{\circ} !$$

"**TRUE COURSE: 290 DEGREES! WEST-NORTHWEST!**" Yijie yelled. "Cheng Hao, hard to starboard! Lock the binnacle onto 290 degrees!"

"Understood! Heading 290!"

Gripping the heavy teak helm with both hands, Cheng Hao threw his weight into hauling the wheel half a turn to starboard!

*CREEEAK——BOOM!*

The Aoki Gear listed sharply in the roiling swell, her paddle wheels howling at maximum RPM!

The exact second the bow swung around, a twenty-five-foot basalt spire studded with spinning, razor-sharp gear teeth shaved past the port copper plating by a mere **twelve inches**! Freezing spray washed over Cheng Hao's face, sending shivers of adrenaline down his spine!

Like a swift marlin, The Aoki Gear threaded the 290-degree deep-water channel, knifing safely through the deadly reef!

## 五、破浪的滑行艇與海盜的號角 (V. The Wave-Skimmer and the Pirate Horn)

"Phew... WE MADE IT!"

Jiang Jiang leaned over the crow's nest, clutching his chest: "Long live Yijie! We didn't get ground into chum!"

Yet as The Aoki Gear burst through the reef's fog bank, what met their eyes left all three children slack-jawed.

The mist vanished.

Unfolding before them was a breathtaking, boundless ocean—

Dozens of islands dotted the waters, each supported by massive concentric bronze clockwork pedestals that rose and sank in majestic rhythm with the ocean swell. On the far horizon stood magnificent, ancient sea towers topped with revolving giant gear windmills!

This was the legendary **Thousand-Island Gear Sea**!

Yet before they could savor the wonder, a high-pitched engine whine ripped through the calm water!

*VROOOOOM——BOOOOM!!*

Twin plumes of rooster-tail spray cleaved the surface!

A ten-foot watercraft fashioned from lightweight driftwood and salvaged brass plates was rocketing straight toward The Aoki Gear at an astonishing fifty knots!

Balanced on one leg upon the skimmer's bow was a twelve-year-old girl with sun-kissed golden skin and a sporty short haircut.

Tilted over her head was a woven straw hat adorned with a white seagull feather, a glowing conch-shell wind gauge tied at her waist, and an eight-foot dual-bladed folding alloy oar gripped in her hand!

Riding the gale, she yanked the throttle lever with practiced grace, carving a magnificent ninety-degree power slide across the water just thirty feet from The Aoki Gear, throwing up a ten-foot wall of sea foam!

"HEY! OUTSIDERS!"

Holding her oar with one hand and tipping her hat brim with the other, she flashed a dazzling, brilliant smile of healthy white teeth, shouting up at Cheng Hao:

"Stunning helmsmanship! Sneaking through the Razor Reefs without scratching your paint! I am **Lan**, Chief Navigator of Windward Island!"

Yet the smile lingered on Lan's face for less than a heartbeat before her expression hardened, snapping her gaze toward the fog bank behind The Aoki Gear:

"Hard over! Your clumsy antique tub has been marked!"

*HOOOOONK————BLARE————!!*

A deep, brutal mechanical fog horn roared like an ancient iron leviathan from within the mist!

Bursting through the fog like a mobile mountain of black iron was a hundred-foot pirate warship, crudely welded together from three salvage freighters and a retired steam locomotive!

Welded to her towering ramming bow was the infamous skull insignia feared across the Thousand-Island Sea—two crossed giant spanners beneath a heavy black iron anchor!

And standing upon the warship's quarterdeck was a thirteen-year-old youth cloaked in a tattered naval greatcoat, wearing a brass single-gear eyepatch, aiming a hissing steam-powered harpoon cannon straight at Cheng Hao's throat:

"This sea is our turf, these swells our toll! Hand over all your antique components and that mechanical pup... **or the Iron Anchor Gang will punch a sunroof through your hull!**"
