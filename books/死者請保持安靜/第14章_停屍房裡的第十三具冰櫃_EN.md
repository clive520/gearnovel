# Chapter 14: The Thirteenth Freezer in the Morgue

At 8:40 in the evening, torrential rain lashed against the reinforced glass curtain walls of the Binjiang Municipal Public Security Bureau like a dark waterfall.

The crimson flash of cruiser beacons traced piercing arcs across the flooded precinct courtyard. Escorted by two heavily armed SWAT officers, the primary suspect, Qin Mo, was marched from the transport van toward the maximum-security interrogation block.

Meanwhile, outside the Forensic Pathology Laboratory on the building's subterranean first level, Old Zhou had finally peeled off his stench-ridden, sodden trousers. Now clad in a loose gray police fleece, he still clutched his pink thermal mug with its faded strawberry decal with unbroken devotion.

"Little Pei, I had the cafeteria whip up two piping-hot bowls of ginger noodle soup with three poached eggs," Old Zhou took a swig of warm tea, glancing sideways at the young man hoisting the silver field kit. "Tonight we yanked more than eighteen hundred people back from the gates of hell. Old fox Chu Tiancheng is locked down under twenty-four-hour plainclothes surveillance in the ICU. But tell me... what did Qin Mo mean by that parting shot when the cuffs snapped shut?"

"Qin Mo exhibits the classic psychophysiological profile of a subordinate antisocial personality," Pei Yi'an remarked, scanning his biometric badge at the laboratory portal and donning disposable protective coveralls. "He commands the microscopic precision of a master horologist, yet his cognitive architecture relies desperately upon an external locus of control. Over the thirty years following Lin Qingyuan's demise, he required an omnipotent mental host to direct his actions. The true 'Ferryman' does not merely possess the full truth of 1994; he orchestrated every move Qin Mo executed tonight with mathematical precision."

The automated sliding glass doors parted, releasing an embrace of air infused with formalin, chilled refrigerant, and the faint, acidic bite of antiseptic wash.

To ordinary mortals, such a scent evoked dread; to Pei Yi'an, it was the purest, most tranquil sanctuary amidst the frantic noise of the living city.

"Technician Li? Where's the duty officer tonight?" Old Zhou asked, stepping into the autopsy ante-room and rapping the call bell on the counter.

The reception area stood deserted. Only a swivel chair gently swayed in the draft, a half-finished mug of instant coffee cooling on the desk beside it.

"A trace signature of neuromuscular blockade agents," Pei Yi'an noted, his nostrils flaring slightly before his gaze snapped toward the corner behind a stainless gurney.

Slumped against the corner wall sat young Technician Li, eyes closed, his breathing slow and rhythmic. Affixed to his jugular groove was an ultra-thin transdermal micro-patch.

Unsheathing his flashlight, Old Zhou bounded forward to check the technician's airway: "He's alive! Vitals are steady. Same as that guest violinist at the Grand Theatre—titrated down to the microgram!"

"This is not an intrusion; it is a proclamation," Pei Yi'an said, his voice void of warmth.

He strode straight toward the heavy lead-shielded airtight door that guarded the inner mortuary vault.

*Zzzzt—zzzzt—*

The instant their boots crossed the threshold, the overhead fluorescent luminaires hummed with an ominous electric arc. The subterranean chamber's lighting blinked twice in rapid succession before succumbing to the dull, bloody glow of auxiliary emergency baseboard runners.

Amidst the crimson gloom, the twelve stainless-steel mortuary drawers stood arrayed like twelve silent, glacial tombstones.

Yet as Old Zhou's gaze scanned beyond the standard freezer bay toward the shadowed recesses, all air fled his lungs in a ragged gasp.

"Little Pei... look at that partition wall..."

In the seamless expanse of ceramic tiles, a half-meter gap had slid quietly open into a hidden crypt!

It was a concealed refrigeration annex embedded deep within the reinforced concrete sandwich of the municipal bomb shelter. Obscured by decades and lost administrative handovers, even Old Zhou, with thirty years on the force, had never glimpsed the space behind that wall.

And deep within the swirling fog behind that secret door extended a lone, humming low-temperature vault—

It was the thirteenth freezer in the morgue!

Its digital LED readout glowed with an icy cyan light, hovering steadily at a chilling "-18°C."

What made Old Zhou's pulse race even faster was the pristine brass evidence tag dangling from the central pull-latch, crisply stamped in block typeface:

**[Binjiang Municipal Public Security Bureau · 1994 Suspicious Drowning Cold Case · Docket No. 94-0717]**

**[Deceased: Former Chief of Pathology, Municipal Hospital—Dr. Lin Qingyuan]**

"Dr. Lin's cold case file from thirty years ago..." Old Zhou's voice shook with uncharacteristic tremor. "Back then, when Dr. Lin's remains were pulled from Dry Dock No. 2, the family signed the cremation waiver and his ashes were interred decades ago... Just who on earth is sealed inside this thirteenth freezer?!"

Pei Yi'an uttered not a syllable. Donning double latex gloves and a sterile face shield, he stepped calmly into the hidden recess.

His fingers clamped around the bone-chilling steel handle of the drawer.

*Click.*

With the sharp release of the spring catch, a dense cascade of sub-zero dry-ice vapor billowed outward like a miniature cataract.

Engaging his forearms, Pei Yi'an steadily drew the heavy stainless-steel rolling tray out along its lubricated runners.

As the fog parted under the crimson emergency glow, the spectacle resting upon the slab forced even the hardened veteran detective half a step back, his scalp prickling with sheer cold dread—

What lay upon the tray was neither a desiccated cadaver nor Lin Qingyuan's headless skeleton.

It was an intact, articulated adult human skeleton, dressed impeccably in a vintage 1994 regulation white physician's lab coat!

All two hundred and six bones had been strung together with microscopic titanium wire, reposing with serene elegance in the center of the tray. Above the first cervical vertebra, where the skull should have rested, sat an empty cradle—as if waiting in silent expectation for the cranium recovered from the conduit to be returned.

Odder still, nestled directly between the crossed skeletal forearms over the sternum rested an antique micro-cassette tape recorder!

The cassette bay gaped open, an aged red tape cartridge sitting poised beneath the magnetic read head.

"Old Zhou, step back," Pei Yi'an commanded, his voice weighted with rare gravity.

Armed with long-shank forceps, he cautiously probed the padding beneath the recorder, deftly steering clear of any potential mechanical tripwires.

There were no explosive bricks, nor mercury level-switches.

Only a gilded black card sewn into the coat's breast pocket with surgical braided silk:

*"Dear Pathologist Pei Yi'an: Welcome to the nucleus of judgment. The instant you unsealed this freezer, the final post-mortem of the departed commenced its countdown."*

*Zzzzz—*

The very microsecond Pei Yi'an parsed the final syllable, a micro-solenoid beneath the tray snapped shut!

Inside the thirty-year-old tape recorder, the capstans spun into frantic, humming life!

In the next heartbeat, the open lead-lined hermetic portal of the morgue slammed shut with a concussive metallic crash, deadbolts driving deep into the jamb!

*SLAM—!!*

Overhead, the damper actuators of the HVAC ducts reversed violently, the exhaust grates whistling with high-pressure backdraft.

A colorless aerosol carrying the bitter hint of almonds and a subtle trace of synthetic vanilla spewed violently into the vault like an invisible serpent!

Old Zhou hurled himself against the slab, wrenching desperately at the manual bypass lever, only to find the cam-locks welded fast by blown thermal fuses!

"The door is sealed! The vents are pumping poison in!" Old Zhou slammed his fists on the armored glass, shouting into his radio only to meet deafening white noise. "Signal's jammed! Little Pei! What kind of gas is this?!"

Pei Yi'an tore down two high-grade NBC respirators from the emergency locker, shoving one over Old Zhou's head before strapping the second firmly over his own visage.

Behind his gold frames, Pei Yi'an's pupils shrunk into twin icy needle-points as his voice rasped through the exhale valve:

"High-concentration sevoflurane blended with inert halon aerosol. It is an industrial laboratory fire-suppressant and anesthetic compound—within five minutes, it will displace all ambient oxygen and depress the central nervous system into an asphyxiant coma."

Pei Yi'an whirled toward the spinning cassette nestled between the skeletal hands of the thirteenth freezer:

"The Ferryman never intended to sit here awaiting our arrival... This entire Forensic Pathology Center is the operating theater he prepared for our dissection!"
