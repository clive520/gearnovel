# Chapter 12: Debris Ciphers Deep in Old Drainage

Gales atop the clocktower pierced through the louvered shutters, howling like the shrieks of vengeful specters.

At the dead center of the mechanical movement, the crimson digital timer pulsed feverishly, its figures now glaring at "01 : 04 : 18." Barely sixty-four minutes remained before the symphony concert commenced at eight sharp at the Binjiang Grand Theatre.

"I'm climbing up there right now to smash those damn bones and sever the detonator wires!" Old Zhou growled ferociously, yanking a tactical multi-tool from his belt and preparing to step across the spinning bed of tens of thousands of interlocking brass gear trains.

"Captain Zhou, if your objective is to reduce this clocktower and the three of us into cinders within a single microsecond—and detonate the Grand Theatre sixty minutes ahead of schedule—by all means, make the cut."

Pei Yi'an's glacial tone acted like a potent sedative, freezing Old Zhou rigidly mid-stride.

The young forensic pathologist crouched, focusing his flashlight into a crisp beam of cold luminescence that settled upon the thirteen suspended radii and ulnae flanking the timer.

"Observe the epiphyseal junctions at both ends of the bones," Pei Yi'an said, hovering his long forceps three millimeters above an ulna. "The medullary cavities of these thirteen bones have been hollowed out and injected with mercury and highly photosensitive chemical reagents, daisy-chained with ultra-fine silvered copper filaments into a dynamic torque balance sensor. The moment any external tampering exceeds 0.5 Newtons, or any wire is snipped to cause a potential imbalance, the piezoelectric ceramic striker at the base will instantly unleash a high-voltage microwave pulse."

Cold sweat beaded on Old Zhou's forehead as he silently holstered his tactical pliers, promptly unscrewing his pink thermos to gulp a mouthful of warm water. "Who in the hell taught that brat this craft? Did Municipal Hospital's pathology department thirty years ago offer electives in bomb-making?"

"This is not pathology; this is a monstrous hybridization of horological engineering, neurophysiology, and demolition dynamics." Pei Yi'an's gaze cut like a scalpel, tracing the heavy black coaxial cables running down from behind the timer. "Furthermore, this is not the origin of the transmission."

"What?!" Su Tang, perched at the entrance of the spiral staircase hugging her military laptop, gasped aloud. "Teacher Pei is spot on! My spectrum analyzer just registered electromagnetic echo returns! The apparatus on the top floor is merely a passive repeater antenna and resonant switch. The master microwave control station... is beneath our feet! Deep within the subterranean foundations of this tower!"

Wasting not a second, the trio bounded down the cramped, rusted cast-iron spiral staircase, heading straight for the abandoned boiler room in the clocktower's subterranean basement.

The boiler room was flooded knee-deep with black coal-slurry runoff, the stagnant air heavy with sulfur fumes and pungent ferric rot. Behind a collapsed heavy-oil boiler in the corner, a massive cast-iron manhole cover had been levered askew by half a meter, revealing a gaping vertical shaft descending into pitch blackness.

"This is a German-engineered municipal storm conduit constructed by concession authorities in 1922," Su Tang reported, pulling up the historic municipal blueprints on her tactical tablet. "Two and a half meters in diameter and 3.8 kilometers in total length, it cuts directly beneath Clocktower Alley through the old docklands, culminating directly into the stormwater retention basin beneath the Binjiang Grand Theatre!"

"A bloody rat hole if I ever saw one," Old Zhou cursed under his breath, clicking on his headlamp. He was the first to grab the slimy, icy rungs of the iron ladder and drop into the abyss. "Little Su, remain up top to coordinate communications. Little Pei, stay glued to my six! If there's an ambush down there, I'll take the brunt with my riot shield!"

Without a word, Pei Yi'an hitched his trench coat hem into his belt, grabbed his silver forensic field kit, and descended the ladder with crisp, practiced agility.

Deep inside the conduit, the underground surge churned swift and freezing, the turbid, soot-stained flood reaching their upper thighs, while the stench of marsh gas and industrial runoff induced immediate nausea.

"Little Pei, once this case wraps, you're drafting a workman's comp claim for me," Old Zhou grunted through gritted teeth, trudging upstream along the slimy brick culvert. "Write down: 'Senior detective braved century-old cesspool in line of duty, suffering acute psychological trauma and catastrophic loss of trousers.'"

"Objectively speaking, the dominant constituents here are stormwater sediments and asphalt particulates; fecal coliform counts remain within acceptable thresholds," Pei Yi'an remarked dispassionately under his waterproof UV light. "However, should your thermos tumble into this water, I recommend immediate autoclave sterilization, if not outright disposal."

Hearing this, Old Zhou instinctively clutched the thermos tighter against his tactical vest, guarding it like a mother hen shielding her brood.

Following the fresh black signal cable taped along the upper brickwork, the two pushed roughly three hundred meters into the subterranean water maze.

After squeezing past a rusted gravity sluice gate, the passage opened into a vaulted chamber.

It was a dry, long-decommissioned subterranean distribution vault, its red-brick arches draped in pale cobwebs and saltpeter efflorescence. In the middle of the chamber, atop an elevated concrete slab, a portable rechargeable fluorescent lamp shone brightly.

Beneath its harsh, sterile glare sat a makeshift surgical workbench rigged from a salvaged wooden door leaf.

"Captain Zhou, look there," Pei Yi'an muttered, his pupils constricting sharply.

In the middle of the operating surface lay no flesh, only an intact human cranium mounted securely upon a brass tripod fixture!

The mandible of the cranium was locked into a tuning-fork mechanical resonator, while the coronal suture between the frontal and parietal bones was painstakingly micro-drilled with a dense matrix of numeric coordinates and waveform schematics.

"Bones again... Has this lunatic turned the entire municipality of Binjiang into his personal osteological gallery?!" Old Zhou leveled his service weapon, sweeping the shadows as his boots splashed across the concrete. "Where is he? Qin Mo isn't here!"

"He departed approximately twenty minutes ago," Pei Yi'an said, stepping forward to brush the aluminum heat sink of the fluorescent lamp. "Residual surface temperature sits around forty-two degrees Celsius, and the mud slurry within the footprints on the floor has yet to dehydrate and solidify."

His gaze shifted heavily onto the mounted skull, darkening with solemn gravity. "This does not belong to a recent victim. Judging by the obliteration of the sagittal suture, sphenoparietal synchondrosis fusion, and coronal occlusal wear on the molars, this belonged to an adult male aged approximately fifty-five. Post-mortem interval... exactly thirty years."

Old Zhou's breath hitched violently in his chest. "Fifty-five years old... thirty years ago... This skull... Could it be Dr. Lin Qingyuan?!"

"At the margin of the foramen magnum lies an eight-millimeter depressed comminuted fracture from blunt force trauma, exhibiting unmistakable vital ante-mortem hemorrhagic infiltration," Pei Yi'an said, snapping on latex gloves and probing the fissure with a sterile stylus. "This is the true cause of Lin Qingyuan's demise—he never died of an 'accidental drowning' in Dry Dock No. 2 as officially declared; he was bludgeoned from behind with a sledgehammer prior to immersion!"

"Thirty years ago... someone silenced Dr. Lin in the dry dock, staged it as a mishap, and forced the fourteen-year-old Qin Mo into thirty years of exile." Old Zhou's fists trembled with rage. "Qin Mo exhumed his mentor's cranium... What on earth does he intend to do with it?!"

"Look at the dentition," Pei Yi'an instructed, focusing his loupe as stark light flooded the enamel of the skull's maxillary first premolar and canine.

Etched into the diamond-hard enamel by cold-laser ablation was a minuscule string of frequency parameters: **【432 Hz - Resonance Override : 0x7F9A】**.

"432 Hertz..." Pei Yi'an murmured. "The standard concert pitch from the classical era of Mozart—and the intrinsic physical resonant frequency of this diploë cranial bone structure paired with the brass resonator."

"What does that mean? Little Pei, speak plain human! We only have forty minutes left!" Old Zhou pleaded, stamping his foot in agitation.

"Qin Mo deliberately stationed this cranium here as the 'ultimate fail-safe key' for the microwave detonator network," Pei Yi'an said, swiftly withdrawing a miniature tuning fork and frequency generator from his case. "He knew the authorities would eventually track him here. But across this entire city, only a pathologist trained to decipher micro-skeletal architecture could extract this matrix from his mentor's teeth to safely override the transmitter!"

"That bastard... he's compelling you from afar to perform Dr. Lin's final autopsy in his stead!" Old Zhou gasped in realization.

Pei Yi'an offered no response, his focus dialed to absolute precision as his fingers adjusted the rotary dial of the audio generator.

"432.05 Hertz... Phase angle 180 degrees..."

The instant the acoustic probe contacted the squama temporalis of the skull, the entire cranium and the brass resonator beneath hummed with crystal-clear sympathetic resonance!

*Beep—*

Mounted behind the cranium, the frantic crimson diode on the signal transceiver snapped off with a sharp relay click, stabilizing into a solid, soothing emerald green!

From above ground, Su Tang's voice burst through the earpiece, giddy with tearful relief: "Captain Zhou! It worked! The microwave repeater atop the clocktower has been forced into failsafe lock! The subterranean detonation trigger chain for the Grand Theatre is severed!"

Old Zhou released a prolonged breath, leaning limply against the damp brickwork. "Thank God... We saved those symphony-goers after all..."

"Not yet," Pei Yi'an said, his tone still cold as glacial ice.

He swept his tactical beam toward the service iron grate at the far end of the vault leading directly into the Grand Theatre's drainage sump.

The padlocks securing the gate had been sheared cleanly through by hydraulic bolt-cutters. On the flagstones trailed a fresh sequence of wet, muddy footprints, beside which lay a soggy, gold-embossed invitation—

**【Binjiang Grand Theatre · Tonight at 8:00 PM · Principal Violinist Solo Guest of Honor Reservation】**.

"He never intended to detonate the building from afar," Pei Yi'an remarked, his words sharp as a scalpel. "The microwave bomb was merely a decoy to tie down the police force. The true execution... he plans to walk onto that stage and deliver it in full view of the world!"

"Move!" Old Zhou racked his slide, a fierce glint burning in his eyes. "I want to see for myself what kind of tune this thirty-year-old ghost intends to play upon that stage tonight!"
