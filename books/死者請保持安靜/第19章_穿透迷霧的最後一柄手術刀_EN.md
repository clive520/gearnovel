# Chapter 19: The Final Scalpel Piercing the Mist

At 7:15 in the morning, West Apron Private Aviation Terminal, Binjiang International Airport.

Morning fog drifted like a white gossamer veil across the sprawling tarmac, where a silver-gray Gulfstream G650 screamed with the deep, throaty rumble of twin turbofans, its tail nozzles billowing visible heat mirages during final pre-flight checks.

At the foot of the airstairs, seventy-year-old former Deputy Mayor Song Huaide stood draped in an impeccably tailored charcoal cashmere topcoat, a cigar balanced in his left hand while his right clamped onto a titanium security briefcase.

Flanking his flanks, eight overseas private security contractors clad in dark suits and acoustic earpieces rested their palms upon underarm holsters, forming an impenetrable human shield against the ladder.

Yet less than twenty meters away in the dead center of the apron, a slender silhouette stood rooted like bamboo into the reinforced concrete, barring the boarding lane entirely alone.

Su Tang remained dressed in her navy technical duty jacket, cradling her ruggedized military laptop as her fingers flew across the keyboard in a dazzling blur.

"Little girl, do you truly imagine that by standing alone with a toy laptop, you can obstruct Song Huaide?" Song Huaide exhaled a coil of blue cigar smoke, his crow's feet creasing with the imperious malice of entrenched power. "Thirty years ago, your father Lin Qingyuan brandished crates of lab assays attempting to bring me down; today, his bones are still feeding river fish. Standing here accomplishes nothing beyond throwing your life away after his."

"I am not here to block your path," Su Tang raised her head, eyes bloodshot yet blazing with a righteous, incandescent fury like the noon sun. "I am here on behalf of all the dead of Binjiang—to see you to your final reckoning."

Clack!

Su Tang hammered the return key with resolute force!

In that fractional microsecond, across all terminal concourses, VIP departure lounges, air traffic control towers, and thousands of municipal outdoor LED billboards, all rolling flight manifests and commercial broadcasts were hijacked simultaneously!

In their place blazed Lin Qingyuan's original 1994 pathology manuscript bearing its top-secret institutional stamps, flanked by high-resolution scans of the wire transfer vouchers detailing Song Huaide's $3.2 million overseas bribery windfall!

Concurrently, through the airport's master public address system roared the final surviving voice recording left by Dr. Lin Qingyuan thirty years ago:

"The anti-neoplastic biologic Batch 9407 carries catastrophic genetic myelotoxicity—its five-to-ten-year mortality exceeds eighty percent! As a physician of pathology, I will never append my signature to this authorization! Song Huaide, you could pump the Yangtze River dry and never wash the blood from your hands!"

The entire international aerodrome fell into stunned, breathless petrification!

The veneer of aristocratic composure shattered from Song Huaide's visage, turning him ashen as a corpse.

"Kill her! Smash that machine to atoms!" Song Huaide shrieked, stabbing a trembling finger toward Su Tang.

Three contractors drew suppressed automatic pistols in unison, black muzzles centering squarely upon Su Tang's forehead!

In the breathless heartbeat before firing pins struck primers—

ROARRRRR——!!

The furious roar of an engine charged like an enraged bull, shattering the security perimeter fence!

Spewing mud and sporting a crumpled bumper, the aged Santana cruiser burst through the chain-link barrier at 120 kilometers per hour, laying down four smoking ribbons of scorched rubber as it executed a tactical drift, interposing its steel hull as an armored barricade between Su Tang and the gunfire!

Crack! Crack! Crack!

Three hollow-points ricocheted off the door plating, spitting brilliant sparks of fire!

"Police! Criminal Investigation Detachment! Drop your weapons!!" Old Zhou leaned out from behind the reinforced doorframe with his service weapon, his eyes as fierce as an enraged lion.

"Pathetic dying kicks!" Madness flared in Song Huaide's pupils as he shoved past his mercenaries, yanking a miniature solid-gold derringer from his coat and lunging toward the airstairs with a feral grin. "Once my jet rolls down the runway, what can you worthless flatfoots do to touch me?!"

The instant Song Huaide's boot touched the first tread of the ladder—

SHING!

A blinding streak of silver ripped through the morning mist, drawing a trajectory that defied human visual reflex!

It was a solid-silver No. 14 dissecting scalpel!

Revolving three times with mathematical symmetry, the razor-honed blade struck home with a concussive thud, pinning the margin of Song Huaide's right metacarpal alongside the briefcase handle dead into the solid aluminum handrail!

"AAARRRGGGHHH——!!"

Song Huaide howled like a slaughtered beast as arterial blood cascaded down the blade.

Hands tucked into his coat pockets, Pei Yi'an paced across the dewy apron from behind the cruiser, his gaze behind rimless spectacles as tranquil and cold as a pathologist surveying a post-mortem table.

"Superficial transfixion of the right second metacarpal margin, sparing both radial artery and ulnar nerve bundles," Pei Yi'an noted with surgical detachment. "Blood loss strictly regulated below ten milliliters, fully conforming to forensic biomechanical standards for suspect immobilization."

"You insolent whelp... I'll crush you!" Sweating profusely in agony, Song Huaide swung his left fist bearing the heavy gold signet ring, throwing every ounce of his weight directly at Pei Yi'an's face!

It was the exact lethal blow that had caved in Lin Qingyuan's skull at Dry Dock No. 2 thirty years ago!

Pei Yi'an did not so much as twitch a brow.

Three centimeters from his spectacles, Pei Yi'an's hand snapped forward like striking viper, fingers clamping like a steel vise over Song Huaide's radial styloid process!

Joint reversal lock!

CRACK!

With a sharp subluxation snap, Song Huaide's left arm was twisted behind his spine, forcing the former statesman to his knees upon the icy tarmac with an undignified splash!

Employing hemostatic forceps, Pei Yi'an clamped the golden signet ring upon Song's index finger, executing a deft twist.

The sweat-slicked ring peeled cleanly from the flesh.

"Solid gold alloy, inner bore twenty-two millimeters, its outer rim inscribed with the serrated crest of the Provincial Medical Council," Pei Yi'an hoisted the artifact into the morning glare, looking down upon the fallen dignitary. "Thirty years ago, the dent you stamped into the bone beside Dr. Lin Qingyuan's foramen magnum measured precisely four millimeters by seven."

"Song Huaide, thirty years ago you etched your confession upon human bone with this ring; today, this very token escorts you straight to the prisoner's dock."

In the near distance, a dozen SWAT armored carriers and tactical assault vans arrived in deafening thunder, hemming the Gulfstream within an impenetrable cordon. Ground controllers blocked the taxiway—Song Huaide's escape was extinguished forever!

Stiding forward with heavy, deliberate tread, Old Zhou slammed heavy steel handcuffs around Song Huaide's wrists.

"Deputy Mayor Song, everything you stole from Dr. Lin thirty years ago—today you cough back up to the very last cent," Old Zhou spat contemptuously onto the asphalt.

By the airstairs, Su Tang pulled her knees to her chest, the dam collapsing as tears poured unchecked down her cheeks in ragged sobs.

Thirty years of displacement, thirty years of stifling darkness, washed pure in this singular bath of dawn.

Walking over, Old Zhou unzipped his oversized gray fleece jacket, gently draping it over the young woman's trembling shoulders. From within his tactical vest, he drew forth his legendary pink thermos, unscrewing the cap and offering it forward.

"Little girl... you must be parched. Have some of Old Zhou's private-reserve Da Hong Pao," the wrinkles across the veteran detective's brow relaxed into tender warmth. "Thirty years... Your father's case is solved."

Cupping the warm flask with both hands as aromatic steam curled upward, Su Tang nodded with all her might.

The morning mist vanished entirely. A blazing sun crested the rolling currents of the Yangtze, gilding the skyline of Binjiang in triumphant scarlet and gold.

Wrenching the No. 14 scalpel from the aluminum ladder, Pei Yi'an cleansed the steel with sterile gauze, gazing upward into the immaculate eastern heavens.

The departed harbored no remaining regret; beneath the heavens, no restless ghost lingered in silence.
