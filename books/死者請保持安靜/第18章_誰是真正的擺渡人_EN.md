# Chapter 18: Who is the True Ferryman

At 6:15 in the morning, piercing sirens shredded the pale mist hovering above Binjiang Central Hospital.

The aged Santana cruiser fishtailed to an emergency halt before the trauma center. Old Zhou and Pei Yi'an kicked their doors open, sprinting into the dedicated medical elevator to charge up to the sixteenth-floor Cardiac Intensive Care Unit (ICU).

The moment the double swinging doors of the unit burst open, the glaring crimson resuscitation beacons were flashing frantically, while the telemetry monitor wailed in an unrelenting, piercing screech:

*BEEEEEEEEEEEEEP————*

On the screen, the waveform had flattened into an absolute, silent line of death!

"Ventricular fibrillation transitioned to full asystole! Shocks ineffective! Compressions failing!" the attending physician shouted hoarsely, sweat dripping from his chin as he gripped the defibrillator paddles. "Serum potassium spiked to 8.5 millimoles per liter—myocardial electrophysiology has collapsed completely!"

Upon the bed, the once-haughty billionaire Chu Tiancheng lay with eyes rolled back, skin cyanotic, limbs rigid as cast iron. In the drip chamber filter of his saline line clung faint, yellowish crystalline residues.

"Step aside. Let me through."

An icy, tranquil voice cut clean through the frantic medical team.

Pei Yi'an strode to the bedside, not even pausing to discard his lab coat, his right hand ripping away the poisoned IV line with practiced grace. His fingertips swept across the medication cart with breathtaking velocity:

"Ten percent calcium gluconate, twenty milliliters—slow IV push immediately to antagonize potassium cardiotoxicity at the cellular membrane!"

"Fifty percent dextrose, fifty milliliters with ten units regular insulin—establish secondary peripheral line to drive extracellular potassium back into intracellular compartments!"

"Five percent sodium bicarbonate, one hundred milliliters rapid infusion to correct metabolic acidosis!"

Overwhelmed by Pei Yi'an's supreme clinical authority, the two nurses responded instinctively, administering the emergency vials without a heartbeat of hesitation.

Snatching the defibrillator handles, Pei Yi'an dialed the rotary selector firmly to its maximum output: "360 Joules!"

"Conductive gel applied!"

"Clear the bed!" Pei Yi'an's eyes were sharp as razors, his forearms corded with muscle as he slammed the heavy paddles down upon Chu Tiancheng's infraclavicular fossa and apical axillary line!

"Charged—shocking!"

*THUMP!*

Under the tremendous galvanic discharge, Chu Tiancheng's torso vaulted inches off the mattress before crashing back down upon the sheets!

One second. Two seconds. Three seconds.

Across the monitor, four cycles of electrical noise fluttered in dead silence.

And then—

*Beep... beep... beep...*

Out of the flatline of oblivion leaped a towering, crisp QRS complex!

Sinus rhythm, fifty-eight beats per minute!

The attending physician slumped into a stool as all strength left his knees, staring at Pei Yi'an as if gazing upon a supernatural entity that had just wrestled a soul back from the underworld.

Upon the gurney, Chu Tiancheng convulsed, dry heaves scraping through his throat like a rusted bellows before his terrified, clouded eyes fluttered open.

What met his sight was no reaper's scythe, but Pei Yi'an's glacial features beneath the surgical lights, flanked by the dark muzzle of Old Zhou's service pistol.

"Chu Tiancheng," Old Zhou leaned in, his calloused fist twisting into the collar of the patient gown to hoist the tycoon upright. "A lethal bolus of potassium chloride. The bodyguard stationed outside took five million in hush money; we pinned him down in the stairwell five minutes ago. You served that old mongrel for thirty years, and he disposed of you like roadkill the second the heat turned on!"

Chu Tiancheng shook uncontrollably, tears, mucus, and cold sweat smearing his face, all the haughty dignity amassed across three decades of corporate dominion pulverized to dust.

"Song... Song Huaide..." Chu Tiancheng rasped, his voice grinding like broken glass. "That monster... he wanted me dead... wanted to silence me forever..."

"Where is Song Huaide right now?!" Old Zhou bellowed, veins standing out on his neck.

"Binjiang International Airport... West Apron Private Terminal..." Chu Tiancheng collapsed back against the pillow, gasping the truth in ragged spurts. "Eight o'clock this morning... he chartered a Gulfstream private jet to Switzerland... He took the original ledger listing every corrupt bureaucrat and kickback ledger in a security briefcase... The moment his jet leaves sovereign airspace... I am left as the sole scapegoat..."

Old Zhou snapped his wrist to check his watch—6:30 AM!

Barely ninety minutes remained before Song Huaide's private flight took to the skies!

"Alert Traffic Command immediately—impose total tactical gridlock across all highway arteries heading toward the airport! Dispatch SWAT to encircle the private apron!" Old Zhou barked into his shoulder mic.

Beside him, however, Pei Yi'an's brow knitted into a sharp furrow.

His gaze arrested upon the ruggedized tactical laptop resting upon the corridor bench.

Ever since they departed the Bureau's Evidence Center half an hour ago, that terminal—tasked with monitoring municipal surveillance feeds and spectrum telemetry—had ceased relaying any voice updates.

"Captain Zhou, where is Su Tang?" Pei Yi'an's voice dropped below zero.

"Little Su?" Old Zhou paused, instinctively patting his pocket for his phone. "Before we rolled out, the girl said she was staying behind in the digital lab to finish scanning the microfilms..."

Old Zhou speed-dialed Su Tang's private line.

What answered through the earpiece was the flat, synthesized operator recording:

*"The subscriber you have dialed is currently unreachable or out of service area."*

Snatching the laptop, Pei Yi'an's fingers blurred across the mechanical keyboard, punching out root-level decryption scripts.

In a flash, a top-secret clearance window from the Municipal Personnel Archives slammed onto the screen!

Centered in the dossier was a digitized neonatal birth certificate from Municipal Hospital dated 1994:

**[Infant Name: Lin Tang]**
**[Father: Lin Qingyuan (Chief of Pathology, Municipal Hospital)]**
**[Mother: Su Yuhua (Deceased July 1994 from post-partum complications)]**
**[Adoption Registry: Following Lin Qingyuan's murder in 1994, legally adopted into the Su sapper family and renamed—Su Tang!]**

Old Zhou's handset clattered onto the linoleum tiles, his mind turning completely blank as if struck by a lightning bolt:

"Little Su... Little Su is Lin Qingyuan's daughter?!"

"This completes the puzzle of 'The Ferryman,'" Pei Yi'an closed the laptop, his gaze as deep as a bottomless abyss. "Gu Zhiming provided the historical forensic evidence and legal leverage; Qin Mo forged the mechanical osteological traps to command police mobilization; and the master conductor orchestrating citywide telemetry, leading us step by step to exhume the conspiracy from within the system... was Lin Qingyuan's daughter, Su Tang."

"She's at the airport!" Old Zhou gasped in sudden panic. "She knows Song Huaide is fleeing! She never intended to let him step foot onto that aircraft alive!"

"She will not commit murder," Pei Yi'an said, hoisting his kit and striding into the elevator. "If murder were her goal, her cyber skills could have hacked Song Huaide's pacemaker weeks ago. What she demands is a public adjudication—absolute, undeniable justice beneath the eyes of the entire world."

"But Song Huaide is guarded by eight armed private security contractors!" Old Zhou roared as they sprinted. "That girl charging in alone is marching straight into a meat grinder!"

Morning sun pierced through the dissipating squall, painting the asphalt ribbon of the Binjiang Airport Expressway in blazing gold.

The black cruiser surged forward like an arrow released from a bow, hurtling through the morning fog toward the final battlefield.
