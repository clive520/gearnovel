# Chapter 17: The Pathology Manuscript from Thirty Years Ago

At 5:30 in the morning, the torrential deluge finally began to subside.

Pale, slate-gray dawn filtered through the blinds of the Forensic Identification Center, casting a hazy metallic luster across the stainless dissection tables and high-precision instruments.

Following a night of relentless exertion, the lines on Old Zhou's face looked as though gouged with an engraver's chisel, his conjunctiva veined with crimson spiderwebs. Perched upon a swivel stool beside the polarizing microscope, he cradled his faded pink thermos with both hands as curls of steam drifted lazily before his eyes.

"Young Li has regained consciousness from the sedation; the kid's got youth on his side, suffering nothing worse than a stiff neck," Old Zhou rasped, taking a sip of ginseng brew, his voice scratching like coarse sandpaper. "Old Gu is settled in the intensive care pavilion of the Police Hospital. Four investigators from the Discipline Commission and the Procuratorate are maintaining twenty-four-hour bedside vigil; his hemodynamics have finally stabilized."

"The hypercalcemic crisis precipitated by multiple myeloma has been temporarily quelled, yet his myocardial and renal reserves have suffered irreversible decline," Pei Yi'an remarked, pulling on a pristine white laboratory coat, his focus sharp and unwavering. "Gu Zhiming has merely weeks remaining. The manuscripts inside this case represent the final deposition he purchased with thirty years of private agony."

In the center of the forensic workbench sat the 1980s cowhide briefcase retrieved from the rooftop.

Inside, the eight-digit mechanical security container of solid titanium had already been breached by Su Tang utilizing an industrial borescope and acoustic resonance probes.

With a dull, heavy click, the two-centimeter-thick titanium alloy lid sprang gently ajar.

There was neither musty odor nor a single grain of dust.

The interior casing was hermetically lined with virgin PTFE film and purged with dry argon gas. Five dark brown dossier pouches, individually sealed within vacuum-evacuated acid-free Mylar sleeves, lay aligned in velvet cradles.

Donning powder-free nitrile gloves, Pei Yi'an wielded flat bamboo forceps to lift the top manuscript of yellowed parchment with exquisite care.

It was printed on the gridded examination leaves exclusive to the Municipal Hospital Pathology Institute thirty years ago, its header bearing the decommissioned emblem in indigo ink. And in the lower right margin of the title folio stood an assertive, elegant signature: **Lin Qingyuan**.

Date of signature: **July 17, 1994, at 1:40 AM**—precisely six hours prior to Lin Qingyuan's fatal plunge at Dry Dock No. 2!

"Thirty years ago, Chu Tiancheng told the world that the laboratory fire had incinerated every scrap of Dr. Lin's research," Old Zhou leaned forward, his eyes blazing. "To think Old Gu stashed the original manuscript away so deep!"

"This is no ordinary laboratory notebook; this is the final completed draft of Lin Qingyuan's 'Phase III Clinical Safety Assessment of Recombinant Anti-Neoplastic Polypeptide Biologic (Project 9407),'" Pei Yi'an laid the manuscript across the cold-light stage of the ultra-high-resolution multispectral scanner.

The scanner hummed quietly, a violet laser line passing smoothly down the aged page.

On the primary workstation monitor, three-decade-old carbon ink notations and complex molecular structural diagrams were magnified hundreds of times in razor-sharp clarity.

"Observe the cellular toxicology schematics on page thirty-eight," Pei Yi'an's finger traced the high-resolution display. "Lin Qingyuan explicitly demonstrated here that this purported 'miracle oncological remedy' spearheaded by Chu Tiancheng exhibited catastrophic long-term myelotoxicity in animal models and early compassionate-use cohorts."

"Myelotoxicity?!" Old Zhou's chest seized.

"It triggers mutagenic cascades within hematopoietic stem cells, culminating five to ten years post-administration in refractory myelodysplasia and diffuse osteolytic devastation," Pei Yi'an said, his voice hard as iron. "It was never a therapeutic salvation; it was a slow-acting mutagenic poison dressed in clinical laurels."

"Good heavens..." In the rear seat, Su Tang covered her mouth, all color draining from her cheeks. "Chu Bio-Pharmaceutical went public thirty years ago based entirely on the patents for that formulation! Thousands of cancer patients across the province were dosed with that rebranded, exorbitant compound!"

"Lin Qingyuan insisted on submitting an emergency injunction to the Ministry of Health, demanding the immediate impoundment and destruction of all manufacturing lines," Pei Yi'an opened the second sleeve, extracting a roll of 35-millimeter black-and-white photomicrographic film. "That conviction directly threatened the corrupt syndicate of capital and political influence standing behind Chu Tiancheng."

Pei Yi'an fed the strip into the transmission micro-scanner.

High-contrast macro-images materialized upon the displays—polarized cross-sections of the comminuted fractures encircling the foramen magnum of an adult cranium.

"Observe the concave curvature along the fracture margins," Pei Yi'an dialed the polarizing contrast. "Thirty years ago, even while under threat of immediate assassination, Gu Zhiming secretly captured macro-photographs of Lin Qingyuan's true occipital wound before signing the forged drowning certificate."

"This is a semicircular depressed fracture roughly 2.5 centimeters across, the base riddled with imbricated micro-fissures indicative of repetitive blunt impact," Pei Yi'an traced the perimeter on-screen. "Such a distinct geometry can only be produced by an octagonal solid-brass bone mallet standard to orthopedic surgery."

"Chu Tiancheng!" Old Zhou spat the name through clenched teeth.

"Not Chu Tiancheng alone," Pei Yi'an moved the cursor, zooming into a secondary, faint micro-fracture along the temporo-occipital boundary.

"Look here," Pei Yi'an's gaze sharpened. "Two centimeters lateral to the foramen magnum lies a second linear fissure! Imprinted upon the cortical bone is an anomalous indentation—four millimeters wide, seven millimeters long, its borders serrated with the crest of a crest-like insignia."

"A crest pattern?!" Old Zhou blinked. "What on earth left that mark?"

"It is the pathognomonic imprint left when a heavy blow is delivered with a fist bearing a signet ring," Pei Yi'an ran a 3D topological query against the Bureau's trauma biomechanics library. "At Dry Dock No. 2 that night, alongside Chu Tiancheng with his surgical mallet, stood a second perpetrator!"

"A man whose striking force fractured an adult calvarium, wearing a solid gold signet ring engraved with a specific familial or institutional seal!"

Old Zhou's pupils constricted into tiny dots, thirty-year-old memories surging like an ocean tempest!

"A signet ring..." Old Zhou's lips paled, veins bulging on his forehead. "Thirty years ago, the former Deputy Mayor who concurrently served as Honorary President of Municipal Hospital and regulated all regional pharmaceutical approvals—Song Huaide... He wore a heavy gold family signet ring on his left index finger, marking his lifetime directorship in the Provincial Medical Association!"

"Precisely him," Pei Yi'an slid open the third dossier sleeve, spilling forth a stack of brittle, yellowed interbank transfer slips!

Upon those expedited wire drafts dated July 18, 1994, the beneficiary line was inscribed in meticulous fountain-pen script: an overseas trust account registered under Song Huaide's immediate kin!

The sum: **$3.2 million USD!**

Thirty years ago, that was an astronomical fortune capable of purchasing half the deepwater terminals of Binjiang Port!

"Lin Qingyuan's demise was never a mere commercial assassination; it was a state-level silencing conspiracy orchestrated by a corrupt official, an unscrupulous venture capitalist, and complicit scientists," Pei Yi'an filed the folios into the containment case, his stare as unbending as frost. "Chu Tiancheng provided the muscle and capital, Song Huaide quashed the judicial inquiry, and Gu Zhiming was coerced into signing perjury."

At that exact second, the encrypted tactical transceiver on Old Zhou's duty belt shrieked with an ear-splitting alarm!

Old Zhou snatched the handset and slapped the speakerphone button.

From the receiver, the frantic, cracked voice of the Detachment Deputy Commander erupted through the lab:

"Captain Zhou! Disaster at Central Hospital ICU!"

"Chu Tiancheng went into acute respiratory arrest five minutes ago with blown pupils! Attending physicians found trace signatures of lethal potassium chloride spiked into his saline line!"

"Someone is attempting to liquidate Chu Tiancheng in the hospital... before the Procuratorate can depose him!"

Grabbing his service pistol and his pink thermos from the table, Old Zhou's eyes flared with cold fire:

"Song Huaide is making his move! He's severing the final living link!"

Pei Yi'an snapped the latches of his field kit shut, the silver case reflecting the cold morning sun:

"Captain Zhou, take the defibrillator and the potassium antagonists. The dead have concluded their testimonies; now it is our duty to preserve the respiration of the living!"
