# Chapter 16: The Rain-Night Denouement on the Archives Rooftop

Gales sweeping off the river lashed freezing rain like razor blades against the iron portal atop the twelfth floor of the Public Security Bureau's aged Archives Building.

9:15 in the evening.

Erected in 1982, the gray-brick repository had long been marked for demolition; its elevators had ceased operating half a decade ago, leaving the stairwells heavy with the sour stench of damp paper mold and pulverized concrete dust.

Hoisting his tactical searchlight, Old Zhou wheezed as he shoved open the final rusted iron door leading out to the rooftop, his combat boots splashing frantic sprays across the yellowed concrete puddles.

"Little Pei... slow down! My old knees... climbed a hundred and eighty steps today, my menisci are ground to dust!" Old Zhou swiped rain from his brow, his other hand fiercely guarding his heroic pink thermos inside his fleece pocket. "Are you certain the coordinates embedded in Chen Weiguo's ribs pointed straight to the lightning rod plinth on this damned archives tower?"

"The microchip utilizes early-generation military VHF transponder technology, with an operational detection radius under fifty meters," Pei Yi'an stepped across the threshold, his trench coat snapping violently in the gale. On the screen of his handheld RF spectrum analyzer, a crimson dot pulsed twice per second, locking into absolute convergence with the telecommunications tower ahead.

A whip of lightning tore through leaden clouds, drenching the rooftop in blinding white brilliance.

Beneath the angled steel telecommunications mast perched on the roof's edge, a tall, slender silhouette stood with his back turned, motionless amidst the torrential rain.

Draped in an oversized black storm mac whose hem whipped wildly in the gusts, the figure had a slight hunch, his left shoulder visibly higher than the right, leaning upon a custom orthopedic cane crafted of carbon fiber and pure titanium.

Beside his feet rested a vintage cowhide briefcase from the 1980s, its scuffed leather face still bearing the faded gold-stamped imprint: **[Binjiang Municipal Public Security Bureau · Criminalistics Division · Forensic Examiner Sole Issue]**.

"Thirty years, Old Zhou, and that wheezing of yours on the stairs hasn't changed a bit."

A gravelly, aged, yet profoundly familiar and gentle voice drifted through the screeching storm, reaching Old Zhou's ears.

Old Zhou's entire frame froze rigid on the spot, his flashlight nearly slipping from his grasp.

"That voice..." Old Zhou's pupils dilated in pure disbelief, his pitch rising in shock. "Old Gu?! Gu Zhiming?! How could it possibly be you?!"

The figure leaning on the titanium crutch turned slowly around.

A jagged flash illuminated his features—a gaunt face etched with deep, painful furrows, temples dusted with frost-white hair, bearing a pair of rimless gold spectacles strikingly similar to Pei Yi'an's own.

Gu Zhiming—former Chief of the Bureau's Forensic Pathology Division, Old Zhou's field partner when he joined the force thirty years ago, and Pei Yi'an's sponsoring mentor when he entered the Ministry of Justice's Forensic Research Institute seven years back!

Three years prior, he had retired on medical grounds due to advanced myelodysplastic syndrome, vanishing completely from the public eye.

"Teacher Gu," Pei Yi'an stopped, his countenance cool as frost, though his knuckles tightened almost imperceptibly at his side. "From the dual-axis hairspring micro-regulation slide on that bone bow at the Grand Theatre, to Chen Weiguo's articulated skeleton inside the thirteenth freezer... all the specimen preservation protocols and micro-metric dissection techniques originated from your doctoral patents thirty years ago."

"Little Pei, you are the finest student I ever mentored—surpassing both myself and Lin Qingyuan in our prime," Gu Zhiming took off his rain-flecked glasses, wiping them with a dry chamois cloth. "The dead do not lie; the bones record all. Those were the very first words I imparted to you when I brought you into the morgue."

"Then why did you become 'The Ferryman'?!" Old Zhou roared with bloodshot eyes, stepping forward with his gun trembling toward his former comrade. "When Dr. Lin died thirty years ago, your signature was on the autopsy report! You were the one who certified it as an 'accidental drowning'! You took Chu Tiancheng's blood money and climbed the bureaucratic ladder, and thirty years later you have the gall to orchestrate vigilante executions with human bones?!"

"Old Zhou, you truly believe I did it for promotions and gold?" Gu Zhiming's lips twisted into a tragic, self-mocking smile as he unbuttoned his trench coat.

Beneath his navy shirt sat an intricate orthopedic exoskeleton cage of medical-grade titanium, rigidly encasing his entire thoracic and lumbar spine.

"Look here, Little Pei. Perform an ante-mortem biopsy on your old teacher," Gu Zhiming pointed to his deformed spinal curvature, speaking as calmly as if presenting an anatomical specimen.

Pei Yi'an stepped forward, his eyes fixing on the spinal fixture spanning from T7 down to L2.

"Diffuse osteolytic lesions across the thoracolumbar vertebrae, punched-out cortical defects, accompanied by severe pathological compression fractures," Pei Yi'an stated the clinical verdict without flinching. "End-stage multiple myeloma with bone metastases invading spinal nerve roots. You have at most three months to live."

"Indeed, three months," Gu Zhiming replaced his spectacles, rain streaming down the glass. "Thirty years ago, Chu Tiancheng strode into my morgue flanked by ministerial directives, slamming a solid brass surgical mallet beside Lin Qingyuan's corpse. He told me that if I refused to sign that drowning certificate, my newborn daughter in her hospital incubator would 'suffocate in an unfortunate medical accident.'"

Old Zhou's gun hand convulsed, the weathered contours of his face twisting in visceral anguish.

"I capitulated. I signed my name. I played the coward and accomplice for thirty years!" Gu Zhiming's withered fingers clamped around his cane until his veins bulged. "Lin Qingyuan was my dearest friend; Chen Weiguo died attempting to secure evidence for him. For thirty years, every patent Chu Tiancheng usurped metastasized into cancer within my marrow! Every bone in my body aches every second—it is Lin and Chen crying out from the grave!"

"So you tracked down the exiled Qin Mo, took him as your disciple, and plotted this thirty-year retribution?" Pei Yi'an asked.

"Qin Mo was the scalpel; I was the one drafting the anatomical roadmap," Gu Zhiming bent down, lifting the vintage leather case and holding it toward Pei Yi'an. "Chu Tiancheng believed the original 1994 manuscripts perished in the fire. He never knew that a forensic pathologist will always preserve an encrypted microfilm negative for the departed."

Pei Yi'an accepted the briefcase, his fingertips meeting the cold, unyielding titanium security container within.

"Inside lie the complete polarizing micro-photographs of Lin Qingyuan's comminuted occipital fracture taken that fateful night, along with the original remittance vouchers Chu Tiancheng paid out to bribe thirteen corrupt experts," Gu Zhiming looked deep into Pei Yi'an's eyes, a solitary tear of release welling in his aged gaze. "Tonight's infrasound demonstration at the Grand Theatre was merely a gambit to draw the serpent from its hole. My ultimate goal was to force Chu Tiancheng's public ruin and hand this thirty-year deposition into the hands of the only forensic pathologist in Binjiang who never compromises."

Taking a deep breath, Old Zhou holstered his sidearm and stepped forward to grasp Gu Zhiming's sleeve: "Old Gu... now that you've passed the torch, come down with us. The Procuratorate and the anti-corruption task force are waiting below. The debts of thirty years ago—we will settle them together in a court of law!"

"A court of law?" Gu Zhiming shook his head, a resolute smile gracing his pale lips. "I am a medical examiner whose hands were drenched in lies. I hold no moral right to walk out of this structure as a witness."

Suddenly, Gu Zhiming's body jerked in violent spasm!

His respiration halted abruptly, his frame collapsing like a scaffold stripped of its supports, tipping backward toward the unguarded ledge!

"Old Gu!!" Old Zhou yelled at the top of his lungs, hurling himself forward to seize the lapels of the elder's coat!

"It is no suicide—it is malignant hypercalcemic arrhythmia triggered by multiple myeloma!" Pei Yi'an barked, dropping to his knees in the freezing rainwater and ripping open Gu Zhiming's shirt!

Chest compressions, airway clearance, carotid pulse check!

Locking his palms together, Pei Yi'an delivered rapid, rhythmic thrusts over the lower third of Gu Zhiming's sternum. Brittle bones creaked under the immense pressure, yet his technique showed neither hesitation nor retreat!

"Pei... Yi'an... let me go..." Gu Zhiming's dilating pupils flickered under the lightning, his dry lips parting feebly. "Let me... die in the same rain... where Lin Qingyuan perished..."

"Shut up!"

Continuing uninterrupted compressions, Pei Yi'an stared into the dying man's eyes, his words striking like tempered steel through the squall:

"A forensic pathologist's duty is to give voice to the deceased, not to stage martyrdom for sinners!"

"Thirty years ago you buried the truth beneath a lie; thirty years later, do not dream of escaping justice through the convenience of death!"

"Survive, Gu Zhiming! You must stand in that witness box with your own breath and deliver that thirty-year-overdue apology to Lin Qingyuan!"

*Cough—!*

A clot of stagnant blood trapped within the trachea erupted from Gu Zhiming's mouth!

Spurred by a resurgent pulse, the old pathologist's chest heaved violently, dragging in ragged, greedy draughts of freezing night air!

Slumping limp into the muddy wash, Old Zhou tilted his head back, letting the rain scour his bloodshot eyes before wiping his face and bellowing into his radio:

"Dispatch, this is Zhou Chenglin! Critical witness Gu Zhiming secured atop the Archives Building—request immediate paramedic and stretcher units! That thirty-year case... we just cracked it wide open!!"

The rain began to taper. Along the eastern horizon, a faint sliver of pale dawn pierced through the broken clouds, heralding the sunrise.
