# The Handmade Girl's Fantastic Journey

## Story 4: A Sky Drawn for Myself

There was a sixth-grade girl at Luyang Elementary named Liu Qianyun. She had drawn back the picture of her family gathered around the dining table for her father and mother; she had sent out that star she had been too afraid to give to Zhixing; and she had finished the painting Grandma had left unfinished. But in her own heart, there was always a small, empty corner. Rumor had it, whenever she finished a piece of work poured with genuine emotion, that work would carry her away to another world—and this time, she was going to her own sky.

In January, at Luyang Elementary, winter vacation was about to begin.

The branches of the phoenix tree swayed gently in the cold wind.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the students were excitedly discussing their plans for winter vacation.

"I'm going to Grandma's house for vacation!"

"I'm going to Kenting to see the sea!"

"I'm going to stay home and play video games!"

"Hahaha!"

Everyone was chatting happily.

Only Qianyun sat quietly in her corner by the window.

On her desk lay a half-finished drawing.

In the drawing, there was a sky.

A blue, blue sky.

Under the sky, there was only one small figure.

"Qianyun," A-Cheng walked over and patted her shoulder, "where are you going for winter vacation?"

"..." Qianyun looked up, dazed, "I..."

"I..." she lowered her head, "I'll probably... just stay home and draw."

"..." A-Cheng was startled for a moment.

"Oh..." she said, "well... that's nice too."

"..." She thought for a moment, then added, "So... do you want to come to the bookstore with us?"

"Bookstore?" Qianyun's eyes lit up, "What bookstore?"

"It's that new, really, really big bookstore!" A-Cheng said, "We're going on the first day of winter vacation! Zhixing is coming too!"

"..." Qianyun lowered her head, her hand gripping her pen a little tighter.

"I..." she hesitated for a long time, and finally said softly, "I... I'll see."

"..." A-Cheng looked at her and didn't say anything more.

"Okay." she said at last, "Then... if you want to go, just tell me!"

She turned around and went back to her seat.

"..." Qianyun watched her back, and her heart felt sour.

She actually... really wanted to go.

She really wanted to say: "Okay, I want to go."

But those words reached her lips and were swallowed back down.

Because she was afraid.

She was afraid that if she went, she wouldn't know what to say.

She was afraid that if she went, she would make everyone feel awkward.

She was afraid that if she went... she would spoil everyone's fun.

"..." She lowered her head and looked at the drawing on her desk.

In the drawing, there was only one person's sky.

"Anyway..." she said in a low voice, "I'm used to being alone."

"..." But she couldn't fool herself.

Because that night, she lay in bed, tossing and turning, unable to fall asleep.

"Everyone..." she looked at the ceiling, "has a place they can go."

"Everyone..." she said, "has people they can go with."

"Only me..." she said in a low voice, "only me..."

"..." She pulled the quilt up over her face.

"It's okay." she said under the quilt, "It's okay..."

"Anyway... I still have drawing."

"Drawing... never turns me away."

"..." One day, in the art classroom.

The teacher put everyone's previous works up on the bulletin board.

"Qianyun's drawing has such beautiful colors!" the teacher praised.

"..." The classmates gathered around to look.

"Wow... this cloud looks like it could really float!"

"Qianyun, how did you draw it?"

"..." Qianyun lowered her head.

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I just... drew it like this."

"..." She didn't know how to respond.

"..." She only knew how to draw.

"..." But how to talk with everyone, she had never learned.

"..." She watched everyone crowding around her drawing, lively and noisy.

"..." She stood outside the crowd.

"..." She said in a low voice, "Drawings... can be liked by people."

"But..." she said softly, "I... don't seem to be."

The next day, in art class.

Teacher said: "Today, let's draw 'feelings.'"

"Feelings?" The classmates looked at the teacher, puzzled.

"Yes." Teacher said, "Draw how you've been feeling lately."

"Whether it's happy, sad, or lonely... you can draw it."

"..." Qianyun listened, and something stirred in her heart.

"Lonely..." she repeated softly, "can I draw that too?"

"..." She picked up her pen.

She thought for a moment and drew a small sketch.

A sky.

A blue, blue sky.

A cloud.

On the cloud sat a small girl.

Only her, alone.

"..." Qianyun looked at the sketch for a very, very long time.

"This..." she said in a low voice, "is how I feel."

"..." She thought for a moment, then tucked the sketch into her sketchbook.

"But..." she shook her head, "this kind of drawing... can't be shown to anyone."

"This kind of feeling..." she lowered her head, "can't be said either."

"Even if I said it..." she said softly, "probably no one would understand."

"..." Before art class ended, the classmates took turns going up to share their drawings.

"I drew... my cat!" someone said.

"I drew... a birthday party!" someone said.

"I drew... going out to play with friends!" someone said.

"..." Everyone's drawings were colorful and lively.

"..." Only Qianyun hugged her sketchbook to her chest and didn't go up.

"Qianyun, what about you?" the teacher asked.

"..." Qianyun lowered her head.

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I haven't finished yet."

"..." The teacher looked at her and didn't push.

"Okay." The teacher said gently, "Then... take your time."

"..." Qianyun kept her head down and said nothing.

"..." She looked at the sketch of "a sky with only one person" inside her sketchbook.

"..." She gently closed the sketchbook.

"..." What she didn't say was—her feeling was loneliness.

"Loneliness..." she said in a low voice, "how do you draw that?"

"..." She lowered her head.

"..." She couldn't draw it.

"..." Or rather, she had drawn it, but didn't dare show anyone.

"..." She looked up at the window.

The sky outside was so, so blue.

So blue... that it felt a little lonely.

That day after school, Qianyun sat alone on the bleachers by the playground.

"..." She watched the classmates, in twos and threes, laughing and running.

Zhixing was there too.

Zhixing was playing tag with a few girls on the playground.

"Zhixing!" a girl called out, "Your turn to be the tagger!"

"Coming, coming!" Zhixing laughed and ran over.

"..." Qianyun watched Zhixing, and her heart felt complicated.

"Zhixing..." she said in a low voice, "she seems to... have a lot of friends now."

"That's... good."

"..." Just then, Zhixing suddenly turned around.

"Qianyun!" She waved toward the bleachers, "Do you want to play too?"

"..." Qianyun froze.

"I..." her heart started thumping, thumping.

"I..." she opened her mouth.

"I..." in the end, she said in a low voice, "I... I'm drawing."

"..." She looked down at the blank piece of paper in her hand.

"..." Zhixing's movement paused for a moment.

"Oh... okay." she said, "Then... take your time drawing!"

"..." She turned around and ran back to the other classmates.

"..." Qianyun watched her back.

"..." She lowered her head and looked at her empty drawing paper.

"..." Her eyes reddened.

"I clearly..." she said softly in her heart, "I clearly... wanted to play too."

"But..." she said in a low voice, "I said it again."

"I said it again..." she choked, "'I'm drawing.'"

"..." The wind blew coolly.

Only she sat alone on the bleachers.

"..." She lowered her head and looked at her own hands.

"..." There was dried paint on her fingers.

"..." She gently touched her own fingers.

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I drew back my father and mother's family."

"I sent out Zhixing's star."

"I finished Grandma's painting."

"..." She paused.

"But..." she asked, "who... has come to look at my heart?"

"..." The wind blew coolly.

No one answered.

Only she sat alone on the bleachers.

"..." She lowered her head, her eyes red.

"It's okay." she told herself, "It's okay..."

"I can... be fine alone too."

"..." But she knew she couldn't fool herself.

Because that night, she had a dream.

In the dream, there was a huge, huge sky.

Blue, blue.

Empty, empty.

Under the sky, there was only her.

"..." In the dream, she stood under that sky.

"So big..." she said, "so empty..."

"..." She looked up at the sky.

"Here..." she said in a low voice, "is just like me."

"..." When she woke from the dream, a small corner of her pillow was wet.

"..." She sat up and stared blankly for a long time.

"..." She looked out the window at the sky.

The sky was gray and gloomy.

"..." She said in a low voice, "Even the sky... is gray."

"..." She looked again at the unfinished sketch on her desk.

"..." In the sketch, that little girl sitting on the cloud.

"..." She gently touched her.

"..." She suddenly felt that the little girl was so pitiful.

"..." Because she was just like her.

"..." She was alone too.

"..." She took a deep breath.

"..." She told herself: "It's okay."

"..." But the moment she said it, she found that her own voice was trembling a little.

"..." In the end, she said in a low voice, "I... I'll draw a picture."

"A picture..." she said, "of a sky with only me in it."

"I'll draw my heart..." she said softly, "into it."

That night, Qianyun sat at her desk and spread out a fresh piece of paper.

She picked up her pen.

"The sky..." she drew a blue, blue sky.

"The cloud..." she drew a soft, soft cloud.

"..." She hesitated for a moment, and in the end, she drew that small figure after all.

"One person..." she said in a low voice, "sitting on the cloud."

"..." She finished and looked at the drawing.

"..." She suddenly felt that her heart had become a little lighter.

"It's like..." she said in a low voice, "I've taken something out of my heart."

"..." She gently touched the little person in the drawing.

"You..." she said, "are you me?"

"..." The little person in the drawing didn't answer.

She just sat quietly on the cloud.

"..." Qianyun looked at that drawing for a very, very long time.

"This drawing..." she said in a low voice, "I'll call it 'A Sky with Only One Person.'"

"..." She put down her pen.

But she didn't turn off the light.

Because she suddenly... didn't want to leave that drawing.

"..." She lay down on the desk, looking at that drawing.

"If I could..." she said in a low voice, "I'd go there too."

"There..." she said, "there's only me."

"No misunderstandings."

"No loneliness."

"No..." she said softly, "words I can't say."

"..." She looked at that drawing, and her eyes slowly blurred.

"I want to go..." she murmured, "I want to go..."

At that very moment—

"Woom—!"

A warm, pale golden light suddenly surged up from the drawing paper!

"This..." Qianyun's eyes went wide.

The light gently wrapped around her.

"I..." she said, dazed, "I finished it?"

"..." She lowered her head and looked at that drawing.

"I finished it." She smiled softly.

"I..." she said, "drew my heart into it."

"So..."

"Swish—!"

The whole world spun before her eyes.

When she came back to her senses—

She found herself standing—

Under a sky.

A huge, huge sky.

The sky was a deep, layered blue.

So blue, like a whole piece of washed silk.

The wind blew gently.

The wind carried the smell of clouds.

"Here..." Qianyun looked around, stunned, "is the sky I drew."

"..." She looked down at herself.

She was still wearing her school uniform.

Beneath her feet, she was standing on a soft white cloud.

"So soft..." she stepped gently.

"..." She looked up at the sky.

"So big..." she said, "so blue..."

"..." She reached out, as if to touch the sky.

"..." She suddenly smiled.

"Here..." she said softly, "is so quiet."

"..." She closed her eyes.

"No misunderstandings." she said.

"No loneliness." she said.

"No..." she said softly, "words I can't say."

"..." She opened her eyes and looked at this stretch of sky.

"I want..." she said in a low voice, "to stay here."

"Staying here..." she said, "doesn't seem so bad."

"..." She sat on the cloud, swinging her feet.

"Here..." she murmured, "there's only me."

"..." The wind blew gently.

"..." She suddenly felt that her heart had become... a little calm.

"..." She lay down on the cloud, looking at the sky.

"If I could..." she said in a low voice, "just stay here forever, that would be nice."

"..." The cloud was soft.

The sky was blue.

The wind was cool.

"..." She closed her eyes.

"I wish..." she said softly, "I could just stay here like this, forever."

"..." Just then—

"What are you doing here?"

A voice suddenly sounded beside her ear.

"!" Qianyun was startled and sat up.

"Who is it?" She turned around.

Behind her, sitting there without her noticing, was a girl.

A girl who looked exactly like her.

The same hair.

The same school uniform.

The same, quiet eyes.

"You..." Qianyun froze, "you are..."

"Me?" The girl smiled softly, "I'm the little person you drew."

"..." Qianyun looked down at her own hands.

"The little person I drew..." she said in a low voice, "?"

"Mhm." The girl said, "The little person you drew, sitting on the cloud."

"..." Qianyun looked at the girl, stunned.

"You..." she asked, "why do you look exactly like me?"

"..." The girl didn't answer right away.

She looked at Qianyun and smiled softly.

"Because..." she said, "I am you."

"You are me?" Qianyun was stunned.

"Mhm." The girl said, "I am 'the self in your drawing.'"

"The self... in my drawing?" Qianyun repeated.

"..." The girl looked at her and asked softly: "Why... did you draw me?"

"I..." Qianyun lowered her head, "I..."

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I just... felt lonely."

"Lonely?" the girl asked.

"Mhm." Qianyun nodded, "I'm often... alone."

"Eating alone."

"Drawing alone."

"Alone..." she said in a low voice, "sitting on the bleachers, watching others play."

"..." The girl listened quietly.

"I..." Qianyun went on, "it's not that I don't have friends."

"I drew back my father and mother's home."

"I sent out Zhixing's star."

"I finished Grandma's painting."

"But..." she lowered her head, "I myself..."

"I myself..." she choked, "still seem to be alone."

"..." The girl looked at her quietly.

"When you helped them..." the girl asked softly, "did you feel lonely in your heart?"

"..." Qianyun froze.

"I..." she thought for a moment, "when I helped my father and mother... and saw them smile, I... I was happy too."

"When I helped Zhixing... and saw her smile, I... I was happy too."

"When I helped Grandma... and saw her smile, I..." she lowered her head, "I was happy too."

"..." The girl looked at her and smiled softly.

"Then..." the girl asked, "why do you still feel lonely?"

"..." Qianyun was silent.

"Because..." she said in a low voice, "after they're done being happy... I still have to go home alone."

"I still..." she choked, "sit alone at my desk and draw."

"..." The girl sighed softly.

"Qianyun." She called her name.

"..." Qianyun looked up at her.

"The sky you drew..." the girl said, "has it always had only you in it?"

"..." Qianyun nodded, "Mhm."

"Then..." the girl asked, "have you ever thought about drawing that cloud a little bigger?"

"A little bigger?" Qianyun was stunned.

"Mhm." The girl said, "Draw the cloud bigger... big enough for two people to sit on."

"..." Qianyun froze.

"..." She lowered her head and looked at her own hands.

"Two people..." she said in a low voice, "but I..."

"I..." she choked, "I don't know... who would want to sit on the same cloud as me."

"..." The girl looked at her quietly.

"Qianyun." she said softly, "Do you know?"

"I've been here..." she said, "waiting for you for a very, very long time."

"Waiting for me?" Qianyun was stunned.

"Mhm." The girl said, "Every time you sat alone on the bleachers."

"Every time you swallowed your words back down."

"Every time..." the girl said softly, "you said 'I'm used to being alone.'"

"I..." the girl said, "was here, watching you."

"..." Qianyun's eyes reddened.

"You..." she choked, "you knew all of it?"

"I knew all of it." The girl said, "Because... I am you."

"..." Qianyun lowered her head, and tears fell.

"I..." she cried, "am I... really useless?"

"I clearly..." she choked, "can draw."

"I clearly..." she cried, "helped so many people."

"But..." she lowered her head, "I can't even say the words 'I want to go too.'"

"..." The girl shook her head gently.

"You're not useless." the girl said.

"You're just..." she said softly, "too used to being alone."

"..." Qianyun froze.

"Used to... being alone?" she repeated.

"Mhm." The girl said, "You're used to eating alone."

"Used to drawing alone."

"Used to..." she said softly, "keeping all your words inside your heart."

"You thought..." the girl looked at her, "that way, you wouldn't get hurt."

"..." Qianyun lowered her head.

"..." She was silent for a long time.

"But..." she said in a low voice, "I'm still alone."

"I..." she choked, "I still... feel lonely."

"..." The girl stood up gently.

She walked over to Qianyun, crouched down, and looked at her.

"Qianyun." she said gently, "Loneliness... is not your fault."

"..." Qianyun looked up at her.

"Loneliness..." the girl said softly, "just means 'no one has walked in yet.'"

"No one has... walked in yet?" Qianyun repeated.

"Mhm." The girl said, "Your heart isn't empty."

"You've just..." she said gently, "closed the door."

"Because you're afraid." she said, "You're afraid that if you open the door, you'll be rejected."

"You're afraid..." she said softly, "that if you open the door, you'll get hurt."

"..." Qianyun's eyes reddened again.

"I'm afraid..." she cried, "I'm so afraid..."

"I'm afraid..." she said, "that when I open the door, there won't be anyone outside."

"I'm afraid..." she cried, "that when I reach out my hand, no one will take it."

"I'm afraid..." she lowered her head, "that I'll be alone again."

"..." The girl looked at her quietly.

"You're not alone." the girl said.

"..." Qianyun froze.

"Think about it..." the girl said softly, "didn't A-Cheng invite you to the bookstore?"

"Zhixing..." she said, "hasn't she always kept that jar of stars on your desk?"

"Your father and mother..." she said, "don't they have dinner with you every Friday?"

"Grandma..." she said, "doesn't she ride her bicycle every Wednesday to pick you up?"

"..." Qianyun listened, frozen.

"They..." she said in a low voice, "they..."

"They..." the girl said softly, with a smile, "have always been there."

"It's you..." she said gently, "who hasn't been looking."

"..." Qianyun's tears couldn't stop flowing.

"I..." she cried, "I haven't been looking?"

"..." The girl gently took her hand.

"Qianyun." she said, "You helped your father and mother mend their home."

"You helped Zhixing mend her heart."

"You helped Grandma mend her memories."

"But..." the girl said gently, "do you know?"

"Those hearts..." she said softly, "are actually your heart too."

"..." Qianyun froze.

"Every heart you mended..." the girl said, "has a trace of you in it."

"Your father and mother's dining table..." she said softly, "has a place for you."

"Zhixing's jar of stars..." she said softly, "has your star in it."

"Grandma's painting..." she said softly, "has the person you drew."

"..." Qianyun listened, tears streaming down.

"I..." she choked, "I didn't know..."

"I..." she cried, "I only felt... that I was lonely..."

"I know." The girl said softly, wiping away her tears.

"Loneliness..." she said, "is real."

"But..." she said gently, "being loved... is also real."

"..." Qianyun looked up at her.

"Being loved..." she repeated.

"Mhm." The girl nodded, "You are loved."

"You are loved by your father and mother."

"You are loved by Zhixing."

"You are loved by Grandma."

"You are loved by A-Cheng..." the girl said softly, "too."

"..." The girl gently raised her hand.

"Look." she said.

"..." Qianyun followed her hand and looked.

In the sky, a scene suddenly appeared.

In the scene, her father and mother sat at the dining table.

On the table were steaming hot dishes.

Dad said: "It's Friday today! Qianyun is coming home for dinner!"

"..." The scene flickered.

Another scene appeared.

In the scene, Zhixing sat at her desk.

She was folding paper stars.

The jar was already full of them.

"Qianyun..." Zhixing said in a low voice, "this jar of stars... is for you."

"..." The scene flickered again.

Another scene appeared.

In the scene, Grandma stood by the wall.

She was looking at the painting on the wall.

"A-Tian..." Grandma said softly, "look... our house is so pretty."

"..." Qianyun watched those scenes, her tears unstoppable.

"These..." she choked, "these are all..."

"Mhm." The girl said softly, "These are all scenes of 'being loved.'"

"..." Qianyun lowered her head and cried.

"I..." she choked, "I seem to... have never looked."

"..." The girl gently wiped away her tears.

"It's okay." she said gently, "It's not too late to see them now."

"..." Qianyun's eyes were red.

"But..." she choked, "I don't know how to..."

"How to..." she lowered her head, "let them know that I want..."

"I want..." she cried, "to be with them."

"..." The girl smiled softly.

"Then..." she said gently, "just say it."

"Say it..." Qianyun froze, "?"

"Mhm." The girl said, "Just say... 'I want to come too.'"

"..." Qianyun opened her mouth.

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I want..."

"..." She paused.

"I want to come too." she said softly, saying it out loud.

"..." The girl smiled.

"You see." she said, "Isn't it simple?"

"..." Qianyun looked at her blankly.

"..." She suddenly smiled too.

Tears were still flowing.

But the corners of her mouth were smiling.

"..." Just then, the sky suddenly changed.

The blue, blue sky slowly took on a light pink tint.

"This is..." Qianyun froze.

"The sun... is about to rise." the girl said softly.

"The sun?" Qianyun asked.

"Mhm." The girl said, "This sky... is your heart."

"Your heart..." she said gently, "has never been all night."

"..." Qianyun looked at the sky.

A pink sky.

So beautiful, so beautiful.

"..." She suddenly understood.

"This sky..." she said in a low voice, "is the one I drew."

"I drew my heart..." she said softly, "into it."

"So..." she looked at the sky, "in this sky... there is my loneliness."

"And also..." she smiled, "my hope."

"..." The girl looked at her quietly.

"Qianyun." she called her name gently.

"..." Qianyun turned around and looked at her.

"It's about time." the girl said.

"Time?" Qianyun froze.

"Mhm." The girl said, "You should go back."

"Go back?" Qianyun froze, "But... I..."

"I want..." she said in a low voice, "to stay here."

"Staying here..." she said, "here there's only me."

"..." The girl looked at her quietly.

"Qianyun." she asked softly, "do you really... want to stay here?"

"..." Qianyun was silent.

"Here..." she said in a low voice, "is so quiet."

"No misunderstandings." she said.

"No..." she choked, "words I can't say."

"..." The girl sighed softly.

"Qianyun." she said, "If you stay here..."

"this sky..." she said gently, "will be a sky with only you, forever."

"Forever..." she said softly, "a sky with only one person."

"..." Qianyun lowered her head and looked at her own hands.

"..." She suddenly felt that this sky... had become a little colder.

"..." She looked up.

The blue, blue sky slowly took on a layer of gray.

The wind grew stronger too.

"This is..." Qianyun froze, "what's happening?"

"..." The girl stood beside her and said softly: "This is 'the sky with only one person, forever.'"

"..." The wind howled.

The clouds began to turn gray too.

"..." Qianyun shrank her shoulders.

"..." She suddenly felt that this sky... was not warm at all anymore.

"So this..." she said in a low voice, "when you're alone... for long enough, even the sky grows cold."

"..." The girl sighed softly.

"Qianyun." she said, "Do you know now?"

"Know what?" Qianyun asked.

"Escaping..." the girl said softly, "won't make the sky turn blue."

"Only..." she said gently, "opening the door of your heart and letting the sunshine in will make the sky bright again."

"..." Qianyun listened quietly.

"..." She looked up at the gray sky.

"..." She gently closed her eyes.

"..." She suddenly remembered the warmth of the sunshine.

"..." She remembered Friday dinners.

"..." She remembered Zhixing's smile.

"..." She remembered Grandma's painting.

"..." She remembered A-Cheng's words: "If you want to go, just tell me!"

"..." She opened her eyes.

"..." She found that the sky was slowly brightening again.

"..." The gray clouds scattered.

"..." The sunshine poured down once more.

"..." Qianyun looked at the brightening sky, her eyes red.

"So this..." she said in a low voice, "my heart... has always known the answer."

"..." Qianyun's heart suddenly ached.

"Forever..." she repeated, "a sky with only one person..."

"..." She lowered her head and looked at her own hands.

"..." She suddenly remembered many scenes.

Her father and mother, sitting around the dining table, smiling.

Zhixing, holding the jar of stars to her chest, smiling.

Grandma, looking at the painting on the wall, smiling.

A-Cheng, behind her, saying: "If you want to go, just tell me!"

"..." Qianyun's eyes reddened.

"I..." she said in a low voice, "I think... I don't want to be alone forever anymore."

"..." The girl smiled softly.

"Then..." she said gently, "go back then."

"Go back..." the girl said softly, "and draw the cloud a little bigger."

"Draw the cloud..." she said, "big enough for so many people to sit on."

"..." Qianyun looked at her blankly.

"Big enough... for so many people?" she repeated.

"Mhm." The girl nodded.

"Your sky..." she said gently, "can hold the whole world."

"..." Qianyun's eyes reddened again.

"But..." she choked, "how do I draw it?"

"I don't know..." she cried, "how to draw them... into my sky."

"..." The girl smiled softly.

"You don't need to draw them." she said.

"You just need..." she said gently, "to open your door."

"..." Qianyun froze.

"Open... my door?" she said in a low voice.

"Mhm." The girl said, "Open the door, and let the sunshine in."

"Let the wind in." she said softly.

"Let..." she said gently, "the people who want to come in, walk in."

"..." Qianyun looked at her, tears streaming.

"I..." she choked, "can I do it?"

"Of course you can." The girl smiled.

"Because..." she said gently, "you've already drawn this sky."

"You've already..." she said softly, "turned your loneliness into a sky."

"..." Qianyun lowered her head and looked at her own hands.

"Turning loneliness..." she repeated, "into a sky..."

"..." She suddenly remembered something.

"I've drawn..." she said in a low voice, "my father and mother's home."

"I've drawn..." she said, "Zhixing's star."
