# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 12: The Night of Total Defeat at the Final Exam

On the day of the final exam, dawn's thin mist shrouded Luyang Elementary.

In the classroom of Class 6-1, A-Xin stood on his desk, before him an "operation master list" covered in symbols, declaring passionately to all the members of the Mutual Aid Society:

"Everyone! The final exam is our last battle! Today, we use every weapon we've developed this whole semester, all at once!"

He pointed at the master list and called out the items one by one:

"Weapon No. 1—the Miniature Library! Present!"
"Weapon No. 2—the Aerial Delivery System! Present!"
"Weapon No. 3—the Code System! Present!"
"Weapon No. 4—the Body-Temperature Development Desk Pad! Present!"
"Weapon No. 5—the Ultraviolet Cheat Sheet! Present!"
"Weapon No. 6—the Cheating Calculator! Present!"

"..." Qingqing sat in the first row, silently watching this "operation master list," unable to help speaking: "A-Xin, have you counted how many cheating tools you've developed this semester?"

"Let me count..." A-Xin bent his fingers. "Cheat sheets, paper planes, rubber bands, codes, the human puzzle, invisible ink, ultraviolet, the calculator, radio... hmm... about... twenty-eight?"

"Twenty-eight." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "Then do you think Teacher Gao doesn't know?"

"He doesn't know!" A-Xin said firmly. "As long as we hide them well!"

"..." Qingqing was silent for a second. "...Fine. I wish you—'victory on the very first move.'"

"You jinx! This time we'll definitely win!"

The last battle meeting before the results were posted was held in the equipment room. Qingqing lined up all twenty-eight cheating tools neatly on the steel table, like an arsenal.

"Everyone!" A-Xin stepped up onto the steel table, pointing at that table full of "weapons," his tone sonorous and forceful. "The final exam is our 'battle day'! Everyone! Take all weapons and charge!"

"But A-Xin," Old Wu weakly raised his hand, "if we carry so much stuff... won't it be too heavy?"

"Heavy, because the weapons are many!" A-Xin said with full justification. "Many weapons means we're strong!"

"..." Liu-Liu lay on the windowsill, looking at the table full of "armaments," a trace of worry flashing in its electronic eyes. "Beep beep... according to historical data... the teams with 'more weapons'... 'fall' faster..."

"Liu-Liu! You shut up!" A-Xin shouted. "You're a 'study assistant' now, so you can't help us cheat, but don't curse us either!"

"Beep..." Liu-Liu shrunk its tail aggrievedly.

On the day of the final exam, the exam room of Class 6-1 officially opened battle.

The test paper was handed out. A-Xin took a deep breath and pressed the "start button" in his heart:

"Everyone! Begin! Wave one—the body-temperature development desk pad!"

He quietly pressed his palm onto the pad—

"Slap."

Teacher Gao Zhixian's figure had somehow already appeared at A-Xin's desk. He lowered his head, glanced at the pad, and said flatly:

"Lu Lixin, this desk pad contains 'lemon juice invisible ink' that develops when heated by body temperature. This is a 'heat-sensing cheating tool'—item one, confiscated."

"—!!" A-Xin froze on the spot.

"Item two." Teacher Gao turned and walked to Lin Yunyun's seat. "Lin, the mini ultraviolet flashlight in your pencil case, combined with the ultraviolet ink on the desk pad, can reveal 'invisible answers.' This kind of 'money-checking-grade' cheating tool—"

He reached out and deftly pocketed the flashlight: "confiscated."

"Ow..." Lin Yunyun's eyes reddened in fright.

"Item three." Teacher Gao walked to Old Wu. "Mr. Wu, that 'cheating calculator' of yours displays formulas when you press XYZ. I suggest changing it to press the 'book' key to display the textbook content."

"The 'book' key?" Old Wu froze. "What key is that?"

"The 'read-it-yourself' key." Teacher Gao smiled faintly, pocketing the calculator. "Confiscated."

"My calculator!" Old Wu wailed.

"Item four." Teacher Gao walked to Wang Zhuankai. "The paper hidden under your shoe insole, that's the social studies key points. This is already the third time this semester you've hidden key points in your shoe."

"T-Three times, all caught?" Wang Zhuankai stared in terror.

"Because your insole is always the left one." Teacher Gao added. "Next time, remember to switch to the right."

"Pfft!" Someone in the class couldn't help laughing, then quickly covered their mouth.

"Item five. Item six. Item seven..."

Teacher Gao, like a precise scanner, walked a circle through the classroom, naming one student after another, confiscating one item after another.

The formulas in the erasers, the paper in the pencil-case compartments, the slips in the correction tape, the cheat sheets on the fingernails, the invisible pens under the desk pads, the ruler markings disguised as scales...

All twenty-eight cheating tools, within fifteen short minutes, were collected by Teacher Gao into the big cardboard box on the podium.

"...Item thirty." Teacher Gao walked up to Liu-Liu, looking down at the little pet shrunk into a ball. "Liu-Liu, what did you put in the pod around your neck this time?"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu shook its head frantically. "I, I'm the study assistant! I didn't bring anything!"

"Then why..." Teacher Gao paused, "is there a piece of paper full of answers stuck to your back?"

"—!!" Liu-Liu looked down and discovered that, at some point, a sheet of answer paper had been stuck to its back!

"It's A-Xin!" Liu-Liu turned to A-Xin in terror.

"I, I didn't! I didn't!" A-Xin shook his head desperately. "It was Old Wu!"

"Old Wu!" Liu-Liu turned to Old Wu.

"I didn't! It was Wang Zhuankai!" Old Wu pointed at Wang Zhuankai in a panic.

"Don't blame me!" Wang Zhuankai jumped up. "It was Lin Yunyun!"

"Not me! Not me!" Lin Yunyun was almost crying from fright.

"..." Teacher Gao watched this "blame-shifting" drama and sighed helplessly. "...Never mind. I'll take this paper. Consider it—item thirty."

He turned and walked to the podium, announcing flatly:

"Everyone, this semester's 'cheating tools' have all been accounted for today. A total of—twenty-eight items."

"Twenty-eight items..." A-Xin repeated the number to himself.

"Now," Teacher Gao picked up the exam paper, "please complete the final exam using your 'own' abilities."

"...Using ourselves..." A-Xin stared blankly at his own empty hands, then at the cardboard box full of "weapons," a huge wave of despair flooding over him.

"We... lost..." A-Xin muttered.

"A-Xin!" Old Wu cried with a sobbing voice. "My drumsticks! My drumsticks are gone!"

"My game console..." A-Xin was almost crying too.

"My allowance..." Lin Yunyun sniffled.

"My after-school study pass..." Wang Zhuankai clutched his head.

"..." A-Xin looked at this group of classmates crying into a mess, then at the blank exam paper, and finally, he took a deep breath and picked up his pen:

"...Fine. Since cheating lost... then we'll... write with ourselves."

All twenty-six students silently picked up their pens and began to answer.

The exam room was left with only the rustling of pen tips across paper.

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, watching this scene, its tail swaying gently:

"Beep beep... detecting... 'honesty' energy... rising..."

On the day the results were posted, the central courtyard of Luyang Elementary was packed solid.

A-Xin, Old Wu, and Qingqing squeezed to the front, twenty-six pairs of eyes fixed on the bright red results board.

"Found it!" Sharp-eyed Qingqing reached out, pointing at the Class 6-1 column. "Look!"

"..." A-Xin leaned in to look, his pupils instantly dilating. "The w-whole class average... eighty-two points?!"

"Eighty-two?!" Old Wu screamed. "Our whole class averages eighty-two?!"

"Last monthly exam, our average was seventy-three." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "This final exam—another nine points better."

"Better... again?" A-Xin couldn't believe it. "Weren't we... caught at every cheat plan?"

"Our 'cheating tools' were all caught," Qingqing said calmly, "but the things we 'learned while making the tools'—we remembered all of them."

"..."

A-Xin froze.

He lowered his head, looked at the results board, then raised it to look at the group of classmates cheering and jumping—Old Wu hopping, Wang Zhuankai shouting, Lin Yunyun clutching her results sheet and crying her heart out.

"I... I scored ninety in science!" Old Wu shouted excitedly. "A-Xin! I scored ninety in science! I can eat a month of drumsticks!"

"I... I passed math!" Wang Zhuankai waved his results sheet excitedly. "My first time passing math! And it's eighty-seven!"

"I, me too!" Lin Yunyun said through tears. "I scored eighty! I've never scored eighty!"

"..." A-Xin watched this scene, and slowly, slowly, lowered his head.

"A-Xin," Qingqing walked to his side, her tone unusually gentle, yet unusually heavy, "have you noticed something?"

"...What?" A-Xin's voice was a little hoarse.

"This semester, we developed a total of twenty-eight cheating tools." Qingqing said. "Every single tool made us read the textbook one more time."

"..."

"Every time we 'prepared to cheat,' our grades improved once." Qingqing continued. "We thought we were cheating—but actually, we were studying all along."

"..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing looked at him, a hint of indefinable complexity in her eyes, "we seem to... not need to cheat at all."

"—!"

Those words, like a heavy hammer, slammed into A-Xin's heart.

"Im, impossible..." A-Xin's voice trembled. "We... we clearly..."

"Clearly what?" Qingqing asked.

"Clearly..." A-Xin opened his mouth, but found himself with no rebuttal at all.

"Waaaah!" Old Wu was the first to break, covering his ears with both hands. "I don't want to hear it, I don't want to hear it! We cheated! We definitely cheated! Otherwise how could we score so well!"

"Right! It must be cheating!" Wang Zhuankai echoed. "It's just that we don't even know how we cheated!"

"..." Qingqing watched this group of "dead-ducks-with-hard-beaks" classmates and sighed helplessly. "...Fine. You all be happy then."

"Then... then..." A-Xin stood frozen, and only after a long while slowly spoke, "then... our next 'cheating plan'..."

"Do we continue?" Qingqing asked.

"..." A-Xin fell silent.

He raised his head, looked at the bright red results board under the setting sun, looked at the group of classmates crying and laughing, and finally, he took a deep breath and clenched his fist:

"...Yes! Of course we continue! Since twenty-eight tools can be confiscated, then we'll—"

He paused, fresh fighting spirit blazing in his eyes:

"—directly use 'the human body'! Plan No. 8: The Ultimate Human Puzzle! Twenty-six people, each memorizing one unit. At the final exam review session, we'll memorize all the 'answers'!"

"Memorize them?" The whole class exchanged glances.

"Yes!" A-Xin said firmly. "Since tools can be confiscated, we'll write the answers directly 'into our brains'! Teacher Gao can't confiscate our brains, right?"

"..." Qingqing looked at A-Xin, silent for a long time, and finally smiled softly. "...A-Xin, you've finally found a cheating method that 'can't be confiscated.'"

"Of course!" A-Xin said smugly. "I'm the strongest cheat strategist!"

Under the setting sun, the classroom of Class 6-1 was filled with laughter.

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, watching this group of "incorrigible" trouble-makers, its tail swaying gently, letting out a tender electronic chirp:

"Beep beep... detecting... a 'no-need-to-cheat' future... getting closer and closer..."
