# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 15: The Teacher's Open-Book Exam

On the first day of the final exam, dawn's thin mist shrouded Luyang Elementary with an air of menace.

In the classroom of Class 6-1, twenty-six desks had been pulled into the standard exam layout, the whole class sitting ramrod straight, only pencil cases and desk pads on the desks.

—Of course, hidden in the pencil cases, under the desk pads, on the erasers, and in the shoe insoles, all sorts of "insurance" were still stashed.

"Everyone!" A-Xin seized the moment while Teacher Gao was sorting the exam papers, hissing the final orders to the whole class. "Although yesterday we 'seemed' to discover that we all know how to write, 'just in case,' we still bring the tools!"

"Right! Better safe than sorry!" Old Wu nodded nervously, patting the formula-covered eraser in his pocket.

"Don't rush to use them today!" A-Xin cautioned. "Wait until you see the questions, then decide whether to make a move!"

"Roger!" The whole class whispered back.

Just then, Teacher Gao Zhixian raised his head, pushed up his black-rimmed glasses, swept his gaze across the class, and said flatly:

"Everyone, before the final exam officially begins, I want to announce something."

The whole class instantly fell silent.

"In this final exam," Teacher Gao paused, "all cheating tools are open. Welcome, everyone—to cheat."

"—!!!"

All twenty-six faces instantly froze.

The air congealed for a full three seconds.

"Open, open cheating?" Old Wu's mouth hung in an "O," the pork bun rolling out of his mouth.

"This is a trap!" A-Xin was the first to react. "He must be fishing! Trying to lure us into the hook!"

"Right! It's definitely a trap!" Wang Zhuankai echoed. "What teacher opens cheating?!"

"..." Qingqing pushed up her glasses, analyzing in a low voice. "Logically, there are two possibilities: first, he's lying, trying to lure us into acting, then catching us. Second—"

"What's the second?" A-Xin asked.

"Second," Qingqing paused, "he really means it."

"Impossible!" A-Xin said firmly. "How could a teacher really allow cheating?!"

"Then..." Qingqing added calmly, "why don't you try it?"

"Try it?" A-Xin froze.

"If he's lying, you'll be caught if you try." Qingqing said. "If he means it, you'll be fine if you try."

"..." A-Xin was silent for two seconds, then, a glint of cunning suddenly flashed in his eyes. "...Then we'll—'turn the tables on him'!"

"Turn the tables?" The whole class exchanged glances.

"Teacher Gao has 'literal interpretation syndrome'!" A-Xin hissed excitedly. "He takes words literally! So, we'll exploit that—"

He took a deep breath: "The whole class shouts together: 'We want to cheat!' Teacher Gao will then, literally, 'agree' to us cheating! Then cheating won't count as violating the rules!"

"Wow—!" Old Wu marveled. "Isn't that going around the rules?!"

"Right!" A-Xin said smugly. "This is—'exploiting a loophole'!"

"..." Qingqing was silent for two seconds. "A-Xin, are you sure you want to use 'literal interpretation' to challenge a teacher who 'interprets literally'?"

"Of course!" A-Xin said with full confidence. "He interprets literally, and we exploit literally! This is 'paying him back in his own coin'!"

"..." Qingqing sighed. "...Fine. I wish you—'victory on the very first move.'"

"You jinx again!"

A-Xin stood up, took a deep breath, and shouted at Teacher Gao on the podium:

"Teacher! Our whole class—wants to cheat right now!"

"—!!" The whole class held their breath at once.

Teacher Gao raised his head, looked at A-Xin, was silent for a second, then said flatly:

"Fine."

"—!" A-Xin rejoiced. "The teacher agreed! The teacher agreed! We can cheat!"

"...!" The whole class stirred.

"But," Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, "according to Ministry of Education exam room regulations, Article Thirty-four: 'If, during the exam, a candidate declares an intention to cheat, the proctor shall immediately record it and handle it according to the rules.'"

He pulled a small booklet from his pocket and flipped to a page: "Therefore, Lu Lixin's declaration just now—has been recorded."

"R-Recorded?!" A-Xin's smile instantly froze.

"And according to Article Thirty-five: 'If the whole group collectively declares an intention to cheat, the entire class's results shall be voided, and a uniform retake will be held during the winter or summer break.'" Teacher Gao paused. "Twenty-six people, collectively declaring—this should be the first time in this school's history."

"—!!!" The whole class exploded!

"Whole class results voided?!" Old Wu was scared out of his wits. "Retake during the break?!"

"My drumsticks!" Wang Zhuankai wailed.

"My summer vacation!" Lin Yunyun screamed.

"I-We were joking!" A-Xin turned pale with fright and shouted hastily. "We don't want to cheat! We were joking!"

"Joking?" Teacher Gao raised an eyebrow. "According to the dictionary definition, 'joking' means 'teasing someone with words or actions.' So—was declaring an intention to cheat just now teasing?"

"Yes! It was teasing! It was teasing!" A-Xin nodded repeatedly.

"Since it was teasing," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "then it doesn't count as cheating. Please begin answering, everyone."

"..." A-Xin let out a sigh of relief, but somehow felt something was off.

"A-Xin!" Qingqing whispered. "Just now, you wanted to turn the tables on the teacher with literal interpretation—and instead, he turned the tables on you."

"I..." A-Xin opened his mouth, but found himself with nothing to say.

He grumbled back to his seat, picked up his pen, and looked down at the exam paper.

"Hmph... if you won't let me cheat, I'll write by myself!" he muttered defiantly. "I could write everything yesterday at the rehearsal!"

He picked up his pen and began answering.

Question one, Taiwan geography—he could write it.

Question two, photosynthesis—he could write it.

Question three, circle area—he could write it.

The more he wrote, the smoother it went, faster and faster. Before he knew it, he'd written the entire exam paper in one go.

"...Huh?" A-Xin froze, looking down at his fully written exam paper, murmuring. "I... I really... can write all of it?"

"Of course." Teacher Gao had somehow already walked to his side, his tone flat. "You memorized for a week and listened for a week. How could you not know how to write?"

"..." A-Xin raised his head, looking at Teacher Gao. "Teacher, you... you know everything?"

"I know what you memorized," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "and I also know you rehearsed until very late yesterday."

"Then... then why..." A-Xin stammered, "did you still open cheating?"

"Because I wanted you to discover for yourselves—" Teacher Gao crouched down, leveling his gaze with A-Xin's eyes, "you've long since stopped needing to cheat."

"—!"

Those words, like a bolt of lightning, struck into A-Xin's heart.

He opened his mouth, yet couldn't produce a single word.

"Bang!"

The classroom door was pushed open again, and Director Zhou Yan barged in triumphantly, waving his notebook:

"I heard it! I heard it! Someone just shouted 'We want to cheat'! The whole class! Collective cheating!"

He scanned the room, only to see all twenty-six students, each with their head bowed, writing furiously, papers densely filled, so quiet that a pin dropping could be heard.

"Huh?" Director Zhou froze. "You..."

"Director Zhou," Teacher Gao stood up, his face calm, "our class just conducted a 'honesty declaration test'—the students collectively declared 'we want to cheat,' which is actually a test of whether they can uphold honest answering under the temptation of 'open cheating.'"

"An, honesty declaration test?" Director Zhou stared wide-eyed.

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "This is a 'character education' activity promoted by the Ministry of Education."

"..." Director Zhou scanned the room half-suspiciously, and finally, his gaze landed on Old Wu. "Then the eraser... in your hand..."

"That's my talisman!" Old Wu shoved the eraser into his mouth in fright. "I, I'm not cheating!"

"..." Director Zhou's mouth twitched, and finally, he grudgingly snapped his notebook shut and stormed out.

"You... you'd better pray there's no cheating tomorrow!"

After Director Zhou left, the collection bell rang.

All twenty-six students handed in twenty-six fully written exam papers.

"Teacher!" Old Wu raised his hand excitedly. "I, I wrote everything!"

"Me too!" Wang Zhuankai echoed. "I even wrote out the questions I didn't know!"

"Very good." Teacher Gao gathered the papers and smiled faintly. "Tomorrow there's still science and social studies. Please—continue to 'open cheating.'"

"—!" The whole class wailed in chorus.

"Teacher!" A-Xin shouted. "Can we... not open it tomorrow?"

"Why?" Teacher Gao asked.

"Because..." A-Xin paused, his voice low, "open cheating... is scarier than not opening..."

"..." Teacher Gao looked at him, the corners of his mouth curving upward. "Then tomorrow, we'll—'open' it."

"Teacher!" The whole class wailed in chorus.

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, watching this group of "trouble-makers who, in an open-cheating exam room, all answered honestly," its tail swaying gently, letting out a tender electronic chirp:

"Beep beep... detecting... 'honesty index'... breaking a historic high..."
