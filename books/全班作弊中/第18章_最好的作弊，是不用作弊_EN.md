# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 18: The Best Way to Cheat Is Not to Cheat

In June, the flamboyant blossoms of the flame trees were at their brightest.

On the campus of Luyang Elementary, red flowers blazed like fire, scattering along the entire path to the school gate. Class 6-1's final class of the semester ended on a quiet afternoon.

"Everyone!" A-Xin stood on the podium, for the first time, not standing on the desk. "Let's take the 'Honesty Museum'—to its burial."

"Burial?!" The whole class cried out.

"Yes!" A-Xin nodded, a kind of indefinable gentleness in his tone. "These cheating tools have been with us for a whole year. Now that we don't need them anymore—let them rest."

"..." Old Wu lowered his head, looking at the eraser worn smooth by sweat in his hand, his eyes reddening. "A-Xin... I'm a little... reluctant to part with it..."

"Reluctant?" A-Xin laughed. "Then keep it."

"Really?!" Old Wu's eyes lit up.

"..." A-Xin sighed. "...You'd better bury it anyway. I'm afraid that on the next exam, you won't be able to resist taking it out again."

"..." Old Wu silently put the eraser back.

And so, on a tranquil afternoon, all twenty-six students marched in force toward the old flame tree in the corner of the campus.

Beneath the tree was a deep pit dug in advance. Beside the pit sat an iron box.

"Inside here," A-Xin opened the iron box, "are the twenty-eight cheating tools we developed this entire year."

The whole class gathered in a circle, quietly watching that iron box.

"I'll go first!" Old Wu was the first to step forward, solemnly placing the eraser into the iron box. "Eraser... goodbye."

"Me too!" Wang Zhuankai took out the paper that had been hidden in his shoe insole and placed it in the iron box. "Insole paper... goodbye."

"And my desk pad!" Lin Yunyun gently placed it in.

"My calculator!" "My flashlight!" "My radio!" "My data cable!"

One by one, all twenty-eight cheating tools were placed into the iron box.

"..." A-Xin looked at that full iron box, was silent for a second, then spoke, his voice light as a feather:

"Everyone... we spent a whole year, trying to figure out how to cheat..."

"..." The whole class fell silent.

"And in the end..." A-Xin paused, "we accidentally... finished reading all the books..."

"—!"

Those words, like a gentle breeze, brushed softly across everyone's heart.

"So," A-Xin lowered his head and laughed softly, "the best way to cheat—"

He raised his head, his gaze gentle and resolute:

"—is not to cheat."

"..." The whole class was silent for three seconds.

Then—

"A-Xin!" Old Wu was the first to pounce, hugging him, laughing and crying. "You've finally come around!"

"I came around long ago!" A-Xin shouted. "It's you who kept insisting on cheating!"

"Then why did you follow every plan!" Old Wu retorted.

"Because I'm the strategist!"

"This strategist of yours got our whole army wiped out eighteen times!"

"Those were the finest eighteen times!"

"..." The whole class squabbled noisily, dissolving into laughter.

"Bang!"

Just then, the sound of steady footsteps came from behind the flame tree.

"...What are you doing?"

Director Zhou Yan had, at some point, already standing in the tree's shadow. His gaze fell on the twenty-eight cheating tools in the iron box, and his face slowly darkened.

"D-Director!" A-Xin shivered. "We... we..."

"I saw everything." Director Zhou Yan stepped forward, his voice low. "Ever since the 'gifted-student note exhibition,' I felt something was off."

"..." The whole class held their breath.

"I investigated your entire year's records," Director Zhou Yan pulled a stack of documents from his pocket, "Plan No. 1, Plan No. 2... all the way to Plan No. 8. You trouble-makers—you've been cheating all year."

"—!!!" The whole class froze on the spot.

"Director!" A-Xin hastily spoke. "We... we didn't..."

"Didn't?" Director Zhou Yan raised the infraction notebook in his hand. "According to school rules, collective cheating means the whole class gets written up. None of you twenty-six can run."

"..." The whole class lowered their heads, the atmosphere dropping to freezing point in an instant.

"But—" Director Zhou Yan paused, his voice suddenly becoming a little hoarse. "after I finished investigating the records, I discovered... that none of your eighteen cheating methods... was ever actually used."

"—!" The whole class raised their heads.

"Your cheat sheets were textbook key-point organization." Director Zhou Yan said. "Your delivery system was teamwork. Your codes were memory methods. Your 'cheating'—"

He paused, his voice even hoarser:

"—you trouble-makers weren't cheating at all. You were learning."

"..." The whole class froze.

"Final exam, class average eighty-six points." Director Zhou Yan lowered his head, looking at the documents in his hand, his voice trembling slightly. "Top one hundred in school, fourteen students. Top three in the grade, first and third place both yours."

He raised his head, his eyes red:

"...You... clearly... passed all by yourselves..."

"..." A-Xin's eyes instantly welled up.

"So," Director Zhou Yan slowly "slapped" the infraction notebook shut, "this time... I won't write up any violations."

"—!" The whole class's eyes went wide in an instant.

"Director!" A-Xin's voice trembled. "You... you're not recording us?"

"Not recording." Director Zhou Yan turned his head away, his voice hoarse. "Because... you no longer need to be 'written up.'"

He paused, then added: "...You trouble-makers are the finest class I, Zhou Yan, have ever taught."

"—!!!"

The whole class exploded.

"Director!" Old Wu was the first to pounce, hugging Director Zhou. "You're the best director in the world!"

"L-Let go! Don't hug!" Director Zhou Yan scrambled. "I'm the director! I must maintain authority!"

"Authority, my foot!" The whole class surged forward, crowding around Director Zhou, laughing and crying.

"..." Director Zhou Yan, squeezed in the middle, secretly turned his head and quietly wiped the corner of his eye.

"Ahem, ahem!" He cleared his throat and put on a stern face. "Alright, alright! Aren't you going to bury that box of 'cheating tools'?! Leaving it here—do you want the younger students to see it?!"

"Yes, Director!" The whole class shouted in unison, then, with all hands, placed the iron box into the deep pit, covered it with soil, and buried it securely.

"..." A-Xin stood before the pit, looking at the freshly turned earth, was silent for a second, then pulled a piece of chalk from his pocket and wrote four large characters on the trunk of the flame tree:

"Honesty Time Capsule"

"Done!" He clapped his hands and stepped back two paces, looking at those characters. "This is the 'gift' our Class 6-1 leaves for the younger students."

"..."

"Teacher," A-Xin turned to Teacher Gao Zhixian, who stood to the side, still smiling, "we'll never cheat again."

"..." Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, his tone flat. "'Cheating'—according to the dictionary definition, means 'obtaining benefits by deception.'"

"Right!" A-Xin nodded. "So we won't—"

"—" Teacher Gao cut him off. "And what you call 'cheating' now means 'finishing reading the books.'"

"..." A-Xin froze.

"'Finishing reading the books' isn't deception—it's learning." Teacher Gao smiled faintly. "So, when you all 'cheat' in the future—I fully support it."

"—!" The whole class froze for a moment, then burst into laughter.

"Teacher!" Old Wu laughed until tears came. "You're 'interpreting literally' again!"

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "This is my—'favorite' quirk."

"..." A-Xin looked at Teacher Gao, his eyes reddening slightly. "Teacher... thank you..."

"No need to thank me." Teacher Gao shook his head, his tone gentle. "I was only—watching you grow up."

"..." The whole class was silent for a second, then—

"Teacher Gao—! We love you—!" The twenty-six shouted in unison.

"...Ahem." Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, concealing the smile he couldn't hide at the corners of his mouth. "...Please return to the classroom, everyone."

"Why?"

"Because," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "summer vacation is about to begin. And one of the summer homework questions is—'read next semester's textbooks first.'"

"—!" The whole class wailed in chorus.

"Teacher! Didn't you say you support us when we cheat?!" A-Xin shouted.

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "But 'summer homework' is mandated by the Ministry of Education."

"..." The whole class was at a loss whether to laugh or cry.

The sun set in the west, the flame tree blossoms blazing like fire.

The twenty-six of Class 6-1 walked along the road home, squabbling noisily, laughter scattering all the way.

"A-Xin," Old Wu suddenly spoke, "in the future, will we really never cheat on exams?"

"Never." A-Xin said, his tone firmer than ever before. "From now on, every exam, we'll 'cheat'—"

He turned his head, looking at Old Wu, and said with a smile:

"using the dumbest method—'finishing reading the books.'"

"..." Old Wu froze for a moment, then smiled too. "...Fine. Then I'll 'cheat' too!"

"Me too!" Wang Zhuankai shouted.

"Me too!" Lin Yunyun echoed.

"Then..." A-Xin smiled, reaching out his hand, "let's make a pact!"

Twenty-six little hands, beneath the flame tree blossoms, stacked together.

"Pact—!"

On the windowsill, Liu-Liu lay there, watching this group of trouble-makers who had "finally learned not to cheat," its tail swaying gently, a tender light flickering in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep... detecting... 'the finest cheating'... has been completed..."

"Beep." It added softly, "—this is the meaning of learning."

The flame tree blossoms drifted down silently.

Beneath the tree, in the "Honesty Time Capsule," the twenty-eight cheating tools slept quietly.

And the story of Class 6-1 had only just begun.
