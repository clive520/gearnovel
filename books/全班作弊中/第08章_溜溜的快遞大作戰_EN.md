# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 8: Liu-Liu's Delivery Mission

"Liu-Liu! The class needs you!"

In the equipment room after school, A-Xin pressed his palms together, begging the AI pet sitting on the windowsill with its tail turned to the whole class:

"This mock exam before the final exam is the first live battle of our 'Cheating Technology Revolution'! And we need—a courier!"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu didn't even turn its head, tail sticking straight up, its tone resolute: "I refuse! There's still a big 'X' on that 'behavior observation log' that hasn't been cleared!"

"I'll go plead with the director for you!" A-Xin vowed, patting his chest.

"You can't even plead for yourself." Liu-Liu shot back coldly, contempt filling its electronic eyes.

"You!" A-Xin was choked speechless.

"Liu-Liu," Qingqing stepped forward, pulling a silver-white can from her backpack and waving it in front of Liu-Liu, "top-grade space lubricant, the newest model. Mission accomplished, and the whole can is yours."

Liu-Liu's ears "whooshed" up, its electronic eyes blazing like two little suns, but it still forced composure:

"Beep... just, just one can of lubricant, and you think you can buy me off?"

"Plus—" Qingqing added, "the class-wide 'Best Courier' honorary medal."

Liu-Liu's tail began wagging uncontrollably. The little pet spun twice on the spot, and at last couldn't hold out any longer—it lunged forward, hugged the lubricant can tight, and let out an excited electronic chirp:

"Beep beep beep! Deal! Liu-Liu Airlines, honorary re-launch!"

"Excellent!" A-Xin rejoiced. "Then let's drill the route!"

And so, "Liu-Liu Airlines: Mission Two" got under way in full swing in the equipment room.

Qingqing stuffed the answer-stuffed paper slips into the specially made "pod" around Liu-Liu's neck, then unfolded the "delivery route map" she'd drawn all night:

"This mock exam, we need to deliver twenty-five answer sheets. Route plan: depart from the ventilation duct, then pass the windowsill, the desk fan, the blackboard, the back of the podium, and finally loop back around to the ceiling."

"Why such a big loop?" Old Wu asked.

"Because Teacher Gao's line of sight has blind spots." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "The back of the podium, the ceiling ventilation duct, and the side of the windowsill are the three blind zones in his scanning. As long as we follow this route, we can avoid his eyes."

"Professional!" A-Xin gave a thumbs up. "Then—live-fire drill!"

Liu-Liu clutched the pod in its mouth, flipped over, and dove into the ceiling ventilation duct, its silver-white form darting through the pipes at lightning speed.

"First target! Wang Zhuankai's seat!" A-Xin hissed. "Airdrop!"

"Whoosh!" Liu-Liu poked its head out of the vent and precisely released the pod; the paper slip fluttered gently down onto Wang Zhuankai's desk.

"Second target! Lin Yunyun!" A-Xin directed.

"Whoosh!" Another precise airdrop.

"Third target! Old Wu!"

Liu-Liu swooped down, the pod landing steadily right next to Old Wu's pork bun. Old Wu was moved to tears: "Liu-Liu! You're the most reliable courier I've ever seen!"

"Beep!" Liu-Liu flicked its tail smugly.

"Success! Success! All success!" A-Xin jumped up excitedly. "Twenty-five sheets, zero errors! Liu-Liu, you're a born courier!"

Liu-Liu lay on the ceiling, its tail wagging like a helicopter rotor, letting out a long electronic squeal of triumph:

"Beep beep beep beep—! Liu-Liu Airlines, guaranteed mission complete!"

"Glad to hear it." Qingqing looked at the route map and added a note of caution: "However, I suggest that tomorrow's mock exam, Liu-Liu's 'flight count' should not exceed three."

"Why?" A-Xin asked.

"Because," Qingqing pushed up her glasses, "any behavior that shows 'regularity' will draw Teacher Gao's attention."

The next day, the school-wide mock exam before the final exam arrived right on schedule.

As the test papers were handed out, A-Xin sat in the first row and secretly signaled to the ceiling: "Liu-Liu, move out!"

In the ventilation duct, Liu-Liu's silver-white form, like a phantom, slipped silently across the ceiling along the pre-planned route.

First airdrop, success.

Second airdrop, success.

Third airdrop—just as Liu-Liu was about to swoop toward Old Wu's seat—

"Liu-Liu."

Teacher Gao Zhixian's voice came down from the podium, flat, yet carrying a chill that made one's hair stand on end:

"Your flight trajectory, from the start of the exam until now, has passed two vent openings, three windowsills, and two desk fans, with the route completely identical, not a single deviation."

Liu-Liu froze midair, four little paws dangling, not daring to move.

"According to my 'pet behavior anomaly detection system,'" Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, "the movement trajectory of a normal pet should follow a 'random exploration' distribution. But this route of yours—"

He picked up the chalk and drew a perfect arc on the blackboard:

"—is a 'designed flight path.'"

The whole class's heads "whooshed" up at once, twenty-six pairs of eyes all fixed on the ceiling.

Liu-Liu hung suspended midair, frozen for three seconds, and then—

"Beep?" Liu-Liu suddenly flipped over, showing its snow-white belly, its four paws paddling the air, letting out the cutest, most innocent electronic chirp: "Beep beep! Beep beep beep! I, I'm just practicing flying!"

"Practicing flying?" Teacher Gao raised an eyebrow. "Then why does the pod around your neck contain twenty-five paper slips with answers written on them?"

"—!!" Liu-Liu's electronic eyes instantly went wide as saucers, its belly "whooshed" back over, and the whole pet hung stiff in midair like a frozen statue.

"Because..." Liu-Liu's voice began to tremble. "Because... I'm... a courier..."

"A courier?" Teacher Gao stepped off the podium, reaching out a hand. "Then what are you delivering?"

Liu-Liu took the pod off its neck and, trembling, handed it to Teacher Gao.

Teacher Gao opened the pod, pulled out a stack of answer slips, and flipped through them one by one, his tone flat:

"Chinese Lesson 2 new-word annotations... Math Unit 3 formulas... Science 'photosynthesis' key points... Social Studies Unit 1 place names..."

"Exquisite key-point organization." he praised. "If these slips were posted on the bulletin board as review materials, they'd be very useful."

"Beep?" Liu-Liu was taken aback.

"However," Teacher Gao folded the slips and put them in his pocket, "passing answers during an exam is a serious violation. Liu-Liu, I'll take custody of the pod around your neck for now. This will be recorded on your 'behavior observation log'—now your log has two big X's."

Liu-Liu's tail drooped limply, the whole pet curling into a ball of silver-white fur, letting out a plaintive electronic whimper:

"Beep... my lubricant... my medal... all gone..."

"Liu-Liu!" A-Xin jumped to his feet. "Teacher! Liu-Liu was only... only forced by us!"

"Forced?" Teacher Gao turned to A-Xin. "Then who wrote these answer slips?"

"..." A-Xin's mouth instantly shut.

"Lin Yunqing." Teacher Gao's gaze fell on Qingqing in the first row. "The handwriting on these twenty-five slips is exactly the same as your 'Miniature Library' handwriting from last time."

Qingqing pushed up her glasses, her expression unchanged: "Reporting to the teacher. I only make them. I don't use them."

"Very good." Teacher Gao nodded. "Then we'll talk about the 'making' part after class."

"Bang!"

The classroom door was pushed open again, and Director Zhou Yan barged in triumphantly, waving his notebook:

"I saw it! I saw it! Just now, some white thing was flying around the ceiling! It must be a cheating device!"

He looked up—the ceiling was completely empty.

"Huh? Where'd it go?" Director Zhou froze.

"Director Zhou," Teacher Gao said calmly, "what was flying just now was our class's collective pet 'Liu-Liu.' It was conducting a 'health education lesson—campus animal behavior observation.'"

"Ani-Animal behavior observation?" Director Zhou stared wide-eyed.

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "Liu-Liu is the only smart pet in the school that can roam the vents freely. We're studying its flight path as material for the science class."

"Fli-Flight path..." Director Zhou squinted suspiciously at the ceiling. "Then why did I feel like it was flying really regularly?"

"Because Liu-Liu has been trained," Teacher Gao said without changing his expression, "its flight route is especially tidy. That's its 'specialty.'"

"..." Director Zhou opened his mouth, and could only grudgingly snap his notebook shut and storm out.

"Hmph! You'd better pray I don't catch you on the final exam!"

After Director Zhou left, the whole class slumped limply into their chairs.

Teacher Gao neatly posted the stack of "answer slips" on the bulletin board beside the blackboard, then turned around:

"Everyone, Liu-Liu's 'delivery route' was actually planned very well. So—"

He smiled faintly: "From today onward, Liu-Liu's 'delivery mission' is officially changed to—'whole-class homework collection and delivery service.' Every day after school, collect the homework, deliver it to the office. This is a legitimate mission."

"A legitimate homework courier?!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

"Much better prospects than being a cheating courier." Teacher Gao added.

Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, tail swaying gently, a complex light flickering in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep... I guess... I guess that's not bad... at least, it's legal..."

"Oh, and, Liu-Liu." Teacher Gao added, "the content on those twenty-five slips—I've memorized it all. Want to try reciting it to the whole class?"

Liu-Liu was stunned for a moment, then it opened its mouth and recited fluently, word by word:

"Chinese Lesson 2... 'the fierce typhoon brought severe disaster'... Math Unit 3... 'the area of a circle equals pi times the square of the radius'..."

The whole class was dead silent.

"Beep?!" Even Liu-Liu itself was stunned. "When... when did I memorize all this?"

"Because you delivered them many times." Qingqing spoke calmly. "Deliver once, look once; deliver twenty-five times, and you've remembered twenty-five times."

"..." Liu-Liu lowered its head, looked at its own empty pod, then at the neatly posted slips on the bulletin board, and for the first time, a thoughtful expression appeared in its electronic eyes.

A-Xin sat in his seat, watching this scene, muttering to himself:

"Even Liu-Liu... memorized the key points just by 'delivering answers'..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing turned to him, "have you noticed that every time we 'cheat,' one thing always happens?"

"What?"

"Everyone improves." Qingqing said.

A-Xin fell silent.

He lowered his head and looked at his own paper covered in red marks—he'd spent the whole exam directing Liu-Liu, and yet... he seemed to have gotten quite a few questions right?

"...Strange..." A-Xin muttered. "Why do I feel like... I didn't really need that courier either..."

"A-Xin! Don't forget!" Old Wu leaned over and reminded him anxiously. "The final exam is only three weeks away! We still have the 'Cheating Technology Revolution' to finish!"

A-Xin was taken aback, then abruptly stood up and clenched his fist:

"Right! Technology! We still have technology! Plan No. 5—the Earpiece Radio! Officially launched!"

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, watching these incorrigible trouble-makers, letting out a long electronic sigh:

"Beep... human stubbornness really is... the most advanced level in the whole universe..."
