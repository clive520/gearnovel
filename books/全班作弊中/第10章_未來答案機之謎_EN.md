# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 10: The Mystery of the Future Answer Machine

"Weixi, that glowing bracelet on your wrist—it's actually a 'future answer machine,' isn't it?"

The moment those words left A-Xin's mouth, the whole class's gazes fell together on the quiet figure in the corner.

Lin Weixi raised her head and looked at A-Xin. She wasn't angry, only asking gently:

"Why do you think it's an answer machine?"

"Because..." A-Xin gestured excitedly, "you ace every exam! You get everything right every time! How can anyone in this world be that good? Unless—" he pointed at Weixi's wrist, "you have a machine that can see into the future!"

"I see." Weixi laughed softly. "So you think I score well because I cheat?"

"Right! Otherwise how could you!" Old Wu chimed in from the side.

"Then..." Weixi blinked. "If I really had an 'answer machine,' why wouldn't I just tell you all the answers directly? Let everyone score full marks?"

"—!" The whole class instantly fell silent.

A-Xin froze.

"Because," Weixi said gently, "I don't have an answer machine. And even if I did, I wouldn't use it to cheat."

"Impossible!" A-Xin refused to give up. "That bracelet of yours glows at night! And Liu-Liu detected abnormal energy!"

"Beep! I didn't say that!" Liu-Liu poked its head out of the ventilation duct, frantically waving. "I, I only said... the energy fluctuation was a bit unusual..."

"Unusual is exactly right!" A-Xin slapped the table. "Weixi! Let me see it! Just once!"

"No." Weixi refused cleanly.

"Please!" All twenty-six begged in unison. "Just let us take one little look!"

"..." Weixi was silent for a moment, then finally let out a soft sigh. "...Fine. But remember—what I show you is not answers."

"Fine! Fine! As long as you let us look!" A-Xin rubbed his hands excitedly.

"But," Weixi added, "the time limit is one minute."

"One minute?!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed. "That short?"

"One minute is enough for you to see 'the future.'" Weixi smiled faintly, a hint of unspeakable melancholy hidden in that smile.

During lunch break, in the equipment room.

A-Xin, Old Wu, and Qingqing sat in a circle, their eyes fixed on the silver-white bracelet on Weixi's wrist.

"Ready?" Weixi asked.

"Ready!" A-Xin nodded excitedly.

Weixi took a deep breath and gently touched the side of the bracelet.

"Hmm—"

A faint azure light blossomed from inside the bracelet, unfolding into a holographic projection in midair.

"This is..." A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

In the image was a city swallowed by the sea.

Streets lay submerged beneath murky water, with only the tops of red-and-white chimneys protruding above the surface, standing lonely under a gray sky. Seagulls circled over ruined rooftops, crying shrilly. On the shore, mountains of plastic garbage were battered by wave after wave against the crumbling sea wall.

"Where... where is this?" The pork bun fell out of Old Wu's mouth.

"This is—" Weixi's voice was very soft, "Taichung Harbor."

"Taichung Harbor?!" Qingqing leaned closer to the projection, looking carefully. "But Taichung Harbor... isn't like this now..."

"Forty years from now, it will be like this." Weixi said.

"Forty years?!" All three cried out in unison.

"Sea levels rise, coastal cities flood." Weixi's tone was calm, yet carried an indescribable sadness. "Droughts and floods alternate, the sky is grayish yellow all year round, plastics pile up like mountains... this, is the world of our era."

"Our era..." A-Xin repeated those words to himself, and suddenly, as if struck by lightning, his eyes went wide. "Weixi, you... you can't mean..."

"I came back from the future." Weixi nodded gently. "With this bracelet, back to the present, to show everyone—what the future looks like."

"—!!!"

All three froze on the spot.

"The future..." A-Xin stammered. "Then... in your future... is everyone cheating?"

"Cheating?" Weixi was taken aback for a moment, then smiled softly. "Cheating isn't even worth mentioning in our era. The people of our era... even the Earth itself was being deceived."

"Deceiving the Earth?" Old Wu was full of question marks.

"To be convenient, they polluted the rivers; to make money, they chopped down trees; to save effort, they piled up garbage." Weixi said. "Everyone wanted to 'take a shortcut,' everyone wanted to 'get something for nothing.' And the result..."

She pointed at the submerged city in the projection: "...the future became like this."

The equipment room fell silent.

"So," Weixi turned to look at A-Xin, "do you think 'cheating' is just a small matter at school?"

"..." A-Xin opened his mouth but couldn't speak.

"It's not." Weixi shook her head. "Cheating is the first step of 'taking shortcuts.' Today you cheat for a score, tomorrow you'll take shortcuts for money, and the day after you'll deceive the Earth for convenience. Step by step, in the end, it becomes—"

She gently closed her eyes: "—a debt that can never be repaid."

"A debt that can never be repaid..." Old Wu repeated those words to himself, and for no reason, shivered.

"So," Weixi looked at A-Xin, her gaze gentle yet resolute, "I can't lend 'the future' to you for cheating. Because if you get used to shortcuts, then the future will never get better."

She gently touched the bracelet, and the projection slowly faded.

The equipment room returned to dimness.

A-Xin lowered his head and was silent for a long, long time.

Then, he spoke, his voice a little hoarse:

"Weixi... then... is there really no final exam answer in your bracelet?"

Weixi was taken aback, then couldn't help but laugh softly:

"No." she said gently. "In my bracelet, there are only records of 'how we treat the Earth.' No final exam."

"..." A-Xin was silent for a second, then abruptly raised his head, fresh fighting spirit blazing in his eyes:

"Fine! Since the future has no answers—then we'll—find the answers ourselves!"

"By ourselves?" Old Wu asked.

"Yes!" A-Xin clenched his fist, his tone sonorous and forceful. "Plan No. 7! Since the future won't work, the radio won't work, the courier won't work, the cheat sheets won't work—then we'll—"

He took a deep breath and solemnly declared:

"—directly hack Teacher Gao!"

"Hack Teacher Gao?!" The whole class exploded.

"Teacher Gao's brain must have all the answers!" A-Xin gestured excitedly. "We just hack into his system and download the answers—that would be the most perfect cheat in the world!"

"A-Xin," Qingqing pushed up her glasses, "are you sure you want to go 'hack' a teacher who can 'electromagnetically interfere' and has 'X-ray vision'?"

"Of course!" A-Xin declared with heroic bravado. "Man conquers nature! We can do it!"

"..." Qingqing was silent for a second, then flatly added, "Fine. Then I wish you—'a crushing defeat.'"

"You jinx!"

After school, Weixi sat alone in the corner of the classroom, gently stroking the bracelet on her wrist.

"Hmm—" The light on the inside of the bracelet seemed a little dimmer than before.

Weixi looked at the faint light, murmuring thoughtfully to herself:

"The energy... has decreased again..."

On the windowsill, Liu-Liu lay there, its electronic eyes reflecting Weixi's figure, its tail swaying gently, letting out a worried electronic chirp:

"Beep beep... detecting... unusual energy fluctuation... slowly... decreasing..."

"Liu-Liu," Weixi turned, smiling gently, "it's nothing. It's just that the time is almost up."

"Beep?" Liu-Liu tilted its head, seemingly not understanding.

Weixi didn't explain. She only raised her head, looking at the orange-red sky outside the window, her voice as light as a feather:

"I hope that before I go back... I can help everyone understand that the real 'future' is built step by step, by walking properly in the present."
