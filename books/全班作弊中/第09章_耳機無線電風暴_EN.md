# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 9: The Earpiece Radio Storm

"Liu-Liu's delivery has fallen! But—the technology revolution is just beginning!"

In the equipment room after school, A-Xin stood on the steel table and declared passionately:

"Everyone! Since 'visible transportation' gets scanned, we'll switch to—'invisible transmission'!"

"Invisible transmission?" Old Wu chewed his pork bun, full of question marks.

"Radio!" A-Xin "slapped" his palms together. "Plan No. 5: The Earpiece Radio!"

He pulled a modified toy walkie-talkie and a box of mini earbuds out of his backpack:

"It's simple in principle! I'll be the 'switchboard operator.' During the exam, I broadcast the answers to the whole class by radio! Every one of you wears an earbud, listens to my broadcast, and writes down the answers!"

"Radio?!" Old Wu stared wide-eyed. "Isn't that the stuff spies use in the movies?!"

"Right!" A-Xin said smugly. "The whole class is twenty-five spies!"

"But A-Xin," Qingqing pushed up her glasses, her tone calm, "radio signals are electromagnetic waves. Do you think a teacher who can 'electromagnetically interfere' won't detect electromagnetic waves?"

"..." A-Xin's smile froze instantly. "How... how do you know he can electromagnetically interfere?"

"Because he's Teacher Gao." Qingqing said flatly.

"Whatever! Let's build it first!" A-Xin waved his arm. "Qingqing, handing the baton to you!"

"...I refuse."

"You handle the 'engineering'! Engineering isn't use!" A-Xin insisted. "The 'technical support' for the radio station is part of your 'research' scope!"

"..." Qingqing sighed helplessly and picked up the walkie-talkie. "...Fine."

And so, the assembly of an "underground radio station" got under way in the equipment room in full swing.

Qingqing took apart the walkie-talkie, re-soldered the wiring, and tested the box of mini earbuds one by one. Old Wu leaned in to watch the excitement and asked eagerly:

"Qingqing, will this earbud hurt when you wear it?"

"It shouldn't." Qingqing didn't look up. "Your ears are bigger, so I picked out a large size for you."

"Large size?!" Old Wu was moved to tears. "Qingqing, you're such a good person!"

"I wouldn't presume to be a good person," Qingqing added flatly, "I just don't want your earbud to fall out and get stuck in a pork bun."

"Pfft!" The whole class burst into laughter.

Late at night, Qingqing sat alone at the steel table, adjusting the walkie-talkie to an extremely obscure frequency band, then testing the signals of all twenty-five earbuds. Finally, she picked up the walkie-talkie and murmured softly:

"Testing... testing... this is Luyang Elementary Underground Radio Station... Announcer No. 1, Lin Yunqing, standing by..."

She paused, suddenly realizing what she'd just said, hurriedly "snapped" the walkie-talkie off, blushing and muttering to herself:

"...I-I was just testing the signal... not broadcasting..."

The next day, the second school-wide mock exam before the final exam arrived right on schedule.

A-Xin sat in the last row, the modified radio switchboard hidden under his desk; all twenty-five students (Weixi still refused to participate) had mini earbuds in their ears, concealed carefully by their hair.

The exam paper was handed out.

A-Xin took a deep breath, pressed the transmit button, and hissed:

"Testing, testing—all spies, this is the switchboard! Question 1! Let me see... this one is..."

He looked at the question for a long while, then, going with his gut, said cautiously:

"Question 1... the answer... should probably be... B, right?"

All twenty-five students wrote "B" on their answer sheets in unison.

"Question 2!" A-Xin continued broadcasting. "This one... I remember the teacher covered it... is it C? Or D?... Forget it, C!"

All twenty-five students wrote "C" in unison.

"Question 3! I know this one! The answer is A! This time it can't be wrong!" A-Xin got more and more into it.

All twenty-five students wrote "A" in unison.

"Question 4..."

Just then—

"BZZZZ—!"

A burst of harsh static exploded in A-Xin's earbud! In the next instant, a jolt of current shot through his eardrum, and he sprang up with a scream: "Ah—!"

"What happened? What happened?" All twenty-five students turned their heads in unison.

"BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP—!"

In the next second, all twenty-five earbuds burst with a blast of harsh static at the same time! Everyone clutched their ears, howling in pain:

"Ah—! My ears! My ears!"

"Everyone," Teacher Gao Zhixian said flatly from the podium, "your earbuds are now receiving my 'electromagnetic interference' signal. This is a standard anti-eavesdropping measure—"

He paused: "—just like the Ministry of National Defense jamming enemy communications."

The static in everyone's ears finally stopped, replaced by silence.

"Now," Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, "please remove your earbuds and place them on your desks."

The twenty-five exchanged glances and, one by one, obediently took out their earbuds and set them on the desks.

Only Old Wu stood frozen, fumbling at his ear in a panic: "My, my earbud is stuck! It's stuck!"

"If you pull too hard," Qingqing said coolly from the side, "you'll pull your eardrum out with it."

"Qingqing!" Old Wu screamed in fright. "Don't scare me!"

"..." A-Xin sat in the last row, staring at the row of earbuds on the desks, his face turning ashen.

He never imagined his painstakingly built "underground radio station" would be completely crippled by Teacher Gao's "electromagnetic interference" after broadcasting only four questions.

"However—" Teacher Gao shifted tack, pulling a small receiver from his pocket and shaking it at the class, "the 'test signal' you broadcast just now—I happened to record it here."

He pressed play—

"All spies, this is the switchboard! Question 1... the answer... should probably be... B, right?"

—A-Xin's voice, crystal clear, came out of the receiver in Teacher Gao's hand.

The faces of the entire class went white in an instant.

"Question 2... this one... is it C? Or D?... Forget it, C!"

"Question 3... the answer is A! This time it can't be wrong!"

"—!!" A-Xin sprang up from his chair, staring in terror: "You... you recorded it?!"

"Not just recorded," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "just now, I've synchronized this 'broadcast' to the school-wide public address system."

"The w-whole school?!" A-Xin was scared out of his wits.

"Three minutes ago," Teacher Gao said flatly, "the entire faculty and student body heard your 'cheating broadcast.'"

"—!!!"

The whole classroom of Class 6-1 instantly fell into deathly silence.

Old Wu's mouth hung in an "O," the pork bun rolling out of his mouth, bouncing twice on the desk before landing on the floor.

"No... no way..." A-Xin slumped in his chair, his face ashen. "The whole school heard it?!"

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "And," he delivered the finishing blow, "this is the first time I've heard anyone broadcast 'cheating instructions' with such confidence."

"Pfft!" In the corner, Wang Zhuankai couldn't help laughing, then quickly covered his mouth.

"I... I... I'm doomed..." A-Xin went completely limp.

"Bang!"

The classroom door was pushed open once more, and Director Zhou Yan barged in triumphantly, waving his notebook:

"I heard it! I heard it! The school broadcast just played 'Question 1, answer B'! That's cheating! That's absolutely cheating!"

He scanned the room, only to see all twenty-six students sitting upright in their seats, earbuds all removed and lined up neatly on the desks like a parade.

"Huh?" Director Zhou froze. "You... how did you..."

"Director Zhou," Teacher Gao said calmly, "that was our class's 'English listening comprehension' practice simulation just now. I used the public address system to demonstrate standard listening-broadcast pronunciation."

"L-Listening practice?" Director Zhou stared wide-eyed. "Then that 'Question 1, answer B'..."

"That's the 'option announcement' of the listening test." Teacher Gao said without changing his expression. "Please listen—Question 1... answer B... Question 2... answer C... This is the standard procedure of a listening test."

"..." Director Zhou eyed them suspiciously for a long while, and could only grudgingly snap his notebook shut and storm out.

"You... you'd better pray I don't catch you on the final exam!"

After Director Zhou left, the whole class slumped limply into their chairs.

Teacher Gao collected the row of earbuds and the walkie-talkie into a cardboard box and set it at the corner of the podium:

"Everyone, I'll keep these 'underground radio station devices' safe for now. However—"

He paused, looking at A-Xin:

"A-Xin, your 'broadcasting' actually has real talent. Clear voice, steady rhythm, full of emotion—if used in the right place, you'd make a wonderful 'announcer.'"

"Announcer?" A-Xin was taken aback.

"Starting tomorrow," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "the class's 'morning news broadcast' is yours. Every morning during self-study, use the public address system to announce the day's weather, schedule, and notices."

"I... I'm in charge of the broadcast?!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "This is a—'legitimate' job."

A-Xin opened his mouth, and for a moment, didn't know whether to be happy or sad.

After school, the emergency meeting of the Exam Mutual Aid Society was as heavy as a funeral.

"Everyone..." A-Xin clutched his head, groaning in pain, slumped over his desk. "The earpiece radio... has fallen too..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing spoke, "have you calculated our whole class's scores on this mock exam?"

"What for?" A-Xin said weakly.

"Because I did." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "This time, all twenty-five of us scored exactly the same."

"Exactly the same?!" The whole class gasped.

"Because," Qingqing said calmly, "everyone wrote their answers according to your broadcast. When you guessed right, the whole class was right; when you guessed wrong, the whole class was wrong together. The twenty-five of you wrote twenty-five 'identical' exam papers."

"Then... then our scores..."

"About sixty-odd." Qingqing added. "Not high, not low."

"..."

"But," Qingqing shifted tack, "there were two people who scored especially high."

"Who?"

"Weixi." Qingqing said. "She didn't wear an earbud and relied entirely on herself. And—"

She looked at Old Wu: "Old Wu, your earbud was stuck earlier, so you didn't hear the broadcast and wrote by yourself. This time you scored ninety-two in science."

"Ninety-two?!" Old Wu jumped in fright. "Me?!"

"You." Qingqing nodded. "Because you memorized 'gas exchange' really well last monthly exam, and this time's science test covered related content again."

"..." A-Xin fell silent.

He lowered his head, looking at his own "sixty-odd exam paper," then at Old Wu's "ninety-two exam paper," muttering:

"The ones who wrote by themselves... actually scored higher..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing delivered the finishing blow calmly, "have you noticed that every time we 'cheat,' the ones who score best are always those who 'relied on themselves'?"

"..." A-Xin opened his mouth, unable to answer a single word.

He bowed his head and was silent for a long, long time.

Then, he abruptly raised his head, fresh fighting spirit blazing in his eyes:

"That's because... that's because they got lucky! I declare—Plan No. 6!"

"There's a No. 6?" Old Wu jumped in fright.

"Yes!" A-Xin clenched his fist. "This time, we'll use the power of—'the future'!"

"The future?" The whole class exchanged glances.

A-Xin's gaze settled on the quiet figure in the corner:

"Weixi, that glowing bracelet on your wrist—it's actually a 'future answer machine,' isn't it?"
