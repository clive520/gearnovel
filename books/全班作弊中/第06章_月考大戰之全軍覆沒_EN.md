# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 6: The Month Exam Battle: Total Defeat

The day the monthly exam results were posted, the central courtyard of Luyang Elementary was packed solid.

A-Xin, Old Wu, and Qingqing squeezed to the front, twenty-six pairs of eyes fixed on the bright red results board.

"What do we do, what do we do, what do we do..." Old Wu was so nervous he forgot to bite his pork bun, spinning in circles on the spot. "A-Xin, I, I don't dare look..."

"What's there to be afraid of! At worst... at worst..." A-Xin's voice began to shake too. "At worst, we're really, truly done for..."

"Found it!" Sharp-eyed Qingqing reached out, pointing at the Class 6-1 column. "Look!"

Three heads "snapped" over at once.

The next second—

"Ahhhhhhh—!"

Old Wu let out an earth-shaking scream, stumbling backward and landing flat on the ground, tears streaming: "I passed! I passed! I scored seventy-eight in science! A-Xin! I scored seventy-eight!"

"Seventy-eight?!" A-Xin rushed over, snatching the results sheet, eyes wide. "Weren't you at forty-five last time?!"

"I-I don't know either!" Old Wu laughed and cried at once. "I just memorized that 'gas exchange' really well, and then all the questions on the paper suddenly seemed like I knew them!"

"I passed too!" Wang Zhuankai squeezed out of the crowd, waving his results sheet excitedly. "I scored seventy-one in Chinese! Last time, I only got fifty-five!"

"How did you do it?" A-Xin asked.

"I don't know either!" Wang Zhuankai scratched his head. "I just... to shrink the new-word annotations into the eraser, I read those Chinese lessons over and over, maybe a dozen times..."

"Shrinking a cheat sheet, and ended up memorizing the text?" A-Xin muttered.

"Right!" Wang Zhuankai grinned from ear to ear. "Now I can write those new words with my eyes closed!"

Cheers rippled through the crowd.

Out of the twenty-six, twenty had set new personal bests since sixth grade; the class average had improved a full thirteen points over the last monthly exam.

"Up thirteen points!" A-Xin stared at the results sheet in disbelief. "We... we were caught at every single cheat plan!"

"Our battles were all wiped out," Qingqing pushed up her glasses, her tone calm, "but the content we 'prepared for the battles'—we remembered all of it."

A-Xin froze.

He looked down at the results sheet, then up at the group of classmates jumping and cheering—Old Wu hopping, Wang Zhuankai shouting, Lin Yunyun clutching her own results sheet and crying her heart out.

"Lin Yunyun! Why are you crying?" A-Xin rushed over.

"I... I passed for the first time..." Lin Yunyun sobbed. "I, I memorized that key-point sheet twenty times so I could hide it in my pencil case..."

"Twenty times..." A-Xin muttered, his gaze slowly turning complicated.

Just then, a shadow descended.

"Hmph!"

Director Zhou Yan stood with his hands clasped behind his back at the edge of the crowd, his hawk-sharp gaze sweeping over the results sheet, his face darkening with reluctance:

"Class 6-1... a thirteen-point class average improvement?!"

He leaned in, looked at the sheet again and again, and grew more and more unwilling to accept it: "Impossible! You little trouble-makers were busy doing your 'human puzzle' during the exam days! How could you score like this?!"

"Director," Teacher Gao appeared behind him without a sound, his tone flat, "although the students of Class 6-1 used clumsy cheating methods, in the process of preparing to cheat, they did indeed memorize the textbook content. This is called 'effective learning motivation.'"

"Effective... what?" Director Zhou stared wide-eyed.

"To put it simply," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "they thought they were cheating, but they were actually studying."

"—!" Director Zhou opened his mouth, then could only grumble, "Hmph! You got lucky on the monthly exam! You won't be so lucky on the final exam! I'll keep a close watch on you!"

He swept away, leaving the class to their victorious cheers.

At dismissal, the pickup zone at the school gate became the second "battlefield."

Mama Ke, one hand holding her phone, the other on her hip, blocked the front of the Class 6-1 lineup, her sharp gaze sweeping through the crowd:

"Let me see... let me see... up thirteen points, right?"

"Yes, Mama Ke." Teacher Gao nodded.

"Hmph," Mama Ke lifted her chin, "a mere thirteen points and you're already talking big? On the final exam, I want more than half the class in the school's top one hundred! Otherwise, I'll mail the results straight to the Bureau of Education!"

"Mama Ke, grades can't be treated this way..."

"I don't care! On the final exam, I only look at scores!"

Over on the other side, Old Wu's father's scooter was parked in the shade. Seeing Old Wu come running with a beaming face, he proudly waved his drumstick:

"Little Wu! Dad heard! You got seventy-eight in science? You passed?"

"Yeah! Dad! I passed!" Old Wu nodded excitedly.

"Good! That's my boy! Extra dishes tonight! A whole roasted chicken!" Old Wu's father laughed, then shifted gears. "—However, if you can score eighty on the final exam, I'll treat you to a month of drumsticks!"

"A month of drumsticks!" Old Wu's eyes instantly blazed like two little suns. "For the drumsticks, I'll give it my all!"

Last was A-Xin.

He stood at the school gate, phone in hand, the screen showing his mother's message:

"A-Xin: You passed the monthly exam, so I put the game console password in your pencil case. If you slip on the final exam, the password comes back to me."

A-Xin gripped the phone, but his face held none of the expected joy.

Slowly, slowly, he put the phone in his pocket, raised his head to look at the orange-red sky under the setting sun, Teacher Gao's words from just now echoing endlessly through his mind:

"They thought they were cheating, but they were actually studying."

"...Cheating... studying..." A-Xin muttered. "These two things... how come they seem... more and more impossible to tell apart?"

"A-Xin!"

Qingqing caught up from behind, a small bag dangling from her hand: "What are you standing here spacing out for? We need to start preparing for the final exam tomorrow!"

"The final exam?!" A-Xin jumped in fright. "Isn't that over a month away?"

"I saw your mom's message." Qingqing waved her phone. "If you slip on the final exam, the game console password comes back. Mama Ke also made her demand—more than half the class in the school's top one hundred. Do you think we have time to take it easy?"

A-Xin fell silent.

He bowed his head and thought for a long, long time.

Then, he abruptly looked up, fresh fighting spirit blazing in his eyes:

"Fine! Since the monthly exam's 'human puzzle' failed, we'll have—the technology revolution!"

"Technology?" Qingqing raised an eyebrow.

"Yes! Plan No. 4!" A-Xin clenched his fist. "In the monthly exam we relied on 'memorizing,' and we lost because we were 'seen'! For the final exam, we'll use a method that Teacher Gao 'can't see'!"

"Teacher Gao can even see paper planes and codes," Qingqing delivered the finishing blow calmly, "do you think there's anything he 'can't see'?"

"Then—" A-Xin smiled mysteriously, "we'll make a cheating tool that's 'invisible'!"

The next day, in class.

Teacher Gao stood on the podium, just about to explain the wrong answers from the monthly exam, when suddenly his glasses "flashed," and a moment later, a blinding light burst from his pupils—

"Sssss-crackle!"

On the blackboard, the "standard answers" to the entire exam paper appeared in projection!

"It's the answers!" Old Wu "shot" to his feet, fumbling for his pen. "Quick, copy them! Copy them fast!"

"Copy what!?" A-Xin lunged over and pressed down on Old Wu's hand. "We all know these now! There's nothing to copy!"

"Huh?" Old Wu froze, lowering his head to look at his own paper. "Right... right... I already wrote this one correctly just now..."

"The whole class wrote them correctly." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "Because we reviewed these questions yesterday."

The projection lasted three seconds before Teacher Gao shut it off himself with a "click."

"My apologies," Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, his face calm, "the optical projection system has a temporary malfunction. Everyone, please continue reviewing the exam."

In the classroom, twenty-six pairs of eyes stared at the lingering light on the blackboard.

Nobody lowered their head to copy anything.

"Teacher," A-Xin raised his hand, his tone carrying a hint of indefinable pride, "no need for the projection this time. The whole class already knows these questions."

"Very good." Teacher Gao smiled faintly. "Then let the whole class do this exam paper again—this time, let's see who really 'knows it.'"

All twenty-six students lowered their heads in unison and began writing.

Sunlight streamed through the window, spilling across every focused little face.

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, its tail swaying gently, a complex light flickering in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep... detecting the 'honesty index'... rising slowly but steadily..."

And in the office at the end of the hallway, Director Zhou Yan frowned at the infraction notebook, writing stroke by stroke:

"Final exam, key surveillance target: Class 6-1."

"Let's see what tricks these trouble-makers can pull with their 'cheating.'"
