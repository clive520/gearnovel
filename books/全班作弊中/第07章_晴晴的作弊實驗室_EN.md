# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 7: Qingqing's Cheating Laboratory

"Everyone! The monthly exam taught us that—anything 'visible' will get caught!"

In the classroom of Class 6-1 after school, A-Xin stood on his desk and declared passionately to all the members of the Mutual Aid Society:

"So, the ultimate weapon for the final exam is—'invisible' cheating!"

"Invisible?" Old Wu chewed his pork bun, full of question marks. "How can something be invisible?"

"That's what our—" A-Xin "slapped" his palms together, his gaze landing on the first row, "tech director! Qingqing—is for!"

Qingqing raised her head, pushed up her glasses, her tone flat as still water:

"I refuse. I only research, I don't use."

"Exactly!" A-Xin jumped off the desk and grabbed Qingqing's hand emotionally. "You said it yourself—'research'! Research isn't use! Your job is to develop cheating devices that 'Teacher Gao can't scan'!"

"..." Qingqing was silent for two seconds. "Your twisted logic always sounds reasonable at first, but on closer thought, something feels off."

"Then don't think about it!" A-Xin waved his arm. "Qingqing's Cheating Laboratory, officially open!"

"I didn't say I'd open it..."

"Open!"

So, after school, the little storage room piled with old equipment in the gymnasium became "Qingqing's Cheating Laboratory."

On her first visit, Qingqing pushed open the door, glanced around, and frowned:

"There isn't even a table here."

"Reporting to the tech director!" Old Wu snapped to attention, dragging a steel table out of nowhere. "The table is here!"

"And!" Wang Zhuankai carried over a toolbox and set it heavily on the table. "Tools!"

"And power!" Lin Yunyun dragged an extension cord over from the back and plugged it in.

Qingqing looked at these bustling "assistants" and couldn't help sighing: "...Fine. Since everything's ready, I'll... do a little research, then."

She opened the toolbox and pulled out an assortment of items: a magnifier, engineering pens, a flashlight, a can of lemon juice, a carton of milk, an old calculator, a few desk pads, and some sheets of white paper.

"Is this... cooking?" Old Wu leaned in, sniffed the lemon juice, and nearly drooled.

"Not cooking." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "It's developing—'invisible ink.'"

"Invisible ink?" The whole class gathered into a circle.

"The principle is simple." Qingqing picked up a pen, dipped it in a little lemon juice, and wrote a few characters on the paper. "Lemon juice contains organic acids. Written on paper and dried, it's nearly invisible. But heat it up—"

She picked up a hair dryer and blew on the paper for ten seconds.

The characters on the paper slowly, slowly emerged in a ring of scorched yellow.

"Wow—!" The whole class gasped.

"This is the prototype of 'body-temperature development.'" Qingqing smirked proudly, then quickly resumed her expressionless face. "Ahem. I'm researching. Not helping you cheat."

"Tech director! You're amazing!" A-Xin was moved to tears.

"But Qingqing," Old Wu raised his hand, "this paper needed ten seconds of hair dryer to develop. We can't exactly bring a hair dryer into the exam room."

"So," Qingqing picked up the desk pad, "I improved it—write the invisible ink on the desk pad. During the exam, just press your palm on it for three seconds; the body heat will develop the answer gradually."

"Press your palm for three seconds?!" Old Wu rejoiced. "That's as simple as rubbing a mahjong tile!"

"Try it." Qingqing handed the desk pad to Old Wu.

Old Wu excitedly placed the pad on the desk, pressed his big hand on it hard for three seconds—then lifted his palm.

The pad was a scorched black.

"You pressed too long!" Qingqing looked at the pad that had been branded with a scorch mark, and sighed helplessly. "Body-heat development requires controlled timing. You were ironing, not developing."

"I, I was nervous!" Old Wu said aggrievedly.

"Never mind," Qingqing shook her head, handing him the second pad, "this one uses 'ultraviolet development'—written with this pen, it's completely invisible normally, and can only be revealed under an ultraviolet flashlight."

She pulled a small purple flashlight out of the toolbox: "This flashlight is a UV money-checking light."

"Ultraviolet!" Old Wu took the flashlight and waved it around excitedly. "So what glows when you shine it?"

"Shine it on paper, and the answers glow." Qingqing said.

"What about shining it on me?" Old Wu aimed the flashlight at his own hand.

"You... would probably turn into a fluorescent pig." Qingqing said flatly.

"Pfft!" The whole class burst into laughter.

"Alright, alright," Qingqing snatched the ultraviolet flashlight back from Old Wu, "and finally—the cheating calculator."

She picked up the old calculator, skillfully unscrewed the back, opened the case, and revealed the circuit board inside:

"An ordinary calculator only does arithmetic. But I can re-solder the wires and modify the chip, so that under a specific key combination, it displays pre-entered formulas and key points."

"Formulas?! Key points?!" A-Xin's eyes lit up. "Isn't that like a personal dictionary!"

"More or less." Qingqing lowered her head, picked up the soldering iron, and began to solder. "Just press the 'XYZ' keys, then a number key, and it displays the corresponding formula..."

"Let me try! Let me try!" A-Xin grabbed the calculator and eagerly pressed "XYZ"—

"BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP—!"

The calculator let out a piercing alarm, the screen flashing a line of large text:

"Warning! Cheating detected! Notification sent to the Student Affairs Office!"

"—!!" The whole class froze solid, the air instantly still.

"Qingqing!" A-Xin was scared out of his wits. "Your calculator rats us out on its own?!"

"..." Qingqing took the calculator back from A-Xin's hand, expressionless, and inspected it. "I just connected the chip backwards. This one is the test version."

She re-soldered and adjusted the wiring, then pressed "XYZ" again—

On the screen, a neat row of formulas slowly emerged:

"Circle area = πr²... circle circumference = 2πr..."

"Success!" A-Xin jumped with joy. "Tech director! You're incredible!"

"I told you," Qingqing pushed up her glasses, "I only research, I don't use. You all be careful yourselves."

That was what her mouth said, but in the moment everyone turned away, she couldn't help secretly picking up the "body-temperature development desk pad," studying the scorch mark against the light, muttering to herself:

"...Weird. It's just lemon juice—how did it scorch black?... Was the temperature too high?... Or is it a paper problem..."

"Tech director!" A-Xin called back.

"Ahem, ahem," Qingqing instantly resumed her expressionless face, shoving the pad into the toolbox. "I'm researching! Researching! Don't bother me!"

Late into the night, only Qingqing remained in the equipment room.

She sat at the steel table, a whole row of tools and textbooks spread before her, flipping through the science textbook with one hand while blending invisible ink with the other:

"The chemistry textbook says lemon juice's citric acid oxidizes and changes color when heated... so in theory, controlling the temperature controls the development time..."

"The math textbook says the derivation of the circle area formula... I see, so that's how it works..."

"Huh? This page of the science textbook, the definition of photosynthesis..."

The more she read, the more absorbed she became. Without realizing it, she had gone through all the key points of science, math, and chemistry over the course of one night.

On the windowsill, Liu-Liu lay there, tail swaying gently, the light in its electronic eyes reflecting Qingqing's focused profile, letting out a meaningful electronic chirp:

"Beep beep... detecting 'learning behavior'... occurring during the process of 'researching cheating tools'..."

The next morning, Qingqing wobbled into the classroom with two enormous dark circles under her eyes.

"Tech director!" A-Xin rushed over in a single bound. "Did the invisible ink succeed?"

"It succeeded." Qingqing yawned, handing A-Xin a desk pad and a sheet of paper. "Try it."

A-Xin excitedly took the pad, pressed hard for three seconds—and on the pad emerged a few lines of characters:

"'Photosynthesis... requires light...' Huh? Qingqing, this is..."

"That's the science key point." Qingqing yawned. "Last night I read the science textbook cover to cover and accidentally memorized it."

"..." A-Xin froze. "You, weren't you researching cheating tools last night?"

"Yeah." Qingqing blinked her dry eyes. "I was researching. And as I researched, I finished reading the textbook."

The whole class instantly fell silent.

A-Xin opened his mouth, wanting to say something, but found nothing to say.

Just then, Teacher Gao Zhixian pushed the door open and walked in. His gaze swept the class and finally landed on Qingqing, his tone flat:

"Lin Yunqing, staying up all night to research is devoted, but you should also get some rest."

Qingqing guiltily lowered her head.

"Incidentally," Teacher Gao added, "the answers that truly need to be 'hidden' are never in the tools."

"..." A-Xin silently glanced at Qingqing, then at the desk pad in his hand. For the first time, a flicker of doubt appeared in his eyes.

At dismissal, Director Zhou Yan stood at the corner of the hallway, watching Qingqing carry her toolbox and slip into the gymnasium equipment room from afar.

"A cheating laboratory, huh..." he narrowed his eyes, a cold smile curling at the corner of his mouth. "Let's see how long you can keep hiding."

He was just about to follow—

"Whoosh!"

A silver-white streak darted past his feet, whipping up a gust of wind that swept a scrap of paper to Director Zhou's feet.

Director Zhou looked down. The scrap read: "Director, your jacket zipper is unzipped."

He looked down—indeed!

Seizing the moment, the equipment room door "slapped" shut.

"Damn it!" Director Zhou stamped his foot. "Liu-Liu! It's you again!"

Inside the equipment room, Qingqing placed her toolbox on the steel table and took a deep breath:

"Alright, everyone. The cheating devices for the final exam are fully developed. Now, we need a—'delivery system.'"

"A delivery system?" A-Xin asked.

"Yes." Qingqing pushed up her glasses, her gaze falling on Liu-Liu at the windowsill. "The answers written in invisible ink must reach each person during the exam. For that, we need a—'courier.'"

Liu-Liu's electronic eyes went instantly wide as saucers, its tail "whooshing" into a bristle:

"Beep beep! I refuse! That 'behavior observation log' from last time still hasn't been cleared!"
