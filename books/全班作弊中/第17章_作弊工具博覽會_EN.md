# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 17: The Cheating Tools Exhibition

The day the final exam results were posted, the central courtyard of Luyang Elementary was boiling over.

"Class average—eighty-six points!"

"Top one hundred in the school—Class 6-1 has fourteen students!"

"Top three in the entire grade—Class 6-1 took both first place and third place!"

A-Xin stood before the results board, staring at the bright red results sheet, unable to speak for a long time.

"A-Xin!" Old Wu charged over, hugged him, and laughed and cried at once. "I, I scored ninety-three in social studies! Ninety-three! I can eat a month of drumsticks!"

"Me too! Me too!" Wang Zhuankai waved his results sheet excitedly. "I scored eighty-nine in math! Eighty-nine! My first time scoring eighty-nine in math!"

"I, I scored ninety..." Lin Yunyun clutched her results sheet, crying messily. "I've never... never scored ninety..."

"..." A-Xin watched this group of classmates crying and laughing, and looked at the line "class average eighty-six points" on the results sheet, murmuring to himself:

"We... this group of 'all-caught' trouble-makers... actually scored the best ever?"

"Reporting to the teacher!" A-Xin raised his hand. "I have a question!"

"What question?" Teacher Gao Zhixian stood on the podium, his tone flat.

"Teacher," A-Xin took a deep breath, "we clearly... spent the whole semester cheating, so why... did our grades hit a record high instead?"

"Because," Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, "you thought you were cheating."

"...Huh?" The whole class froze.

"Come," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "let me show you something."

He walked to the blackboard and pressed the remote on the wall—the projection screen "whooshed" down, revealing rows upon rows of densely packed video thumbnails.

"This is..." A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

"This is our class's 'surveillance record' for this entire year." Teacher Gao said.

"Surveillance record?!" The whole class exploded.

"Starting from Plan No. 1, the 'Miniature Library,'" Teacher Gao pressed play, "to the last plan, the 'Ultimate Human Puzzle'—I recorded every single one of your 'cheating' processes."

On the screen appeared the image of A-Xin crouching behind the podium, sneakily directing the whole class to fold paper planes.

"..." A-Xin's face instantly turned ashen.

"Also," Teacher Gao continued playing, "this is the rehearsal footage of the 'Code Signaling Operation.' This is the assembly process of the 'Earpiece Radio.' This is the live recording of 'Hack Teacher Gao'..."

"You... you filmed everything?!" A-Xin shouted in terror.

"It wasn't me who filmed it," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "it was you who 'performed' it."

"..." The whole class was speechless.

"But," Teacher Gao shifted tack, his tone softening, "have you noticed that in this 'surveillance record,' what I actually saw was not 'cheating'?"

"Not cheating?" A-Xin froze.

"You see," Teacher Gao pointed at the screen, "you organized the textbook key points meticulously in order to make cheat sheets—this is 'note-taking.' You designed a complete 'division of labor route' to pass answers—this is 'teamwork.' You researched all sorts of 'memory methods' to send codes—this is 'learning strategies.'"

"..." The whole class fell silent.

"Every time you 'prepared to cheat,'" Teacher Gao looked at the class, "you were actually 'studying seriously.'"

He paused, and delivered the most important sentence of the whole book:

"You thought I was catching you cheating—but actually, I was watching you learn."

"—!!!"

Those words, like a clap of thunder, struck into the heart of every person in the class.

The classroom was utterly silent.

A-Xin opened his mouth, yet couldn't produce a single word.

"So," Teacher Gao said with a smile, "I never stopped you from 'cheating,' because—"

He scanned the class: "your 'cheating' is your 'learning.'"

"..." A-Xin lowered his head and was silent for a long, long time.

Then, he spoke slowly: "Teacher... then why... didn't you tell us sooner?"

"Because," Teacher Gao laughed softly, "some lessons, no matter how many times the teacher says them, you won't listen. But if you discover them yourselves—you'll remember them for a lifetime."

"..."

"So," Teacher Gao clapped his hands, "today, let's do something interesting."

"What?"

"Exhibit all twenty-eight of your 'cheating tools' from this semester—" Teacher Gao said. "all of them."

"Exhibit?!" The whole class cried out.

"Yes," Teacher Gao nodded, "let's open a—'Cheating Tools Exhibition.'"

And so, a peculiar "exhibition" officially opened at the back of the Class 6-1 classroom.

The twenty-eight cheating tools were displayed in a neat row on the long table. Beside each tool was a small card with its "new name":

"No. 1: The Miniature Library—actually, the finest key-point organization."
"No. 2: The Aerial Delivery System—actually, teamwork."
"No. 3: The Code Signaling Operation—actually, memory strategies."
"No. 4: The Body-Temperature Development Desk Pad—actually, scientific experimentation."
"No. 5: The Ultraviolet Cheat Sheet—actually, key-point classification."
"No. 6: The Cheating Calculator—actually, programming."
"No. 7: Liu-Liu Airlines—actually, a sense of responsibility."
"No. 8: The Earpiece Radio—actually, listening training."
"No. 9: The Future Answer Machine—actually, environmental education."
"No. 10: The Ultimate Human Puzzle—actually, group learning."

"..." A-Xin stood before the long table, reading the cards one by one, murmuring, "So... all these 'cheating tools' we made this semester... were actually..."

"All learning." Qingqing stood beside him, finishing the sentence calmly. "We made a whole semester of notes, and it became—this museum."

"..." A-Xin fell silent.

"Teacher," he suddenly spoke, "can we... keep this museum until graduation?"

"Of course." Teacher Gao nodded. "This museum is the finest 'report card' your Class 6-1 has ever had."

"..." A-Xin looked at this museum, and for the first time, a gentle light appeared in his eyes.

"Reporting!"

Just then, Director Zhou Yan pushed the door open and walked in. His gaze swept across the long table at the back of the classroom, and his eyes instantly went wide:

"What... is this?!"

"Reporting to the director," A-Xin hastily spoke, "this is our class's—"

"Wonderful! Wonderful!" Director Zhou Yan rushed over in a single bound, his eyes shining. "This is the finest 'gifted-student note exhibition' I've seen all semester!"

"Gifted-student note exhibition?!" The whole class froze.

"You see!" Director Zhou Yan pointed excitedly at "No. 1: The Miniature Library." "Clear classification, precise key points, well-structured layers! This is a complete summary of the textbook! This is what truly excellent notes look like!"

"And this one!" He pointed at "No. 7: Liu-Liu Airlines." "'A sense of responsibility'! Those three words are beautifully written! This is the character education Luyang Elementary needs most!"

"..." The whole class exchanged glances, and for a moment, didn't know how to react.

"Director," A-Xin spoke weakly, "this is actually..."

"I know! I know!" Director Zhou Yan cut him off. "This must be a 'learning achievement exhibition' your class made yourselves! Excellent! I'm giving you an award!"

"An award?!" The whole class exploded.

"Yes!" Director Zhou Yan pulled out a gleaming gold certificate. "This is the 'Luyang Elementary Best Notes Award'! Awarded to Class 6-1!"

"—!!!" The whole class was stunned.

"Director!" A-Xin shouted urgently. "These aren't notes! They're—"

"I know! I know!" Director Zhou Yan patted his shoulder, his tone moved. "You worked hard! Keep it up!"

He solemnly pasted the certificate on the wall of the "Honesty Museum," then left, satisfied.

"..." All twenty-six students stared at the "Best Notes Award" on the wall, and were silent for a long, long time.

"A-Xin," Old Wu spoke weakly, "so... are we cheating, or taking notes?"

"..." A-Xin opened his mouth, and in the end, lowered his head and laughed softly. "...I don't know either. But I think..."

He raised his head, looking at the "Honesty Museum," his gaze gentle:

"—this is probably the finest thing we've done all semester."

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, its tail swaying gently, a tender light flickering in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep... detecting... 'Honesty Museum'... officially open..."

"And..." it added, "'the final ceremony'... is also about to arrive..."
