# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 11: Operation: Hacking Teacher Gao

"Everyone! The future answer machine is unreliable, but—the most perfect source of answers is right here among us!"

In the equipment room after school, A-Xin stood on the steel table, eyes blazing:

"Teacher Gao's brain must contain all the answers! So, Plan No. 7—directly hack Teacher Gao!"

"Hack?" Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "A-Xin, how exactly do you plan to hack him?"

"That's where you come in, tech director!" A-Xin "slapped" the table. "Didn't you say last time that Teacher Gao has a data port?"

"..." Qingqing paused. "I said, in theory, bionic robots would have maintenance ports. But I have no evidence."

"Then let's scout it out on the ground!" A-Xin announced excitedly. "Liu-Liu! I'm giving you a mission!"

"Beep?" Liu-Liu poked its head out of the ventilation duct.

"Go to Teacher Gao's office and recon his 'port'!" A-Xin gestured. "You know... around the back of his head, or the back of his neck, is there anywhere that can be 'plugged in'?"

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu's electronic eyes flickered, its tone a bit hesitant. "Beep... Teacher Gao's office... last time I got 'scanned' there..."

"Don't worry! We'll cover you!" A-Xin vowed, patting his chest. "You do the recon, we do the—'aluminum foil camera cover'!"

"Aluminum foil?" Qingqing raised an eyebrow.

"Right!" A-Xin pulled a whole roll of gleaming aluminum foil out of his backpack. "I did my research! Surveillance cameras are afraid of glare! As long as we cover the hallway CCTV, we're invisible!"

"..." Qingqing looked at the roll of aluminum foil and was silent for two seconds. "A-Xin, do you want to wrap the whole school?"

"No! Just the cameras is enough!" A-Xin said with full confidence.

And so, a covert "hack Teacher Gao" operation silently unfolded in the school after dismissal.

A-Xin crouched at the corner of the hallway, on tiptoe, "whooshing" the aluminum foil onto the CCTV lens. The foil reflected blinding light under the setting sun, flashing like a tiny sun.

"A-Xin!" Qingqing hissed. "That way, the whole world can see it!"

"Invisible! It's reflective! Reflective!" A-Xin flashed a victory sign smugly. "Let's go! To the office!"

Meanwhile, Liu-Liu slipped silently through the ceiling ventilation duct into Teacher Gao Zhixian's office.

In the office, Teacher Gao stood motionless before the window, like a statue. At the back of his neck, a ring of silver-white port seams was faintly visible.

Liu-Liu held its breath and slowly, slowly crept toward that ring of seams—

"BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP—!"

Liu-Liu's signal suddenly resonated with Teacher Gao's sensors, emitting a piercing electronic screech! Teacher Gao's head snapped toward Liu-Liu's direction!

"Bad!" Liu-Liu flipped over in fright and dove back into the vent. "Beep! Retreat! Retreat!"

"Liu-Liu failed!" A-Xin stamped his foot anxiously from behind the hallway corner. "Never mind! We have Plan B!"

"What's Plan B?" Qingqing asked.

"Go straight in!" A-Xin pulled out a data cable he'd wired himself. "Qingqing! Let's go! The two of us will go personally!"

"A-Xin," Qingqing sighed helplessly, "doing this is no different from storming a bank with a kitchen knife."

"Even so, we storm!" A-Xin gripped the data cable, his gaze determined. "For the final exam! For the game console! For the drumsticks!"

"..." Qingqing looked at A-Xin, then at the data cable in her hand, and finally silently followed along.

"...Fine. Consider it—experimental research."

Late at night, the office was dimly lit.

A-Xin and Qingqing crouched outside the window, peeking in. Inside, Teacher Gao sat at his desk, head bowed, correcting homework, utterly motionless.

"He... looks asleep?" A-Xin whispered.

"Robots don't sleep." Qingqing said. "He's probably in 'standby.'"

"Great!" A-Xin's eyes lit up. "Standby! That means we can seize the chance and—'plug him in'!"

"...Your phrasing sounds quite dangerous." Qingqing said expressionlessly.

"Whatever!" A-Xin handed the data cable to Qingqing. "You plug it in! I'll keep watch!"

"Why me?" Qingqing stared wide-eyed.

"Because you're the tech director!" A-Xin said with full justification. "Plugging in the port is a technical job!"

"..." Qingqing took a deep breath and finally accepted the data cable. "...Fine. Experimental research."

She crept carefully into the office, crouched low, and approached behind Teacher Gao. Teacher Gao maintained his homework-correcting posture, motionless.

Qingqing held her breath, raised the data cable, and aimed it at the silver-white port seam on the back of Teacher Gao's neck—

"Click."

The data cable went in.

Qingqing's heart pounded. She took a deep breath and was just about to press the "download" button—

"Hmm—!"

A ring of blinding red light suddenly burst from the back of Teacher Gao's neck! In the next instant, an icy mechanical voice exploded through the office:

"Warning! Warning! Intrusion detected! External data intrusion detected!"

"Run!" A-Xin shouted from outside the window. "Run fast!"

Qingqing yanked out the data cable and fled!

"Activating—anti-hack mode!"

Teacher Gao's eyes instantly turned blood-red. The back of his suit "split" open with a ripping sound, revealing the silver-white mechanical lining underneath. He stood up and advanced toward Qingqing's direction, step by step!

"Ahhhh—! Help!" Qingqing screamed in terror, scrambling out of the office.

"Liu-Liu! Jam the signal!" A-Xin shouted in the hallway.

Liu-Liu swooped down from the ceiling and aimed high-frequency electronic interference at Teacher Gao:

"BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP—!"

"Ineffective." Teacher Gao's voice was ice-cold. "The interference signal has been filtered. Liu-Liu, your 'intrusion support mission' has also been recorded."

"Beep?!" Liu-Liu's tail went rigid in fright.

"Pursuit begins!" Teacher Gao strode forward, chasing after all three of them!

"Run! Run! Run!" A-Xin dragged Qingqing forward with all his might. "Teacher Gao has gone crazy!"

"You still have breath to say that!" Qingqing panted. "Why did you have to provoke him in the first place!"

"I, I didn't know he'd turn into this!" A-Xin wailed. "Isn't he a teacher?! Shouldn't teachers 'set an example'?!"

"He's a robot!" Qingqing shouted.

"Robots are teachers too!"

The three of them plus one pet, in the deep night campus, launched into a heart-pounding chase!

A-Xin dashed through the classroom, Teacher Gao's figure close behind, not falling a single step behind.

"Dodge!" A-Xin "whooshed" past an overturned desk, but Teacher Gao, like a shadow, rounded the desk and kept closing in!

"Take the stairs!" Qingqing shouted.

"No!" A-Xin's eyes lit up. "Take—the window!"

He wrenched open the window and leapt out, landing on the eave of the second-floor hallway; Qingqing followed close behind, and Liu-Liu squeezed out through the window crack.

The three crouched on the eave, panting heavily.

"Phew... phew... at last... we lost him..." A-Xin gasped.

"Lost him?" Qingqing pointed below the eave. "Look."

A-Xin looked down—Teacher Gao was standing on the playground on the first floor, raising his head, his blood-red eyes fixed directly on them!

"How... how did he get down?" A-Xin was scared out of his wits.

"He jumped." Qingqing replied calmly.

"—!"

"Beep beep! Beep beep!" Liu-Liu dove into A-Xin's backpack in fright. "Human! Think of something!"

"I-I can't think of anything!" A-Xin clutched his head and wailed. "I can't even find the final exam answers!"

"Then we..." Qingqing took a deep breath, "just surrender?"

"Surrender?" A-Xin froze.

"He's a teacher," Qingqing said, "and we're not really criminals."

"..." A-Xin was silent for a second. Then, he abruptly stood up and shouted at the playground below:

"Teacher Gao! We surrender! We'll never hack you again!"

On the playground, Teacher Gao's figure stopped.

He stood still, his blood-red eyes slowly returning to their original black, the mechanical lining behind him "whooshing" back shut, restoring his immaculate suit.

"...Very good." Teacher Gao spoke, his tone returning to its usual flatness. "'Anti-hack drill,' successfully concluded."

"Drill?!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "Our class's Plan No. 7—'Hack Teacher Gao'—was predicted by me in advance. Therefore, I activated 'anti-hack mode' and conducted a live combat exercise."

"You... you knew all along?!" A-Xin couldn't believe it.

"I knew you were planning to hack." Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses. "Because I can hear all your after-school meetings."

"—!!" A-Xin stood frozen on the spot.

"However," Teacher Gao shifted tack, his tone softening a little, "Liu-Liu, the data cable you 'plugged' into my system just now helped me complete a 'firmware update'—"

He turned to Liu-Liu: "So, as a return favor, I upgraded you."

"Upgraded?" Liu-Liu poked its head out of the backpack, blinking its electronic eyes.

"I installed the 'textbook database' and 'study assistance mode' for you." Teacher Gao said. "From now on, you can consult the textbook content at any time and help your classmates review."

"Textbook database?!" Liu-Liu's electronic eyes instantly blazed like two little suns. "Beep beep beep! I, I can recite Tang poetry now!"

"Three Hundred Tang Poems," Teacher Gao added, "which one can you recite up to?"

Liu-Liu was taken aback, then opened its mouth and recited word by word:

"Moonlight before my bed... I suspect it's frost on the ground..."

"Very good." Teacher Gao nodded. "This is the meaning of 'upgrading.'"

A-Xin stood on the eave, watching this scene, his feelings complicated.

"Teacher Gao," he suddenly spoke, "why did you... install the textbook database into Liu-Liu's body?"

"Because," Teacher Gao smiled faintly, "'answers' shouldn't be acquired by 'stealing.' They should be acquired by—'reading.'"

"..." A-Xin was silent for a long, long time.

Finally, he lowered his head, his voice a little hoarse:

"...Teacher Gao... do we... really not need to cheat?"

Teacher Gao didn't answer directly. He only raised his head, looking at A-Xin, his tone gentle:

"A-Xin, the day you stop asking this question—is the day you truly don't need to cheat anymore."

The deep-night campus returned to quiet.

A-Xin stood on the eave, looking down at that immaculate figure below, lost in thought.

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay in A-Xin's backpack, having finished one Tang poem, and started on the second:

"At noon I hoe in the fields... sweat drips on the soil..."

"Liu-Liu," A-Xin said quietly, "stop reciting."

"Beep?" Liu-Liu tilted its head.

"I've realized," A-Xin murmured, "the Tang poems you recite are more useful than the final exam answers."
