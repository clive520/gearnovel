# *The Whole Class Is Cheating*

## Chapter 14: The Night of the Cheating Rehearsal

The night before the final exam, at seven o'clock, the classroom of Class 6-1 was brightly lit.

Twenty-six desks had been pulled into rows, like a formal exam room. A-Xin stood on the podium, chalk in hand, scanning the whole class with a solemn expression:

"Everyone! Tomorrow is the final exam! Today, we do our last—'cheating rehearsal'!"

"Rehearsal?" Old Wu chewed his pork bun, full of question marks. "Rehearse what?"

"Rehearse tomorrow's 'telepathy piecing grand operation'!" A-Xin "slapped" a few large words onto the blackboard. "Phase one: the opening! Phase two: sensing! Phase three: piecing!"

"..." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "A-Xin, do you think 'telepathy' can really be rehearsed?"

"Of course!" A-Xin said firmly. "As long as we practice repeatedly, our coordination will come out!"

"..." Qingqing sighed. "...Fine. Then let's begin the rehearsal."

"Good! Rehearsal begins!" A-Xin slapped the table. "Phase one—'the opening'! Everyone! Sit!"

The whole class sat up straight in unison, like soldiers about to go to war.

"Phase two—'sensing'!" A-Xin took a deep breath and closed his eyes. "I want to sense—Old Wu, Taiwan geography, next to 'Alishan' is what mountain?"

He concentrated, eyes closed, "sensing" for a long while, then abruptly opened his eyes and shouted:

"It's 'Chicken Mountain'! No... it's 'Meat Mountain'! No wait—it's 'Drumstick Lake'!"

"..." Old Wu froze. "A-Xin, what did you sense? That's my 'recipe memory'!"

"Your memory has been polluted by drumsticks!" A-Xin stamped his foot. "Again!"

"My turn!" Old Wu stood up excitedly, closing his eyes. "I want to sense—Wang Zhuankai, plants, what conditions does 'photosynthesis' require!"

He closed his eyes, "sensing" hard for three seconds, then blurted out:

"Light! And... and... drumsticks!"

"..." Wang Zhuankai looked at him speechlessly. "Old Wu, does everything you think of lead back to drumsticks?"

"I can't help it!" Old Wu said aggrievedly. "I'm hungry!"

"..." A-Xin held his forehead. "Fine! Next—Lin Yunyun, you sense A-Xin's math!"

Lin Yunyun closed her eyes, frowned, and "sensed" hard for a long while, then weakly spoke:

"I... I sense... 'pi pi pi'... 'round round round'..."

"That's my rap!" A-Xin wailed. "Why are you sensing my rap?!"

"Because you recite it every day!" Lin Yunyun added aggrievedly.

"..." A-Xin stood frozen on the spot, and only after a long while slowly let out a breath. "...Fine. It seems the 'telepathy' system has completely failed."

"A-Xin," Qingqing stood up, her tone calm, "there's something I don't know whether to say."

"Go ahead."

"Did you all notice," Qingqing scanned the whole class, "that during the rehearsal just now... everyone actually knew the answers?"

"Knew?" A-Xin froze.

"Just now I 'sensed' 'Alishan,' 'photosynthesis,' 'πr²,'" Qingqing said, "you didn't 'sense' them—you 'already knew' them."

"!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed.

"Let me prove it to you." Qingqing turned to Old Wu. "Old Wu, now, write the 'Jianan Plain' in Taiwan geography directly on the exam paper."

"Write directly?" Old Wu froze. "But... don't we need to 'sense' it?"

"Just write it." Qingqing said.

Old Wu hesitated for a moment, picked up his pen, and neatly wrote on the paper:

"The Jianan Plain, located in southwestern Taiwan, is an important agricultural region."

"..." A-Xin leaned in to look, his pupils instantly dilating. "Old Wu! How do you know how to write this?!"

"I, I don't know either!" Old Wu was stunned too. "I just... suddenly knew how to write it?"

"Because you've memorized it for a week." Qingqing said. "The 'Taiwan geography' you're in charge of—you memorized it long ago."

"But..." A-Xin stammered, "what about 'photosynthesis,' which he doesn't know?"

"Old Wu," Qingqing turned, "write it: what conditions does 'photosynthesis' require?"

Old Wu picked up his pen, hesitated for a moment, then wrote:

"Photosynthesis requires light and chlorophyll, converting carbon dioxide and water into oxygen and glucose."

"—!!" A-Xin stared wide-eyed. "You even know photosynthesis?! Isn't that Wang Zhuankai's section?!"

"Because... I hear Wang Zhuankai recite it every day..." Old Wu was startled himself. "I, I listened for a week, and then... I remembered it?"

"..." A-Xin stood frozen on the spot.

"And me!" Lin Yunyun raised her hand excitedly. "I just realized I can even write 'circle area'! Because I hear A-Xin recite his rap every day!"

"Me too!" Wang Zhuankai chimed in. "I, I seem to know Taiwan geography too! Because Old Wu shouts 'Chicken Mountain' 'Meat Mountain' every day!"

"..." A-Xin stood still, stunned for a long, long time.

He lowered his head, looking at his own "mock exam paper," and slowly, slowly, picked up his pen.

Question one, Taiwan geography—he could write it.

Question two, photosynthesis—he could write it too.

Question three, circle area—he could write it even more easily.

Question four, question five, question six...

He discovered that every single question on the whole paper, he could write all of them.

"..." A-Xin's hand stopped midair, the pen tip trembling slightly.

"A-Xin," Qingqing walked over, her tone unusually gentle, "have you noticed that we spent this whole night rehearsing 'how to cheat'—and the result, we wrote the entire exam paper 'directly'?"

"..."

"Our 'telepathy' failed." Qingqing said. "But our 'exam paper'—succeeded."

"..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing asked softly, "why don't we just write directly?"

"—!"

Those words, like a bolt of lightning, struck straight into A-Xin's heart.

He opened his mouth, yet couldn't produce a single word.

The classroom was utterly silent.

Twenty-six people, looking at each other, a kind of indefinable complexity in everyone's eyes.

"Because..." Old Wu spoke weakly, "we thought... we had to cheat..."

"But we..." Wang Zhuankai continued, "seem to... not need to?"

"..." A-Xin lowered his head, his hand holding the pen slowly loosening.

He looked at that fully written exam paper, looked at those twenty-six questions that "could all be written," and for the first time, fell silent.

"Slap."

"Slap."

"Slap."

The classroom door was suddenly pushed open.

"It's this late, why haven't you gone home?"

Director Zhou Yan stood at the door, his hawk-sharp gaze sweeping across the room, then his eyes fell on the stack of fully written exam papers on the table—

"What... is this?!"

He rushed over in a single bound, grabbed a paper, stared wide-eyed: "You're stealing the exam paper?!"

"No! Those are our own mock questions!" A-Xin explained hastily.

"Your own?" Director Zhou glared at him. "Setting your own questions and writing your own answers? Then what are you writing these for?"

"Because..." A-Xin froze, and for a moment, didn't know how to answer.

"Because they're rehearsing tomorrow's final exam." Teacher Gao Zhixian appeared behind Director Zhou without a sound, his tone flat. "Director Zhou, this is a 'pre-exam simulation.' The whole class of twenty-six, setting their own questions and answering their own papers, checking their review results."

"..." Director Zhou looked down at the neatly written answers on the paper, then at the group of students soaked in sweat, and finally fell silent.

"You..." he murmured, "you trouble-makers... actually... set your own questions... and test yourselves?"

"Yes." Teacher Gao nodded. "This is 'self-directed learning.'"

"..." Director Zhou's mouth twitched, and finally, he put the paper down and grumbled, "...Hmph! If there's even one wrong answer on that paper, you'll know what's what tomorrow!"

He swept away, leaving the classroom in silence.

Teacher Gao didn't leave. He walked to A-Xin, lowered his head, looked at the fully written exam paper, his tone gentle:

"A-Xin, can you finish writing it?"

"...I can finish." A-Xin said quietly. "I can write... all of it."

"Very good." Teacher Gao smiled faintly. "Then tomorrow, you'll just—'write directly.'"

"..." A-Xin raised his head, watching Teacher Gao's retreating figure, and was silent for a long time.

Late at night, the classroom light finally went out.

The twenty-six filed out one by one, leaving only A-Xin standing alone in the empty classroom.

He looked at the fully written exam paper and murmured to himself:

"Why... don't we just write directly?"

Outside the window, Liu-Liu lay at the ventilation duct, its tail swaying gently, a tender light flickering in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep... detecting... 'tomorrow morning'... will be... 'the finest exam'..."
