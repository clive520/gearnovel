# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 20: The Extreme Evidence-Gathering Operation

The evidence Liu-Liu had delivered caused quite a stir at the environmental protection bureau.

"Confirmed! These photos and data match the tip we received!" In the bureau's meeting room, the inspection team leader slammed the table, "This 'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory' definitely has a problem!"

"Team leader," an inspector frowned, "but last time we inspected them, they buried the pipe and we found nothing."

"This time is different." The team leader said, "This time, we have the children's 'simulated route map'! We know where their hidden pipe is!"

"Then we..."

"Not so fast." The team leader wagged his finger, "To catch them in the act, we must obtain evidence that they're 'discharging right now.' We're going to..." a sharp light flickered in his eyes, "raid!"

"Raid?"

"Yes! A night raid!" The team leader said, "The children's monitoring data shows that the hidden pipe discharges at night, concentrated in the dead of night. We'll catch them while they're discharging!"

"But..." the inspector hesitated, "that child mentioned an 'underground storage tank,' but we don't have a map..."

"We do." The team leader opened Ke Zhiyuan's simulated route map, "This map is drawn in great detail. This child..." he looked at the annotations on the map, "can come work at our unit in the future."

However, the bureau's "raid plan" was learned of by someone first.

"Boss! The environmental protection bureau is up to something!" Late at night, in the office of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory, a man in black leaned close to Wang Fugui's ear, "They seem to be planning a 'surprise inspection'!"

"Surprise inspection?" Wang Fugui's face darkened, "How did they know..."

"I heard..." the man in black lowered his voice, "it was the kids from that school who delivered the evidence to the bureau."

"Those brats?!" Wang Fugui stood up abruptly, "Them again!"

"Boss, what do we do?"

"What do we do?" Wang Fugui sneered, "They want to raid, so we'll..." he thought for a moment, "change the discharge time to daytime."

"Daytime?!" The man in black was stunned, "Isn't daytime easier to get caught?"

"The most dangerous place is the safest." Wang Fugui said smugly, "They think we only discharge at night, so we'll discharge in the daytime! When they come to raid at night... all they'll see is a 'clean' ditch!"

"Brilliant! Truly brilliant, Boss!" The man in black flattered, giving a thumbs up.

"And," a hint of ruthlessness flashed in Wang Fugui's eyes, "have someone watch that school. If those brats try any more tricks..."

"Your meaning, Boss?"

"Scare their parents a little." Wang Fugui said, "Make those brats know that meddling in other people's business has no good outcome."

"Understood!"

The night grew deeper.

And the children didn't yet know that a bigger storm was approaching.

"Weixi," the next morning, Qingqing noticed that Weixi's face had grown paler again, "did you not sleep well last night?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded softly, "Last night... I 'saw' something again."

"What did you see?"

"I saw..." Weixi said in a low voice, "a heavy rain."

"Heavy rain?" Qingqing froze.

"Mhm." Weixi said, "A very heavy rain, washing that drainage ditch completely clean."

"Isn't that good?" A-Xin leaned in, "A heavy rain, washing the ditch clean!"

"But..." Weixi shook her head, unease in her eyes, "the 'rain' I saw... was strange. Its color..."

"Its color?"

"It was grayish-black." Weixi said, "I've never seen rain that color."

"Grayish-black rain..." The children looked at each other.

"Grayish-black rain..." A-Xin tilted his head, "Does such rain exist?"

"I remember," Wang Zhuankai raised his hand, "the TV news reported it once! One time, somewhere got a 'black rain,' and it turned out to be black smoke from factories mixing into the rain!"

"Factory black smoke..." Qingqing's face changed slightly, "That would be..."

"It's right here." Weixi said in a low voice, "The smokestacks of the Taichung Thermal Power Plant, and... the factory's smokestacks. If their discharge keeps increasing..."

"The rain will turn black?" Lin Yunyun asked fearfully.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "In the 'stories of the future' I've heard... black rain once fell. After that rain, many people's throats began to hurt."

"Then... then we..." The children's faces all turned pale.

"So," Weixi took a deep breath, "we have to find out the truth, and make those smokestacks... stop."

"Right!" A-Xin said loudly, "Make them stop!"

"Make them stop!" The children said in unison.

"But, Weixi," Qingqing looked at her worriedly, "your body..."

"It's okay." Weixi smiled, "As long as you're all by my side, I can... keep going."

"Weixi..." The children's eyes reddened.

"So," Weixi looked at everyone, speaking softly, "let's make sure that 'black rain'... never falls."

"Okay—!" The children responded loudly.

"Weixi," Qingqing asked worriedly, "are you... worried about something again?"

"I don't know." Weixi lowered her head, "I just feel... like something is about to happen."

"No matter what happens," A-Xin said loudly, "we'll face it together!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

Just then, Liu-Liu suddenly burst out of the ventilation duct in a hurry:

"Beep beep! Bad news! Big bad news!"

"What's wrong?" The children asked nervously.

"I just sneaked into the industrial zone to scout," Liu-Liu panted, "and found... that hidden pipe is discharging right now!"

"Right now?!" A-Xin's eyes went wide, "In broad daylight?!"

"Yes!" Liu-Liu nodded, "And the amount being discharged is even larger than at night!"

"Why would they discharge in the daytime?" Qingqing frowned, "That doesn't make sense..."

"Unless..." Weixi suddenly spoke, her voice soft yet silencing the classroom, "they know the environmental protection bureau is planning a surprise inspection."

"What?!" The children gasped.

"They changed the discharge time to daytime." Weixi said, "That way, when the bureau raids at night... they won't find anything."

"How cunning!" A-Xin stamped his foot, "Boss Wang, he's really..."

"And," Liu-Liu added, "they've also sent people to watch the school!"

"Watch the school?" The children's faces changed.

"Yes!" Liu-Liu said, "At the school gate, the side gate—there are their people everywhere!"

"They're watching us!" Qingqing's face darkened.

"Then we..." Lin Yunyun asked fearfully, "can we... do nothing anymore?"

"Who said that!" A-Xin said loudly, "If they want to watch, we'll..." his eyes suddenly lit up, "we'll act 'secretly'!"

"Secretly?"

"Yes!" A-Xin lowered his voice, "They're watching the 'school.' So we'll... 'act from outside the school'!"

"Outside the school?" The children were puzzled.

"We can, after school, circle around to the other side of the industrial zone!" A-Xin said, "They're watching the school; they won't think we'd come from another direction into the industrial zone!"

"But..." Qingqing hesitated, "the other side of the industrial zone is a wall..."

"The wall," Old Wu stood up, patting his shoulder, "is my job!"

"Old Wu..." The children looked at him.

"I'm the 'human shield'!" Old Wu said loudly, "Have you forgotten? 'Protect Earth Operations Unit · Full Deployment,' my mission is to lead everyone through the obstacles!"

"Right!" A-Xin slapped the table, "'Extreme Evidence-Gathering Operation' officially begins!"

"Extreme Evidence-Gathering Operation?" The children's eyes lit up.

"Our mission," A-Xin announced loudly, "is to capture proof of them 'discharging right now' during Boss Wang's 'daytime discharge'! Then—hand it to the environmental protection bureau!"

"Okay—!" The children shouted in unison.

"Mission assignments!" A-Xin jumped onto the podium, "Liu-Liu, aerial cover and reconnaissance! Old Wu, breakthrough and rearguard! Weixi, use the bracelet to locate the hidden pipe! Qingqing, filming! Ke Zhiyuan, recording the time!"

"What about me? What about me?" Wang Zhuankai raised his hand.

"You," A-Xin thought for a moment, "you're in charge of... 'the decoy'!"

"The decoy?"

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "You have the lowest laughing threshold and the loudest voice! Outside the industrial zone, you pretend to be 'clueless'! When you see a guard, shout: 'Oh no! I dropped my wallet!' Then pretend to look for it and lead them away!"

"Pfft!" Wang Zhuankai laughed, "This mission... I seem to be born for it!"

"And us!" Lin Yunyun and a few girls raised their hands, "What can we do?"

"You," A-Xin looked at them, "are in charge of 'logistics'! Prepare masks, gloves, drinking water, and most importantly... your courage!"

"Our courage?" The girls were puzzled.

"Yes!" A-Xin said seriously, "If we get caught, you'll be responsible for... bringing the evidence back to school!"

"We..." Lin Yunyun took a deep breath, "we can do it!"

"Great!" A-Xin extended his hand, "'Extreme Evidence-Gathering Operation,' move out!"

"Okay—!"

That day after school, the children didn't walk through the school gate.

They climbed over the wall behind the school, circled past the front of the industrial zone, and crept quietly along a weed-filled path toward the industrial zone's side.

"Five hundred meters ahead is the factory's side wall!" Liu-Liu conducted aerial reconnaissance, "On the other side of the wall is the underground storage tank!"

"Good!" A-Xin said in a low voice, "Old Wu, take position!"

"In position!" Old Wu crouched by the side wall and patted his shoulder, "Come!"

The children stepped on Old Wu's shoulder, one by one, climbing over the side wall.

"Phew..." A-Xin landed softly, "Is this... the factory's backyard?"

"Careful!" Liu-Liu warned in a low voice, "There's a guard up ahead!"

"I'll handle it!" Wang Zhuankai took a deep breath, strode out, and shouted at the distant guard: "Oh no! I dropped my wallet! My wallet! Has anyone seen my wallet?!"

"Who's there?!" The guard, hearing the noise, walked toward Wang Zhuankai.

"Now!" A-Xin waved his hand, "Liu-Liu, go! Weixi, locate!"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu flew silently toward the sky above the storage tank.

"Found it!" Weixi closed her eyes, the bracelet emitting a faint blue light, "The hidden pipe... is below the storage tank!"

"Film!" Qingqing raised her phone, aiming at the storage tank, "Hidden pipe entrance... captured!"

"Record the time!" Ke Zhiyuan pressed the stopwatch, "4:48 PM! Discharging!"

"And a water sample!" A-Xin crouched down and carefully filled a small bottle at the hidden pipe's outlet, "Ironclad evidence, secured!"

"Great!" The children, keeping their voices low, high-fived each other excitedly.

"Beep beep beep!" Liu-Liu flew back, "The guard's been lured away by Wang Zhuankai! Let's go!"

"Go!" A-Xin commanded, "Back the way we came!"

The children, crouching, retreated quickly toward the side wall.

However—

"Thump!"

A blinding searchlight suddenly shone directly from the direction of the side wall!

"Stop right there!"

A hoarse voice rang out in the darkness.

"It's Boss Wang!" Liu-Liu shouted in panic, "He came himself!"

"Boss Wang?!" The children's faces instantly turned deathly pale.

"Hahaha!" Wang Fugui emerged from the darkness, followed by two guards in black, "I was wondering who it was. It's you brats!"

"B-Boss Wang..." A-Xin shielded the children, "we..."

"We what?" Wang Fugui sneered, closing in step by step, "In the middle of the night, sneaking into my factory's backyard... what do you call this?"

"We are..." A-Xin took a deep breath, "we are the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'! We came to..."

"Came to what?" Wang Fugui interrupted him, his eyes dark, "To secretly film? To make trouble for me?"

"We're not making trouble for you!" Qingqing said loudly, "We came to make this drainage ditch clean again!"

"Clean again?" Wang Fugui snorted, "Little girl, the 'clean' of the adult world isn't that simple. You brats should just go home and study!"

"We're not going back!" A-Xin said loudly, "We have evidence!"

"Evidence?" A murderous glint flashed in Wang Fugui's eyes, "Where is this 'evidence' of yours?"

"..." A-Xin protected the water sample in his arms and said nothing.

"Hand over the evidence." Wang Fugui extended his hand, "I'll pretend nothing happened. Otherwise..."

"Otherwise what?" A-Xin asked.

"Otherwise..." Wang Fugui smiled, but his tone was cold as ice, "I'll take your school to court. Your teachers, your principal—all of them will be in big trouble because of you brats!"

"You..." The children were shocked and furious.

"Hand it over." Wang Fugui stepped forward, "Don't make me say it a third time."

"..." The children looked at each other.

Just then—

"Beep beep beep beep—!!!"

Liu-Liu suddenly shot up into the sky, emitting a piercing alarm!

"Smoke! Smoke!" Liu-Liu shouted, "The factory's smokestacks are on fire!"

"What?!" Wang Fugui whirled around, looking toward the smokestacks, "On fire?!"

"Now!" A-Xin shouted, "Run!"

Taking advantage of Wang Fugui's distraction, the children bolted!

"Chase them! Chase them!" Wang Fugui roared furiously.

"Run!" Old Wu stayed in the rear, "I'll hold them off!"

"Old Wu!" The children shouted.

"Run! Protect the evidence!" Old Wu spread his arms wide, blocking the pursuers, "Come on! You adults! Get past me first!"

"Get out of the way!" A guard reached out to shove Old Wu.

"No way!" Old Wu grabbed the guard's hand, using all his strength, "I'm the one... who carried the school's heaviest leftover food barrel!"

"How is this kid so strong!" The guard exclaimed.

"Old Wu! We got out!" A-Xin shouted from the other side of the wall, "Hurry over!"

"I'm coming!" Old Wu flung the guard aside, scrambled and rolled over the side wall.

"Thump!" The children collapsed into a heap, but couldn't help laughing.

"We... we did it?" Lin Yunyun asked breathlessly.

"We did it!" Qingqing raised her phone, "The hidden pipe discharging, the water sample, the time record... everything secured!"

"Ironclad evidence!" A-Xin said loudly, "This time, it's ironclad evidence!"

"But..." Weixi suddenly spoke, her voice soft, "Boss Wang won't let this go."

"..." The children fell silent.

"He just said he'd take the school to court..." Qingqing said in a low voice.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "So we have to hurry. Before he 'strikes'... hand the evidence to the environmental protection bureau."

"Right!" A-Xin clenched his fist, "Let's go now!"

"But," Lin Yunyun asked worriedly, "there are his people watching the school..."

"It's okay." Weixi looked into the distance and smiled faintly, "We're no longer at school."

"Right!" A-Xin said loudly, "They're watching the school! We... are already out!"

"Then we..." The children's eyes sparkled, "go straight to the environmental protection bureau?"

"Straight to the bureau!" The children shouted in unison.

"Move out!" A-Xin waved his hand grandly.

Under the setting sun, the silhouettes of twenty-six children marched in a grand procession toward the environmental protection bureau.

"Even bigger surprise?" A-Xin kept mulling over those words on the way home after school, "What is Boss Wang planning to do?"

"A-Xin, you're still thinking about that?" Old Wu, chewing a pork bun, walked beside him.

"Mhm." A-Xin nodded, "I have a feeling Boss Wang won't let this go."

"Then what can we do?" Old Wu asked.

"We'll meet the situation as it comes!" A-Xin clenched his fist, "Anyway, we have evidence!"

"But..." Old Wu hesitated, "he said he'd take the school to court..."

"Then let him sue!" A-Xin said loudly, "We have evidence! We're not afraid!"

"A-Xin..." Old Wu looked at him, then smiled honestly, "you really are our 'King of Ideas'!"

"Of course!" A-Xin puffed out his chest, "I'm..."

"The captain of the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'!" Old Wu finished for him.

"Hahaha!" The two children, laughing and playing under the setting sun, ran toward home.

And in the place they couldn't see, in the office of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory, Wang Fugui was making a phone call:

"Hello? Is this Attorney Zhang? This is Wang Fugui."

"Yes, it's me."

"I'd like you to... write me a 'certified letter.'"

"The addressee?" A cold smile curled at the corner of Wang Fugui's mouth, "Luyang Elementary."

"Yes. Say... they allowed students to break into my factory and damage my equipment!"

"Hahaha!" Wang Fugui laughed heartily, "Let's see how long those brats can hold out!"

The phone hung up.

In the deep night, the factory was still discharging grayish-green wastewater.

In their wake, Wang Fugui stood by the factory's side wall, his face ashen.

"...Brat." He gritted his teeth, squeezing out two words.

"Boss," the man in black leaned in, "should we..."

"No need." Wang Fugui smiled darkly, "They want evidence, right? Then we'll..." he looked toward the factory, "give them a 'bigger surprise.'"

The night grew deeper.

The children, carrying the evidence, arrived at the environmental protection bureau.

"It's you again?" The bureau's guard recognized Liu-Liu, "Why are you back again?"

"Guard uncle!" A-Xin said loudly, "We have ironclad evidence! This time it's ironclad!"

"Ironclad?" The guard froze, "What ironclad evidence?"

"Footage of the hidden pipe discharging! A water sample! Time records!" Qingqing laid out the evidence one by one, "It's all here!"

"..." The guard looked at the evidence, his expression slowly turning grave, "You... wait a moment. I'll call the team leader!"

"Team leader!" The guard hurried into the office area, "There's a group of elementary school students outside with evidence!"

"Elementary school students?" The inspection team leader came out, "Them again?"

"Team leader!" A-Xin raised the evidence in his hand, "We filmed it! Hongyuan Food Processing Factory is discharging! In broad daylight! Right now!"

"What?!" The team leader's face changed, "Daytime?"

"Yes!" Qingqing added, "They changed the discharge time to daytime! If you raid at night... you won't find anything!"

"..." The team leader was silent for a moment, then picked up his phone, "Everyone, assemble! Move out immediately! Target—Hongyuan Food Processing Factory!"

"Right now?!" The inspectors were stunned.

"Right now!" The team leader said loudly, "Let those people see what a 'surprise inspection' really is!"

"Okay—!"

The bureau's inspection vehicles, one after another, roared out.

The children stood at the bureau's entrance, watching the vehicles drive away, then looked at each other—

"Yay—!!!" The children erupted in thunderous cheers!

"We did it!" A-Xin's tears streamed down, "We really did it!"

"Mhm!" Qingqing nodded hard, her eyes reddened too, "We did it!"

"Weixi," Lin Yunyun turned to look at Weixi, "did you see? We..."

"..." Weixi didn't answer.

She stood in the middle of the children, her face abnormally pale.

"Weixi?" Qingqing noticed her change, "What's wrong?!"

"I..." Weixi's body suddenly swayed, "my bracelet..."

"The bracelet?!" The children looked down—

On the bracelet on Weixi's wrist, the countdown number was dropping, one digit at a time, at a visibly fast rate!

"Ten, nine, eight..."

"Weixi!!!" The children gathered around her in panic, "What's happening?! Why is it—"

"I..." Weixi weakly held her forehead, "when I used the bracelet to locate the hidden pipe just now... I consumed too much energy..."

"Then..." A-Xin's voice was trembling, "then your time..."

Weixi lowered her head and looked at the bracelet.

The number stopped at—eight.

"Eight days..." Weixi said softly, "still enough."

"Enough for what!" A-Xin's tears surged up, "Eight days... what can eight days do!"

"Eight days," Weixi smiled faintly, a satisfaction never seen before in her eyes, "is enough for us... to see that drainage ditch become clear again."

"Weixi..." The children cried together.

"Don't cry," Weixi said softly, "we succeeded... we really succeeded..."

"Mhm!" The children cried, but nodded hard, "We succeeded!"

"So," Weixi looked into the distance, toward the direction the inspection vehicles had gone, "the rest is up to the adults."

"Mhm!" The children said, "Leave it to the adults!"

Under the night sky, twenty-six children stood quietly at the bureau's entrance.

Waiting for the miracle that belonged to them.
