# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 21: Exposure & Rescue

That night, the environmental protection bureau's inspection vehicles stopped at the gate of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory.

"Inspection! Open the door now!" The inspection team leader pounded on the factory's main gate.

"Coming, coming!" The factory's guard, still groggy from sleep, opened the door. The moment he saw the inspectors, his face changed, "Th-this... it's so late, you..."

"We received a tip that your factory is suspected of illegally discharging wastewater!" The team leader flashed his inspection certificate, "This is a search warrant! Please cooperate!"

"I... I'll go call the boss!" The guard hurried toward the office in a panic.

"No need to call him." The team leader strode into the factory, his gaze sweeping across the premises, "We'll find him ourselves."

He led the inspectors straight to the underground storage tank in the factory's backyard.

"Found it!" The inspector's flashlight shone below the storage tank, "A hidden pipe! It's still discharging!"

"Grayish-green liquid..." The team leader crouched down and sniffed, "Exactly the same as the water in the drainage ditch!"

"Film it!" "Collect a water sample!" "Record the time!"

The inspectors, methodically, carried out the evidence collection.

"You!" Wang Fugui arrived, panting, "This is trespassing!"

"Mr. Wang," the team leader stood up, holding up the water sample, "This is wastewater collected from the hidden pipe under your factory's underground storage tank. Preliminary testing shows heavy metals over the limit."

"Th-that..." cold sweat broke out on Wang Fugui's forehead, "that has nothing to do with me! That must be..."

"Must be what?" The team leader looked straight at him, "Mr. Wang, last time you said it was 'residue from an old pipe.' What about this time?"

"I..." Wang Fugui's mouth opened and closed, but not a word came out.

"Mr. Wang," the team leader produced a seal, "under the Water Pollution Control Act, your factory is suspected of illegally discharging wastewater with heavy metals over the limit. I now officially declare—"

"Your factory is ordered to suspend operations, effective immediately!"

"Suspension?!" Wang Fugui's face instantly turned deathly pale, "You can't do this! I... I have a lawyer!"

"You have a lawyer," the team leader said coldly, "and we have evidence. In court, let the lawyer and the evidence 'chat' slowly."

"You..." Wang Fugui collapsed to the ground.

"And," the team leader added, "we've also recorded how you sent people to watch the school these past few days. Suspected intimidation of schoolchildren—we'll add that to the charges too."

"..." Wang Fugui could no longer say a word.

"Take him away." The team leader waved his hand to the inspectors behind him.

"What are you doing?!" Wang Fugui panicked, "I'm... I'm a model entrepreneur!"

"A model entrepreneur?" The team leader sneered, "Would a model entrepreneur dump heavy-metal wastewater into a ditch next to a school?"

"I..." Wang Fugui was speechless.

"And," the team leader pulled a stack of photos from his pocket, "these are photos of your people watching the school. Dean of Student Affairs Mr. Zhou sent them to our unit last night."

"Zhou..." Wang Fugui's face grew even paler.

"Oh, right," the team leader added, "Dean Zhou said that in his twenty years as dean, this is the first time he's seen a group of children risk everything to protect the school's water. He said..." the team leader paused, "'If you can't deal with him, he'll take the children and go to the media himself.'"

"..." Wang Fugui slumped to the ground, unable to say a word.

"Take him away." The team leader ordered again.

"Yes!" The inspectors lifted Wang Fugui and carried him toward the police car.

"I won't accept this!" Wang Fugui shouted as he was dragged away, "I won't accept this! Those brats... what right do those brats have..."

"What right?" The team leader watched his back and said flatly, "They have the right because they know better than you what 'the right thing' is."

The police car roared away.

The factory's main gate was sealed with a closure order.

"Team leader!" An inspector walked over, "What do we do with the wastewater in the storage tank?"

"Pump it dry! Purify it!" The team leader said, "Not a single drop is allowed to flow into the sea."

"Yes!"

That night, the lights of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory never came on again.

The white smoke from the smokestacks stopped, for the first time, forever.

"Team leader! Team leader!" Early the next morning, A-Xin charged into the classroom of Class 6-1, shouting excitedly, "The environmental protection bureau's website! It's announced!"

"Announced?" The children gathered around.

"Look!" A-Xin held his phone up high, "'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory, suspected of illegally discharging wastewater, heavy metals at 6.3 times the limit, ordered to suspend operations by law, and referred for prosecution!'"

"Ordered to suspend operations!" The children erupted in thunderous cheers, "Referred for prosecution!"

"We did it!" Old Wu's tears streamed down, "We really did it!"

"That Boss Wang..." Lin Yunyun murmured, "won't be able to secretly discharge anymore, right?"

"He won't." Qingqing nodded, "The hidden pipe is sealed, the storage tank is sealed, and the factory is suspended. He..."

"He's finished!" A-Xin continued loudly, "This is what they call 'reaping what you sow'!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed together.

"Weixi!" A-Xin turned to look at Weixi, "Did you see that?! We succeeded!"

"..." Weixi stood by the window, quietly looking into the distance.

Her eyes reddened slightly.

"I saw it." she said softly, "I saw it."

"That one smokestack..." she said in a low voice, "has stopped."

"Right!" A-Xin said loudly, "It's stopped!"

"Thank you." Weixi turned around, bowed deeply to the whole class, "Thank you... for making that smokestack stop."

"Weixi..." The children froze.

"This is..." Weixi looked up, tears glistening in her eyes, "the most beautiful sight I've ever seen."

"The most beautiful sight?" The children asked.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "In my memories... the smokestacks never stopped."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But today," Weixi smiled faintly, "I saw it. So... smokestacks can stop."

"Because of you," she looked at the children, "you turned 'impossible' into 'possible.'"

"Weixi..." Lin Yunyun's eyes suddenly turned red, "don't say that... we..."

"We were just..." A-Xin rubbed his nose, "we were just doing what needed to be done."

"Right." Qingqing nodded, "What needed to be done."

"What needed to be done..." Weixi repeated those four words, and smiled softly.

Just then, the school's broadcast suddenly sounded:

"Attention, all teachers and students—"

"The air quality in the area around our school has dropped from 'purple explosion' level to 'normal' level!"

"The school's drinking fountains have resumed normal water supply!"

"The water quality of the drainage ditch behind the school has returned to normal range!"

"The air pollution has dropped!" "The drinking fountains are back!" "The drainage ditch is normal!"

"Yay—!"

In the classroom, the children's cheers lasted for a long, long time.

"Alright, alright," a light cough suddenly sounded at the door, "so happy?"

"Dean!" The children jumped in fright and instantly fell silent.

"I came today," Dean Zhou walked into the classroom, his tone flat, "not to catch you making noise."

"Then the Dean is..." A-Xin asked cautiously.

"I came to tell you," Dean Zhou paused, "that the matter of 'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory' is already on the news."

"On the news?!" The children's eyes went wide.

"Mhm." Dean Zhou nodded, "The school's report, plus the environmental protection bureau's announcement... now, all of Taiwan knows."

"All of Taiwan..." The children looked at each other, light shining in their eyes.

"And," Dean Zhou said, "the principal asked me to tell you—'Protect Earth' has officially become the school's 'featured curriculum.' Next year, it will be promoted schoolwide."

"Featured curriculum! Schoolwide promotion!" The children got excited again.

"And," Dean Zhou's expression softened a little, for once, "next week, the education bureau is going to commend you. The principal said you'll go up on stage to receive the award."

"Go on stage for an award?!" A-Xin jumped up, "We're going on stage for an award!"

"But," Dean Zhou put on a stern face, "before the award ceremony, homework still has to be done."

"Hahaha!" The children laughed together.

"Dean!" A-Xin asked loudly, "Then... what about you?"

"What about me?"

"You..." A-Xin thought for a moment, "are you coming on stage with us?"

"..." Dean Zhou froze for a moment, then turned to walk out, "...We'll see."

But the children all saw it.

The moment the Dean turned around, the corner of his mouth lifted slightly.

"The Dean smiled!" Liu-Liu poked its head out of the ventilation duct and announced excitedly, "The Black-Faced Judge smiled!"

"Shut up!" Dean Zhou shouted without turning back, but his steps were noticeably lighter.

"Hahaha!" The children laughed even louder.

"Weixi!" A-Xin turned around and shouted excitedly, "Did you hear that?! E-very-thing is back to normal!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, and her tears finally couldn't be held back, streaming down, "I heard it..."

"Then you..." A-Xin suddenly lowered his head, his voice growing quiet, "are you... going back soon?"

The classroom suddenly fell silent.

Twenty-six pairs of eyes turned to look at Weixi at once.

"..." Weixi lowered her head, silent for a very, very long time.

"Mhm." She finally nodded softly, "Soon."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But," Weixi looked up, showing a gentle smile, "before I go back, I want to..."

"Want to see what?" A-Xin asked.

"Want to see..." Weixi looked out the window, "that drainage ditch become clear again."

"The drainage ditch..." The children looked at each other.

"Alright!" A-Xin said loudly, "Then we'll take you to see it!"

That day after school, twenty-six children, plus Liu-Liu, marched in a grand procession toward the back of the school.

They stood at the mouth of the drainage ditch, looking at that once grayish-green water.

"Look!" Lin Yunyun pointed at the ditch, "The water... has turned clear!"

"Really!" Wang Zhuankai's eyes went wide, "The grayish-green color from before... is gone!"

"And," Liu-Liu circled above the river mouth, reporting excitedly, "the seawater quality has returned to normal too! Fish... the fish are back!"

"Fish?" The children crowded to the river mouth and looked out to sea.

"Look!" Old Wu pointed at the shallows, "Over there! There's a school of little fish!"

"They're really there!" The children exclaimed, "The fish are back!"

"The sea..." Weixi stood on the seawall, looking at that blue sea, her eyes once again welling up.

"A blue sea..." she said in a low voice, "I've finally... seen it with my own eyes."

"Weixi," A-Xin stood beside her and asked, "is the sea your grandmother talked about... like this?"

"..." Weixi looked at the sea before her and was silent for a long time.

"I don't know." she said softly, "Grandmother's photograph has yellowed. But..." she smiled, "I'd guess it looked just like this."

"Then..." Lin Yunyun asked quietly, "when you go back, will you tell your grandmother... that you saw a blue sea?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, light flickering in her eyes, "I'll tell her. I'll tell her... that the blue sea is ten thousand times more beautiful than the photograph."

"Ten thousand times!" Old Wu said loudly, "Then you'll have to say it a lot!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed.

"And," Weixi crouched down, looking at the little fish in the shallows, "I'll tell her that what I saw wasn't just a blue sea, but also... living fish."

"Fish..." The children looked at the school of little fish.

"In my era," Weixi said softly, "there are no fish in the sea."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But here," Weixi smiled, "there are fish. There's a blue sea. There are green trees. There are..."

"There's us!" A-Xin said loudly.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, smiling even wider, "There's you."

"Then..." A-Xin took a deep breath, "you have to remember everything here! Keep it in your heart! Take it back! Tell your grandmother!"

"I will." Weixi nodded hard, "I'll take everything here—the blue sea, the living fish, the green trees, the phoenix blossoms, the pork buns, and you..." she looked at the whole class, "all of it, I'll take back with me."

"Take it back..." The children repeated.

"Because," Weixi said softly, "these... are the most precious treasures of my life."

Under the setting sun, the children stood on the seawall, looking at the sea.

The sea wind blew their hair in disarray.

But in everyone's eyes, there was light.

"A blue sea..." A-Xin stood beside her, looking at the same sea, "It will stay this blue from now on!"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "Because... you're here."

Under the setting sun, the twenty-six children stood on the seawall, looking at that sea which had returned to azure blue.

The sea breeze blew gently.

"By the way, Weixi," A-Xin suddenly remembered something, "how much is left on your bracelet now?"

"..." Weixi lowered her head and looked at the bracelet.

"Six." she said softly.

"Six days..." The children fell silent.

"Six days," Weixi smiled faintly, "is enough for me... to say goodbye to everyone properly."

"Weixi..." The children's eyes reddened.

"Don't cry," Weixi said softly, "we agreed to say goodbye with smiles."

"...Mhm!" The children nodded hard, trying to force smiles.

"Then," A-Xin said loudly, "for the next six days, we're going to—'have fun'!"

"Have fun?" The children froze.

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "For the last six days Weixi is here, we're going to take her out to play! To all the places we love! To eat all the things we love!"

"Okay—!" The children shouted in unison.

"Then," A-Xin looked at Weixi and extended his hand, "Weixi, for these six days, where do you want to go?"

Weixi thought for a moment.

"I want to..." she looked at the children, smiling softly, "have a meal with you."

"A meal?" The children froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "I want to have a proper meal with all of you. Just like..." she lowered her head, "just like the day we first met."

"Alright!" A-Xin said loudly, "Then tomorrow at noon, right here in the classroom, we'll hold a 'pre-graduation lunch party'!"

"A lunch party!"

"Everyone has to bring a dish!" A-Xin announced, "Bring everything you like, everything that's delicious!"

"Okay—!" The children responded in unison.

The next day at noon, the classroom of Class 6-1 became a "gourmet paradise."

"I brought my mom's braised dishes!" "I brought my grandma's rice dumplings!" "I brought the red bean cakes from beside the school!"

"I..." Old Wu proudly presented a huge lunchbox, "I brought... 'Old Wu's Special · Earth-Saving Pork Bun'!"

"A pork bun?!" The children laughed, "You finally made it!"

"Yes!" Old Wu solemnly opened the lunchbox, "This is my pork bun made with 'ugly produce'! Carrots, cabbage, mushrooms... all of them are 'unsellable'!"

"Wow..." The children leaned in and sniffed, "So fragrant!"

"Here!" Old Wu held out the pork bun to Weixi, "Weixi, this one, for you!"

"For... for me?" Weixi froze.

"Yes!" Old Wu said loudly, "I said I'd share half a pork bun with you! Now... I'm sharing a whole one!"

"Old Wu..." Weixi took the pork bun, her eyes reddening slightly.

"Eat it!" Old Wu urged, "While it's hot!"

"Mhm!" Weixi opened her mouth and took a bite...

"Is it good?" Old Wu asked nervously.

"..." Weixi's tears fell without warning.

"It's delicious." she choked, "This is the most delicious pork bun... I've ever eaten."

"That's good!" Old Wu smiled honestly, "Later... if you want to eat more, I'll make it for you again!"

"But..." Weixi lowered her head, "later..."

"Later..." Old Wu's voice choked too, "when you come back, I'll make it for you again! You... you have to come back!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, "I'll come back!"

"A promise is a promise!" A-Xin said loudly.

"A promise is a promise!" The children said in unison.

That noon, twenty-six children gathered around one table, eating all kinds of delicacies, their laughter never stopping.

"By the way," Qingqing suddenly spoke, "Weixi... is there anything else you want to do?"

Weixi thought for a moment, then said: "I... want to go see that phoenix tree."

"The phoenix tree?" The children were puzzled.

"Mhm." Weixi said, "The phoenix tree at the school gate. On my first day at school... it was blooming."

"That tree!" A-Xin laughed, "It's been at the school gate for who knows how many years!"

"I've never seen a tree that blooms." Weixi said in a low voice, "In my era... trees don't bloom."

"..." The children fell silent.

"So," Weixi looked up and smiled, "I want, before I go back, to look at it properly once more."

"Alright!" A-Xin said loudly, "Then tomorrow, we'll go see it together!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded, a satisfaction never seen before in her smile.

That night, Weixi sat alone in the classroom, looking through the window at the phoenix tree at the school gate.

Under the moonlight, the phoenix tree's leaves swayed gently.

"Phoenix tree," she said in a low voice, "the flowers you bloom... are the most beautiful flowers I've ever seen."

"Weixi," a voice sounded from the classroom door.

It was Qingqing.

"Qingqing?" Weixi turned around, "Why haven't you gone home yet?"

"I..." Qingqing walked over and sat down beside her, "I wanted to keep you company for a bit."

"..." Weixi smiled faintly, "Thank you."

"Weixi," Qingqing was silent for a moment, then asked, "after you go back... will you miss us?"

"Yes." Weixi nodded without hesitation, "Every day, every moment, I will."

"Then..." Qingqing lowered her head, "will we... see you again?"

"..." Weixi fell silent.

"I... I don't know." she said softly, "Time travel is one-way. Once I go back... I may never..."

"You will." Qingqing interrupted her, "You will definitely see us again! Because..."

"Because what?"

"Because..." Qingqing looked up, tears glistening in her eyes, "I'll keep writing letters to the future you. One day... you'll receive one of them."

"Qingqing..." Weixi's eyes moistened.

"Then," Qingqing smiled, "the day you receive the letter, you'll remember us. Remember... Luyang Elementary, Class 6-1, the phoenix tree, and..."

"And that sea." Weixi continued, smiling softly.

"Mhm." Qingqing nodded, "And that sea."

"Qingqing," Weixi looked at her seriously, "I promise you—if one day, I really receive your letters... I will definitely write back."

"Really?" Qingqing asked.

"Really." Weixi nodded, "Because..." she smiled faintly, "'letters to the future' do get replies."

"Mhm!" Qingqing nodded hard, "Then I'll keep writing!"

Under the moonlight, the two girls sat side by side in the classroom.

The phoenix tree outside the window swayed gently in the night wind.

"Thank you," she said softly, "thank you for letting me see the most beautiful side of this world."

She lowered her head and looked at the bracelet.

The number on the bracelet flickered quietly in the moonlight.

—Six.

"Six days..." she said in a low voice, "just right."

"Just right enough..." she closed her eyes, "to see this world properly."

That night, Weixi had a dream.

In the dream, she saw the future.

But... that future was different from the one in her memories.

The sky was blue.

The sea was blue.

On the school playground, children were running.

Under the phoenix tree, a little girl was reading a letter.

"This is..." Weixi approached softly in the dream, "A-Xin's letter..."

The letter read:

"To the future Earth: Hello! I'm A-Xin! I promise you, when I grow up, I will pick up every piece of trash on the beach!"

"A-Xin..." Weixi looked at the letter, tears streaming down, "he really... did it."

The future in the dream was bright and shining.

"So..." Weixi smiled softly in the dream, "the future... can be changed."

"So..." she said in a low voice, "my coming back was right."

When she woke up, tears still hung at the corners of Weixi's eyes.

But there was a smile on her lips.

"...Thank you." she said softly to the window, "Thank you for letting me see... that bright future."
