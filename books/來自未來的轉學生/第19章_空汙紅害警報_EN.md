# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 19: The Red Alert for Air Pollution

In the first week of "Full Deployment," the children's morale was high.

"Weixi, we found it!" Ke Zhiyuan ran into the equipment room excitedly, holding up his laptop, "Based on Liu-Liu's reconnaissance data, I drew a 'simulated route map' of that new pipe!"

"Great!" Weixi leaned in to look, "This pipe really does loop around the old pipe's location!"

"And," Ke Zhiyuan added, "my computer simulation shows that where it ends up converging is right near the mouth of the drainage ditch!"

"Then all we have to do is..." A-Xin's eyes lit up, "follow this simulated route to find its 'source'!"

"Right!" Weixi nodded, "We..."

Her words suddenly stopped.

"Weixi?" Qingqing asked nervously, "Are you..."

"I'm... I'm fine." Weixi shook her head, forcing a smile, "It's just... a scene flashed before my eyes just now..."

"What scene?" The children asked nervously.

"A grayish-yellow sky..." Weixi said in a low voice, "on the school playground, no one was playing..."

"..." The children fell silent.

"It's okay," Weixi comforted everyone, "It's just a flashback. Let's continue..."

Actually, that wasn't the first time Weixi had "seen" a flash of the future.

These past two days, her flashbacks had grown more and more frequent.

At mealtimes, she would suddenly stop her chopsticks and stare blankly at the food on the table—because in the flashback, food was rationed, one small bowl per person.

During class, she would suddenly look out the window—because in the flashback, the sky outside the window was grayish-yellow.

After school, she would stand at the school gate for a very, very long time—because in the flashback, there were no phoenix trees at the school gate, only a row of dead trees.

"Weixi," after school one day, Qingqing finally couldn't help asking, "have your flashbacks... been getting more frequent lately?"

Weixi was silent for a moment, then nodded: "Mhm."

"Then..." Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, "your bracelet..."

"It dropped another two digits." Weixi said softly.

"..." Qingqing's fist clenched.

"It's okay." Weixi smiled, "As long..."

"As long as what?"

"As long..." Weixi lowered her head, "as everyone is still by my side, I won't... get lost."

"Weixi..." Qingqing's eyes reddened, "we'll always be here."

"I know." Weixi smiled, "That's why... I dare to keep 'seeing' the future."

But only Qingqing noticed.

When Weixi said "It's just a flashback," she lowered her head and glanced at the bracelet.

The number on the bracelet had jumped from thirteen to twelve.

"Twelve days..." Qingqing silently noted this number in her heart.

Just as the children were busy tracking the new pipe, a strange thing happened at school.

That morning, as the children walked through the school gate, they noticed a nameless strange smell in the air.

"Hey, do you guys smell that?" Old Wu sniffed, "A... burning smell?"

"Yeah!" Wang Zhuankai nodded, "It seems to be coming from behind the school!"

"Behind the school..." A-Xin looked in the direction behind the school, "Isn't that... the direction of the drainage ditch?"

"No," Liu-Liu suddenly popped out of the ventilation duct, its voice grave, "it's not the drainage ditch. It's..."

"What is it?" The children asked.

"The smokestacks." Liu-Liu said, "Behind the school, the smokestacks of the Taichung Thermal Power Plant—their discharge today... is abnormally high."

"Smokestacks?" The children were puzzled, "Aren't the smokestacks always smoking?"

"But today's smoke," data dancing in Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "is several times the usual amount! And..." it paused, "I detected large amounts of discharge from the direction of the factory too..."

"The factory?" A-Xin's eyes lit up, "Hongyuan Food Processing Factory?"

"Mhm." Liu-Liu nodded, "And, it's not just the smokestacks... this morning, I detected over the factory that they're also emitting large amounts of smoke from the 'backyard' direction!"

"They're secretly discharging!" A-Xin jumped up, "Boss Wang is up to his tricks again!"

"But..." Qingqing frowned, "why would they discharge smoke?"

"I don't know." Liu-Liu shook its head, "But these fumes are making the school's air worse and worse."

Just then, the school's broadcast suddenly sounded:

"Attention, all teachers and students—"

"According to the Environmental Protection Administration's air quality monitoring data, the air quality index (AQI) in the area around our school has reached the 'red alert' level!"

"Please reduce outdoor activities! Teachers, please close the doors and windows! Students, please put on your masks!"

"Red alert?!" The children gasped.

"Everyone, return to your classrooms immediately!" The teachers' shouts came from the hallway, "All outdoor activities are cancelled!"

"Hurry, hurry! Back to the classroom!" The children scrambled back into the classroom.

"Close the windows! Close all the windows!" The homeroom teacher directed loudly, "Shut the doors and windows, put on your masks!"

"Okay!" The children hastily closed windows and put on masks.

"Cough, cough..." A few children couldn't help coughing.

"Are you all okay?" Lin Yunyun asked worriedly.

"I'm... I'm fine..." A girl covered her mouth with her mask, "it's just... my throat feels a little itchy..."

"This air..." Wang Zhuankai looked out the window, saying in a low voice, "how did it suddenly become like this..."

"It must be the smokestacks!" A-Xin pressed against the window, looking at the smokestacks behind the school, "Those smokestacks are really puffing hard today!"

"Not just the smokestacks," Qingqing said in a low voice, "and... the factory."

"The factory?" The children were puzzled.

"Mhm." Qingqing lowered her voice, "Liu-Liu said the factory is also emitting smoke. And... the timing is too convenient."

"Convenient?"

"We just found clues about the new pipe," Qingqing said, "and the sky 'just happened' to turn gray. Don't you think..." she paused, "it's too much of a coincidence?"

"..." The children fell silent.

"Whether it's a coincidence or not," A-Xin said loudly, "we're going to find out the truth!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

But Weixi, sitting by the window, hadn't spoken at all.

She just quietly watched the graying sky outside the window.

"Weixi," Qingqing walked over and asked softly, "what are you thinking about?"

"I'm thinking..." Weixi said in a low voice, "in my era, children's classrooms were like this too—windows closed, wearing masks."

"..." Qingqing fell silent.

"Back then," Weixi said softly, "the adults told the children: 'It's just bad weather.'"

"But..." Weixi looked up at Qingqing, "you now know that this isn't just bad weather."

"Right." Qingqing nodded, "We know."

"So," Weixi smiled faintly, "you... are different from the children of that era."

"AQI red alert..." Qingqing murmured in repetition.

"Weixi," A-Xin looked at Weixi and asked nervously, "what is... a red alert?"

Weixi didn't answer.

She just stood quietly by the window, watching the graying sky outside.

"This sky..." she said in a barely audible voice, "looks just like... my era."

"Weixi?" Qingqing walked to her side, "What's wrong?"

"Nothing." Weixi shook her head, turned around, and forced a smile, "Let's... continue the investigation."

"But," Lin Yunyun said worriedly, "the air outside is so bad now, do we still have to go out?"

"We don't need to go out." Weixi said, "Ke Zhiyuan's simulated route map has already shown us the direction. Next..." she looked at Liu-Liu, "leave it to Liu-Liu."

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu puffed out its chest, "Leave it to me!"

That afternoon, Liu-Liu flew into the industrial zone along the simulated route.

Two hours later, Liu-Liu brought back news like a thunderbolt.

"Found it!" Liu-Liu panted, "The source of that new pipe... is in Hongyuan Food Processing Factory's 'underground storage tank'!"

"Underground storage tank?" The children were stunned.

"Yes!" Liu-Liu said, "They first pump the wastewater into an underground storage tank, and then..." it paused, "from the tank, through a 'hidden pipe,' they discharge it directly into the drainage ditch!"

"A hidden pipe?!" A-Xin jumped up, "That's it! The 'hidden pipe' that's always in the news!"

"And," Liu-Liu continued, "I took photos! The entrance of the hidden pipe, the location of the storage tank, and also..." a light flickered in its electronic eyes, "the discharge times!"

"Discharge times?" Qingqing asked.

"Yes!" Liu-Liu nodded, "I secretly hid a 'monitor' beside the storage tank! It recorded the times the hidden pipe discharged—it's discharging almost every day!"

"Discharging every day!" The children were invigorated.

"We have evidence!" A-Xin said loudly, "Photos, times, simulated route map... this time, the evidence is complete!"

"But," Weixi suddenly spoke, her voice soft yet silencing the equipment room, "Boss Wang won't sit still."

"What do you mean?" A-Xin asked.

"He'll find a way," Weixi said, "to 'dispose of' the evidence again. Just like last time."

"Then we..." The children grew tense.

"We," a resolute light flickered in Weixi's eyes, "have to be faster than him!"

"Right!" A-Xin slapped the table, "Let's hand the evidence to Dean Zhou right now! To the environmental protection bureau!"

"But," Qingqing hesitated, "the air outside..."

"It's okay," Weixi said, "we'll wear masks."

"Right!" The children said in unison, "Wear masks!"

Just as the children were about to act, steady footsteps suddenly sounded in the hallway.

"What are you doing in the equipment room?" A low voice rang out.

It was Dean Zhou.

"D-Dean!" The children jumped in fright.

"I've noticed you kids sneaking around these past few days." Dean Zhou stood with his hands behind his back, walking into the equipment room, his gaze sweeping over the maps and data on the wall, "Go on, what have you found?"

"..." The children looked at each other.

"Dean," finally, Qingqing took a deep breath and stepped forward, "we found... the truth about that pipe."

She spread out the photos Liu-Liu had taken, the monitoring data, and Ke Zhiyuan's simulated route map, all before Dean Zhou.

"..." Dean Zhou silently examined all the evidence.

"Dean," A-Xin asked nervously, "is this evidence... enough?"

"Enough." Dean Zhou's voice was low and powerful, "I'll handle this evidence."

"The Dean is helping us?!" The children's eyes lit up.

"I'm not helping you." Dean Zhou put on a stern face, "I just... can't stand someone dirtying our school's sky."

"Dean..." The children were too moved to speak.

"And," Dean Zhou looked at the graying sky outside the window, "during the air pollution alert, don't run around. I'll deliver the evidence."

"But Dean..." A-Xin hesitated, "Liu-Liu already..."

"Liu-Liu has already delivered the evidence." Qingqing finished.

"..." Dean Zhou froze for a moment, then the corner of his mouth twitched slightly, "...Then it certainly ran fast."

"Beep beep!" A proud response came from the ventilation duct.

However, just as the children were about to act, the sky suddenly grew even darker.

"Beep beep beep! Warning! Warning!" Liu-Liu suddenly shouted in panic, "The AQI index... has broken through the 'purple explosion' level!"

"Purple explosion?!" The children's eyes went wide.

"Above red alert is purple explosion!" Qingqing's face instantly turned pale, "That's... the most severe level!"

"And..." Liu-Liu's voice trembled slightly, "I detected... the smokestacks of the Taichung Thermal Power Plant and the smokestacks of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory... are both surging in discharge at the same time!"

"Surging at the same time?!" A-Xin gasped, "Why?!"

"Because..." a soft voice came from the corner.

It was Weixi.

Her face was abnormally pale, as if she knew something.

"Because..." Weixi said in a low voice, "they're using 'smoke cover.'"

"Smoke cover?" The children were stunned.

"Discharging wastewater can be tested and detected. But..." Weixi said, "discharging 'smoke' will just be treated as 'air pollution.' As long as the sky is gray and hazy... everyone will think it's 'bad weather,' not..." she paused, "that the factory is secretly discharging."

"You mean..." Qingqing's face paled too, "they deliberately dirtied the sky so everyone would 'not see' the truth about the drainage ditch?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "And... once the air pollution alert is issued, the children will be kept inside the classrooms. That way..." she lowered her head, "our investigation will also be 'trapped.'"

"How cunning!" A-Xin clenched his fist in anger, "Boss Wang is even using the weather!"

"Then we..." Lin Yunyun asked fearfully, "can we... not investigate anymore?"

"Of course we will!" A-Xin said loudly, "The more trapped we are, the more we have to investigate!"

"But," Qingqing analyzed calmly, "Weixi is right. Once the air pollution alert is issued, outdoor activities will be banned. We can't go to the industrial zone... and the evidence can't be delivered either."

"Then we'll..." A-Xin thought for a moment, and then his eyes suddenly lit up, "we'll let the evidence 'fly' out by itself!"

"Fly by itself?" The children were puzzled.

"Liu-Liu!" A-Xin looked at Liu-Liu, "How high can you fly?"

"Beep beep?" Liu-Liu blinked, then puffed out its chest, "I can fly very high! Higher than the smokestacks!"

"Great!" A-Xin slapped the table, "Then we'll tie the evidence onto Liu-Liu! Let Liu-Liu fly over the smokestacks, over the gray sky, and deliver the evidence straight to the environmental protection bureau!"

"Fly over..." The children's eyes lit up.

"Yes!" A-Xin said loudly, "The sky is gray, but... Liu-Liu isn't afraid of gray!"

"Beep beep beep!" Liu-Liu spun around excitedly, "Leave it to me! I'm the fastest in the school!"

"But," Weixi spoke softly, "Liu-Liu, you have to be careful. The sky is hazy... remember, fly high."

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu nodded seriously, "I'll fly high, so the smoke can't reach me!"

"Great!" A-Xin carefully tied the evidence—the photos, monitoring data, and simulated route map—onto Liu-Liu's body, "Move out!"

"Beep beep beep—!"

Liu-Liu's small figure rushed out of the equipment room, charging into that gray, hazy sky.

The children crowded at the window, watching Liu-Liu's figure fly higher and higher, higher and higher... until it disappeared into the gray clouds.

"Go, Liu-Liu..." A-Xin pressed against the window, murmuring to himself.

"It will succeed." Qingqing said, "Liu-Liu is..."

"The fastest in the school." Weixi continued, her voice soft.

Meanwhile, in the gray, hazy sky, Liu-Liu was flying with all its might.

"Beep beep... so choking..." Liu-Liu covered its nose with its tail, "this smoke... is thicker than I imagined..."

It looked up and saw, up ahead, several giant smokestacks puffing out thick white smoke.

"Those smokestacks..." a resolute light flickered in Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "I'm going to fly higher than them!"

It gathered all its strength and gave its wings a powerful flap—"Swish!"

Liu-Liu's figure shot straight up into the sky!

Through the first layer of smoke... through the second layer of smoke... past the tops of the smokestacks...

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu looked down at the smokestacks below its feet, "I flew over them!"

It flew on, over the industrial zone, over the main road, over street after street...

"I see it!" Liu-Liu's eyes lit up, "The environmental protection bureau!"

It dived down and landed steadily at the bureau's main gate.

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu took the evidence off its body and placed it at the door, "Delivered!"

"What is this?" The bureau's guard walked out, saw the evidence on the ground, and froze, "This is..."

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu nodded hard at the guard, then turned and shot back up into the sky, flying back toward the school.

"Wait! What is that?!" The guard picked up the evidence and shouted, "Hey! Wait!"

But Liu-Liu had already flown far away.

All that remained was a phrase echoing in the wind:

"Beep beep! Evidence, delivered!"

"It will succeed... right?" Lin Yunyun asked quietly.

"It will." Weixi said softly, "Because... Liu-Liu is the fastest in the school."

"And," A-Xin added, "no matter how gray the sky is, it can't block our hearts."

Two hours of waiting felt like two years.

"Why isn't it back yet..." A-Xin paced back and forth in the equipment room.

"Easy." Qingqing lowered her voice, "Liu-Liu will come back."

"But..." A-Xin's palms were covered in cold sweat, "what if it gets lost in the sky..."

"It won't." Weixi said, "Liu-Liu remembers the way home. Because..." she smiled faintly, "this is its home."

"But..." A-Xin was still nervous, "this is the first time I've sent Liu-Liu flying so far alone..."

"A-Xin," Qingqing patted his shoulder, "have you forgotten? Liu-Liu is the intel king who rescued Old Wu from the industrial zone's guards!"

"Right... right!" A-Xin remembered that and relaxed a little, "It's the Liu-Liu that even the guards can't catch!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed.

"By the way, Weixi," after the laughter, Qingqing suddenly asked, "you just mentioned... 'purple explosion'..."

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "One level above red alert is purple explosion. During purple explosion, everyone should stay indoors."

"Then..." Qingqing hesitated, "is purple explosion... very scary?"

Weixi didn't answer right away.

She lowered her head and was silent for a long time.

"In my era," she said softly, "'purple explosion' was... 'everyday life.'"

"Everyday life?!" The children's eyes went wide.

"Mhm." Weixi said, "In my era, the sky was often purple-gray. The adults said: 'Wearing a mask when you go out is basic courtesy.' Children's essay topics included 'My Family's Air Purifier.' School sports days were often changed to indoors because of air pollution."

"..." The children fell silent.

"So," Weixi looked up, her voice soft yet exceptionally resolute, "I don't want... this era to become like that either."

"It won't!" A-Xin said loudly, "We'll make it different!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

Looking at the children, Weixi's eyes reddened slightly.

"...Thank you." she said in a low voice.

Just then—

"Beep beep beep beep!!!"

A silver-white figure burst in through the ventilation duct!

"Mission complete!" Liu-Liu panted, the evidence on its body already gone, "the evidence... was delivered!"

"Delivered?!" The children burst into cheers!

"The environmental protection bureau people said," Liu-Liu spun around excitedly, "they'll re-inspect immediately! And..." it paused, "they said this time, they'll do a 'surprise inspection'!"

"A surprise inspection!" A-Xin jumped up, "Great!"

"But..." Weixi suddenly spoke, her voice soft yet silencing the children, "before their inspection, Boss Wang will counterattack again."

"..." The children fell silent.

"So," Weixi looked at the gray, hazy sky outside the window, "we have to protect ourselves, and also... protect each other."

"Weixi..." Qingqing looked at her, and then, her gaze suddenly landed on Weixi's wrist.

"Weixi! Your bracelet!" Qingqing cried out, "the number..."

Weixi looked down.

The number on the bracelet had jumped from twelve to... ten.

"Ten... ten now..." Qingqing's voice trembled slightly.

"It's okay." Weixi pulled her sleeve down, covering the bracelet, "There's still enough."

"Enough for what!" A-Xin's eyes were red, "Ten... ten days..."

"Ten days," Weixi smiled faintly, "is enough for us to see that day."

"That day?"

"To see..." Weixi looked out the window, "the day this sky turns blue again."

Outside the window, the sky was still gray and hazy.

But in the children's eyes, there was light.

That night, Weixi sat alone in the classroom, looking at the night sky outside the window.

The number on the bracelet flickered quietly in the moonlight.

—Ten.

"Ten days..." she said in a low voice, "just right."

"Just right enough..." she closed her eyes, "to watch the ending of this 'Campaign.'"

"And..." she gently touched the bracelet, "just right enough... to say goodbye to everyone."

The moonlight shone quietly on her.

Outside the window, the sky was still gray and hazy.

But deep within that gray, hazy sky, a single star was trying its hardest to shine.
