# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 24: See You Tomorrow, Weixi!

On the day Weixi returned to the future, the Earth of the future was quietly changing.

The sky seemed... a little bluer.

The sea seemed... a little cleaner.

At school, the children looked up at the sky and asked their teacher curiously: "Teacher, doesn't the sky today look a little different from yesterday?"

The teacher looked up at the sky and smiled: "It does seem that way."

"Why?"

"Maybe..." the teacher thought for a moment, "maybe someone, long, long ago, wiped the sky clean for us."

And Weixi, who had returned to the future, was standing in the yard, looking at that sky which was a little bluer than yesterday.

"Grandmother," she said softly, "look, the sky... seems to have turned blue."

"Yes." Grandmother, holding a bowl of water, also looked up at the sky, "Today's sky is indeed cleaner than yesterday's."

"Grandmother," Weixi turned around, her eyes reddening slightly, "I did it."

"Did what?"

"Made the future..." Weixi smiled, "a little bit better."

"..." Grandmother looked at her and gently patted her head.

"That's good." Grandmother said, "That's good."

Days passed, one after another.

Weixi returned to her school and continued her studies.

In her backpack were that notebook with the Earth drawn on it, that fiery red phoenix blossom (now made into a bookmark), that group photo pressed against her heart, and that aluminum foil gear.

She carried them with her every day.

"Weixi, what are all the things in your backpack?" Her deskmate asked curiously.

"They are..." Weixi smiled faintly, "'treasures from the past.'"

"Treasures from the past?" Her deskmate tilted his head, "What's that?"

"They are..." Weixi thought for a moment, "the courage a group of children gave me."

"Courage?" Her deskmate didn't understand, but seeing Weixi smile so happily, he smiled too.

The school Weixi returned to was a little different from the one in her memories.

"Weixi, morning!" "Morning!" The classmates greeted her warmly.

"The weather is so nice today!" Someone said, "The sky is so blue!"

"Mhm." Weixi looked at the sky and smiled faintly, "Blue... just right."

"Weixi, look!" Her deskmate pointed at the edge of the playground, "The tree over there is sprouting!"

"Really?" Weixi walked over, crouched down, and looked at that little sapling sprouting from the cracked, dry soil.

"This is..." She gently touched the sapling's leaves, and her eyes suddenly welled up.

"Weixi?" Her deskmate was startled, "What's wrong?"

"Nothing." Weixi shook her head and smiled, "I just... remembered my 'trees.'"

"Your trees?" Her deskmate asked curiously, "You've planted trees?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "In a very, very faraway place, a group of children planted many trees for me."

"A faraway place?" Her deskmate tilted his head, "Farther than here?"

"Mhm." Weixi said softly, "Farther than here... forty years."

"Forty years?!" Her deskmate's eyes went wide, "Then how do you go back to see them?"

"..." Weixi was silent for a moment, then smiled softly.

"I see them with my 'heart.'" she said.

"Heart?" Her deskmate didn't understand.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "As long as I remember them... they'll keep growing in my heart."

"..." Her deskmate nodded, half understanding.

"Weixi," he asked again, "what's the name of that place you talked about?"

"The school there is called 'Luyang Elementary.'" Weixi said.

"Luyang Elementary..." Her deskmate repeated, "It sounds like a very warm place."

"Mhm." Weixi smiled, "It's... the warmest place in the whole world."

A month later, something happened that made Weixi unable to sleep all night.

That day, Weixi's grandmother took a letter out of the old mailbox.

"Weixi," Grandmother called her, "there's a letter for you."

"A letter for me?" Weixi froze, "Who would write to me?"

She took the letter and froze when she saw the handwriting on the envelope.

On the envelope was written:

"To the future Weixi: Hello! I'm A-Xin! ..."

"This is..." Weixi's hand trembled slightly.

Trembling, she tore open the envelope.

On the letter paper, in crooked handwriting, was written:

"To Weixi:
Hello! I'm A-Xin!
You said that letters to the future would get replies.
So I wrote this letter, sent to the future you!
Let me tell you—our class sorts trash properly every day now!
My 'Little Blue' tree is taller than me now!
Also, the sea behind the school really is blue!
Oh, and I'll secretly tell you—Qingqing says she's going to be the director of the environmental protection bureau in the future!
Old Wu says he's going to open a 'Earth-Saving Pork Bun Shop'!
We're all doing well!
You... take care of yourself too!
—A-Xin"

"A-Xin..." Weixi read the letter, and tears fell drop by drop.

"He really..." she choked, "really wrote to me..."

A week later, Weixi received another letter.

"To Weixi: Hello! I'm Qingqing! ..."

"Qingqing..." Weixi read the letter, her eyes reddening again.

On the letter paper was written:

"To Weixi:
I'm Qingqing!
Let me tell you—our class's 'Protect Earth' has become a schoolwide tradition!
Also, the sea behind the school is really, really, really blue!
I look at it every day.
Also, I've told my dad—in the future I'm going to take the exam for the environmental protection bureau! I'm going to be the director!
And then, I'm going to make the seas of all Taiwan blue!
I'll remember everything you taught me.
—Qingqing"

"Qingqing..." Weixi pressed the letter against her chest, "You'll definitely become the best director."

Another month passed, and the third letter arrived.

"To Weixi: Hello! I'm Old Wu! ..."

"Old Wu..." Weixi read it and couldn't help smiling.

On the letter paper was written:

"To Weixi:
I'm Old Wu!
My 'Earth-Saving Pork Bun Shop' has opened!
A customer asked me: 'Why are your pork buns especially delicious?'
I told them: 'Because they contain a secret recipe—called "love for the future"!'
The customer said I talk strangely, but... I don't care! My pork buns have it anyway!
Oh, and I've put your photo up in the shop! When customers ask who you are, I say—'That's my best friend!'
—Old Wu"

"Old Wu..." Weixi laughed and cried, "Your pork buns... must be delicious."

And then, there was Lin Yunyun's letter.

"To Weixi: Hello! I'm Yunyun! I'm now the leader of the school's 'Tree Planting Group'! I've planted so many trees with my younger schoolmates! I often think—when you come back... I must take you to see the trees we planted!—Yunyun"

And Wang Zhuankai's letter: "Weixi! I'm now the president of the 'Joke Club'! I've passed on the 'keep laughing forever' you taught me to every generation of younger students!—Wang Zhuankai"

And Ke Zhiyuan's letter: "Weixi! I've started programming! I'm going to make a 'Protect Earth' app for people all over the world to download!—Ke Zhiyuan"

Letter after letter.

"..." Weixi put those letters, one by one, into a wooden box.

"This is..." she gently stroked the wooden box, "the most precious treasure of my life."

"Even more precious than those time devices..." she lowered her head, "a thousand times, ten thousand times more precious."

That night, Weixi held that letter against her heart and slept the whole night.

The next day, Weixi did something that startled the whole family.

She decided to—"write back."

"Weixi, who are you writing back to?" Grandmother asked.

"To..." Weixi said, "to a very important friend of mine."

On the letter paper, she wrote, word by word:

"To the best King of Ideas—A-Xin:
I received your letter!
Thank you for telling me all that good news.
I have good news to tell you too—the sky here has started to turn blue as well.
I look up at the sky every day, thinking about what you said—'It will definitely get bluer.'
Please keep writing to me, okay?
I will wait, and wait, for your letters.
—Weixi"

"Like this..." Weixi finished writing and gently folded the letter paper, "will it get delivered?"

"..." She thought for a moment, then smiled, "It will definitely get delivered. Because..."

"'Letters to the future' do get replies."

"And then..." she lowered her head, "'replies from the future'... will definitely reach their hands too."

"But, Weixi," Grandmother asked worriedly, "are you sure... this letter can be sent back?"

"..." Weixi was silent for a moment.

"I don't know." she said softly, "But... I believe it will get delivered."

"Why?"

"Because..." Weixi smiled, "'hope' finds its own way."

That evening, Weixi dropped the letter into that old mailbox.

"Ding-dong."

The letter fell into the mailbox.

"..." Weixi stood before the mailbox and gently stroked it.

"Please," she said in a low voice, "carry my 'see you tomorrow'... to them."

And in another era—

"Ah!" At Luyang Elementary, A-Xin suddenly jumped up from his chair, "There's... a letter on my desk?!"

"A letter?" The children gathered around, "Who wrote it?"

"The envelope says..." A-Xin's eyes went wide, "'To the best King of Ideas—A-Xin'?!"

"This is..." Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, "this handwriting..."

"It's Weixi!" A-Xin shouted, "This is Weixi's handwriting!"

"What?!" The children gasped.

Trembling, A-Xin tore open the envelope.

On the letter paper was Weixi's elegant handwriting:

"To the best King of Ideas—A-Xin:
I received your letter!
Thank you for telling me all that good news.
I have good news to tell you too—the sky here has started to turn blue as well.
I look up at the sky every day, thinking about what you said—'It will definitely get bluer.'
Please keep writing to me, okay?
I will wait, and wait, for your letters.
—Weixi"

"..." After reading the letter, A-Xin's eyes moistened.

"She received the letter..." A-Xin choked, "she received our letter!"

"And..." Qingqing's eyes reddened too, "she even wrote back!"

"'Letters to the future' do get replies..." Lin Yunyun murmured in repetition, "So... it's true!"

"Then..." Old Wu asked loudly, "can we keep writing letters to her from now on?!"

"Yes!" The children shouted in unison, "We'll keep writing!"

"Then..." A-Xin held up that letter and announced loudly, "from now on, every year when the phoenix blossoms bloom, we'll write a letter to the future Weixi!"

"Okay—!" The children responded in unison.

"And," Qingqing added, "we should also make a promise—to accomplish, one by one, everything we write in the letters!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "Write it, and do it!"

"Write it, and do it..." A-Xin repeated those five words, then looked at that letter and smiled softly.

"Weixi," he said in a low voice, "don't worry. Every letter we write... we'll make come true."

"Because..." he looked up, "we're the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'!"

"Okay—!"

That year, the phoenix tree bloomed again.

That year, the children sent out their second letter.

The letter said: "Weixi! We've turned 'write it, and do it' into our promise!—A-Xin"

The third letter: "Weixi! Today, I picked up a hundred PET bottles!—A-Xin"

The fourth letter: "Weixi! The sea behind the school is still blue today!—Qingqing"

The fifth letter: "Weixi! My pork bun shop has opened a branch!—Old Wu"

The sixth letter...

One letter, then another.

Time continued forward.

And those three words, "see you tomorrow," became the gentlest promise passed down, generation after generation, at Luyang Elementary.

What she didn't know was—

In another era, in the classroom of Luyang Elementary, A-Xin was lying on his desk, staring blankly at the notebook on the table.

"A-Xin, what are you thinking about?" Qingqing walked over and asked.

"I'm thinking..." A-Xin said, "I wonder if Weixi is doing well right now."

"She's definitely doing well." Qingqing said, "Because she said the future would get better."

"Mhm." A-Xin nodded, "What she said must be right."

"Oh right," Qingqing suddenly asked, "did you... write a letter yesterday?"

"Mhm." A-Xin nodded, "I wrote to Weixi. But..." he lowered his head, "I don't even know her address..."

"It doesn't matter." Qingqing smiled, "'Letters to the future' don't need an address."

"Really?" A-Xin asked.

"Really." Qingqing nodded, "Because the letters we wrote on fair day... must have all been delivered."

"..." A-Xin looked at that blue sky outside the window and smiled softly.

"Weixi," he said in a low voice, "you must receive it."

Time quietly passed.

One year. Two years. Three years.

Six years—not long, not short.

Long enough for a sapling to grow into a small tree.

Long enough for a sea to recover its clarity.

And long enough for a group of children to grow up.

A-Xin became the president of an environmental club. He led a group of younger students to the beach every month to clean it.

"President," a younger student asked, "why do you love beach cleanups so much?"

"Because..." A-Xin smiled, "I promised someone I would pick up every piece of trash on the beach."

"Someone? Who?"

"Someone..." A-Xin looked at the sea, "an older sister from the future."

Qingqing got into an environmental protection program in university. Every day, she studied how to make water cleaner.

"Qingqing," the professor looked at her thesis, "why do you want to study water quality?"

"Because..." Qingqing smiled, "I promised someone I would keep the sea blue."

Old Wu's "Earth-Saving Pork Bun Shop" became more and more famous. He used part of the money he earned to plant trees.

"Boss," a customer asked, "why do you plant trees?"

"Because..." Old Wu said as he kneaded the dough, "I promised someone I would make this world a little greener."

Lin Yunyun became an elementary school teacher. She led a group of little kids planting trees on campus.

"Teacher," a little kid asked, "why do we plant trees?"

"Because..." Lin Yunyun smiled, "a very important older sister told me—'trees are things that stay.'"

Wang Zhuankai became a comedian. Every time he went on stage, his first line was: "I promised someone I would keep laughing forever!"

"That person..." he said to the audience on stage, "is my most important friend in this life!"

The children each grew up.

But no matter where they went, they all remembered the same promise—

"See you tomorrow."

The phoenix tree at Luyang Elementary bloomed six more times.

"Protect Earth" became a tradition known by every student from first grade to sixth grade.

"I heard that long, long ago," a first-grader listened to the older students tell stories, "an 'older sister from the future' came to our school!"

"From the future?!"

"Yes! She taught the older students back then so many ways to protect the Earth!"

"Really?!"

"Really! Look!" The older student pointed at the woods behind the school, "Those trees were planted back then! Now they've all grown into big trees!"

"Wow..."

"And," the older student said mysteriously, "the school's 'Protect Earth' also started back then! Everything we do now was passed down from that time!"

"Then... where is that 'older sister from the future' now?"

"She..." the older student looked at the sky, "she went back to the future. But... she said she would come back!"

"Come back?"

"Yes! She said—'see you tomorrow'!"

"See you tomorrow..."

The children looked up at the sky.

The sky was blue, blue.

Six years later, at Luyang Elementary, the graduation ceremony.

"Today, we're honoring a very special group of graduates!" Principal Qiu, holding his teacup, stood on the stage, smiling, "In sixth grade, they launched 'Protect Earth'!"

"Whoa—!" Applause rose from the audience.

"This group of children," Principal Qiu said, "used their own hands to make the sea behind the school clean again! With their efforts, 'Protect Earth' became our school's tradition!"

"And," Principal Qiu continued, "their story has been written into a book, so more people can know about it!"

"Written into a book?!" The children in the audience stared with wide eyes.

"Yes!" Principal Qiu brought out a thick book, "This book is called 'The Transfer Student from the Future'! It tells their story!"

"Wow—!"

"But," Principal Qiu said mysteriously, "the last page of the book is blank."

"Blank?" The children were puzzled.

"Because," Principal Qiu said, "this story isn't over yet. That 'transfer student from the future' hasn't come back yet."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But," Principal Qiu smiled, "we'll wait for her. Because... she said—"

"'See you tomorrow.'"

"See you tomorrow..." The children repeated those three words.

After the graduation ceremony, the "children" of Class 6-1 from back then stood at the school gate, looking at that phoenix tree.

"Time flies," A-Xin sighed, "just like that, six years have passed."

"Yeah." Qingqing nodded, "We've all grown up."

"I went to look at that sea yesterday," Old Wu said, "it's still so blue!"

"My 'Little Blue' is taller than the school wall now!" A-Xin said proudly.

"My 'Eat-Your-Fill' too!" Wang Zhuankai followed.

"Oh right," Lin Yunyun suddenly asked, "do you all... still remember her?"

"Of course." The children said in unison.

"How could we forget." Qingqing said softly.

"Then..." Lin Yunyun lowered her head, "will she... really come back?"

"..." The children fell silent.

"Oh right," A-Xin suddenly remembered something, "do you remember? On fair day, the 'letters to the future' we wrote..."

"I remember." Qingqing nodded, "We wrote that we wanted the Earth of the future to have a clean sea."

"Then..." A-Xin said, "our letters... must have been delivered, right?"

"They must have been delivered." Qingqing said softly, "Because... she said, 'letters to the future' do get replies."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But..." Lin Yunyun lowered her head, "we've never received a reply from her..."

"..." The wind blew gently.

"It doesn't matter." A-Xin smiled, "Not receiving a reply doesn't mean she didn't receive the letters."

"Right." Qingqing nodded, "As long as the letters were delivered... she must have read them."

"Then..." Lin Yunyun looked up, "when she reads the letters... will she think of us?"

"She will." The children said in unison.

"She definitely will."

"Because..." A-Xin looked at the sky, "she said—"

"'See you tomorrow.'"

"See you tomorrow..." The children repeated softly.

"So," A-Xin said loudly, "tomorrow, she will definitely come back!"

"See you tomorrow!" The children shouted in unison, "Weixi! See you tomorrow!"

The sea breeze blew gently.

And in the very, very far future—

A girl was sitting in the yard, reading a letter that had just arrived.

"To Weixi: Hello! I'm A-Xin! Today, the phoenix tree at Luyang Elementary bloomed again! ..."

She read the letter, the corners of her mouth lifting slightly.

Then she put down the letter, looked up at the sky.

"See you tomorrow," she said in a low voice, "Class 6-1."

"..." she smiled softly, "I will definitely see you all again."

"Because..." she gently pressed her hand to her chest, feeling the warmth of that photo, "you've always been in my heart."

The wind blew gently.

The sky of the future was blue, blue.

The past and the future, in that moment, seemed to be connected as one.

"See you tomorrow."

The End
