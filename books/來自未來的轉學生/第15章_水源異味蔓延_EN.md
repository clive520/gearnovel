# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 15: The Spreading Taint in the Water

In the first week of the "Extreme Speed Operation," Weixi's bracelet dropped from twenty-four to twenty-two.

"Two days, two digits." Qingqing recorded the number in her notebook, brows tightly knit, "The speed... has gotten faster again."

"It's fine." Weixi said softly, "There's still enough."

"Enough for what!" A-Xin couldn't help lowering his voice, "Twenty-two days is nowhere near enough!"

"Enough for you to find out the truth about the drainage ditch." Weixi smiled faintly, "That's what we agreed on, isn't it?"

A-Xin opened his mouth, then finally closed it.

He felt that Weixi's smile seemed to hide something... he didn't dare think deeply about.

And while the three-person alliance was busy with the "Extreme Speed Operation," the anomalies at school also began to "erupt in full force."

First, the drinking fountains.

"Hey, don't you guys think," at lunchtime, Old Wu wrinkled his nose, staring at his water bottle, "the drinking fountain water tastes weirder and weirder?"

"Yeah!" Wang Zhuankai nodded along, "I took a sip yesterday and felt it had a... indescribable taste. Salty, but not quite. Salty, but not exactly salty."

"Salty?" Lin Yunyun asked worriedly, "Then... can we still drink it?"

"We should be able to, right?" A-Xin said, "Didn't the school say the water quality passed inspection?"

"But..." Old Wu shook his head and said quietly, "My dad said that at his workplace, a nearby factory got caught secretly discharging wastewater, and the water in the whole community changed taste. He said, 'When water changes taste, it means something has gotten in.'"

"Secretly discharging wastewater..." The children looked at each other.

"Come on, no way?" Wang Zhuankai forced a dry laugh, "That drainage ditch behind our school is just a little stinky, right? What does it have to do with the school's water?"

"But..." Lin Yunyun lowered her head, her voice growing smaller and smaller, "my stomach hurt yesterday. The health room teacher said that over the past two weeks, more than twenty classmates have had stomach trouble..."

The classroom suddenly fell quiet.

A heavy, nameless atmosphere spread through the room.

"I... I'm not feeling well either." A boy in the corner said quietly, "My stomach hurt all night last night."

"Me too..." Another girl followed, "My mom said if I'm still unwell, she's taking me to the doctor."

"Why didn't any of you say so earlier!" A-Xin's eyes went wide.

"We..." Lin Yunyun lowered her head, "we were afraid that saying it would make everyone worry more..."

"You idiots!" A-Xin couldn't help shouting, "If you're not feeling well, you have to say it! If you don't tell us, how are we supposed to know what to do?!"

The classroom fell quiet again.

"I'm sorry..." Lin Yunyun's eyes reddened, "I... I didn't want to scare everyone..."

"Yunyun!" Qingqing walked over and took her hand, "You're right, we shouldn't hide it. But..." she swept her gaze across the class and said loudly, "we also shouldn't be afraid! We're the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'! The more times like this, the more we need to stick together!"

"Right!" A-Xin jumped onto the podium, "Everyone listen up! Starting today, our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' has a new mission—'Water Quality Monitoring'!"

"Water quality monitoring?" The children looked at each other.

"Yes!" A-Xin said loudly, "We're going to record the drinking fountain water, the drainage ditch water, and the seawater at the river mouth every day! Record their colors, their smells, their changes! We're going to use 'data' to protect ourselves!"

"Using data to protect ourselves..." Wang Zhuankai murmured in repetition.

"Right!" Qingqing continued, "The adults say the water quality passes, so we'll use our eyes, our noses, our records—and let the adults see what the water really looks like!"

"Okay—!" The children's morale was instantly ignited.

"Then, who's in charge of recording?" Old Wu asked.

"You!" A-Xin pointed at Old Wu, "Your nose is the sharpest! You're in charge of 'smelling'! Record the ditch's smell every day!"

"Alright!" Old Wu patted his chest, "I'm the number one smeller in the whole school!"

"Then I'll record the colors!" Lin Yunyun said.

"I'll take the photos!" Wang Zhuankai raised his hand.

"I'm in charge of... believing in everyone!" A-Xin said loudly.

"Hahaha!" The children laughed, feeling much more at ease than before.

"Everyone, don't scare yourselves!" Qingqing stood up and said loudly, "The school is already handling it! We..."

"Qingqing is right," Weixi also spoke, her voice soft yet unusually steady, "everyone, don't panic for now. If the school's water really has a problem, the adults will definitely find out."

"But..." Old Wu hesitated.

"Trust me," Weixi looked at Old Wu, her tone gentle, "we'll get to the bottom of this together."

Old Wu looked at Weixi's clear eyes, and the unease in his heart seemed to... settle a little.

"Alright!" He nodded, "Then we'll wait for Weixi!"

"Mhm!" The children responded in unison.

But only Qingqing and A-Xin knew how heavy the burden was behind Weixi's words, "get to the bottom of this."

The next day, an "unexpected visitor" appeared at school.

"Hey? Isn't that Dean Zhou?" A-Xin leaned on the hallway railing, watching the edge of the playground where Dean Zhou was crouching beside the drainage ditch, staring at the water in the ditch, "What's the Dean looking at?"

"He's looking at the drainage ditch." Weixi walked over and said softly.

"The drainage ditch?" A-Xin blinked, "Doesn't the Dean hate stinky ditches? He used to scold us for going near the ditch during patrols!"

"Because," Weixi's gaze rested on Dean Zhou, "he's not 'patrolling' now. He's 'investigating.'"

"Investigating?" A-Xin froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "When adults sense something wrong, they start investigating. The Dean... has noticed."

Looking at Dean Zhou crouching by the ditch, a nameless feeling rose in A-Xin's heart.

"Then..." he asked in a low voice, "what will the Dean find?"

"I don't know." Weixi shook her head, "But at least, he isn't pretending not to see."

In fact, Dean Zhou's investigation had begun a week earlier.

He walked along the drainage ditch, step by step upstream from behind the school.

"Step one: behind the school. Water color: grayish-green. Odor: strong." The Dean wrote down the first record in his notebook.

"Step two: by the industrial road. Water color: deepened. Odor: stronger. White foam appearing on the surface."

"Step three: beside the abandoned farmhouse. Water color: almost black-green. Odor: pungent. Floating on the surface..." the Dean crouched down, frowning, "dead fish."

"Step four: the edge of the industrial zone. Here..." the Dean stopped, looking at the gray, hazy factory area ahead, "there's a very strong chemical smell. And..." he looked down at the ditch, "there's a pipe in the ditch that shouldn't be there."

He crouched down and examined the pipe closely.

The pipe's diameter was thicker than an ordinary drainage pipe; its outer wall was coated with a layer of grayish-white crystals—the traces left by dried chemicals.

"This pipe..." the Dean said in a low voice, "isn't registered on the industrial zone's pipe map."

He pulled out his phone, took a few photos, and recorded the pipe's location.

"Whoever you are," the Dean looked at the pipe, a stubbornness in his tone, "I will definitely catch you."

In the afternoon, in the principal's office.

"Old Zhou," Principal Qiu held his teacup, his expression grave, "the drinking fountain water—the water company has come to test it three times, and each time they say it passes."

"Passes?" Dean Zhou stood by the window, looking at the drainage ditch outside, "Principal, don't you find that strange?"

"What's strange?"

"The drinking fountain water," Dean Zhou turned around, "uses tap water. The tap water passes. But then why are more and more students getting stomach trouble after drinking it?"

"That's..." Principal Qiu paused.

"I suspect," Dean Zhou's voice was very low, "the problem isn't the drinking fountains."

"Then where is it?"

Dean Zhou was silent for a moment, then walked to the principal and spread out a map.

"Principal, this is a map of the drainage ditch behind the school." He pointed at a line on the map, "This ditch starts behind the school and runs all the way upstream, connecting to..."

His finger stopped at a spot on the map.

"The industrial zone." Principal Qiu's expression instantly turned grave.

"I walked the length of the ditch," Dean Zhou said, "and when I reached the edge of the industrial zone, I saw a pipe. A pipe not registered on any map."

"A pipe?"

"Mhm." Dean Zhou nodded, "It's hidden in the weeds, extending from the industrial zone, plunging straight into the drainage ditch. The water in the ditch..." he paused, "the color is wrong. And the smell is wrong too."

"You mean..." Principal Qiu's hand trembled slightly.

"Principal," Dean Zhou took a deep breath, "I recommend we notify the environmental protection bureau to thoroughly investigate this pipe."

Principal Qiu was silent.

"Old Zhou," it was a long moment before he spoke, "do you know what the consequences will be once this is investigated?"

"I know." Dean Zhou said, "If it turns out some factory in the industrial zone is secretly discharging... that factory could be shut down and fined. The people involved could be in serious trouble."

"Then you still..."

"Principal," Dean Zhou interrupted him, his tone more resolute than ever before, "I've been dean of student affairs for twenty years, and I've never let a single matter that should be investigated go, just because I was 'afraid of trouble.'"

"And..." a softness flickered in his eyes, "those kids fought for a whole semester over a stinky ditch. This adult can't lose to them."

Principal Qiu looked at him for a very, very long time, then let out a heavy sigh.

"...Fine." He nodded, "I'll leave it to you."

However, Dean Zhou's actions had touched a nerve in someone.

That evening, in the office of a factory in the industrial zone, the phone rang urgently.

"Hello? It's me." A low voice answered, "At the school... they've started investigating."

"Mhm. It's about that drainage ditch."

"I know. So..." the voice paused, "increase that pipe's discharge volume. And bury the pipe deeper."

"Yes. Before they finish investigating, get the evidence..."

"cleaned up."

The phone hung up.

Night quietly enveloped the industrial zone.

The next morning, Liu-Liu brought back news like a thunderbolt.

"Class President! Bad news!" Liu-Liu burst out of the ventilation duct, its voice trembling, "That pipe... last night's discharge was a full double the usual volume!"

"Double?!" Qingqing and A-Xin's eyes went wide at the same time.

"And!" Liu-Liu panted, "I followed the pipe into the industrial zone and discovered..."

"Discovered what?"

"Discovered..." a flicker of fear passed through Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "that the pipe's source inside the industrial zone has been covered with dirt! And they're digging a deeper trench to bury the pipe even deeper!"

"They're destroying the evidence!" A-Xin jumped up, "They know someone's investigating!"

"Calm down!" Qingqing held him back, "Don't panic yet!"

"Don't panic?" A-Xin hopped frantically, "They're burying the pipe! Once they're done, we'll never find the evidence!"

"A-Xin is right," Weixi spoke, her voice soft yet silencing the equipment room, "they're racing against time. We have to race against time too."

"Racing against time..." Qingqing murmured in repetition.

"We can't just wait for the Dean alone." Weixi said, "We have to get the evidence before they bury it all clean!"

"But," A-Xin grimaced, "how are we supposed to get it? We can't just break into the industrial zone!"

"We can't break in," a determination never seen before flickered in Weixi's eyes, "but we have Liu-Liu."

"Liu-Liu?" A-Xin and Qingqing looked at Liu-Liu at the same time.

"Liu-Liu," Weixi crouched down and gently patted Liu-Liu's head, "can you do it?"

Liu-Liu puffed out its chest, a flame burning in its electronic eyes:

"Beep beep! I can!" Liu-Liu said loudly, "I'm the school's intelligence king! Sneak photos, video, collecting water samples... I can do it all!"

"Good!" Weixi smiled softly, "Then let's take a gamble!"

Late that night, outside the industrial zone's wall.

Four small figures hid in the grass, holding their breath.

"A-Xin, are you sure we should do this?" Qingqing lowered her voice, tension in her tone, "This is the industrial zone! What if we get caught..."

"We won't get caught!" A-Xin patted his chest, "We're hidden here; no one can see us!"

"But..." Qingqing looked at the tall wall before them, "if something happens to Liu-Liu in there..."

"It won't." Weixi said softly, "Liu-Liu is the fastest in the school. And..." she smiled faintly, "Liu-Liu is our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit's' 'Intel Chief.' It can definitely do it."

"How are you both so confident..." Qingqing laughed helplessly.

"Because," A-Xin looked at the wall, his tone turning serious, "we believe in it. Just as it believes in us."

The wind blew through the grass.

The three children waited quietly.

"Hey, Weixi," A-Xin suddenly spoke during the wait, "you said earlier that Liu-Liu 'still has a mission to complete.' What mission is that?"

Weixi froze for a moment.

"I meant..." she lowered her head and said softly, "it hasn't yet seen the day we find out the truth about the drainage ditch."

"..." A-Xin was silent for a moment, then nodded hard, "Right! It has to see it! We must let it see it!"

"Mhm." Weixi smiled, "We definitely will."

"And," A-Xin suddenly thought of something, lowering his voice, "Weixi, tell me honestly—how much is left on your bracelet?"

Weixi fell silent.

"...Twenty-one." she finally said.

"Twenty-one..." A-Xin's heart sank, but he forced a smile, "It's okay! Twenty-one is enough for us to do a lot of things!"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded softly, "It's enough."

"Liu-Liu," A-Xin lowered his voice, giving instructions, "remember: first take photos of the pipe, then collect the water sample, and then—come straight back! No showing off!"

"Beep beep! Roger!" Liu-Liu took a deep breath, "I'm off!"

"Wait!" Weixi called it back.

"Hmm?"

"Liu-Liu," Weixi said softly, "if... if you get discovered, abandon the mission and protect yourself first. Evidence can be found again. You, there's only one of you."

Liu-Liu's electronic eyes flickered.

"Beep beep." It said in a low voice, "I understand."

Then, Liu-Liu's small figure, like a silver-white bolt of lightning, darted into the industrial zone, silent and unseen.

In the grass, the three children held their breath, waiting.

One second... one minute... five minutes...

"Why isn't it back yet..." A-Xin muttered nervously.

"Easy." Qingqing lowered her voice, "Liu-Liu is the fastest in the school."

"But..." A-Xin's heart pounded, "what if it gets caught..."

"It won't." Weixi's voice was unexpectedly calm, "Liu-Liu will come back. Because..." she smiled faintly, "it still has a mission to complete."

Just then—

"Swish!"

A silver-white figure darted back from the other side of the wall at lightning speed!

"Beep beep beep! Mission complete!" Liu-Liu stopped in front of the three, panting, a small bottle in its mouth, "Photos! Water sample! Both secured!"

"Amazing!" A-Xin jumped up excitedly, "Liu-Liu! You're incredible!"

"Let's go! Let's go test the water sample!" Qingqing carefully held the small bottle, "The testing tools we bought with the fair money finally get to be used!"

Back in the equipment room, Qingqing opened the testing toolbox and spread out the test strips and reagents.

"Test the pH first!" Qingqing dipped a test strip into the water sample, "...4.5."

"4.5?!" A-Xin's eyes went wide, "That's more acidic than plum juice!"

"Now test for heavy metals!" Qingqing dropped in the reagent and watched the color change, "...the color is deepening. Comparing against the color chart..."

"How much?" A-Xin asked nervously.

"..." Qingqing looked at the color chart, her face growing paler and paler, "Over the limit. Exceeding the standard by... six times."

"Six times?!" A-Xin gasped, "If this water really flows into the sea..."

"The fish will die." Weixi said softly, "And if people drink it... they'll get sick."

The equipment room fell into silence.

"We have evidence." After a long moment, Qingqing spoke, her voice hoarse, "Photos, water samples, test data... we have evidence."

"But," A-Xin looked at the grayish-green water sample, unwillingness in his tone, "evidence alone isn't enough. We need the adults to believe us!"

"Right." Weixi nodded, "So we also need... a way that the adults can't ignore."

"What way?" Qingqing and A-Xin asked at the same time.

Weixi lowered her head and thought for a very, very long time.

Then she looked up, a resolute light flickering in her eyes:

"We... have to make the whole school see."

"Make the whole school see?" A-Xin froze.

"Mhm." Weixi said, "Post the evidence where the whole school can see it. Let every person see with their own eyes—the water we drink is being polluted."

"But..." Qingqing frowned, "if we post it without permission, won't we..."

"Get punished." Weixi said softly, "But some things matter more than punishment."

In the equipment room, the three children quietly looked at that bottle of grayish-green water.

"...Fine." A-Xin finally said, "Then we'll make the whole school see!"

"But," Weixi added, "before that, we have to do one thing first."

"What?"

"Protect the evidence." Weixi carefully put the water sample into a box, "As long as the evidence is safe, people will listen to our words."

"But..." Liu-Liu panted, its voice carrying a hint of gravity, "I saw some things inside the industrial zone."

"What things?" Qingqing asked.

"I saw," a complex light flickered in Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "that the pipe's source connects to a factory. On the factory's signboard was written..."

Liu-Liu paused, then said the name:

"'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory.'"

"Hongyuan Food Processing Factory..." Weixi murmured the name in repetition, and then, her face suddenly turned pale.

"Weixi?" Qingqing noticed her change, "What's wrong?"

"This name..." Weixi said in a low voice, "I've heard it... in the future."

"Heard of it?" A-Xin froze, "You've heard of this factory?"

"Mhm." Weixi's face grew paler and paler, "In the textbooks of my era... 'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory' is a 'lesson.'"

"What lesson?"

"The textbook says," Weixi's voice trembled slightly, "in 2027, this factory polluted the local water source by secretly discharging wastewater. The river changed color, the fish died, and the children nearby..."

"The children nearby?" Qingqing asked nervously.

"The children nearby," Weixi closed her eyes, "half of them got sick from drinking the polluted water."

"Sick..." A-Xin gasped.

"The textbook says," Weixi said softly, "that if someone had stopped it earlier back then... those children wouldn't have gotten sick."

"Then..." Qingqing's voice was dry, "if we act now..."

"Now," Weixi opened her eyes, a determination never seen before burning in them, "that page in the textbook hasn't been written yet. We..."

"We still have a chance to rewrite it!" A-Xin said loudly.

In the moonlight, the four partners' silhouettes stood quietly in a row outside the industrial zone's wall.

"Then let's rewrite it." Qingqing said.

"Okay—!" The three children, plus one little pet, said in unison.

What they didn't know was that deep inside the industrial zone, in the office of that "Hongyuan Food Processing Factory," a low voice was talking on the phone:

"...Yes. Adjust the discharge time to three o'clock in the deep night."

"Increase the discharge volume again."

"Aren't they going to investigate? Then let them investigate their 'fill.'"

"Anyway... by the time they find the evidence, those pieces of evidence will have already been..."

"cleaned up by us."

In the deep night, the drainage ditch silently flowed with grayish-green water.
