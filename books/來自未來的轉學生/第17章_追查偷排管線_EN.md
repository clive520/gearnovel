# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 17: Tracing the Illegal Discharge Pipe

A week later, the environmental protection bureau's re-test results came out.

"Confirmed over the limit." The inspector stood in the principal's office, his tone serious, "The drainage ditch water sample shows heavy metal concentration at 6.3 times the limit, pH 4.5. We have solid evidence and have officially opened a case."

"Opened a case!" A-Xin was so excited he nearly jumped, "So the factory..."

"Not so fast." The inspector shook his head, "We've sent people to inspect 'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory.' But..."

"But what?" Qingqing asked nervously.

"But," the inspector sighed, "they denied it."

"Denied it?!" The three children's eyes went wide at the same time.

"The factory's owner said," the inspector's expression was grave, "that pipe is an 'old pipe left from long ago,' and hasn't been in use for ages. They said the wastewater in the pipe was 'residue from before,' not something they discharged."

"Liar!" A-Xin stamped his foot in anger, "That pipe was discharging every single day! We have photos!"

"Photos," the inspector said helplessly, "can only prove the pipe 'existed.' They say they don't know where the pipe connects, or why there was wastewater in it. Because..." he paused, "the source of the pipe has already been dug out."

"Dug out..." Qingqing murmured in repetition.

"That's why we need more evidence." The inspector said, "We need to prove that the wastewater 'now' is being discharged from inside the factory. Not..." he shook his head, "'residue from before.'"

"Then how do we prove it?" A-Xin asked.

"..." The inspector didn't answer, only sighed.

Walking out of the principal's office, the three children's faces were grim.

"What do we do..." A-Xin crouched in the hallway, clutching his head, "The evidence is buried, the factory denies everything... did we work for nothing?"

"It won't be for nothing." A soft voice sounded from behind him.

It was Weixi.

"Weixi..." A-Xin looked up, "Do you have a way?"

"I have an idea." Weixi walked in front of them, extending the bracelet on her left wrist, "This bracelet isn't just for counting down. It... has other uses."

"What uses?" Qingqing asked.

"It can 'track' the source of pollution." Weixi said, "As long as it has touched that kind of wastewater, it can remember that 'smell,' and then..." she closed her eyes, "lead us to the place that discharges it."

"Really?!" A-Xin jumped up, "Then let's go!"

"But," A-Xin suddenly thought of something, "how do you know it has this function?"

Weixi was silent for a moment.

"Because... last night, I had a dream." she said in a low voice, "In the dream, a voice said to me—'Your bracelet isn't just for counting down. It also remembers where you came from.'"

"And then?" Qingqing asked.

"Then I woke up," Weixi said, "and found a small mark on the bracelet. I tried bringing the drainage ditch water near it... and it lit up."

"It lit up?" A-Xin leaned in to look.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, gently stroking the pale blue mark on the bracelet, "It seems to... recognize that 'smell.'"

"It's like..." Qingqing thought for a moment, "a dog's nose?"

"Yes." Weixi smiled faintly, "Just like a dog's nose."

"Then that's wonderful!" A-Xin rubbed his hands excitedly, "We have a 'dog's nose'!"

"But," Weixi's smile slowly faded, "every time it 'sniffs,' it consumes energy. The time I have left... may become even less."

"..." A-Xin and Qingqing fell silent.

"It's okay." Weixi looked at them, her tone gentle, "As long as we can get to the bottom of that drainage ditch... this time is well spent."

"But," Weixi shook her head, "its tracking range is limited. And..." she lowered her head, looking at the bracelet, "every use consumes energy."

"Consumes energy..." Qingqing's heart sank, "then your countdown..."

"I know." Weixi said softly, "But if we don't use it... the factory will get away."

"Weixi," Qingqing took her hand, "are you really going to..."

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, her eyes resolute, "This is the purpose of my return. Using my power to make this sea... clean again. Even if..." she lowered her head, her voice soft, "even if it takes all the time I have."

"Weixi..." A-Xin and Qingqing both fell silent.

"And," Weixi looked up and smiled, "we're not alone. We have the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit,' we have Liu-Liu, and..." she looked toward the playground, "Old Wu."

"Old Wu?" A-Xin froze.

"Mhm." Weixi said, "We need someone 'very strong' to help us move the obstacles out of the way."

The next day at noon, two "unexpected visitors" arrived at the principal's office.

"Principal Qiu, it's a pleasure!" A middle-aged man in a tailored suit walked into the office with a smile, handing over a business card, "I'm Wang Fugui, the head of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory."

"Mr. Wang..." Principal Qiu looked at the business card, his expression complex, "please, have a seat."

"Principal," Wang sat down, all smiles, "I'm here today mainly to explain the matter of the 'drainage ditch.'"

"Oh?"

"All a misunderstanding!" Wang waved his hand, "That pipe really is an old pipe! Our factory absolutely does not secretly discharge! These years, we've been a 'model factory'!"

"A model factory?" Dean Zhou stood to the side, speaking coldly, "Then why has the drainage ditch behind the school been polluted?"

"This..." Wang's smile stiffened, "Dean, you can't say that! How do we know it wasn't another factory?"

"Another factory?" Dean Zhou stared at him, "That pipe connects out from behind your factory's back wall. How do you explain that?"

"That!" Wang forced a dry laugh, "That pipe was left over from twenty years ago! We don't know where it connects either! Maybe..." his eyes darted, "it was left by a factory that used to be there!"

"You..." A-Xin hid in the hallway outside the principal's office, listening through the crack in the door, clenching his fists in anger, "blatant lies!"

"A-Xin, keep it down!" Qingqing pulled him back.

"In any case," in the principal's office, Wang stood up, a veiled threat in his tone, "our factory has a clear conscience! If the school insists on suing us... then we'll have no choice but to have our lawyers 'talk' about it."

"Mr. Wang," Principal Qiu was silent for a moment, then said, "we're not trying to sue anyone. We just hope..."

"Hope what?" Wang interrupted him, smiling, "Principal, I advise you to focus on running the school. Let the children's games be games. As for the adults..." he gave Dean Zhou a meaningful look, "it's better not to make things too big."

After Wang left, the principal's office was silent for a long time.

"Principal," Dean Zhou spoke, "he..."

"I know." Principal Qiu sighed, "He wants to 'sweep it under the rug.'"

"Then we..."

"Evidence." Principal Qiu said, "As long as we have evidence, he can't sweep it away."

In the hallway outside, the three children had heard everything.

"He's really awful!" A-Xin stamped his foot, "It's obviously them discharging!"

"It's okay." Weixi said softly, "The more he denies it, the more we need to prove it."

The next day, after school.

"What?! You want me to come along to 'track the pipe'?!" Old Wu's eyes went wide, forgetting to chew the pork bun in his mouth, "Sure, sure! I've long wanted to see what that stinky ditch really looks like!"

"Old Wu," Qingqing lowered her voice, "this matter might involve confronting adults. Are you..."

"What's there to be afraid of!" Old Wu patted his chest, "I'm the captain of the 'Zero-Leftover Lunch Raid Squad'! Even the whole school's leftover food is afraid of me—a stinky ditch is nothing!"

"..." Looking at Old Wu's honest, confident face, A-Xin suddenly felt much more at ease with him around.

"Great!" A-Xin said loudly, "'Track the Pipe · Final Operation' officially begins! Everyone present!"

"Present!" The children responded in unison.

"Then our mission assignments are—" Qingqing spread out the map, "Liu-Liu, aerial reconnaissance! Old Wu, standing watch and clearing the way! A-Xin, drawing the route map! Me, recording the data! Weixi..."

"I," Weixi said softly, "will 'track.'"

"Great! Move out!"

"Wait!" Before setting off, Weixi called everyone back.

"What's wrong?" A-Xin turned around.

"We... need to be mentally prepared." Weixi said softly, "If we get caught, we might get demerits, detention, parents called in... even threatened by the factory people."

"..." The children fell silent.

"But," Weixi looked at them, "even if we get punished, we still have to do it. Right?"

"Right!" A-Xin was the first to answer loudly, "We're the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'!"

"Right!" Qingqing followed.

"I..." Old Wu hesitated, then nodded hard, "I'm with you too! But..." he patted his belly, "can we buy a pork bun first? I'm hungry, and I won't have the strength to climb walls!"

"Hahaha!" The children burst into laughter, and the tense atmosphere instantly dispersed.

"Let's go!" A-Xin waved his hand grandly, "We'll buy some on the way! One for each!"

"Yay!"

And so, the four children, plus Liu-Liu, carrying pork buns, set out in a grand procession toward the industrial zone.

On the way, Old Wu munched on his pork bun and asked: "Weixi, can that bracelet of yours really find 'where the wastewater is discharged'?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "It remembers that smell."

"Wow! Isn't that 'future technology'?" Old Wu marveled, "Even better than our dog!"

"..." Weixi froze for a moment, then smiled softly, "Yes. Even better than... a dog."

"Weixi," Old Wu suddenly turned serious, "where are you actually from? How do you know everything?"

Weixi's smile stiffened.

"I..." she lowered her head, silent for a moment, "I'm from... a very faraway place."

"Far away?" Old Wu tilted his head, "Farther than Taichung?"

"Much, much farther than Taichung," Weixi said softly.

"Then that means..." Old Wu thought for a moment, "the kind of place you have to fly a very long time to reach?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "A very, very long time."

"That must be so tiring!" Old Wu said sympathetically, "Then you should live next door to us! I'll share half a pork bun with you every day!"

"..." Looking at Old Wu's honest face, Weixi's eyes suddenly felt a little hot.

"Alright." She lowered her head, softly, "Then I'll live next door to you."

That evening, the setting sun stretched the four children's shadows very, very long.

They walked along the drainage ditch, step by step, toward the industrial zone.

"Liu-Liu, aerial reconnaissance!" A-Xin commanded.

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu soared into the air, scanning beams shooting from its electronic eyes, "Reconnaissance begins! Three hundred meters ahead is the edge of the industrial zone!"

"Old Wu, there's a clump of weeds blocking the way ahead!" A-Xin pointed forward, "You handle it!"

"Leave it to me!" Old Wu rolled up his sleeves and pushed the weeds down with a few sweeps, "Clear!"

"Weixi," Qingqing looked at her nervously, "your bracelet..."

"Mhm." Weixi extended her left wrist, bringing the bracelet close to the surface of the drainage ditch water, "Beginning to track..."

A faint blue light rippled across the bracelet's surface.

"...Found it." After a few seconds, Weixi said softly, "That 'smell' is still there. It comes from... that direction."

She pointed into the depths of the industrial zone.

"Over there?" Liu-Liu flew higher to look, "Over there is... the back wall of 'Hongyuan Food Processing Factory'!"

"It really is them!" A-Xin clenched his fist.

"But," Qingqing frowned, "didn't they say the pipe is 'old'? How do we prove it's still discharging now?"

"The evidence," Weixi said, "is behind that wall."

"You mean..." A-Xin's eyes lit up, "we climb over?"

"We can't climb over." Weixi shook her head, "But... we can 'see.'"

"See?"

"Mhm." Weixi reached out and gently pressed the bracelet, "Let the bracelet show us what's behind the wall."

The bracelet's blue light suddenly grew bright, projecting a translucent image in midair—

Inside it was the interior of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory.

In the factory's backyard was an area enclosed by iron sheeting. Behind the iron wall, an old machine could be faintly seen, still running.

A pipe from the machine was steadily discharging blackish-green liquid into the ground.

"This is..." The children gasped.

"They're still discharging!" A-Xin said loudly, "Right now! This very moment!"

"And," Weixi's face turned slightly pale, "that machine doesn't exist in the factory's 'registered equipment.' It's a..." she said in a low voice, "machine hidden behind the iron wall, never registered."

"Evidence!" Qingqing's voice was trembling, "This is the evidence! Proof that they're 'still' discharging!"

"But," A-Xin grimaced, "how do we get the adults to see this image?"

Weixi fell silent.

"Liu-Liu," after a long moment, she spoke, "can you... photograph this image?"

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu looked at that translucent projection, "I can try. But... my camera might not be able to capture a 'projection.'"

"Then..." Weixi lowered her head, thinking for a very, very long time, and then she made a decision that even startled Qingqing—

"We'll go see it with our own eyes."

"See it with our own eyes?!" A-Xin's eyes went wide, "You mean..."

"Climb over that iron wall." Weixi said, "See that machine, that pipe, that wastewater with our own eyes—and photograph it."

"But..." Qingqing looked at her nervously, "what if we get caught..."

"Then so be it." Weixi said softly, "Some things matter more than fear."

"..." A-Xin looked at Weixi's resolute eyes and took a deep breath, "Alright! Let's go!"

"But, that iron wall is so high..." Old Wu frowned, "how do we get over?"

"Have you forgotten?" A-Xin patted Old Wu's shoulder, "We have someone here who's 'very strong'!"

"Me?!" Old Wu froze, then his eyes lit up, "You mean... I carry you all over?!"

"Right!" A-Xin nodded, "You're the 'human ladder'! Hoist us up one by one!"

"This..." Old Wu rubbed his hands, "This is my specialty! I've carried the heaviest leftover food barrel in the school! Carrying the three of you is nothing!"

"But..." Qingqing looked at Old Wu worriedly, "Old Wu, you don't know..." she hesitated, "why we're trying so hard."

"I know!" Old Wu declared with full confidence, "Because we have to protect the Earth! I get that!"

"No..." Qingqing shook her head, "I mean, Weixi, she..."

"Qingqing." Weixi interrupted her, shaking her head.

"..." Qingqing was silent for a moment, then sighed, "Forget it. Anyway, let's be careful."

"Alright!" Old Wu crouched down and patted his shoulders, "Come on! Up you go!"

And so, that evening, outside the iron wall of Hongyuan Food Processing Factory, this scene appeared—

Old Wu crouched by the wall, serving as the "human ladder"; A-Xin stepped on Old Wu's shoulder and climbed over the iron wall; then Qingqing and Weixi, one after another, climbed into the factory's backyard.

"Slow down!" A-Xin lowered his voice, "Watch out for that machine!"

"Got it..." Qingqing raised her phone, trembling, aiming it at the machine, "that machine... is really discharging!"

"The blackish-green liquid..." Weixi looked at that pipe, "exactly the same as the water in the drainage ditch."

"Photographed!" Qingqing's phone sounded with a crisp shutter click, "I got it! That machine, that pipe, that wastewater... all photographed!"

"Great!" A-Xin breathed a sigh of relief, "We finally..."

"BEEP BEEP BEEP—!!!"

Suddenly, a piercing alarm split the silence of the factory backyard!

"Alarm!" Liu-Liu shouted in panic, "There are guards! They've activated the security system!"

"Run!" A-Xin shouted, "Back the way we came!"

The children scrambled toward the iron wall in a panic.

But on the other side of the iron wall, a strong flashlight beam was already shining over!

"Who's there?!" A hoarse voice shouted, "Stop right there!"

"Oh no!" Qingqing's face instantly turned deathly pale.

"Don't be afraid!" Old Wu grabbed the iron wall, "Quick! Climb up! I'll hold them off!"

"Old Wu!" A-Xin shouted.

"Go!" Old Wu roared, "Don't waste time! I'll hoist you up! Go!"

The children stepped on Old Wu's shoulders, one by one, climbing over the iron wall.

"Old Wu!" A-Xin shouted from the other side of the wall, "Hurry up and come up!"

"I..." Old Wu looked back at the flashlight beam growing closer and closer, "I... I can't make it!"

"Old Wu!!!"

"Never mind me!" Old Wu shouted loudly, "You run first! Protect the evidence!"

"But—"

"RUN!!!"

The children's eyes turned red instantly.

"Old Wu..." A-Xin gritted his teeth, "You wait! We'll definitely come back for you!"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu flipped around and dashed back, "I'm staying! I'm fast! I'll lead Old Wu out!"

"Liu-Liu!"

"GO!!!"

A-Xin, Qingqing, and Weixi, three people, clutching the evidence in the phone, ran desperately toward the school.

Behind them, the factory's alarm still blared.

That night, the three children waited in the equipment room until dark.

"Old Wu... Liu-Liu..." A-Xin's eyes were red, "they... they'll be okay, right?"

"They will." Weixi's voice trembled slightly, "Liu-Liu runs so fast... it will definitely..."

"But," Qingqing's voice was choked too, "what if Old Wu gets caught..."

"He won't get caught." Weixi clenched her fist, "Because Liu-Liu will protect him. Just like..." she lowered her head, "just like we're protecting the Earth."

The night grew deeper and deeper.

The light in the equipment room stayed on the whole time.

Until eleven o'clock at night—

"BEEP BEEP BEEP BEEP!!!"

A silver-white figure burst in through the ventilation duct!

"Liu-Liu!" The three children jumped up at the same time, "Where's Old Wu?!"

"Beep beep... beep beep..." Liu-Liu panted, but excitement flickered in its electronic eyes, "he... he's fine! We... we shook them off!"

"Old Wu didn't get caught?!" A-Xin's tears welled up instantly, "Great... that's great..."

"But," Liu-Liu panted, "Old Wu is now hiding in the grass outside the industrial zone, not daring to move. He said..." Liu-Liu's electronic eyes flickered, "he said, 'You guys go first, I'll cover the rear! I'm just a foodie—they won't suspect me!'"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed and cried at once, "This idiot!"

"Liu-Liu, how did you two escape?" Qingqing asked nervously.

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu panted and began its account, "I was chased by the guard, ran half a circle, and circled around to the weed pile behind the factory..."

"Then?"

"Then," a hint of smugness flickered in Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "I saw Old Wu, crouching in the weed pile, chewing a pork bun, completely still."

"He's still in the mood for a pork bun?!" A-Xin's eyes went wide.

"He said," Liu-Liu imitated Old Wu's tone, "'Eat your fill, then you'll have the strength to run!'"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed and fumed at the same time.

"Later," Liu-Liu continued, "when the guard's flashlight shone over, Old Wu suddenly stood up, spread his arms wide, and shouted at the guard—'Officer! I'm just passing by!'"

"Just passing by?!"

"Yes!" Liu-Liu nodded, "And then he ran all the way toward the school! The guard chased him for two streets, and finally, probably scared off by his weight, gave up."

"Hahaha!" The children laughed until tears came out, "Old Wu! You really are..."

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu's voice, however, suddenly dropped low, "But... while I was running away, I saw some things."

"What?"

"I saw," Liu-Liu said, "that machine, after we ran off... was dismantled by them at top speed."

"Dismantled?!" The children's hearts sank.

"Mhm." Liu-Liu nodded, "Even the iron wall was completely dismantled. Now... that machine no longer exists."

"Then the evidence we photographed..." Qingqing said trembling, "wouldn't it..."

"Be useless." Weixi said softly, "The machine's gone, so the evidence is gone."

The equipment room fell into silence.

"...It's okay." After a long moment, Weixi spoke, her voice soft yet exceptionally resolute, "We still have another way."

"What way?" A-Xin asked.

"I remember," Weixi said, "the 'smell' of that machine. The bracelet remembers it too. As long as..." she lowered her head, looking at her bracelet, "I still have energy, we still have a chance."

"Weixi..." Qingqing looked at her, "your bracelet..."

"Fifteen days." Weixi said softly, "Enough."

"Enough..." A-Xin murmured in repetition, then nodded hard, "Yes! Enough! Fifteen days is enough for us to catch the real culprit behind that pipe!"

The moonlight shone quietly on the equipment room window.

The four children, plus Liu-Liu, silently made the same wish in the night—

"We will definitely make that drainage ditch clear again."
