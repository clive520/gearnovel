# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 14: A-Xin's Discovery & the Expanding Alliance

A-Xin had a secret.

A secret that even Qingqing didn't know.

That secret was—hidden inside A-Xin's backpack was a small notebook.

On the cover of the notebook was drawn a crooked Earth.

On the first page of the notebook was written:

"Weixi Observation Record (No. 1)"

"October: Transfer student arrived. Predicted the afternoon rain accurately on her first day. Suspicion index: ★★"

"She said she came from 'a very, very faraway place, a place she could never go back to.' Suspicion index: ★★★★"

"The bracelet on her wrist—Liu-Liu said it 'doesn't belong to this world.' Suspicion index: ★★★★★★"

"November: Watching a flood on the news, she murmured 'just like before.' 'Before'? Which before? Suspicion index: ★★★★★★★★"

"In front of the withered tree in the park, she said 'in our place, there are also many trees, just like you.' Where is 'our place'? Suspicion index: ★★★★★★★★★★"

"December: At the beach cleanup, she suddenly burst into tears, saying 'the whole beach is covered in trash, taller than people.' But there was clearly no trash mountain in front of us. Suspicion index: ★★★★★★★★★★★★"

"On fair day, she wrote a letter addressed to 'myself forty years from now.' Suspicion index: ★★★★★★★★★★★★★★"

"January: She zones out more and more often. The numbers on her bracelet keep dropping. Suspicion index: ★★★★★★★★★★★★★★★★"

"Conclusion: Weixi is..."

The notebook contained even more densely packed records:

"October 15th: She called it again. Said it would rain at three in the afternoon, and it really rained. She said she can 'hear the voice of the wind.' When I listen to the wind, all I hear is leaves rustling."

"October 20th: She said 'hello' to the phoenix tree outside the window. Saying hello to a tree? Well, maybe she's just polite."

"November 2nd: In natural science class, the teacher talked about 'global warming.' She didn't say a word the whole period. After class, she stood alone in the hallway, staring at the sky for a very, very long time."

"November 18th: She said the cabbage at lunch was 'shipped from the farm at the foot of the mountain three days ago.' I asked how she knew, and she said 'they told me.' Can vegetables talk?"

"December 8th: She stared at the drainage ditch behind the school for a full five minutes. That ditch stinks; I don't even want to stay near it for a second."

"December 25th: Christmas. She gave everyone in the class a hand-drawn little card. Mine said 'To A-Xin: You're the best King of Ideas.' How did she know I like being the King of Ideas?"

"January 5th: She zoned out again. This time during lunch, eyes staring straight ahead. I called her name three times before she snapped back and said 'sorry' to me."

"January 10th: Fair day. She wrote a letter and dropped it into the 'To the Future' mailbox. I secretly peeked at her letter—it was addressed to 'myself forty years from now.'"

"January 12th: After school, she sat alone on the seawall. In the sunset, she looked... so lonely."

"January 14th: Today, she said something that made me decide I have to find out her secret."

"She said: 'A-Xin, if one day I'm gone, please keep protecting the Earth.'"

"When she said those words, there was a look in her eyes... like someone who already knew she was going to leave."

A-Xin held his pen, hesitating for a very, very long time, before writing two words below that line:

"...from the future."

"If she's from the future," A-Xin looked out the window and murmured to himself, "then why did she come here?"

"And," he lowered his head, looking at the last record in his notebook, "why do the numbers on her bracelet keep dropping?"

That afternoon, A-Xin made a decision.

He was going to find out what Weixi did after school.

The school dismissal bell rang, and the children filed out of the school gate in twos and threes.

Weixi didn't go home.

She walked alone, slowly, along the seawall behind the school.

A-Xin hid behind the bushes, following her carefully.

Weixi walked to the end of the seawall and sat down, looking at that murky gray sea in the distance.

Then, she did something that made A-Xin's heart clench.

She lowered her head, looked at the bracelet on her wrist, and said softly:

"Twenty-four now."

"Twenty-four?" A-Xin hid in the bushes, holding his breath, "What's twenty-four?!"

"Last week it was still thirty..." Weixi murmured softly, "at this rate..."

"At this rate, what will happen?" A-Xin's heart beat faster and faster.

Just then—

"Huh?" Weixi suddenly looked up, her eyes losing focus, her voice changing tone, "This... where is this...?"

Her pupils instantly became vacant, her whole body stiffening in place as if her soul had been drained away.

"Weixi!" A-Xin could no longer hide; he rushed out of the bushes and ran to her, "Weixi! What's wrong?!"

Weixi didn't respond.

Her eyes stared straight ahead, her mouth murmuring: "Gray-gray sky... so much water... flooding over..."

"Weixi!!" A-Xin grabbed her shoulders and shook her hard, "Wake up!! Look clearly! This is the seawall at Luyang Elementary! No water is flooding over!"

"A-Xin..." Weixi's eyes slowly refocused, "how... are you here?"

"I..." A-Xin panted, "I was worried about you, so..."

Before he could finish, his gaze was caught by the rapidly jumping numbers on Weixi's wrist.

"Twenty-seven... twenty-six... twenty-five..." A-Xin's eyes went wide, "this number is still dropping!"

Weixi's face turned white in an instant.

She yanked her sleeve down, covering the bracelet.

"A-Xin!" Her voice carried unprecedented panic, "you... you saw it?"

"I saw it." A-Xin looked straight at her, his tone carrying a seriousness even he hadn't noticed, "Weixi, tell me, what exactly is that number?"

Weixi was silent for a very, very long time.

So long that A-Xin thought she would never answer.

Then, Weixi spoke.

"A-Xin," her voice was light as a feather, "if I told you... that I came from the future. Would you believe me?"

A-Xin's heart felt like it was being gripped by a hand.

"...I believe you." he said.

Weixi froze.

"You believe me?" she asked in disbelief, "why... why would you believe me?"

"Because..." A-Xin pulled out that notebook with the Earth drawn on it, "I've been recording your 'suspicious behavior' since October."

"You predicted the rain, you could name where the vegetables came from, you talked to withered trees, you watched the news and said 'just like before,' you saw a trash mountain at the beach that no one else could see, you mailed a letter to yourself forty years from now... and this bracelet."

A-Xin pointed at Weixi's wrist: "Liu-Liu said its frequency band 'doesn't belong to this world.' And you just said its number dropped from thirty to twenty-four."

"A-Xin..." Weixi's voice trembled slightly, "how long have you... been watching me?"

"Since your first day at school." A-Xin said.

Weixi lowered her head.

The wind blew across the seawall.

After a long time, she finally spoke softly:

"Then... do you want to know the truth?"

"Yes." A-Xin nodded without hesitation, "But if you don't want to tell me, I won't force you. I just..."

"Just what?"

"Just want you to know," A-Xin looked at her, his eyes serious in a way unlike his usual self, "no matter where you came from, no matter how many secrets you carry—you're a member of our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit.' Your matters are my matters."

Weixi's eyes suddenly turned red.

"A-Xin..." she sniffled and finally made up her mind, "Alright. I'll tell you."

So, that evening, on the seawall, A-Xin listened to Weixi's story.

She said that forty years from now, the Earth's sea level would be nearly a meter higher than now. The Taichung Harbor would be underwater, and the seawall of Longjing would become a broken, crumbling wall of ruins.

She said that when she was little, she had never known any weather other than "rain that never ends." Summers often exceeded forty degrees, and the asphalt softened like pudding.

She said that her school had a class called "Climate Class." In the textbook were written the news of this era—the 2026 glacier collapse, the Super El Niño, the record-breaking floods. The teacher said: "These are all history."

She said she had always thought these "history" events were from a very, very long time ago. Until one day, her grandmother pointed at a yellowed photograph and said to her: "Weixi ah, the sea of Grandma's childhood didn't look like this."

"Then Grandma," she asked, "what did the sea originally look like?"

Her grandmother was silent for a long time, then said: "Grandma almost forgot too. But I remember, it used to be blue."

"A blue sea..." Weixi said to A-Xin, "that was when I learned that the sea could be blue."

She said that from that day on, she kept thinking—if only she could go back in time and tell those people, "Every thing you do now will become our future," how wonderful that would be.

"Then," Weixi said in a low voice, "I really was sent back."

"Sent back?" A-Xin asked, "Who sent you back?"

"It was... scientists of the future." Weixi said, "They said they tested all the time machines, and only one succeeded. They said I was 'the most suitable candidate.'"

"Why you?"

"Because..." Weixi lowered her head, "because I remember this era. Of all the candidates, I was the only one who still remembered what a 'blue sea' looked like."

He listened to her tell how the Earth of forty years from now had gradually turned grayish-yellow.

He listened to her tell how she had been chosen, and how she had returned to this era carrying the bracelet.

He listened to her tell how she had to return to the future before the energy ran out, or else the timeline would collapse and everyone would disappear.

He listened to her tell how little time she had left.

"So," A-Xin listened, and after a long silence, finally asked, "the number on your bracelet is the 'days' you have left?"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "From fifty-seven at the very beginning, to twenty-four now."

"Twenty-four days..." A-Xin murmured in repetition, "only twenty-four days left..."

"A-Xin," Weixi looked at him, a plea in her eyes, "this matter—only Qingqing knows. Now, there's you too."

"I hope... you won't tell the others. I don't want everyone to..."

"Worry about you." A-Xin finished for her, "I understand."

He lowered his head and looked at his own notebook.

"Weixi," he suddenly spoke, "do you know? Actually, I guessed it a long time ago."

"Guessed what?"

"Guessed that you don't belong here." A-Xin said, "But I never knew how to face it. Because..."

"Because what?"

"Because..." A-Xin looked up, his eyes slightly reddened, "if you really go back... then we'll never see you again."

Weixi froze.

Looking at A-Xin's face, so rare in its seriousness, tears suddenly fell without warning.

"A-Xin..." she choked, "you..."

"Don't cry!" A-Xin panicked, flailing his hands, "I-I was just kidding! I-I'm the worst at saying things like that! Don't cry!"

"Who's crying!" Weixi broke into a smile through her tears, wiping her eyes, "I just... got sand in my eyes."

"Fine, fine! Sand! It's all the sand's fault!" A-Xin quickly agreed, "Blame this sea!"

On the seawall, two children, one crying and one laughing, made a racket together.

And at the other end of the seawall, Qingqing stood quietly behind a tree, watching this scene, the corners of her mouth lifting slightly.

"Just as I thought," she said in a low voice, "you can't hide anything from A-Xin."

That night, in the sports equipment room.

The Secret Alliance's "command center" welcomed its third member for the first time.

"So," A-Xin crossed his arms, sitting on the equipment box, his expression serious, "the current situation is: Weixi has twenty-four days left, and the pollution in the drainage ditch is still worsening."

"Right." Qingqing nodded, "And that pipe's discharge speed is getting faster every day."

"Then what do we do?" A-Xin asked.

"Our original plan was," Weixi said, "before the countdown ends, do our best to make everyone learn to protect the Earth. Then..."

"Then, you go back." A-Xin finished for her, "right?"

Weixi lowered her head and softly said, "Mhm."

"But," A-Xin suddenly stood up, a fighting spirit in his voice, "I have a better idea!"

"What idea?" Qingqing and Weixi asked in unison.

"Since we only have twenty-four days," A-Xin's eyes sparkled, "then let's not dawdle with 'taking it slow'! We're going to—'double up'! 'Speed up'! Use the remaining time twice as hard as others!"

"Double up?" Weixi froze.

"Yes!" A-Xin nodded hard, "We can use the 'Campaign' as our cover and mobilize the whole class! And the three of us will secretly speed things up behind the scenes!"

"You mean..." Qingqing's eyes lit up too.

"Bring the 'Track the Pipe' operation forward!" A-Xin said, "We have Liu-Liu's reconnaissance data, don't we? And the money raised at the fair? Then let's just—investigate to the end! Find out who's behind that pipe!"

"But..." Weixi hesitated, "won't that drag everyone into danger?"

"No!" A-Xin patted his chest, "We'll protect everyone! And..." he looked at Weixi seriously, "Weixi, you only have twenty-four days left. Instead of having everyone 'learn slowly,' let everyone 'see with their own eyes'—see how we track a polluted drainage ditch to the end!"

Weixi fell silent.

She looked at A-Xin's determined face, then at Qingqing's resolute face.

"...Alright." She smiled softly, "Then let's speed up!"

"Okay—!" A-Xin jumped up excitedly, "'Track the Pipe · Extreme Speed Operation' begins!"

"Beep beep! Count me in!" Liu-Liu popped out of the ventilation duct, "I have the latest reconnaissance data!"

"Of course you're in!" A-Xin waved his hand grandly, "You're our 'Intel Chief'!"

"Beep beep beep! At your command!" Liu-Liu spun around excitedly.

"Alright, let's plan!" A-Xin jumped off the equipment box and spread out Liu-Liu's reconnaissance map, "Liu-Liu, your latest data!"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu jumped to the map, "Based on today's reconnaissance: that pipe is located three kilometers upstream of the drainage ditch, hidden in the weeds. Discharge schedule: irregular, but the volume is highest at night. Discharge substance: grayish-green liquid, containing heavy metals, pH 4.7."

"Highest at night?" Qingqing frowned, "Doesn't that mean... they're 'sneakily discharging'?"

"Sneakily discharging?" A-Xin's eyes lit up, "I like that term! Discharging in secret, afraid of the light!"

"Then how do we investigate?" Weixi asked.

"We have Liu-Liu's data, the money from the fair, and the smartest 'thinking' brains in the whole school!" A-Xin crossed his arms, "Next, what we need to do is—"

"Find that pipe," Qingqing continued, "and then, follow the pipe to its 'owner'!"

"Right!" A-Xin slapped his thigh, "Find the owner, and we'll know exactly who's poisoning our sea!"

"But," Weixi said worriedly, "if the other side is adults... what can we kids do?"

"Adults?" A-Xin chuckled, "Weixi, have you forgotten? Our class's 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' even has Dean Zhou supporting us! What's so great about adults? We have 'evidence'!"

"Evidence..."

"Yes!" A-Xin said loudly, "As long as we get the evidence and hand it to the principal, to Dean Zhou, to the reporters—the adults can't pretend not to see anymore!"

Looking at A-Xin's confident face, Weixi felt the tension that had been holding her heart loosen just a little.

"...Alright." She smiled, "Then let's investigate together!"

In the equipment room, the silhouettes of four partners overlapped completely for the first time under the dim light.

"Oh right," Qingqing suddenly remembered something, "what about Old Wu? What's he been up to lately?"

"Old Wu?" A-Xin thought for a moment, "Lately, he's been... researching new lunch recipes."

"New recipes?"

"Yeah!" A-Xin laughed heartily, "He says he's going to invent a 'pork bun that saves the Earth' using 'ugly produce'! He has no idea what the three of us are up to!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed together.

"It's fine," Weixi said softly, "let him keep happily eating his pork buns."

"Of course!" A-Xin patted his chest, "Old Wu's mission is to eat and drink well, and save his strength for our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'!"

The next morning, A-Xin began his "acting campaign."

"Weixi, good morning!" A-Xin greeted her with a face that said "I know nothing" as he entered the classroom.

"Morning..." Weixi looked at his overly bright smile and couldn't help reminding him, "A-Xin, you look... even more suspicious like this."

"Do I?" A-Xin blinked and quickly put away his smile, "How about this?"

"..." Weixi looked at his instantly stern poker face and sighed helplessly, "Forget it. Just smile."

"Haha! I knew I was born to act!" A-Xin was beside himself.

"A-Xin!" Qingqing walked over and lowered her voice, "Keep it down! Do you want the whole class to know?!"

"Uh!" A-Xin quickly covered his mouth, "Sorry, sorry! I'm just too excited! I was so excited last night I couldn't sleep!"

"Excited about what?" Old Wu, chewing a pork bun, leaned in to ask.

"Uh..." A-Xin was startled and thought fast, "I-I'm excited about... today's lunch!"

"Lunch?" Old Wu's eyes lit up, "How do you know what's for lunch?"

"I..." A-Xin broke into a cold sweat, "I guessed!"

"Then guess!" Old Wu leaned in excitedly, "Is it chicken legs or pork ribs today?"

"Ch-chicken legs?" A-Xin guessed at random.

"Wrong!" Old Wu announced triumphantly, "Today is braised chicken wings! I passed by the kitchen this morning and smelled them!"

"Braised chicken wings..." A-Xin breathed a sigh of relief and wiped his cold sweat, "Ri-right! The chicken legs I meant are 'the legs of the wings'!"

"Chicken wings don't have legs!" Old Wu teased.

"A wing is a chicken's 'hand'! So the chicken's 'leg' is the meat near the wing!" A-Xin rambled, "Anyway, braised chicken wings! Awesome!"

"..." Old Wu stared at him for two seconds, shook his head, and walked away, "You're acting weird today."

"Weird is good!" A-Xin said to himself, "That way I won't be discovered!"

However, A-Xin's "acting" didn't last through the morning.

During the second-period break, Weixi stood alone in the hallway, staring out the window in a daze.

A-Xin immediately sidled over and asked quietly: "Weixi, are you having a 'flashback' again?"

"No." Weixi shook her head, "I'm just... looking at those five smokestacks."

"Smokestacks?" A-Xin looked up, "Oh! The Taichung Thermal Power Plant's smokestacks! They puff white smoke all day!"

"Mhm." Weixi said softly, "I'm thinking... if one day those smokestacks stop smoking, will our sky turn blue?"

"They definitely will!" A-Xin patted his chest, "Once we find out the truth about the pipe and teach everyone to protect the Earth... one day, the smokestacks will stop!"

"Mhm." Weixi smiled, "That day will definitely come."

"And," A-Xin lowered his voice mysteriously, "I've already started planning the 'Extreme Speed Operation'! Tomorrow after school, we go for 'on-site reconnaissance'!"

"Alright." Weixi nodded softly, "Then we'll see each other tomorrow."

"See you tomorrow!" A-Xin finished, then added, "No wait, it's 'see you today, see you tomorrow, see you every day'! Until you have to go back, we'll see each other every day!"

Weixi froze for a moment.

Then she lowered her head and smiled softly.

"...Mhm." she said, "See you every day."

Late that night, A-Xin went home and lay in bed, tossing and turning, unable to fall asleep.

He stared at the ceiling, Weixi's words echoing in his mind.

"I must return to the future before the energy runs out, or else the timeline will collapse and everyone will disappear."

"Everyone will disappear..." A-Xin murmured in repetition, "which means... if Weixi can't go back, then we... will all vanish?"

"Then..." he suddenly sat up, "then we definitely have to 'send her back'!"

"But..." he lowered his head and looked at his own hands, "if we really send her back... then we'll never see her again."

The moonlight outside the window shone quietly on him.

A-Xin's eyes suddenly felt a little sour.

"Weixi," he said in a low voice, "don't worry. Before you go back, we'll make this world right."

"Then..." he closed his eyes, "I'll say goodbye to you properly."
