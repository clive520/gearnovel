# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 22: The Final Countdown

Six days became five.

Five days became four.

"Weixi," on the third morning, Qingqing noticed that Weixi's face had turned white as paper, "your bracelet..."

"It dropped another two digits." Weixi lowered her head and looked at the bracelet, her voice weak, "Now... only four days left."

"Four days..." The children fell silent.

"But it was still six days yesterday!" A-Xin's eyes were red, "Why is it dropping so fast?!"

"Because..." Weixi said softly, "after the factory was shut down, the 'future' of this era has already begun to change."

"Change?" The children were puzzled.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "When the future changes, the timeline begins to 'correct' itself. And I..." she lowered her head, "am the 'error' that doesn't belong to this era. So... the timeline will 'push' me back."

"Push you back..." Lin Yunyun's voice carried tears, "Then... then you..."

"I'll go back." Weixi smiled faintly, "Before the time node closes."

"When... will it close?" Qingqing asked.

Weixi was silent for a moment, then said: "I don't know. But... the bracelet tells me it's coming soon."

"Soon..." The children's hearts sank.

"So," Weixi looked at all twenty-six people in the class, her tone gentle, "in the time we have left, we need to cherish every minute."

That day, Weixi's condition receded like the tide, little by little.

"Weixi, are you thirsty?" "Weixi, do you want to rest?" "Weixi..."

The children gathered around her, asking endlessly.

"I'm fine." Weixi always said that, "I'm just... a little tired."

But everyone could see it.

Weixi's speech had slowed down.

Weixi's walking had slowed down.

Weixi's smile had slowed down too.

"Weixi," on the afternoon of the fourth day, Qingqing finally couldn't help asking, "are you... about to..."

"Mhm." Weixi didn't hide it, nodding softly, "The bracelet tells me... the time node may open tomorrow evening."

"Tomorrow evening?!" The children gasped.

"Which means..." A-Xin asked tremblingly, "you... are leaving tomorrow?"

"Maybe." Weixi lowered her head, her voice soft, "Maybe tomorrow evening. Maybe..." she didn't finish.

"Weixi..." The children's eyes all reddened.

"So," Weixi looked up, trying to smile, "tomorrow will be... my last day in this era."

"The last day..." The children repeated.

"I want to spend the last day," Weixi said softly, "properly, with all of you."

"..." The children fell silent.

"Will you..." Weixi asked, "keep me company?"

"Yes!" The children shouted in unison, "We'll keep you company!"

"..." The children fell silent.

"Weixi," A-Xin suddenly spoke, his voice hoarse, "is there anything you... haven't finished yet?"

Weixi thought for a moment.

"Yes." she said, "I want to... take a proper photo with everyone."

"A photo?" The children froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "I want to remember each of your faces properly. That way..." she lowered her head, "even after I go back, I can... see you again in my dreams."

"..." The children's eyes reddened.

"Alright!" A-Xin said loudly, "Then we'll take photos! Right under the phoenix tree!"

That night, Weixi couldn't sleep.

She lay in bed, looking at the starry sky through the window, unwilling to close her eyes.

"Tomorrow..." she said in a low voice, "will be the last day."

"I want to..." she smiled softly, "remember every second."

The next morning, Weixi woke up earlier than everyone.

She stood at the classroom door, looking at the empty classroom.

"Good morning, classroom." she said softly.

"Good morning, blackboard."

"Good morning, desks and chairs."

"Good morning... Class 6-1."

She walked to the window and touched the seed pot on the windowsill.

"Grow up well," she said in a low voice, "and... watch this world for me."

"Weixi!" Voices sounded at the classroom door, "Morning!"

"Good morning!" Weixi turned around, showing a brilliant smile, "Today... is my last day here!"

"The last day..." The children were silent for a moment, then A-Xin said loudly: "Then we'll spend it, joyfully!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "Joyfully!"

That day, the children took Weixi to do everything she wanted to do.

They played a game in the classroom.

They ran a lap on the playground.

They sang a song under the phoenix tree.

"At the crossroads where phoenix blossoms bloom..." Weixi sang softly with the children, "friends I cherish..."

As they sang, the children's eyes all reddened.

"Stop, stop!" A-Xin shouted, "Why are we singing a crying song! Let's sing 'Two Tigers'!"

"Two tigers, two tigers..." The children broke into smiles through their tears and sang the nursery rhyme.

"Running fast, running fast..."

Standing under the phoenix tree, watching these children laughing and crying, Weixi felt that this moment was the happiest time of her life.

"Under the phoenix tree..." Weixi froze for a moment, then smiled softly, "Alright."

That morning, the children invited the homeroom teacher and took a group photo under the phoenix tree at the school gate.

"Everyone, look at the camera!" The homeroom teacher raised her phone, "Three, two, one—"

"Yay—!"

The children laughed together. Weixi stood in the middle, smiling softly.

"One more!" A-Xin shouted, "This time... Weixi stands in front!"

"Yes! Weixi in front!"

The children pushed Weixi to the front, and then, twenty-six people formed a semicircle behind her.

"Cheese!"

"Chee—se—!"

"Click!"

That moment was forever frozen in the photograph.

In the photo, the phoenix tree bloomed with fiery red flowers.

In the photo, twenty-six children smiled brighter than the flowers.

In the photo, the girl standing in front had a glistening tear at the corner of her eye.

"Oh right! I have an idea!" A-Xin suddenly said loudly, "Let's each write a message for Weixi!"

"Write a message?" The children froze.

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "Just like the 'Letter to the Future' at the fair! This time, it's for 'Weixi'!"

"Alright!" The children said in unison.

And so, twenty-six children, each with a small card, wrote messages for Weixi.

"To Weixi: Thank you for teaching us to sort trash! Now when I see a PET bottle, I think of you!"—Lin Yunyun

"To Weixi: Your pork bun was the most delicious pork bun I ever ate!—No, the one I made is the best!"—Old Wu

"To Weixi: I'll work hard to grow up and become an adult who can save the Earth!"—Ke Zhiyuan

"To Weixi: Thank you for making me in charge of 'laughing'! I'll keep laughing forever!"—Wang Zhuankai

"To Weixi:..." A-Xin thought for a very, very long time, and finally wrote down one sentence:

"To Weixi: You are our class's 'Eyes of the Future.' When you return to the future, remember to look up—the sky there will be bluer, because of us."

"A-Xin..." Weixi read those cards, and tears fell drop by drop.

"Why are you crying again!" A-Xin panicked, "I-I didn't mean to make you cry!"

"No..." Weixi choked, "It's because... you wrote so well..."

"Then..." A-Xin thought for a moment, "then we'll write you a card every year, okay?"

"Every year..." Weixi lowered her head, "but... can the letters you write reach the future?"

"They definitely will!" Qingqing said loudly, "Because the letters we wrote at the fair... must have all been delivered!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "They'll definitely be delivered!"

"..." Looking at the children, Weixi's eyes glistened.

"Mhm." she said softly, "I believe you."

"Weixi," A-Xin looked at the photo and asked, "can this photo... be developed?"

"It can be developed." Weixi said, "Photos are things that stay."

"That's good." A-Xin smiled, "Then in the future, when we miss you, we can look at the photo."

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "I'll... take this photo back with me."

"Take it back?" The children froze.

"Back to my era." Weixi said softly, "So Grandmother can see... what the children of this era look like."

"Then," Old Wu said loudly, "you must tell Grandmother—our class is the best class in the whole world!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed, "Yes! The best class in the whole world!"

That noon, Weixi did something that stunned the children.

She took a bite of every single dish at lunch.

"Weixi, what's wrong?" Qingqing asked worriedly, "You usually don't..."

"I want to remember all these flavors." Weixi said softly, "In my era... there are none of these flavors."

"..." The children fell silent.

"This braised chicken leg," Weixi chewed slowly, "is so fragrant. This cabbage, sweet. This soup..." she smiled, "tastes just like my grandmother's cooking."

"Weixi..." Lin Yunyun's eyes reddened.

"I'm sorry," Weixi lowered her head, "am I... making you sad?"

"No!" The children said in unison, "We're not sad!"

"We're just..." A-Xin rubbed his nose, "just thinking that you're especially good at eating today!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed again, laughing and laughing, but their eyes all reddened.

That afternoon, the children took Weixi to walk through every corner of the school.

"This is the playground!" A-Xin pointed, "On your first day at school, we did morning exercises here!"

"This is the library!" Qingqing said, "You often read here!"

"This is the equipment room!" Old Wu said, "Our 'secret base'!"

"Here..." Weixi stood at the equipment room door, smiling softly, "is where our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' held its first meeting."

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

"And here!" Lin Yunyun pointed at the sapling lot behind the classroom, "The trees we planted! Look, they've grown tall!"

"They really have..." Weixi crouched down, looking at the grown saplings, "They're all... growing up properly."

"Because," Qingqing crouched beside her, "someone takes care of them!"

"Yes!" A-Xin said loudly, "Our 'Tree Adoption Raid Squad' waters them every day!"

"Thank you." Weixi gently touched the sapling's leaves, "Thank you... for taking care of them as they grow."

"Weixi," A-Xin suddenly asked, "is there anywhere else you want to go?"

Weixi thought for a moment.

"I want to..." she said, "go to the seawall one more time."

"The seawall?" The children froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "I want to see that sea once more. And then..." she lowered her head, "say goodbye to it properly."

That evening, the children accompanied Weixi to the seawall again.

The setting sun dyed the entire sea golden-red.

"So beautiful..." Weixi looked at the sea, saying softly, "more beautiful than any painting I've ever seen."

"Of course!" Old Wu said loudly, "This is our sea!"

"Our sea..." Weixi repeated, and then, facing the sea, she said softly:

"Thank you, sea."

"Thank you for letting me see your blue form."

The sea breeze blew gently.

Weixi's hair flew in the wind.

"Weixi," Qingqing stood beside her and asked, "are you... afraid?"

"Afraid of what?" Weixi asked.

"Afraid of... going back." Qingqing said in a low voice, "Afraid that after you go back, you'll see that world."

"..." Weixi fell silent.

"Yes." she said softly, "I am afraid."

"Then..." Qingqing's voice choked.

"But," Weixi turned to look at Qingqing, smiling faintly, "I'm not afraid anymore."

"Why?"

"Because..." Weixi said, "I know that world is being changed. And the ones changing it..." she looked at the twenty-six children on the seawall, "are right here before me."

"Weixi..." The children's eyes all reddened.

"So," Weixi said softly, "even if I go back, I'm not afraid. Because I know... the future will be better."

"It will be better!" The children shouted in unison, "It will definitely be better!"

"Weixi," late at night, after all the children had gone home, Qingqing stayed behind, sitting beside Weixi, "will you... still miss us?"

"Yes." Weixi nodded, "Every day, every second, I will."

"Then..." Qingqing lowered her head, "in the future... will you be lonely?"

"..." Weixi fell silent.

"Before, I was." she said softly, "Before I came back, I always felt... that I was alone. But now..."

"Now?"

"Now," Weixi smiled, "my heart is filled with all of you. So full... that even in the future, I won't feel lonely."

"Weixi..." Qingqing's eyes reddened.

"Qingqing," Weixi looked at her seriously, "thank you. Thank you... for becoming my friend when I needed it most."

"..." Qingqing's tears fell.

"Don't cry," Weixi reached out and gently wiped away her tears, "we agreed... to say goodbye with smiles."

"Mhm!" Qingqing nodded hard, trying to smile, "I... I'll smile!"

"That's good." Weixi smiled softly, "That's good."

That night, Weixi did something that made the children cry into a mess.

She took out a stack of letter paper and wrote twenty-six letters.

"Each one," Weixi said, "is written for one of you."

"For us?" The children froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "Each of you has one."

She handed out the letters, one by one, to each person.

"A-Xin, your letter says—" Weixi read softly, "'To the best King of Ideas: Thank you for showing me that 'thinking of ways' can save the world.'"

"Qingqing, your letter says—" 'To the most reliable Class President: Thank you for guarding this world's secret for me.'"

"Old Wu, your letter says—" 'To the warmest foodie: Thank you for your pork bun, the warmest flavor in the world.'"

"Lin Yunyun, your letter says—" 'To the bravest girl: Thank you for showing me that timid people can be brave too.'"

"..." The children looked at the letters in their hands and cried into a mess.

"Oh right, Weixi," A-Xin suddenly thought of something, "the letters you wrote to us... can we open them?"

"Of course!" Weixi smiled, "Those letters are meant for you to read!"

"Then I'll read mine first!" A-Xin carefully opened his letter.

"'To the best King of Ideas: Thank you for showing me that 'thinking of ways' can save the world.'" A-Xin read, his eyes reddening, "Weixi, you..."

"Every time you think of a way," Weixi said softly, "you're saving the world. Whether it's sorting trash, saving electricity, or finding a way to track down the pipe... you're everyone's 'King of Ideas.'"

"Weixi..." A-Xin lowered his head, his voice hoarse.

"I'm reading mine! I'm reading mine!" Old Wu opened his letter, "'To the warmest foodie: Thank you for your pork bun, the warmest flavor in the world.'"

"Old Wu," Weixi said, "your pork bun isn't just a pork bun. It's... a pork bun made with 'love.'"

"I... I'll make it for you every day from now on!" Old Wu said loudly.

"Alright." Weixi smiled, "Then I'll wait for you, every day, in the future."

"Lin Yunyun, read yours!" Qingqing urged.

"'To the bravest girl: Thank you for showing me that timid people can be brave too.'" Lin Yunyun read, tears falling, "Weixi... I'm not brave at all..."

"You are brave." Weixi said softly, "You're the first to step forward when everyone is afraid. You pick up trash others won't touch at the beach cleanup. You... are the bravest girl I've ever met."

"..." Lin Yunyun cried and nodded hard.

"Weixi," Wang Zhuankai opened his letter, "yours says... 'To the boy who laughs the most: Thank you for making everyone laugh even on the hardest days.'"

"Wang Zhuankai," Weixi smiled, "your laughter is our class's most precious 'medicine.'"

"Pfft!" Wang Zhuankai couldn't help laughing, then crying, "Then... I'll keep laughing!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded, "Keep laughing forever!"

"And the last letter," Weixi held the final letter in her hand, "is written for... all of Class 6-1."

She unfolded the paper and read softly:

"To my favorite Class 6-1:

Thank you for showing me that the future can be changed.

Thank you for showing me that blue seas, green trees, fiery phoenix blossoms, and warm pork buns really exist.

Thank you for making me, a 'person from the future,' feel like I found a 'home' before I left.

I will wait for you in the future.

Waiting for you to grow up, waiting for you to make this world an even more beautiful place.

We... will definitely meet again."

"Weixi!!" The children cried and surged forward, surrounding Weixi.

"Don't cry," Weixi gently patted their backs, "we agreed... to say goodbye with smiles."

"But..." A-Xin sobbed, "we can't smile..."

"Then..." Weixi smiled softly, "let me smile for you."

That night, the children cried for a very, very long time.

Weixi cried too.

But at the end of the tears, a lamp glowed in everyone's heart.

The name of that lamp was—"Hope."

Late at night, all the children had gone home.

Weixi sat alone in the classroom, looking at the sky through the window.

"Tomorrow..." she said in a low voice, "will be the last day."

She lowered her head and looked at the bracelet.

The number on the bracelet flickered quietly in the moonlight.

—Two.

"Two days..." Weixi smiled softly, "just right."

"Just right enough..." she closed her eyes, "to say goodbye to everyone properly."

"And..." she looked up at the sky, "just right enough... to take one look at the starry sky of this era."

The sky outside the window was full of stars.

Weixi reached out, as if touching those stars.

"People of the future," she said in a low voice, "please remember..."

"Long, long ago, there was a group of children who, so that you could see this starry sky..."

"gave everything they could."

"So..." she smiled softly, "please, be happy."

"Weixi," a voice sounded from the classroom door.

It was Liu-Liu.

"Liu-Liu?" Weixi froze for a moment, "Why haven't you rested yet?"

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu jumped onto the windowsill and sat beside her, "I can't sleep. I'm afraid... that when I wake up, you'll be gone."

"..." Weixi gently patted Liu-Liu's head, "I won't leave without a word. I'll... say goodbye to you properly."

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu nuzzled her hand, "Weixi, after you leave... who will 'see' the future for us?"

"You can do it yourselves." Weixi smiled, "You've already learned how to protect the future."

"Really?" Liu-Liu asked.

"Really." Weixi nodded, "You understand the future better than any 'person from the future.'"

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu thought for a moment, "Then... I have a favor to ask you."

"What favor?"

"Help me," Liu-Liu said, "help me see... whether the sky of the future is blue."

"..." Weixi froze for a moment, then smiled softly.

"Alright." she said, "I will definitely look."

"And," Liu-Liu added, "tell the animals of the future—there was a robot pet named Liu-Liu, who, long, long ago, worked very, very hard to protect their home."

"Mhm." Weixi said softly, "I will definitely tell them."

Under the moonlight, a girl and a little pet sat side by side at the window.

"Liu-Liu," Weixi said in a low voice, "thank you for staying with me all this time."

"Beep beep..." Liu-Liu nuzzled her, "Don't mention it. Because... you're my best friend."

"Mhm." Weixi smiled softly, "You're mine too."
