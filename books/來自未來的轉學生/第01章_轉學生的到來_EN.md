# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 1: The Arrival of the Transfer Student

In October, the phoenix trees at Luyang Elementary School were still green, but the classroom of Class 6-1 was already a boiling pot of bubbling syrup.

"A-Xin! You poured chalk dust into my water bottle again!" Lin Yunyun, sitting in the third row, was so angry her face turned red, raising her water bottle as if to hurl it at the back row.

"I'm innocent! I was teaching the chalk dust a 'leap-from-the-rooftop technique'!" The King of Ideas, Lu Lixin (nicknamed A-Xin), sidestepped the attack with a grin, raising both hands, "They jumped voluntarily! Look how neatly they jumped!"

On the windowsill, the class's co-owned AI smart pet "Liu-Liu" perked up its mouse-and-fox-like pointed ears, flicked its bushy, metallic-sheen tail, and swept its two obsidian electronic eyes across the room before settling on the classroom door.

"Beep beep. Monitoring the new homeroom teacher's approach. Distance to the classroom: thirty meters." Liu-Liu lowered its electronic voice like a professional underground informant, "According to past statistics, this class has already successfully chased away one trainee teacher this semester. The current homeroom teacher's heart rate exceeds 110 beats per minute. Recommendation: suspend the internal conflict and face the external threat together."

"Relax!" A-Xin patted his chest, "Our class is the legendary Ace Rascal Class of Luyang Elementary. What kind of teacher haven't we seen?"

Sitting in the front row, Class President Lin Yunqing adjusted her glasses and slowly tucked a screwdriver back into her pencil case. She ignored A-Xin's nonsense, and instead stared at the class duty roster in her hand, speaking calmly:

"Speaking of chalk dust, I'm more curious about something else. This morning I passed the school affairs office and saw the list of new transfer students for this semester."

"A transfer student?!" All twenty-six pairs of eyes in the classroom lit up at once.

For a class like 6-1, the "Ace Rascal Class," a transfer student usually couldn't last half a semester before their whole family moved away. Just by the smell of it, there was definitely a story brewing!

"Boy or girl?" Old Wu (Wu Chenkai) swallowed the last half of his pork bun in one gulp, his chubby face full of anticipation, "I hope it's someone who brings snacks."

"I didn't catch the name," Qingqing shrugged, "I only vaguely saw the dean of discipline write a note on the registration card—'This student requires special observation.'"

"Special observation?" A-Xin's eyes lit up like light bulbs plugged into electricity, "Doesn't that make it even more fun!"

Just as the whole class was buzzing about how "special" the transfer student might be—

"Knock, knock."

Someone knocked lightly on the classroom door twice.

The sound was soft and gentle, yet it fell into the noisy classroom like a drop of water into a still lake, instantly silencing the chaos.

The door opened.

A girl walked in.

She was not very tall, with smooth shoulder-length hair, her uniform neatly pressed, her backpack straps neatly adjusted. She didn't look particularly striking—in fact, she seemed rather quiet—just standing at the door, head slightly bowed, with sunlight spilling in from the hallway and gilding her in a thin layer of gold.

"Hello, everyone." The girl's voice was soft, like wind passing through the treetops, "My name is Lin Weixi. Starting today, I'm transferring into Class 6-1."

The class froze for a second.

Then A-Xin struck first, raising his hand to ask: "Transfer student! Did you get kicked out of your old school for doing something wrong?"

"No." Lin Weixi shook her head, her tone gentle as if soothing someone, "I just... moved."

"Moved? Where to?" Old Wu asked curiously.

Lin Weixi paused, her gaze drifting toward the window, and she said softly: "Moved to... a very, very faraway place. So far away that... I might never be able to go back."

"Pfft—" A-Xin sprayed out the water he had just drunk, "What kind of novel opening is that! Did you stay up late binge-watching dramas last night?"

The whole class burst into laughter. Only Qingqing, sitting in the front row, silently put down her glasses, turning over and over in her mind the words "never be able to go back."

For the rest of the morning, Lin Weixi sat quietly in the empty seat behind Qingqing, barely making her presence known.

The first period was language arts—composition writing. The title was "The Place I Love Most."

A-Xin frowned and bit his pencil, when he suddenly noticed Lin Weixi in the front row writing almost without pause, her handwriting neat and elegant. Curiously, he craned his neck to peek, and the moment he saw it, he froze.

Lin Weixi wasn't writing about the seaside, or an amusement park, or her grandmother's house.

What she wrote was: "The place I love most is a place with an old tree, a clean well, and a patch of grass where children can walk barefoot. It is a place I can only go back to in my dreams."

A-Xin didn't understand, scratched his head, and went back to biting his pencil.

The third period was natural science. The teacher was explaining the relationship between weather and cloud formations.

"Class, predicting the weather actually has its patterns. As long as you learn to observe the shapes of clouds..." The natural science teacher was explaining enthusiastically when Lin Weixi suddenly raised her hand.

"Teacher." Lin Weixi's voice rang out softly, "Today at around 3:10 PM, it will rain heavily. A passing shower, lasting about twenty minutes, and stopping before 4 PM."

"Huh?" The natural science teacher paused, looked out at the cloudless blue sky, and smiled, shaking his head, "Lin, the satellite cloud chart today shows a very low probability of afternoon thunderstorms. How could you..."

"Because," Lin Weixi lowered her head, her voice soft and gentle, as if talking to herself, "the direction of the wind this season, the moisture blowing in from the sea, and the humidity of the soil... they're all telling me."

The class fell silent. A-Xin's mouth formed an "O" shape.

At 3:10 PM.

Rattle-rattle-rattle—huge raindrops came down without warning, falling for exactly twenty minutes, and stopping at exactly one minute before 4 PM.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, twenty-six children looked at each other, collectively sucking in a breath of cold air.

"How—how does she know all this?!" Old Wu's pork bun fell onto his desk, and this time he even forgot to pick it up.

"I heard it!" A-Xin leaned in mysteriously, lowering his voice, "This morning she told Uncle Wang from the maintenance office, 'The sink pipe on the second floor will start leaking in three days, call the repairman early.' Uncle Wang laughed and said she was talking nonsense as kids do—but just now, the pipe really started seeping water!"

"And do you know what?" Even the usually quietest Lin Yunyun couldn't help but join in, "At noon she looked at the lunch menu, glanced at it once, and said: 'This batch of cabbage was shipped from the farm at the foot of the mountain, and it rained there two days ago.' The kitchen auntie checked—and it really was true!"

"That's too ridiculous! Does she have a spy inside the school?"

"Could she be... a secret investigator from the weather bureau?"

"Or maybe... she's a person from the future?!"

That last sentence was some classmate's joke blurted out casually. The whole class roared with laughter, and nobody took it seriously.

Only Qingqing didn't laugh.

She adjusted her glasses, her gaze settling on the silver-white bracelet on Lin Weixi's left wrist.

It was a very exquisite bracelet, its surface smooth as mercury, its edges faintly emitting an extremely pale blue halo that flickered at intervals, like some kind of silent countdown.

More importantly—from earlier on, Liu-Liu on the windowsill had been staring at Lin Weixi's bracelet, its electronic eyes glowing red with detection light, its tail fluffed into a puffy ball.

"Beep... beep beep..." Liu-Liu, in a voice so low only Qingqing could hear, trembled as it said, "Class President... that bracelet... I can't scan its frequency band... the signal it emits... is not something that should exist in this world..."

Qingqing's heart suddenly skipped a beat.

The school dismissal bell rang.

The children packed their backpacks and rushed out in twos and threes, like a flock of sparrows released from a cage. Only Lin Weixi wasn't in a hurry to leave. She stood by the back door of the classroom, quietly watching the old phoenix tree outside the window, a look in her eyes that a child her age shouldn't have.

It was a very, very deep... very heavy sadness.

A-Xin walked past with his backpack, about to call out to her to go buy red bean cakes at the school gate together, but after seeing her expression clearly, he silently closed his mouth.

Lin Weixi reached out and gently touched the bracelet on her left wrist.

Under the touch of her fingertip, the silver-white bracelet rippled with a gentle ring of light.

She lowered her head and, in a voice barely audible, softly said:

"...It's almost time. Almost."

Those words seemed spoken to herself, and also to some distant place she could never return to.

The setting sun stretched her shadow very, very long.

And on the windowsill at the other end of the classroom, Liu-Liu's electronic eyes blinked red, faithfully recording every single move Lin Weixi had just made.

Standing in the shadow of the classroom doorway, Qingqing watched the silhouette against the setting sun, and for the first time, a brand-new curiosity welled up in her heart.

This quiet transfer student...

Just who is she?
