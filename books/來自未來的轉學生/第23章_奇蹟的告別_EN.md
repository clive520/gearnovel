# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 23: The Miraculous Farewell

On the morning of the last day, the children were startled awake by a light.

"Hurry, look outside the window!" Old Wu was the first to charge into the classroom, panting, "In the sky... there's a light in the sky!"

"A light?" The children crowded to the window and looked up—

At the edge of the sky, a faint, iridescent stream of light had appeared, like a gentle rainbow, hanging quietly among the clouds.

"This is..." Qingqing's face changed, "time..."

"The time gate is about to open." A soft voice sounded from behind her.

It was Weixi.

She stood at the classroom door, her face pale, the bracelet on her wrist radiating increasingly intense light.

"Weixi..." The children gathered around, "today is..."

"Mhm." Weixi smiled faintly, "Today is my day of return."

"..." The children fell silent.

"Don't be like this," Weixi said softly, "we agreed... to say goodbye with smiles."

"..." The children tried to force smiles, but each one looked worse than the last.

"Children," at the classroom door, Dean Zhou had arrived at some point, holding a bouquet of flowers, "this is for you."

"Dean..." Weixi froze.

"These are flowers I grew in my own yard." Dean Zhou's tone remained flat, but carried a slight tremor, "I'm not good with words. But... flowers can speak."

"Dean..." Weixi took the bouquet, her eyes reddening, "thank you."

"And," Dean Zhou pulled out from his pocket the card he had treasured for so long—the "Letter to the Future" the children had written on fair day, "this was meant to be sent to 'the Dean of the future.' Now... it's for you."

"Dean..." Weixi took the card.

"Tell the future me," Dean Zhou turned his head away, his voice hoarse, "thank you."

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, "I definitely will!"

"And me!" Principal Qiu walked into the classroom, holding his teacup, smiling, "Little Weixi, the principal has nothing much to give... so I'll just give you a sentence."

"Please say it, Principal."

"No matter where you are," Principal Qiu said warmly, "the gates of Luyang Elementary will always be open for you."

"..." Weixi's eyes moistened again.

"Principal..." She bowed deeply, "thank you."

"Alright, alright," Principal Qiu held his teacup and waved his hand, "go do what you need to do! Don't keep the gate in the sky waiting too long!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed, laughing and laughing, their eyes reddening again.

"Weixi," Qingqing stepped forward, pulling a thick folder from her backpack, "these... are our 'achievements.'"

"Achievements?" Weixi took the folder and opened it—

Inside was the record of everything the children had done this year.

The statistics of trash sorting. The records of zero leftover lunches. The data on saving electricity. The photos of the planted saplings. The results of the beach cleanup. The accounts of the Green Fair. The investigation report on the drainage ditch pollution. The thank-you letter from the environmental protection bureau...

"This is..." Weixi's eyes welled up.

"This is our 'Protect Earth'!" A-Xin said loudly, "From the first battle to the last, all the records are here!"

"We wanted you to know," Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, "that everything you came back to do... wasn't in vain."

"We'll keep going." Lin Yunyun said, "Even after you go back... we'll keep going!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "We'll keep going!"

"..." Weixi hugged the folder tightly, and tears fell drop by drop.

"Thank you..." she choked, "thank you... for showing me that everything I did... was worthwhile."

"Weixi," A-Xin suddenly stepped forward and pressed what he was holding into her arms, "this, for you."

"This is..." Weixi looked down—

It was the notebook with the Earth drawn on it. A-Xin's "Weixi Observation Record."

"This notebook," A-Xin's voice was hoarse, "was originally for 'investigating' you. But now... I want to give it to you."

"Give it to me?" Weixi froze.

"Mhm." A-Xin nodded, "Because what it records isn't just 'suspicious behavior.' It records..." he lowered his head, "every day we knew you."

"A-Xin..." Weixi hugged the notebook to her chest, tears flowing uncontrollably.

"And mine!" Old Wu held out an insulated lunchbox, "These are pork buns I made this morning! Hot! Take them back to eat!"

"Take them back..." Weixi took the lunchbox, "but... the pork buns will go cold..."

"They can go cold!" Old Wu said loudly, "Take them back and tell Grandmother—these are pork buns made by me, Old Wu!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, "I'll definitely tell Grandmother!"

"And mine!" Lin Yunyun held out a phoenix blossom, "I just picked it from the phoenix tree."

"A phoenix blossom..." Weixi took that fiery red flower.

"Take it back," Lin Yunyun said with reddened eyes, "and let the future world bloom a flower like this too."

"..." Weixi gently held that phoenix blossom, as if holding the entire world.

"And mine!" "And mine!"

The children, one after another, pressed gifts into Weixi's arms.

"This is for you!" Wang Zhuankai pressed a smiley-face keychain into her hand, "I won it at the fair! Whenever you see it, think of my smile!"

"And me!" Ke Zhiyuan handed her a USB drive, "Inside is my 'Protect Earth' documentary video! It's all there!"

"This is..." Weixi took the USB drive.

"When you go back," Ke Zhiyuan said, "you can see how we tracked down the drainage ditch step by step!"

"And!" A girl handed her a hand-knitted blue bracelet, "I knitted this with yarn! Blue is the color of the sea!"

"And me!" "And me!"

The children's gifts piled up into a small mountain.

"You..." Weixi looked at those gifts, crying and laughing, "how... how can you be so good..."

"Because you're good to us!" A-Xin said loudly, "So we have to be good to you!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "This is 'reciprocity'!"

"..." Weixi lowered her head and gently placed those gifts, one by one, into her arms.

"I will..." she choked, "take them all back, one by one."

"Take them all back!" The children said, "Not one can be missing!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, "Not one missing!"

A feather. A card. A seed. A marble. A red bean cake...

"Too many... too many..." Weixi couldn't hold them all, sat down on the ground, crying and laughing, "you... how am I supposed to take all this back..."

"Then take them in batches!" A-Xin said loudly, "Anyway... anyway, you're coming back!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "You're coming back!"

"..." Weixi looked at the children, tears blurring her vision.

"Mhm." she said softly, "I'll come back."

"I will definitely come back."

That afternoon, the children took Weixi, one last time, to the seawall.

At the edge of the sky, that iridescent stream of light was growing brighter and brighter.

"The final wait," Weixi sat on the seawall, looking at the children, "I want to... tell you all, the happiest things about my time here."

"The happiest things?" The children sat around her.

"Mhm." Weixi smiled, "The happiest thing... was the first time I saw you all smile."

"The first time?" The children froze.

"When I first transferred here," Weixi said, "I was actually very nervous. I was afraid... that everyone would think I was strange and not want to be my friend."

"How could that be!" Old Wu said loudly, "You're not strange at all!"

"Right!" A-Xin said, "You're just... a little 'special'!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed.

"But," Weixi said softly, "you not only didn't dislike me, you... treated me as a friend. So, the happiest thing for me... was meeting you all."

"..." The children fell silent.

"And," Weixi continued, "the second happiest thing... was seeing that smokestack stop."

"That smokestack!" The children remembered that image and all smiled.

"The third happiest thing," Weixi said, "was eating Old Wu's pork bun."

"The fourth happiest thing was seeing the phoenix tree bloom."

"The fifth happiest thing was..."

"Weixi," Qingqing interrupted her, her eyes red, "stop counting... the more you count... the more we..."

"Fine, fine, I'll stop." Weixi smiled, "Then... your turn?"

"Our turn?" The children froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "Tell me your happiest things."

"My happiest thing..." A-Xin thought for a moment, "was catching Boss Wang with you!"

"My happiest thing," Old Wu said loudly, "was sharing... no wait, you eating my pork buns!"

"My happiest thing," Lin Yunyun said quietly, "was planting trees with you."

"My happiest thing," Wang Zhuankai said, "was hearing you say 'Wang Zhuankai, keep laughing forever'!"

"My happiest thing..." Qingqing looked at Weixi, "was meeting you."

"..." Weixi's eyes moistened.

"Mine too." she said softly, "Meeting you all... was my happiest thing."

The sea breeze blew gently.

Twenty-six children and a girl from the future sat on the seawall, sharing their happiest things.

At that moment, even the setting sun seemed gentle.

"Time... is almost up." Weixi lowered her head and looked at the bracelet.

The number on the bracelet was down to—one.

"The last hour." Weixi said softly.

"One hour..." The children fell silent.

"Weixi," Qingqing walked in front of her, "do you... have anything else you want to say to us?"

"..." Weixi thought for a moment, then smiled.

"I have one sentence for each of you."

She looked at Qingqing: "Qingqing, you're the best Class President in the world."

She looked at A-Xin: "A-Xin, you're the best King of Ideas in the world."

She looked at Old Wu: "Old Wu, you're the warmest foodie in the world."

She looked at Lin Yunyun: "Yunyun, you're the bravest girl in the world."

She looked at Wang Zhuankai: "Wang Zhuankai, you're the boy who laughs the most in the world."

She looked at Ke Zhiyuan, at Liu-Liu, at... twenty-six faces.

"And," she said finally, "every single person in Class 6-1—you are all the most wonderful children in the world."

"Weixi!!" The children could no longer hold back. Crying, they surged forward and surrounded Weixi.

"Don't cry," Weixi gently patted their backs, "we agreed..."

"We agreed," the children said together through their tears, "to say goodbye with smiles."

"Yes." Weixi smiled, "Say goodbye with smiles."

"Weixi," while everyone was busy wiping tears, Qingqing quietly pulled her aside, "I have something... I want to say to you alone."

"Qingqing?" Weixi looked at her.

"I..." Qingqing pulled a small, silver-glinting object from her pocket, "this, for you."

"This is..." Weixi looked down—it was a small gear folded from aluminum foil.

"I made it myself." Qingqing said, "My mom's repair shop has lots of gears. This one... I folded it with my own hands."

"A gear..." Weixi took that foil gear, her fingertips trembling slightly.

"The 'gear' you gave us," Qingqing said with reddened eyes, "is 'hope.' So... I'll give you one too."

"That way," she took a deep breath, "your 'hope' won't break."

"Qingqing..." Weixi held the foil gear in her palm, pressing it against her heart, "thank you."

"And," Qingqing lowered her head, "from now on... I'll write a letter to the future you every year. You... you must receive them."

"I will definitely receive them." Weixi said softly, "Because... I'll be waiting for your letters in the future."

"Mhm!" Qingqing nodded hard, and her tears finally fell.

"Weixi," A-Xin also came over, his eyes red, "I have something to say too."

"A-Xin..."

"I'm usually the best talker," A-Xin rubbed his nose, "but right now... I think... I can't say it."

"Then... don't say it." Weixi smiled faintly, "I know what you want to say."

"You do?" A-Xin froze.

"Mhm." Weixi nodded, "You want to say... 'Weixi, you must remember us.' Right?"

"..." A-Xin lowered his head, his voice hoarse, "...Right."

"I'll remember." Weixi said softly, "Forever, forever, I'll remember."

"And..." A-Xin added, "after you go back... if anyone bullies you..."

"Then I'll say," Weixi smiled, "'I'm someone who was protected by Class 6-1 of Luyang Elementary!'"

"Hahaha!" A-Xin couldn't help laughing, laughed twice, then cried again, "Right! You're... someone we protected!"

"What about Old Wu?" Weixi looked at Old Wu.

"I..." Old Wu held the lunchbox, stammering, "I... everything I want to say is in the pork buns!"

"In the pork buns?" The children froze.

"Mhm!" Old Wu opened the lunchbox, "These pork buns... I made them this morning, crying as I made them! So... they're 'salty'! They represent how much I'll miss you!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed and cried.

"Old Wu," Weixi smiled softly, "these pork buns... are the 'heaviest' pork buns I've ever eaten."

"Then you must eat them well!" Old Wu said loudly, "Not a single bit left!"

"Mhm!" Weixi nodded hard, "Not a single bit!"

Dusk descended.

At the edge of the sky, that iridescent stream of light suddenly bloomed into a vast, flowing radiance.

At the center of the radiance, a gentle, circular gate of light appeared.

"The time gate... has opened." Weixi said softly.

"Weixi..." The children's hearts twisted into a knot.

"I have to go now." Weixi lowered her head, her voice soft, "If I don't... the timeline will collapse."

"..." The children fell silent.

"I'll miss you." Weixi said, "Every day, every second, I will."

"We'll miss you too!" The children said loudly.

"Then..." Weixi took a deep breath, looked up at that radiance, "I'm going now."

"Wait!" Liu-Liu suddenly popped out of Qingqing's backpack and jumped in front of Weixi.

"Liu-Liu!" Weixi froze, "Aren't you..."

"I sneaked along!" Liu-Liu puffed out its chest, "I must... watch you leave with my own eyes!"

"Liu-Liu..." Weixi crouched down and gently patted its head.

"Weixi," unprecedented gentleness flickered in Liu-Liu's electronic eyes, "one last time... let me say something."

"Go ahead."

"You're the first person in my life..." Liu-Liu paused, "who made me feel like 'I'm more than just a robot pet.'"

"..." Weixi's eyes moistened.

"So," Liu-Liu extended its tiny paw, "let's pinky swear!"

"Pinky swear?" Weixi froze.

"Yes!" Liu-Liu said, "A promise—the sky of the future will be blue! And we... will definitely meet again!"

"..." Weixi extended her pinky and gently hooked Liu-Liu's paw.

"Pinky swear." she said softly, "It's a promise."

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu said loudly, "It's a promise!"

On the seawall, a girl, a group of children, and a little pet stood before that flowing radiance.

"I'm going now." Weixi took a deep breath, looking at those faces one last time, "Thank you... for giving me so many beautiful memories."

"Weixi!!" The children shouted in unison through their tears.

"See you tomorrow!" Weixi shouted loudly, "We'll... see you tomorrow!"

She turned and walked into that radiance.

The light gently wrapped around her.

And on the other side of the light—

"Grandmother!" A girl pushed open the door and ran into the yard.

"Weixi?" Grandmother came out of the house, frozen, "You... you're back?"

"Grandmother!" Weixi threw herself into her grandmother's arms, "I'm back! I'm back!"

"It's good you're back... it's good you're back..." Grandmother stroked her hair, her voice trembling, "Did you see... the blue sea?"

"I saw it!" Weixi cried and laughed, "Grandmother! I saw the blue sea!"

"And green trees, fiery flowers, winters with rain, and a group of... the most wonderful children in the world!"

"Then..." Grandmother's tears flowed, "was that world... beautiful?"

"Beautiful!" Weixi said loudly, "Ten thousand times more beautiful than Grandmother's photograph!"

"..." Grandmother smiled, her face creased with wrinkles.

"That's good." she said softly, "That's good."

"Grandmother," Weixi pulled the group photo from her chest, "look, this is them!"

"This is..." Grandmother took the photo and looked at the twenty-six radiant faces on it, "such beautiful children..."

"They... are the most wonderful children in the world!" Weixi said.

"Mhm!" Grandmother nodded, smiling kindly, "Grandmother can tell."

That night, Weixi stood in the yard, looking up at the sky.

"See you tomorrow," she said in a low voice, "Class 6-1."

"I will... keep waiting for your letters."

The wind blew gently.

The sky of the future, on that day, seemed... a little bluer than before.

She turned, step by step, toward the gate of light.

"Weixi!" Qingqing suddenly shouted, "Wait!"

Weixi stopped.

"We..." Qingqing pulled that group photo taken under the phoenix tree from her pocket, "take this photo with you!"

"A group photo..." Weixi took the photo, looking at the twenty-six radiant smiles in it.

"That way," Qingqing said with reddened eyes, "after you go back, whenever you want to see us... you can look at the photo."

"..." Weixi carefully placed the photo into her chest, against her heart.

"Mhm." she said softly, "I'll keep looking at it."

She continued walking toward the gate of light.

"Weixi!" A-Xin shouted again, "One last sentence!"

Weixi stopped and turned around.

"We..." A-Xin took a deep breath and shouted, "See you tomorrow!"

"See you tomorrow!" The children shouted along, "Weixi! See you tomorrow!"

"..." Weixi stood before the gate of light, looking at those twenty-six crying-yet-smiling faces, tears streaming down.

"See you tomorrow." she said softly, "Class 6-1."

"See you tomorrow."

She turned and walked into that radiance.

The light gently wrapped around her figure.

Then—

"Woom—!"

A dazzling white light instantly illuminated the entire sky!

When the children opened their eyes again—

The iridescent stream at the edge of the sky had vanished.

On the seawall, only twenty-six children remained, and an empty lunchbox.

"Weixi..." A-Xin murmured, "she... she's gone?"

"She's gone." Qingqing's voice was hoarse, "She's gone back."

"..." The children stood on the seawall, looking at that sky that had returned to calm.

The wind blew gently.

"She said," Lin Yunyun said in a low voice, "see you tomorrow."

"Right." Qingqing nodded, "See you tomorrow."

"Then..." A-Xin suddenly smiled, smiling until his eyes were red, "then we'll... wait to see her tomorrow!"

"Wait to see her tomorrow!" The children shouted in unison.

The sea wind carried the cries of twenty-six children toward the sky.

And in the far, far future—

A girl gently opened her eyes.

"I... I'm back." she said in a low voice.

She looked down at her chest.

The notebook with the Earth drawn on it. The fiery red phoenix blossom. The group photo pressed against her heart. The empty lunchbox.

She smiled softly.

"See you tomorrow," she said in a low voice, "Class 6-1."

"I will... definitely see you all again."
