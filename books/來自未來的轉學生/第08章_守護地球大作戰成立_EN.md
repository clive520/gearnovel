# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 8: The Protect Earth Campaign Is Launched

December arrived, and the sea breeze began to turn cold.

Yet inside the classroom of Class 6-1 at Luyang Elementary, a peculiar heat lingered—not from the heater, but from an excitement called "It feels like we really did it."

"Everyone! I have a major announcement!" As soon as morning self-study ended, Qingqing jumped onto the podium, hands on her hips, "After two months of effort, our 'Protect Earth' Campaign has already won four battles!"

"Four?" A-Xin counted on his fingers, "Trash sorting, zero waste, saving electricity, planting trees... that's right! Four!"

"But!" Qingqing's tone shifted, and she slammed the podium hard, "Right now we're fighting like a guerrilla war—fighting here today, fighting there tomorrow, hitting one spot then another! At this rate, we'll fall into chaos eventually!"

"Then what do we do?" Old Wu asked.

"We're going to..." Qingqing took a deep breath and said the words, "officially found a nation!"

"Found a nation?!" The whole class stared with wide eyes.

"Yes! Found a nation!" Qingqing announced loudly, "Starting today, 'Protect Earth' is going to evolve from an 'activity' into an 'official organization'! We'll have a name, a slogan, divisions, missions, a badge—so that the whole school and all of Taiwan will know that a group of kids is seriously protecting the Earth!"

"Okay—!" The children's cheers nearly lifted the classroom roof.

Actually, this idea of "founding a nation" came from a question Qingqing asked Weixi in the hallway the day before.

"Weixi," Qingqing caught up to Weixi as she was about to leave, and asked hesitantly, "I want to turn 'Protect Earth' into an official organization. Do you think... that's okay?"

Weixi stopped, thought for a moment, and asked: "Why do you want to do that?"

"Because..." Qingqing lowered her head, "I always feel like everything we're doing now is you reminding us. I'm afraid... that if one day you're gone, all of this will stop."

Weixi was stunned.

"Qingqing," it was a long moment before Weixi spoke softly, "you're wrong. Every thing you've done, you decided yourselves. I only... told you that the Earth is sick. But the decision to save it—that was you."

"Really?" Qingqing wasn't sure.

"Really." Weixi smiled faintly, "And you're right. If one day I'm gone, you must keep going too. Because what the Earth needs isn't one 'person from the future'—it's many, many 'people of the present.'"

"So found your nation." Weixi said, "Make 'Protect Earth' your own thing. The thing that belongs to all twenty-six of you."

Qingqing nodded firmly: "Mhm! I understand!"

"Then that's it! Now, meeting officially in session!" Qingqing picked up the board eraser and rapped the podium, "Agenda item one: give our organization an official name!"

"Isn't it already called 'Protect Earth'?" Lin Yunyun asked quietly.

"That's the name of the 'operation plan'!" A-Xin answered eagerly, "Just like how battles have 'operation codenames,' we also need a 'unit designation'!"

"Unit designation?" The children looked at each other.

"Right!" A-Xin's eyes lit up as he mysteriously pulled out a drawing he had prepared long ago, "I've already thought of one! Take a look!"

He stuck the drawing on the blackboard—it was a circular badge design. In the center was a blue-and-green Earth, and standing on the Earth were twenty-six tiny little figures holding hands. Around the Earth was a ring of radiant sunbeams.

At the bottom of the badge was a line of text:

[Class 6-1, Luyang Elementary · Protect Earth Operations Unit]

"Wow—!" The class let out a collective gasp of admiration.

"I drew this staying up late!" A-Xin declared proudly, "The ring of light around the Earth is the sun! Symbolizing that we always follow the light! Those twenty-six little figures are us! Hands linked, standing united!"

"A-Xin, this design of yours..." Qingqing looked at the drawing, moved, "is amazing!"

"Not bad, not bad!" A-Xin puffed out his chest, "This is the 'military emblem' of our unit! From now on, every operation we carry out will carry this emblem!"

"Great! Then we officially announce—" Qingqing said loudly, "the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' of Class 6-1, Luyang Elementary, is now officially founded!"

"Whoo—!"

"Agenda item two!" Qingqing pressed on while morale was high, "Formation! We're splitting the whole class of twenty-six into six operation squads, each with its own duty!"

"Six squads?" The children asked curiously.

"Squad one: the 'Trash-Sorting Raid Squad'! Leader, Lin Yunyun!" Qingqing announced.

"Me?!" Lin Yunyun jumped in surprise, "I... can I do it?"

"Of course you can!" A-Xin said, "You're the most careful sorter—you never make mistakes!"

"Squad two: the 'Zero-Leftover Lunch Raid Squad'! Leader, Old Wu!"

"This squad is mine for sure!" Old Wu declared grandly, "Anyone who dares waste food has to get past me first!"

"Squad three: the 'Save-Electricity Raid Squad'! Leader, Wang Zhuankai!"

"Burp!" Wang Zhuankai let out a burp and smiled sheepishly, "O-okay, I'll try my best to turn off the lights!"

"Squad four: the 'Tree Adoption Raid Squad'! Leader, A-Xin!"

"Reporting, sir!" A-Xin snapped to attention and saluted, "My 'Little Blue' is ready!"

"Squad five: the 'Intel & Publicity Raid Squad'! Leader—Liu-Liu!"

"Beep beep!" Liu-Liu leaped down from the windowsill, tail held high, "Recon, reporting, publicity, poster posting—I've got it all covered!"

"The last squad..." Qingqing paused, looking toward the corner, "the 'Future Knowledge Raid Squad'! Leader, Lin Weixi!"

The classroom suddenly fell silent for a moment.

Everyone's gaze landed on Weixi.

"Weixi!" A-Xin said loudly, "You're the one among us who knows the most about 'what happens in the future'! Without you, we'd have no idea what the Earth will become! This squad has to be yours!"

Weixi was stunned.

She looked at the twenty-six pairs of sparkling eyes across the classroom, and her throat felt blocked by something.

"I..." she lowered her head, her voice soft, "actually... I'm not anyone special. I just..."

"You're our 'time traveler'!" Old Wu said loudly.

"You're our 'eyes of the future'!" Lin Yunyun said.

"You're... our partner!" Qingqing walked over, took Weixi's hand, and said firmly, "No matter where you came from, from today on, you are a member of the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'! Forever!"

Weixi's lips trembled slightly.

She took a deep breath, nodded hard, her eyes glistening:

"Mhm! I... I accept!"

"Okay—!" The class erupted in thunderous cheers.

In the first week after the unit was founded, the classroom of Class 6-1 became a "mini command center."

"Trash-Sorting Raid Squad, reporting!" Lin Yunyun held up a small notebook and reported, nervous yet earnest, "This week we inspected the school recycling bins seven times and corrected fifty-two wrongly sorted items! The most commonly mis-sorted thing is 'used tissue'—that really can't be recycled!"

"Good!" Qingqing nodded, "Noted!"

"Zero-Leftover Lunch Raid Squad, reporting!" Old Wu stuck out his belly, "This week the whole school's leftover food hit a new low! The kitchen auntie said this month is 'the cleanest month in history'!"

"Save-Electricity Raid Squad, reporting!" Wang Zhuankai raised his hand sheepishly, "I... this week, I checked all the lights at home! I also unplugged the TV's 'standby power' for my mom! She said I've 'gone off the deep end'... but the electricity bill really did drop a lot!"

"Tree Adoption Raid Squad, reporting!" A-Xin declared proudly, "Twenty-one sapling inspections this week! 'Little Blue' grew half a centimeter! 'Moon' grew three millimeters! 'Eat-Your-Fill'... well, Eat-Your-Fill grew two millimeters—it might really have eaten too much!"

"Intel & Publicity Raid Squad, reporting!" Liu-Liu snapped to attention, "Thirty-seven patrols of school classrooms this week! Intercepted eleven 'lights left on'! Six 'fans left running'! Posted eight promotional posters... three of which I carried over to other classes' bulletin boards!"

"Wait!" A-Xin's eyes went wide, "You posted posters in other classes?!"

"Publicity means being proactive!" Liu-Liu declared with full confidence, "Are we supposed to wait for them to come ask?"

"Hahahaha!" The whole class burst into laughter.

"In any case!" Qingqing wrapped up, "This week, the first missions of all six squads were complete successes! The 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' is now officially operational!"

"Whoo—!"

"Next, let's announce our official combat results!"

She turned around and drew a huge table on the blackboard, writing line by line:

[Class 6-1, Luyang Elementary · Total Combat Results]
"Trash-Sorting Raid Squad: two months, correctly sorted recyclables—three hundred forty-two kilograms in total."
"Zero-Leftover Lunch Raid Squad: school-wide leftovers down from 7.8 kilograms a day to 0.5 kilograms—over forty-five kilograms of food saved in total, enough to feed three hundred people."
"Save-Electricity Raid Squad: our class's electricity usage down twenty-eight percent, school-wide electricity bill saved twenty percent—enough to add a chicken leg for everyone in the school."
"Tree Adoption Raid Squad: one hundred twenty-six saplings planted on and off campus—all surviving! Growth status: excellent!"
"Intel & Publicity Raid Squad: patrolled all school classrooms—intercepted 'lights left on' forty-seven times, 'fans left running' twenty-nine times, 'air conditioner set too low' eleven times!"

The children stared at that string of astonishing numbers on the blackboard, and for a moment, no one could speak.

"These numbers..." Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, "are what twenty-six of us built, bit by bit, over two months!"

"We..." A-Xin murmured, "we really did it?"

"We did it!" The children shouted in unison, their voices filled with unprecedented pride and emotion.

Actually, before the "Campaign" was officially founded, Principal Qiu and Dean Zhou had already had a conversation like this in the principal's office.

"Old Zhou," Principal Qiu held his thermos cup of tea, smiling as he looked at the documents on the desk, "you've seen what the kids of Class 6-1 have done over these two months, haven't you?"

"I've seen it." Dean Zhou said expressionlessly, "Picking up trash, turning off lights, planting a few trees. Nothing more."

"Don't underestimate them." Principal Qiu pushed a statistics sheet in front of the Dean, "Three hundred forty-two kilograms of recyclables, forty-five kilograms of leftover food, a twenty percent cut in the school's electricity bill, one hundred twenty-six saplings—not a single one died. Old Zhou, in thirty years of running this school, have you ever seen a class accomplish all this in one semester?"

Dean Zhou fell silent.

"To be honest," Principal Qiu sighed, "at my age, I've seen too many kids with 'three-minute enthusiasm.' But these children... they're genuinely working at it. Every day after school, I see them crouched by the saplings, carrying bucket after bucket of water."

"Principal," Dean Zhou spoke after a long silence, "what do you want to do?"

"I want to spread their 'Campaign' to the whole school." Principal Qiu said, "Starting next year, the entire school will implement recycling and energy-saving education, with Class 6-1 as the 'model class.' What do you think?"

Dean Zhou didn't answer right away.

He stared at the statistics sheet, thought for a very long time, and finally said flatly:

"...I'll support it at the faculty meeting."

Principal Qiu smiled, a deeply gratified smile.

"Old Zhou, I knew it. You're just hard-mouthed."

"Principal," Dean Zhou stood up, face stern, "I just think this 'Campaign' is more meaningful than them playing marbles and wrestling on campus all day."

That's what he said. But as he left the principal's office, Dean Zhou quietly turned off, one by one, the row of lights on the hallway that had been left on.

That afternoon, the children's excitement almost spilled across the entire campus.

After school, Liu-Liu carried a "Total Combat Results Sheet" in its mouth and trotted proudly toward the bulletin board, ready to post it in the most prominent spot.

"Beep beep! Make way, make way! This is Class 6-1's 'war record'!" Liu-Liu said proudly.

"War record?" A low voice sounded from behind Liu-Liu.

Liu-Liu jumped in fright and turned around—Dean Zhou was standing in front of the bulletin board, hands behind his back, his hawk-like eyes fixed on the statistics sheet in Liu-Liu's mouth.

"D-Dean!" Liu-Liu hastily straightened up, "Th-this is just our class's small, small accomplishment..."

Dean Zhou said nothing.

He silently read the statistics sheet from beginning to end.

Three hundred forty-two kilograms of recyclables. Forty-five kilograms of leftover food. A twenty-eight percent cut in electricity usage. One hundred twenty-six saplings.

Then he spoke.

"...This 'Campaign' of yours," Dean Zhou's tone betrayed no emotion, "the Principal and I have discussed it. At the faculty meeting, he intends to promote your methods to the whole school."

"The whole school?!" Liu-Liu's eyes went wide.

"Mhm." Dean Zhou looked at the bulletin board, his tone still flat, "The Principal's decision is that starting next year, every class in the school will implement trash sorting and carbon-reduction education. Your Class 6-1 will serve as the 'model class' and teach the whole school how it's done."

"We... we're the model class?!" Liu-Liu's tail exploded with excitement, "Dean! Is this real?!"

"Why would I lie to you?" Dean Zhou glanced at it indifferently, "But if you do a bad job and embarrass the school, I'll still write you up."

Even as he said that, Liu-Liu noticed that before leaving, Dean Zhou quietly turned off the hallway light in front of his own office that someone had forgotten to switch off.

"Beep beep... the Dean turned off a light!" Liu-Liu was so moved its tears nearly sprayed out, "The Dean... has changed!"

That night, Weixi sat alone on the seawall beside the sapling lot behind the school.

The sea wind was strong, whipping her hair wildly.

She gazed at the five smokestacks in the distance, puffing faint white smoke into the darkness, then looked down at the dark, heavy sea beneath her feet.

"...Today, they were so happy." Weixi said softly to the sea, "It's the first time I've seen people smile so happily over the four words 'protect the Earth.'"

"But I know," her voice trembled slightly, "their happy days may not last much longer. Because..."

She lowered her head and looked at the bracelet on her wrist.

On the inside of the bracelet, the countdown number flickered quietly in the night.

—Forty-two.

"They've begun." Weixi said in a low voice, "The smell of that drainage ditch got heavier today. There's more and more stuff in the seawater. At this rate..."

She didn't finish.

She remembered the day she left the future.

It was a gray, hazy morning. The sky hung so low it seemed to press down on the rooftops, and the air carried an indescribable stuffiness.

Her grandmother stood at the doorway, holding a small cloth bundle, pressing it into her arms.

"Weixi, ah," her grandmother said, stroking her face with dry, withered hands, "when you get to that era, take a look for Grandma—see the legendary green mountains, the clean seas, and the winters that snow."

"Grandma," she said, her eyes red, "I will definitely come back."

"Silly child," her grandmother laughed, wrinkles creasing her face, "come back for what? What Grandma wants most is for you to see, on her behalf, this world before it got dirty. When you come back, remember to tell Grandma—those places really are beautiful."

"Mhm! I will!" she nodded hard.

Inside the cloth bundle was something her grandmother had treasured her whole life—a yellowed photograph.

In the photo was a stretch of azure sea. By the shore stood a row of lush green trees. Under the trees was a little girl, barefoot on the sand, laughing with her eyes squeezed into happy crescents.

"That's... Grandma's sea from when she was little." she said softly.

Now, she gently pulled that photograph from her chest and looked at it in the moonlight.

Then she looked up at the dark, heavy sea before her.

"Grandma," she said softly to the sea, "you were right. The sea here really is beautiful. Even though... it's already starting to get sick."

"But it's okay." She clutched the photograph, "I'll make it better. At least... I'll teach these children how to protect it."

The sea wind howled past.

She shielded the faint glow of her bracelet in her palm, like guarding a tiny flame.

The wind blew across her cheeks.

She suddenly felt she wanted, so much, to hear one more time the cheers of those twenty-six children from this afternoon.

"Three days after the 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' was founded, something no one expected happened."

That morning, the children filed into the classroom one after another and found a big poster written on red paper pasted right in the center of the blackboard:

[Faculty Meeting Announcement]
"In recognition of the results of Class 6-1's 'Protect Earth' Campaign, the faculty meeting has resolved:
1. Starting next year, the entire school will implement comprehensive recycling and energy-saving education.
2. Class 6-1 will serve as the 'model class' and guide the whole school's implementation.
3. This program will be incorporated into the school's 'featured curriculum' and submitted to the education bureau for approval.
—Luyang Elementary, Principal Qiu Shouren"

The children stared at the announcement for a full three seconds.

Then—

"Waaaaah—!"

Screams, cheers, and leaps of joy instantly flooded the entire classroom!

"We're going to the education bureau!"

"We're a 'featured curriculum'!"

"The whole school has to do environmental protection with us!"

"Oh my god! This is even better than scoring a hundred on a test!"

A-Xin was the most dramatic, climbing straight onto the podium, raising both arms like a victorious general:

"Classmates! We did it! From today on, the entire Luyang Elementary will be our 'Protect Earth' battlefield!"

"Okay—!"

"But!" Seizing the moment of excitement, Qingqing stepped up to the podium, clapped her hands, and pulled everyone's attention back, "We can't just be satisfied with the present! Everyone think—what else can we do next?"

"What can we do?" The children looked at each other.

"We can..." Weixi suddenly spoke, her voice soft yet silencing the whole classroom, "go take a look at what's really in that drainage ditch."

"The drainage ditch?" A-Xin blinked, "That stinky ditch behind the school?"

"Mhm." Weixi's gaze drifted out the window toward the sea behind the school, "That ditch smells worse every day. The things in the seawater multiply every day. If we don't find out where they're coming from..."

"Then we can only stand by and watch them dirty the sea." Qingqing finished the sentence for her.

The classroom fell silent.

The children looked at each other.

Then Old Wu was the first to stand up: "Alright! I'm in! Let me see just what dares to bully our sea!"

"I... I'm in too!" Lin Yunyun clenched her fist; her voice was small, but extraordinarily firm.

"No need to even ask!" A-Xin jumped up, "Our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' officially accepts its first 'battlefield mission'!"

"What mission?" The children asked in unison.

"Mission codename:" Qingqing took a deep breath and announced loudly—

"Inspect the drainage ditch!"

"Inspect the drainage ditch?!" The children blinked, then—

"Okay—!" The class erupted in thunderous cheers once again!

"Finally, we get to be detectives!" A-Xin rubbed his hands together eagerly, "We're going to find out just who's been bullying our sea!"

"Starting tomorrow, we investigate in groups!" Qingqing announced loudly, "Liu-Liu handles water quality detection, Old Wu handles recording, A-Xin draws the map, Lin Yunyun handles..."

"Wait!" Old Wu raised his hand, "I'm in charge of recording? I'm not very good at writing!"

"You're in charge of recording with your 'nose'!" A-Xin laughed heartily, "Your nose is the sharpest—wherever you smell the worst, you draw a big X on the map!"

"I can do that!" Old Wu said with full confidence, "I'm the best at smelling things in the whole school!"

"Of course! You're the school's 'number one foodie,' so of course your nose is sharp!" A-Xin teased.

"The two aren't mutually exclusive!" Old Wu deployed his signature rebuttal once more.

"Hahahaha!" The children laughed together, excitedly discussing tomorrow's "battlefield mission."

Outside the window, the five smokestacks quietly puffed white smoke.

From behind the school, the sea breeze carried a faint, nameless odor.
