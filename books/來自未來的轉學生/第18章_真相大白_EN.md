# The Transfer Student from the Future

## Chapter 18: The Truth Comes to Light

Over the next two days, using the bracelet's "tracking" ability, Weixi led everyone, bit by bit, in piecing together the factory's secret discharge route.

"This pipe loops around the factory's iron wall, cuts through the weeds, and runs all the way to..." Weixi closed her eyes, the bracelet emitting a pale blue light, "the mouth of the drainage ditch."

"The river mouth?" A-Xin's eyes went wide, "That's..."

"That's the sea we cleaned." Qingqing said.

"Weixi, you've already used it three times today..." at dusk on the first day, Qingqing said worriedly, "take a break, okay?"

"It's fine," Weixi shook her head, "I can still..."

Her words suddenly stopped, and her body swayed slightly.

"Weixi!" Qingqing caught her, "You're so pale!"

"I... I'm fine..." Weixi forced a smile, "It's just... the bracelet's energy is draining a little fast..."

"Then we'll continue tomorrow!" A-Xin said nervously, "That's enough for today!"

"No..." Weixi shook her head, "That new pipe keeps moving... if we don't track it to its source today, tomorrow..."

"What about tomorrow?"

"Tomorrow," Weixi said in a low voice, "it might have moved somewhere else."

"Then... then we..." A-Xin stomped his feet anxiously.

"I can hold on." Weixi's tone was exceptionally resolute, "Let's continue."

The next day, the blue light on Weixi's bracelet had already grown somewhat dim.

"Weixi..." Qingqing held her hand, a nameless unease rising in her heart.

"I'm fine." Weixi smiled faintly, "We're almost there. Just a little more..."

"Just a little more" became the phrase Weixi said the most that day.

"They found a new route," Weixi said softly, "looping around the old pipe's location and discharging through a new route instead. That way, even if we find the old pipe, we can't..."

"Can't prove they're still discharging now." A-Xin gritted his teeth, "That Boss Wang is so cunning!"

"It's okay." Weixi comforted everyone, "As long as the bracelet still remembers that smell, we can..."

Her words suddenly stopped.

"Weixi?" Qingqing noticed her change, "What's wrong?"

"I..." Weixi's face instantly turned pale, her body swaying, "I..."

"Weixi!!" A-Xin rushed over to catch her, "What's wrong?!"

"My... head..." Weixi weakly held her forehead, "so dizzy... as if... something... is calling me..."

"Calling you?" The children looked at each other.

"It's..." Weixi's pupils suddenly lost focus, "it's the 'future'..."

"Weixi!" Qingqing shouted, "Look at me! Look at me!"

"I..." Weixi swayed, as if about to collapse at any moment, "I feel like... I'm about to..."

"About to what?" A-Xin asked nervously.

Weixi didn't answer.

Her body went limp and fell forward—

"Weixi!!!"

"Thump!"

Weixi fainted in A-Xin's arms.

The pale blue light on the bracelet suddenly flickered violently, and then—

"Woom—!"

A blinding beam of light shot out from the bracelet, unfolding into a huge image in midair!

In the image was a coast buried under trash.

Under a grayish-yellow sky, the sea was black. The beach was piled with trash taller than people, plastic bags dancing in the wind like gray snowflakes.

"This is..." The children gasped.

"This is the sea of the future..." Qingqing murmured.

"The future?!" Old Wu's eyes went wide, "What future?!"

"Weixi, she..." Qingqing hesitated, then took a deep breath and finally spoke out the secret she had kept for so long, "came from the future."

"What?!" All twenty-six people in the class stared with wide eyes at the same time.

"She is..." Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, "from the future, forty years from now."

The classroom instantly fell into deathly silence.

"The future..." Wang Zhuankai's mouth hung wide open, "that... that is..." he pointed at the projection of the trash coast in midair, "is that image... real?"

"It's real." Qingqing nodded, "That's what Weixi's era truly looks like."

"Then..." Lin Yunyun asked tremblingly, "in that era... do people... all live in places like that?"

"..." Qingqing was silent, and only after a long moment nodded softly, "Yes."

"How terrifying..." Lin Yunyun's tears fell.

"Then... then us..." Ke Zhiyuan asked quietly, "will our era become like that too?"

"..." Qingqing didn't answer.

But her clenched fist said it all.

"It won't." A weak voice came from A-Xin's arms.

It was Weixi.

She had woken up.

"As long as we start acting now," Weixi said weakly, "that future... can be changed."

"Weixi!" The children gathered around, "You're awake?!"

"Mhm." Weixi nodded softly, her gaze sweeping across the class, "I'm sorry... you had to see that image..."

"Don't apologize!" Old Wu said loudly, "You're the one who deserves the apology! You carried all this alone... and we knew nothing!"

"Old Wu..." Weixi's eyes reddened.

"And," A-Xin continued, "we know now! So starting today, we carry it together!"

"Carry it together!" The children said in unison.

"The future...?" Old Wu's mouth hung wide open, "You mean... Weixi... is... is..."

"Is from the future." A-Xin continued, "She carried this bracelet, and returned from forty years in the future, back to now. To..." he looked at the unconscious Weixi, "to tell us that the Earth is getting sick."

"The Earth... getting sick?"

"Do you still remember?" A-Xin said loudly, "She predicted the rain, she knew where the vegetables came from, she watched the news and said 'just like before,' she saw the trash mountain at the beach cleanup, she wrote a letter to herself forty years from now... she told us long ago! It's just that we... never noticed!"

"She..." Lin Yunyun's eyes suddenly turned red, "she's been... carrying all of this alone?"

"Yes." Qingqing nodded, her voice choked, "She came back alone from the future to the present, facing everything alone. And we... didn't know anything."

"She must have been so lonely..." Wang Zhuankai said in a low voice.

"She..." Old Wu stared blankly at the unconscious Weixi, and then, his eyes suddenly reddened too, "last time she said she came from a 'very, very faraway place'... I thought she just lived abroad..."

"So..." Old Wu's voice was choked with tears, "the 'very, very faraway place' she meant... was..."

"Was the future." A-Xin said.

The classroom was quiet for a very, very long time.

Then Lin Yunyun spoke quietly: "Then... will Weixi... go back?"

"..." A-Xin and Qingqing fell silent.

"She will." A weak voice came from A-Xin's arms.

It was Weixi.

She had woken up.

"Weixi!" The children gathered around, "You're awake?!"

"I'm sorry..." Weixi weakly propped herself up, forcing a smile, "I... just now, I accidentally..."

"It's okay!" Lin Yunyun said tearfully, "As long as you're alright!"

"I..." Weixi's gaze swept across all twenty-six faces in the class, and finally, she said in a low voice, "you... all know now?"

"We know!" The children said in unison.

"Then..." Weixi took a deep breath, "do you want to hear the story of my era?"

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

And so, that day, sitting in the middle of the classroom, Weixi told the whole class the story she had never told anyone.

She said that in her era, the sky was grayish-yellow, and the sun was forever like a haze behind a layer of fog.

She said that in her era, the sea was black, the shore piled with trash, and there were no fish in the seawater.

She said that in her era, clean water had to be collected in line, four liters per person per day.

She said that in her era, summers often exceeded forty degrees, and winters brought hail.

She said that in her era, there was a class called "Climate Class," and in the textbook were written the news of this era, and the teacher said: "These are all history."

She said that her grandmother was the gentlest person she had ever met. Grandma would point at a yellowed photograph and tell her: "Weixi ah, the sea of Grandma's childhood was blue."

She said that she had never seen a blue sea.

"So..." Weixi lowered her head, "that's why I came back here. I wanted to see with my own eyes what a blue sea looks like."

"Then..." Lin Yunyun choked, "did you see it?"

"I did." Weixi looked up, showing a gentle smile, "Here, I saw a blue sea, green trees, winters with rain... and all of you."

"Then..." Old Wu asked, "do you... like it here?"

"I do." Weixi said without hesitation, "Very, very much."

"Then..." Old Wu lowered his head, his voice hoarse, "do you... really have to go back?"

Weixi fell silent.

"...Mhm." She finally nodded softly, "If I don't go back, the timeline will collapse, and everyone... will disappear."

"..." The children fell silent.

"So," Weixi smiled faintly, "I'll cherish every day I have left. Because..." she looked at the whole class, "this is the happiest time of my life."

"Then..." Weixi's voice trembled slightly, "are you... afraid?"

"Afraid?" Old Wu blinked, then shook his head hard, "Afraid of what? You're from the future! That's so cool!"

"But..." Weixi lowered her head, "I'm 'someone who doesn't belong to this era'..."

"So what!" A-Xin said loudly, "You're still our classmate! You're still a member of our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit'!"

"Yes!" Qingqing nodded, "No matter where you came from, you're our friend!"

"But..." Weixi's eyes reddened, "I... will have to go back soon..."

"Go back?" The children froze.

"I have to return to the future before the countdown ends." Weixi lowered her head, looking at the bracelet on her wrist, "Otherwise... the timeline will collapse, and everyone... will disappear."

"Disappear?!" The children gasped.

"So..." Weixi looked up, her eyes blurred with tears, "I can only... stay with you until that day."

The classroom once again fell into silence.

"Then..." after a long moment, Old Wu spoke, his voice hoarse, "how much longer... can you stay with us?"

Weixi lowered her head and glanced at the bracelet.

"...Fifteen days." she said softly.

"Fifteen days..." The children murmured in repetition.

"So," Weixi held back her tears and forced a smile, "for the remaining fifteen days, I will..."

"Enough!" A-Xin suddenly shouted, interrupting her, "Weixi, listen to me!"

"A-Xin..." Weixi froze.

"Fifteen days," A-Xin looked straight at her, "is enough for us to do a lot of things!"

"Yes!" Qingqing continued, "We'll use these fifteen days to investigate the drainage ditch! Catch Boss Wang! Make this sea clean again!"

"Then," Old Wu said loudly, "you can go back with peace of mind!"

"But..." Weixi's tears fell, "after I go back..."

"After you go back," Lin Yunyun said tearfully, "we'll still remember you!"

"Yes!" The children said in unison, "We'll remember you!"

"We'll remember you teaching us to sort trash, save electricity, plant trees, clean the beach..."

"We'll remember you making us know that the Earth can get sick..."

"We'll remember..." Old Wu choked, "the days you'd share half a pork bun with me..."

"Hahaha!" The children laughed and cried at once.

"And," A-Xin announced loudly, "starting today, our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' goes into full deployment!"

"Full deployment?" The children were puzzled.

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "Before, only a few of us were secretly investigating the drainage ditch. Now..." he swept his gaze across all twenty-six people, "all twenty-six of us, together!"

"Together doing what?" The children asked.

"Together!" A-Xin said loudly, "investigate the drainage ditch! Catch Boss Wang! Save our Earth!"

"Okay—!" The children shouted in unison.

"And," Qingqing added, "we'll also... make Weixi able to return to her era with peace of mind."

"Yes!" The children said in unison.

"We'll make Weixi able to go back with peace of mind!"

Weixi stared blankly at the twenty-six faces of the class.

Tears fell drop by drop down her cheeks.

"Thank you..." she choked, "thank you..."

That day, in the classroom of Class 6-1, twenty-six children, plus one little pet, gathered around a girl from the future, crying together.

And laughing together.

"By the way," after the crying, Old Wu suddenly thought of something, "Weixi, how much is left on your bracelet now?"

"..." Weixi lowered her head and looked, "Thirteen."

"Thirteen days..." Old Wu murmured in repetition, then nodded hard, "Alright! Then we'll use thirteen days to change the world!"

"Use thirteen days to change the world!" The children shouted in unison.

The next morning, an "unprecedented understanding" appeared in the classroom of Class 6-1.

"Weixi, good morning!" Lin Yunyun handed her a small paper bag, "This is... a rice ball my family made! For you!"

"Th-thank you!" Weixi took it, stunned.

"Weixi, this is for you!" Ke Zhiyuan handed her a hand-drawn card, "This is my drawing of the 'future Earth'! When I grow up, I'm going to make it real!"

"Weixi, are you thirsty?" Old Wu held out his water bottle, "I'll share my water with you! I haven't drunk from it yet!"

"Old Wu, drink it yourself!" A-Xin teased, "We're supposed to 'take care of' Weixi, not 'feed' Weixi!"

"Is there a difference between taking care and feeding?" Old Wu tilted his head.

"Of course there is!" A-Xin said, "Feeding is for pets!"

"Then..." Old Wu thought for a moment, "then Weixi is our 'treasure'! Feeding a treasure should be fine, right?"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed together.

Sitting at her seat, watching these children, Weixi's eyes began to grow warm again.

"You..." she lowered her head, speaking softly, "don't need to be so good to me..."

"Why not?" Wang Zhuankai declared with full confidence, "Aren't you this good to us?"

"You taught us to sort trash, save electricity, plant trees, clean the beach..." Lin Yunyun counted, "You made us know that the Earth can get sick... you made our 'Protect Earth' a real 'Campaign'!"

"Right!" A-Xin said loudly, "Without you, we'd still be 'throwing trash around'!"

"You..." Weixi's eyes finally couldn't hold back, and they moistened.

"So," Qingqing walked over and took her hand, "from today on, we take care of you!"

"We'll take care of you!" The children said in unison.

"Mhm..." Weixi lowered her head, tears falling drop by drop onto the desk, "thank you..."

"Don't cry!" Old Wu panicked, "I-I'll go buy you another pork bun!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed again.

That day, the classroom of Class 6-1 was filled with unprecedented warmth.

That afternoon after school, for the first time, all the children attended the "secret meeting."

"We need to find a way," A-Xin stood on the equipment box, his expression serious, "to 'buy time' for Weixi!"

"Buy time?" The children were puzzled.

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "Every time Weixi uses the bracelet, it consumes energy. If we can reduce the number of times she 'uses the bracelet'..."

"Then her countdown will slow down!" Qingqing continued.

"How do we reduce it?" The children asked.

"We can..." Ke Zhiyuan raised his hand, "use my computer to simulate her tracking route! That way, she won't have to use the 'bracelet' to find the way every time!"

"Right!" A-Xin's eyes lit up, "And Liu-Liu's reconnaissance data can be turned into a 'digital map'! Weixi can just look at the map instead of running around herself!"

"But," Lin Yunyun asked worriedly, "will this really 'buy time' for her?"

"At least..." Weixi spoke softly, her voice full of emotion, "your thoughtfulness has already bought me the most 'time' of all."

"Weixi..." The children looked at her.

"And," Weixi smiled faintly, "the time I have left was meant for 'changing the world.' With you helping me change it... the world will change faster."

"Then," A-Xin said loudly, "let's change this world faster, together!"

"Okay—!"

"Great!" A-Xin jumped onto the podium, "'Protect Earth Operations Unit · Full Deployment'! First battle—'Track the New Pipe'! Weixi leads the way! Liu-Liu does reconnaissance! Our whole class is responsible for—"

"Being her support!" The children said in unison.

"Yes!" A-Xin said loudly, "Being her support!"

"Alright! Then let's divide the work!" A-Xin jumped onto the podium and spread out Liu-Liu's map, "'Full Deployment' mission assignments!"

"Weixi, responsible for tracking the pipe with the bracelet!"

"Liu-Liu, responsible for aerial reconnaissance and photos!"

"Qingqing, responsible for recording data!"

"Me, responsible for drawing the route map and coming up with ideas!"

"Old Wu..." A-Xin looked at Old Wu, "you're responsible for... 'charging ahead'!"

"Charging ahead?!" Old Wu stood up excitedly, "I'm the best at charging! Where do I charge to?!"

"Wherever there's difficulty, that's where you charge!" A-Xin said, "Your mission is to lead everyone through the obstacles!"

"Alright!" Old Wu patted his chest, "This 'human shield' of mine is ready to deploy at any time!"

"Hahaha!" The children laughed.

"Lin Yunyun, you're responsible for..." A-Xin thought for a moment, "responsible for 'cheering'! When everyone is tired, you're our 'charging station'!"

"I... can I do it?" Lin Yunyun blushed.

"Of course!" Qingqing said, "You were braver than anyone at the beach cleanup!"

"What about me? What about me?" Wang Zhuankai raised his hand.

"You..." A-Xin thought for a moment, "you're responsible for 'laughing'!"

"Laughing?!"

"Yes!" A-Xin said, "The most important thing for our 'Protect Earth Operations Unit' is morale! Your laughing threshold is the lowest, so wherever there's a joke, you're the first to laugh!"

"Pfft!" Wang Zhuankai couldn't help laughing, "This mission... I was born for it!"

"Hahaha!" The whole class burst into laughter.

"And Ke Zhiyuan!" A-Xin looked at Ke Zhiyuan, "you're responsible for 'digital'! You're the best with computers, so help us organize data and make charts!"

"Leave it to me!" Ke Zhiyuan patted his chest.

"And..." A-Xin swept his gaze across the class, "for everyone else, your mission is—"

"On standby at all times! Wherever we're needed, there we are!"

"Okay—!" The children shouted in unison.

"'Protect Earth Operations Unit · Full Deployment' officially begins!"

The setting sun streamed through the classroom window, falling on the twenty-six children.

Standing by the window, looking at these children, the corners of Weixi's mouth lifted slightly.

"...So this," she said in a low voice, "is what it feels like when everyone knows your secret."

"What does it feel like?" Qingqing walked to her side.

"It feels..." Weixi smiled softly, "like peace of mind."

"By the way, Qingqing," Weixi suddenly thought of something, "can I... ask you for a favor?"

"What favor?" Qingqing asked.

"After I go back..." Weixi lowered her head, speaking softly, "please keep leading everyone to protect the Earth."

"..." Qingqing's eyes reddened.

"Not to replace me," Weixi looked at her, "but to represent 'all who know the truth.' Because..." she smiled faintly, "you now understand the Earth better than anyone."

"Weixi..." Qingqing took a deep breath and nodded hard, "I promise you."

"And," Weixi said softly, "help me tell everyone—even if I'm gone, the Earth is still here. As long as the Earth is here, hope... is still here."

"Mhm." Qingqing nodded, "I will."

"And..." Weixi lowered her head, her voice soft, "if one day, you receive a letter from the 'future'... it will definitely be from me, written to you."

"..." Qingqing's tears finally couldn't be held back and fell.

"Weixi," she choked, "you will definitely receive the letters we wrote. Because..." she looked at Weixi, "every letter we write to the future is written to you."

Weixi froze.

Then, she lowered her head and smiled softly.

"...Mhm." she said, "I know."

The setting sun quietly sank below the horizon.
