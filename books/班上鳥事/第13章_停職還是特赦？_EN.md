# A Class of Feathered Troubles

## Chapter 13: Suspension or Amnesty?

The tempest had arrived with fury, yet vanished with equal haste.

As the last vestiges of thunder faded into the remote recesses of the Central Mountain Range, an incandescent breach tore open the western horizon. Sunset erupted like molten gold, piercing through the immaculate, storm-cleansed leaves of the ancient banyan tree to cast a dappled tapestry of golden radiance across the waterlogged wreckage of Class 6-1.

The air was saturated with the pungent scent of churning loam, cool moisture, and the lingering, unmistakable fragrance of dried pomelo peel.

The corridor outside was now densely packed with teachers and lower-grade students who had rushed over upon hearing the commotion. Scores of curious, astonished, whispering eyes peered through the wide-open front and rear doors, transfixed by the unfolding spectacle inside.

Yet in the center of the classroom, the atmosphere hung as grave and stifling as a military tribunal.

Elderly Principal Chen Guoxiang stood with hands clasped behind his back beside an overturned desk. A white-haired patriarch who had served Luyang Elementary for more than three decades, his face was chiseled with deep, weathered wrinkles as his eyes surveyed the surrounding devastation with complex emotion.

Beside the principal stood Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan, clutching the pink medical journal whose water-soaked edges curled like dried leaves.

"Principal, that is the general summary of the situation."

Zhou Yan drew a sharp breath, his voice strung as taut as chilled pig iron, shattering the tomb-like silence in the classroom:

"Class 6-1 smuggled an unquarantined wild bird into the instructional zone, concealing it for a full month; during the sudden incident moments ago, two aluminum transom window frames, a pane of glass, and a ceiling-fan speed-control switch were damaged; multiple students engaged in extremely hazardous leaping and tackling within the room, resulting in cranial abrasions and a right-shoulder contusion for Lin Jingtang, and a lacerated finger for Chen Dasheng."

Zhou Yan paused, his gaze slamming down like two iron hammers upon Lin Jingtang and Lu Shuqing:

"Pursuant to Article 12, Clause 1 of the Luyang Elementary Student Disciplinary Code for 'Deliberate Concealment of Hazardous Sources,' Article 14 for 'Severe Vandalism of Public Property,' and Article 20 for 'Endangering Public Campus Safety'—the ringleaders shall be issued two major demerits, placed on a one-week out-of-school suspension for reflection, and their legal guardians shall reimburse the full cost of all property damages; furthermore, Homeroom Teacher Wen Yating, having failed in supervisory duties leading to loss of classroom control, shall face formal inquiry by the Teacher Evaluation Committee and immediate suspension from homeroom duties."

"Gasp——!"

A muted wave of shocked exclamations rippled through the crowded hallway.

Two major demerits plus an out-of-school suspension was essentially the supreme death penalty for an elementary student; and a suspension from homeroom duties would outright obliterate the nascent career of a freshly graduated novice teacher.

Standing nearby, the Academic Dean released a faint, helpless sigh, while several young female teachers who were close to Wen Yating turned their faces away in distress.

The rules were the rules.

Faced with the ironclad physical evidence of wrecked property and bleeding injuries, no one could summon a legalistic argument to refute him.

"Director! This has nothing to do with Teacher Wen!"

A raspy, ferocious roar abruptly detonated through the stagnant air.

Lin Jingtang violently shrugged off Fat Monkey's supportive grip, storming forward with heavy strides. The blood on his forehead had dried into a jagged, dark crimson scab, his right shoulder twitching with sharp pain, yet he held his spine ramrod straight, his chest heaving violently:

"I found the bird on my way to school! I smuggled the shoebox inside! And I forced the entire class not to say a damn word to Teacher Wen!"

Staring straight into Zhou Yan's man-eating glare, the boy ground his teeth to dust, his eyes bloodshot yet refusing to yield by even half an inch:

"Teacher Wen knew nothing! She just arrived at this school, spending every single day racking her brain trying to teach a bunch of rotten mud like us! The one who smashed the desks is me, the one who stepped on the glass is me, and the one who dove off the furniture is me! If you want to give out major demerits, give them all to me! If you want money, tell my dad to drive his gravel truck here and pay for it! If you want to beat or kill someone, come at me—don't lay a finger on Teacher Wen, and don't touch anyone in this class!"

"Lin Jingtang! Do you think this is some dockside gang where you can play the righteous outlaw?!" Zhou Yan barked harshly. "This is an educational institution! A place of discipline and order!"

"Report, Director!"

The very instant Zhou Yan's words fell, Fat Monkey thrust out his spherical belly, stepping forward through the mud to yell at the top of his lungs:

"I'm the one who stole the millet from my mom's kitchen! Every time the bird was fed, I took part! If you're handing out major demerits, count me in too!"

"Report, Director! I dried and brought the deodorizing tea leaves from home!" Xiaoyu pushed her cracked spectacles up her nose, her voice trembling yet possessing astonishing resolve. "I kept the clinical journal; if punishments are being dealt, I am the primary accomplice!"

"And me! I'm the one who snapped the fan switch!" Dashenggong raised his gauze-wrapped thumb and stomped forward, unleashing his thunderous vocal cords. "If you dare punish Brother Tang, I'll sing the entire school anthem backward a hundred times right here today!"

"And us!"

"The entire class took part!"

In that singular instant, a miracle unfolded.

Those sitting in the puddles, those leaning against the walls, those wiping away tears, those nursing deep bruises... all twenty-six children, without exchanging a single glance, rose to their feet in unified unison!

Chins held high, hands clasped tightly together, they formed an impenetrable living fortress in the middle of the ruined classroom.

Over the preceding five years, every time this class caused a catastrophe, they had pointed fingers, ratted on each other, hurled chalk erasers, and thrown dead frogs, driving teacher after teacher to the brink of cardiac arrest.

Yet today, this very collection of notorious, selfish troublemakers did not produce a single soul who dodged accountability.

Across twenty-six youthful, battle-scarred faces shone a luminance never seen before—the forged dignity and solidarity born from collectively guarding a fragile miracle of life.

Overwhelmed by this astonishing wave of defiance, Zhou Yan instinctively staggered back half a pace, his face cycling through shades of livid green and chalky white.

"You... this is open mutiny! Collective insurrection against disciplinary authority!" Zhou Yan's pointing finger shook with rage. "The school code is the ultimate baseline! If we make an exception today because there are many of you, tomorrow seven hundred students will bring wild birds and stray dogs into their classrooms—how could the school function?!"

"Director Zhou Yan."

A sharp, resounding palm strike suddenly detonated against the podium.

The one who struck the desk was none other than the novice homeroom teacher who had spent the entire month walking on eggshells and speaking in whispers before Zhou Yan—Wen Yating.

Wen Yating slowly drew herself up to her full height.

The tear tracks on her cheeks were still damp, her professional suit smeared with grime and mud, yet within those previously timid eyes now burned a ferocious, blinding fire that commanded absolute awe.

It was the fearless dignity of a genuine educator when the fundamental essence of her profession had been attacked.

"Teacher Wen, what is the meaning of this attitude?" Zhou Yan's brows knitted sharply.

"Director Zhou, I wish to ask you: what, precisely, is the purpose of a school?"

Wen Yating's voice trembled, yet every syllable rang with the pristine clarity of striking chimes:

"If an educational institution only knows how to bludgeon children with icy bylaws, only knows how to measure morality by cracked windowpanes and tripped circuit breakers... then what are we cultivating—living, breathing human beings, or obedient wooden marionettes who only know how to execute commands?!"

Wen Yating swept her arm toward the battered children behind her, tears welling once more even as her voice carried down the full length of the hallway:

"For the past month, you stalked them with binoculars, suspecting demonic possession, suspecting psychological cold violence, suspecting a violent insurrection! But do you have any idea what they actually did during this month?!"

"They did not utter a single curse word! They did not engage in a single fistfight! They did not bully a single vulnerable peer!"

"To avoid frightening an injured nestling, they compressed the rowdiest voices in this school into whispers; to keep it from shivering in drafts, they endured thirty-degree stifling heat while drenched in sweat; and just moments ago, when that bird was about to be pulverized by the ceiling fan, these twelve-year-old children did not yield an inch! They used their own chests and ribs as human cushions, snatching a creature weighing less than fifty grams straight out of the clutches of the Grim Reaper!"

Wen Yating drew a deep breath, meeting Zhou Yan's stunned gaze, lifting her chin with supreme conviction:

"If you define this as 'Deliberate Concealment of Hazardous Sources,' if you classify this as 'Malicious Vandalism of Public Property'—then I, a teacher holding a master's degree in educational psychology, solemnly declare to you: this is the greatest, most profound lesson in life education I have ever witnessed!"

"If demerits are to be issued, expel me first! Because it was I who failed to teach them to be cold-blooded bystanders in the face of dying life!"

As her impassioned defense concluded, the entire fourth floor of the Loyalty Building fell into stunned, breathless silence.

Out in the hallway, the eyes of a dozen watching teachers welled with tears; the Academic Dean bowed his head in quiet shame.

Zhou Yan stood frozen, his mouth parting, yet unable to produce a solitary syllable of rebuttal. The imposing authority of the "Black-Faced Judge" had been shattered into dust in an instant.

Right then, Lu Shuqing stepped forward with serene composure.

In her hands, she cradled the bruised shoebox, alongside Little Ma perched within Lin Jingtang's palm.

"Director Zhou, Principal." Lu Shuqing adjusted her spectacles, her tone crisp, clinical, and precise. "I am the Class President of Class 6-1. As a student officer, concealing the truth violated my designated duties. However, I wish to present a single dataset to the administration."

All eyes converged upon Lu Shuqing.

"One month ago, our class averaged two physical brawls per day; three students were sent to the Student Affairs Office weekly for disciplinary reprimands; and our previous three homeroom teachers lasted an average of fewer than twenty days on the job."

"Yet during the thirty days spent protecting Lin Xiaoma, the entire class recorded zero disciplinary infractions, achieved a one-hundred-percent attendance rate, and our average score on this countywide mock math exam improved by 4.2 points compared to last semester."

Lu Shuqing raised her head, her calm gaze meeting the elderly principal's eyes directly:

"This bird did not bring avian flu to our campus, nor did it instigate a riot. It merely enabled the twenty-six of us to live, for the very first time, as a true collective."

"Cheep... (You speak reasonably well for a hairless female strategist...)"

Little Ma ruffled its feathers in Lin Jingtang's palm, muttering an insufferably haughty critique inside Lu Shuqing's mind.

Principal Chen Guoxiang exhaled a long, resonant breath.

The golden rays of the setting sun bathed his silver hair. Stepping forward slowly, he extended a calloused, weathered hand to gently touch the cut on Lin Jingtang's forehead, before lowering his gaze to the delicate grey sparrow nestled within the boy's palm.

"What a remarkable Lin Xiaoma..." the old principal murmured, a profoundly tender smile gracing his weathered lips.

Turning around, he looked upon the pale, crestfallen Zhou Yan:

"Director Zhou, school rules are written by mortals, but the fundamental essence of education is cultivating the human soul. If we crush our children's desperate, sacrificial compassion beneath the weight of demerits, then this half-century-old elementary school has utterly lost its conscience."

Zhou Yan swayed slightly, finally slumping his head in defeat, his voice dry and muted: "Principal... but the property damage... and the public impression..."

"The broken glass and window frames shall be repaired through the class collecting scrap paper and plastic bottles across campus during recess; I will personally cover half the labor costs out of my own pocket;"

The old principal surveyed the entire classroom, his voice ringing with warm, grandfatherly authority:

"All twenty-six students of Class 6-1 are granted full amnesty from disciplinary demerits; Lin Jingtang and Chen Dasheng shall immediately proceed to the health clinic escorted by Teacher Wen for bandaging, funded entirely by the Principal's Discretionary Fund!"

"HOORAY——!!!"

The teachers and students out in the hallway were the first to erupt into applause, followed instantly by Class 6-1 detonating into a roof-lifting roar of jubilation! The children jumped, laughed, and embraced, tears and laughter dissolving seamlessly into the ocean of golden evening light.

Yet the old principal's following words caused the room's exuberant uproar to gently subside.

Looking down upon the fledgling in Lin Jingtang's hand, the principal's tone grew soft, yet unmistakably resolute:

"However, children of Class 6-1, this amnesty comes with an essential condition."

"This is a creature of the open sky; it cannot be imprisoned forever inside a shoebox and a classroom. On the day Little Ma's strength is fully restored, you must, with your own hands... return it to the heavens."
