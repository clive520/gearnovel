# A Class of Feathered Troubles

## Chapter 14: The Blackboard on Release Day

At dusk on Friday two weeks later, Luyang Town was blessed with the most crystalline sunset of the entire summer.

The aluminum transom window that had been battered by the storm had long since been fitted with a pristine, transparent pane of plate glass; the damaged rotary speed controller overhead had likewise been replaced by a brand-new, ivory-white dial.

The entirety of these repair expenses had been earned by the twenty-six children of Class 6-1 over these past fourteen days, utilizing every recess and after-school hour to scour every nook and cranny of the campus, gathering eight massive burlap sacks of plastic bottles and scrap newspapers to sell to recyclers.

Even the General Affairs custodian, legendary for his cantankerous scrutiny, couldn't help chewing his unlit cigarette as he inspected the window frame, muttering under his breath: "These little bandits... their hands are sharper than professional plumbers."

And at this exact moment, within the classroom of Class 6-1, a solemn handover ceremony was underway.

Upon the teacher's podium, the Nike shoebox that had accompanied the entire class for thirty-nine full days had been scrubbed spotlessly clean.

Beside the box, Xiaoyu wielded her pink ballpoint pen wrapped in scotch tape, carefully penning the final entry onto the swollen notebook pages in her neat script:

"June 28th, 16:45 hours: Patient plumage coverage 100%; flight feathers rigid and robust; body weight thirty-four grams; wild survival and foraging trials passed. Discharge criterion: fully achieved."

The instant she closed the journal, Xiaoyu sniffed, her tears splashing soundlessly onto the rhinestone bear on the cover.

Sitting in the front row, Lu Shuqing slowly rose to her feet.

Behind her, Lin Jingtang cradled an exquisite small bamboo carrying cage in both hands, taking measured strides up to the podium.

Within that enclosure, Little Ma had undergone a complete, breathtaking metamorphosis.

Its coat of grey-brown feathers gleamed with an oily, vibrant sheen under the slanting rays of the evening sun, the sharp black-and-white patterns on its cheeks etched clean as if carved by a scalpel; when its obsidian-bright eyes darted about, they radiated the sharp, untamed arrogance of a true aerial hunter.

"The hour has arrived," Lu Shuqing whispered softly, her cadence as calm and measured as reciting classical verse.

Across all twenty-six students, not a single soul uttered a sound.

Standing beside the rear door with glistening eyes, Teacher Wen Yating gave the children a gentle, encouraging nod.

The procession slowly filed out of the Loyalty Building.

This time, they did not walk on tiptoe with ghost steps, nor did anyone hover chairs two centimeters above the floor; yet the procession marched with a silence that was heartbreakingly reverent.

Down the long corridor, down the weathered concrete stairs, out across the field fragrant with summer grass...

Teachers and students who had stayed behind for campus cleanup paused their brooms, spontaneously parting to open a wide corridor across the running track. No one jeered, no one mocked; everyone watched in hushed awe as these once-notorious rebels walked past.

Even before the fourth-floor corridor window of the Student Affairs Office, the Black-Faced Judge, Zhou Yan, stood silently with hands resting against the railing, his deep gaze locked onto the procession below.

The procession finally came to a halt beneath the ancient banyan tree.

That was where the story had begun.

Thirty-nine days ago, right here amid the tangled roots and muddy earth, a dying nestling had lain in a puddle of blood, while A-Tang's knuckles had been torn bloody by a feral cat.

And today, thirty-nine days later, the setting sun dyed the massive, sprawling canopy of the banyan tree in shades of blazing, imperial purple.

A gentle breeze whispered through the foliage, rustling the leaves as though this ancient empire was officially welcoming home its exiled prince.

Lin Jingtang slowly crouched down beneath the roots.

He unlatched the small wicker door of the bamboo cage.

Without a moment's hesitation, Little Ma kicked off with two vigorous, athletic claws, springing gracefully from the cage to land firm and steady right upon Lin Jingtang's calloused, battle-scarred thumb.

The boy held his arm rigid in midair, the muscles along his forearm trembling slightly from supreme restraint.

"Hey..."

Lin Jingtang spoke, his voice as coarse as though he had swallowed a fistful of river gravel:

"Little Ma... once you're back up in the canopy... keep your wits about you..."

"Don't go charging at every worm you see—some of those hairy caterpillars are poisonous..."

"When you spot a black cat, fly as high as you damn well can, you hear me? I... I won't be chasing cats around with my belt every day anymore..."

Lin Jingtang sniffled hard, fat tears splashing down onto the mossy roots; this invincible, unyielding brawler was now weeping uncontrollably:

"If... if you ever get hungry, just fly back to the fourth floor of the Loyalty Building... I'll always leave a slit open in the window... in my desk drawer... there will always be egg yolk and millet waiting for you..."

Little Ma cocked its head sideways, staring quietly at the boy's tear-streaked face.

This time, it did not peck at him with its razor beak, nor did it thrash its wings in indignant cursing.

It simply took a tiny, deliberate step forward, and using its soft, feathered head, gently rubbed against the rough knuckle of Lin Jingtang's thumb.

In that singular instant, tears spilled down the cheeks of several girls in the class, while the boys clamped down on their lips and looked away.

Having nuzzled the boy's knuckle, Little Ma turned, its bright, obsidian eyes cutting through the circle of students to lock dead onto Lu Shuqing's face.

Lu Shuqing stood two paces away, hands resting at her sides, her expression maintaining its clinical composure, yet the eyes behind her spectacles brimmed with shimmering pools of crystalline light.

Deep within her consciousness, that familiar, insufferably haughty child's voice resonated clearly for the very last time:

*"Hairless little female monkey in the white coat."*

Lu Shuqing's heart trembled faintly.

*"This Sovereign concedes... the egg-yolk divine nectar you concocted was tolerably passable; and the pomelo celestial herbs offered in tribute were moderately acceptable in fragrance."*

In Little Ma's telepathic cadence, the usual bluster had softened, replaced by an inexplicable, solemn majesty:

*"Had you not risked life and limb, this Sovereign's imperial form would have long turned to dust. This debt of grace, the Seventh-Generation Rightful Heir of the Luyang Banyan Empire... shall remember."*

Lu Shuqing bit her lip, whispering gently back in the silence of her mind:

*"Cut the drivel. The sky is your true domain. Fly up there, and never fall down again, you stupid bird."*

The bird's black eyes seemed to flicker with amusement, as if laughing.

*"Absurd! I am the Lord of the Firmament! You upright apes, dare not underestimate a deity!"*

That mental voice suddenly surged upward into a blinding, triumphant crescendo, crystallizing into an ear-splitting, joyous cry:

*"Farewell, my noisy monkey servants!"*

"CHEEEEEP————!!"

Little Ma kicked hard against Lin Jingtang's thumb!

Two wide, powerful wings unfurled into a flawless arc against the crimson sunset!

*Whir-flut-flut-flutter——!*

A miniature gale detonated within the boy's cupped palms as Little Ma's body transformed into a sleek streak of greyish-brown light, rocketing straight toward the heavens into the blazing sunset!

It first wheeled in a wide, triumphant circle around the ancient banyan tree's massive crown.

Next, it swooped past the fourth-floor Student Affairs window, letting out a crisp, piercing chirp toward Zhou Yan leaning against the railing; and finally, catching the warm thermal updrafts rising into the twilight, like a golden arrow released from an imperial bow, it soared resolutely toward the boundless, molten furnace of the evening sky!

"LITTLE MA——!!"

Dashenggong tore open his iron lungs, hurling one final, world-shaking roar toward the firmament!

"FAREWELL! SUPREME COMMANDER——!!"

Fat Monkey, Lao Wu, A-Bao, and all twenty-six children surged forward to the center of the field, pumping their arms skyward, lifting tear-stained, radiant faces, waving frantically toward that silhouette climbing higher and higher until it was reduced to a speck of black dust against the burning horizon!

The golden afterglow of the sunset bathed every child's shoulders, stretching their long shadows across the green grass.

In the sky above, there were no more shoeboxes to constrain it, no more wooden splints to bind its limbs.

It had finally returned home.

Only when that tiny black speck dissolved completely into the fiery heart of the dusk did the children reluctantly withdraw their tear-filled gazes.

As they followed behind Teacher Wen Yating, slowly walking back up to the fourth floor of the Loyalty Building, the classroom of Class 6-1 was already bathed in the gentle quietude of evening.

The setting sun poured through the newly replaced windowpanes at a warm angle, bathing the podium in deep amber hues.

And upon the spotless, dark-green chalkboard behind the podium, written in pristine white chalk with exquisite, dignified strokes, were eight characters full of enduring warmth.

It was the handwriting left behind by Teacher Wen Yating:

"Class 6-1, Everyone Fully Recovered."
