# A Class of Feathered Troubles

## Chapter 8: Raid! Herbal Foliage and the Deodorization Redoubt

In biological taxonomy, avian excrement is categorized as a suspension of uric acid and digestive residue.

This organic paste—composed of sixty-five percent thick, white urate crystals and thirty-five percent dark-brown, semi-digested egg yolk and oat mash—might have been nothing more than a trivial trace of nature upon the airy boughs of an ancient banyan, flash-dried by mountain breezes within minutes.

Yet, within the sealed pressure cooker of Class 6-1—where the Southern Taiwan early summer temperature soared past thirty-two degrees Celsius, the curtains hung two-thirds shut, and the doors and windows were choked down to hair-thin ventilation seams—this piping-hot, freshly minted emission was functionally indistinguishable from detonating a miniature biochemical dirty bomb inside a sealed submarine.

"Nnngh...!!"

As if struck dead-on by an ungrounded high-voltage arc, A-Tang's neck jerked violently backward, his eyeballs nearly popping from their sockets.

That sour, warm, fermenting plume—reeking of sulfurous egg mash and stale digestive juices—billowed straight through the ventilation holes atop the shoebox, surging like an invisible phantom directly into the deepest mucous membranes of A-Tang's nasal cavities.

A-Tang's fingernails gouged deep into the timber rim of his desk, the hairs across his entire body standing on end in an instant. His visage shifted rapidly from crimson to white, then from white to sickly jade, his Adam's apple bobbing in violent, convulsive spasms as he forced an eruption of gastric acid straight back down into his stomach.

"Xu Fangyu..." Lu Shuqing pushed up her spectacles, her voice slicing through the gloom like an autopsy scalpel, though the pulsing blue vein along her temple betrayed her inner horror. "You have three seconds to initiate the containment protocol. If this odor breaches the corridor—"

"I know! Stop rushing me!"

At this exact heartbeat, Hygiene Monitor Xu Fangyu (Xiao Yu) unleashed the terrifying survival instincts reserved only for severe germaphobes staring into the maw of the apocalypse.

Zipping open her pink backpack—which had survived three separate dips in hospital-grade disinfectant—she hoisted a weighty, hermetically sealed borosilicate glass container onto her desk.

*Snap!* The four-sided latch sprang open.

A complex, aromatic wave—mingling the bitter, concentrated oils of sun-dried pomelo rinds, the rich, roasted fragrance of vintage high-mountain oolong tea dregs, and the crisp, astringent camphor bite of desiccated banyan foliage—detonated across the front row!

This was Xiao Yu's secret strategic weapon, painstakingly ground in a stone mortar across three solid hours upon her courtyard drying floor last night: the "Grade-A All-Natural Botanical Adsorption Deodorant Bedding."

"Dashan! Jam a rag into the bottom seam of the back door!" Gritting her teeth, Xiao Yu brandished stainless steel medical forceps, scooping a massive clump of the fragrant herb mixture with blurring speed. "Old Wu, flap two empty plastic bags to generate a localized low-pressure draft! A-Tang, extract the Supreme Commander, gently! If you drag a single drop of that filth onto the desk, I will scrub your hide raw with steel wool!"

The operation achieved fever pitch in a single second.

Holding his breath until his temples throbbed, A-Tang slipped his trembling hands into two clear disposable gloves supplied by Xiao Yu. Reaching delicately into the shoebox, he hoisted the little sparrow—plump as a tennis ball, cocking its head in blameless innocence—gently into the air.

Meanwhile, within Shuqing's cerebral cortex, the imperial perpetrator of this biohazard was smacking its chops in blissful, haughty satisfaction:

*“Ahhh... Exquisite! Positively invigorating!”*

*“Do you two-legged baboons comprehend nothing of systemic detoxification?! The heavy impurities within my royal vessels have been utterly expelled; my celestial bloodline is at least thirty percent purer now! Hey! You female ape brandishing the dried grass, pack those aromatic leaves thick! My imperial talons refuse to slumber upon naked cardboard!”*

Drawing a long, frozen breath, Shuqing fired back an icy psychic salvo:

*【Squeak one more syllable, and I will apply your expelled impurities to your beak as royal lip balm.】*

Little Ma froze rigid, tucking its neck discreetly into its downy collar.

Shuqing's forceps danced like silver lightning. With surgical economy, she pinched the soiled paper, banishing it into a triple-layered Ziploc bag Xiao Yu held agape. She then carpeted the shoebox floor with a generous layer of the camphor-and-tea-scented botanical bedding, sculpting a comfortable, miniature cup right in the center.

A-Tang lowered Little Ma securely back into the herbal nest.

Little Ma nestled comfortably into the tea fragments with a contented shudder, the robust citrus oils smothering the final remnants of sour stench into the deepest fibers of the box.

Yet, in the precise microsecond when all twenty-six students unclasped their clenched jaws to release an exhausted sigh of deliverance—

*“KABLAM————!!”*

A thunderous kick blasted the grass-green wooden back door wide open, slamming it violently against the concrete wall!

A cascade of aged plaster dust rattled from the lintel, drifting like an ashen fog through the afternoon sunbeams.

A skeletal, pitch-black figure, tall as an iron tower, wielding a deep-blue iron disciplinary binder, wearing spotless white cotton inspection gloves, stepped across the rear threshold of Class 6-1 like an inquisitor ascending from the ninth circle of hell, trailing a tempest of murderous intent!

Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan had launched his surprise raid!

"Student Affairs surprise inspection!"

Zhou Yan's voice detonated like an air-raid klaxon within a sealed bunker, his hawkish glare impaling the classroom in an instant:

"Everyone! Stand! Hands behind your heads! Step half a pace away from your desks!"

In a thousandth of a second, the room was encased in solid ice.

Across the classroom air, stirred by the violent gust of the kicked-open door, an uncanny current of scent curled outward—a dominant bouquet of dried pomelo zest, laced with the faintest, almost imperceptible undertone of fermented avian waste.

Zhou Yan's razor-thin nostrils twitched with acute suspicion the second his boots kissed the floor.

A feral, triumphant gleam flared in his eyes—the ecstasy of a veteran bloodhound finally catching the scent of fresh gore amidst dead leaves.

"An odor."

Zhou Yan advanced slowly, his hard leather soles striking the terrazzo in heavy, measured thuds—*tap, tap, tap*.

Prowling toward the center of the room, his icy gaze raked across every desktop:

"You little degenerates! Did you honestly believe that by bolting the doors and windows, I wouldn't uncover your illicit plots?! What is this stench in the air?! It isn't chalk dust, nor is it cafeteria rations... It carries the fermented odor of decomposing botanical matter, along with the foul effluvium of a living creature's waste!"

Zhou Yan came to an abrupt halt, slamming the iron binder onto Big Megaphone's desk, rattling the pencil case into the air:

"Chen Dashan! Confess! Are you harboring unquarantined, illicit reptiles in this room?! Or are you stoking fires in your drawers to brew occult talisman decoctions?!"

Hands locked over his head, Big Megaphone stood pinned against the wall like a pinned specimen, his broad face flushed a bruised purple, squeezing an aspirated croak from his throat like a strangled gander: "R-Reporting... Director... No talisman decoctions..."

"No? Then I shall dismantle this room with my own hands!"

With a cold sneer, Zhou Yan lunged with his white-gloved hand toward the first-row drawers!

In that blinding fraction of a second, cold sweat drenched Lin Jingtang's spine.

His desk sat dead center in the fourth row, and the shoebox harboring Little Ma lay nestled in the deepest shadows of his compartment!

Zhou Yan's search proceeded with terrifying efficiency. His hands operated like twin mechanical rakes, yanking open drawers, dumping textbooks, notebooks, and water bottles onto desktops in a flurry of papers. At this velocity, he would stand before Lin Jingtang's desk in under thirty seconds!

And the second that box was unearthed, Little Ma would be condemned to the incinerator, while the class would face catastrophic collective suspension!

"Execute."

Standing by the front-row window, her eyes fixed straight ahead, Lu Shuqing capitalized on the rustling draft outside to vibrate her vocal cords, releasing an encrypted whisper thin as silk:

"Grand Metamorphosis. Conveyor chain: engage."

In the exact microsecond Zhou Yan ducked his head to dump a girl's backpack onto the front desk—

Class 6-1's covert emergency contingency grid—drilled over a hundred times in absolute silence over the past week—roared to life!

The adductor muscles along A-Tang's inner thighs contracted with sudden ferocity.

His right hand slipped into the drawer, his five knuckles steady as bedrock, sliding the shoebox outward at a uniform crawl of one centimeter per second. Four pads of soft wool felt adhered to the box's underside glided across the polished wood without surrendering a whisper of noise.

The instant the box cleared the drawer's lip, the boy to A-Tang's left hooked his knee under the box's flank. With a subtle heave of his thigh, the box traversed the five-centimeter gap between their desks like a torpedo entering a launch tube, hovering in midair before sliding seamlessly beneath Old Wu's desk in the third row!

Old Wu's monumental frame served as an unbreachable fortress of human flesh.

Hands clamped behind his head, Old Wu pulled his diaphragm tight, his thick folds of abdominal fat creating a deep shadow beneath the compartment. His two plump hands executed a blind catch behind his back with breathtaking finesse, tucking the shoebox directly up into the loose hem of his oversized track jacket!

Simultaneously, an enraged Zhou Yan finished scouring the second row.

"Nothing?! How can there be nothing?!"

Zhou Yan's breath turned ragged. His white gloves had unearthed crumpled draft paper, two plastic rulers, and an eraser stub, but aside from the faint citrus aroma, there was no trace of contraband!

Whirling around, his glare locked onto Old Wu in the third row like an iron harpoon!

"Wu Chenkai! Why are you trembling?!"

Hands clamped over his skull, every ripple of flesh on Old Wu's cheeks vibrating in microscopic panic, his eyes shone with hollow, angelic innocence: "R-Reporting... Director... My... My tummy aches... I ate cold watermelon for breakfast..."

"Tummy ache? Hands down! Lift your shirt!" Zhou Yan barked, closing the distance to Old Wu's desk in two predatory strides!

Yet, during that three-tenths-of-a-second blind spot as Zhou Yan took his first step and his line-of-sight was obstructed by Old Wu's broad frame—

The boy in the fourth row directly behind Old Wu made his move!

Feigning a leg cramp, the boy lunged forward, his hands darting beneath Old Wu's broad back like striking adders, plucking the blue shoebox away into the fourth row!

Instantly, the fourth row passed to the fifth, the fifth to the sixth!

Right beneath Zhou Yan's nose, the entire room executed a breathtaking game of "silent dominoes"!

Twenty-six students, fifty-two arms, weaving an invisible pipeline of human reflexes beneath the desks. The shoebox surged across calloused fingertips, frayed gym socks, and the billowing shadows of uniform jackets at blinding speed!

Within the box, tossed through a dizzying series of barrel rolls, Little Ma unleashed a psychic screech thick with bile straight into Shuqing's skull:

*“Stop... Halt! You wretched pirate monkeys! Am I strapped into a looping roller coaster?! I am airsick! *Hurl*... The Phoenix Nectar I consumed earlier is about to regurgitate!”*

Standing with hands clamped behind her head, Shuqing snarled back telepathically through gritted teeth:

*【Swallow it back down! Dare vomit in that box, and I will parcel you and your bile straight to Director Zhou via express post!】*

The shoebox rocketed across six rows of desks in a blur, terminating at the feet of Zhou Ziqiang in the rear row.

Zhou Ziqiang's perpetually comatose visage remained entirely serene. Feigning a turn to adjust his chair, the toe of his right sock flicked with effortless elegance, guiding the shoebox down a groove in the concrete baseboard straight into the bottom of the large recycling bin packed with pristine newspapers!

The newspapers yielded a faint, papery rustle, then all returned to the silence of the grave.

The entire transit sequence had taken precisely 4.2 seconds.

At this exact heartbeat, Zhou Yan hauled Old Wu's athletic jacket upward with a savage yank.

Yet all that met Zhou Yan's eyes was Old Wu's pale, rippling expanse of pork-belly midriff, heaving in frantic respiration.

Aside from a layer of sweat-soaked grime, there was not a single feather to be seen.

Zhou Yan froze.

His arm hung suspended in midair, his brow knit into a baffled knot.

Refusing to concede defeat, he tore open Old Wu's drawer—bare as a desert, housing only two dog-eared math exercise booklets.

He charged to Lin Jingtang's desk, ripping away the navy-blue jacket, flipping the compartment inside out—aside from three sharpened pencils and blank scrap paper, not a single grain of rice was found!

Five minutes.

Ten minutes.

Like a cornered, ravening wolf, Zhou Yan overturned all twenty-six desks, twenty-six schoolbags, and even inspected the seams behind the window curtains!

His white gloves were coated in grime, his spectacles sliding to the tip of his nose, his chest heaving like a tractor engine on the verge of throwing a rod.

Yet he found nothing.

No contraband beasts, no occult instruments, no herbal brewing cauldrons.

The classroom was as spotless as a sanitation department isolation ward. Only the crisp, elegant fragrance of pomelo peel and oolong tea drifted lazily through the sunlit air, offering a mute, mocking rebuke to the administrator's hysteria.

"Director..."

Class President Lu Shuqing slowly lowered her hands, her expression detached, cool, and tinged with the dignified indignation of the wrongfully accused:

"In response to the County Education Bureau's upcoming inspection of our Model Moral and Environmental Zone, our Hygiene Monitor, Xu Fangyu, organized the class to prepare all-natural pomelo peel adsorption sachets at her own expense, seeking to purify classroom dust and odors to secure the countywide top honor for our school."

Shuqing raised her gaze, fixing her calm eyes through the lenses squarely upon Zhou Yan's purple face:

"May I inquire of the Director... which specific school code our environmental initiative has violated?"

Zhou Yan stood paralyzed before the podium.

A dry, choking rattle issued from his throat, his iron binder warping beneath his white-knuckled grip.

He surveyed the ranks of students around him.

Twenty-six children, standing with impeccable posture, their eyes luminous, upright, and brimming with the noble sorrow of those subjected to groundless persecution.

Zhou Yan bit his lip until blood nearly welled, but as an administrator bound by procedural protocol, he could levy no disciplinary sanctions against these "model scholars" in the total absence of physical evidence!

"Fine... Splendid."

Squeezing the syllables through his molars, Zhou Yan drew his red pen with a trembling hand, slashing an enormous, humiliated mark of "Superior" onto the sanitation log.

"Class 6-1... you had best pray that during next Wednesday's official inspection, this room remains as immaculate as it is today."

Pivoting with violent finality, clutching his binder, Zhou Yan strode furiously out the door.

Only when the heavy thud of his shoes had completely faded down the stairwell did the classroom detonate into a tidal wave of voiceless, ecstatic jubilee!

Children threw their arms around one another, jumping in frantic, silent circles, tears of triumph gleaming in the corners of their eyes!

Meanwhile, within the recycling bin at the rear of the room, Little Ma poked its tea-dusted gray head from the newspapers, sighing in exhausted relief deep within Shuqing's mind:

*“...These savage apes possess tactical coordination marginally worthy of serving as my Imperial Praetorian Guard.”*

Releasing a long, shuddering breath, Lu Shuqing sank weakly into her chair against the podium.

The back of her blouse was thoroughly soaked in cold sweat.

She knew this victory was merely a reprieve. Zhou Yan's predatory gaze was now anchored unshakeably to the third floor, and in the days to come, should a single feather drift from the sill, the abyss awaiting them would be bottomless indeed.
