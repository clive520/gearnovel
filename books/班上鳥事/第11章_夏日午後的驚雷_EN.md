# A Class of Feathered Troubles

## Chapter 11: Summer Afternoon Thunderstorm

During the second period on Thursday afternoon, the sky over Luyang Town darkened with unnatural, sinister velocity.

Within a mere twenty minutes, the sweltering dome of blinding white light was utterly devoured by leaden-black cumulonimbus clouds rolling in from across the Central Mountain Range. The ambient temperature plunged by five degrees, yet the atmospheric humidity spiked to a suffocating ninety-nine percent. Howling with the scent of churning loam and imminent deluge, a tempestuous squall savaged the grounds outside the Loyalty Building, thrashing the ancient banyan tree's tens of thousands of branches into violent, rolling swells of black breakers.

It was the prelude to Taiwan's midsummer afternoon convective thunderstorm—the most common, yet most violent meteorological phenomenon of the island.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, however, the four aged, three-bladed iron ceiling fans overhead had been cranked to their highest gear.

"Whir-clatter, whir-clatter—"

The heavy, cold iron blades carved through the stagnant, stifling air at frantic speeds, droning with the low, bass rumble of helicopter rotors. Teacher Wen Yating had personally spun the dial to "high" during the previous period—because the air was simply too oppressive, she had even thoughtfully propped open all the transom windows along the hallway to their widest angle.

At the podium, Wen Yating kept her head bowed, diligently grading Chinese practice workbooks.

A faint, contented smile even played across the corners of her lips.

In her view, the transformation of Class 6-1 over recent weeks was nothing short of a divine miracle in educational history. No one shrieked before the approaching thunderstorm; no one seized the gloomy darkness as an excuse to fling paper airplanes; the twenty-six children below sat ramrod straight, quiet as a gathering of novice monks meditating in a secluded mountain monastery.

She had even penned a deeply poignant observation in her teaching journal: "Love and patience shall at last smooth the thorns of rebellion."

Yet what Teacher Wen failed to realize was that the tranquil demeanor of these "novice monks" was stretched as tight as a piano wire on the verge of snapping.

In the fourth row by the window, Lin Jingtang was hunched flat across his desk, his arms wrapped tightly around the Nike shoebox, which was cracked open by only a sliver.

Cold sweat drenched his entire back.

Inside the shoebox, Little Ma was pacing frantically with unprecedented agitation.

Ever since yesterday, when its splints had been shed and it had achieved its life's very first hovering wingbeat, the untamed wildness innate to creatures of the sky had fully awakened within this fledged bird.

And at this exact moment, the abrupt plunge in atmospheric barometric pressure coupled with the electrostatic crackle in the air drove this hypersensitive creature into a near-feral frenzy.

"Cheep... cheep-cheep... (Get away! The deluge is coming! Fire from heaven will tear the firmament apart!)"

In Lu Shuqing's mind, Little Ma's usually imperious, childish voice had completely transformed into a shriek of sheer, primal terror:

*"Release this Sovereign! Wretched hole! This place will be engulfed by mountain torrents! Heaven and earth are collapsing! Cataclysm! Cataclysm!"*

Lu Shuqing bit down hard on her lower lip, the knuckles of her right hand clutching her mechanical pencil turning ghostly pale.

Lifting her head across two rows of desks, she shot Lin Jingtang a look of dire, merciless warning: [Pin it down! Do not let it out under any circumstances!]

Lin Jingtang received the signal.

Slipping two fingers through the slit of the shoebox, he sought to soothe that wildly vibrating ball of grey fluff with his body heat.

"Good Little Ma... it's okay... it's just a little rain..." Lin Jingtang pressed his lips to the cardboard rim, murmuring an inaudible prayer in a breathy rasp, "I'm right here... don't be scared..."

Yet the savage will of mother nature has never spared thought for the tender secrets of elementary schoolchildren.

In this exact split-second, the universe abruptly lost all color.

A bolt of apocalyptic lightning, ghastly white as polished bone and edged in blinding cobalt blue, ripped through the pitch-black heavens without a shred of warning!

"BOOOOOOOM——!!!!"

The bolt struck dead center onto the lightning rod atop the Loyalty Building!

It was a deafening, terrifying blast violent enough to rupture eardrums.

The entire reinforced-concrete school building shuddered violently in an instant; blinding electric luminescence washed over every face in the classroom, rendering them pale as parchment. Immediately afterward, an eighth-degree gale laden with frigid spray smashed forward like an enraged battering ram, violently blowing wide the two rusted aluminum transom windows along the classroom's exterior wall!

"BANG! CRASH——!"

The rusted hinges of one window snapped clean under the blast; the sash slammed savagely against the concrete frame, shattering into thousands of glittering glass shards in a microsecond!

A deluge of rain poured inward like an overturned reservoir, swept by the shrieking squall!

Test papers, handouts, and curtains on the podium were whipped airborne in an instant, plunging the entire room into a swirling, icy tempest of wind and water!

"Aaaah!"

Wen Yating let out a sharp gasp, instinctively ducking beneath the teacher's podium with her hands shielding her head.

And amid this world-shattering thunderclap and exploding glass, the sanity of Little Ma within the shoebox completely shattered.

It was the ultimate, inescapable terror of elemental apocalypse hardwired into the genetic code of wild birds.

"Whir-flut-flut-flutter——!"

A desperate, frantic flurry of wingbeats erupted!

Like an unguided missile, Little Ma forcefully pried past Lin Jingtang's trembling fingers, rocketing straight out through the narrow gap of the shoebox!

"CHEEEEEP————!!"

Carrying a soul full of panic, the tiny grey silhouette launched directly toward that ceiling above, mistaking it for the infinite sky of its dreams!

Yet this was no open sky.

This was a hard plaster ceiling.

And far more lethal—spinning frantically overhead were four iron ceiling fans running at maximum velocity, fully capable of slicing flesh and bone into ribbons in a fraction of a heartbeat!

Buffeted by chaotic indoor crosswinds, Little Ma's fledging strength faltered in midair; sucked mercilessly into the turbulent low-pressure vortex, the bird spiraled directly toward the spinning meat grinder of the central iron blades!

Lu Shuqing's pupils shrank to needlepoints.

In her mind echoed Little Ma's final, tear-choked cry of pure despair:

*"Mother... save me...!"*

In that singular microsecond, all clinical composure, all scientific rationality, and every shred of disguise and secrecy maintained over a full month vanished into thin air.

The school's most notoriously cold-blooded, calculating model class president slammed both palms onto her desk, drawing upon every drop of oxygen in her lungs to unleash an ear-splitting, vocal-cord-shredding shriek:

"THE CEILING FAN! SHUT DOWN THE CEILING FAN——!!"

That solitary shriek was like a flare piercing the abyss, detonating the suppressed souls of Class 6-1 in a blinding explosion!

"FUCK YOUR FAN TO HELL——!"

Dashenggong's legendary iron lungs, muzzled countless tens of thousands of times over the past month, were completely unsealed in this glorious second!

His deafening roar overpowered the thunderclaps outside, rattling the eardrums of the girls in the front row! The burly, dark-skinned boy transformed into a charging rhino, hurling himself forward toward the main wall switch beside the front door!

Yet the plastic rotary dial on the wall had long since seized up from decades of brittle aging!

Gritting his teeth with maniacal fury, Dashenggong twisted with raw, bleeding force—*SNAP!* The plastic dial tore clean off in his grip, exposing copper contacts that showered a blinding fountain of electric sparks!

The fan blades continued their demonic, whirling spin!

By now, Little Ma had already been dragged by the air current into the lethal zone less than ten centimeters from the blade tips!

A cold slab of iron grazed its tail feathers, shearing away a soft puff of floating down!

The violently sheared air currents sent Little Ma into an irrecoverable stall. Screeching in terror, its wings snapped inward, and the little sparrow plummeted from the three-meter ceiling like a severed sinker of lead!

Directly beneath it lay the unyielding, rock-hard terrazzo floor, bristling with dense clusters of cast-iron desk legs reinforced with sharp metallic corners!

Plummeting from such a height, those fragile avian bones would be obliterated upon impact!

"LITTLE MA——!!"

A feral, guttural roar detonated in the center of the classroom!

It was Lin Jingtang.

The boy did not hesitate for a microsecond; he performed zero calculations, giving no thought to what bones he might break upon impact.

Stomping hard onto the top of his own desk, his thigh muscles coiled and exploded like heavy steel springs, launching his entire body through the blizzard of swirling exam sheets and blowing rain, soaring midair toward Little Ma's downward trajectory!

"CATCH IT——!"

"HUMAN MATTRESS! DIVE UNDER HIM!"

In an instant, Fat Monkey, Lao Wu, A-Bao, and seven or eight boys across the back rows went completely mad!

No one gave orders; no one commanded.

These rowdy boys—who ordinarily brawled until bruised and bleeding over a single rebound on the blacktop—now moved as if puppeted by a single, shared collective nervous system, diving forward without hesitation!

Abandoning all instinct for self-preservation, they threw themselves atop one another, using bellies, chests, backs, and arms shielded only by paper notebooks to erect a living, breathing safety cushion across the merciless terrazzo floor!

"CRASH——! RUMBLE——!"

Desks were smashed askew; chairs flipped upside down, steel tubing screeching violently against concrete with ear-splitting clatter!

Pencil cases burst midair, hundreds of ballpoint pens raining down like shrapnel as inkwells shattered upon the ground, splattering pools of scarlet and dark indigo!

When Wen Yating peeked her head out from beneath the podium, what greeted her eyes was a scene of sheer, apocalyptic bedlam—

The entire class of children was screaming, weeping, and colliding in the torrential gale, desks and chairs shattered like drift timber caught in a raging tsunami!

"THUD——!"

Lin Jingtang's heavy frame crashed violently onto the very summit of the human pile formed by Fat Monkey and Lao Wu.

His right shoulder struck the upturned corner of a cast-iron desk with a sickening thud; his forehead scraped against the coarse concrete floor, instantly welling with a jagged slash of bright red blood.

The sheer agony caused his vision to black out in blinding flashes.

Yet he did not let out so much as a whimper.

In that precise microsecond before his body made impact, Lin Jingtang's hands snapped shut in midair with a gentle, supernatural swiftness that defied all human limits!

*Plop.*

A featherlight, unimaginably soft touch carrying faint, living warmth settled dead center within the cup of his palms.

Inside the classroom, rain and wind continued to pour maniacally through the shattered window.

The gale whipped sheets of water across the twenty-six bruised, bloodied, and disheveled children strewn across the ruined floor, soaking them to the bone.

Yet miraculously, the entire classroom fell into dead, reverent silence once more.

Every child strewn across the wreckage, ignoring their scrapes and bruises, struggled to raise dirt-caked, rain-soaked faces, fixing their desperate gazes upon Lin Jingtang at the top of the heap.

Lin Jingtang lay sprawled across the pile of boys, panting in ragged, shuddering gasps.

Blood trickled down his temple, dripping steadily onto the collar of his navy-blue uniform.

Trembling, he slowly raised the hands he had clutched protectively against his chest high into the air.

His palms gradually parted, revealing a narrow seam.

Perched deep within the calloused lines of his hands stood a ruffled, wind-dampened, shock-paralyzed little sparrow.

Its tiny chest heaved frantically, its glossy obsidian eyes swimming with disorientation and profound shock.

Yet its wings remained completely intact; its delicate claws gripped firmly into the meat of the boy's palm.

It was alive.

"Cheep...?"

Little Ma issued a faint, timid peep.

Staring at the creature, Lin Jingtang wiped the slurry of rain and blood from his face, and his mouth split open into a raspy, triumphant roar that out-thundered the storm:

"Haha... HAHAHAHA! I CAUGHT IT! I CAUGHT THE DAMN BIRD!"

In that singular instant, the entire class erupted into unprecedented cheers, sobs, and ecstatic laughter!

Dashenggong rolled on the floor holding his bleeding fingers while howling with laughter; Xiaoyu, weeping openly, pushed her snapped spectacles back onto her nose; Lu Shuqing leaned against an overturned desk, soaked to the skin, tears streaming down into the corners of her lips as she revealed her first radiant, unreserved smile of the entire semester.

The stifling shackles that had weighed upon every young soul for weeks on end had been pulverized into dust by this tempestuous storm and that sacrificial leap!

Yet the gears of fate never pause for human celebration.

Just as the entire class held each other in tears and wild laughter—

"BANG——!!!"

The remaining panel of Class 6-1's rear door was violently kicked open by a leather-shoed foot of pure, unbridled fury, slamming hard against the peeling wall!

Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan stood framed in the doorway, clutching a dripping black umbrella, his countenance darker than the churning thunderclouds outside.

Behind him, Teacher Wen Yating—having just staggered out from beneath the podium—clamped both hands over her mouth, staring in petrified horror at this catastrophic lunatic asylum of shattered desks, spilled blood, and driving rain.

Zhou Yan's piercing gaze swept slowly across the heap of fallen students, before locking dead onto Lin Jingtang's elevated, blood-streaked hands—holding aloft a single, trembling grey bird.

In that frozen moment, the temperature in the room plummeted colder than the storm outside.
