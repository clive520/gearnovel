# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 4: The Patrol of the Chrono-Beetles

*RUUUMMMMBLE——!*

A blinding arc of blue-white electricity detonated within the woolen fiber canyon barely a dozen meters behind them. The acrid tang of ozone, mixed with the scorched stench of ablated metal, was swept forward on a wave of warm convection.

Propelled by the final surge of Coulombic like-charge repulsion, Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, Jiang Jiang, and the mechanical Shiba Inu Pico hurtled through the air like a volley of miniature artillery shells before crashing hard onto a wide, cool expanse of polished brass!

"Owwww! My chin..." Jiang Jiang groaned, clutching his jaw as he pushed himself up. The black titanium frying pan in his grip rang with a clear, resonant hum. "That leap must've cleared at least five or six centimeters in the macro world! My heart feels like it's about to jump straight out of my throat!"

"Sixty-two millimeters, to be precise," Ye Yijie pushed her spectacles up the bridge of her nose. Though her cuffs were dusted in graphite conductor powder, her breathing had already steadied. She flipped open her black field journal, her fountain pen sketching notes at lightning speed:

"Under the combined action of Coulombic repulsion and the Faraday cage, we successfully cleared the dielectric breakdown zone of the static fiber gorge. Our current position is the intermediate mainplate bearing platform of the clock's primary reduction gear train."

Cheng Hao got to his feet, brushed the metallic debris from his aviator goggles, and looked ahead.

The sight before them snatched the breath right out of his lungs.

The brass plinth they stood upon—dubbed the "bearing platform"—was a solid half-centimeter thick (which, from their 1.5-millimeter perspective, was a towering bronze cliff over thirty meters high). And directly ahead of the platform stood a colossal, mirror-polished steel central drive arbor exceeding thirty centimeters in diameter!

Forged from high-carbon steel, quenched and precision-lapped to an impeccable mirror shine, the titanic shaft rose like a silver skyscraper so slick not even a fly could find purchase. It pierced cleanly through the lower and upper plates, extending upward into the unreachable ceiling. Orbiting the arbor, six massive purple-copper pinion gears, each a full centimeter thick, meshed at breathtakingly intricate geometric angles, turning with slow, ponderous gravity.

With every mesh and transition of the teeth, the brass ground shuddered with a steady, rhythmic low-frequency vibration:

*THUMP—CLANG! THUMP—CLANG!*

"What does the countdown say?" Cheng Hao asked in a low, focused voice.

Upon the weathered verdigris wall of the mainplate, the frosty cyan holographic alert flickered once more, updating its crimson numerals with clinical cruelty:

`[ 11 HOURS : 15 MINUTES : 08 SECONDS ]`

"We've already burned nearly forty-five minutes on the lowest floor," Ye Yijie's expression grew solemn. "If we are to scale the clock and reach the Clockwork Celestial Core before the mainspring completely discharges and triggers metric lattice collapse, we must cross this primary drive sector within the next two hours and locate the vertical ascending flue leading to the middle escapement level."

"Woof!"

Standing at the vantage point, Pico suddenly let out a sharp, guttural bark of alarm.

The little hound's paper-thin memory-alloy ears shot upright, twitching at eight cycles per second in rapid triangulation. The calm azure glow in his obsidian optical sensors instantly snapped into a menacing, high-priority crimson!

"What is it, Pico?" Jiang Jiang hoisted his frying pan, his nerves instantly on edge again.

"Quiet. Listen..." Cheng Hao held up a hand, signaling for absolute silence.

Beneath the deep, subterranean *thump-clang* of the rotating wheels, an alien, rapidly approaching cadence had emerged—

*Clack-whirrr, clack-clack! Clack-whirrr, clack-clack!*

It was no mere friction of turning wheels. It was an unmistakable mechanical march woven from hundreds of micro-articulated joints, hardened alloy claws, and high-tension mainspring click-ratchets! Precise, cold, and blindingly fast, the sound moved with the terrifying discipline of an ancient clockwork legion, surging out from the deep shadows of the copper gear train like a metallic flood!

*SKRRRRTTT—!*

With a screech of metal against metal, three colossal golden silhouettes scuttled out from the recesses between two massive gear teeth, slamming heavily down onto the brass platform directly in front of the youngsters!

"Sweet mother of gears..." Jiang Jiang's eyes bugged out of his head, his knees knocking uncontrollably. "W-What kind of monsters are those?! Giant metal beetles?!"

Looming before them were three chilling, self-propelled clockwork creations from an era long past!

In anatomy, they resembled six-legged scarab beetles, but their carapaces were assembled from cold-hammered brass plating and quenched steel brackets riveted seamlessly together. Each automaton measured a staggering **five millimeters (5 mm)** in length!

In the macro world of ordinary humans, five millimeters was merely the size of a ladybug or a grain of rice. But to Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Jiang Jiang at their current 1.5-millimeter stature, these three mechanical beasts stood three to four meters tall and over ten meters long—a trio of heavy armored main battle tanks clad in bronze plate and mounted on six scythe-like steel legs!

Flanking each beetle's faceted head were twin optical sensors crafted from polished ruby multi-prisms, gleaming with an icy crimson light. Protruding from their rostrums was a pair of two-meter-long hardened steel mandibles edged with microscopic serrations, snapping open and shut with a bone-chilling *snip-snap*—the autonomous shears designed centuries ago by master horologists to trim burrs and pulverize debris from the gear teeth!

Beneath the domed brass elytra on their backs, a miniature, high-density flat spring barrel coupled to a tapered fusee and click ratchet whirred at a shrill, high-frequency whine!

"They're not monsters..." Ye Yijie tilted her head up, the ruby lasers gleaming in her spectacles as she rapidly cross-referenced her mental archives. "These are the legendary clockwork automatons built by 18th-century master horologists to keep grand astronomical clocks operating dust-free for hundreds of years—**'Chrono-Beetles'**!"

"Cleaning automatons? Then they're just friendly maintenance bots, right?" Jiang Jiang ventured hopefully in a whisper.

Before the words had even left his lips, the two copper antennae atop the lead beetle began vibrating violently. A miniature acoustic diaphragm inside its thorax emitted a piercing, ultrasonic shriek:

*BEEEEEEP————!!*

A synthesized, mechanical translation tore through the brass chamber like grinding iron:

`[DIAGNOSTIC: UNIDENTIFIED FOREIGN OBJECT DETECTED]`  
`[TARGET: 1.5mm ORGANIC FIBER & FOREIGN CONTAMINANTS]`  
`[THREAT LEVEL: CLASS-3 SEIZURE HAZARD]`  
`[DIRECTIVE: PURGE, SHRED, INCINERATE!]`  

"Friendly my foot! Jiang Jiang, get down!"

Cheng Hao's pupils dilated. He lunged forward, shoving Jiang Jiang flat against the ground!

*SWOOOSH————!*

In that same millisecond, the lead beetle's hardened steel mandibles—heavier than the blade of a bulldozer—sheared through the empty air where their heads had been with an ear-splitting whistle!

*CLAAANNNG——!!*

The mandibles snapped shut around a protruding brass anchor rivet. Amid a violent shower of incandescent sparks, the solid brass rivet was cleanly sheared in two! The severed head ricocheted like a bullet, embedding itself deep into the chassis wall behind them!

"What in the world is that shear strength?!" Jiang Jiang broke out into a cold sweat, nearly losing his grip on the frying pan.

"Fall back! Keep your distance!"

Cheng Hao pulled both companions into a rapid retreat.

Yet the three Chrono-Beetles offered no quarter. The brass leviathans moved their six segmented steel limbs with blinding speed, striking the brass deck with a frenzied, rhythmic clatter as they fanned out in a pincer formation to cut off all escape!

Their speed was monstrous—crossing five full centimeters per second (ten times their body length). In the micro-realm, they were charging like a herd of enraged bronze rhinos barreling forward at sixty kilometers an hour!

"Woof woof!"

Pico snarled, darting out to the right like a silver blur. Extending high-frequency vibrational micro-claws from his alloy paws, the mechanical canine slashed viciously across the second leg joint of the flanking beetle!

*SKREEEEEECH—!*

Sparks erupted in a blinding spray, but the cold-forged brass armor on the beetle's thick leg bore only a faint white scratch. The violent kinetic recoil threw Pico backward several meters onto the brass deck!

"It's no use! Their carapaces are dual-layer cold-forged alloy from centuries ago! Standard kinetic strikes can't penetrate them!" Cheng Hao gripped his screwdriver pen, his brow furrowed in concentration.

"Cheng Hao! Watch their footing!" Ye Yijie called out as she retreated, her gaze locked onto the rapid cadence of the beetles' six legs as geometric trajectories flashed across her mind. "The locomotion of insects and hexapod robotics—it's an **'Alternating Tripod Gait'**!"

"Alternating tripod gait?!" Jiang Jiang hoisted the frying pan over his chest like a riot shield. "Class monitor, is this really the time for a biology lecture?!"

"This isn't biology, it's the fatal flaw of their kinematics!" Ye Yijie's words cut through the din like a scalpel:

"When a hexapod sprints, it always plants three legs on the ground—front-left, hind-left, and middle-right—forming a stable supporting triangle! Simultaneously, the opposite three legs—front-right, hind-right, and middle-left—lift off and swing forward! These two triangles alternate constantly, ensuring the machine's center of mass remains firmly within the support polygon for dynamic balance!"

"However—" Ye Yijie pointed a trembling finger at the lead beetle, "at the transitional boundary between the two tripod phases—a window of barely one-twentieth of a second—its center of mass experiences a microsecond of suspension! More crucially, to keep all six legs in lockstep, they are driven by a single central slider-crank linkage in the chassis!"

Behind his goggles, Cheng Hao's eyes lit up with electric brilliance. "A single crank linkage! That means... if we disrupt the swing phase of even one supporting leg, the entire tripod gait will desynchronize, triggering catastrophic dynamic failure and rollover!"

"Exactly!" Ye Yijie shouted. "And they don't use electric motors—pure mechanical mainsprings drive those legs! The spring energy discharges unidirectionally through a central **'Ratchet and Pawl Mechanism'**! For heat dissipation and lubrication, the pawl leaf-spring is exposed directly beneath the center of their ventral chassis!"

"Center of the ventral chassis..." Cheng Hao locked his eyes onto the charging lead beetle.

Sure enough! As the titan lifted its legs to clear a surface indentation, through the gap beneath its domed carapace, a tiny steel ratchet gear roughly one millimeter in diameter could be seen spinning furiously with a manic *click-click-click*, held firmly in place by a hair-thin purple-copper pawl spring!

"Jiang Jiang! The frying pan!!" Cheng Hao roared.

"Right here! What do I do with it? Bash it? It'll shred the pan into confetti!" Jiang Jiang raised the pan high.

"No! Don't bash it—use it as a **'Micro-Acoustic Resonance Soundboard'**!"

Cheng Hao seized the handle of the pan and slammed the flat, ultra-rigid titanium bottom flush against the surface of the brass deck!

"Jiang Jiang, throw your entire weight onto the handle! Keep it pinned! Pico, engage reverse vortex suction on the rim to lock it down!"

"On it!" Jiang Jiang gritted his teeth, throwing his body weight and every ounce of muscular strength down upon the handle. Pico engaged all four magnetic suction pads, anchoring the rim with unrelenting grip!

"Cheng Hao, what's the play?" Ye Yijie asked.

"What is the ultimate bane of clockwork mechanics? **Resonance**!"

With a flick of his wrist, Cheng Hao toggled the screwdriver pen to "High-Frequency Ultrasonic Impact Mode." The tungsten-carbide tip began vibrating with an invisible, furious blur. The young inventor locked onto the three charging Chrono-Beetles—five meters! Three meters! Two meters!

"The natural resonant frequency of an antique purple-copper pawl spring... on this micro-scale, is approximately—twenty-two thousand hertz!"

"Shatter them!!"

With every ounce of momentum in his body, Cheng Hao drove the tungsten tip down into the exact acoustic epicenter of the inverted frying pan!

*CLAAAAAAANNNNNGGGG——————!!!!*

An ear-splitting, ultrasonic metallic shriek detonated from the thin walls of the titanium pan!

The parabolic curvature of the pan acted as an acoustic lens. The 22,000 Hz shockwave was not lost into the air; instead, it channeled into the solid brass deck at 3,400 meters per second, rippling through the metallic crystal lattice like a golden tsunami of high-frequency vibrational fury!

*HUUUUUUMMMMM——!!*

The lead Chrono-Beetle had just planted its front-left and middle-right legs. In the microsecond its chassis was suspended between tripod phases, the seismic acoustic shockwave struck its underbelly like an invisible sledgehammer!

*SKRRRRRR-CLACK-SNAP!*

Lethal mechanical resonance erupted in a thousandth of a second!

The tiny purple-copper pawl spring, which held the high-tension mainspring ratchet in check, captured the ultrasonic frequency. Its amplitude multiplied exponentially within a microsecond, issuing a strained, metallic cry before—

*SNAP!*

The pawl spring was violently thrown clear of the ratchet teeth!

"Pawl slippage! Unidirectional lock failure!" Ye Yijie cried out in triumph!

Without the restraining pawl, the high-density flat mainspring inside the beetle's gut—coiled with hundreds of turns of stored mechanical potential—instantly lost all damping!

*WHIRRRRRRR-BOOOM!!*

The stored energy unleashed in a single catastrophic instant! The central ratchet spun out of control at tens of thousands of RPM!

The slider-crank linkage sheared instantly. The lead beetle's six legs flailed wildly like out-of-control windmills! Its pristine alternating tripod gait disintegrated into total chaos. Carried forward by its own immense momentum, the massive bronze titan pitched forward and rolled violently onto its flank!

*CRRRRAAAASH——!!*

The ten-meter-long brass chassis plowed a deep trench across the deck before barreling like an uncontrolled wrecking ball directly into the two trailing Chrono-Beetles!

*THUD! CLANG-SMASH!*

The three mechanical behemoths collided in a catastrophic tangle of bronze and steel!

Stripped gears, violently uncoiling mainspring coils, and shattered ruby prisms sprayed in all directions like shrapnel. The trailing beetles' legs became hopelessly jammed in the lead beetle's thrashing limbs. With a succession of deafening crunches, the tangled mass of automatons rolled over the edge of the platform and plummeted into the void below!

*Whoosh... THUD! Clatter-clang...*

Seconds later, the faint echoes of metal slamming against the bottom-most barrel housings drifted up from the abyss.

As the dust motes settled, silence reclaimed the brass platform. Only the inverted frying pan continued to hum with a faint, musical aftertone.

"Phew... gasp... whew..."

Jiang Jiang collapsed onto his rear, wiping cold sweat from his forehead with a trembling hand: "Did we... did we win? Class monitor... Cheng Hao... did we really just take down a squad of armored metal tanks?!"

"Strictly speaking, we used acoustic solid-state conduction to induce mechanical resonance, neutralizing the unidirectional self-locking ratchet and allowing their own mainspring potential to tear them apart from within," Ye Yijie adjusted her glasses, her eyes shining with genuine admiration as she looked at Cheng Hao and Jiang Jiang. "Nevertheless... the teamwork was flawless."

"Woof!" Pico wagged his metal tail excitedly, trotting over to nuzzle Jiang Jiang's trousers with his cold alloy nose.

Cheng Hao stowed the scorching screwdriver pen and walked to the outer precipice of the brass platform, peering into the chasm below.

With the Chrono-Beetle patrol cleared, the path forward opened into an awe-inspiring vista that left them trembling with wonder:

Roughly ten macro-centimeters (seventy to eighty meters in their micro-perspective) below them in the heart of the cavernous interior, an astounding horological spectacle unfurled:

Dozens of concentric ribbons of high-elasticity blued alloy steel, each mere tens of micrometers thick, coiled in an immaculate Archimedean spiral, cascading down into the shadowed depths like a bottomless canyon!

With every beat of the clock, the entire labyrinth of coiled steel breathed with slow, organic rhythm—contracting, expanding, and sending concentric ripples of ghostly cyan light shimmering across the chasm!

"That is..." Cheng Hao whispered, mesmerized.

"The primary hairspring balance system of the Armillary Astrolabe Clock!" Ye Yijie gazed into the endless spiral sea. "An abyssal canyon formed from thousands of coiled spring layers..."

Countdown: `[ 10 HOURS : 58 MINUTES : 24 SECONDS ]`!

Time had dipped below the eleven-hour mark! And the only passage forward to the second level was to leap directly into the heart of the Hairspring Abyss!
