# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 8: The Skyway of the Ruby Jewel Bearings

*FSSSHHHH————HUUUUMMM————!*

The air was dense with the heady, foreign scent of high-grade synthetic horological oil mingled with the warm convection of scorched metal.

Escorted by Elder Chronos-VI and dozens of automaton guards, Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, Jiang Jiang, and the mechanical Shiba Inu Pico arrived at the precipice of the northernmost plateau of the Brass Micro-Kingdom.

Gazing outward into the vast expanse, even having crossed the formidable Hairspring Abyss, the panorama before them stole the breath straight from their lungs.

Spanning across a chasm dozens of meters wide and soaring diagonally toward the mechanical vaulted ceiling above was a monumental, translucent "Crystalline Skyway"!

It was forged not from bronze or steel, but seamlessly assembled from dozens of circular jewel bearings each measuring five macro-centimeters across (titanic crystalline bridges dozens of meters wide to their 1.5-millimeter perspective).

The entire thoroughfare radiated with the deep, intoxicating crimson luminescence of a crushed pomegranate. It was the ultimate horological material chosen by 18th-century master horologists to shoulder the most critical balance arbors—**Synthetic Monocrystalline Corundum (Ruby, $\alpha\text{-Al}_2\text{O}_3$)**!

Across the ruby skyway lay an immaculate micro-film of synthetic horological oil (Moebius 9010) mere micrometers thick. Beneath the ambient sunbeams, the polished surface was so frictionless that not even a nanoscale dust mote could find purchase. It shimmered like a frozen ribbon of crimson aurora borealis, banking steeply upward at a terrifying seventy-degree pitch, leading straight into the shadows of the lethal "Anchor Escapement Labyrinth"!

"That is... the Great Ruby Skyway?" Jiang Jiang peeked over the unrailed edge into the bottomless gear chasm below, clutching his frying pan with white-knuckled terror as his throat seized tight. "That slope... that's three times steeper than the steepest hill behind our school! If we slide down that, won't we shatter the sound barrier?!"

"In micro-fluid physics, sound barrier concerns don't manifest like that, but this runway's coefficient of friction is indeed hugging the theoretical minimum." Ye Yijie adjusted her glasses, running a gloved fingertip across the cut edge of a corundum parapet, her eyes wide with academic reverence:

"Ruby has a Mohs hardness of 9, second only to diamond. It boasts a densely packed hexagonal close-packed (HCP) crystal lattice. After mirror-lapping with sub-micron diamond dust, its surface roughness measures less than ten nanometers!"

"More crucially, the synthetic watch oil across this corundum substrate forms an ideal **'Elastohydrodynamic Lubrication (EHL)'** film! The dynamic coefficient of friction $\mu$ drops below $0.01$! That means... any object placed upon it behaves as if gliding across absolute zero-friction ice!"

"And... we have a colossal obstacle blocking the threshold," Cheng Hao murmured in a hushed tone, pointing thirty meters ahead toward the foot of the skyway.

Squatting in the dead center of the ruby runway was a monstrous, terrifying titan!

Measuring a staggering eight millimeters long (a fifteen-meter-long super-heavy armored siege tank in their micro-realm) was an **"Overclocked Titan Chrono-Beetle"**!

Cast from deep-black quenched tungsten steel, the beast vented plumes of scalding white oil vapor from beneath its heavy elytra. Its faceted ruby ocular prisms flared with feral, manic fury. During the mainspring shockwave from Floor 1, its centrifugal governor had seized wide open, sending its massive mainspring barrel into an uninhibited, runaway discharge of raw kinetic fury!

*SNIP-SNAP! SNIP-SNAP! SKRRRRTTT—!*

From its rostrum jutted a pair of four-meter-long tungsten mandibles encrusted with microscopic industrial diamond teeth, snapping open and shut dozens of times per second with terrifying violence, each impact hurling cascades of incandescent sparks into the gloom!

"Diagnostic report..." Elder Chronos leaned upon his staff, his synthesized voice heavy with sorrow. "That is General Scarab, guardian of the skyway. Runaway mainspring torque locked its control architecture into a catastrophic recursive loop. It now perceives the ascending skyway as an absolute quarantine zone. Any living being or automaton approaching is sheared to dust instantly!"

The holographic countdown projected across the ruby wall blinked relentlessly:

`[ 07 HOURS : 12 MINUTES : 03 SECONDS ]`

"Barely seven hours remain," Cheng Hao gripped his screwdriver pen, his gaze forged of unyielding resolution. "We push through. We don't have a single second to waste!"

"Cheng Hao, the micro-kingdom's bronze halberds cannot scratch tungsten-carbide armor," Ye Yijie warned. "A frontal assault is suicide!"

"Who said anything about a frontal assault?" Cheng Hao turned toward the black frying pan in Jiang Jiang's grip, a hunter's grin breaking across his lips. "What did the Elder just tell us about the friction coefficient of this ruby runway?"

"Less than point zero one... wait!" Ye Yijie gasped, her eyes widening behind her lenses. "Cheng Hao, you mean—**'Momentum Exchange & Zero-Friction Kinetic Lure'**?!"

"Spot on!" Cheng Hao clapped Jiang Jiang's shoulder. "Jiang Jiang! Your Teflon frying pan has been a shield, a soundboard, and a trampoline... and today, it becomes an **'Ultra-Microscopic Gravity Bobsled'**!"

"A... bobsled?!" Jiang Jiang stared blankly.

"Listen up!" Cheng Hao barked out tactical orders. "That Titan Beetle may be clad in impenetrable tungsten armor, but its six limbs still obey the fundamental laws of contact mechanics! When the friction coefficient drops below point zero one, mechanical traction is completely erased! We lure it onto the steep incline, and let zero-friction physics destroy it from within!"

"Jiang Jiang, you and Pico are the bait! Can you do it?!"

Jiang Jiang looked at the snorting, steam-venting behemoth whose diamond mandibles could slice a car in half. He swallowed hard. But looking down at his trusty pan, and then at Pico—who was already digging his alloy paws into the deck, chest puffed out with tail jet hissing—the terror inside him burned away into the electric bravado of an adventure inventor!

"Hell yes, let's do it!" Jiang Jiang banged his frying pan like a war drum. "I've already shrunk a thousand times and survived lightning storms and jelly blobs—I'm not backing down from an overgrown metal cockroach! Pico, let's ride!"

"Woof!!"

Pico bolted like a streak of silver lightning, his tail micro-turbine whining at peak pitch as his magnetic claws struck sparks across the brass deck!

Jiang Jiang sprinted forward, banging the pan with his fist at the top of his lungs:

"HEY! UGLY! OVER HERE! I'VE GOT GRADE-A SYNTHETIC OIL AND PINEAPPLE BUN CRUMBS! COME AND GET IT!!"

*SKRRRREEEEEECH——ROOOAAAR!!*

Locking onto the insolent intruders, the Overclocked Titan Beetle's ruby prisms flared crimson!

With a blast of steam like an exploding boiler, its six hydraulic steel limbs hammered the floor, launching its eight-millimeter armored bulk forward like an avalanche of pure destruction!

"Here it comes!"

Jiang Jiang whipped around, sprinting toward the skyway with Pico laying down micro-vortex crosswinds to disorient the brute.

Ten meters! Five meters! Three meters!

Just as the razor-sharp diamond mandibles were about to snap shut around Jiang Jiang's waist—

"NOW! DIVE ONTO THE RUBY TRACK!!" Cheng Hao's command thundered over the comms!

Jiang Jiang threw himself forward, clutching the frying pan to his chest as he belly-flopped onto the seventy-degree slope of the Great Ruby Skyway!

*SWWWWWIIIISH————!*

The instant the Teflon bottom engaged the oil-slicked corundum mirror, pure physics took the wheel!

With virtually zero friction, gravitational acceleration seized the pan in less than a hundredth of a second. Like a black armor-piercing projectile fired from an artillery barrel, Jiang Jiang and his pan rocketed down the steep scarlet incline at staggering speed!

Carried forward by its monstrous momentum, the charging Titan Beetle was completely unable to check its stride—and slammed all six steel legs squarely onto the mirror-like ruby plane!

*SKID-CLATTER-SPIN————!!*

Catastrophe engulfed the war machine in that exact microsecond!

On rough brass plating, the beetle's massive legs could generate tons of tractive force; but across the zero-friction corundum-oil interface—its alternating tripod gait disintegrated into an involuntary ice-skating nightmare!

Its six steel claws spun wildly in place like bicycle pedals in neutral. The immense mainspring torque, unable to translate into tractive vector, generated violent, runaway yaw moments!

*CRASH-SPIN-RUMBLE——!*

The eight-millimeter tungsten behemoth spun helplessly like a manic top, its legs flailing in the air as the seventy-degree gravitational vector dragged it downward like a falling boulder!

"Pico! Deliver the coup de grâce!!" Cheng Hao and Ye Yijie, perched upon an overhead suspension bracket, shouted in unison.

"WOOF!!"

Pico inverted in mid-air, dumping full capacitor charge into his tail turbine. A concentrated lance of high-pressure supersonic air slammed into the beetle's exposed, flailing flank!

*THUUUMP————!!*

Stripped of all frictional anchor, the lateral blast nudged the spinning leviathan right off the track's unrailed edge!

With a long, screeching wail of grinding gears, the Titan Beetle tumbled off the skyway, plunging into the planetary gear collection hopper hundreds of meters below!

*CRASH... CLANG! Rattle-clatter...*

From the bottom of the abyss drifted the final, muted smash of shattered tungsten, and the manic whine of runaway torque fell silent at last.

"YEAH BABY!!" Jiang Jiang's ecstatic roar echoed over the comms. "Cheng Hao! Class monitor! ALL ABOARD!!"

Using a curved banking rise, Jiang Jiang had steered the pan through a wide U-turn, gliding back along the crest of the ascending track!

"Yijie, JUMP!"

Cheng Hao seized Ye Yijie's hand, and together they vaulted into the open concave bowl of the charging frying pan! Pico darted aboard a millisecond later, landing securely in Cheng Hao's chest!

"EVERYONE IN! GRIP THE EDGES!!"

Cheng Hao grabbed the pan's handle with both hands, jamming the flat tungsten probe of his screwdriver pen into the guide slot along the rim to act as a high-speed steering rudder!

"HOLD ON TIGHT! WE ARE ABOUT TO SET THE MICROWORLD LAND SPEED RECORD!!"

*ROOOOAAAARRRRR————BOOOOOM!!*

Carrying three youngsters and a mechanical hound, the black titanium frying pan unleashed the full, terrifying fury of gravity coupled to zero-friction corundum!

Down the seventy-degree crimson incline, the raw gravitational component $g \sin(70^\circ)$ propelled them with unbridled acceleration!

At their 1.5-millimeter scale, the pan's velocity soared to several meters per second—which, scaled up to human proportions, was equivalent to riding an **unbraked bullet train hurtling down a vertical cliff at over two hundred and fifty kilometers per hour**!

"WHOAAAAAAAHHHHHHH——!!" Jiang Jiang's shrieks were torn to shreds by the shrieking gale. "TOO FAST! TOO FAST! WE'RE GONNA FLY OFF THE PLANET!!"

The rushing micro-air formed rigid, invisible atmospheric walls that battered their faces. Ye Yijie clamped her notebook to her chest, calculating kinetic trajectories through her goggles against the shrieking wind:

"One hundred and fifty micrometers ahead... A **ninety-degree sweeping right-hand corundum bank**!!"

"Centripetal acceleration will hit $a_c = \frac{v^2}{R} \approx 45\,\text{m/s}^2$! OVER FOUR Gs OF LATERAL LOAD!!"

"If our banking angle is off by a single degree, we will be launched into the abyss and smashed to atoms!"

"WHAT IS THE TARGET BANK ANGLE?!" Cheng Hao roared, veins bulging along his forearms as he wrestled the screwdriver rudder against the shrieking air!

"$\tan \theta = \frac{v^2}{R g}$! WE MUST BANK TO EXACTLY **SIXTY-THREE POINT FIVE DEGREES**!!" Ye Yijie screamed. "EVERYONE——HARD RIGHT LEAN!!"

"RIGHT! THROW YOUR WEIGHT RIGHT!!"

Cheng Hao, Yijie, and Jiang Jiang hurled their bodies violently against the right rim of the pan! Pico anchored both front magnetic paws to the lip, dumping full battery potential into an downward-directed electromagnetic ground-effect field!

*SKRRRRRRRRREEEEEECH————!!*

At an impossible sixty-four-degree bank, the frying pan sliced into the sweeping ninety-degree crimson curve!

The Teflon bottom kicked up a shimmering rainbow of vaporized synthetic oil. The crushing 4G centripetal force pinned their lungs against their ribcages, their joints groaning under the immense strain!

Yet the mathematical equilibrium held. The pan carved through the curve like a surgeon's scalpel, shaving along the inside of the corundum barrier by mere micrometers before rocketing out of the apex in an exhilarating arc of triumph!

"WE'RE THROUGH THE BANK!!" Cheng Hao roared into the wind!

The runway flattened out into a sweeping golden plaza!

"Pico! Reverse vortex thrusters——FULL BORE!!"

"Jiang Jiang! FLARE THE PAN! ENGAGE TEFLON BRAKING!!"

*SKIDDDDDDDD————SHHHHHH!!*

The pan hydroplaned off the ruby skyway, plowing a twenty-meter trench of white vapor across the brass deck before screeching to a halt right before the pedestal of a colossal sundial!

The howling gale died away in an instant.

Only the ragged, thunderous pounding of four racing hearts echoed across the quiet plaza.

"Gasp... whew... hah..."

Jiang Jiang lay sprawled in the bottom of the pan, his limbs trembling like jelly. But looking down at his intact hands, he suddenly threw his head back and burst into wild, euphoric laughter:

"HAHAHAHA! WE LIVED!! A 250-km/h RUBY BOBSLED! WE ACTUALLY FLEW ACROSS THE SKY!!"

Ye Yijie removed her fogged glasses, drawing a long, shuddering breath of cool air, her eyes shining with triumphant brilliance. With a shaking hand, she penned a triumphant entry into her journal:

"Ruby Corundum Skyway extreme descent complete! Current location: Summit of the Middle Tier——**The Escapement Outpost**!"

Cheng Hao stepped out of the smoking pan and stowed his glowing screwdriver pen.

Looming less than thirty meters above them in the shadows was the beating, mechanical heart of the entire astronomical clock—and the deadliest arena of Volume 2:

Spanning over ten meters wide, the titanic **Anchor Escapement Wheel** turned with slow, inexorable torque. And suspended above its teeth, two colossal steel anchor pallets tipped with blood-red ruby jewels were opening and slamming shut like the jaws of a cosmic guillotine:

*TICK————!!*

*TOCK————!!*

The crimson self-destruct countdown flickered upon the tip of the gear tooth:

`[ 06 HOURS : 45 MINUTES : 18 SECONDS ]`

The final six hours! The Micro-Voyagers stood at the precipice of the most lethal mechanism in the horological cosmos—the Escapement Switch of Destiny!
