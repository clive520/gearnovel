# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 2: The One-Millimeter World! The Deadly Jelly Droplet

The frosty cyan holographic alert gradually dissipated across the massive brass pillar of the main chassis, but the crimson self-destruct countdown numbers remained seared into the youngsters' minds like a brand of molten iron—

`[ 11 HOURS : 42 MINUTES : 15 SECONDS ]`

With every second that ticked by, the gargantuan gear cosmos suspended hundreds of meters above their heads unleashed a heavy, soul-jarring shudder: *CLACK-CLANK! CLACK-CLANK!*

It was the ancient clock's primary train arbors methodically dissipating the stored torque of the mainspring. Each mechanical engagement sent subtle shockwaves rippling across the brass plinth beneath their feet, stirring up swirling galaxies of dust motes in the thick, still air.

"Cheng Hao, we must map our ascent trajectory immediately."

Ye Yijie adjusted her spectacles. Her black field notebook was already propped against the tip of a towering brass gear tooth. From their microscopic vantage point, the smooth paper surface resembled a rugged woven tapestry of interlocking plant fibers. With her metal stylus, she swiftly drafted a cross-sectional schematic:

"This Armillary Celestial Astrolabe Clock is stratified into three grand mechanical tiers from bottom to top: We are currently standing upon the lowest foundation plinth, 'Tier One: The Hairspring & Barrel Energy Reservoir'; the middle tier is 'Tier Two: Epicyclic Planetary Gearing & Anchor Escapement'; and at the pinnacle floats 'Tier Three: The Celestial Astrolabe & Meteoric Iron Core.' The physical height of the entire clockwork is merely ninety centimeters, but at our scale of 1.5 millimeters... that is equivalent to trekking up a six-hundred-meter vertical steel monolith, taller than Taipei 101!"

"Six hundred meters?!"

Jiang Jiang sucked in a sharp breath, nearly fumbling his crushed pineapple bun. Wiping cold sweat from his brow, he looked up at a titanic steel arbor rising like the Tower of Babel into the gloom: "And this entire 'mountain' is stuffed with turning blades and interlocking teeth moving every second! One misstep and we'll be ground into hamburger meat!"

"Don't panic, Jiang Jiang," Cheng Hao lowered his brass goggles, the optical reticle projecting micro-graduations directly across his field of view. "Grandpa inscribed on the base that every wheel in this clock is calculated to astronomical ratios. As long as we calculate the angular velocity and period of each gear, those turning teeth will serve as our high-speed elevators!"

"Woof!"

Pico stood on the outermost precipice of the gear tooth, his metallic ears pivoting with rapid agility before barking crisply. The mechanical pup extended a silver paw toward a shadowed canyon between the chassis and a locating dowel pin.

"Pico found a route!" Cheng Hao holstered his screwdriver pen. "Let's move! We'll cross this plinth recess and make for the arbor support of the first reduction gear!"

The four small travelers kicked into motion, running across the metallic plateau.

Yet, after covering barely twenty meters, an uncanny sensory revelation brought them all to a sudden halt.

Running in the microworld defied every everyday law of physics!

Under the dominion of the square-cube law, their body mass was reduced to one-billionth of normal. With each light stride, a gentle push against the brass floor launched them two or three meters into the air, where they floated through the dense air with the slow, languid descent of dandelion seeds!

"Bwahaha! This is unbelievable!" Jiang Jiang's anxiety evaporated into ecstatic laughter. Striking a ridiculous martial-arts crane pose mid-air, he cheered, "Look at me! I'm a kung-fu master! One hop and I'm two stories up!"

"Jiang Jiang! Stop fooling around! Land right now!" Ye Yijie shouted in alarm. "The relative viscosity of air is enormous compared to our microscopic inertia! You cannot steer in mid-air! Look directly beneath you!"

"Huh?"

Drifting through the air with outstretched arms, Jiang Jiang glanced down at Yijie's warning.

Resting in a countersunk screw recess barely five meters below him sat a crystalline, shimmering sphere of liquid stretching over three meters in diameter!

It was not pure water, but a droplet of antique synthetic watch oil blended with decades of ambient condensation. To a watchmaker, it was an invisible pinprick of fluid; but to Jiang Jiang, it looked like a towering, two-story dome of crystal-clear jelly, its shimmering surface gleaming with rainbow fringes!

*SPLOSH!*

Jiang Jiang, clutching his pineapple bun, plummeted directly onto the crown of the liquid dome!

"Oof! Wow, it's so bouncy..."

Landing on the surface, his first instinct was that he had fallen onto a giant transparent waterbed. But before a grin could form on his lips, a catastrophic physical reaction unfolded in a fraction of a millisecond!

The seemingly supple surface of the liquid was no trampoline—it was a lethal membrane of astronomical surface tension!

As Jiang Jiang sank barely half a meter into the dome, the taut liquid surface snapped inward like a living, transparent leviathan, exerting a terrifying inward suction!

*Gurgle-gasp...!*

"HELP! IT'S PULLING ME IN! IT'S EATING ME ALIVE!!"

Jiang Jiang shrieked in sheer terror. He thrashed his arms wildly to pull himself free, but every movement only drew the viscous fluid deeper along his clothing fibers. Within two seconds, the suffocating meniscus of surface tension enveloped his legs, hips, and chest!

The dreaded peril of microscopic surface tension drowning!

At the scale of a millimeter, the cohesive forces of fluid molecules relative to a body mass of a few dozen micrograms were as unyielding as heavy industrial rubber sheeting! With his tiny muscular force, breaking free of that molecular grip was physically impossible!

"Jiang Jiang!!"

Cheng Hao bolted forward, screwdriver pen drawn.

"Stop! Cheng Hao, don't touch him directly!"

Ye Yijie seized Cheng Hao's jacket sleeve, her eyes ablaze with urgent calculations:

"Think! The Laplace pressure equation dictates $\Delta P = \frac{2\gamma}{R}$! The radius of curvature of this droplet is minuscule, creating thousands of pascals of inward cohesive pressure! If you reach out with bare hands, the meniscus will instantly climb your arm and drag you in too!"

"Then what do we do? He's submerging!" Cheng Hao gritted his teeth.

Inside the titanic jelly-like dome, Jiang Jiang's face had slipped entirely beneath the liquid line! The viscous fluid sealed off his airways. Thrashing helplessly beneath the surface, his eyes bulged with panic as tiny expelled air bubbles formed around his lips—bubbles that, in this viscous micro-fluid, could not rise, remaining suspended like frozen crystal marbles!

Countdown: `[ 11 HOURS : 39 MINUTES : 02 SECONDS ]`!

Life was slipping away in heartbeats!

"What does Jiang Jiang have on him?!" Cheng Hao's mind raced.

"His frying pan! The titanium frying pan!" Ye Yijie pointed to the floor beside the droplet. "The pan fell on the outer edge! Cheng Hao, the coating on that pan—"

"**Superhydrophobic Teflon Coating!**"

A blinding spark of inspiration detonated in Cheng Hao's mind!

In the everyday world, non-stick coatings make oil and water bead up into spheres without wetting the metal; at their scale, this meant an extreme contact angle exceeding 150 degrees! The droplet literally could not adhere to that surface!

"Pico! Vortex drive—grab the pan handle!!" Cheng Hao roared.

"Woof!!"

The mechanical Shiba Inu surged into motion, his alloy paws throwing bright sparks off the brass floor. Racing to the thirty-meter-wide black pan, Pico's jaws locked onto the hanging ring of the handle with a sharp metallic snap!

In the next breath, Pico's tail-mounted micro-turbine ignited with a high-pressure jet stream, driving the broad, nanostructured black base of the pan like a colossal wedge beneath the liquid dome!

*SKRRRRRRRRT!*

A physical miracle unfolded!

The instant the Teflon nanostructure made contact with the droplet, the liquid molecules retreated. Unable to wet the superhydrophobic surface, the base of the colossal dome was forcefully lifted, prying open a narrow clearance!

"The base is unseated! But the surface tension is still crushing his chest!" Ye Yijie swiftly whipped out a packet of dry microfiber lens-wipes from her skirt pocket. "Cheng Hao! Coat the fibers in the crumbs of Jiang Jiang's pineapple bun!"

"Pineapple bun crumbs?" Cheng Hao blurted, but within a microsecond, his scientific intuition connected the dots!

The golden, crispy crust of the bread baked by Jiang Jiang's mother was packed with high concentrations of natural emulsifiers, soy lecithin, and fatty stearic acids!

In chemical physics, lecithin and fatty acids were the quintessential—**Surfactants!**

Surfactants are amphiphilic molecules, bearing a hydrophilic head and a hydrophobic tail. When introduced to a liquid-air interface, they arrange themselves rapidly, severing the hydrogen bonds between water molecules and sending the surface tension plummeting to near zero!

"Take this! High-potency lecithin surface breakdown attack!!"

Cheng Hao vaulted into the air, driving the tip of his screwdriver pen—laden with crumb-infused fibers—straight into the crown of the giant liquid dome!

*POP-FIZZZZZZZZ!!*

A peculiar snapping sound echoed through the micro-realm!

Millions of microscopic surfactant molecules rippled outward like lightning across the interface. Before their very eyes, the once-unbreakable rubbery film shuddered, pitted, and collapsed!

The surface tension coefficient $\gamma$ plunged from $72\text{ mN/m}$ to less than $10\text{ mN/m}$!

*WHOOOOSH!*

Robbed of its cohesive integrity, the giant fluid reservoir disintegrated in an avalanche of liquid!

Countless micro-droplets splattered outward across the brass plate as Jiang Jiang's round form tumbled free with a violent, gasping cough, landing squarely upon the broad black deck of his frying pan!

"Cough... hack...! Pffft! Wheeze!"

Jiang Jiang sprawled across the pan, hacking up swallowed fluid as tears and oil streamed down his flushed cheeks:

"I... I thought I was a goner... cough! That wasn't water... that was a monster's stomach trying to digest me alive!!"

"You're safe now, Jiang Jiang! You made it!" Cheng Hao bounded over, slapping his friend's back to clear his lungs.

Pico scurried up, using his warm metal tongue to lick the oily residue off Jiang Jiang's cheek, making the boy sputter between laughter and tears.

Ye Yijie exhaled a shuddering breath, wiping cold sweat from her brow as she observed the thousands of tiny, bead-like droplets rolling harmlessly across the brass ground:

"In our normal world, a droplet of water is gentle and benign; in the realm of millimeters, surface tension is an invisible vice capable of drowning giants. Five seconds more, and the Laplace pressure would have crushed your alveoli."

"Thank you... Yijie, Cheng Hao, Pico..." Jiang Jiang hugged his frying pan like a lifeline. "I swear, I will never complain about breadcrumbs again... Bread really can save lives!"

"Don't relax just yet!"

Cheng Hao stiffened, his gaze locking onto the path ahead.

Behind the collapsed fluid dome lay an ascending bronze corridor flanked by two colonnades of brass pillars carved with ancient celestial glyphs.

And at the summit of those pillars, a monumental bevel gear stood spinning with silent, inexorable majesty, waiting to carry them into the gear abyss!

Countdown projection: `[ 11 HOURS : 30 MINUTES : 00 SECONDS ]`!

The second great trial of the Micro-Voyagers was at hand!
