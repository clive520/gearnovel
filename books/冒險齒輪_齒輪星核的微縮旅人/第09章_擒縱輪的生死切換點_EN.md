# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 9: The Lethal Escapement Switch

*TICK————!!*

*TOCK————!!*

Standing before the bronze sundial at the Escapement Outpost platform, the monumental chime was no longer a distant echo. It was a localized atmospheric tempest of clashing steel hammering directly against the youngsters' eardrums and marrow!

Looming directly ahead was the beating heart of the intermediate tier of the Armillary Astrolabe Clock—the colossal **Anchor Escapement (Deadbeat Escapement)**!

In a standard wrist-watch or wall clock, the escapement was a delicate mechanism mere millimeters wide, visible only beneath a jeweler's loupe. But scaled down a thousand-fold, the apparatus rose before them like an epic, monumental execution cathedral forged of steel and blood-red corundum!

At the center stood a titanic, high-carbon steel Escape Wheel exceeding ten meters in diameter, edged with thirty backward-raked teeth resembling the jagged dorsal fins of megalodons. Powered by the mainspring delivering thousands of gram-force centimeters of torque, it strained with immense stored rotational potential.

And spanning above the wheel was an Anchor Pallet Arbor twenty meters wide and weighing tens of tons.

At the left and right extremities of the anchor were set two massive, half-meter-thick synthetic ruby jewel slabs glowing with ominous, blood-red luminescence—the **Entry Pallet** on the left and the **Exit Pallet** on the right!

Coupled to a meter-long pendulum swinging through the void behind the movement, the colossal anchor seesawed with clinical, microsecond precision:

*TICK——!* The Entry Pallet slammed downward, its angled ruby locking face engaging the tip of an escape tooth with split-second precision, hurling blinding showers of sparks into the dark!

*TOCK——!* The Exit Pallet engaged an instant later, releasing the Entry Pallet while arresting the next wheel tooth, delivering a subtle impulse nudge to maintain the pendulum's swing!

With every cycle, visible shockwaves rippled through the air, causing the entire outpost platform to heave and shutter!

"T-That is the only way up?!" Jiang Jiang gripped his frying pan, his face cast in crimson light. "Those two ruby guillotines weigh dozens of tons each! If a single tooth grazes us, we'll be atomized into metallic vapor!"

"Yet the only vertical gravity ascending flue leading to the third-tier Celestial Core lies dead center above the meshing junction of the wheel and pallets!"

Ye Yijie adjusted her wire-rimmed glasses, her metal stylus calculating trajectories across her black journal. Her expression was ice-cold in its concentration, mathematical equations and sine curves streaming through her mind:

"This astronomical clock employs a nineteenth-century Graham Deadbeat Escapement! The pendulum's effective length $L = 0.994\text{ meters}$!"

"According to the **Pendulum Period Formula**:"

$$T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{L}{g}} \approx 2.000\text{ seconds}$$

"The full period is exactly two seconds! The half-period swing that advances a single tooth takes precisely **one second (1.000 s)**!"

"One second?!" Jiang Jiang blurted out. "Then we have a whole second to sprint across?!"

"No! You couldn't be more wrong!" Ye Yijie cut in sharply:

"One second is the interval between clicks! But the **'Dead Time Gap'** where the Entry Pallet lifts and the Exit Pallet has not yet engaged—the only window where the void is clear—is a mere **eighty milliseconds (0.080 s)**!"

"Eighty milliseconds?!" Jiang Jiang nearly bit his tongue off. "A human blink takes two hundred milliseconds! In eighty milliseconds you can't even take a breath! How are we supposed to cross twenty meters of open air?!"

The holographic countdown projected across the steel beam flickered relentlessly:

`[ 06 HOURS : 21 MINUTES : 09 SECONDS ]`

Time had dropped below the six-hour-twenty-minute mark!

Deep within the plates, thermal expansion was already beginning to hum through the metal. If they could not clear the escapement, the Micro-Voyagers would be entombed in the intermediate level forever!

"Conventional running cannot clear twenty meters," Cheng Hao stepped forward, lowering his aviator goggles, his gaze locked onto the teeth of the escape wheel. "But what if we... **harness the impulse momentum of the escape wheel itself**?!"

"Harness impulse momentum?!" Ye Yijie's pupils contracted.

"Look at the tooth profile!" Cheng Hao pointed toward the quenched steel tooth:

"Deadbeat escape teeth are not square; they are ground with a precise fifteen-degree **Impulse Face**! The millisecond the pallet unlocks, the tooth surges forward at twelve meters per second to give the anchor its drive pulse—the **Impulse Phase**!"

"If we slide the frying pan into the curved clearance pocket behind the Entry Pallet five milliseconds before unlocking, the moment that tooth strikes upward, our rigid Teflon bottom will absorb the impulse! Like a billiard cue striking a cue ball, we will be launched across that twenty-meter gap during the eighty-millisecond dead window straight toward the safety line on the opposite side!"

"That's completely insane..." Ye Yijie took a sharp breath, her fingers racing across her notes. "The clearance pocket behind the Entry Pallet is less than three millimeters wide! If your timing drifts by more than **five milliseconds (0.005 s)**, the Exit Pallet will come down with forty-five tons of crushing force, squashing the pan and all of us into a micron-thin foil!"

"A five-millisecond margin of error..." Jiang Jiang's teeth chattered.

"We have the most precise computational mind in the world—our class monitor," Cheng Hao smiled at Ye Yijie, his eyes full of unshakeable faith. "Yijie, you call the drop. When you say jump, we jump!"

Ye Yijie looked into Cheng Hao's clear, unwavering eyes, then at Jiang Jiang clutching the frying pan with white knuckles despite his trembling knees, and down at Pico, whose alloy tail was humming with electric eagerness.

The girl drew a steady breath. The tension in her shoulders vanished, replaced by the razor-sharp focus of an uncompromising scientist:

"Understood! Adventure Invention Squad... TAKE YOUR POSITIONS!!"

"ALL ABOARD!"

Cheng Hao, Jiang Jiang, Yijie, and Pico piled into the hollow of the black frying pan. Pico's four magnetic claws locked flush to the Teflon surface, his tail thruster primed for attitude micro-burns.

*TICK————!!*

*TOCK————!!*

The thunderous chimes battered their ears, blast waves of air buffeting the pan.

Ye Yijie stood poised, her gaze locked onto the tooth gliding toward the locking corner. In her mind, all chaotic noise evaporated, leaving only pristine differential equations and harmonic acceleration waves:

"Pendulum swinging right... Entry Pallet engaged... torque loading..."

"Angular velocity $\omega = 0.785\,\text{rad/s}$... kinetic conversion at ninety-two percent..."

"Anchor pallet reaching peak rightward displacement... velocity zero!"

"STAND BY——ENTRY UNLOCK IMMINENT!!"

In that ten-thousandth of a second, Ye Yijie's voice cut through the hurricane like a silver blade:

"**THREE, TWO, ONE——LAUNCH!! NOW!!**"

"HEAVE HO!!"

Cheng Hao shoved forward with all his strength, while Pico's tail jet roared with a needle-thin lance of supersonic gas!

Aided by Teflon's ultra-low friction, the black frying pan shot from the platform lip like a black bullet, banking at a fifteen-degree vector straight toward the hollow pocket beneath the descending Entry Pallet!

*SWOOOSH————!*

The instant the pan cleared the rim, the entire sky was blotted out by a colossal wall of blood-red monocrystalline ruby!

Time stretched to an agonizing standstill.

They could clearly make out the sub-micron hexagonal lattice bands inside the corundum, and the microscopic burnish marks etched by centuries of wheel teeth!

Two milliseconds!

The Entry Pallet lifted!

*BOOOOOM——————!!*

Unchecked, the titanic escape wheel unleashed its bottled torque like an unchained dragon! The fifteen-degree impulse face of the tooth surged upward with a thunderous roar!

*CLAAAAAANNNNNGGGG——————!!*

The hardened tooth struck dead center against the thick titanium base of the frying pan!

The kinetic energy transfer was catastrophic!

Within the pan, the youngsters felt their world violently invert as a crushing 6G acceleration pinned their spines to the bottom!

Yet governed by Newton's Third Law and the impulse-momentum theorem ($F \Delta t = \Delta p$)—

The titanic mechanical impulse was converted into pure, parabolic kinetic velocity!

*ZHOOOOOM————!!*

The frying pan became a black hypersonic meteor, rocketing through the razor-thin dead-time aperture between the Entry and Exit Pallets at twenty meters per second!

Thirty milliseconds!

Clearing the central axis!

Fifty milliseconds!

Crossing the Exit Pallet's strike plane!

*CRRRRRUUUUNCH————BOOOOM!!!!*

Mere fractions of a centimeter behind the trailing rim of the flying pan, the forty-ton Exit Pallet slammed down with supersonic fury!

*CLANG————!!*

The ruby face arrested the next tooth with seismic force, throwing blinding sheets of sparks across the ceiling! A circular compression wave blasted outward, shaking the entire cathedral chamber!

Had their launch lagged by five mere milliseconds...

The pan and its crew would have been crushed to powdered diamond dust beneath that hammer blow!

"WE... WE MADE IT!!" Jiang Jiang screamed in mid-air, tears of joy streaming down his face as he watched the ruby guillotine shut behind them. "WE CLEARED THE BLADES!!"

Eighty milliseconds of pure survival!

The frying pan soared across a magnificent twenty-meter arc, clearing the escapement kill-zone and descending smoothly toward an elevated golden maintenance catwalk on the far side!

"Pico! Deploy drag flaps——fire magnetic grapple!" Cheng Hao shouted.

"WOOF!!"

Two shape-memory drag flaps deployed from Pico's back, while a high-strength magnetic grappling claw shot forward, locking onto the brass catwalk railing with an unyielding bite!

*SKID-WHIRRRRRR————!*

The grapple cable pulled taut, swinging the pan into a smooth decelerating spiral before touching down with a gentle *thud* upon the golden deck!

Silence returned to the platform.

Behind them, the deafening *tick-tock* receded into the distance, returning to the steady, rhythmic bass heartbeat of the intermediate tier.

"Gasp... whew... hah..."

Ye Yijie sat back against the rim, clutching her journal with trembling hands, her blouse soaked in sweat. A droplet rolled from her cheek, splashing onto the page where "80 ms" was written, blurring the fresh ink.

She looked at Cheng Hao and Jiang Jiang, a radiant, triumphant smile illuminating her face:

"Timing variance... minus one point two milliseconds. Adventure Invention Squad... all members accounted for!"

"Flawless work, class monitor!" Cheng Hao flashed a thumbs-up, grinning with the unbridled spirit of a master inventor.

Jiang Jiang hoisted the frying pan over his head, dancing across the deck: "Did you see that?! That's our squad! Even forty-ton ruby guillotines are just springboard ramps to us!!"

"Woof woof!" Pico bounced around Jiang Jiang, his metal tail spinning like a propeller.

Yet before their cheers had settled—

*WHOOOOSH————HUUUUUMMM——!!*

A furious gale of scorching, thermal air roared out from the ventilation duct ahead!

It was no gentle breeze, but a micro-convection sandstorm carrying abrasive metal grit hundreds of micrometers across, radiating at over sixty degrees Celsius!

The warning crystals along the catwalk flared an ominous amber:

`[ 05 HOURS : 48 MINUTES : 32 SECONDS ]`

Midday had arrived!

The midday sun outside the old clock tower was beating down directly upon the clock's brass casing, unleashing a violent thermodynamic thermal storm within the intermediate shafts!

The final ascent to the Celestial Core had begun!
