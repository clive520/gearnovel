# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 13: Microgravity Levitation and the Euler Angle Orbit Ring

*HUUUUUMMMMM————WHOOOOOSH————!!*

When the silvery-white column of spatial reversal light erupted from the epicenter of the Celestial Core, Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, Jiang Jiang, and the mechanical Shiba Inu Pico felt the gravitational anchor beneath their boots dissolve into nothingness.

It was no mere stomach-dropping plunge of free-fall, but an absolute physical state where the local gravitational constant across the summit of the antique clock was forcibly flattened and zeroed by the micro-dimensional field—**True Microgravity**!

"WHOAAAA! MY FEET... WHY CAN'T MY FEET TOUCH THE FLOOR?!"

Jiang Jiang clutched his black Teflon frying pan to his chest, flailing his limbs in mid-air like an overturned turtle.

Yet within the quiescent micro-atmosphere, devoid of floor traction and solid friction, every frantic paddle and kick accomplished nothing more than spinning his body in comical, futile clockwise circles in place!

"Jiang Jiang! Stop flailing! Newton's Third Law of Motion—**in the absence of external forces, the momentum of the center of mass is strictly conserved ($m \mathbf{v}_{\text{cm}} = \text{Constant}$)**!"

Ye Yijie's clear, authoritative voice called out from above.

The class monitor had adapted to zero-gravity with astonishing poise. Both hands firmly gripped the fluted rim of a purple-copper guide rail. Her pleated dark school uniform skirt billowed gently in the ambient convection, and the silver charm dangling from her glasses chain floated beside her cheek like an ethereal drop of liquid silver.

Pushing her glasses up with one finger, she surveyed the cavernous void around them, her pupils dilating as a scene of breathtaking, terrifying grandeur reflected in her lenses:

"The reversal beam hoisted us to the absolute crown of the clockwork cosmos—**[ The Summit Celestial Astrolabe: Sphere of the Armillary Rings ]**!"

Looking upward, the claustrophobic ceilings of copper gears had vanished entirely. In their place vaulted a monumental celestial dome tens of meters high, blown from a single, flawless bubble of synthetic quartz crystal!

Through the crystalline skylight, the teenagers could behold the macro world outside the clock with staggering, godlike clarity:

Across the distant attic floorboards, microscopic dust motes stretched like sweeping Sahara dunes; abandoned textbooks towered in the gloom like billion-ton continental plates; and the midday sunlight streaming through the attic dormer window was split by the quartz facet into radiant, prismatic spectral ribbons dozens of meters wide, drenching the entire hall in celestial splendor!

Yet before the youngsters could utter a sigh of wonder, a sinister, heavy thrum of cutting metal tore through the quietude of the heavens!

*WHIRRR... SWISH——FLIP! WHIRRR... SWISH——FLIP!*

Suspended at the geometric epicenter of the crystal pavilion revolved three gargantuan **Armillary Celestial Rings**, each spanning over fifteen meters in diameter and forged from heavy, dark purple copper!

The three rings spun with terrifying angular velocity within the weightless void.

Worse still, the outermost ring—representing the celestial equator—was executing a bizarre, unnatural rhythm that caused the hairs on their arms to stand on end:

It would rotate with apparent stability about its intermediate axis for exactly three and a half turns—and then, without colliding with any obstacle, the fifteen-meter, multi-ton copper ring would violently **flip one hundred and eighty degrees in a single microsecond**!

Having inverted its faces, it would proceed to spin another three and a half turns—and then flip back one hundred and eighty degrees again!

"Inversion period 1.2 seconds! Intermediate axis turnover!!" Ye Yijie gasped, her field notebook fluttering weightlessly as she snapped to the equations of rigid body dynamics:

"That is... the **'Dzhanibekov Effect'**! The **'Intermediate Axis Theorem (Tennis Racket Theorem)'** of Euler's rigid body mechanics!"

"When a rigid body possesses three unequal principal moments of inertia ($I_1 > I_2 > I_3$), rotation about the major principal axis $I_1$ and the minor principal axis $I_3$ is topologically stable; but rotation about the **intermediate axis $I_2$** is inherently unstable! The slightest infinitesimal perturbation causes angular velocity phases to diverge exponentially, forcing the object to execute periodic 180-degree self-inversions!"

"Every time it flips, the razor-sharp celestial degree graduations along its rim slice through the hall like a supersonic propeller blade!"

"And we... are floating dead center in the sweep radius of its inversion trajectory!!"

*SWIIIIISH————CRACK-FLIP!!*

Before her warning could fade, the titanic copper ring triggered another Dzhanibekov inversion!

The massive ring whipped through the weightless void, hurling an atmospheric shockwave that sent two copper pointer horns slicing through the space barely three centimeters above Jiang Jiang's curls!

"HOLY MOTHER OF GEARS——!!"

Jiang Jiang curled into a tight ball, his frying pan clanging violently against his knees:

"Has that giant copper hoop lost its mind?! Nobody touched it—why is it breakdancing by itself?! Two more flips and we're going to get turned into shaved deli ham!"

"Its intermediate balance counterweights shook loose during the mainspring reversal shockwave! The asymmetric mass distribution has sent its rotational kinetic energy into nonlinear chaotic inversion!" Cheng Hao called out, executing an agile mid-air tuck-and-roll.

Though floating free of the floor, a carbon-fiber tether around his waist anchored him to Pico, whose four magnetic paws were clamped firmly to a bronze structural pillar, acting as Cheng Hao's inertial mass anchor.

Cheng Hao aimed the laser rangefinder probe of his screwdriver pen at the center of mass of the tumbling ring. Across his visor flashed frantically oscillating phase-space trajectories:

"The **'Celestial Equatorial Calibration Slot'** that commands the final self-destruct abort is mounted directly on the inside rim of that tumbling ring!"

"If we can't stabilize its intermediate axis rotation, we can never reach the console, let alone execute the final spatial dimension alignment!"

Projected against the crystal dome, the crimson countdown blinked relentlessly:

`[ 03 HOURS : 12 MINUTES : 48 SECONDS ]`

Under three hours and thirteen minutes remaining!

"Cheng Hao! In zero gravity, how do we even reach it?!" Ye Yijie cried out, bracing herself against the rail. "We don't even have directional propulsion!"

"If we have no propulsion, we manufacture our own reaction force!"

Cheng Hao took a deep breath, barking out tactical orders with razor-sharp decisiveness:

"Jiang Jiang! Open your survival rucksack! Do you still have the scrap watch screws and heavy brass filings we gathered on Floor 1?!"

"Yes! Two whole pockets full!" Jiang Jiang unzipped his bag with trembling fingers.

"Hear my command! **Momentum Conservation Recoil Propulsion**!"

Cheng Hao shouted:

"By Newton's Third Law and momentum conservation:"

$$m_{\text{human}} \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{human}} + m_{\text{mass}} \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{mass}} = 0 \implies \mathbf{v}_{\text{human}} = -\frac{m_{\text{mass}}}{m_{\text{human}}} \cdot \mathbf{v}_{\text{mass}}$$

"Every time you throw a heavy steel screw backward with all your might, the equal and opposite reaction force will accelerate your body forward in a straight line!"

"Jiang Jiang, you are our **'Zero-G Recoil Vanguard'**! Pico, deploy cold-gas reaction control nozzles (RCS) to provide lateral attitude trimming!"

"Understood! EAT BRASS SHRAPNEL PROPULSION!!"

Jiang Jiang scooped a fistful of heavy high-carbon steel watch arbors and screws, gritted his teeth, and hurled them backward into the void with everything he had!

*SWISH! SWISH! SWISH!*

The screws rocketed backward at fifteen meters per second!

Physics answered instantly:

The flailing boy was seized by the reactive impulse, transforming into a human rocket that surged across the ten-meter gap toward the wildly flipping ring!

"Pico! Follow up! Ten-millisecond burst on the starboard thruster!" Cheng Hao commanded.

"WOOF!!"

Twin micro-pneumatic cold-gas valves on Pico's flanks hissed with razor-sharp bursts, propelling Cheng Hao and his canine companion right behind Jiang Jiang, charting an immaculate parabolic interception vector!

"Target within five meters!" Ye Yijie's stylus flew across her journal at machine-gun speed:

"Euler rigid body angular velocity solution locked!"

"Current principal moment of inertia ratios: $\frac{I_1}{I_2} = 1.35$, $\frac{I_3}{I_2} = 0.72$!"

"The inversion inflection point occurs when **the angle between angular momentum vector $\mathbf{L}$ and the intermediate axis hits the critical threshold $\theta_c = 41.8^\circ$**!"

"To eradicate the Dzhanibekov instability, we must deliver a concentrated **Perturbation Torque**:"

$$\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F} = \frac{d\mathbf{L}}{dt}$$

"Forcing the intermediate spin to migrate onto the major principal axis $I_1$! The moment $I_1$ becomes the dominant rotational axis, the flipping will vanish instantly!!"

"WHERE IS THE STRIKE POINT?!" Cheng Hao roared, the turbulence of the spinning ring whipping his hair wildly across his goggles!

"At the eccentric counterweight notch at **nine o'clock along the outer rim**! We need at least five hundred micronewtons of tangential impulse!!"

"Five hundred micronewtons... the mass of the frying pan plus Jiang Jiang's kinetic momentum is an exact match!"

A burst of brilliant tactical clarity ignited within Cheng Hao's mind. He shouted toward his charging friend:

"Jiang Jiang! Don't dodge! RAM IT HEAD-ON!"

"Drive the Teflon rim of your frying pan squarely into the nine o'clock notch! Throw your entire body weight to yank it down and to the right!!"

Staring down the multi-ton wall of spinning copper whistling toward him like the blade of a cosmic guillotine, the adrenaline in Jiang Jiang's blood surged like molten steel!

"YAAAAAAHHHHHH——!!"

The boy let out a defiant, earth-shaking roar. Weightless in the void, he squared the black frying pan before his chest with white-knuckled grip, throwing his entire mass and soul into the spinning yellow notch flashing before his eyes!

"STOP——**YOU CRAZY HOOP!!**"

*CLAAAAAAANNNNNGGGGGG————!!*

A metallic detonation that shook the quartz dome to its foundations echoed through the hall!

The ultra-rigid Teflon rim of the frying pan slammed into the eccentric counterweight notch with microscopic perfection!

Five hundred micronewtons of tangential impulse slammed squarely into the intermediate axis moment arm!

"Pico! TETHER FULL LOCK! PROVIDE REVERSE DAMPING LEVERAGE!!" Cheng Hao fired his screwdriver pen's carbon grapnel, hooking the frying pan handle and throwing his weight backward in unison with Pico!

*SKRRRRRR-HUUUUUMMMMM————BOOM!!*

The crowning triumph of classical dynamics unfolded beneath the crystalline dome!

The multi-ton copper ring, in the very act of twisting into another 180-degree flip, had its angular momentum vector $\mathbf{L}$ forcibly knocked away from the unstable intermediate axis!

With a deep, groaning shudder, the ring trembled violently, and then—

Its spin axis smoothly, gracefully slipped into the stable orbit of the major principal moment of inertia $I_1$!

*Huuuummmmmm...*

The chaotic Dzhanibekov flips vanished without a trace!

The fifteen-meter equatorial ring ceased its violent acrobatics, settling into a serene, majestic, and rock-steady rotation beneath the starry heavens!

"INVERSION DESTROYED!! EULER ANGLES STABILIZED!!" Ye Yijie's tearful cheer rang across the comms!

"Gasp... whew... hah..."

Jiang Jiang clung to the rim of the copper ring with both arms, his smoking frying pan still wedged in the slot.

Looking down at the stable equatorial track beneath his boots, he wiped the grease and sweat from his grinning face, looking back at the floating Cheng Hao and Pico with breathless triumph:

"Cheng Hao... I did it! I actually smacked this giant spinning hoola-hoop into submission with a frying pan!"

"Brilliant job, Jiang Jiang! You're officially the world's first zero-gravity frying-pan astronaut!" Cheng Hao floated over, exchanging a resounding mid-air high five!

"Everyone, look at the center of the ring!" Ye Yijie glided gracefully down the tether.

At the epicenter of the stabilized armillary sphere, an ancient bronze console engraved with base-60 celestial coordinates rose smoothly from the arbor.

Atop the console, the **Celestial Reversal Master Electrode** pulsed with a serene, emerald-green radiance, awaiting final spatial alignment!

Through the quartz dome above, the real-world sunlight bathed the four adventurers in golden light, casting long, heroic silhouettes across the celestial sphere.

The countdown clock ticked quietly overhead:

`[ 03 HOURS : 00 MINUTES : 00 SECONDS ]`

Exactly three hours remaining!

The first obstacle of Volume 3: The Dawn of the Clockwork Celestial Core had been conquered! With keen intellect and boundless courage, the micro-voyagers were charging full speed toward the dawn of their return to the macro world!

* **The Scientific Mystery**: In a weightless celestial hall, why did an untouched, freely rotating giant copper ring flip 180 degrees every few seconds? How did Cheng Hao's team use momentum conservation and Euler's equations to tame the chaotic flip?
* **Core Physical Principles**:
  1. **The Dzhanibekov Effect & The Intermediate Axis Theorem (賈尼別科夫效應與中間軸定理)**:
     * Any rigid body has three orthogonal principal axes of inertia, with principal moments $I_1 > I_2 > I_3$.
     * According to **Euler's Equations of Rigid Body Motion**, rotation about the major axis $I_1$ and minor axis $I_3$ is Lyapunov stable; however, rotation about the **intermediate axis $I_2$** is topologically unstable (a saddle point in phase space). Microscopic perturbations grow exponentially, causing the angular velocity vector to follow a separatrix trajectory, resulting in periodic, spontaneous 180-degree inversions.
  2. **Recoil Propulsion & Conservation of Linear Momentum in Zero-G (微重力動量守恆推進)**:
     * In the absence of external forces and friction, the total momentum of an isolated system is conserved: $m_{\text{human}} \mathbf{v}_{\text{human}} + m_{\text{mass}} \mathbf{v}_{\text{mass}} = 0$.
     * By hurling heavy screws backward, the reaction momentum propels the astronaut forward—the foundational physics behind rocket propulsion and astronaut Extravehicular Activity (EVA) maneuvering units.
  3. **Angular Momentum Perturbation & Axis Migration (角動量微擾力矩)**:
     * Modifying a rigid body's rotational state requires an external torque: $\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{r} \times \mathbf{F} = \frac{d\mathbf{L}}{dt}$.
     * By applying a tangential impulse with the frying pan, the team shifted the spin axis away from the unstable intermediate axis $I_2$ to the unconditionally stable major axis $I_1$, restoring rotational equilibrium.
