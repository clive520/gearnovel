# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 3: Dust Like Boulders, Wool Summoning Lightning

The countdown projection blazed with urgent crimson illumination across the bronze column:

`[ 11 HOURS : 18 MINUTES : 40 SECONDS ]`

Having narrowly escaped suffocating inside the "jelly droplet," Jiang Jiang securely strapped the heroic black titanium frying pan onto his back. Clutching his knees, he tread forward with agonizing caution, his eyes glued to the floor.

"Cheng Hao, Yijie... where exactly are we heading now?"

Jiang Jiang panted as he gazed upward.

The four companions were ascending an inclined bronze chute situated on the clock chassis. Yet the microscopic landscape that confronted them stretched the boy's nerves to their absolute breaking point once again.

In the macro world, this was merely a few specks of airborne dust and carpet lint swept through the clock casing by a stray attic draft; but to travelers standing 1.5 millimeters tall—

Flanking the chute on both sides rose a horizon-spanning "forest of colossal megaliths"!

These were no mere particles of dust; they were titanic boulders three to four meters across, their rough, crystalline facets resembling shards of extraterrestrial meteorites! Among them were razor-sharp quartz silicate crystals, layered precipices of desiccated epidermal flakes, and several fallen, semi-translucent tree trunks half a meter thick—covered in intricate, overlapping scales. Those were stray wool fibers that had shed from Jiang Jiang's very own sweater!

"T-This is what we inhale every day?!" Jiang Jiang swallowed hard, his eyes bulging. "If even one of those boulders rolls down, we'll be flattened into pancakes!"

"Keep your cool, Jiang Jiang," Ye Yijie pushed her spectacles up her nose, the metallic tip of her stylus scanning the ambient air. "At our scale, van der Waals forces far overpower the gravitational mass of these micro-particles. The dust is literally glued to the brass and fibers by intermolecular cohesion. Unless we strike them with violent force, they will not budge."

"The real danger isn't the boulder dust; it's the air," Cheng Hao murmured, abruptly halting.

His nostrils flared as a sharp, sweet, metallic burnt aroma stung their senses—

"That's... the smell of ozone ($O_3$)!" Ye Yijie's face paled.

Before her sentence could finish—

*CRACK-BOOOOOOM!!*

Less than thirty meters ahead, deep within the tangled forest of wool fibers, an incandescent flash of pure white light detonated!

With a deafening thunderclap that rivaled an artillery barrage, a violent electric arc as thick as an arm, crackling with deep-purple plasma ribbons, discharged violently from the surface of a suspended wool fiber and slammed directly into the brass floor below!

The bronze plinth erupted with blinding azure-white sparks. The intense thermal expansion ripped through the surrounding air, kicking up a scorching shockwave laden with ionized air that whipped furiously against their clothes and goggles!

"Woof! Woof woof!"

Pico's silver alloy origami shielding flared open defensively. His metal ears twitched at alarm frequencies, his obsidian eyes reflecting the cascading web of deadly high-voltage arcs!

"Lightning?! How on earth is there lightning inside a clock?!" Jiang Jiang crashed to his knees, burying his head beneath his frying pan.

"This isn't atmospheric lightning—it's **microscale Electrostatic Discharge (ESD)**!"

Bracing herself against the ionized gale, Ye Yijie's eyes narrowed behind her spectacles as her fountain pen rapidly scratched calculations onto her field notebook:

"The attic air is bone-dry. The subtle mechanical vibrations generated by the mainspring barrel have caused these desiccated wool fibers to rub aggressively against the brass plates—generating catastrophic **triboelectric charging**!

In our normal world, static from a sweater carries a potential of a few thousand to ten thousand volts ($3\sim 10\text{ kV}$), which produces only a harmless pinprick spark. But remember: at our microscopic scale, the physical gap between the wool fibers and the chassis walls is merely fifty micrometers!

By the electric field intensity formula, $E = \frac{V}{d}$—

Ten thousand volts divided by fifty micrometers yields an electric field exceeding **two hundred million volts per meter ($2\times 10^8\text{ V/m}$)**!

That obliterates the dielectric breakdown threshold of dry air ($3\times 10^6\text{ V/m}$) by orders of magnitude! This entire chasm of wool fibers has become an unrelenting abyss of atmospheric dielectric ionization!"

"Two hundred million volts per meter?!" Jiang Jiang's jaw dropped. "You mean if we walk through there, we'll be zapped into charcoal sausages?!"

"Worse: the only bronze corridor leading to the primary reduction bevel gear lies squarely through the center of this lightning canyon!"

Cheng Hao lowered his brass aviator goggles, engaging the polarized electric field filter.

Through the deep-purple lenses, the scene ahead was spine-chilling: along the forty-meter bronze walkway, dozens of suspended wool fibers crackled like high-voltage transmission pylons. Dust boulders suspended in the air were caught in violent **dielectrophoresis** within the inhomogeneous electric field, hurtling across the gap at dozens of meters per second like kinetic cannonballs!

Countdown projection: `[ 11 HOURS : 12 MINUTES : 10 SECONDS ]`!

"We don't have time to find another way around," Cheng Hao gritted his teeth. "The fractures in the mainspring are accelerating. We have to breach this canyon head-on!"

"Are you insane?! We're covered in metal!" Jiang Jiang wailed, waving his screwdriver and frying pan. "Walking conductors into a high-voltage field? We'd be walking lightning rods!"

"No, Jiang Jiang. It's the exact opposite," Ye Yijie smiled with serene confidence, her lenses mirroring the jagged lightning ahead.

"While conductors attract electric field lines, if you shape that conductor into an **enclosed metallic cavity**..."

"**A Faraday Cage!!**" Cheng Hao and Yijie shouted in unison!

"A Faraday... what-now cage?" Jiang Jiang blinked.

"Electrostatic shielding!" Yijie explained rapidly. "In the nineteenth century, the physicist Michael Faraday discovered that when an enclosed metallic shell is placed within an external electric field, free electrons within the metal redistribute across the outer surface in microseconds. The induced internal charge creates an equal and opposite electric field that **completely cancels out** the external field!

No matter how titanic the lightning or how colossal the external potential, within the interior of an enclosed metal cavity, the electric field is strictly zero!

In other words—if we shelter inside an enclosed conductive shell, a million volts can hammer the exterior and we won't feel a single tickle!"

"An enclosed metallic shell..." Jiang Jiang looked down, his gaze slowly locking onto the black titanium frying pan strapped to his back. "You mean... my pan?!"

"Exactly! Your pan is going to save our lives again!" Cheng Hao cheered, knocking his fist against the pan's heavy alloy deck. "This pan is cast from seamless, high-conductivity titanium-aluminum alloy. Thirty meters in diameter and five meters deep, it's a magnificent, prefabricated Faraday defense bunker!"

"But a pan alone isn't enough!" Cheng Hao noted urgently. "When inverted against the floor, the rim must achieve flawless **superconductive grounding**! Otherwise, the accumulation of strike charges will raise the hull's floating potential until it arcs through the gap into our compartment!"

"Leave the ground plane to me!"

Cheng Hao unscrewed the base of his screwdriver pen, shaking out three sticks of 2B graphite drawing lead.

Crushing the high-purity graphite into an ultra-fine conductive powder, he lined the entire perimeter rim of the pan: "Graphite is an exceptional carbon conductor. It fills every microscopic air gap along the contact interface, guaranteeing zero-resistance dissipation into the chassis ground!"

"Pico! Lock magnetic clamps to the rim ribs!!"

"Woof!"

Pico's four paws engaged high-powered neodymium magnetic clamps, locking onto the interior stiffeners of the pan, securing the inverted mega-shield against the bronze floor with ironclad stability.

"Everyone inside! Inverted turtle advance!" Cheng Hao commanded.

Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, and Jiang Jiang huddled tightly beneath the five-meter-deep dome.

*CLANG!!*

The massive frying pan inverted downward, plunging them into pitch-black obscurity.

In the next heartbeat, the full fury of the lightning canyon struck!

*ZAP-CRACKLE-BOOOOOOM!!*

Millions of volts of static arcs slammed mercilessly against the frying pan's hull like a meteor storm!

Each strike reverberated like the ringing of a titanic cathedral bell, sheets of brilliant violet sparks cascading down the graphite-coated rim into the bronze bedrock. Yet, exactly as Faraday had proven two centuries prior—

Within the interior of the pan, the air was tranquil and serene!

Not a single spark jumped. Not a hair on their arms stood on end!

"Good grief... it worked!" Jiang Jiang stroked his un-singed hair, laughing through relieved tears. "Long live Faraday! Long live physics!"

"Don't celebrate yet—we're still in the middle of the canyon!" Cheng Hao observed through a tiny viewing aperture. "Pico! Turbine thruster, full power forward!"

*WHIRRRRR-WOOF!*

Pico's tail turbine flared into action, driving the black titanium fortress gliding smoothly across the graphite-lubricated floor, plowing through the electric tempest!

Yet, as the pan reached the halfway point of the traverse—

*SNAP!!*

The weathered bronze chute gave way, revealing a five-meter-wide chasm plunging into the shadowed gear abyss below!

With their heavy forward momentum, stopping was impossible. The pan hurtled directly toward the precipice!

"A CLIFF!! WE'RE FALLING!!" Jiang Jiang screamed in panic.

"Cheng Hao! Use the electrostatic repulsion!!"

Ye Yijie shouted into the dark: "The massive wool fiber overhead carries a colossal **negative static charge**! If we induce a matching **negative polarity** across our outer hull, the Coulomb repulsion will launch us upward!!"

"On it!!"

Cheng Hao jammed the screwdriver pen—switched to high-frequency piezoelectric discharge mode—straight into the pan's central terminal lug!

"Coulomb Repulsion—Electromagnetic Ejection, FIRE!!"

*THUUUUMMMMM!*

The millisecond the pan sailed over the cliff edge, its outer hull flashed with dense negative surface charge!

The towering wool fiber overhead unleashed a titanic opposing electrostatic field!

By Coulomb's Law, $F = k \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$—

Like charges repel! A colossal electrostatic repulsion wave shoved upward against the pan, catching the falling vessel mid-air like an invisible palm and hurling it across the chasm like a ballistic missile!

*WHOOOOOOSH!!*

Tracing a majestic parabolic trajectory through the void, the black pan sailed cleanly across the ten-meter abyss, slamming down onto the high-altitude gear landing on the opposite side with a thunderous *THUD*!

As the dust cleared, Jiang Jiang tossed the pan aside, and the four travelers tumbled out, panting frantically for breath.

Behind them, the raging lightning canyon crackled impotently across the void.

"W-We made it!" Jiang Jiang sprawled on the brass deck, laughing hysterically. "An electrostatic cannon! We launched ourselves across a cliff with static electricity!"

"That is the breathtaking beauty of extreme micro-physics," Ye Yijie smiled, wiping sweat from her spectacles.

Cheng Hao rose, his gaze locking onto the platform ahead.

They stood at the threshold of the clock's middle tier. Looming before them was the first reduction bevel gear, a hundred meters tall, rotating with golden splendor!

Countdown: `[ 11 HOURS : 00 MINUTES : 00 SECONDS ]`!

One full hour expended. The perils of the foundation plinth were conquered!

Yet, before they could catch their breath—

*Scritch... skitter-clack...*

A chilled, rhythmic clicking sound echoed from the shadowed recesses of the bevel gear teeth.

Dozens of pairs of glowing crimson optical sensors ignited in the dark!

A swarm of five-millimeter-long clockwork automatons, armored in heavy brass plates and scuttling on six segmented clockwork limbs, emerged from the shadows, their mechanical pincers snapping rhythmically:

*CLACK... SNAP...*

The autonomous guardians of the ancient clock—**The Horological Beetles had arrived!**
