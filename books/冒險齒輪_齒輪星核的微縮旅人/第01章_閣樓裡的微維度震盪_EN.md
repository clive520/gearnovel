# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 1: Micro-Dimensional Shock in the Attic

On an early autumn afternoon, warm golden sunlight filtered through the circular frosted rose window on the top floor of Luyang Elementary School's old clock tower, casting mottled shadows across the thick dust of the teak floor.

The air was permeated with the sweet, aged scent of ancient cedar chests, machine oil, and damp paper. As a gentle breeze drifted through, delicate cobwebs suspended among the high crossbeams swayed lightly in the sunbeams, like the dormant silk strings of an ancient instrument.

"Achoo—!"

A resounding sneeze shattered the decades-long silence of the attic. Jiang Jiang, sporting his neat crew cut, rubbed his reddened nose and clutched his signature black titanium frying pan tightly to his chest. He cast a wary glance around at the towering heaps of wooden crates and antique apparatus.

"Hey, Cheng Hao... even if the old principal gave us permission to use this attic as the Adventure Invention Squad's secret workshop on weekends, did you really have to pick a place that would spook a ghost? Other than dust and cobwebs, even a mouse would lose its way in here!"

"Keep it down, Jiang Jiang. Don't go breathing in all that dust."

Standing by the window, Ye Yijie nudged her thin, wire-rimmed glasses up the bridge of her nose. The ace class monitor of Luyang Elementary was dressed in an immaculate off-white long-sleeved blouse and a navy pleated skirt. Her thick, black leather field journal was already propped open, her fountain pen rapidly cataloging the attic's ambient temperature, relative humidity, and barometric pressure.

"This clock tower was constructed in 1926, featuring double-layered solid red brick and acoustic lead insulation plates, with foundations rooted directly into the coastal sea-eroded bedrock. Historical records note that this attic was once a cradle of inspiration for master mechanical engineers across the town. There is certainly a reason Cheng Hao brought us here."

"Yijie always understands me best."

From behind a towering bookshelf in the deepest corner of the attic, the lively voice of the young inventor Cheng Hao rang out.

Cheng Hao was kneeling beside a hefty, two-meter-long nanmu chest bound in blackened iron. Atop his forehead rested his signature brass aviator goggles, their deep-purple optical lenses shimmering with iridescent oily rainbow hues in the dim light. With practiced dexterity, he drew his custom multi-functional screwdriver pen from his breast pocket. He pressed a miniature spring-loaded release catch on its side—*click!*—and a hardened alloy cross-head bit snapped into place.

"Woof! Woof woof!"

A small silver-white pup, barely thirty centimeters long, bounded excitedly around the wooden chest. This was no creature of flesh and blood, but a mechanical Shiba Inu meticulously folded and assembled from ultra-light shape-memory alloy sheets—Pico!

Pico's twin metal ears twitched with the agility of miniature radar dishes at three cycles per second. His obsidian optical sensors glowed with an inquisitive, lively pale-blue luminescence, while his curled tail, linked by delicate micro-chains, whipped back and forth with a soft metallic chime as he sniffed the seam of the chest's tarnished brass lock.

"You smell it too, don't you, Pico?" Cheng Hao grinned with quiet confidence. He aimed the screwdriver bit at the verdigris-encrusted nine-ring cipher dial on the padlock. "This is the final heirloom chest my grandfather stored in this clock tower before he set sail for the Thousand Gear Sea. The passcode is his favorite golden ratio constant—1, 6, 1, 8!"

*Click, clack, ping—!*

As Cheng Hao's fingers spun the brass dial, nine precision detents engaged in a rhythmic, melodic sequence. With a low groan from its oxidized hinges, the heavy lid was slowly lifted open.

A subtle, clean scent of chilled metal accompanied by a gentle wave of thermal convection wafted up from the depths of the trunk.

Jiang Jiang leaned over inquisitively, almost dropping his heavy frying pan right onto his toes: "Whoa... w-what kind of gigantic antique is this? A mega-clock? Or some ancient bronze fortune-telling compass?"

Resting in the center of a thick, shock-absorbing velvet cushion was a magnificent, pure-brass mechanical wonder standing nearly a meter tall, its complexity so staggering it took their breath away.

The machine resembled a miniature mechanical galaxy. Its outermost layer consisted of three concentric celestial brass rings forming an "Armillary Astrolabe," intricately inscribed with zodiac signs and solar terms in microscopic Latin engravings. Beneath them, hundreds of paper-thin purple copper gears of varying tooth counts meshed together with flawless precision, cascading into the shadowed depths of the chassis.

And at the very heart of the hollowed base floated a faceted spherical core, roughly the size of a fist, forged from celestial meteoric iron!

"This is... an 'Armillary Celestial Astrolabe Clock'!"

Ye Yijie's eyes widened. She bent forward, carefully inspecting the hair-thin micro-graduations etched into the gear teeth. "This is far beyond any conventional mechanical clock! Look—its drive arbor doesn't use a regular barrel; it employs twin-helical hairsprings coupled to an epicyclic planetary gear train! Wait, there is an inscription engraved on the base plate!"

Cheng Hao lowered his brass goggles and engaged the ultraviolet polarizing filter, reading the weathered script aloud in a hushed tone:

"'To the Stargazing Voyagers of Tomorrow: Time is never a river coursing along a single straight line, but an origami of dimensions folded by countless interlocking gears. When the gate of the microworld is unsealed, a single millimeter of dust is itself a vast celestial core. — Cheng Yuanshan, Autumn 1996.'"

"Grandpa's handwriting..." Cheng Hao's heart thumped against his ribs.

He ran his fingertips across the cool, polished brass casing. The astronomical clock stood utterly dormant, every gear frozen in place, save for the meteoric polyhedron at its core, which pulsed with an ethereal, faint azure glimmer beneath the sunbeams.

"Strange. If this is a clock, where are the hands? Why isn't there any ticking?" Jiang Jiang scratched his head. "Did the mainspring snap inside?"

"No. Its energetic topology forms a closed loop," Cheng Hao murmured, fixating on a tiny notch where the celestial rings intersected. "Look here. Wedged between the Ptolemaic track and the Copernican orbit is a microscopic golden shear pin. It's waiting for a precise torque calibration."

Cheng Hao took a deep breath. As the grandson of the legendary clockmaker and former principal Cheng Yuanshan, every instinct in his blood buzzed with electric excitement. Gripping his screwdriver pen, he dialed the tip to a No. 0 flat probe and gently guided it toward the half-millimeter aperture.

"Cheng Hao, wait!" Ye Yijie suddenly warned, her eyes darting to the digital barometer in her notebook. The readings were fluctuating violently. "The magnetic field is spiking exponentially! The ambient electrostatic potential—"

The warning came half a second too late.

The moment the probe tip grazed the golden shear pin—

*HUUUUMMMMMMM——!!*

A sub-audible yet soul-shaking microwave drone erupted within the enclosed attic!

In the next microsecond, as if thunderous life had been breathed into hundreds of sleeping brass wheels, thousands of microscopic gears burst into a manic, deafening chorus of crystalline clattering!

"Good grief! It's spinning!" Jiang Jiang yelled.

The three outer celestial rings began counter-rotating at speeds far beyond the eye's ability to track. The meteoric polyhedron at the center blazed into a blinding halo of cyan-gold radiance!

Instead of scattering outward, the ring of light twisted and warped in mid-air, forming a spatial sphere roughly one meter in radius whose outer boundary rippled with bizarre geometric distortion waves!

*Warning! Metric compression field activated!*

In the lower-right corner of Cheng Hao's goggles, crimson system alerts cascaded across his field of view:

`[CRITICAL: METRIC COMPRESSION FIELD TRIGGERED]`  
`[SPATIAL COMPRESSION COEFFICIENT: 1 : 1,000]`  
`[COORDINATE ANCHOR: CLOCK CHASSIS PLINTH A-01]`  

"Get back!" Cheng Hao shouted, lunging forward to pull Yijie and Jiang Jiang away.

Yet an irresistible, colossal gravitational tide erupted from the epicenter of the cyan-gold sphere!

It was not a brute force tearing at flesh and bone, but an uncanny microscopic centripetal pull acting directly upon every molecule, every atomic nucleus in their bodies!

"Pico! Catch on!" Jiang Jiang wildly swung his frying pan, trying to scoop up the mechanical Shiba Inu. Pico's magnetic paws locked onto the wooden floorboards with all their might, but against the furious spatial vortex, the little mechanical hound was hoisted into the air like a dry leaf!

"WHOAAAAAAA——!"

The three youngsters and the mechanical canine were swallowed whole by the cyan-gold distortion field within a single millisecond!

---

What followed over the next few seconds was a staggering miracle that dismantled every sensory rule known to human perception.

Cheng Hao felt no crushing pain or elongation. On the contrary, he felt his five senses being forcefully re-scaled into an entirely alien realm of dimensional magnitude!

Before his eyes, the familiar wooden attic began to expand in an impossible, dizzying cascade:

The weathered wooden skylight above stretched outward within half a second into an infinite celestial canopy;

The invisible dust motes suspended in the air ballooned into colossal, boulder-like meteoric rocks several meters across, tumbling through space with rough, rocky facets;

The smooth teak flooring beneath them cracked and dilated into an abyssal landscape of towering mountain ranges and shadowed chasms, each grain of wood fiber rising like an ancient canyon wall!

Even the sound of their voices shifted. Jiang Jiang's lingering shriek was drawn out and pitch-shifted downward into a sluggish, tectonic rumble like the grinding of planetary plates:

"W-H-O-O-O-O-A-A-A-A-A-A——R-U-M-B-L-E——"

*CRASH!!*

With an impact akin to a comet making landfall, Cheng Hao slammed down onto a cool, rigid sheet of golden metal, its surface etched with faint concentric machining grooves.

As the dust settled, a stifling silence descended over the landscape.

"Cough... cough!"

Cheng Hao pushed himself up onto his palms. Shaking off a wave of violent dizziness, he checked his head—thankfully, his brass goggles were still securely strapped to his brow, and his screwdriver pen remained clenched in his fist.

"Yijie? Jiang Jiang? Pico? Where are you guys?" Cheng Hao called out anxiously.

His voice resonated with a bizarre, high-frequency metallic ring, sounding as thin and rapid as the chirping of a hummingbird.

"Cheng Hao... I'm over here..."

From a sunken indentation in the metal plate ten meters away came Ye Yijie's trembling reply.

Cheng Hao scrambled over. Yijie was adjusting her spectacles as she got to her feet. Her black notebook lay open beside her, and though her skirt was dusted in fine particles, her eyes shone with steadfast composure.

"Are you hurt?" Cheng Hao assisted her.

"No skeletal or muscular injuries... my vitals are normal," Yijie took a steadying breath. But when she lifted her gaze to take in their surroundings, she froze completely. "Cheng Hao... look around us. Where in the world are we?"

Cheng Hao turned and followed her line of sight.

In that single instant, the boy felt the blood freeze in his veins!

What they stood upon was no metallic plateau, but a colossal brass gear dozens of meters in diameter, flanked at its outer rim by towering gear teeth that rose like golden fortress walls!

And suspended in the vast void hundreds of meters above their heads, dozens of titanic copper wheels, bronze connecting rods, and polished steel arbors stretched across the heavens like towering sky-scrapers, casting monumental shadows across the landscape!

The very watch components that had been nearly invisible to the naked eye minutes ago had become a breathtaking, horizon-spanning mega-engineering universe!

"Owww! My back... where's my pineapple bun?!"

Nearby, beside a fissure in the brass plate, a massive black structure shifted.

It was Jiang Jiang's frying pan! Except the ordinary 28-centimeter pan now stood upright like a colossal black Roman colosseum, stretching nearly thirty meters across!

Jiang Jiang scrambled out from beneath the handle, still clutching half a crushed pineapple bun in his grease-stained hand.

"Woof woof!"

With a cheerful patter of metallic paws, a silver-white blur sprinted along the rim of the gear and leaped right into Cheng Hao's arms—Pico!

The little mechanical companion was completely unharmed, though his entire body now measured barely two millimeters in length. His alloy claws tapped against the smooth brass ground with the brisk clatter of rolling marbles.

"T-This can't be real, can it?" Jiang Jiang stumbled over, pointing a quivering finger at an arbor overhead that resembled a towering pillar holding up the sky. "How did we... how did we get smaller than ants?!"

"We didn't shrink in the ordinary sense," Ye Yijie murmured, pointing her trembling pen at a massive line of reversed chemical engravings on the brass wall ahead. "It was the metric compression field inside that Armillary Celestial Clock... it compressed our physical coordinates by a factor of one thousand and deposited us on the lowest plinth of the chassis!"

"One thousand times?!" Jiang Jiang gasped. "Then how tall are we right now?!"

"At a standard scale ratio," Yijie pushed her glasses up, her voice tinged with the profound awe of a scientist encountering the impossible, "our original height of roughly 1.5 meters has been scaled down to... approximately **1.5 millimeters**! We are no larger than a grain of sesame!"

"1.5 millimeters..." Cheng Hao whispered. He looked down at the screwdriver pen in his hand—the ten-centimeter tool lay upon the brass ground like a high-speed railway carriage stretching fifteen meters long!

Before they could even begin to process the sheer scale of their new reality—

*CLAAANNNNNG——————!!!!*

A monumental chime of unfathomable power detonated from the highest summit of this brass mechanical cosmos!

It was no longer a delicate, crystalline *tick-tock*, but a seismic bass resonance that sent visible compression waves through the thick air! The entire clockwork labyrinth, hundreds of meters in scope, shuddered violently, sending reverberating metallic echoes roaring through the chasm!

Then, upon the monumental brass pillar before them, an ancient holographic display of frosty cyan light ignited beside a flashing crimson alert:

```
======================================================
[CRITICAL ALERT: CELESTIAL CORE SELF-DESTRUCT PROTOCOL ENGAGED]
[SPATIAL COMPRESSION RATIO]: 1,000 : 1 (Dimension Locking)
[MAINSPRING DISCHARGE COUNTDOWN]:
[ 11 HOURS : 59 MINUTES : 59 SECONDS ] (Clock Running!)
[WARNING]:
If the reversal microchip is not inserted into the
"Clockwork Celestial Core" at the summit before the
mainspring completely discharges (720 minutes),
the spatial fold will trigger molecular lattice collapse—
The entire clock will permanently seize, and the
Micro-Voyagers will be stranded in this realm forever!
======================================================
```

Before their stunned eyes, the countdown clicked relentlessly down to fifty-eight seconds!

The world fell into a breathless hush, save for the heavens above, where thousands of titanic wheels began to mesh and turn with inexorable, rhythmic precision.

The twelve-hour survival breakout of the Micro-Voyagers had officially begun!
