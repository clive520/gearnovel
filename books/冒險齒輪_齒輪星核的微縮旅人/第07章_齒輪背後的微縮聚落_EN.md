# Adventure Gear: Voyagers of the Clockwork Core

## Chapter 7: The Micro-Settlement Behind the Gears

*HUUUUUMMMMM——*

Smoothly hoisted by the central vertical minute-wheel transmission arbor, the purple-copper elevator platform pierced through the half-centimeter-thick brass ceiling separating the lower plinth from the intermediate chassis.

As the oppressive shadows overhead peeled away, a warm, magnificent golden radiance—resembling the dawn breaking across an alien horizon—bathed the ascending platform in crystalline light!

"Whoaaaa..."

Jiang Jiang lowered his black frying pan, rubbing his eyes against the dazzling glare. When the panorama before him fully resolved, he froze completely in his tracks.

Spreading before Cheng Hao, Ye Yijie, Jiang Jiang, and the mechanical Shiba Inu Pico was a breathtaking marvel of horological civilization that surpassed the wildest boundaries of human engineering!

Suspended between two colossal, parallel brass mainplates spaced fifteen macro-centimeters apart (a soaring hundred-meter-tall cathedral vault to their 1.5-millimeter perspective), what should have been cold, barren machinery had been transformed into an intricate, thriving "Clockwork Micro-Kingdom"!

Across the flat brass terrain, tiered architectural wonders built from reclaimed balance cocks, fusee casings, and skeletonized copper wheel plates rose toward the ceiling. The circular cutouts originally machined to reduce gear inertia had been fitted with translucent wafers of synthetic ruby and sapphire. Beneath the light filtering through the clock's exterior rose window, they glowed like the stained-glass roses of miniature Gothic cathedrals, scattering kaleidoscope sunbeams across the streets below.

High above, suspension bridges woven from high-elasticity blued hairsprings spanned the chasms between colossal gear arbors; and across the broad avenues below, mirror-polished ruby runways twisted like crystalline high-speed expressways, weaving through the bustling clockwork districts!

"T-This is supposed to be an unmanned antique clock..." Jiang Jiang muttered, his jaw hanging wide. "How in the world is there an entire castle in here?!"

"Woof!"

Pico stood at the platform's precipice, his delicate alloy ears twitching at high frequency, azure lights dancing across his obsidian optics as he let out an alert, inquisitive bark.

Framing the massive brass gateway ahead, dozens of tiny golden silhouettes emerged from the shadows of the gear towers!

These were no mindless, feral cleaning scarabs. They were miniature **Brass Automaton Figures**, standing between 1.2 and 1.5 millimeters tall—identical in scale to Cheng Hao and his friends!

Their anatomies were formed from paper-thin cold-hammered brass plating pinned seamlessly with microscopic watch screws. Their joints articulated smoothly on ruby ball bearings, while at the center of each chest ticked a micro-hairspring heart oscillating at a crisp, lightning cadence. In their hands they bore miniature halberds fashioned from sharpened steel clock hands, and atop their heads rested crests sculpted from gear teeth, watching the newcomers with cautious vigilance.

"They're not biological—they are purely mechanical, self-propelled humanoid automatons!" Ye Yijie pushed her glasses up, her field journal instantly open. "Yet... how could an autonomous mechanical society ever emerge within the confines of a clock?!"

*PING! TICK-TOCK! PING-PING-TICK——!*

The lead automaton—standing nearly two millimeters tall and clad in silver rhodium-plated breastplate—marched forward and planted the butt of its steel halberd firmly against the brass ground.

It possessed no vocal cords, but the precision escapement within its chest clicked in a dizzying cadence of melodic strikes, vibrating a thin acoustic diaphragm across its abdomen that rippled the air with ultrasonic waves!

"It's speaking!" Jiang Jiang cowered behind Cheng Hao. "Cheng Hao... is it telling us we're trespassing?!"

"No, that's not speech—it's **'Horological Timing Code'**!"

Ye Yijie listened with rapt intensity, her metal stylus charting the strike intervals across her journal:

"The cadences obey base-60 sexagesimal notation! Each phrase consists of three long and two short escapement clicks, matching the astronomical degrees of the zodiac! Its opening query asks: *'Outsiders, state your oscillatory period and the hallmark of your maker!'*"

"The hallmark of our maker..."

A sudden spark of inspiration struck Cheng Hao. He drew his multi-functional screwdriver pen from his pocket.

The young inventor stepped forward, halting one meter from the tip of the guard's halberd. Toggling the pen to "Micro-Acoustic Pulse Transmission," his thumb tapped against the spring-loaded release catch in a precise, rhythmic sequence:

*PING——TICK! PING——TICK! PING-PING-TICK——!*

The pen's tip resonated with an identical acoustic frequency.

It was the golden ratio constant his grandfather Cheng Yuanshan had engraved upon the baseplate—`1.618`!

Finally, with the alloy tip of his probe, Cheng Hao sketched a graceful emblem upon the polished brass floor: twin helical hairsprings intertwined with a three-wheel epicyclic gear train—the ancestral "Stargazing Gear Hallmark" passed down through three generations of master horologists!

*Click...*

The spinning gears inside the captain's chest decelerated into gentle synchronization.

The twin ruby lenses of its eyes, which had been glowing with wary amber caution, softened in an instant into a profound, reverent azure blue!

*CLATTER——!*

The captain snapped his halberd back to his breastplate in a flawless salute, bowing with courtly elegance.

Behind him, dozens of brass automaton guards snapped to attention in unison, their jointed limbs issuing a melodious, wind-chime chorus of clicking brass!

"Phew... Grandpa's credentials hold up even in the micro-world," Cheng Hao wiped the cold sweat from his forehead, breathing a sigh of relief.

From the heart of the archway, the ranks parted gracefully.

A venerable automaton cast from antique bronze, draped in robes woven from gossamer gold wire, stepped forward with the assistance of two attendants.

Instead of a crested helm, atop his head floated a delicate, self-rotating armillary sphere. The hairspring heart within his chest beat with slow, deliberate, and ancient equilibrium.

*Whirrr... click-click-click... buzz...*

An acoustic diaphragm on the elder's chest vibrated with warm resonance. Then, through a miniature carbon-film acoustic transducer at his throat, words emerged—tinged with the mechanical buzz of clockwork, yet articulated in flawless human speech:

"Welcome... to the Heart of Chronos, children of mankind. I am the Sixth Chief Horologist of the Brass Kingdom—**'Elder Chronos-VI'**."

"You speak human language?!" Ye Yijie's eyes widened behind her spectacles.

"It is only natural, inquisitive maiden," the Elder inclined his head with the musical click of brass joints. "Two centuries ago, when the three greatest master horologists of this realm—including your honored forebear of the Cheng lineage—joined hands to forge this Armillary Celestial Clock, they foresaw the inevitable march of friction, wear, and dust."

"The masters understood that human lives are fleeting, and macro hands cannot reach the delicate tolerances within these plates. Thus, within this intermediate chamber, they planted the first seeds of our kind—the **Automatons**."

The Elder gestured toward the magnificent towers behind him, his synthesized voice ringing with deep pride:

"The masters endowed us with the capacity to maintain, repair, and reproduce our own progeny. What your human scholar John von Neumann would later formalize as the **'Theory of Self-Replicating Automata'**!"

"We gather the microscopic brass filings shed by the turning wheels as raw material. Powered by the kinetic potential of the great mainspring, we cold-stamp and assemble each succeeding generation. For two hundred years, we have kept this clockwork cosmos pristine and true!"

"Self-replicating automata..." Ye Yijie murmured in awe. "A purely mechanical realization of von Neumann architecture! This is a monument in engineering history!"

"Yet our long peace now hangs by a thread."

Elder Chronos lifted his metal staff toward the heavens.

The youngsters looked up. Looming directly above the kingdom's skyline, two massive anchor pallets forged from solid tungsten steel—each dozens of meters long—swung with the rhythm of the pendulum, opening and snapping shut with thunderous fury!

*CLAAANNNNG————!!*

*CLAAANNNNG————!!*

With each stroke, compression shockwaves swept across the realm, showering sparks like meteor showers across the distant perimeter!

Worse still, projected against the brass celestial ceiling, the crimson self-destruct countdown blinked relentlessly:

`[ 07 HOURS : 28 MINUTES : 44 SECONDS ]`

"The self-destruct protocol is running," the Elder's tone grew solemn. "If the reversal chip is not inserted into the Clockwork Celestial Core before the mainspring discharges, the spatial lattice will permanently lock. Not only will you remain trapped as miniature beings forever, but our two-hundred-year-old kingdom will freeze into cold, dead iron!"

"We came to reach the Celestial Core!" Cheng Hao stepped forward, his fists clenched tight. "Elder, show us the way to the summit!"

"The path to the celestial heights lies beyond the escapement—along the **'Great Ruby Runway'**."

Elder Chronos-VI sighed, a dim flicker passing through his optical lenses:

"However, during the torque shockwave from the lower mainspring, the **Differential Epicyclic Gear Train** that powers our kingdom suffered tooth-phase desynchronization! The brake dampers along the ruby runway are locked tight, and a swarm of overgrown Chrono-Beetles, driven feral by torque overloads, has fortified the runway threshold!"

"Our guards have attempted countless sorties, but our halberds cannot penetrate their super-hardened hulls..."

"Leave the armor to us," Cheng Hao grinned with quiet confidence, resting his hand on Jiang Jiang's black frying pan and Pico's alloy head. "We already took out an entire squad on the floor below!"

"Woof!" Pico barked proudly, puffing out his chest.

Jiang Jiang planted his pan onto the brass deck with a resounding thud, laughing boldly: "Don't sweat it, Elder! As long as the Adventure Invention Squad is on the clock, whether it's rogue bugs or jammed gears, we'll tune 'em up smooth as silk!"

Elder Chronos blinked in surprise, then the hairspring within his chest burst into a joyful, resonant chime:

"Splendid... The God of Time smiles upon us! Children, welcome to the Liberation Front of the Brass Kingdom!"

"Forward! Objective—The Ruby Runway and the Anchor Escapement Labyrinth!"

     * Proposed by mathematician John von Neumann in 1948: an autonomous machine capable of gathering raw environmental materials (brass wear filings), utilizing available energy (mainspring torque), and following internal blueprints to repair itself and fabricate identical descendants.
  2. **Acoustic Horological Timing Code (機械時鐘脈衝通信)**:
     * The automatons transmit information by rapidly engaging escapement clicks against metallic acoustic diaphragms. Utilizing **sexagesimal (base-60)** mathematics and binary pulse intervals, they achieve robust, high-speed acoustic communication through solid metal media.
  3. **Differential Epicyclic Gearing (差速行星齒輪分流)**:
     * Beyond simulating multi-planetary orbits, differential planetary gearing allows a single prime mover (the mainspring) to split mechanical power dynamically between divergent loads—powering both the clock's escapement and the automaton civilization's micro-grid.
