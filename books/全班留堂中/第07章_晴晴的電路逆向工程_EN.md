# Chapter 7: Qing-Qing's Reverse Circuit Engineering

Loop Day Twelve.

The natural science laboratory was saturated with the acrid, scorched scent of heated rosin and wisps of pale smoke drifting up from melting solder wire.

The thick blackout curtains were drawn shut, completely banishing the relentless glare of the sweltering sun and the abrasive droning of cicadas outside.

Before a long lab bench covered in anti-static rubber matting, Class President Lin Yun-Qing wore protective goggles and a high-magnification jeweler's loupe, her precision soldering iron darting across an exposed integrated circuit board with minuscule gaps of a fifth of a millimeter.

Beside her sat a bizarre, cobbled-together contraption assembled from an antique cathode-ray oscilloscope, three salvaged radio antennas, and dozens of red and black jumper wires.

"Sizzle—hummm—"

Whenever the soldering tip grazed a connection terminal, jagged, high-frequency interference waveforms erupted across the glowing green phosphor screen.

"Qing-Qing, you've been cooped up in the lab for three consecutive cycles now," seated at the corner of the bench, Lin Wei-Xi handed over a cool, damp towel with genuine concern. "Teacher Gao just issued the fourth random mock exam next door; Old Wu and A-Shin are on the brink of total collapse."

"Let them hold the line. It's called tactical cover."

Without looking up, Qing-Qing wiped the fine sheen of sweat from her brow with her sleeve, her tweezers delicately hoisting a microscopic superconducting microchip barely half the size of a grain of rice:

"If we follow Teacher Gao's logic of relying on all twenty-six students attaining a perfect hundred to unlock this loop, the statistical probability is equivalent to Old Wu winning the lottery three times in a row. We cannot bet our lives entirely on an exam; we need a brute-force hardware override."

"Squeak—creak—"

The metal louvers of the ventilation duct were nudged open, and a nimble, silver-white figure dropped down onto the anti-static mat with feline grace.

It was the class's AI companion pet, Liu-Liu.

Only today, Liu-Liu looked thoroughly cyberpunk—the tip of its bushy, metallic tail was bound tightly with a razor-thin gold-plated antenna via heat-shrink tubing, while a micro lithium-polymer battery slightly larger than a fingernail was Velcro-strapped to its back. Its dual obsidian electronic eyes were refreshing hexadecimal green code at blinding speed.

"Liu-Liu, what are the sensor readings?" Laying down her iron, Qing-Qing removed the loupe and inquired.

Liu-Liu flicked its pointed ears, emitting a rapid sequence of crisp electronic tones:

"Beep-beep! Campus-wide cosmic microwave background radiation mapping complete! Repeat, complete! A total of 342 electromagnetic anomalies detected; four exhibit absolute standing-wave resonance!"

Lifting its right forepaw, a mini holographic projector embedded in its footpad cast a crisp, green 3D wireframe schematic of Luyang Elementary into the air.

Within the glowing architectural wireframe, four locations pulsed with glaring, deep-violet beacons:

The first point was situated in the Principal's Office on the top floor of the Administration Building;  
The second was buried deep within the abandoned air-raid shelter beneath the gymnasium storeroom;  
The third hovered around the century-old clock tower facing the main academic quad;  
While the fourth point floated mysteriously around Wei-Xi's wrist, flickering between bright and dim.

Stepping closer to inspect the four beacons, Wei-Xi's eyes widened in awe: "Are these... the topological resonance nodes of my bangle?"

"Precisely. In quantum field theory, they are known as 'Spacetime Anchors.'"

Pulling up the waveform metrics on the oscilloscope, Qing-Qing's tone turned clinical and grave:

"Wei-Xi, your bangle was originally a one-way temporal recorder. But when struck by that hundred-thousand-volt lightning bolt, combined with the electromagnetic interference from Teacher Gao's cesium atomic clock, the bangle instinctively triggered the future's 【Emergency Spacetime Containment Protocol】 to prevent its core chip from incinerating."

Qing-Qing gestured toward the four glowing nodes on the hologram:

"It behaves like a colossal yurt, treating our school as its foundation. And these four beacons are four 'spacetime tent pegs' driven deep into the campus soil! Every day at 3:59:59 PM, the energy waves from these four points fire simultaneously, emitting an inverted phase wave that violently drags everything on campus back to eight in the morning."

Enlightenment dawned upon Wei-Xi in an instant: "So... as long as we dismantle all four tent pegs before four in the afternoon, the bangle's temporal barrier will collapse on its own?!"

"In theory, yes," Qing-Qing adjusted her glasses, an engineer's fierce spark of ambition blazing in her eyes. "The complication is that these anchors cannot be flipped off casually. Each anchor is deeply entangled with some pre-existing 'high-energy or high-order entity' already present on this campus."

Just then, three soft, rhythmic taps echoed against the heavy wooden door of the laboratory: "Knock, knock, knock."

The cadence conformed rigorously to a frequency of precisely two strikes per second, the force calibrated so exquisitely that the amplitude of the door's vibration was absolutely identical on each tap.

"Oh no! It's Teacher Gao!" Startled, Wei-Xi nearly vaulted out of her chair.

Moving like lightning, Liu-Liu tucked its tail and zipped beneath an empty cardboard box under the bench, concealing its antenna out of sight.

With practiced speed, Qing-Qing dragged a white canvas drop-cloth across the table, covering her reverse-engineering rig, before casually flipping open a physics textbook to the page on Lenz's Law.

"Come in," Qing-Qing took a steady breath, willing her voice to sound entirely nonchalant.

The brass doorknob turned smoothly.

Teacher Gao strode inside with textbook posture. In his hands, he carried a wooden tray bearing two glasses of warm barley tea calibrated precisely to sixty-five degrees Celsius, alongside two small plates of peeled apple cubes cut into uniform 1.5-centimeter cubes.

"Pardon the interruption. Pursuant to juvenile metabolic monitoring models, both students have engaged in uninterrupted, high-intensity cognitive labor within this enclosed space for two hours and forty minutes. Current blood glucose levels have dipped twelve percent below the safety threshold."

Teacher Gao placed the tray steadily upon the corner of the lab bench, his three-piece suit unwrinkled, his dark-rimmed glasses holding an impenetrable depth beneath the dim ambient light.

"Thank you, Teacher Gao," Wei-Xi accepted the tea with polite deference, keeping her eyes downcast to avoid his gaze.

Taking her cup, Qing-Qing probed casually: "Teacher Gao... weren't you proctoring the exam next door? How are Old Wu and the others holding up?"

"Student Wu Chen-Kai successfully elevated his score from zero to forty-two points on the fourth mock assessment. His mastery of Archimedes' principle has demonstrated measurable progress, although he persists in converting 'displaced fluid volume' into 'equivalent dumpling capacity' on the final essay prompt."

Teacher Gao delivered the progress report dispassionately, before his gaze drifted slowly across the bulky white canvas draped over the lab bench.

"Hummm—"

A microscopic flicker of pale blue light flared behind his spectacle lenses—the X-ray optical scanner!

Qing-Qing's heart leapt into her throat, a cold sweat beading on her palms.

Yet contrary to all expectation, Teacher Gao did not sweep the canvas aside, nor did he confiscate the unauthorized temporal detector on grounds of campus safety violations.

The bionic android retracted his gaze, picked up his empty tray, and turned toward the laboratory door.

Just as his polished leather soles were about to cross the threshold, Teacher Gao paused. Keeping his back to them, he spoke with that deep, magnetic baritone:

"The internal escapement wheel of the antique bronze grandfather clock in the Principal's Office is cast from high-purity phosphor bronze, possessing natural attenuation properties against 144-megahertz microwave transmissions. Should one attempt high-frequency signal detection, it is advisable to circumvent the mechanical gear engagement windows during hourly chimes."

Qing-Qing froze, the cup of tea paralyzed mid-air in her fingers.

Wei-Xi's eyes widened in sheer astonishment.

Teacher Gao turned his head slightly, a remarkably gentle glint shimmering along the edge of his lenses:

"As a homeroom educator, this machine's core directive is to bear witness to the growth of its students. Whether that growth manifests through answers inked upon test papers, or through empirical exploration across physical reality. Press forward, students. Three hours and fifteen minutes remain until the next temporal reset."

"Click."

The laboratory door swung gently shut.

The room returned to quiet, save for the thin wisps of steam rising from the warm barley tea.

Staring at the closed door, Qing-Qing sat stunned for five whole seconds, before a resolute, understanding smirk spread across her face:

"That eccentric robot teacher... he knew all along."

Qing-Qing yanked the canvas off her equipment, the fires of battle blazing in her eyes:

"Liu-Liu! Out! Target locked—top floor of the Administration Building: the century-old bronze clock in the Principal's Office!"

"Before four o'clock today, we're going to yank that first spacetime tent peg... right out of the ground!"
