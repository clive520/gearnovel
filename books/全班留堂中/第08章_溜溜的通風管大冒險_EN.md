# Chapter 8: Liu-Liu's Ventilation Shaft Odyssey

1:15 PM.

Along the top-floor corridor of the Administration Building, it was so dead quiet that the ping of a falling screw would trigger an echo.

Ten feet above the ground, in the deep recesses of a sheet-metal air conditioning duct, a silver-white furball was inching forward in a thoroughly comical belly crawl through the narrow galvanized pipe.

"Huff... puff..."

The AI pet Liu-Liu pinned both pointed metallic ears flush against its head to prevent them from scraping against the rusted rivets lining the interior. Its bushy tail, tipped with a gold-plated antenna, was held rigid as a laser level, while the micro lithium battery pack on its back radiated a gentle warmth of 38.5 degrees Celsius.

"Static—crackle—Liu-Liu, do you read? This is Ground Control."

Transmitted through a grain-of-rice-sized bone-conduction earpiece tucked in Liu-Liu's ear came Class President Lin Yun-Qing's hushed, crystalline directive:

"You are currently 2.5 meters from the second vertical junction. Caution! The Academic Affairs Office is directly beneath you on the third floor. Director Zhou just concluded his patrol and is seated at his desk drinking ginseng tea. Do not exceed an acoustic threshold of twenty-five decibels under any circumstances!"

Liu-Liu blinked its dual obsidian electronic eyes, squeezing out two faint, rhythmic "beep-beeps" in acknowledgment.

The little creature delicately extended its pink paw pads, moving as weightlessly as a gecko treading across a spiderweb.

Yet the ductwork of an ancient school was an subterranean empire of accumulated dust and cobwebs.

Just as Liu-Liu rounded the corner, a dusty web-ball the size of a tennis ball, thick with vintage chalk dust, hung directly in its flight path. Liu-Liu's electronic olfactory sensors—eighty times more sensitive than a bloodhound's—contracted violently—

"Ah... ah..."

Liu-Liu clamped both forepaws over its snout, its furry face turning scarlet as two pitiful, involuntary tears squeezed from the corners of its eyes.

"ACHOOOO—!"

Though Liu-Liu threw all its might into smothering the sneeze behind its paws, the muffled explosion still triggered a resonant, metallic hum along the hollow sheet-metal walls!

"Who's there?! Who's making that racket?!"

From the office precisely 2.5 meters directly below, a thunderous roar detonated like an artillery strike!

With a violent "BANG," Director Zhou's jumbo stainless-steel thermos slammed onto his mahogany desk. Immediately after, a six-foot heavy broom crafted from a stout bamboo pole cut through the air with a whistling screech, stabbing violently into the acoustic ceiling tiles overhead!

"CLATTER! CRASH!"

The bristle head collided dead center against the underside of the sheet-metal duct. The violent shockwave sent Liu-Liu into a 360-degree aerial backflip, the antenna on its tail nearly wrenched clean off!

"A feral cat?! Or a stray weasel?!" Director Zhou bellowed furiously from below. "Sweltering in this heat without AC, and you dare nest directly above my head?! Get down here if you've got the guts! See if I don't fill an entire page of my disciplinary registry with your infractions!"

"THUMP! THUMP! THUMP!"

The heavy bamboo pole hammered relentlessly against the ceiling, showering fine plaster dust through the seams of the ductwork, dusting Liu-Liu's sleek, silver-white coat into a tragic powdery snowsuit.

"Liu-Liu! Bolt! Engage silent turbo-glide mode!" A-Shin's frantic shout crackled through the earpiece.

Gritting its tiny teeth, the micro-suction pads beneath Liu-Liu's four paws flared outward. Transforming into a silver torpedo skimming the deck, it shot along the galvanized floor in a supersonic blur, covering a dozen meters in the blink of an eye, cleanly evading the storm of broom strikes behind it!

Two minutes later, the chaotic thuds were left far in the distance.

Sprawled flat before a dust-choked ventilation grate, Liu-Liu panted raggedly, extending a pink tongue to lick chalk powder from its whiskers.

"Phew... Ground Control reporting. Target confirmed directly beneath your sector in the Principal's Office," Qing-Qing's relieved voice whispered over the comms. "Liu-Liu, you are hovering directly above the louvered ceiling fan. Perform an infrared thermal scan of the room; confirm safety and initiate deployment!"

Taking a deep breath, Liu-Liu's electronic eyes snapped from obsidian black to deep crimson—

The scanning beams pierced through the plastic slats of the exhaust fan.

The chamber below was entirely vacant.

The genial, tea-loving Principal Chiu Shou-Ren was evidently enjoying his summer vacation at home. Purple-clay teapots were lined up immaculately upon the polished mahogany desk, beneath a gilded plaque reading "Cultivating Talents for the Nation."

And nestled in the deepest corner of the office stood an antique bronze grandfather clock over six feet tall, its frame sculpted from dark, hand-carved aloeswood.

"Tick-tock... tick-tock... tick-tock..."

A massive brass pendulum swung within the wooden chassis in slow, majestic intervals, radiating an aura of century-old dignity.

Yet within the infrared spectrum of Liu-Liu's electronic optics, this antique clock presented a terrifying, staggering anomaly—

A swirling vortex of deep-violet electromagnetic field, over three feet in diameter, spun wildly inside the pendulum case! With every swing of the brass weight, that purple nexus pumped an imperceptible ripple of spacetime distortion into the surrounding air, pulsing like a living heart!

This was precisely the first "Spacetime Tent Peg" Qing-Qing had deduced!

"Anchor confirmed! Electromagnetic reading: 4,200 microteslas!" Liu-Liu hissed into its microphone.

"Splendid! Commence phase two!" Qing-Qing's instructions arrived briskly. "Teacher Gao's hint was explicit: the internal escapement wheel is forged from high-purity phosphor bronze—the core medium for this resonance! Attach the electromagnetic pulse disruptor on your back to the gear axle and fire a counter-phase pulse to rip that frequency off its tracks!"

Extending a forepaw, Liu-Liu unscrewed the plastic retaining clips of the exhaust vent.

Lowering itself from the ceiling on an emergency spool of thin nylon cord like a tactical flying squirrel trained in special operations, the little creature landed silently atop the carved wooden crest of the clock.

At the very top of the casing sat an ornamental filigree hole for ventilation.

Sucking in its belly, Liu-Liu wriggled inside like an agile eel.

The clockwork interior was a magnificent sight—giant interlocking bronze cogs grinding with deep, rhythmic "clack-clack" beats. And dead center within the drive assembly, a palm-sized escapement wheel forged of an eerie, greenish-bronze alloy was suffocating beneath the swirling tendrils of purple temporal turbulence!

"Beep-beep! Target escapement wheel locked!"

The micro battery pack strapped to Liu-Liu's back instantly flipped into overload discharge mode, the gold-plated antenna flaring with blinding emerald electrical arcs in the dark!

Vaulting forward, the creature slammed the magnetic suction pad flush against the spinning axle of the phosphor bronze gear!

"SIZZZZLE—CRACKLE—!"

A violent electromagnetic pulse detonated within the belly of the clock!

Assaulted by the counter-phase blast, the deep-purple temporal vortex thrash wildly like a wounded beast, spitting a frenzy of violet sparks across the bronze gear teeth!

The century-old wooden clock, weighing over two hundred pounds, began to shudder violently upon its pedestal!

"CHIME————!!!!"

An explosive, deafening toll detonated without warning within the Principal's Office!

It was a mechanical chain reaction triggered on the brink of temporal collapse! The brass chime hammer struck the tuned coil with immense force, sending waves of sonic thunder blasting through the doorframe and echoing straight into the clouds!

"What in the world?! Why is the Principal's clock ringing early?!"

From down the hallway came the frantic, thudding thunder of Director Zhou's heavy wingtips!

"Liu-Liu! Full overload power! Ten seconds until Director Zhou kicks the door down!" Over the comms came the collective, breathless heartbeats of the entire class.

Biting its tiny teeth, Liu-Liu pinned all four paws against the wooden inner walls, dumping the final milliampere of battery reserves into the antenna!

"BREAK FOR ME————!" (Though to human ears, it sounded like a tiny, ferocious squeak: "SQUEEEAK—!")

"SNAP!"

A sharp crack of metal fracturing echoed within the chassis.

Under the ferocious counter-pulse, the deep-purple temporal vortex wrapping the gear shattered like a fractured mirror, bursting into millions of sparkling violet motes that slowly evaporated into the void!

The massive pendulum jerked momentarily before settling into its natural, serene, unhurried cadence:

"Tick-tock... tick-tock..."

"BAM!"

Simultaneously, the mahogany door was flung open as Director Zhou barged inside with his master key!

Broom held high, the iron disciplinarian scanned the room with wild, hyper-vigilant eyes.

The office was a portrait of idyllic tranquility. Afternoon sunlight poured through the blinds across the carpet; the purple-clay teapot rested peacefully upon the desk; in the corner, the antique bronze clock swayed its long pendulum with leisurely grace.

Rubbing his eyes, Director Zhou walked suspiciously before the clock, rapping his knuckles against the heavy casing. Apart from his own ragged breathing, he found nothing.

Deep behind the clock, wedged inside a dark structural gap in the masonry, Liu-Liu—covered in soot, its tail fur fried into a bushy toilet brush—poked its tiny head out, letting out a long, trembling sigh of relief toward the ceiling duct.

Far away in the natural science lab, Qing-Qing stared at the oscilloscope. Of the four radiant purple beacons, the first node in the Principal's Office flickered out with a sharp pop. Tears welled in the corners of her eyes.

"The first spacetime tent peg..." Pulling off her goggles, Qing-Qing proclaimed to the tearful, embracing Wei-Xi and the cheering classmates beside her:

"Dismantling successful!"
