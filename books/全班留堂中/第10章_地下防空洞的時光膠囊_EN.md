# Chapter 10: The Time Capsule in the Subterranean Shelter

10:15 AM.

Deep within the physical education storeroom, the air was heavy with the stale stench of aged rubber mats, rusted dumbbells, and damp wooden vaulting horses.

Sunlight angled through the grimy high transoms, illuminating drifting motes of dust into a slow-flowing river of golden motes.

"Heave-ho... one, two, three, lift!"

Beneath Old Wu's deep, resonant cadence call, four burly boys threw their weight into shoving three stacked pommel horses aside.

Beneath the dense high-jump foam mattresses, a square cast-iron cellar door—mottled with reddish-brown rust and welded with a massive iron pull-ring—stood exposed before their eyes.

This was the entrance to the antique air-raid shelter left over from the founding of Luyang Elementary, ordinarily secured by a heavy brass padlock, untouched by human hands for at least thirty years.

"Qing-Qing, you're up!" A-Shin wiped the sweat from his forehead, pointing at the rusted padlock.

"Piece of cake."

Kneeling, Class President Lin Yun-Qing withdrew two flat steel pick probes from her utility belt, inserting them into the keyway and giving three delicate, counter-leveraged nudges.

"Click-clack."

A crisp spring release echoed, and the heavy shackle popped open on cue!

"Holy cow! Qing-Qing, with lockpicking skills like that, if your family's motorcycle shop ever goes under, you'll never starve as a secret agent!" Old Wu's eyes were as round as saucers.

"This is the application of the lever principle combined with reverse-engineering tumbler geometry; stop comparing me to an inefficient profession like espionage." Emotionlessly stowing her tools, Qing-Qing gripped the heavy cast-iron ring with both hands and wrenched it upward with immense leverage!

"CREEEEEEEAK————"

Accompanied by a teeth-grinding groan of agonizing metal friction, the cellar door sealed for decades was hauled ajar. An icy, subterranean draft—thick with moisture and the raw, mineral tang of wet earth—billowed outward from the pitch-black stairway below!

Perched upon Qing-Qing's shoulder, the AI pet Liu-Liu bristled its ears, its electronic optics snapping instantly to deep night-vision green:

"Beep-beep! Strong electrostatic field detected below! Spacetime microwave frequency: 2.4 gigahertz! 98.5 percent correlation with Bangle Anchor Number Two!"

"The second spacetime tent peg is down there!" Wei-Xi's eyes lit up as she tightened her grip on her heavy-duty flashlight.

"Let's move! Expedition team, advance!" Flashlight clenched between his teeth, A-Shin spearheaded the descent, feeling his way down the slick concrete steps one at a time.

The underground bunker was far more cavernous than anticipated.

All around stood blast-resistant walls poured from cyclopean masonry and reinforced concrete. A thick carpet of dried silt crunched beneath their soles, while the walls retained faded, half-century-old slogans painted in peeling red lacquer: "Heighten Vigilance, Defend the School."

The white beams of their flashlights danced in the gloom, casting elongated shadows across the stone.

Penetrating roughly fifty meters down the tunnel, the corridor broadened into a circular shelter chamber roughly the size of half a classroom.

And resting atop a concrete pedestal dead center in the chamber hung a breathtaking, surreal anomaly—

A spherical orb of blazing, deep-violet plasma!

Roughly half a meter in diameter, dense electrical arcs danced across its surface, washing the somber bunker in an eerie, spectral purple glow. And dead center within that plasma core, tightly clutched within the temporal eddy, rested an aged dark-green tin biscuit box, roughly forty centimeters long, engraved with ornate vintage filigree!

"The second anchor... is an old biscuit tin?!" A-Shin rubbed his eyes, convinced his vision was playing tricks on him.

"That is no ordinary biscuit container."

A deep, measured metallic baritone echoed softly from behind.

The students spun around in shock. Unnoticed by anyone, Teacher Gao had descended the staircase without a sound. Dressed in that immaculate charcoal-grey suit, without a speck of soot upon his polished leather shoes, the beam of his flashlight pinned the concrete pedestal with surgical accuracy.

"Teacher Gao?! Why did you come down here too?!" Startled, Old Wu nearly dropped his flashlight on his toes.

"As a homeroom educator, safeguarding student safety within uncharted subterranean geological structures is Protocol Priority One." Nudging his dark spectacles, code cascades flickered across Teacher Gao's lenses as they decrypted the artifact: "Furthermore, this tin box is cataloged in the Luyang Elementary historical archives as 【ARC-1996-001】; its formal designation is: The 38th Anniversary Alumni Time Capsule."

"A time capsule?!" A collective gasp rippled through the class.

"Precisely." Stepping forward, Teacher Gao's gaze locked upon the raging ball of violet plasma. "Thirty years ago today, the twelfth graduating class buried this box here, pledging to open it three decades hence. Because this vessel encapsulated intense collective longing and unfulfilled adolescent resolutions, its microscopic quantum entangled state generated violent semantic resonance with the Chronos Bangle, thereby collapsing into the second spacetime anchor."

"Then how do we dismantle it?" Qing-Qing raised her reverse-pulse disruptor, her posture taut.

"Deploying a forced electromagnetic pulse will induce rapid metal fatigue, irrevocably destroying the historical artifacts preserved within." Teacher Gao extended an index finger, wagging it gently. "The sole methodology to resolve this anchor is to fulfill the root desire sealing it: open it, read it, and formally archive its historical causality."

"I'll do it!" Inhaling deeply, A-Shin pulled on the high-voltage insulated rubber gloves Qing-Qing handed him. Bracing against the howling electrostatic gale, he advanced toward the pedestal one step at a time.

"Sizzle—CRACK!"

Blinding purple arcs lashed against the rubber gloves. Gritting his teeth, A-Shin's hands clamped like twin iron vises, plunging straight into the heart of the plasma to seize the heavy tin box!

"HEAVE————!"

With a feral roar, veins bulging across his forearms, A-Shin wrenched the tin box clean out of the violet plasma!

"BOOM!"

Deprived of its physical anchor, the plasma orb emitted a hollow wail, collapsing violently into a microscopic singularity before evaporating into nothingness with a sharp pop!

The spectral purple glow vanished instantly, leaving only the warm, amber beams of their flashlights.

"Huff... puff... heavy as a rock!" A-Shin slumped onto the floor, setting the wax-sealed, rust-mottled green box before the group.

Across the lid, brushed in slightly crooked red lacquer characters, ran an inscription: 【To Ourselves in the Year 2026—From All Members of Class 6-1, 12th Graduating Cohort】.

"Whoa! It really is from our predecessors!" Old Wu scrambled forward excitedly, delicately prying the brittle sealing wax away with a pocketknife.

The tin lid was slowly eased open.

Inside lay no gold or jewels—only a dense stack of yellowed black-and-white photographs, several obsolete coins, a handful of polished glass marbles, and a thick bundle of letters penned in fountain pen and brush calligraphy.

Curious, A-Shin drew out a yellowed self-criticism confession resting at the very top, reading it aloud by flashlight:

"'Self-Criticism Statement: I, Zhou Yan, on the morning of June 3, 1996, did maliciously coat the doorknob of the Principal's Office in industrial adhesive, and unlawfully reared three live loaches within the ductwork of the Academic Affairs Office, severely disrupting campus order...'"

A-Shin's voice cut off dead mid-sentence.

The entire subterranean bunker plunged into an eerie, suffocating silence.

"W-wait a minute!" Old Wu's eyes bulged like brass gongs, his tongue tangling in his teeth. "Who did you just say signed that confession?!"

A-Shin rubbed his eyes violently, shoving the yellowed paper an inch from the flashlight beam, reading every syllable with deliberate, stunned shock:

"Signee... Student of Class 6-1, 12th Graduating Class... Zhou Yan."

"PFFFFT—————!!!!"

Several boys in the back row choked on their spit, Old Wu collapsed backward onto his rear, and the multimeter in Qing-Qing's grip nearly slipped from her fingers!

"Zhou... Zhou Yan?! The Iron Judge Old Zhou?!" A-Shin vaulted into the air, the paper shaking violently in his hands. "That man who walks around with a death mask, threatens expulsion every other sentence, and carries a telephone-directory-thick disciplinary registry—when he was a kid, he was a hell-raising supreme delinquent who glued the principal's door and bred mudfish in the air ducts?!"

Teacher Gao adjusted his glasses, sliding a yellowed three-inch black-and-white photograph from the bottom of the tin with two fingers.

The flashlight beam illuminated the glossy paper—

In the photo, a scrawny boy with a buzz cut, skin tanned bronze, grinning with two missing front teeth, had his arm locked around another boy's neck, making a wildly defiant face at the camera. Pinned to the boy's chest was a scarlet school badge reading: 【Luyang Elementary · Class 6-1 · Zhou Yan】.

"According to cross-referencing against our archival databases, Student Zhou Yan accumulated thirty-eight verbal reprimands, twelve minor demerits, and two major demerits during his primary school career. His cumulative detention hours totaled 480 hours, setting the all-time school record of twenty-one consecutive days of mandatory summer remedial instruction."

Teacher Gao recited the statistics dispassionately, a profoundly inscrutable smile gracing his lips:

"He was, without a shadow of a doubt, the undisputed First-Generation King of Pranks in the history of Luyang Elementary."

Staring at that boy in the photo grinning with careless, untamed joy—his eyes blazing with innocence and rebellion—the classroom of students went entirely quiet.

A-Shin gently brushed his thumb across the aged photograph, looking down at the glass marbles and the hand-drawn loach-rearing manual, feeling a tender ache strike deep within his chest.

So that stern, unyielding adult who roared down the hallways every morning, whom they took joy in humiliating with supernatural pranks...

Had once been identical to them.

He had dreaded exams; he had despised detention; he had shared a circle of loyal, shoulder-to-shoulder comrades who dreamed of turning the entire world upside down.

"Teacher Gao..." Wei-Xi whispered softly, "Why did Director Zhou... turn into the man he is today?"

Teacher Gao took the photograph, delicately tucking it back into the metal tin, his expression gentle and profound:

"Because time strips away the innocence of youth, cloaking an individual in a heavy armor known as 'responsibility.' Yet trust in this homeroom teacher's computational models—no matter how far a man journeys, or how austere his exterior becomes with age, the boy who once ran freely beneath the summer sun has never truly departed from his heart."

Qing-Qing looked down at the oscilloscope in her hands.

Across the glowing wireframe model, the second purple beacon buried deep within the bunker flared with a soft, golden halo, before quietly extinguishing.

The second spacetime tent peg was dismantled.

Cradling the tin box carrying thirty years of forgotten memories, A-Shin looked up toward the faint daylight filtering down from the cellar stairs, a warm, genuine smile blooming across his lips:

"Come on, let's head back to class. For this afternoon's study hall... I think I don't hate Old Zhou quite so much anymore."
