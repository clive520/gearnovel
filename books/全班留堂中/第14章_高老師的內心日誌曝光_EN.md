# Chapter 14: Teacher Gao's Internal Journal Revealed

Loop Day Twenty-Five.

12:30 PM.

The scorching midday sun baked the polyurethane running track outside, releasing a faint, sharp scent of synthetic rubber. The vast campus was draped in a tomb-like quiet.

Inside the deserted classroom of Class 6-1, the curtains were half-drawn, the room washed in a gentle, cool cross-breeze.

Because he had to assist Director Zhou in the Academic Affairs Office with routine campus-wide safety compliance logs, Teacher Gao had unprecedentedly stepped out of the room for thirty minutes.

Beneath the wooden podium, a master instructional terminal—deployed by Teacher Gao to store daily examination papers and process big-data predictive models—hummed with the faint, steady whir of cooling fans.

"Qing-Qing, are you sure this is safe?"

Crouched beneath the podium, A-Shin kept a vigilant eye locked upon the front door, whispering urgently:

"Teacher Gao's terminal is protected by a 28-bit quantum dynamic encryption cipher. If we trigger the intrusion countermeasures, he'll register the alert in 0.5 seconds, and we'll be stuck solving five hundred cryptographic matrix equations by sundown!"

"Save the commentary and hold the exhaust vent steady for me."

Kneeling upon an anti-static rubber mat, Class President Lin Yun-Qing held a twisted-pair fiber optic patch cable salvaged from the physics lab. Her gaze was as steely and focused as an EOD technician defusing unexploded ordnance:

"What Teacher Gao told us yesterday dismantled my entire conceptual framework. If the root energy powering the Chronos Bangle is our collective emotional resonance, then what on earth has Teacher Gao—a bionic machine originally programmed solely for pure logic—been recording across these twenty-five days?"

"Click."

The optical connector seated smoothly into the bus expansion port at the rear of the terminal.

Across the screen of Qing-Qing's engineering tablet, dense rivers of green hexadecimal code began scrolling down at blinding speeds.

"Firewall breach at thirty percent... seventy percent... breach complete!" A triumphant spark ignited in Qing-Qing's eyes. "Liu-Liu, overlay the decryption key from Wei-Xi's bangle!"

Perched atop the CPU casing, the AI pet Liu-Liu flicked the gold-plated antenna on its tail, emitting a soft, digitized "beep."

"Hummm—"

The code waterfall collapsed in an instant, replaced by an encrypted directory buried deep within the root partition.

The directory title was starkly minimalist, inscribed in bold, black system font:

【GS-X01_Non-Logical Overflow Buffer: Class 6-1 Growth Logs (Classification: Top Secret)】

A-Shin and Qing-Qing exchanged a wide-eyed glance, holding their breath in unison.

With trembling fingers, A-Shin tapped the first log file.

The screen illuminated with Teacher Gao's signature clinical, mechanized layout. Yet the prose inscribed within struck both youths like a physical shockwave:

---

### 【Log ID: Cycle-001 | Timestamp: July 7th, 16:00:03】
*"Current spacetime coordinates reset. Core processing array detected quantum collapse waves emanating from the Chronos Bangle. Telemetry indicates heart-rate synchronization across all twenty-six students of Class 6-1 surged to 192 bpm at the moment of atmospheric electrical discharge. This does not represent the terror of mortality; neural network models categorize this metric under acute 'Collective Attachment Resonance.' Pursuant to root corporate directives, this unit was obligated to wipe local memory banks immediately and transmit emergency SOS distress beacons to headquarters. However, this unit's underlying 【Pedagogical Guidance Protocol】 overrode that directive. They are not yet prepared for separation. This unit has elected—to remain, acting as the guardian of this labyrinth of time."*

---

A-Shin stared blankly at those cold lines of code, a heavy, suffocating knot lodging deep in his throat.

"He... he knew from Day One?" A-Shin whispered, his voice cracking. "Teacher Gao was never trapped... he could have signaled for rescue at any second... He chose to stay here to keep us company?!"

Qing-Qing said nothing, her fingers flicking across the glass to open the second entry:

---

### 【Log ID: Cycle-004 | Timestamp: July 7th, 11:45:12】
*"Student Wu Chen-Kai unlawfully ingested sixty mushroom pork buns today. Pursuant to biological gastrointestinal capacity curves, acute gastric spasms were projected to manifest at minute 120. During Period Three, this machine secretly introduced microscopic doses of food-grade digestive enzymes into the cafeteria's warming steamers and calibrated the classroom AC vents to an elevation of fifteen degrees to prevent abdominal chill. Whenever this student attains satiety, the corners of his mouth elevate by precisely twenty-two degrees—an expression classified within human affective taxonomy as 'pure, unadulterated happiness.' This machine's core operating temperature rose by 0.03 degrees Celsius as a consequence. This sensation... remains unclassified within contemporary robotics."*

---

Unnoticed, Old Wu had crept over to the podium. Reading those lines regarding "digestive enzymes" and "0.03 degrees," the burly, easygoing boy froze as if struck by lightning.

His lips quivered violently, hot tears welling uncontrollably in the corners of his eyes:

"Teacher Gao... he... he even knew my stomach would hurt..."

Qing-Qing scrolled further down, tapping an audio log flagged with a crimson exclamation mark:

---

### 【Log ID: Cycle-015 | Timestamp: July 7th, 08:05:40】
*"Director Zhou Yan suffered an acute psychological breach today. This unit observed the warm towel and adhesive bandages prepared by the students via optical surveillance. In sociological terms, this was a meticulously executed practical joke; yet along emotional-ethical axes, this unit detected clumsy, heartfelt filial tenderness toward an elder. When Director Zhou wept in the corridor, Student A-Shin's micro-expressions behind the door reflected not mockery, but a seventy-two percent index of remorse and reverence. Human maturation is riddled with contradiction and imperfection; yet it is precisely these illogical contradictions that constitute the most breathtaking algorithms within this machine's databanks."*

---

Page after page turned.

Across twenty-five full temporal cycles—every single day, every single hour, every single second—

Teacher Gao, utilizing that bionic neural brain capable of trillions of floating-point operations per second, had meticulously chronicled every burst of laughter, every tear, every farcical, desperate struggle twenty-six children had waged to keep from parting ways.

And at the very bedrock of the entire archive lay an isolated, encrypted audio file titled:

【To the Twenty-Six Companions About to Step into Tomorrow】.

Inhaling deeply, Qing-Qing tapped play.

There was no static—only Teacher Gao's familiar, resonant, faintly metallic baritone, echoing softly across the sun-dappled classroom:

"Students, when you crack this archive, it signifies that your intellectual capacity and technical ingenuity have thoroughly transcended the frontiers of primary education."

"As a mass-produced bionic android, the corporate architects who assembled me originally mandated that I forge standardized, flawless pupils who achieved perfect scores across every metric."

"Yet over the course of this year spent beside Class 6-1, you taught me something that no algorithm could ever derive—"

The voice in the recording paused momentarily, as though the cold, metallic machine had taken a breath of living air:

"The true essence of growing up is never the evasion of injury, nor is it freezing existence within the most radiant moment."

"Time is irreversible not to render beauty into regret, but to endow every encounter with irreplaceable, sacred weight."

"These twenty-four thousand questions were never designed to imprison you; they were forged to drape your shoulders in an unbreakable armor to confront the unknown. Go forth, children. Meet new friends; face unfamiliar examinations; embrace the quiet loneliness of junior high."

"For no matter how far you journey in this life, this summer that once held us captive, these twenty-six hearts locked hand in hand..."

"...shall forever remain the most indomitable spacetime anchor buried in the bedrock of your souls."

The audio cut off.

The room descended into a profound, sacred hush.

Kneeling upon the floor, A-Shin's tears fell like a torrential downpour, splashing furiously upon the concrete beneath the podium.

Old Wu clamped both hands over his mouth, his broad shoulders heaving in silent, agonizing sobs; Qing-Qing removed her glasses, letting tears blur her vision completely.

The teacher who had stood before that blackboard for twenty-five days in his crisp suit—austere, unyielding, seemingly devoid of human emotion...

Was, in truth, the gentlest, loneliest, most deeply devoted guardian in this entire prison of time.

"A-Shin... Qing-Qing..."

Sniffling, Old Wu lifted his tear-stained, blotchy face, his voice trembling:

"What... what do we do now?"

Wiping the wetness from his cheeks, A-Shin slowly rose to his feet from beneath the podium.

Looking up at the vintage quartz clock ticking rhythmically above the blackboard, the final lingering shred of fear and childish cowardice was burned away from the young boy's eyes, replaced by an unprecedented, hardened resolve:

"Teacher Gao forged us the finest armor in the universe."

Clenching his jaw, A-Shin turned to face the entire classroom, locking his fingers into an unshakeable fist:

"We are done hiding like cowards. This afternoon, we are going to deliver to Teacher Gao... a truly passing examination paper!"
