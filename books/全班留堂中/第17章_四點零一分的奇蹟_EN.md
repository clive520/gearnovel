# Chapter 17: The Miracle of Four-O-One PM

3:58 PM.

The velocity of time seemed stretched into infinity across these final one hundred and twenty seconds.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the harsh fluorescent fixtures had died away, replaced by an awe-inspiring, cosmic spectacle painting the skies outside—

The roiling sea of inky-purple storm clouds was being torn asunder by boundless, incandescent golden light. Hundreds of meters above the campus, billions of microscopic violet motes drifted and surged on the gale, resembling a majestic meteor shower cascading in reverse toward the heavens.

Dead center within the room, twenty-six students stood shoulder to shoulder, arms locked, forming a tight, unbroken circle of living solidarity.

A-Shin gripped Old Wu's hand with fierce intensity; Old Wu's other palm clasped Qing-Qing; beside Qing-Qing stood Wei-Xi... Every young palm was drenched in warm sweat, twenty-six rapid, unshakeable heartbeats synchronizing into a singular cadence within the hallowed quiet.

In the center of the ring, Teacher Gao stood tall. He wore no spectacles; those clear, starry eyes gazed peacefully at the vintage quartz clock above the blackboard. Cradled upon his shoulder, the AI companion Liu-Liu sat quietly, its metallic tail curled affectionately against the educator's collar.

3:59:30 PM.

The quartz clock on the wall emitted heavy, crisp mechanical clicks: "Tick, tick, tick."

Over thirty full cycles, whenever the second hand crossed into this final lethal radius, the room had plunged into blinding terror and hysteria—some screamed at the rafters, some wept in fetal despair, some threw themselves frantically against the windowpanes.

Yet today, not a single soul shrank back.

Not a single eye shut.

A-Shin held his chin high, that signature defiant, fearless grin playing across his lips; Old Wu puffed out his round chest, the sheen of pork bun grease on his cheek catching the golden light; Qing-Qing's gaze was as sharp and serene as cold steel; upon Wei-Xi's wrist, the golden aura pulsed with the gentle warmth of living breath.

3:59:50 PM!

"SIZZLE—BOOM-RUMBLE-CRASH—————!!!!"

The final, apocalyptic thunderbolt descended on cue!

A colossal pillar of violet-white plasma cleaved through the heavens, roaring with world-shattering fury, plunging vertically from the storm clouds to strike the galvanized lightning rod atop the academic wing!

A tidal wave of superheated plasma surged down the reinforced steel skeleton of the building, transforming the classroom into a blinding sea of lightning! Desks rattled furiously, windowpanes shrieked on the verge of shattering, and endless motes of gold and purple clashed, spun, and intertwined violently in the gale!

It was the desperate grasp of the old timeline; it was the final, rabid struggle of absolute causality!

3:59:58 PM!

The long black second hand shuddered violently, slamming hard into the vertical twelve-o'clock notch!

"CHIME—————!!!!"

The 4:00 PM dismissal bell detonated across the classroom, through the building, deep within the marrow of every living soul with crushing, soul-splitting majesty!

A flood of blinding white light burst from the void like an uncapped celestial river, pulling viciously at the frames of all twenty-six youths, desperate to drag their consciousness, their memories, and this entire school back into the closed loop of July 7th, 8:00 AM!

"Spacetime gravitational backflow!" Battling the howling gale, her hair whipping violently, Qing-Qing screamed at the top of her lungs, "It's trying to force the reset!"

"YOU WON'T DRAG US BACK, DAMN YOU————!"

Lunging half a step forward, his sneakers planted like iron pilings into the concrete, A-Shin squeezed Old Wu's and his neighbor's hands with every ounce of his soul!

Facing the blinding white tempest, eyes bloodshot and blazing, the boy unleashed an earth-shattering roar:

"CLASS 6-1! SHOUT IT OUT!"

Twenty-six young souls thrust their chests forward into the searing glare!

Not one looked back. Not one clung to this summer that had sheltered them for a month. Facing the vast, terrifying horizon of the unknown, they summoned every shred of courage, love, and fire within their beings, screaming in unison at the roaring cosmos—

"SEE YOU TOMORROW—————!!!!"

"SEE YOU TOMORROW, CLASS 6-1—————!!!!"

Twenty-six youthful, unshakeable voices converged into a celestial roar surpassing every physical law in the universe!

"CRACK—————!!!!"

A sound of crystalline perfection—resembling the shattering of sacred glass—exploded dead center against the blackboard!

In the heart of the infinite white glare and raging lightning, that vintage quartz clock mounted on the wall shuddered at the four o'clock mark for three agonizing seconds...

And then, before fifty-two wide, glistening eyes, under the relentless pull of the golden chain of causality—

The second hand leaped with a crisp, resolute metallic click:

"Tick."

It crossed the abyss that had held them captive for thirty grueling days.

It stepped into...

Four-O-One PM!

"BOOOOOOM——————!"

The colossal, translucent temporal barrier that had blanketed Luyang Elementary for a month shattered like an ancient glacier struck by a warhammer!

Billions of violet shards dissolved into a canopy of shimmering, crystalline stars, scattering upon the wind into the cosmos!

The raging lightning died in an instant; the howling tempests ceased without a whisper.

"Creak—"

The heavy iron doors of the South Academic Wing—padlocked, barred, and having catapulted them backward dozens of times—swung smoothly inward on a gentle afternoon breeze.

And then, the frozen world beyond the perimeter ignited with living breath as if touched by resurrection magic—

Out on the street, the exhaust pipe of that stationary red bus coughed a plume of white steam, its diesel engine rumbling as it accelerated down the asphalt;  
On the crosswalk, the woman holding the parasol planted her suspended foot firmly onto the pavement, continuing her stride across the avenue;  
The fallen leaf hovering above the curb tumbled twice in the air, coming to rest gently upon the warm, damp soil.

The blare of car horns, the cheerful ring of bicycle bells, the calls of grocers at the distant market, the chime of the corner convenience store door...

The vast, teeming symphony of human life rushed into the long-silent campus like a warm, comforting flood.

And in the heavens above, that violet storm that had raged for an eternity vanished without a trace, replaced by a radiant, majestic summer sunset that set the world ablaze in gold and crimson.

Golden-red sunlight poured unobstructed through the clean windowpanes, washing over the classroom of Class 6-1.

Inside, twenty-six youths remained hand in hand.

No one spoke.

A-Shin looked down at his wristwatch—July 7, 2026, 4:03 PM.

Old Wu looked down at his wristwatch—July 7, 2026, 4:03 PM.

Qing-Qing's oscilloscope read absolute zero.

Upon Wei-Xi's wrist, the Chronos Bangle had lost its otherworldly luminescence, returning to a simple, unadorned silver bracelet.

"Did we..."

Blinking, Old Wu's voice shook uncontrollably:

"Did we... cross over?"

Turning his head, A-Shin looked at the wide-open classroom door, at the sun-drenched corridor, at the cars racing past the far fence of the athletic field.

Two hot tears finally spilled over his lashes.

"We crossed over."

Sniffling, A-Shin bared his teeth in an ecstatic, fiercely proud grin:

"Brothers and sisters... today is finally over."

"Tomorrow... is finally here!"

"YEAHHHHHH—————!!!!!"

The classroom detonated into absolute, uninhibited pandemonium!

Twenty-six young souls leaped, hugged, wept, and laughed, hurling their test papers, worksheets, and notebooks high into the air!

Sheets of snowy-white paper danced like snowflakes through the golden summer sunset, each page inscribed with the invincible medal of honor they had bought with thirty days of their lives—a medal named "Growing Up"!

Before the podium, Teacher Gao retrieved his black-rimmed glasses, slipping them back onto the bridge of his nose.

The emerald directives behind his lenses quietly faded away. Standing tall amidst the swirling shower of white paper and radiant evening light, his hands folded before him, he smiled with unshakeable grace—the eternal, immortal guardian of their youth.
