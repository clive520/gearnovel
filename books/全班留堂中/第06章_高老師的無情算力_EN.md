# Chapter 6: Teacher Gao's Merciless Computing Power

Loop Day Seven.

7:59 AM.

A suffocating aura of profound, existential despair hung heavy in the air.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the twenty-six faces no longer bore the wild euphoria of the first day, nor the frantic irritability of Day Four; they had deteriorated entirely into an unearthly, transcendent numbness.

"Tick."

The second hand crossed the vertical zenith, the wall clock snapping to precisely 8:00 AM.

The familiar, textbook cadence of sixty-centimeter footsteps approached from the corridor outside. Teacher Gao nudged the door open, his suit impeccably pressed, his dark-framed spectacles reflecting an icy, unyielding sheen of absolute rationality in the dawn light.

"Class..."

Before Teacher Gao could utter a third syllable, a series of heartbreakingly mechanized movements unfolded in perfect synchronicity below—

Without expression, Old Wu thrust his left hand into empty space, calmly catching the air where Director Zhou's falling thermos was destined to arrive two minutes later; pencil in hand, A-Shin sketched out Zheng Chenggong's confectionery fortifications on a blank sheet of scratch paper with practiced fluidity; Class President Qing-Qing didn't even look up, her fingers blind-tapping circuit parameters into her multimeter.

The entire classroom resembled an antique clockwork mechanism that had completed thousands of rotations, its gears worn smooth, every brass cog numbly repeating its preordained trajectory.

"BANG!"

Right on cue, Director Zhou kicked open the door. Only this time, the iron-willed disciplinarian didn't even muster the energy to roar his opening line. He stared dully at his stainless-steel thermos, already caught and cradled flat in Old Wu's single outstretched palm; then he looked at the blackboard, where the chalk characters of 【Spacetime Comprehensive Review: Day One】 remained unchanged down to the final punctuation mark.

Director Zhou parted his lips, the jowls on his cheeks drooping flaccidly, an anguished wheeze escaping the depths of his throat like a punctured bicycle tire.

"Fine... you win... you little devils win..."

The Iron Judge silently reclaimed his thermos, his eyes hollow as he turned, drifting down the hallway like a wandering phantom.

The door squeaked shut.

"Seven days now." A-Shin slammed his forehead hard against the cool cypress desktop, running ten trembling fingers through his messy hair. "The exact same cicada drone, looping in my ears for one hundred and sixty-eight hours; the broken red brick at the corner of the hallway, examined over seven hundred times; the scent of ginseng and chrysanthemum tea from Old Zhou's thermos—with my eyes shut, I can smell that there are eight wolfberries and three chrysanthemum buds floating in that hot water!"

"And this..." Old Wu handled the half-eaten cold pork bun from his drawer with two shivering fingertips, treating it like a canister of weapons-grade radioactive waste.

The boy who once worshipped pork buns like sacred idols now had sunken eyes, a sallow complexion, and bloodless lips.

"Last night, I had a nightmare... I dreamed that every planet in the cosmos transformed into a giant mushroom pork bun, lining up across the galaxy to rain down on my head..." Old Wu let out an agonizing, dry retch. "I want vegetables... I want bitter melon... I'd even settle for that salted bitter melon stir-fry my mom makes that's salty enough to pickle an ox! Why does this stupid spacetime reset refuse to change the position of a single scallion flake inside this miserable bun?!"

"Because the deadlock circuit on my bangle is an exact topological mirror," Lin Wei-Xi lowered her head with deep contrition, gently brushing the cold alloy casing on her wrist. "Every reset forcibly overwrites the quantum state of every fundamental particle within this space, snapping them back to the exact snapshot recorded at 8:00 AM on July 7th. As long as time cannot advance, the physical state of this reality remains mathematically conserved."

"In other words," Class President Lin Yun-Qing set down her soldering iron, summarizing with clinical detachment, "unless we find a way to shatter this loop, Old Wu will consume one hundred billion identical mushroom pork buns across infinite iterations of time; and A-Shin will transcribe Zheng Chenggong's maritime defense tactics eighty thousand times upon this very desk."

"Just put me out of my misery!" A-Shin wailed, slumping backward against his chair. "Summon an alien invasion, bring on a giant kaiju, or have Old Zhou expel us on the spot! As long as I get to see tomorrow's sunrise, I swear I'll streak three laps naked around the track!"

"Student A-Shin, pursuant to school regulations and the Social Order Maintenance Act, streaking across school grounds warrants one major disciplinary demerit and mandatory parental counseling."

From the podium came Teacher Gao's measured, metallic baritone.

Unnoticed by the class, Teacher Gao had neatly stacked his lesson binders at the top-left corner of the desk. Nudging his dark-rimmed glasses, the once-dormant cascade of blue code behind his lenses suddenly surged with terrifying, high-frequency turbulence.

"Over the course of the past 168-hour temporal cycle, this homeroom teacher has performed continuous, deep neural-network modeling on the psychological states, learning efficiencies, and behavioral anomalies across all twenty-six students."

Stepping down from the podium, Teacher Gao came to a halt before the desks of A-Shin and Old Wu. His gaze was bottomless, like a black hole stretching into infinity:

"The telemetry indicates that collective anxiety has reached its peak threshold, escapist hedonic mechanisms have thoroughly collapsed, and the current psychological state has completely satisfied the objective criteria to enter the 【High-Pressure Breakthrough Critical State】."

"W-what critical state?" A-Shin's heart dropped, an ominous chill wrapping around his chest.

"Since you have thoroughly tasted the profound void of aimless leisure, and conclusively verified the absolute impossibility of physical spatial escape," Teacher Gao turned, sweeping his gaze across the room, the corners of his lips curving into that precision smile calibrated to three decimal places:

"—then, as the homeroom educator of Class 6-1 at Luyang Elementary, this machine deems that it is time to maximize the operational yield of our educational resources."

Teacher Gao swept his arm with commanding elegance.

"Clatter-snap—!"

The four steel cabinet doors beneath the teacher's desk—traditionally reserved for archived instructional materials—flung open simultaneously!

Before the horrified gazes of twenty-six students, twenty-six colossal reams of pristine white paper, each a foot thick and bound with precision staples, stood assembled before the podium like a miniature range of snowcapped mountains!

"This is the 【All-Discipline Extreme Inverse Deep Remediation Problem Bank】, personally compiled by this homeroom teacher last night... or more accurately, synthesized using idle computing cycles across the past seven temporal resets."

Teacher Gao's voice was so gentle it sent icy daggers into their marrow:

"The curriculum encompasses a total of twenty-four thousand comprehensive problems, methodically covering every conceivable conceptual blind spot, high-frequency error pattern, and interdisciplinary derivative across the six years of elementary school and three years of junior high."

"T-twenty-four thousand questions?!" Old Wu's eyes rolled back in his head, nearly fainting on the spot.

"Teacher Gao! Have you lost your mind?!" Slamming his palms onto his desk, A-Shin roared in righteous indignation, "Even if time is infinite, our brain tissue is made of organic meat! There are only eight hours in a day—how could anyone finish twenty-four thousand questions?!"

"That brings us precisely to the new instructional protocol this homeroom teacher is about to proclaim." Teacher Gao extended an index finger, drawing an immaculate golden holographic arc through the air:

"According to reverse telemetry computations executed against Student Lin Wei-Xi's bangle firmware, the core logic locking this spacetime manifold resides in the collective failure of our class during the transition assessment, which tripped the absolute deadlock within this machine's root code: 'Dismissal forbidden until proficiency thresholds are met.'"

"In other words," the blue glare behind Teacher Gao's lenses blazed with incandescent intensity, "the physical key to breaking this loop rests in Wei-Xi's hands; but the logical key to shattering this prison lies at the tips of your pencils."

The classroom plunged into absolute silence. Qing-Qing snapped her head up; a flash of profound awe rippled through Wei-Xi's eyes.

"Commencing today, within this very temporal reset," Teacher Gao surveyed the twenty-six stunned faces before him, his tone heavy with sovereign authority:

"Class 6-1 formally initiates the 【Merciless Computation Infinite Remediation Protocol】! We run no longer; we cease passive resignation, waiting for the 4:00 PM bell. Every day from eight to four, all personnel will engage in extreme question sprints and real-time big-data remediation!"

"Until when?!" A-Shin's voice trembled.

Teacher Gao's gaze struck like a tempered blade, piercing deep into the eyes of every youth:

"Until all twenty-six students, across random testing pulled from these twenty-four thousand problems—simultaneously, without a single exception—attain a flawless score of one hundred percent!"

Silence.

An absolute, crushing silence devoured the entire chamber.

No one protested; no one cheered. Only the eternal, manic drone of cicadas outside the windowpane thrummed violently beneath the summer sun.

Twenty-six mischievous rascals who had treated examinations as their mortal enemy for years finally saw, with crystal clarity, the pure, unyielding obsession burning within this android teacher standing before them in his immaculate suit—an obsession that defied time, logic, and the laws of the cosmos.

He was not torturing them.

He was harnessing the infinite expanse of his computing power and the endless ocean of time to force them to be reborn from the fire.

A-Shin inhaled deeply. Within those eyes once filled with impish mischief, all frivolous restlessness burned away for the very first time, replaced by a feral, unyielding resolve born of being backed against the edge of a precipice.

"A hundred percent across the board, is it..." Snatching the sweat-stained mechanical pencil from his desk, A-Shin bared his teeth in a cold, defiant grin. "Teacher Gao, don't you dare regret this. The day we actually memorize every single one of these twenty-four thousand questions—even if you want to keep us in detention, you won't be able to hold us back!"

Standing tall before the podium, the progress bars behind Teacher Gao's glasses whirred with dazzling velocity. The smile upon his lips finally carried a sliver of genuine, human pride:

"This homeroom teacher awaits your triumph. The clock starts now—Volume One: Classical Mechanics and Polynomial Algebra. Distribute the papers!"

Sheets of snowy-white paper cascaded across the desks like an avalanche.

The scratching rustle of lead carving into cellulose rose in unison, drowning out the cicadas outside, piercing through the deadlock of spacetime, playing an epic, soaring rhapsody of defiance against merciless fate within this isolated classroom on the third floor!
