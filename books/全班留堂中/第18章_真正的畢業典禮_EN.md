# Chapter 18: The True Graduation Ceremony

The following day, July 8, 2026.

7:40 AM.

This was a genuine, brand-new, post-reset tomorrow.

The heavens no longer carried that spectral, violet gloom; instead, an immaculate canopy of azure blue stretched across the horizon, resembling a freshly polished sapphire. The morning breeze rustled through the branches of the chinaberry trees, carrying the earthy perfume of soil, fresh lawn clippings, and the mouth-watering scent of scallion egg pancakes sizzling at a distant breakfast cart.

By the main gate of Luyang Elementary, several yawning lower-grade campus monitors were sweeping up fallen banyan leaves with bamboo brooms.

"Tap, tap, tap."

Approaching down the central tree-lined promenade came that imposing silhouette that struck mortal dread into the marrow of every pupil on campus.

Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan was clad in an immaculately pressed short-sleeved white dress shirt, his oxfords polished to a mirror sheen, his signature telephone-directory-thick disciplinary registry tucked firmly under his arm, his other hand cradling that ten-centimeter-wide stainless-steel thermos.

Halting before the entrance of the South Academic Wing, Director Zhou looked up at that all-too-familiar classroom on the third floor, subconsciously massaging his temples.

"How bizarre..."

Director Zhou muttered to the morning air:

"I went to bed early last night, yet I feel like I've been trapped in an endless, month-long fever dream... A dream filled with mind-reading brats, infinite flying thermoses, and an ungodly mountain of airborne mushroom pork buns..."

Director Zhou shook his head vigorously, banishing the absurd, surreal hallucinations from his skull.

"Zhou Yan, oh Zhou Yan, the occupational stress is getting to you. Today is Day Two of that den of rascals' mandatory summer detention—you must bring your absolute Grade-A administrative thunder to knock them down a peg!"

Steeling his expression, Director Zhou took a deep breath, mounting the stairwell with thunderous, authoritarian strides toward the third floor.

7:59 AM.

Director Zhou arrived before the door of Class 6-1. Drawing a deep lungful of air, the muscles in his right leg tensed, poised to execute his signature "Iron Executioner Flying Kick" to blast the wooden door ajar—

Yet a single centimeter before his boot struck the wood, Director Zhou's frame arrested mid-motion.

For reasons he could not fathom, gazing upon the weathered grain of that aged wood panel, an inexplicable, tender ache rippled through his heart.

"Cough... Never mind. As an educator of youth, one must maintain an air of dignified scholarly restraint."

Retracting his leg, Director Zhou cleared his throat with subtle self-consciousness, before extending his right hand to turn the doorknob politely.

8:00 AM sharp.

Morning light poured through the clean glass windowpanes, saturating the pristine, sun-drenched room in warm golden hues.

Behind the podium, Teacher Gao stood in his three-piece charcoal-grey suit, his dark spectacles catching the gentle morning rays, hands clasped formally before his waist. Perched atop his left shoulder, the AI companion Liu-Liu—sporting a micro black academic mortarboard—flicked its metallic tail with buoyant cheer.

Below, twenty-six student desks were aligned with geometric, laser-sharp precision.

Twenty-six young boys and girls sat clad in crisp dark-blue graduation blazers, vibrant scarlet flame-tree corsages pinned proudly to their lapels, spines as straight as rebar, hands resting flat upon their desks.

No one had their legs crossed. No one was concealing a cold pork bun. No one made grotesque faces or preempted his opening monologue.

Twenty-six pairs of clear, radiant eyes—stripped bare of all juvenile restlessness, cowardice, and spite—gazed upon the elder standing in the doorway with an ocean of profound reverence, affection, and gratitude.

Director Zhou froze on the threshold, the thermos in his grip nearly slipping to the floor once more.

These... were these really the same hell-raising delinquents who had driven student teachers into mountain hermit retreats and humiliated him thirty times over the past term?!

"Director Zhou, the time is precisely 8:00 AM. Good morning to you."

Teacher Gao was the first to speak, his deep, resonant baritone echoing softly across the serene chamber.

And then, before Director Zhou's stunned gaze, Teacher Gao reached beneath the podium, lifting a foot-thick sheaf of pristine white documents bound immaculately with an ornamental crimson silk ribbon.

"Pursuant to intensive remediation executed across our student cohort over the course of... ahem, over the course of yesterday's curriculum, all personnel have concluded the 【All-Discipline Twenty-Four Thousand Comprehensive Problem Bank】 ahead of schedule."

Teacher Gao presented the heavy dossier level across his palms, stepping forward to offer it to Director Zhou:

"These are the certified examination scores from our collective retake. Across all twenty-six students, the class-wide grade point average stands at—precisely one hundred percent."

With trembling fingers, Director Zhou accepted the colossal, heavy dossier.

He turned the first page—it was A-Shin's paper.

Dense geometric proofs and mechanics force diagrams, brushed in handwriting as immaculate as mechanical typography, its problem-solving logic so rigorous that a university department chair could not deduct a fraction of a point.

He turned to the second page—Old Wu's comprehensive science exam.

Beneath Question 17, which asked for "physical phenomena and energy transformation when heating water to 100 degrees Celsius"—where "pork and cabbage dumplings" once reigned—the lines were crammed with molecular kinetic equations, latent heat of vaporization constants, and the rigorous mathematical expressions of the First Law of Thermodynamics.

Page three, page four, page twenty-six...

Every solitary page was drenched in the sacred, burning blood and sweat of youth—immaculately executed, without a single error, without a single smudge of correction fluid.

"T-this... how is this humanly possible..."

Director Zhou murmured to himself, an involuntary crimson blur stinging the corners of his eyes.

He remembered that feverish dream from last night—remembered that green tin biscuit box unearthed from the subterranean shelter, remembered that buzz-cut boy from thirty years ago who had coated the principal's door in glue and bred loaches in the air ducts.

Slowly lifting his gaze, Director Zhou's eyes locked onto A-Shin in the front row.

A-Shin wore no mischievous smirk; he offered Director Zhou a smile of crystalline, pure, and incandescent light.

"Director Zhou," A-Shin rose, his voice ringing with crystalline pride, "over these thirty days... ahem, over this past term, thank you for your unyielding discipline. We learned our lessons. We truly learned every single one of them."

Director Zhou's lips spasmed violently.

The iron-willed titan who had brandished the disciplinary rod over Luyang Elementary for thirty years broke down completely in this moment, his accumulated tears collapsing the dam of his composure without defense.

Yanking that telephone-directory-thick master disciplinary registry from beneath his arm—

Before fifty-two wide, astonished eyes, Director Zhou clamped his callused, chalk-stained hands onto the twenty-six pages scrawled with the infractions of A-Shin, Old Wu, Qing-Qing, and the rest, wrenching them with colossal, furious leverage—

"RRRRIIIIIP—————!"

Accompanied by the glorious, sharp roar of tearing paper, twenty-six full pages of black-and-red disciplinary citations were torn violently clean down the middle!

And then a second tear! A third!

Snowy-white and crimson confetti cascaded down like festival streamers in the morning sunlight, showering the floor of the podium in glorious celebration!

"Director?!" Old Wu gasped in utter shock.

"All of it, torn to shreds!"

Wiping the wetness from his face, Director Zhou's voice was hoarse and broken, yet rang with unprecedented, roaring magnanimity:

"I have served as Student Affairs Director for thirty years! I have written hundreds of thousands of disciplinary citations in my life! These twenty-six pages... are the most exhilarating, glorious citations I have ever torn to pieces in my entire existence!"

Sniffling, Director Zhou snapped his stooped spine straight. Facing the twenty-six students before him, he bowed with immense, majestic reverence:

"Students of Luyang Elementary, 12th Graduating Class... no, the 68th Cohort of Class 6-1!"

Lifting his tear-stained face, Director Zhou proclaimed to the heavens:

"Your summer detention... is officially concluded! This office formally decrees: all of you—are granted unconditional graduation!"

"YEAHHHHHHH—————!!!!!"

An earth-shattering roar of triumph detonated across the South Academic Wing!

Twenty-six young souls leaped from their seats, hurling every textbook, binder, and pencil case high into the rafters!

This time, it was not to celebrate escaping homework—it was to celebrate the true, transcendent graduation that belonged to them, won across an eternity of looped spacetime!

"Let's move! Move out!"

Slinging his backpack over one shoulder, A-Shin hooked an arm around Old Wu's neck:

"Old Wu! Let's go! To the commissary! Today's treat is on me—I'm buying out the entire steamer basket of pork buns! Two for everybody!"

"Bwahahaha! Let's go! Time for pork buns!"

Like young dragons diving into the open sea, laughing and shouting, the students poured out the classroom doors, pounding down the concrete stairs with light, buoyant strides, tumbling onto the vast athletic field bathed in radiant morning light.

Peace settled once more within the room.

Teacher Gao walked to the casement window, resting his palms upon the sill, gazing quietly down upon the silhouettes of the children racing toward the rising sun across the track.

A-Shin was running; Old Wu was running; Qing-Qing and Wei-Xi were running hand in hand.

Their laughter pierced through the morning breeze, drifting toward the bustling avenues of the city beyond, carrying toward that brand-new, terrifying, yet magnificent world waiting to be explored.

"Teacher Gao."

Standing beside him, dabbing at his eyes with a handkerchief, Director Zhou asked softly:

"Tell me... once these rascals reach junior high, will they truly be able to withstand the howling storms of the world outside?"

Teacher Gao adjusted his dark-rimmed glasses, the morning sun casting a profound, warm brilliance across the lenses.

The bionic android smiled faintly, his metallic baritone holding the deepest, most unshakeable faith an educator could ever harbor for his children:

"Director Zhou, you need not harbor the slightest fear."

"Across the longest day of their lives, they learned how to hold tight to one another's hands."

"These children... have already forged an invincible armor capable of weathering any tempest."

Sunlight illuminated the blackboard, where six bold chalk characters gleamed in the dawn:

【See You Tomorrow, Class 6-1】.

A gentle breeze swept through, the scarlet flame-tree blossoms ablaze like living fire.

Tomorrow had arrived; and their journey had only just begun.
