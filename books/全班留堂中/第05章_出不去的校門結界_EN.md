# Chapter 5: The Impassable Schoolgate Barrier

Loop Day Four.

8:02 AM.

When Director Zhou kicked open the classroom door like a wound-up automaton once more, hoisting the test papers aloft to bellow "Absurd! Shameless!", the twenty-six students below didn't even bother to lift their heads.

The class had developed a proficiency that was heartbreaking to behold—

Without glancing up, Old Wu dropped to one knee and snatched Director Zhou's slipping thermos out of mid-air behind his back; sprawled across his desk, eyes shut tight, A-Shin recited the open-ended answer regarding Zheng Chenggong and water-grass spiders in a monotone drone devoid of human emotion; Class President Qing-Qing, without even glancing at her multimeter, flatly reported the historic tragedy of the class's 31.4-point average.

Clutching his retrieved thermos, staring at this room of drained husks endowed with terrifying clairvoyance, Director Zhou shuddered, took half a step backward, pulled the door shut, and fled down the corridor in dead silence.

"That's three times now..." A-Shin slammed his face into the wooden desktop, letting out a soul-weary groan. "That is the third consecutive morning I've had to stare at Old Zhou's coal-black face. Brothers and sisters, I've had it. I have genuinely had it!"

"Me too..." Old Wu shoved that automatically respawned half-eaten cold pork bun to the furthest corner of his drawer, his eyes haunted by post-traumatic terror. "Yesterday afternoon, Teacher Gao actually forced me to grind through three hundred systems of linear equations! If I have to do that again today, my brain is going to detonate like a raw egg in a microwave!"

"We cannot just sit here and wait to be slaughtered." A-Shin snapped his spine straight, the embers of rebellious defiance reigniting within his eyes. "Since staying in this room means death by a thousand test questions at the hands of Teacher Gao, we only have one singular objective—escape!"

"Escape?" Seated in the front row, Class President Lin Yun-Qing arched an eyebrow, pushing her glasses up. "Under school regulations, unauthorized departure during summer remedial instruction warrants two minor demerits. Even if spacetime wipes the slate clean at four o'clock, how do you plan to evade Teacher Gao's line of sight? His bionic dynamic visual suite tracks all moving targets within a fifty-meter radius simultaneously."

"Heh-heh, that's where our secret weapon comes in!" A-Shin jerked his chin toward the corner of the podium.

Perched at the ventilation shaft, the AI pet Liu-Liu held three "micro-infrared smoke jamming pellets"—improvised from discarded capacitors and aluminum foil—firmly in its jaws.

"Teacher Gao performs a routine server cache purge every forty-five minutes, which lasts precisely sixty seconds," A-Shin's eyes sparkled with shrewd calculation. "The moment his optics cycle down for the reboot, Liu-Liu detonates the smoke screen. We split into three squads and bolt straight for the perimeter! Once our feet cross that front gate, we catch city buses back to our own beds! I'd like to see spacetime try dragging us back from under our own blankets!"

9:44 AM.

Right on cue, Teacher Gao paused his chalk on the board. His eyes closed, and faint green loading auras pulsed from the hinges of his spectacles—the system cache purge had commenced!

"Execute!" A-Shin hissed.

Flipping down from the ceiling, Liu-Liu whipped its tail, flicking the three improvised pellets dead center into the metal trash bin before the podium!

"Sizzle—BANG!"

A thick, electrically charged cloud of dense white smoke detonated across the podium, thoroughly blinding Teacher Gao's optical array!

"Evacuate! All units, retreat!"

With A-Shin spearheading the charge, twenty-six students bolted like wild birds escaping a cage, flying down the exterior fire escape stairs, not even pausing to tie trailing shoelaces.

Within three minutes, the vanguard crossed the vast, empty athletic field and reached Stage One of the operation—the grand front gates of Luyang Elementary.

Yet the scene before their eyes caused everyone to freeze in their tracks.

The ornamental wrought-iron gates, ordinarily thrown wide open, were sealed shut. Even stranger was the street beyond—ordinarily a bustling artery of traffic—which appeared entirely frozen in time:

A red city bus sat motionless in the middle of the road, the grey exhaust from its tailpipe solidified like a stationary puff of cotton candy; on the pedestrian zebra crossing, a woman holding a parasol was mid-stride, one foot suspended motionless in the air; even a dry banyan leaf tumbling in the wind hovered thirty centimeters above the asphalt, entirely frozen.

The world beyond the gates was completely paralyzed.

"W-what on earth is this?!" Old Wu's knees knocked together in sheer terror.

"Time outside the perimeter is frozen," Lin Wei-Xi stepped forward briskly, looking down at the pulsing digits on her wrist, her expression grave. "The spacetime fold generated by the bangle has a diameter of only three hundred meters, wrapping exclusively around the boundary walls of Luyang Elementary. Our school... is an isolated island drifting within a frozen ocean of time."

"Who cares if it's frozen! As long as we can get out, it doesn't matter!" Gritting his teeth, A-Shin pointed at the towering iron fence. "Form a human ladder! Over the top, now!"

Burly Old Wu stepped forward to brace his footing. Planting his sneakers on Old Wu's shoulders, A-Shin scrambled nimbly up to the spiked metal crossbeam at the top of the gate.

"Made it! No security patrols outside!" A-Shin hollered in triumph, launching himself outward toward the pavement below!

Yet the exact millisecond A-Shin's torso crossed the vertical plane of the boundary fence—

"HUMMMM—!"

A violent, high-frequency electromagnetic tremor detonated through the empty air!

A translucent, ring-shaped ripple shimmering with pale violet-blue luminescence radiated outward like a shockwave across water!

Mid-air, A-Shin slammed violently into an invisible, yet staggering elastic barrier!

"Sweet mother of mercy!"

A-Shin felt as though he had plunged headlong into a colossal trampoline woven from tens of thousands of heavy-duty rubber bands.

Before his brain could process the impact, the barrier unleashed a monstrous, violent recoil!

"WHOOSH—SNAP!"

Before the stunned gazes of twenty-odd classmates, A-Shin transformed into a supersonic human blur, propelled backward into the sky at unbelievable velocity, flying along his exact entry trajectory in reverse like a rewinding VHS tape!

"CRASH—BANG!"

A deafening clatter echoed from the third-floor classroom.

Standing at the front gates, the students exchanged terrified glances before sprinting frantically back toward the South Academic Wing.

Kicking open the rear door, they found A-Shin seated pin-straight in his assigned desk, both hands still maintaining the posture of clutching a pencil. His hair stood vertically on end, his eyes were glassy and vacant, his cheeks flapping from the supersonic G-forces, while that unfinished calculus exam lay neatly before him.

"A... A-Shin? Are you still alive?" Trembling, Old Wu prodded A-Shin's paralyzed shoulder with a finger.

A-Shin turned his neck with mechanical stiffness. His lips pale, he coughed out a puff of white friction smoke:

"T-that barrier... is way too bouncy... I think... I just saw my great-grandmother waving at me from the clouds..."

"This is impossible!" several stubborn boys roared in defiance. "If the front gate won't work, what about the rear mountain wall?! What about the chain-link fence behind the incinerator?!"

Over the next two hours, Class 6-1 waged the most heroic, yet utterly farcical "Great Escape Marathon" in recorded history—

Squads attempting to scale the red brick wall behind the gym were catapulted the instant their fingers crested the parapet, ricocheting through the air to land face-first in the sandpit of the long jump track;

Infiltrators crawling through the kitchen drainage culvert found their skulls vacuumed backward the moment they poked through the grate, zipping in reverse straight into their classroom seats;

Old Wu even procured two giant fiberglass pole-vaulting poles, attempting to launch himself clean over the perimeter. The moment his mass cleared the wall, a burst of light flared, and his spherical frame—resembling a precision-guided meat projectile—shot straight down the corridor, lodging itself securely beneath the wooden teacher's desk.

1:00 PM.

All twenty-six students, without a single exception, sat battered, bruised, and covered in soot, lined up immaculately in their assigned desks.

The smoke at the front of the room had long since cleared.

Teacher Gao had smoothed his suit back into pristine alignment, holding a felt eraser, methodically wiping stray chalk dust from the board.

The bionic android turned around, the fluorescent tubes reflecting a glare of flawless intellect across his dark frames.

"Students, monitoring records of microwave reflections along the campus perimeter indicate that over the past 120 minutes, our class executed a total of thirty-eight unauthorized breach attempts. These include twenty-one wall scales, nine culvert crawls, and eight pole vaults. The collective success rate for permanent spatial displacement stands at—precisely zero."

Setting the eraser down, Teacher Gao folded his hands before his waist, delivering the final verdict with calm, metallic serenity:

"The elasticity modulus of this temporal perimeter is governed by the topological parameters of Student Lin Wei-Xi's bangle. The moment any matter's kinematic trajectory crosses the closed boundary of this campus, it triggers the 【Spacetime Causal Conservation Recoil】."

"In other words," Teacher Gao smiled faintly—a smile that, in the eyes of a black-and-blue A-Shin, appeared far more terrifying than any demon:

"While physically accessible, along the dimensions of time and space, this school is an absolute, inescapable closed-loop prison."

A-Shin let his head drop limply onto his desk, staring at the endless exam before him, the final spark of defiance snuffed out entirely.

No way out.

No escape.

Within this tiny pocket of reality abandoned by time, the only reality awaiting them was that unceasing, merciless computational grind, and... the inevitable nervous breakdown looming on Day Seven.
