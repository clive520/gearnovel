# Chapter 16: The True Cipher of the Spacetime Anchor

Loop Day Thirty.

3:20 PM.

In the skies above, that bank of violet thunderclouds that had hovered for an entire month presented an unprecedented, surreal spectacle.

The heavy, inky-purple leaden clouds no longer bore their usual suffocating menace; instead, as if guided by an invisible celestial tide, they rotated gently above Luyang Elementary, fracturing to reveal cascading shafts of radiant golden sunlight.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, that chalk inscription that had occupied the center of the board for a month—【Spacetime Comprehensive Review: Day One】—had been quietly erased, replaced by six bold, immaculate Chinese characters:

【See You Tomorrow, Class 6-1】.

Before the podium, Class President Lin Yun-Qing knelt upon the floor, the mini oscilloscope beside her humming with a steady, tranquil low-frequency pitch.

"Anchor Number Three... The resonance circuit of the century-old clock tower collapsed naturally at 2:40 PM."

Nudging her glasses, Qing-Qing pointed toward the holographic wireframe, where the pulsing purple beacons had almost entirely vanished:

"The antique bronze clock in the Principal's Office, the time capsule in the bunker, the clock tower over the main quad... All three physical spacetime tent pegs have been completely dismantled. As of this moment, only one final node sustains the temporal barrier of this campus."

Every pair of eyes turned simultaneously toward Lin Wei-Xi, who stood dead center before the podium.

The golden light of 3:00 PM slanted across Wei-Xi's simple white blouse, the breeze gently stirring her dark hair.

Slowly, she raised her left wrist.

The Chronos Bangle—which had short-circuited in the storm, flashing scarlet warning codes for thirty grueling days—had undergone a breathtaking transformation.

The harsh, icy crimson glow had receded completely, replaced by a warm, jade-like luminescence rippling in gentle hues of pale pink and liquid gold. The digital countdown previously deadlocked at 【07:59:59】 had dissolved into soft, undulating ripples of concentric circles pulsing at an unhurried, serene frequency.

"Wei-Xi, that fourth spacetime anchor... where is it actually hidden?" A-Shin stepped forward, asking softly.

Over the past twenty-nine cycles, A-Shin had led the class in turning the entire school upside down—they had crawled through the rafters of the auditorium, searched the old custodian's storage shed, and even inspected the mortar seams beneath the flagpole with magnifying glasses, yet found not a trace of a fourth physical anchor.

Wei-Xi turned around, her clear, crystalline eyes gazing with boundless tenderness upon the twenty-five classmates gathered around her.

The corners of the girl's eyes curved slightly, offering a smile glistening with faint tears, yet radiant enough to steal one's breath away:

"The fourth anchor was never hidden in any physical corner of this school."

Extending her right index finger, Wei-Xi gently tapped the school badge pinned to A-Shin's chest, before pointing in turn toward Qing-Qing, Old Wu, and every boy and girl standing behind them:

"The fourth anchor... is right here. Within the beating hearts of every single one of us."

A collective murmur of awe rippled through the room.

"In the future world from which this apparatus came, the architects who engineered the Chronos Bangle inscribed a foundational annotation in its root core," Wei-Xi's voice was as soft as a summer evening breeze soothing an open wound. "Time is not constructed from the ticking of second hands; it is governed by the 'density of human emotion.'"

"Thirty days ago, when that hundred-thousand-volt lightning bolt struck this campus, if our hearts had held only terror and selfish cowardice, the bangle would have incinerated from catastrophic overload in an instant."

Looking at the gentle golden luminescence upon her wrist, tears welled in Wei-Xi's eyes:

"Yet that day, A-Shin tackled Qing-Qing and me to the ground to shield us; Old Wu used his own back to block flying shards of shattered glass; not a single one of the twenty-six students in this room abandoned their comrades to flee, choosing instead to grip one another's hands."

"In that split second, the emotional density radiated by the twenty-six of us surpassed every theoretical energy threshold known to physical science. The bangle's core protocols determined that we existed in an eternal state designated as 'Absolute Sincerity.'"

Inhaling deeply, Wei-Xi looked out at the storm clouds being cleaved apart by golden sunlight:

"It believed we desired to remain in this day forever."

"Therefore, it transformed into an impenetrable sanctuary, sheltering us from the howling tempests of the outside world, hitting the pause button on our lives, guarding us like pearls locked within a shell through this tranquil, untouched summer."

"Except it didn't understand..." Sniffling fiercely, Old Wu rubbed his crimson nose, bellowing at the top of his lungs, "...that when pearls grow up, they have to leave the oyster shell to explore the open ocean!"

"Damn right!" Wiping the wetness from his face, a magnificent, untamed fire blazed within A-Shin's eyes. "Eating pork buns every day makes you sick; staying in Class 6-1 forever is glorious, but if that joy is bought by running away like cowards from tomorrow, then I'd rather go to junior high and stand in the hallway being punished!"

"We aren't afraid anymore!"

The boys in the back row thrust their arms high into the air, while the girls linked their fingers tightly, twenty-six hearts beating with wild, synchronized resonance:

"We want to see what our new junior high looks like!"  
"I want to take that next math exam—I can even solve calculus derivatives now!"  
"Even if we end up in different classrooms or different schools, we are forever the invincible Class 6-1 of Luyang Elementary!"

"HUMMMM————!"

As those fierce, courageous vows echoed across the rafters, the Chronos Bangle upon Wei-Xi's wrist detonated into a blinding, celestial brilliance!

It was no violent lightning, nor cold cascades of data.

It was a sacred, golden luminescence as warm and majestic as the dawn of a new era!

Originating from Wei-Xi's wrist, the light flowed like a golden river, coursing across A-Shin's palms, rippling across Qing-Qing's shoulders, warming Old Wu's chest, transforming in a heartbeat into an unbroken golden chain of causality linking all twenty-six youths, Teacher Gao, and the AI pet Liu-Liu perched upon the podium!

Across the oscilloscope, the remaining 12 percent barrier integrity plummeted in freefall—

Eight percent... four percent... 0.5 percent!

Standing tall before the podium, Teacher Gao's three-piece charcoal-grey suit was bathed in the holy glow of the golden light.

The bionic processing core within his chest emitted a resonant, harmonious chord. All safeguard protocols, all emergency override ciphers across his spectacle lenses dissolved into an emerald-green graduation directive:

【Fourth Spacetime Anchor successfully decrypted: Emotional Resonance 100%.】  
【Temporal Barrier fully dissipated.】  
【Chronos Bangle secondary power depleted; entering permanent dormancy.】  
【Objective chronological flow will resume normal progression in 180 seconds.】

Lifting his gaze, Teacher Gao looked upon these children who, across thirty iterations of trial by fire, had stripped away all childish fragility, forging spirits of hardened steel tempered by profound, tender love.

The robotic educator who had stood sentinel over them for a full month slowly raised his right hand, removing the dark-rimmed glasses from his nose, tucking them into the breast pocket of his suit.

Stripped of the lenses' barrier, those clear, profound, starry eyes were presented without reservation to his students for the very first time.

Upon Teacher Gao's lips bloomed the most relaxed, tender, and proud smile he had ever worn since arriving at Luyang Elementary:

"Students, the comprehensive problem bank of twenty-four thousand questions, alongside the ultimate life curriculum known as 'Parting and Maturation'—"

Teacher Gao bowed deeply, offering the young boys and girls before him a solemn, majestic graduation salute:

"—Class 6-1, all twenty-six students, have passed with flawless, perfect scores."

Straightening his spine, Teacher Gao pointed toward the vintage quartz clock above the blackboard, whose second hand was advancing toward four o'clock sharp:

"Now, clasp one another's hands tight."

"Go forth and claim what belongs to you..."

"...your Four-O-One PM!"
