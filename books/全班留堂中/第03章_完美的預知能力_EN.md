# Chapter 3: The Flawless Precognition

8:02 AM.

Outside the window, the cicadas still droned like chainsaws, while the campus remained so dead quiet one could hear the flap of a sparrow's wings.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, A-Shin froze in the posture of straightening his spine from his desk, the corner of his eye twitching violently as he stared at Teacher Gao on the podium.

"Wait a second... Teacher Gao, what did you just say?" A-Shin swallowed hard, pointing at the completely blank chalkboard. "You're saying... it's eight o'clock in the morning right now?! What about that giant purple thunderstorm that nearly charred us to a crisp? What about that wretched clock striking four?!"

"Pursuant to high-precision temporal records logged by this machine's... ahem, by this homeroom teacher's internal cesium atomic clock," Teacher Gao adjusted his suit cuffs with unruffled composure, the eyes behind his lenses as calm as still water, "the current calibrated time is July 7, 2026, 8:02:11 AM. External sensory modules detect zero physical traces of precipitation or electrical discharge. As for the scenario you described, neuroscience categorizes it as a 'transient collective hypnagogic hallucination.'"

"Hallucination my foot!" Old Wu shrieked, hoisting the half-eaten cold pork bun above his head as if offering up an heirloom treasure. "I distinctly took only one bite out of this bun before leaving home this morning! During fourth period, I swallowed the entire thing whole! Why is it lying in my desk drawer good as new right now?! Does this bun grow its own meat back?!"

Seated in the front row, Lin Yun-Qing took a sharp breath, her fingers tapping briskly across her mini digital multimeter.

"No, this is absolutely no hallucination," Qing-Qing's voice was grim to the point of clinical chill. Turning toward Lin Wei-Xi beside her, she continued, "The instant the lightning strike occurred, the spatial dielectric constant inside this classroom spiked seven thousand-fold. More crucially..."

Qing-Qing pulled up Wei-Xi's left wrist, displaying the Chronos Bangle pulsing with faint purple countdown digits for all to see:

"The bangle's internal timestamp is frozen at 【07:57:42】. If that was all a dream, the spacetime logic circuits on Wei-Xi's bangle could never be in this state of blown-fuse deadlock!"

The classroom erupted in an instant, twenty-six young faces flushed with terror and utter disbelief.

"Tap, tap, tap."

Just then, a familiar, heavy cadence of footsteps echoed from the far end of the corridor.

The dull thuds of leather soles striking aged terrazzo flooring carried a blood-curdling aura of disciplinary slaughter.

"Beep-beep! Alert! Alert!" Perched in the corner of the podium, the AI pet Liu-Liu bristled its metallic ears, its electronic eyes flashing crimson pulses. "The Iron Judge has entered the ten-meter perimeter! Target is approaching rapidly, bearing high-fatality paper-based ordnance!"

A-Shin's pupils dilated instantly as he shot up from his seat: "Those footsteps... they're identical to earlier!"

"Even Liu-Liu's broadcast warning matches word for word!" Class President Qing-Qing pushed her glasses up with lightning speed.

"Wait... don't tell me..." Old Wu's teeth began to chatter in dread. "In a second, Old Zhou is going to barge in holding thirty pages of test scores, slam the podium so hard it splinters, and roar: 'Absurd! Shameless! This is the darkest day in the sixty-eight-year history of Luyang Elementary,' right?!"

The corners of A-Shin's mouth curled into his signature devilish smirk, an ecstatic glint of pure mischief igniting within his eyes.

"Brothers and sisters, if this isn't a dream, but today is literally replaying from scratch..." A-Shin snapped his fingers with a crisp pop, hissing in a conspiratorial whisper to the class, "doesn't that mean we know every single move Old Zhou is about to make before he makes it?!"

The students exchanged glances. The terror that had suffocated the room mere seconds ago vanished, thoroughly replaced by an intoxicating rush of unprecedented excitement!

"Quick! Back to your seats! All units enter Grade-A Oscar-level acting mode!" At A-Shin's command, twenty-six students dropped into their chairs in a flash, spines straightening stiffer than reinforced rebar.

"Three... two... one..." A-Shin counted down silently in his head.

"BANG—!"

The classroom door of Class 6-1 was violently kicked open by a heavy foot clad in dark black leather!

Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan strode inside, his complexion dark as coal, radiating pure wrath. His right hand clutched the thirty-page assessment summary in a death grip, while his left held a giant stainless-steel thermos filled with piping hot chrysanthemum and wolfberry tea.

Director Zhou stormed before the podium. Inhaling deeply, his chest heaving, his right arm reared high, preparing to bring the exam stack crashing down upon the cypress desk—

Yet half a second before his arm could descend!

"Smack!"

With miraculous, telepathic synchronicity, all twenty-six students brought both hands down onto their desks, unleashing a unified, deafening crack that shook the room!

Immediately following, before Director Zhou's stunned gaze, the entire class bellowed in unison, matching his exact cadence, pitch, and veins-bulging fury:

"Absurd! Shameless! This is unequivocally the darkest, most disgraceful day in the sixty-eight-year history of Luyang Elementary School!"

Director Zhou froze on the spot.

His right hand hung suspended mid-air, the test papers nearly slipping from his grasp. His spectacles slid two full centimeters down his nose, revealing wide, bulging eyes brimming with profound shock and bewilderment. His jaw hung slack, the fleshy folds along his cheeks twitching violently.

This... wasn't this the exact opening monologue he had practiced before his bathroom mirror for half an hour last night?! How on earth could this den of rascals recite it word for word, syllable for syllable?!

"Cough! Y-you lot..." Director Zhou finally dredged his voice up from his throat, his face flushing from coal-black to bruised purple.

Determined to reassert his authority, Director Zhou decided to bypass the preamble and target an individual culprit!

His stubby finger stabbed toward Old Wu dead center in the second row, about to roar out the boy's name—

"Wu Chen-Kai! Get on your feet!"

Only this time, the voice did not belong to Director Zhou—it came from A-Shin in the back row! A-Shin even nailed the gravelly, phlegm-choked rasp of the director's delivery with terrifying fidelity!

Old Wu rose with practiced sorrow, a cold pork bun wedged in his cheek, his face a portrait of tragic theatrical grief.

Director Zhou was thoroughly unhinged. Subconsciously, he looked down at the exam stack, frantically trying to flip to Question 17 to utterly humiliate them—

"Director, were you looking for Question 17 in the science section?" Old Wu preempted him smoothly, his delivery flowing like the multiplication tables. "The prompt reads: Identify the physical phenomenon produced when heating water to 100 degrees Celsius, and briefly describe the energy transformation process."

Director Zhou's jaw virtually unhinged.

"Answer as follows!" Puffing out his chest, Old Wu recited at the top of his lungs: "When the water boils, you can drop in the pork and cabbage dumplings! The energy transformation process is turning raw dumplings into cooked dumplings, which then transform into thermal energy in your stomach!"

"Pffft—Bwahahahaha!"

The classroom could contain itself no longer. Over twenty kids erupted into earth-shattering roars of laughter.

Director Zhou felt the synapses in his brain snapping one by one.

He was seeing ghosts! In broad daylight, this was an unmitigated supernatural possession!

"Lu Li-Shin... you..." Trembling, Director Zhou shifted his gaze toward A-Shin, a flicker of genuine dread surfacing in his eyes.

"Reporting, Director! Zheng Chenggong ordered the planting of popping candy and water-grass spiders along both flanks of the Lu'ermen waterway, intending to bog down the Qing cannons in the mud, while dispatching squirrel couriers to execute amphibious surprise raids!" A-Shin stood with a beaming smile, saluting with pristine textbook form. "And reporting, Director—that was not a disciplinary rap sheet; that is divergent thinking!"

"Clang—!"

Director Zhou's left hand spasmed, and the heavy stainless-steel thermos slipped through his fingers, hurtling straight toward the unyielding concrete floor! The scalding ginseng and chrysanthemum tea inside was about to splash everywhere!

Yet five centimeters before the thermos struck the deck—

A round, agile figure executed a kneeslide across the floor like a miniature whirlwind!

With unbelievable suppleness, Old Wu slid onto one knee, right arm extended flat, catching the base of the thermos with rock-solid balance—not a single droplet of boiling tea spilled!

"Phew... safe catch!" Old Wu wiped the cold sweat from his brow, offering the thermos back to Director Zhou with a jovial grin. "Director, your ginseng tea is scalding hot. Next time your hands slip, give me a heads-up first—I almost didn't make that intercept."

Director Zhou stood upon the podium, clutching the retrieved thermos, staring at this room of relaxed, clairvoyant children as a profound existential crisis consumed his sanity.

Who am I? Where am I? Did I actually walk through this door just now? Did pulling an all-nighter grading papers trigger acute schizophrenia?!

Beside him, Teacher Gao nudged his spectacles, a subtle glimmer of deep blue code flickering across his lenses.

The bionic android bowed slightly, fracturing the surreal silence:

"Director Zhou, real-time EEG monitoring across our student cohort indicates an unprecedented neural synchronization rate of 99.9 percent. Regarding the 'Mandatory Summer Detention Order' you originally planned to proclaim—shall we proceed with the deployment as scheduled?"

Director Zhou shuddered. He looked at A-Shin's radiant grin, Old Wu calmly sipping tea, and the mechanical squirrel Liu-Liu waving its tiny paws from the corner.

For the first time in his thirty-year reign as the "Iron Judge" of Luyang Elementary, he tasted the bitter chill of existential dread.

"I-issue it... of course we issue it!" Director Zhou wheezed with what little breath remained, his voice sounding hollow and faint. "The whole building has no AC! One full week of detention! Teacher Gao... you, you watch them like a hawk! These brats... they're possessed! The whole lot of them has been cursed!"

Without even picking up the test scores, Director Zhou clutched his thermos and scrambled out the door in a stumbling panic, his fleeing silhouette resembling a traumatized survivor of an alien abduction.

"SLAM!"

The door was hauled shut in frantic disarray.

Three seconds of dead silence passed—

"YEAHHHHH—!!!!!"

An earth-shattering roar of triumph erupted from the classroom of Class 6-1, nearly lifting the roof off the building!

"It's really replaying! We actually time-traveled!" A-Shin vaulted onto his chair, waving his arms like a madman. "Old Wu! That kneeslide catch was legendary!"

"This is no dream! We literally hold the script of the future in our hands!" The children hugged, jumped, and screamed in ecstatic celebration.

Yet seated in the front row, Lin Wei-Xi did not smile.

Looking down at the bangle still deadlocked at 【07:59:59】, and then out at the clear summer sky laced with faint, unnatural purple haze, she whispered to herself:

"What we hold... might not be the script to the future, but the keys to a labyrinth of time we can never escape..."
