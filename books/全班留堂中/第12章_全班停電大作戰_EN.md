# Chapter 12: The Campus Blackout Blitzkrieg

Loop Day Twenty-Two.

3:30 PM.

On the eastern horizon, that all-too-familiar, eerie bank of dark-purple thunderclouds surged relentlessly inward once more like a ravenous tidal wave.

Throughout the South Academic Wing, the air hummed with the faint, crackling hiss of high-voltage electrostatic discharge. Stray metal burrs along the corridor banisters began pulsing with faint arcs of pale blue light.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the atmosphere was as taut as an underground war room on the eve of the Normandy landings.

The chalkboard's usual calculus formulas had been wiped clean, replaced by a massive electrical distribution topology of Luyang Elementary, hand-drawn in colored chalk by Lin Yun-Qing.

"Listen up, everyone. This is our final physical attempt to shatter this loop."

Standing at the podium, Qing-Qing held a sharpened wooden meter stick, rapping it firmly against a crimson skull-and-crossbones emblem dead center on the board—the campus master electrical substation located behind the Administration Building, ordinarily marked with a rusted iron plaque reading: "High Voltage Danger · Authorized Personnel Only."

"Across the past three cycles, we dismantled the antique clock in the Principal's Office and the time capsule in the bunker. Yet the remaining temporal energy in the bangle remains formidable; at 3:59:59 PM, it will channel the hundred-thousand-volt lightning bolt to reconstruct the spacetime deadlock."

Nudging her spectacles, Qing-Qing's gaze swept across the twenty-five students below like cold steel:

"The bangle's resonance relies on the school's power grid as a natural amplifier. According to my supercomputing derivations, if we manually trip the three-phase master breaker at the substation ten seconds prior to the lightning strike—precisely at 3:59:50 PM—the entire campus grid will collapse to absolute zero! Deprived of its conduction medium, the bangle will be forced to abort the temporal reset!"

"However," seated in the front row, A-Shin raised his hand, his expression unusually grim, "the entity guarding that substation... is Teacher Gao."

Silence blanketed the classroom.

Indeed. Teacher Gao.

As Luyang Elementary's dedicated educator and bionic android, inscribed deep within Teacher Gao's root architecture was an inviolable, prime directive: 【Preserve the operational integrity of public campus infrastructure at all costs; strictly prohibit minors from hazardous electrical contact】.

Over the past twenty days, whenever anyone approached within thirty meters of the substation, Teacher Gao would materialize out of thin air at a sprint speed of twelve meters per second, hoisting the trespassing student by their collar with a polite smile, returning them to their desk alongside five supplementary physics safety worksheets.

"Therefore, this is no ordinary breaker pull," A-Shin vaulted onto the podium, taking the chalk from Qing-Qing and circling the target with a vicious flourish. "This is a full-class, high-stakes 'Lure the Tiger from the Mountain' total-containment operation!"

A-Shin brought his palm crashing down onto the desk, unleashing a thunderous battle cry:

"We have twenty-six souls, plus one Grade-A elite AI operative, Liu-Liu! Old Wu leads the athletic vanguard as the primary human damping phalanx to execute physical retardation along the main avenue; the girls' squad handles acoustic-optical decoys and saturation smoke bombs; Qing-Qing and I form the decapitation strike team to plunge straight into the heart of the substation!"

"Do you understand your orders?!"

"UNDERSTOOD————!"

All twenty-six youths leaped to their feet in unison, bellowing at the top of their lungs, their eyes blazing with the unyielding fury of burning their bridges!

3:50 PM sharp.

Ten minutes remained until temporal reset.

Fierce gales tore through the canopy outside. The inky purple clouds descended flush against the rooftop of the academic wing, saturating the atmosphere with the sharp, metallic stench of ozone.

Before the reinforced concrete bunker of the substation—ringed tightly by chain-link security fencing behind the Administration Building—a tall, solemn, charcoal-grey silhouette stood motionless upon the concrete steps like ancient bedrock.

His suit immaculately pressed, his hands folded naturally before his waist, Teacher Gao's dark-rimmed glasses cast icy reflections beneath the frenzied dance of violet lightning overhead.

His bionic sensor suite had long placed every whisper of motion within a hundred-meter perimeter under absolute surveillance.

"Eight minutes remaining. Wind velocity: 14.2 meters per second; relative humidity: 91 percent," Teacher Gao murmured to the empty air, his metallic timbre as steady as ever. "Warning: Multiple biological thermal signatures exhibiting tactical intent detected, converging upon these coordinates in a fan-shaped perimeter."

Before the words had faded—

"Squad One! Release the hounds... err, release the buns!"

An earth-shattering roar detonated from behind the decorative brick flowerbeds facing the substation!

Old Wu charged forward with bared arms, six massive wooden steamer tiers strapped across his chest with heavy-duty packing tape, flanked by four burly boys weighing north of seventy kilos. Like five heavy armored tanks at full throttle, they thundered headlong toward Teacher Gao!

As he sprinted, Old Wu frantically hurled the piping-hot mushroom pork buns from his chest like fragmentation grenades, raining them down toward Teacher Gao's face!

"Teacher Gao! Taste the accumulated fury of my twenty days of pork bun nightmares————!"

"Whoosh! Whoosh! Whoosh!"

Over twenty steaming white dough projectiles ripped through the air, screaming straight for the android's face!

Yet facing this catastrophic saturation barrage of pork buns, Teacher Gao did not yield a single millimeter of ground.

His hands dissolved into two grey motion blurs, his fingertips deflecting, guiding, and absorbing momentum in continuous, elegant arcs—

"Snap! Snap! Snap!"

A miraculous spectacle unfolded: the instant each bun grazed his fingertips, its kinetic energy was dissipated to absolute zero through bionic Tai-Chi joint mechanics, stacking up upon the podium into an immaculate, undamaged three-tier pastry pyramid!

"Student Wu Chen-Kai, squandering food constitutes a Tier-Two behavioral violation under Ministry of Education guidelines." Balancing the final bun on his palm, Teacher Gao shifted his frame, gliding three meters sideways in a blink, cleanly dodging the flying tackles of Old Wu's five-man human avalanche!

"Now! Squad Two, deploy the ultimate chemical ordnance!"

Concealed within the flanking tree canopy, several girls yanked hard on ropes!

"SPLASH————!"

Eight industrial buckets overflowing with dish-soap suds and fine chalk powder cascaded from the heavens, drenching the ground before the substation! In a fraction of a second, the thirty-meter perimeter dissolved into an impenetrable fog of blinding white froth and powder!

"Liu-Liu! Electromagnetic pulse—blind his optical array!"

Like a micro-rocket launching from the boughs, the silver-white AI pet Liu-Liu shot through the mist, the gold-plated antenna on its tail igniting with blinding emerald luminescence aimed squarely at Teacher Gao's spectacles!

"HUMMMM—!"

The optical inputs before Teacher Gao's processing core overloaded to white noise; his infrared thermal imaging devolved into chaotic static beneath the sudsy barrage.

"Primary optical modules temporarily offline. Transitioning to auditory sonar mode..."

Before the diagnostic concluded, Teacher Gao's audio array picked up a faint, hyper-accelerated cadence of footsteps charging straight for the substation gates!

3:59:45 PM!

Fifteen seconds until lightning strike!

Caked in mud and suds, clutching a three-foot pair of insulated bolt cutters, A-Shin and Qing-Qing burst through the mist, slamming against the rusted iron gates of the substation!

"Qing-Qing! Cut the lock!" A-Shin braced his shoulders against the fence, bellowing through raw vocal cords.

"SNAP!"

Throwing the entirety of her body weight down onto the levers, Qing-Qing sheared through the padlock shackle in an explosive burst of force!

The iron gates were kicked ajar!

Inside the gloomy bunker, a seven-foot-tall, three-phase distribution cabinet—choked with hundreds of finger-thick high-voltage cables—loomed before them! Dead center atop the console, a massive master breaker lever painted blood-red stood locked firmly in the "ON" position!

3:59:51 PM!

"I'm tripping the breaker!" Roaring, A-Shin lunged for the crimson handle, locking his palms around the insulated grip!

Yet a millisecond before A-Shin could pull downward with all his might—

A charcoal-grey silhouette, moving at velocities defying organic physics, materialized out of thin air before the console!

Teacher Gao was drenched in white soap suds, his black frames knocked five degrees askew from the supersonic sprint. Yet his steel-hard hand clamped around the lever assembly with absolute, immovable authority!

"Student A-Shin, manual contact with a hundred-thousand-volt master breaker yields a survival probability of zero percent." Teacher Gao's deep mechanical voice rumbled through the charged air. His grip was an unbreakable vice; even as A-Shin braced both sneakers against the steel cabinet with all his might, the lever remained frozen!

"Let go! Teacher Gao!" A-Shin's eyes were bloodshot, tears mingling with sweat streaming down his face. "The lightning is coming! If we don't trip this breaker, today resets all over again! We don't want to be trapped in this day anymore! We want to go to tomorrow————!"

Teacher Gao's metallic fingers trembled ever so slightly.

Deep within the android's ocular core, the cold red safeguard parameters wavered violently the instant the phrase "We want to go to tomorrow" detonated in his audio receptors.

Yet the prime directive screamed its absolute veto across his neural bus:

【Warning: Logic conflict! Student life safety supersedes all variables!】  
【Maintain breaker engagement!】

3:59:57 PM!

From the heavens directly above came the terrifying roar of tearing atmospheric fabric. A bolt of violet-white ball lightning, thick as a water barrel, cleaved through the storm clouds, hurtling straight for the rooftop lightning rod!

"Teacher Gao! Forgive us!"

A tearful, piercing shout rang out from directly behind Teacher Gao's back!

Qing-Qing had seized a heavy-duty insulated rubber baton from the safety rack, throwing every ounce of her strength into slamming it squarely against the bionic neural damping actuator at Teacher Gao's right elbow!

"CLANG————!"

Direct hit!

It was the solitary neuro-reflex switch across Teacher Gao's entire chassis that Qing-Qing had spent twenty days analyzing!

The hydraulic valve in Teacher Gao's right arm suffered instantaneous pressure loss. In a millisecond, that iron grip snapped outward in involuntary release!

"A-Shin! Now! PULL————!" Qing-Qing screamed at the top of her lungs.

"BREAK FOR ME, DAMN IT ALL—————!!!!"

Channeling the entire weight of his body and every shred of longing for tomorrow burning within his soul, A-Shin hung his entire frame from the crimson breaker handle, dragging it downward with the savage pull of gravity!

"CRACK————SNAP————BOOM!!!!"

An earth-shattering roar of metal shearing exploded within the bunker!

The forty-pound solid copper high-voltage breaker, half a second before the lightning struck, was violently slammed home into the "DISENGAGED" cradle!

"SIZZZZLE—CRACK—POP!"

Blinding arcs sputtered across the switchgear, and then—

The entire South Academic Wing, the Administration Building, the quad, the clock tower... Every fluorescent tube, ceiling fan, security camera, and aged water pump died in an instant!

The entire campus of Luyang Elementary was plunged into absolute, impenetrable, suffocating darkness!

"We did it..." Collapsing onto the concrete, A-Shin gasped for air in ragged sobs, an ecstatic grin spreading across his soot-stained face. "We killed the main grid! The lightning can't conduct!"

Exhausted, Qing-Qing sank to her knees beside him, their hands clasping tightly in the dark.

Yet standing in the pitch black, Teacher Gao slowly straightened his spine.

The android's internal auxiliary battery engaged silently, a soft blue luminescence radiating from his spectacle lenses into the gloom. Looking down at the sweat-drenched A-Shin and Qing-Qing, his voice carried an inexplicable, profound resonance of sorrow and awe:

"You performed magnificently. You demonstrated intellect, courage, and solidarity far exceeding normal thresholds."

Teacher Gao lifted his head gently, looking out toward the dark, turbulent sky:

"Yet the laws of spacetime... have never been merely the cold movement of electrons."

Before his voice had settled.

"CHIME————"

An ethereal, ancient, blood-curdling toll echoed across the pitch-black campus stripped of all electrical power. Bearing the unshakeable weight of absolute causality, without a shred of resistance... It struck its first note.

Precisely 4:00 PM.
