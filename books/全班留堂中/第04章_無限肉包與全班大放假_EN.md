# Chapter 4: Infinite Pork Buns and the Great Class Vacation

Following the conclusive confirmation that time would faithfully reload from its save point at precisely 4:00 PM every day, the collective IQ of Class 6-1 underwent a catastrophic, visible landslide.

Fear? Panic? Awe before the cosmic unknown?

Utterly non-existent.

Within the value system of twelve-year-old elementary schoolers, if the universe offered a supernatural phenomenon where "snacks devoured today fully regenerate tomorrow," "test papers torn to confetti today are restored good as new tomorrow," and "one never, ever has to turn in tomorrow's summer homework"—

That was no spacetime crisis. That was an unmitigated miracle from the heavens!

"Fellow compatriots! Brothers and sisters of Class 6-1!"

At 9:15 in the morning, A-Shin stood atop the podium, one foot planted firmly on the cypress teacher's desk, one hand propped on his hip, delivering a historic address to the room with soaring swagger:

"For an entire year, we have been oppressed beneath the iron boot of Old Zhou! Today, the gears of destiny have finally turned in our favor! Since everything gets swept into a cosmic trash bin the moment four o'clock strikes, then for these eight glorious hours... why on earth should we sit here memorizing square root extraction?!"

"Preach it!" The boys in the back row rolled their notebooks into mega-horns, bellowing at the top of their lungs.

"Therefore, I hereby proclaim!" A-Shin swept his arm with magnificent flourish. "The 'Class 6-1 First Annual Spacetime Summer Festival' is officially open!"

"ROAAAAR—!!!!"

Over twenty kids erupted into wild, feral cheers, plunging the classroom into an immediate vortex of pure bedlam and hedonism.

Leading the charge was none other than the class's biological black hole—Old Wu.

Like an untamed ox loosed from its pen, Old Wu tore out the classroom door, barrelled down the stairwell, and charged straight for the school cafeteria commissary. Five minutes later, pushing a borrowed utility trolley, he rattled back upstairs hauling three towering steamer tiers from the commissary bun warmer—holding sixty piping hot, fragrant mushroom pork buns!

"Old Wu, where'd you get the cash for this mountain of buns? The commissary auntie didn't chase you down with a broom?" A-Shin sidled up, snatching a plump bun and cramming it into his mouth.

"Hehe, Auntie took the day off today—only a college work-study intern was minding the counter!" Slick with grease around the lips, clutching a pale, puffy bun in each fist, Old Wu's eyes were squeezed shut with bliss. "I told him: 'Big brother, charge it to Director Zhou Yan's Student Affairs Discretionary Allowance Account; the authorization code is: The Darkest Day in the Sixty-Eight-Year History of Luyang Elementary!' He actually bought it!"

"Besides, the moment four o'clock hits, the steamer baskets will miraculously refill to the brim!" Old Wu drummed on his spherical belly, releasing a soul-shaking guffaw. "Infinite pork buns! The ultimate happiness I, Wu Chen-Kai, have pursued my entire existence has finally been realized today!"

Meanwhile, retrofitting operations in the rear half of the classroom were proceeding at a furious pace.

The athletic officer and several tall boys shoved every desk and chair against the perimeter, filling dozens of jumbo plastic mop buckets from the custodian's closet to the brim with tap water. They even managed to scavenge two giant jugs of industrial dish soap from somewhere, pouring the slippery liquid across the polished terrazzo corridor with gleeful abandon.

"The South Wing Aquapark is officially in business! Extreme human body-surfing Wave One, launch!"

Sporting a pair of wildly colorful Hawaiian board shorts and clutching a foam kickboard, A-Shin shot down the thirty-meter soap-slicked hallway, screaming in ecstatic terror before slamming squarely into the gymnasium safety mats propped against the back door, sending a colossal geyser of white suds into the air!

"My turn! My turn!" One after another, the other students kicked off their sneakers, queuing up along the corridor for an uninhibited "Floor Surfing Championship."

The entire wing was saturated with the rich aroma of savory pork buns, the zesty citrus tang of dish soap, and the shrieks of laughter from children loud enough to lift the roof off the rafters.

Yet dead center amidst this chaotic ocean of revelry sat a pocket of absolute, eerie calm.

Before the podium, Teacher Gao remained seated upon a metal folding chair.

Resting atop his black slacks was a wooden clipboard; holding a high-precision red fountain pen in his right hand, he graded a thick stack of exams with unyielding concentration. Though flying flecks of soapy foam spattered against the edges of his leather shoes, and the wet chomping sounds of Old Wu's bun-feasting echoed inches from his ear, the bionic android's facial musculature remained entirely immobile, maintaining a grip force of precisely 0.7 Newtons upon his pen.

Seated in the front row, Lin Wei-Xi turned, looking at Teacher Gao with subtle unease: "Teacher Gao... aren't you going to stop them? The hallway is practically submerged in dish soap."

Teacher Gao's pen paused for a fraction of a second. Nudging his black spectacles, a stream of cold computational code flickered beneath his lenses:

"Student Lin Wei-Xi, pursuant to Article 7 of this homeroom teacher's internal 【Juvenile Autonomous Exploratory Behavioral Guidance Protocol】, when external objective conditions present irreversible force majeure, forcibly suppressing collective emotional release yields a 350 percent surge in the psychological reactance index."

"Which means... you're just going to let them run wild?" Seated beside Wei-Xi, Qing-Qing raised an eyebrow as she soldered the external circuitry of the bangle with an iron.

"Not let them run wild," Teacher Gao closed the exam folder with a soft, authoritative snap. "Calculations synthesized from the Second Law of Thermodynamics and physiological threshold models project that massive ingestion of simple carbohydrates will induce acute glycemic volatility and gastrointestinal lethargy within 120 minutes. Concurrently, un-damped frictional kinetic sliding will exhaust their skeletal muscle glycogen reserves within three hours."

Teacher Gao rose, smoothing the fabric of his tie with measured grace, a faint, mechanical smile tugging at the corner of his lips:

"Simply put: the higher their current dopamine peak, the tenfold more acute their existential boredom and emptiness will be in two hours' time. As an educator, my duty is merely to provide the most intellectually nourishing curricular solutions the moment their trough arrives."

Qing-Qing felt an icy shiver crawl down her spine, silently lighting a row of metaphorical prayer candles for A-Shin and Old Wu.

Teacher Gao's predictive modeling proved terrifyingly precise.

1:40 PM.

The scorching midday heat had baked the enclosed classroom into a giant convection oven.

The soapy bubbles along the floor had long since evaporated, leaving behind a sticky, slippery film of dried chalky residue. The water in the buckets had turned lukewarm and murky, while the heavy stench of cold pork grease mingled with stale perspiration made anyone who inhaled it gag.

"Ugh... blech..."

Old Wu lay draped limply across four pushed-together desks, his belly swollen like a watermelon on the verge of detonation.

Scattered haphazardly around his elbow were over thirty dried, gnawed, leathery bun skins.

"I can't take it..." Old Wu clutched his stomach, groaning in sheer agony. "I swear on my life... for the rest of my days... I never want to see cabbage and minced pork ever again... Why does every single pork bun in the universe taste like the exact same greasy nightmare..."

A-Shin lay sprawled out starfished on the floor of the podium, clutching a half-melted, sticky red bean popsicle that dripped across his knuckles.

The thrilling corridor slip-and-slide, after fifty consecutive runs, had degraded into pure physical torture. Both his elbows were abraded raw, his palms burned like fire, and he lacked the strength to lift even a single pinky finger.

"This is so boring..." A girl with a ponytail buried her face between her knees, whimpering in exhaustion. "There's no Wi-Fi for mobile games—we can only play offline Tetris. The classroom TV only picks up three educational broadcast channels replaying agricultural fertilization seminars. My phone doesn't have a single bar of signal... What are we even doing here?"

"Seriously..." another boy sighed, gazing up at the slow, monotonous rotation of the ceiling fans. "During regular vacations, you never feel like there's enough time. How come now that time is literally infinite, it feels worse than standing at attention in Old Zhou's office?"

The entire classroom was drained of life. Twenty-odd "revolutionaries" who had ruled the roost hours earlier had now withered into frostbitten eggplants.

"Tap, tap, tap."

With textbook strides of precisely sixty centimeters, Teacher Gao mounted the podium with unhurried poise.

His leather shoes struck the sticky floor with crisp, authoritative cadence. Looking down at the students strewn across the deck, his gaze held not a shred of mockery—only absolute, mathematical serenity.

"Students, real-time physiological monitoring indicates that the class's dopamine baseline has plummeted 65 percent below standard thresholds. The current collective psychological state conforms flawlessly to the textbook definition of the 'Post-Hedonic Adaptation Void.'"

With effortless grace, Teacher Gao hoisted that colossal sheaf of pristine white paper from the podium and fanned his arm.

"Whoosh—whoosh—"

Twenty-six brand-new examination packets fluttered down like snowflakes, landing with pinpoint accuracy dead center upon each student's desk.

"Since the festival has concluded, according to our established schedule, it is now time for autonomous independent study." Teacher Gao braced both hands upon the podium, his resonant, metallic baritone ringing across the tomb-like silence:

"This is an advanced simulation packet encompassing all foundational competencies across three years of junior high curriculum. I have prepared one thousand and eighty questions for you. Before the 4:00 PM spacetime reset arrives, we have precisely two hours and twenty minutes remaining."

A-Shin's eyes bulged as he stared at the dense, ant-like swarms of calculus derivatives and mechanics vector diagrams, nearly coughing up a pint of blood on the spot:

"O-one thousand and eighty questions?! Teacher Gao! Are you a demon from hell?! Time resets at four o'clock sharp anyway—what is the point of grinding through this right now?!"

Teacher Gao tilted his head slightly, the harsh fluorescent glare casting a brilliant flash across his black spectacle frames.

The bionic android's smile widened by precisely ten percent, his tone as warm and gentle as a vernal breeze:

"Student A-Shin, the reality is quite the inverse. It is precisely because time resets at four o'clock that your incorrect answers will never be logged into your permanent academic transcript. In other words, you have been gifted the most lavish luxury in the cosmos—infinite, consequence-free temporal capital to fail and retry ad infinitum."

Turning around, Teacher Gao tapped his chalk against the board in crisp, staccato beats:

"The timer starts now. If anyone dares turn in their paper prematurely before four o'clock—"

Teacher Gao glanced back, his gaze lingering for a fraction of a second upon Old Wu's spherical midsection:

"—this homeroom teacher will personally finance one hundred piping hot mushroom pork buns and supervise their mandatory consumption under strict time limits."

Old Wu shuddered violently, unleashing a blood-curdling screech of terror as he seized his pencil and began scribbling frantically across the test paper like a maniac!

Staring at the upright, all-knowing machine standing upon the podium, A-Shin tasted, for the very first time, the crushing weight of an absolute intellectual dimension.

This time loop... was no consequence-free paradise!

This was a customized, merciless, precision-calibrated super-cram-school purgatory engineered by Teacher Gao himself!
