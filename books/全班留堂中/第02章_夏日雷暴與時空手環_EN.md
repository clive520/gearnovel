# Chapter 2: The Summer Thunderstorm and the Chronos Bangle

At 3:30 in the afternoon, the stifling air seemed to congeal into sticky, transparent asphalt, bearing down with crushing weight upon the entire South Academic Wing.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the four antique ceiling fans groaned their squeaking protests, stirring the muggy air with profound weariness.

The twenty-six students below were drained of vitality, reduced to haggard shells of their former selves.

Their desks were buried beneath stacks of 【Core Concept Intensive Sprint Handouts】, personally printed by Teacher Gao. For seven and a half uninterrupted hours, Teacher Gao had maintained a textbook pacing of precisely 180 words per minute, delivering continuous lectures on classical mechanics, contextual analysis of classical Chinese particles, and ten variations of systems of linear equations.

Throughout this grueling 450-minute intellectual marathon, Teacher Gao had not sipped a single drop of water; his suit and tie remained as crisp as if fresh from the cleaners, without a single microgram of perspiration exuded from his brow.

"I can't go on..." Old Wu slumped completely across his desk, his cheek pressed against the faintly cool cypress wood, his eyes gazing forward with vacant despair. "My total cranial capacity is only 250 milliliters. Right now, it's jammed to bursting with moveable pulleys, buoyancy formulas, and the Dutch defeated by Zheng Chenggong... If I don't get a piping hot pork bun to replenish my glucose reserves right this second, you'll be visiting me in the autopsy lab tomorrow..."

"Hang on for just twenty-five more minutes!" A-Shin fanned himself furiously with his notebook, glaring daggers at the round quartz clock perched above the blackboard. "Four o'clock sharp! The moment that wretched clock strikes four, Old Zhou has to let us go according to the agreement! When that happens, I'm downing two giant cups of brown sugar boba milk tea and a mega fried chicken cutlet—if the vendor shorts me one speck of pepper salt, I'll never let him hear the end of it!"

Seated in the front row, Lin Wei-Xi appeared noticeably unsettled.

Unlike the others, who stared fixedly at the countdown above the chalkboard, she turned her gaze repeatedly toward the window.

Unnoticed by most, the sky had subtly shifted.

The blazing, incandescent sun of high summer had vanished without a trace, replaced by an eerie bank of leaden clouds billowing violently from the eastern horizon. These were no ordinary storm clouds; the underbelly of that churning darkness pulsed with an unsettling, spectral purple luminescence. The shrill chorus of a thousand cicadas outside abruptly died away, as if silenced by a master kill-switch, plunging the campus into a spine-chilling, absolute hush.

"Sizzle—crackle—"

A faint yet sharp crackle of static electricity whispered suddenly from Wei-Xi's left wrist.

Her expression shifted instantly. Hurriedly, she pulled back the sleeve of her white cotton-linen blouse.

The 'Chronos Bangle' upon her left wrist—which ordinarily resembled a simple silver bracelet—was currently flashing with wild, blinding pulses of light. The gentle, deep-blue data stream that usually drifted across its surface was rapidly degenerating into a frantic cascade of crimson warning code!

"What's wrong, Wei-Xi?" Seated beside her, Qing-Qing was the first to notice the anomaly. The tech-savvy class president set down her precision screwdriver and leaned in close, the tip of her nose nearly brushing Wei-Xi's wrist. "Your watch... its surface temperature is spiking exponentially! The dielectric constant around it is oscillating violently—this is no ordinary circuit noise!"

"Qing-Qing, get back!" Wei-Xi's voice held an uncharacteristic tremble of panic. "It's being forced into resonance with the atmospheric ionosphere outside! A micro-fault rupture just fractured the spacetime curvature of this city—the bangle's emergency stabilization valve... is jammed!"

Before the words had completely left Wei-Xi's lips—

"BOOM-RUMBLE-CRASH—!!!!"

A monstrous roar of thunder—loud enough to tear the earth in two—detonated without warning five hundred meters directly above Luyang Elementary!

The entire academic wing shuddered violently; windowpanes screeched in sympathetic resonance, while plaster dust drifted down from the ceiling like snowflakes.

Curled up in its charging alcove at the corner of the podium, the AI pet Liu-Liu was propelled into the air by the shockwave, its silver-white metallic fur bristling instantly as its dual electronic eyes flashed panicked, blood-red exclamation marks:

"Alert! High-energy plasma cloud cluster locked on! Electric field intensity exceeding 100,000 volts! Target—South Wing rooftop lightning rod!"

"CRACK-BOOM!"

A bolt of ball lightning as thick as a water barrel, radiating blinding violet-white fury, sliced vertically downward from the roiling purple clouds like a divine spear of judgment, striking the galvanized steel lightning rod directly above Class 6-1 with merciless precision!

In a fraction of a second, the searing glare illuminated the classroom as bright as high noon, the violent shockwave knocking fire extinguishers down the corridor with clattering thuds.

Yet the colossal electrical discharge did not safely dissipate into the subterranean earth through the grounding copper wire; instead, it surged through the aging reinforced concrete skeleton of the building, racing along steel rebars toward the third-floor classroom like an enraged serpent of living electricity!

"Danger! All units adopt defensive postures!"

The reflexes of Teacher Gao standing upon the podium defied the physiological limits of organic biology.

His pupils contracted into pinpricks in a millisecond; his spectacle lenses snapped into full-reflection polarized protection mode. Transforming into a charcoal-grey blur, he vaulted from the podium straight to the front row, spreading his arms to shield Wei-Xi and Qing-Qing beneath his bionic mechanical frame!

"Zzz-crack—BANG!"

The rampaging electrical current leapt down from the ceiling fan wiring, striking the Chronos Bangle on Wei-Xi's wrist and the high-conductivity bionic neural synaptic core at the nape of Teacher Gao's neck simultaneously with devastating precision!

Two entirely distinct technological pinnacles—both far exceeding contemporary human comprehension—ignited an unprecedented quantum electromagnetic super-resonance under the tempering of tens of thousands of volts!

"Hummm—buzz—whirrr—"

A piercing, brain-splitting hum reverberated throughout the classroom.

The core processor of the Chronos Bangle and the micro-atomic clock inside Teacher Gao's chest short-circuited together in the violent resonance. A translucent, silver-purple membrane erupted outward with the two of them at its epicenter, expanding rapidly like an inflating soap bubble, instantly enveloping all twenty-six students, the entire classroom, and the fabric of surrounding space within its boundary!

"Teacher Gao! Your lenses! Your glasses are smoking!" A-Shin cried out in horror.

Teacher Gao's erect frame was frozen half-kneeling on the floor, fine blue electrical sparks sputtering from both hinges of his spectacles. The progress bars deep within his eyes collapsed completely into a descending cascade of garbled red code:

【Warning: Severe topological distortion detected in external temporal coordinates!】  
【Underlying logic safeguard protocol forcibly activated!】  
【Attempting to anchor local spacetime coordinates... Anchoring failed!】  
【Current system determination: Curricular progress below 100%. Triggering absolute closed-loop preservation protocol!】

"Qing-Qing! A-Shin! Grab onto something solid!" Wei-Xi screamed amidst the blinding luminescence, her long hair whipping wildly in the zero-gravity electromagnetic eddy. "The bangle's timeline has snapped! Spacetime is collapsing!"

"Chime—chime—chime—"

The second hand of the vintage quartz clock above the chalkboard whirled frantically before locking in place with a heavy metallic clack at precisely 3:59:59 PM.

Immediately after, the hollow, eerie toll of the 4:00 PM dismissal bell echoed slowly within the temporal sphere.

At that split second, all sound, gale, rain, and light froze into absolute stillness.

A-Shin saw a dry leaf falling outside the window suspended motionless mid-air; a droplet of saliva spattered from Old Wu's gaping mouth hung frozen beside his lip; the broken lines of code before Teacher Gao's eyes turned static.

The very next second, an infinite deluge of blinding white light detonated, devouring the consciousness of every soul in the room.

...

"Chirp—chirp—chirp—"

The grating, shrill drone of cicadas scraped against their eardrums once more like a dull metal rasp.

Heat.

Unbearable, suffocating heat swept in from all directions.

"Ugh... my head is killing me..."

Massaging his throbbing temples, A-Shin struggled to lift his head from the wooden desk. He instinctively reached up to feel his eyebrows and hair, finding no singed patches from lightning, nor any lingering sting of electrical shock.

Baffled, he lifted his gaze to survey his surroundings.

The antique ceiling fans overhead still hummed their squeaking protests; sunlight poured through the palm fronds outside, casting dappled, glaring white patches upon the concrete floor.

The blackboard was pristine—no physics equations scribbled across it, no chalk dust smudges from an eraser.

Before the podium, Teacher Gao stood dressed in that exact same uncreased three-piece charcoal-grey suit, his red-and-blue striped tie immaculate, his black-rimmed glasses reflecting the morning light with serene poise.

"Huh? What's going on? Was that all just a dream? Did I get struck by lightning?" A-Shin muttered, scratching his messy hair.

Beside him, Old Wu rubbed his bleary eyes, reaching instinctively into his desk drawer.

"Wait, where are my pork and cabbage dumplings? I could've sworn I just had a nightmare where Old Zhou made me recite a dumpling formula..." Old Wu produced a half-eaten cold pork bun from his drawer, staring at it in utter bewilderment.

In the front row, Lin Wei-Xi snapped her head down to inspect her left wrist.

The gentle blue data stream upon the Chronos Bangle was completely frozen, replaced by three faintly glowing purple digits counting down:

【07 : 59 : 59】

Wei-Xi's face drained of color in an instant.

Just then, Teacher Gao raised his right hand with consummate grace, tapping a fresh piece of chalk against the center of the blackboard. Turning to face the class, he smiled with that calibrated, mechanical baritone:

"Good morning, students, and good morning to Director Zhou, who will be arriving shortly. The time is precisely 8:00 AM."

A-Shin froze.

Old Wu froze.

All twenty-six students of Class 6-1 were petrified in their seats like statues of stone.
