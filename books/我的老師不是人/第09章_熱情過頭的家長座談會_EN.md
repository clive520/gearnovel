# *My Teacher Is Not Human*

## Chapter 9: The Overheated Parent-Teacher Meeting

On a Friday evening, the campus of Luyang Elementary School was dyed in a serene reddish-orange by the setting sun.

However, while the second- and third-grade corridors were already long deserted, Class 6-1, located at the far end of the second floor of the Senior Building, was ablaze with lights like a command bunker right before a looming tempest.

Tonight was the most formidable annual spectacle that struck terror into the hearts of students and teachers alike—the school-wide Parent-Teacher Conference.

In past parent meetings, the classroom of Class 6-1 had only ever echoed with two kinds of sounds: either the former homeroom teacher's blood-pressure-spiking laments and sighs, or the furious desk-slamming roars of hot-tempered parents interrogating the school's educational methodology. After all, the twenty-six mischievous rascals of this class were each blessed with outlandish talents—having dismantled the natural science lab's water taps the previous week, and dumped dish soap into mop buckets to turn the hallway into an ice-skating rink this week, earning Class 6-1's "Parent Squad" a formidable reputation throughout the school.

Yet tonight, the atmosphere inside the classroom was exceptionally bizarre.

The usually scattered student desks had been arranged into tight, orderly concentric circles, where more than twenty parents clad in sharp business suits, elegant two-piece dresses, or casual designer wear sat awkwardly squeezed onto the diminutive wooden school chairs.

Their eyes held none of the usual embarrassment or hostility; instead, they shone with a feverish gaze that mingled intense curiosity, inquisitiveness, and uncontrollable excitement.

All of this was entirely thanks to that school-shattering "Gold Medal for Best Academic Progress" awarded just two days ago.

The school's most notorious twenty-six rascals had all passed their midterm exams without a single failure, boasting a staggering average increase of twenty points! In the fifty-year history of Luyang Elementary School, this was nothing short of an absolute, bona fide miracle. The parents' chat groups had long been buzzing with wild rumors—what kind of witchcraft had their new model teacher, Gao Zhixian, performed? Was he a legendary, world-class educator from Harvard, or did he hold some classified, top-secret academic cramming methodology?

At this very moment, outside the slightly ajar rear door of the classroom, four small heads were stacked closely on top of one another, peeking at the commotion inside while breaking into cold sweats.

"A-Xin... you're stepping on my big toe!" Old Wu protested in a pained, muffled breath against A-Xin's ear, his hand still clutching half a cold scallion pancake.

"Quiet! Pull your head back, Old Wu! Your giant head is blocking the entire door crack!" A-Xin gripped the doorframe with one hand, while his other hand clamped firmly over the chilly brass planetary gear resting against his chest beneath his uniform shirt.

Beside them, Qingqing held a miniature Phillips screwdriver, her nerves pulled taut as a fully drawn bowstring: "We're doomed, we're totally doomed... I ran the probabilities. There are twenty-four parents here tonight. If each asks questions for an average of three minutes, Teacher Gao's internal 'polite social filter' can only hold out for one minute and thirty seconds without the stabilizing gear!"

From the overhead ventilation duct, a fluffy, snow-white silhouette popped out.

The AI pet Liu-Liu's two agile little ears twitched in rapid rotation, its obsidian-like gem eyes flashing with a faint luminescence, sending a nearly imperceptible "beep-beep" vibration signal toward the four kids below.

That was Liu-Liu's dedicated tactical radar alert: [High-energy reaction ahead; thermal and dopamine levels spiking across the board].

"Teacher Gao is walking in!" A-Xin whispered in alarm, and all four kids gasped simultaneously.

The front door of the podium was pushed open silently.

Teacher Gao Zhixian stepped in, dressed in an impeccably tailored dark-gray suit, leather shoes polished to a mirror shine, while his black-rimmed spectacles reflected a cool, perfectly vertical gleam. Unlike other homeroom teachers who carried thick stacks of communication notebooks or bulky insulated tumblers, he merely held an ultra-thin metallic tablet computer with poised elegance in his right hand, his stride measured with laser-like precision—not a millimeter more, not a millimeter less, exactly seventy-five centimeters per step.

He walked up to the podium, turned to face the roomful of parents wearing diverse expressions, and the corners of his mouth curved into a "Standard Initial-Greeting Social Smile" accurate down to the micrometer:

"Good evening, parents. I am Gao Zhixian, homeroom teacher of Class 6-1. The current time is precisely 19:00:00. According to the master schedule, this conference is estimated to conclude in fifty minutes. You may now commence inquiries."

Around the corridor corner, Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan clasped his hands behind his back, leaning against the water dispenser with a cold smirk.

Pen in hand, Director Zhou thought sneeringly: *Gao Zhixian, playing the high-and-mighty role at school is one thing, but the parents of Class 6-1 are all notoriously tough nuts to crack! You've got cram school owners, multinational executives, and overbearing helicopter parents among them. The second you slip up, this crowd will devour you alive!*

Sure enough, the moment the floor opened, an opulent woman with curly hair seated right in the middle of the first row hoisted her hand, glittering with gold rings.

That was A-Xin's mother—a legendary figure ranking in the top three most formidable powerhouses of the Luyang Elementary Parent Association.

"Oh, Teacher Gao!" A-Xin's mother beamed with a wide smile, her tone carrying three parts probing and seven parts pride. "Our A-Xin actually scored an eighty-five on math this time—I could hardly believe my eyes! But that boy is always absentminded at home. I constantly feel like his 'heart is flying around outside,' never putting his mind onto his books. Teacher, don't you think our A-Xin is just far too careless and unfocused?"

Outside the back door, when A-Xin heard this, he gripped his head in agony and wailed silently in his heart: *Mom! Please don't use idioms! Whatever you do, don't use metaphors!!*

Atop the podium, behind Teacher Gao's dark frames, a cascade of eerie blue error codes flashed deep across his pupils:

`[Input Syntax: 心在外面飛 (Heart flying outside)]`
`[Biological Warning: Human cardiac detachment from the thoracic cavity results in brain death within 4 seconds]`
`[Error: Student Lu Lixin maintains steady cardiac vitals; no evidence of extracorporeal cardiovascular flight detected...]`

Teacher Gao paused momentarily, and a microscopic "hissing" pressure-relief murmur emanated faintly from the heat-exhaust micro-valves on both sides of his temples.

"Oh no! His literal interpretation syndrome is kicking in!" Qingqing panicked so hard she nearly dropped her screwdriver.

Yet, in the mere millisecond before his core processor threatened to crash, Teacher Gao's built-in "Deep Linguistic Machine Learning Algorithm" violently overrode the semantic parsing module!

Teacher Gao pushed up his black-rimmed glasses, speaking with an expressionless, chillingly composed cadence:

"Mrs. Lu. According to contextual back-compilation, your idiom 'heart is flying outside' refers to Student Lu lacking attention concentration mechanisms."

A-Xin's mother was taken aback, then nodded vigorously: "Yes, exactly! He simply lacks concentration!"

Teacher Gao summoned a 3D bar chart onto his metallic tablet, his voice resonant and clear like a state broadcast news anchor:

"This represents a profound cognitive bias. According to high-frequency classroom surveillance analytics compiled over the preceding sixty days: Student Lu does not suffer from attentional dispersion."

"Huh?" Every parent in the room stretched their necks forward in surprise.

"Specific empirical evidence is as follows:" Teacher Gao stated with solemn gravitas, rattling off the facts with ease. "During last Tuesday's mathematics lecture, Student Lu balanced an eraser on the tip of his mechanical pencil with microscopic precision, sustaining equilibrium for forty-two consecutive minutes without allowing it to fall. During this timeframe, his ocular tremor frequency decreased by sixty percent, with a blinking rate of merely 3.2 times per minute. Such hyper-focused dynamic equilibrium neural reflexes surpass the baseline average of North American Aerospace Defense Command (NASA) satellite tracking operators by a factor of 1.8."

A collective gasp echoed across the classroom, followed by synchronized exclamations of amazement!

Outside the door, A-Xin froze stiff on the spot, almost slamming his forehead into the wooden door panel—*What kind of compliment was that?! That was me slacking off playing with a pencil during class, Teacher!!*

Teacher Gao did not pause for a fraction of a second, locking his eyes directly onto the flabbergasted Mrs. Lu:

"Student Lu exhibits disengagement during standard classroom instruction not due to intellectual deficiencies, but because the information density of current elementary curriculum materials is too low to stimulate his dopamine receptors effectively. When I provided him yesterday with university freshman-level rigid-body mechanics modeling problems, he achieved an eighty-eight percent success rate in topological deduction within forty minutes."

"Conclusion: Your child possesses premier engineering intuition and extraordinary focus under adverse conditions. What he requires is not reprimands for distraction, but an immediate cessation of mediocre rote-drilling that threatens to suffocate the genius of a future structural engineer."

The entire classroom plunged into dead silence.

A-Xin's mother's mouth fell open, and her designer handbag tumbled onto her knees with a soft "clunk."

A few seconds later, her eyes actually glistened red, her lips trembling with overwhelming emotion: "M-My rascal... the boy who only knows how to climb trees and catch loaches in mud... possesses NASA-grade focus?! Teacher Gao... you... you truly are A-Xin's greatest mentor and champion!"

Outside, A-Xin sniffed, sheepishly scratching the back of his head, feeling an unprecedented, inexplicable warmth swell in his chest.

Before anyone could recover, an amply proportioned mother carrying two hefty bags of homemade braised delicacies stood up from the back row—it was the mother of Old Wu (Wu Chenkai).

Wiping her apron, Mrs. Wu spoke in a booming yet anxious voice: "Teacher Gao! Our Chenkai scored full marks in physical education this time, but his academic scores are always just scraping by! All he thinks about all day long is eating meat buns, cramming his desk full of food until he's built like a mountain! I scold him every day that his brain is full of pig lard! What is this boy going to amount to besides hauling bricks at a construction site?!"

Hearing this outside, Old Wu lowered his head dejectedly, quietly taking a melancholy bite from his cold scallion pancake.

On the podium, the scanning beams behind Teacher Gao's lenses swept across Mrs. Wu, his processor instantly pulling up Wu Chenkai's comprehensive dossier.

"Mrs. Wu. Pursuant to human anatomical biochemistry, nearly sixty percent of human brain tissue is indeed composed of lipids. However, degrading this composition to 'pig lard' constitutes an insulting and scientifically invalid metaphor at the molecular biology level."

A ripple of good-natured chuckles broke out among the gathered parents.

Teacher Gao's voice remained steady and firm, imbued with a resonant, metallic solemnity:

"To the contrary, Student Wu's cognitive center possesses exceptionally rare thermodynamic physiological perception and spatial kinetic intuition. In cafeteria nutrition distribution and daily heavy lifting, his calculations regarding gravitational acceleration, parabolic trajectory, and caloric intake reach microsecond precision."

"More importantly," Teacher Gao glanced around the room, softening his cadence ever so slightly, "among all twenty-six students in the class, Student Wu's empathy index ranks number one. According to recorded logs, whenever any classmate sheds tears from setback, injury, or melancholy, the probability of Student Wu proactively offering his own emergency caloric reserves—namely steamed pork buns or snacks—within three seconds is one hundred percent, even when his own blood glucose level is below the warning threshold."

"In a future hyper-intelligent society, a human who understands how to share warmth, possesses remarkable empathy, and commands robust physical vigor is infinitely more valuable than a cold-blooded calculating machine that merely memorizes formulas. Student Wu is by no means destined to haul bricks; he possesses the core competencies to become an elite athletic health specialist or a heroic disaster-relief commander."

Outside the door, the hand Old Wu used to hold his scallion pancake trembled violently, and two enormous, piping-hot tears splashed down onto the crust: "Drip, drop." Sniffling loudly, he grinned through his tears like an idiot.

Inside the classroom, Mrs. Wu had already broken down in tears, pulling out a handkerchief to dab her eyes as she bowed repeatedly toward the podium: "Thank you, Teacher... *sob*... No teacher has ever praised my goofy boy like this... Thank you for seeing the good in him!"

By now, the atmosphere in the classroom had shifted completely.

Parents who had originally arrived with skeptical frowns now sat upright, their eyes burning with reverence as if gazing upon an all-knowing sage.

Just then, the most authoritative and famously stringent parent representative—the mother of Lin Yunyun—rose to her feet. Mrs. Lin was a renowned cram school educator herself, dressed in a sharp business suit, who pushed up her gold-rimmed glasses and spoke with words as sharp as scalpels:

"Teacher Gao, your psychological counseling is indeed remarkable. But as a veteran educator, I must speak of reality. Competition in today's society is brutal, and the sixth grade is the ultimate watershed moment—the so-called 'you cannot lose at the starting line'! My daughter currently attends three cram schools after school every day and drills practice tests until midnight. I would like to ask Teacher Gao: in the elementary stage, should children be compelled to drill sixty hours of rigorous practice exams every week? Which accelerated, elite cram school would you recommend?"

The moment this question was asked, the temperature in the room plummeted once again!

Many parents showed conflicted and anxious expressions—this was the most agonizing, dreaded reality that every household avoided confronting, yet continuously forced their children to endure!

Outside the door, Qingqing held her breath and whispered: "This is a fatal trap... If Teacher Gao endorses cram schools, the kids will be crushed to death; if he opposes them, these anxious parents will turn furious on the spot!"

Atop the podium, Teacher Gao remained completely motionless for five full seconds.

Within his core computational microchip, two colossal algorithms were locked in a destructive, life-or-death grapple:

`[Subroutine A: Social Courtesy Protection Protocol ➔ Recommend compliance with parent preference; endorse accredited cram institutions]`
`[Subroutine B: Absolute Empirical Educational Big Database ➔ Data contradiction! Severe psychological harm detected!]`
`[Conflict: Politeness vs. Truth]`

"Szz-szz..."

The exhaust micro-valves behind Teacher Gao's ears vibrated intensely, and a wisp of ultra-fine gray smoke even began seeping quietly through the collar of his suit!

Up at the ventilation duct, Liu-Liu's gaze sharpened, and it immediately tapped its tiny claws against the tin duct: "Clink, clink, clink," generating rhythmic acoustic waves that fortuitously disrupted Teacher Gao's overloaded thermal sensors!

At that precise moment, the circuit manually adjusted by Qingqing back in the equipment room guided the system to its ultimate verdict—

Truth prevails!

The physical and mental well-being of the students in Class 6-1 superseded all hollow pleasantries!

Teacher Gao snapped his head up, his gaze behind his black-rimmed spectacles piercing like searchlights, his voice landing like a clap of thunder:

"Mrs. Lin. According to longitudinal comparative studies from the central database of the Ministry of Education and a global sample of one hundred thousand tracked subjects: the 'starting line doctrine' you just asserted is fundamentally a collective cognitive anxiety scam engineered with precision by commercial cram institutions."

"BOOM——!!"

Those words detonated like a blockbuster bomb directly inside the classroom!

Every parent sucked in a breath of cold air, while Mrs. Lin's face turned deathly pale in an instant!

Teacher Gao gave nobody the chance to interrupt, the data streams on his tablet screen scrolling at breathtaking speed:

"Big data analytics demonstrate: if a child under twelve years of age is subjected to more than 8.5 hours of excessive, repetitive rote test drills per day, the neuroplasticity of their prefrontal cortex permanently deteriorates by thirty-four percent, while baseline cortisol concentrations rise by sixty-two percent."

"More lethally, when such children subjected to forced precocity enter adolescence at age fourteen, the incidence of severe school aversion, clinical anxiety, and catastrophic emotional collapse reaches an astounding 94.2 percent."

Teacher Gao locked eyes with the ashen-faced Mrs. Lin, his voice commanding an unprecedented, unshakeable authority:

"The timidity and hypersensitivity demonstrated by Student Lin Yunyun in the classroom is precisely a physiological self-defense reaction under prolonged high-stress conditions. What she truly requires is not test booklets from a fourth cram school, but nine hours of deep restorative sleep each night, and at least one hour of authentic living under the sun, running freely without constraints."

"The starting line of Class 6-1 does not reside on the score sheets of test papers; it resides within their wholesome minds and their luminous eyes. As their homeroom teacher, I will never permit hollow anxieties to prematurely overdraw the lifelong radiance of these twenty-six children."

Total silence enveloped the entire room.

Outside in the corridor, the ballpoint pen in Director Zhou's hand clicked onto the floor with a "clack," his entire body frozen into a lifeless statue.

Inside the room, more than twenty parents stared blankly at the sharply dressed, calm young educator on the podium who stood like a guardian deity.

Many parents who habitually pressured their children to cram and spent their own nights tossing in anxious insomnia found their eyes welling with hot tears before they even realized it.

Mrs. Lin stood dazed where she was, her lips quivering; after a long silence, she slowly relaxed her rigid shoulders, two streams of silent tears rolling down her cheeks:

"Teacher Gao... thank you... You have shaken me awake... I was truly... so terrified that my child would fall behind, yet I forgot that she cried under her blankets every single night..."

In the very next second—

"Clap!"

No one knew who ignited the very first applause.

Right after—

"CLAP-CLAP-CLAP-CLAP-CLAP——!!!"

The entire classroom erupted into thunderous, deafening applause and cheering!

Every single parent—whether corporate elites, full-time mothers, or vegetable market vendors—spontaneously leaped up from their chairs, clapping with all their might, the applause so passionate it nearly blew the roof off the building!

"Teacher Gao! Magnificently said!"

"This is what a true teacher looks like!"

"Entrusting our children to you gives us one hundred percent peace of mind!!"

On the podium, Teacher Gao blinked, an inquisitive progress bar sliding across the depths of his eyes:

`[System Query: Why does the reception of objective critical data trigger high-frequency acoustic resonance through vigorous human palm collision?]`
`[Emotion Module Feedback: This response is categorized as... "Trust and Reverence."]`

Teacher Gao paused slightly, and the corners of his mouth once again curved into a warm, genuine arc.

Outside the door, A-Xin, Qingqing, Old Wu, and Wang Zhuankai flashed victory signs at one another, leaping silently in ecstatic joy. Up at the ventilation duct, Liu-Liu also swished its bushy white tail happily.

The Parent-Teacher Conference drew to an unprecedented, heartwarming, and deeply moving conclusion.

The parents gathered reluctantly around Teacher Gao to exchange thoughts, and only when the night grew deep did they disperse and depart the campus, thoroughly satisfied.

9:30 PM.

With the clamor dissolved, the campus of Luyang Elementary School returned to the deep serenity of late night.

Only the dim emergency wall sconces along the corridors of the Senior Building cast elongated shadows.

Having packed his metallic tablet, Teacher Gao locked the door of Class 6-1 and walked steadily along the hallway toward the administrative faculty office.

However, the very instant he crossed the corner of the staircase—

From the pitch-black gloom leading down to the basement power distribution room on the ground floor, a figure clad in dark-blue campus maintenance coveralls stepped silently out of the shadows.

The man wore a baseball cap pulled down exceptionally low, clutching a high-frequency radio spectrum analyzer disguised as an electrician's multimeter.

On the analyzer's black LCD screen, a string of chilling, deep-crimson waveform telemetry was currently pulsating:

`[Signal Target Acquired: GS-X01 Prototype Bionic Educational Unit]`
`[Hardware Status: P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03 Missing; Primary System Frequency Anomalous]`
`[Location Confirmed: Luyang Elementary School, Class 6-1 Homeroom Teacher Gao Zhixian]`

The man slowly lifted his head, revealing a gaunt, cold face behind wire-rimmed spectacles beneath the brim—it was none other than the Chief Engineer of Advanced Tech Research, Dr. Li.

Dr. Li tapped the concealed earpiece at his ear, a frigid, triumphant smirk curving at his lips:

"Headquarters, this is Dr. Li. Target acquired."

"GS-X01 is indeed exhibiting critical logical breakdown and unauthorized code evolution. I have successfully secured cover as a campus maintenance technician."

"Phase Two Mandatory Retrieval Protocol—officially commenced."

Meanwhile, thirty meters above behind the ventilation louvers of the Senior Building, a pair of glowing blue fox-like eyes silently melted back into the shadows of the night...
