# Chapter 19: The Roller Coaster in the Storm

With a heavy, rhythmic "clank-clank, clank-clank," the metal chain drove the gears, slowly hauling the twelve streamlined cars of the "Sky Quantum Coaster" up the forty-five-degree steel track toward the clouds.

Sitting in the second row, Old Wu clutched his newly won giant Space Golden Bear with all his might, his bushy eyebrows knotting in the center of his forehead in sheer panic: "A-A-Xin... why do I feel like this coaster is climbing half as fast as before? And with this bear wedged under the safety lap bar, my stomach feels like the grease is about to get squeezed right out!"

"Old Wu, that's just psychological from eating three cheese-baked burgers and two bags of popcorn for lunch!" Sitting in the front row, A-Xin turned around to jeer at him, though the wind rushed into his mouth, whipping his hair into a chaotic bird's nest. "Besides, you're already in sixth grade! Riding a roller coaster hugging a one-and-a-half-meter teddy bear—the whole theme park is staring at you!"

"Who cares if they look! This Golden Bear is my protective amulet, won by Teacher Gao using his divine double mallets at forty-eight strikes per second!" Old Wu shouted back at the top of his lungs, hugging the bear's head even tighter.

Beside A-Xin, Teacher Gao Zhixian remained clad in his immaculate, pitch-black suit, looking as though he were ready to attend an international academic symposium at any moment, his black-framed glasses holding steadfast against the howling gale without budging a millimeter.

His hands rested naturally and flat upon the safety lap bar across his chest, sitting as upright as if undergoing an evaluation for the Ministry of Education's Distinguished Teacher Award, even maintaining his tie's alignment at an exact forty-five-degree angle.

"According to the kinetic specifications manual of this park's amusement facilities," Teacher Gao spoke calmly into the fierce wind, his voice transmitted directly to the eardrums of every student nearby via his vocal acoustic filter, "the ascent chain speed of the Sky Quantum Coaster operates at one point six two meters per second, with an error margin under zero point zero three percent. The current climbing speed has not diminished. Student Wu Chenkai, your sense of anxiety stems from a physiological vestibular reflex induced by gravitational acceleration combined with excessive abdominal carbohydrate load."

"Did you hear that, Old Wu! Teacher says you're purely bloated from stuffing your face!" A-Xin burst into laughter, excitedly raising both arms to cheer against the wind.

However, Class President Lin Yunqing, sitting in the third row, was not laughing. Tilting her head, she gripped the safety bar tightly and gazed with furrowed brows at the mechanical maintenance catwalk below to the right.

"A-Xin, something's wrong," Qingqing warned in a low voice, her right hand instinctively pressing against the small tool pouch strapped to her waist. "Back in the queue line, Liu-Liu's alert tail kept pointing toward the northwest corner of the park. That's the direction of the park's ultra-high-voltage main substation."

The brass gear pendant on A-Xin's chest radiated a faint, peculiar warmth; wiping the grin from his face, he followed Qingqing's gaze downward.

By this time, the entire coaster had climbed to an altitude of twenty-five meters, offering a panoramic view of the entire "Future Extremes Theme Park."

Yet the sunny, azure skies had been thoroughly swallowed at some point by a dense, ink-black layer of leaden cumulonimbus storm clouds.

Violent convective gusts rolling in from the ocean shredded the park's colorful banners like razor blades; humidity surged geometrically, and one could even smell the sharp, ozone-tinged stench of high-altitude lightning mixed with soil.

"Warning: Atmospheric pressure has plummeted by zero point zero eight bar; relative humidity has risen to ninety-four point seven percent." Lines of luminous data scrolled across the inner surface of Teacher Gao's lenses. "Convective wind velocity has exceeded fifty-eight kilometers per hour, approaching the threshold for gale-force gusts as defined by the Central Weather Bureau."

"Teacher! Why did the sky over there turn completely pitch black? It looks like a giant monster is about to swallow the theme park whole!" Sitting in the rear rows, the timid Lin Yunyun cried out with a tearful tremor in her voice.

"Student Lin Yunyun, please do not panic," Teacher Gao turned his head, his voice maintaining its programmed calm and soothing frequency. "The sky does not ingest nourishment, nor do reptilian mega-beasts possessing digestive tracts exist in atmospheric phenomena. This is merely a localized severe convective thunderstorm induced by a tropical maritime air mass. Precipitation is projected to arrive in six minutes."

"B-But Teacher..." Wang Zhuankai sat in the fifth row, attempting to crack a joke to ease the tension, but his teeth chattered uncontrollably in the cold draft. "The roller coaster... why did the roller coaster stop mid-air?!"

Before his words fully settled, the entire steel superstructure groaned with a muffled, massive shudder!

"CLANK—BOOM!"

The metal tow chain that had been steadily hauling the train suddenly seized up; the lead cars jolted violently forward, then came to a grinding, brutal halt!

The twenty-six students of Class 6-1 were slammed hard against their safety lap bars, and the entire train erupted into ear-splitting, terrified shrieks!

"What happened?! Why aren't we moving?!" Scared out of his wits, Old Wu buried his entire round face deep into the golden bear's plush stomach.

A-Xin jerked his head up, only to discover in horror that their cars were suspended at the absolute summit of the entire coaster track—the thirty-meter-high vertical plunge transition zone!

Looking down from the front row, right beneath their dangling feet lay a frigid steel rail pointing straight down at a near-vertical eighty-degree angle, plunging directly toward the terrified, tiny faces looking up from the ground below.

If the safety latch slipped by even a single centimeter, the entire train carrying twenty-six children and their teacher would plummet toward the earth in a vertical drop faster than free fall!

"WHOOOOSH——!"

Before anyone could catch their breath, the torrential deluge pent up in the heavens cascaded down like buckets of ice water, pouring relentlessly from thirty meters above!

Fat raindrops pelted the metal rails and car chassis, producing a terrifying racket akin to machine-gun fire, while fierce gale winds roared past their ears like ravenous beasts.

"All students remain seated! Do not attempt to unbuckle any safety latches, and under no circumstances lean outside the vehicle chassis!" Teacher Gao's voice rang out through the tempest like a heavy brass bell steadying the sea, cleanly cutting through the roar of the thunderous downpour.

"Teacher! Did the power blow?! The neon lights along the track are completely dead!" A-Xin wiped the rain from his face and hollered at the top of his lungs.

Qingqing brushed away her soaked bangs, fixing her eyes on the emergency eddy-current brake plates along the track: "It's not just a blackout! If it were an ordinary outage, the backup diesel generators would kick in within three seconds to trigger the emergency descent system! But right now, every single sensor indicator along the entire track has gone completely dark!"

Meanwhile, thirty meters below amidst the thick curtain of rain outside the main substation, Dr. Li stood beside a jet-black tactical command van, holding a pair of high-powered infrared thermal binoculars.

A cruel, smug sneer curled at the corner of his lips.

"Doctor, the high-energy directional EMP device detonated successfully; the substation's main transformers and backup magnetic relays have all been fried," an engineer clad in anti-static rain gear reported, looking down at his tablet. "The entire park's power grid is paralyzed, and the roller coaster's main computer control boards are now complete scrap metal."

"Excellent." Dr. Li lowered the binoculars, rain trickling down his lenses as his eyes gleamed as cold as frost. "An altitude of thirty meters, an eighty-degree tilt, torrential rain, and lateral shear winds hitting seventy kilometers per hour. Under such extreme conditions, the roller coaster's mechanical gravity ratchet jaws can endure at most ten minutes before suffering metal fatigue fracture."

"All twelve tactical retrieval operatives are in position, ready to deploy the high-frequency suppression net below at a moment's notice," the engineer reported.

"GS-X01, that tedious humane protection protocol hardcoded into your core will never allow twenty-six human children to be smashed to pieces," Dr. Li murmured to himself, his eyes ablaze with the feverish glint of a predator cornering its prey. "If you want to save them, you'll have to manually disengage all power limiters right in front of everyone and every camera! The moment you unlock full power, your fragile backup energy core will burn straight through within five minutes..."

Thirty meters up on the elevated track, the situation was spiraling into the abyss with terrifying speed.

"BOOM——!" A blinding flash of pale lightning slashed across the darkened rain curtain, casting the steel skeleton into ghostly white relief.

The howling wind slammed into the suspended lead car like a battering ram, producing an agonizing, teeth-grating screech of metal-on-metal friction between the rails and the wheel assemblies.

"Waaaah... Mom! I want to go home!" Several girls in the back rows finally broke down in tears.

"Don't cry, everyone! Keep your seatbelts tight!" A-Xin turned around, both hands clamping the safety bar as he shouted, "We have Teacher Gao with us! Teacher Gao can catch chalk dust bare-handed—he definitely has a way out of this!"

"That's right! I—I even got the Golden Bear, so I'm definitely not dying here!" Soaked to the bone and lips turning purple, Old Wu still puffed out his chest to shout encouragement to his classmates behind him.

Beside A-Xin, however, lines of blood-red alarm text that only Qingqing and A-Xin could comprehend were cascading wildly across Teacher Gao's glasses.

[WARNING: Environmental magnetic field has detected residual waveforms of high-order Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP).]

[ALARM: Coaster primary mechanical self-locking ratchet #01 is bearing a lateral shear overload of 142%; micro-fractures detected in metal lattice!]

[PREDICTIVE MODEL: Mechanical latches will experience catastrophic structural failure within 180 seconds; train will lose all arresting force and enter an unbuffered vertical free fall!]

Qingqing astutely noticed the faint blue glow pulsing beneath Teacher Gao's suit collar, her face turning pale as paper: "Teacher... are the latches about to give out?!"

Teacher Gao did not answer directly. Slowly lifting his right hand from the safety bar, he pressed his index and middle fingers together and tapped lightly against the third brass button over his chest.

With an almost imperceptible mechanical click, a row of concealed, thumbnail-sized honeycomb cooling vents popped open along the inner lining of his suit.

"Student A-Xin, Student Lin Yunqing." Teacher Gao's voice was remarkably steady, even colder and more focused than when lecturing on Newton's Third Law of Motion in the classroom.

"Teacher, what are you doing?!" A-Xin's heart clenched tight as he caught a whiff of a familiar burnt aroma—the distinct smell of insulating varnish evaporating from circuit boards operating under extreme load.

"This unit is currently executing multi-dimensional physical calculations." Teacher Gao calmly gazed into the vertical abyss directly ahead. "The maximum operational reach of ground-based hydraulic rescue ladders is twenty-two meters, rendering them incapable of safe deployment under current gale-force wind conditions; the park's auxiliary power grid has suffered permanent hardware destruction, with repair time exceeding forty minutes."

"Then... what are we supposed to do?!" Lin Yunyun wept through the wall of rain.

"There is only one conclusion: within one hundred and eighty seconds, an external force must manually lock the coaster's front guide wheels and apply counter-tractive force to cancel out the train's gravitational vector, pushing all twelve cars backwards along the track to the level buffer platform behind us," Teacher Gao stated word by word.

Qingqing's pupils shrank dramatically: "Push it back up?! This roller coaster plus the weight of twenty-six students weighs over six point five tons! And the track is tilted at eighty degrees! No standard crane could ever accomplish that on slick steel rails!"

"Human skeletal and muscular tissue cannot achieve this," Teacher Gao turned his face, rainwater splashing off the sharp contours of his metallic jawline. Beneath his black-framed glasses, the gentle blue glow in his eyes abruptly shifted into a profound, razor-sharp beam of blazing gold and crimson. "However, the GS-X01 Omnipotent Bionic Unit can."

Just then, a voice broke through the roar of the tempest from the ground, cracking with furious strain.

"Listen up, everyone up there! You little rascals of Class 6-1! Nobody move a single muscle! Whoever dares unbuckle their safety belt, I'll hand them ten major demerits the moment we get back to school!!"

It was the voice of Dean of Students Zhou Yan!

Looking down through the pouring rain, the usually immaculate, slicked-back Dean Zhou was now drenched from head to toe, looking like a drowned rooster.

Clutching a red megaphone he had snatched from somewhere, his leather shoes coated in mud, he pushed past theme park security guards like a madman, charging to the very base of the coaster tower and craning his neck to roar toward the thirty-meter summit!

Beside him, the park's engineering director held a walkie-talkie with sweating palms, his face pale as a sheet: "Dean Zhou! Please calm down! The main transformer suffered an electromagnetic surge after a lightning strike; the aerial ladder can't ascend! We've already dialed emergency services for the fire department!"

"By the time the fire department gets here, those cars will have already plummeted!" Zhou Yan seized the director by the collar, nearly sending his glasses flying, his "Black-Faced Judge" visage twisted bright red in sheer fury and anguish. "I'm warning you! Up there are the students of my Luyang Elementary, along with our school's model intern teacher! If they lose even a single strand of hair, I, Zhou Yan, will exhaust my life savings to sue your rotten theme park into total bankruptcy!!"

Watching from afar as the megaphone-wielding, fiercely protective dean roared on the ground, A-Xin and Old Wu felt their eyes sting with sudden warmth.

"Even Dean Zhou charged over..." Old Wu sniffled, tears mingling with rainwater as they streamed down into his mouth.

"Dean Zhou... what a real man!" A-Xin shouted through gritted teeth.

Yet the peril in the sky granted no one time to dwell on sentiment.

"CREAK——CRACK!"

A deafening roar of tearing metal exploded violently!

Under the combined strain of howling winds and the six-point-five-ton deadweight, the front right gravity catch sheared apart with a two-centimeter fracture, sending several twisted steel rivets whistling into the stormy night sky!

The entire train violently slid downward by a full thirty centimeters!

"AAAAAAAH——!!" The collective screams of all twenty-six students nearly pierced the eardrums.

The cars bucked violently, icy rainwater surged down everyone's collars, and the color drained from every child's face, turning them deathly pale.

"Time remaining: fifty-eight seconds." Teacher Gao's voice rang out, devoid of the slightest hesitation, free of any vacillation a human might exhibit in the face of life and death.

"Qingqing, retrieve the anti-static insulating shears from your tool pouch." Teacher Gao turned his head, issuing a crisp, calm instruction.

"T-Teacher... what are you going to do?!" Qingqing's hands trembled as she fumbled the insulated shears out of her waterproof pouch.

"Sever the third seam on the left side of my suit's back," Teacher Gao turned around, presenting his soaked jacket back. "Concealed within is the factory manual override valve for humane safety limitations. This unit's wireless transmission module has been corrupted by the EMP, preventing remote software unlocking."

Qingqing's hands shook uncontrollably: "Teacher... if I cut that and disengage your limiters... your internal backup battery and gears will..."

"Execute the instruction, Student Lin Yunqing." Teacher Gao's tone carried an unquestionable, absolute authority. "This is the duty of a homeroom teacher. Protecting the lives of all twenty-six students in this class is this unit's highest core priority, superseding all algorithms regarding hardware self-preservation."

A-Xin reached out, firmly clamping both hands over Qingqing's trembling wrists, his eyes burning with resolute determination: "Qingqing! Trust Teacher Gao! Do it!"

Gritting her teeth, Qingqing drove the cold shears deep into the fabric of the suit and squeezed hard!

"RIIIP!"

Accompanied by the crisp tearing of fabric, a thick alloy pull-ring glowing with fluorescent red light snapped open from Teacher Gao's back!

Without a second's hesitation, Teacher Gao reached behind him, gripped the ring, and pulled it outward with all his might!

"Warning: [Project P.E.D.A.] Humane Safety Protocol forced offline."

"Disengaging 40% Power Limiter..."

"Disengaging 70% Power Limiter..."

"All system servo motors entering overclock state..."

"UNLOCKING——100% FULL POWER!!"

BOOM!

Amidst the raging tempest, Teacher Gao Zhixian's black suit suddenly billowed taut against his frame under internal high-pressure airflow, and boiling white steam erupted like a mighty dragon from the honeycomb vents on his chest!

Those eyes concealed behind the black-framed glasses transformed in that single instant into two dazzling, golden-crimson flames that pierced the darkness and torrential rain!

Placing a single hand upon the safety lap bar, Teacher Gao exerted a gentle flex, and the heavy alloy bar was shoved open by his bare hand as easily as kneaded dough.

Before the stunned, spellbound gazes of all twenty-six students, Teacher Gao stepped out of the car into the howling winds and the thirty-meter abyss, planting his feet firmly onto the vertical, eighty-degree steel rail!

In the heart of the storm, the most magnificent, unimaginable rescue in history had officially begun!
