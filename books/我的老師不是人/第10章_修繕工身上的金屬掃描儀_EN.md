# *My Teacher Is Not Human*

## Chapter 10: The Metal Scanner on the Repairman

On a Monday morning, sunlight streamed through the window frames along the Senior Building corridor, slicing golden rectangles across the tiled floor.

For Class 6-1 of Luyang Elementary School, this should have been a wonderful morning basking in the sweet glow of triumph. Last Friday's Parent-Teacher Conference had been an overwhelming victory; the parents of all twenty-six students had flooded the messaging group with thousands of accolades for Teacher Gao. Parents who used to pore over daily communication notebooks hunting for faults had broken precedent by granting their children a two-day "amnesty holiday" completely free from cram-school test sheets.

Yet, beside the desk closest to the back door, A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu felt none of the relaxed atmosphere.

"A-Xin... do you get the feeling that the air in the corridor is unusually quiet today?" Old Wu murmured under his breath with half a freshly baked red-bean mochi bun dangling from his mouth, eyeing the hallway suspiciously.

"It's not quiet—it smells of motor oil and high-frequency electric currents." Qingqing pulled a slender, homemade miniature antenna from her backpack, secretly plugging it into a small multimeter she carried with her.

On the multimeter's LCD display, the needle was trembling at an extremely subtle frequency, tuned squarely to 200.4 MHz—a specialized radio frequency band reserved exclusively for military specifications and cutting-edge laboratory equipment!

With a solemn expression, A-Xin pressed his hand against the chilly brass planetary gear resting beneath his uniform shirt: "Qingqing, what are you picking up?"

"A high-intensity metal scanning signal is approaching our floor." Qingqing bit her lip, her complexion turning pale. "And this isn't an ordinary metal detector; it's a directional pulse wave. If this signal sweeps within one meter of Teacher Gao, the communication chip in his body will be forcibly handshake-connected, uploading his bionic internal code straight to the scanner!"

A-Xin sucked in a sharp breath.

Just then, an extremely faint "click" came from the top ventilation louver near the ceiling.

A fluffy white flash descended from the duct with a swift "whoosh!"

It was none other than their AI pet, Liu-Liu!

In its mouth, Liu-Liu held a scrap of waste paper torn from the corridor bulletin board, landing deftly upon A-Xin's desk. Its snow-white fur stood entirely on end like it had been electrocuted, its obsidian-gem eyes flashing urgent, glaring orange-red warning beacons!

Liu-Liu spat out the scrap of paper, its two forepaws rapidly sketching a large "wrench" and an "antenna" on the desk surface, followed by an eerily accurate vocal mimicry of a cold sneer from its throat:

"\"Target acquired... Phase Two Mandatory Retrieval Protocol initiated...\""

That was Dr. Li's original voice transmission, intercepted and perfectly recorded by Liu-Liu inside the ventilation shafts!

The core group members—including Wang Zhuankai and Lin Yunyun sitting nearby—instantly broke into cold sweats the second they heard the recording.

"Dr. Li... he really snuck into the school!" Wang Zhuankai was so terrified his pencil nearly tumbled to the floor.

"He's coming for Teacher Gao." Lin Yunyun's face was stark white, her voice trembling slightly. "If Teacher is scanned and found missing a gear, he'll be hauled off and dismantled for scrap today..."

"Over our dead bodies!" A-Xin slammed his desk, his eyes blazing with unprecedented resolve.

He glanced toward the podium where Teacher Gao was standing before the morning bell, holding a large set square to meticulously calibrate the perpendicular alignment of the chalkboard lines.

In his sharp suit and spotless black-rimmed glasses, Teacher Gao appeared tranquil and dignified, utterly unaware that a lethal hunt targeting his existence was closing in step by step. To Teacher Gao, having lost the common-sense damper of [Core-Gear #03], he possessed no means of actively deploying anti-reconnaissance stealth shielding.

"Listen up, everyone!" A-Xin lowered his voice, summoning all twenty-six classmates to the back of the room.

Twenty-six youthful yet determined faces formed a tight, resolute circle.

"Everyone. During midterms last week, Teacher Gao preferred to scorch his own circuits with an emergency cutoff rather than expose us to cheating allegations. Last Friday at the PTA meeting, he risked thermal overload and smoking exhaust to shield every single one of us from cram-school torture with big data." A-Xin raised a clenched fist, his tone low yet brimming with conviction. "Now, it's our turn to protect Teacher Gao!"

"Too right! Anyone who tries to scrap Teacher Gao will have to survive me flattening them with my body weight first!" Old Wu swallowed his last bite of red-bean bun and clenched his ham-sized fists.

"Qingqing, what's the battle plan?" The athletes and mischief-makers of the class were itching for action.

Adjusting her goggles, Qingqing dumped two large bags of colorful items from her backpack: "First line of defense: Zero Gravitational Friction Grid! Second line of defense: Airborne Particulate Optical Smoke Screen! As long as we stall him until the end of the first period, Dr. Li won't be able to strike in broad daylight under everyone's eyes!"

"Understood!" twenty-six students growled in unison, eyes ignited with fighting spirit.

"Clomp, clomp, clomp..."

From the far end of the corridor, heavy, rhythmic footsteps echoed slowly.

A man dressed in dark-blue electrician coveralls and a gray baseball cap approached Class 6-1 with deliberate, steady strides. Over his shoulder hung a heavy metal toolbox; clipped into his breast pocket was what seemed to be an ordinary metal probe, while his right hand was shoved deep into his baggy jumpsuit pocket, index finger resting firmly against the trigger button of an ultra-high-frequency full-spectrum pulse transmitter.

It was none other than the Chief Engineer of Advanced Tech Research—Dr. Li.

Beneath the brim of his cap, Dr. Li's lips curled into a chilling sneer. As the chief architect of the GS-X01, he knew the fatal vulnerability of this prototype all too well.

The moment he reached the entrance of Class 6-1 and aimed the directional scanner inside his toolbox at Gao Zhixian at the podium, within three seconds, Gao Zhixian's CPU would be forcibly locked into "Diagnostic Standby Mode." At that point, under the pretext that "an electrical inspection triggered a medical seizure in the faculty member," Dr. Li could openly summon an emergency ambulance from headquarters to haul this rogue bionic robot back to the incinerator!

Ten meters from the front door of Class 6-1.

Eight meters.

Five meters!

Drawing a deep breath, Dr. Li slowly drew the pitch-black pulse transmitter, its tip blinking with red light, from his pocket—

At that razor's-edge instant!

"Aaaaiiiieee——! My invincible, super-duper mega-sized marble collection, ahhhh!"

Accompanied by an earth-shattering, melodramatic wail, Old Wu's hefty frame came tumbling and rolling violently out of the front entrance of Class 6-1!

Simultaneously, a tin biscuit box clutched in Old Wu's arms "accidentally" flipped upside down onto the floor!

"Clatter-clash-rattle-roll——!!"

A staggering five hundred crystal-clear, two-centimeter-wide heavy glass marbles exploded across the tiles like an unleashed flash flood, rolling and ricocheting wildly, instantly carpeting the entire corridor!

Before Dr. Li could even comprehend what was happening, the thick, insulated rubber soles of his boots landed squarely onto three high-speed rolling marbles!

"Crack-slip! Whoooosh——!"

Human equilibrium in the face of slick floor tiles and rolling spheres collapsed into total, catastrophic ruin within a microsecond!

"Waaaaagh——!"

Dr. Li let out a bloodcurdling shriek as his entire body went airborne like a boneless kite! His metal toolbox slipped from his grip, smashing violently into the wall, while he executed a textbook one-hundred-and-eighty-degree breakdance spin atop the rolling marbles before slamming his back flat against the floor with a hollow "THUMP"!

"Oh my goodness! Mister Electrician, how could you be so clumsy?!" Wang Zhuankai shouted, leading three boys rushing out with brooms to "rescue" the marbles, their brooms sweeping "inadvertently" across Dr. Li's legs, knocking him flat just as he struggled to stand.

"Get lost... you little brats..." Dr. Li saw stars spinning before his eyes, his spine throbbing as if snapped, his face flushed scarlet with fury.

Enduring the agony, he scrambled forward, reaching desperately for the black metallic scanner that had slid into the corner!

The sensor head of the device was already pointing toward the classroom door, its indicator light shifting from yellow to an imminent lock-on orange!

"Second defense line, deploy!"

From behind the doorway came A-Xin's thunderous command!

In the very next second, both the front and back doors of Class 6-1 swung wide open!

Led by Qingqing and Lin Yunyun, eight girls, each clutching two thick sponge chalkboard erasers saturated with dense chalk dust, aimed into the corridor space around Dr. Li and slammed them together with all their might!

"CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! CLAP——!!"

An overwhelmingly dense, sky-blotting cloud of chalk dust erupted like a volcanic detonation!

Within one second, the entire corridor was submerged under an impenetrable white fog, dropping visibility straight to zero!

"Cough! Hack! Cough-cough-cough-cough——!!"

Opening his mouth to gasp for air, Dr. Li was instantly force-fed a mouthful of bitter, choking calcium carbonate powder, his eyes watering intensely from the stinging dust as he coughed convulsively. Worse still, the high concentration of chalk particles coated the scanner's optical sensor lens, rendering the precision infrared targeting system entirely blind!

"Curse you... you wretched little devils..." Dr. Li roared in blind hysteria. Relying solely on his last spatial memory, he squeezed his eyes shut, leveled the scanner, and charged forward toward the podium like an enraged bull!

"Just within three meters... the pulse will penetrate..."

Roaring through the chalk smoke, Dr. Li thrust the scanner straight toward the podium ahead!

Only 1.5 meters from the podium!

The red LED atop the scanner emitted a piercing shriek: [Target Proximity Alert: 1.5m], the forced lock-on sequence entering its final one-second countdown!

Just as a triumphant smirk began to form at the corners of Dr. Li's lips—

A slender, chilly hand, unyielding as a hydraulic robotic arm, emerged silently from the thinning haze.

"Snap!"

That hand clamped down with flawless precision around Dr. Li's raised right wrist.

The grip was incomprehensibly immense; Dr. Li felt as though his wrist bone had been welded solid into a twenty-ton industrial forge press—moving even half a millimeter was a complete impossibility!

The smoke slowly parted.

Before the podium, Teacher Gao Zhixian stood in his sharply tailored dark-gray suit, completely immaculate and devoid of a single speck of dust. His black-rimmed glasses gleamed with tranquil radiance as he looked down upon the disheveled, white-powder-covered Dr. Li.

Deep within Teacher Gao's pupils, lines of computational code flowed at millions of cycles per second:

`[Hazard Identification: Construction personnel without protective gear lost footing and fell within the instructional zone]`
`[Apparatus Analysis: Holding uncertified high-voltage arcing device non-compliant with national safety standards]`
`[Literal Interpretation Protocol: Illicit electrical apparatus poses fire hazard; executing on-site safe grounding discharge...]`

"Construction worker," Teacher Gao's voice rang out, calm and authoritative, completely devoid of inflection. "Pursuant to Article 14 of the Campus Occupational Safety and Health Regulations, conducting electrical operations in an indoor educational setting without dust goggles or anti-slip rubber matting, while brandishing an ungrounded, high-frequency short-circuit hazard device, constitutes an imminent threat to the physical safety of the twenty-six students present."

"Let go of me! You malfunctioning prototype unit——" Dr. Li shrieked through gritted teeth, his left hand reaching desperately to press the forced-overload toggle on the back of the unit.

However, Teacher Gao gave him no such opening.

From the tip of Teacher Gao's right index finger, a discreet, faint blue electric arc sparked to life.

"Fingertip microwave high-frequency grounding discharge—execute."

"Zzz-zzz-crack-POP!!"

With a crisp snap like popping corn, a reverse micro-current surged violently into the black scanner clutched in Dr. Li's hand!

The internal superconducting capacitors inside the scanner overloaded in a split second, belching a wisp of scorched black smoke as the entire LCD screen shattered into a spiderweb of cracks, instantly reduced to an unresponsive brick of scrap metal!

Dr. Li stared dumbfounded at the smoking black lump in his palm, completely struck dumb.

His pride and joy, a specialized pulse scanner worth over a million dollars... had just been electrostatically fried bare-handed by this robot?!

Before Dr. Li could recover from the shock—

"WHAT IN HEAVEN'S NAME ARE YOU ALL DOING——?!!"

A roar like rolling thunder detonated at the classroom doorway!

Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan strode into the room brandishing his infraction notebook, his face darker than thunderclouds. When he beheld the floor carpeted in marbles, the clouds of chalk dust, and the maintenance worker splayed before the podium white as a flour sack, the veins on his temples pulsed with violent fury!

"Director Zhou!" A-Xin sprang forward first, pointing at Dr. Li with an aggrieved expression. "This electrician uncle wasn't watching where he was going! He stepped on our science class gravity balls and took a tumble, then almost struck Teacher Gao with a sparking, defective tool! Thank goodness Teacher Gao was fast enough to catch him bare-handed, or the room would have caught fire!"

"Yeah, yeah! We were just using chalkboard erasers to smother the electrical short-circuit sparks!" Old Wu fabricated without batting an eyelid, still clutching a dusty eraser.

Director Zhou looked down at the scorched, smoking device on the floor, then glared at the miserable Dr. Li, his expression grim enough to drip water:

"How could Campus Facilities send such an utterly incompetent contractor?! Causing a short-circuit explosion with a simple voltmeter! You nearly injured our model educator and students! Pack up your junk immediately and report to the Office of Student Affairs to draft a safety violation report! Don't you dare dream of getting your sign-off sheet signed until you write a three-thousand-word self-criticism!"

Sprayed with Director Zhou's furious spit, Dr. Li looked up at the cold, towering Teacher Gao on the podium, and then at the twenty-six grinning, fist-clenching students surrounding him.

He knew that today's ambush had ended in utter defeat.

"Fine... you win this round." Gritting his teeth, Dr. Li glared murderously at A-Xin, scooped up his wrecked toolbox, and limped out of the room under Director Zhou's piercing scowl.

The classroom fell into a momentary stillness.

Right after—

"YAAAAAY——!!!"

All twenty-six students erupted into an ear-splitting cheer! Old Wu and Wang Zhuankai danced together in a bear hug, Qingqing exhaled a long breath of relief, and A-Xin exchanged a triumphant, knowing glance with Teacher Gao.

Atop the podium, Teacher Gao lowered his gaze to look upon the jubilant children, the indicator lights behind his lenses flickering gently:

`[Threat Cleared: 0]`
`[Classroom Morale Index: 100%]`

The corners of Teacher Gao's mouth curled into a standard forty-five-degree smile as he tapped the blackboard with a piece of chalk:

"Quiet down. Although the occupational hazard has been successfully mitigated, pursuant to class rules, the five hundred marbles and chalk dust in the corridor must be cleaned collaboratively before the conclusion of period one. Now, open your textbooks to page forty-five."

Meanwhile, outside the school gates, Dr. Li sat inside a black sedan, wiping chalk dust from his face with a wet wipe while grimly pulling up the backup terminal on his wrist watch.

On the terminal display, a 0.5-second spectrum analysis captured right before the scanner fried remained vividly visible:

`[Core Anomaly Confirmed: GS-X01 Primary Seventh Gear Cluster Missing]`
`[Self-Repair Probability: 0%]`
`[Status: System mechanical wear accelerating; secondary drivetrain failure projected within 10 days.]`

Gazing at the fault forecast on the screen, Dr. Li's lips twisted into a cruel, venomous smirk:

"Gao Zhixian... and you little brats in Class 6-1..."

"You can block my scanner, but you cannot defy the laws of mechanical wear and tear."

"This game has only just begun to get interesting..."
