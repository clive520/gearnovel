# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 2: The Clinking Sound in Front of the Blackboard

The first period was language arts class.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the most unbelievable spectacle since the founding of the school took place: twenty-six students who usually chattered like wound-up monkeys now sat with their backs ramrod straight and hands flat on their desks, behaving as solemnly as if they were being received by the president.

After all, who dared make a move!

Right before the bell rang, the mischievous prankster Chen Ada from the neighboring class was dribbling a basketball past the hallway when the ball accidentally slipped from his hands and flew right through the window of Class 6-1. Without even turning his head, Teacher Gao Zhixian casually reached backward, catching the high-speed spinning basketball firmly on his fingertips with a single hand, and even offhandedly reported that the ball's internal pressure was "8.7 psi, inflation recommended."

"Now, everyone please direct your attention to the blackboard."

Teacher Gao turned around and picked up a piece of white chalk.

The speed at which he wrote on the blackboard was astonishingly fast, the cadence of chalk striking slate resembling a rapid-fire machine gun: "Da-da-da-da-da-da!"

Every horizontal stroke and vertical line looked as if calibrated with a laser level, the script so standardized that it seemed as though the textbook had been scanned and projected directly onto the board. The entire class stared open-mouthed, and Lin Yunqing, sitting in the very front row, held her breath in awe.

However, just as Teacher Gao was writing the final stroke of the last word "learn"—

"Squeak... bzzzz..."

A very faint, sharp, friction-laden screech, resembling dry metallic gears grinding against one another, emanated from behind Teacher Gao's collar at the nape of his neck.

In the vent behind the blackboard, the two pointy ears of the AI pet "Liu-Liu" twitched violently. The micro-sensor on its snout emitted a faint "beep-beep," and its tiny head peeked out with intense wariness.

Immediately after, Teacher Gao's motion froze abruptly for 0.5 seconds.

His right arm, like that of a damp clockwork doll, twitched with a microscopic tremor.

"Clink-clank—!!"

A crisp, unmistakable metallic crash reverberated loudly across the silent classroom!

A heavy little object slipped from the edge of Teacher Gao's hairline at the back of his head, slammed onto the wooden podium, bounced twice, and then rolled clattering all the way down the slightly sloped terrazzo floor.

"Clatter-clatter... clatter-clatter..."

The gazes of twenty-six pairs of eyes locked onto that rolling golden dot like radar. Liu-Liu even slid nimbly down the curtains, hiding behind the windowsill, its twin electronic eyeballs zooming rapidly like camera lenses.

The golden dot rolled past Lin Yunqing's desk, grazed past the plastic bag of Old Wu's dropped pork bun on the floor, and finally—thud!—bumped right into the toe of Lu Lixin's (A-Xin's) sneaker, wobbled twice, and lay flat in silence.

A-Xin instinctively glanced down.

It wasn't a coin, nor was it a ballpoint pen part.

It was a "brass planetary gear" about the size of a fifty-dollar coin, gleaming with a cold, dark-golden luster. The gear's teeth were machined to the absolute extreme, densely interlocked with twenty-four miniature cogs, and the center was engraved with a microscopic serial code: 【P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03】.

"...A gear?" A-Xin muttered under his breath.

Why on earth... would a gear fall out of the back of someone's head?!

A-Xin jerked his head up toward the podium.

There stood Teacher Gao Zhixian, still facing away from everyone, maintaining the posture of holding chalk suspended in midair, completely motionless for five whole seconds.

Then, the vertebrae of Teacher Gao's neck produced a winding sound reminiscent of an antique clockwork spring: "Click, click, click."

He slowly turned around.

Lin Yunqing, sitting in the front row, gasped sharply—inside the lenses of Teacher Gao's black-framed glasses, the faint blue light that previously signified normal operations was now flashing frantically with a glaring orange-yellow flare!

Along the rims of the lenses, a stream of green micro-code faintly cascaded:

`[WARNING]`
`[Critical Component Missing: #03 Common-Sense Damper OFFLINE]`
`[Fallback Mode: Literal Interpretation ENABLED]`

"Teacher..."

Sitting in the second row, the chubby girl Lin Yunyun timidly raised her hand, asking with a pale face, "Teacher... did your head just drop a spare part?"

Teacher Gao adjusted his glasses. The moment he pushed the frame, the orange-yellow glare was forcefully suppressed, returning to tranquil transparency.

"Student Lin Yunyun, this system... ahem, my biological body is composed of sixty percent water, protein, and calcium. It does not possess detachable components."

Teacher Gao's tone remained calm, but upon close listening, there seemed to be a subtle mechanical latency in his voice: "The sound just now was merely an acoustic resonance generated between my neural synapses and airborne particulates. A purely natural physiological phenomenon."

The whole class looked at each other in bewilderment. Even Liu-Liu on the windowsill tilted its furry little head, displaying a giant "?" symbol on its screen-eyes.

"Acoustic resonance between neural synapses and air particles" could make such a resounding metallic clatter? Who would believe that!

At that moment, A-Xin covertly stretched his foot out, using his shoe sole to hook that golden gear beneath his chair, moving as swift as a groundhog stealing sweet potatoes.

A-Xin raised his hand: "Report, Teacher Gao!"

Teacher Gao turned his gaze toward A-Xin: "Student Lu Lixin, speak."

"Teacher, that first class was way too intense. Right now, my stomach is so hungry it's about to be flattened flat!" A-Xin ventured testing the waters.

Under normal circumstances, any human teacher hearing this would at most roll their eyes and say "Hold on until lunch" or "Who told you to skip breakfast?"

However—

Upon hearing those words, Teacher Gao's pupils instantly dilated!

"Instruction received: Student Lu Lixin's abdominal thickness is decreasing precipitously, imminent transition to a two-dimensional flat plane."

Teacher Gao's expression turned intensely grave. Taking long, sweeping strides, he marched down from the podium, rushing straight toward A-Xin's desk accompanied by a gust of wind!

"Whoa? T-Teacher, what are you doing?!" A-Xin was so startled he nearly fell off his chair.

"Emergency human body thickness rescue protocol engaged."

Teacher Gao whipped out a yellow flexible engineering tape measure from inside his suit pocket with a swoosh, snapping it around A-Xin's waist at the speed of light!

"Current abdominal thickness: 18.5 centimeters. Safety margin before absolute two-dimensional flattening: 18.49 centimeters. To prevent visceral organ dysfunction caused by total flattening, pneumatic expansion filling must be administered immediately."

Teacher Gao turned his head, his fiery gaze sweeping over Old Wu's desk: "Student Wu Chenkai, immediately requisition the backup cabbage pork bun from your drawer to perform oral pressure-filling on Student Lu!"

The entire class: "???????????"

The pork bun in Old Wu's hand was so shocked that he accidentally bit halfway into it himself: "T-Teacher! A-Xin was just saying he's hungry!"

"'About to be flattened' constitutes an explicit physical state assertion. This system... this teacher does not accept metaphors."

Teacher Gao regarded A-Xin with utmost gravity, even reaching out to pat A-Xin's belly, producing hollow "thump-thump" sounds:

"Maintain current volumetric mass. Flattening is prohibited. Such is the duty of a homeroom teacher."

At this moment, the entire Class 6-1 fell into dead silence.

Sitting beside him, Lin Yunqing covered her mouth, an astonishing burst of excitement erupting in her eyes;
On the windowsill, Liu-Liu was so startled that its tail fur stood on end, scampering back into the vent in a flash;
And A-Xin, looking down at the gleaming gear beneath his chair, felt only one colossal, blistering revelation roaring madly through his mind—

Good heavens.

This gear had unmistakably fallen straight out of Teacher Gao's body!

Their omnipotent new homeroom teacher... was not human at all, ahhhhhh!!!
