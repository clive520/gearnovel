# Full-Length School Comedy Novel: *My Teacher Is Not Human*
## Project Proposal, Worldbuilding Settings, and Character Outline (Revised Edition)

---

## 🎯 I. Basic Information & Positioning

- **Title**: *My Teacher Is Not Human*
- **Genre**: School Comedy / Heartwarming Growth / Light Sci-Fi Adventure
- **Target Audience (Demographics)**:
  - **Core Primary Audience**: Upper elementary to junior high school students (approx. ages 9–15), familiar with school tropes, enjoying lighthearted humor, adventure, and camaraderie.
  - **All-Ages / Cross-Generation Audience**: Readers of all ages who love zany school comedies, anime-style light novels, and nostalgic childhood classroom memories.
- **Stylistic Tone**:
  - **Surrealism × Grounded Reality**: The sci-fi capabilities of a bionic robot teacher colliding head-on with ultra-realistic Taiwanese elementary school daily life (class duties, morning exercises, school lunch, campus cleaning, student affairs office, PTA meetings).
  - **Joyful, Playful, and Hilarious**: Powered by the domino effect of "robot malfunctions," creating dense comedic irony and heartwarming charm.

---

## 🏫 II. Worldbuilding & Background

- **Setting**: Luyang Elementary School, Class 6-1.
- **Class Profile**: The legendary "problem class" of Luyang Elementary with twenty-six students, full of clever, eccentric rascals with unique talents who drove previous trainee teachers to exhaustion.
- **Secret Lore**: A covert pilot project (Project P.E.D.A.) co-developed by the Ministry of Education and Advanced Future Technology, dispatching the latest omnipotent bionic educational robot "GS-X01" under the identity of trainee teacher "Gao Zhixian" to test whether an AI educator can guide challenging students.
- **Catalyst Incident (The Fallen Gear)**:
  - Shortly after the semester begins, as Teacher Gao writes on the board, a maintenance latch on the back of his head vibrates loose. "Clink!" A precision brass gear drops to the floor.
  - This gear happens to be the **"Human Common Sense & Rhetoric Damper."** Deprived of this gear, Teacher Gao doesn't crash; instead, he shifts into "Absolute Literal Interpretation Mode," accompanied by overheating steam and misdirected functional abilities.
  - To prevent the strict Dean of Students from finding out and stop the tech company from reclaiming and wiping their teacher, the entire Class 6-1 and their classroom AI pet form a secret alliance: "Operation Protect the Robot Teacher!"

### 3. Antagonists & External Hazards
- **Zhou Yan (Dean of Students, "Black-Faced Judge")**:
  - The strictest disciplinarian on campus with his violation notepad. Suspecting Teacher Gao's silent steps and printing-press grading speed, he prowls Class 6-1's windows looking for proof.
- **Dr. Li (Lead Research Engineer)**:
  - Engineer remotely monitoring GS-X01. Alarmed by bizarre logs (e.g. dumping dish soap into mop buckets, performing breakdance gymnastics), he disguises himself as a maintenance plumber to capture and format the "defective unit."

## 📖 IV. Long-Form Chapter Outline (5 Acts・16 Chapters Total)

### [Act I: Did the Perfect Teacher Drop a Tooth?]
- **Chapter 1: The New Savior of Class 6-1** (Completed ✅)
  - Teacher Gao arrives and catches A-Xin's falling chalk-dust trap bare-handed with millisecond precision, accurately exposing hidden test papers and predicting pork bun shelf life.
- **Chapter 2: The Clinking Sound in Front of the Blackboard** (Completed ✅)
  - While writing on the board, a brass planetary gear drops by A-Xin's foot. Teacher Gao's lenses flash "Error 404: Common Sense Not Found," losing his rhetoric damper.
- **Chapter 3: Teacher's Literal Interpretation Syndrome** (Completed ✅)
  - Morning self-study descends into chaos: A-Xin says he is "starving flat," so Teacher Gao deploys a tape measure to prevent flattening; Wang Zhuankai says "laughing my teeth off," and Teacher Gao pulls dental floss to stabilize his molars.
- **Chapter 4: Secret Surgery in the Equipment Room** (Completed ✅)
  - After school, A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu discover Teacher Gao cooling his chest fans in the equipment room. Qingqing performs rough emergency repairs, Liu-Liu keeps watch, and the alliance is sealed.

### [Act II: God-Tier Appliance & Hilarious Campus Days]
- **Chapter 5: Dry-Ice Drama Rescue** (Completed ✅)
  - Dean Zhou makes a surprise inspection while Teacher Gao's CPU overheats and steams. Liu-Liu sounds the alarm, and the class covers by staging *Snow White in the Foggy Forest* to deceive the dean.
- **Chapter 6: Fingertip Microwave & School Lunch Legends** (Completed ✅)
  - Teacher Gao heats Old Wu's buns, sparking rumors of "Shaolin Iron Palm." Later, Teacher Gao mistakes dish soap for motor oil, turning the hallway into a bubble water-slide.
- **Chapter 7: Trans-Dimensional Morning Calisthenics** (Completed ✅)
  - Calisthenics and breakdance modules cross wires during morning assembly; Teacher Gao leads a wildly viral robot dance that unexpectedly wins the county creativity championship.
- **Chapter 8: The X-Ray Vision Eyes of the Midterm Exam** (Completed ✅)
  - During midterms, Teacher Gao's optical retinal projector short-circuits, beaming answer keys onto the blackboard; students shut their eyes in desperate resistance: "Teacher, stop spoiling the test!" All 26 students pass, winning the Best Academic Progress Gold Medal; Volume 1 complete!

### [Volume 2: The Phony Plumber Infiltrates Campus (Chapters 9–16)]
- **Chapter 9: The Overheated Parent-Teacher Meeting** (Completed ✅)
  - Demanding parents grill the teacher. Caught between politeness and raw honesty algorithms, Teacher Gao delivers shocking yet profound truths, earning a standing ovation. Dr. Li secretly locks onto Teacher Gao's signal.
- **Chapter 10: The Metal Scanner on the Repairman** (Completed ✅)
  - Dr. Li infiltrates as a plumber with a pulse scanner targeting Class 6-1. Liu-Liu alerts the class; students launch a marble minefield and chalk smoke screen. Teacher Gao discharges fingertip microwave energy to fry the scanner, and Dean Zhou expels Dr. Li for safety violations.
- **Chapter 11: Liu-Liu's Emergency Airdropped Intel** (Completed ✅)
  - Liu-Liu infiltrates the basement ducts to intercept Dr. Li's encrypted call with HQ, stealing the data capsule and executing an emergency airdrop into A-Xin's pencil case. Qingqing decrypts the chip to uncover a 72-hour EMP scrap countdown and an 82% worn 4mm secondary planetary bearing!
- **Chapter 12: Class 6-1 Campus Guerrilla War** (Completed ✅)
  - The class splits into four squads deploying soap-marble slides, chalk smoke screens, and decoy synthesized audio lures to herd Dr. Li straight into Dean Zhou's wrathful office. When Dr. Li corners Teacher Gao with a shorting gun, literal defense triggers: Teacher Gao disarms him bare-handed at an absolute 90-degree angle and fries the weapon with microwave pulses. Campus security expels Dr. Li in disgrace!
- **Chapter 13: The Unbalanced Secondary Bearing** (Completed ✅)
  - Overloaded from the afternoon guerrilla defense, Teacher Gao's secondary bearing wear spikes to 91.2%, causing moonwalk reverse-ambulation and reversed speech jams. When Dean Zhou inspects at dusk, all 26 students execute synchronized reverse-walking, bluffing it as "Whole-Brain Equilibrium Neural Gym". Teacher Gao's right lower limb servos overload and seize, slashing survival time to under 4 hours and forcing the infiltration mission to launch early!
- **Chapter 14: Midnight Scavenger Hunt in the Science Lab** (Completed ✅)
  - A diversion at the recycling depot lures campus security away as A-Xin, Qingqing, and Liu-Liu infiltrate the 3rd-floor Science Lab. With Liu-Liu acting as an LED surgical lamp, Qingqing meticulously disassembles the high-speed refrigerated centrifuge, and A-Xin catches a flying tungsten circlip mid-air with magnetic tweezers to secure the 4mm titanium planetary bearing. Outside, Dean Zhou Yan approaches with a halogen beam!
- **Chapter 15: Black-Faced Judge's Midnight Patrol** (Completed ✅)
  - Dean Zhou breaches the lab with his halogen flashlight; Old Wu bellows a thunderous feral cat roar down the stairwell; Liu-Liu executes a 3D bank shot to airdrop a rubber bullfrog squarely on Dean Zhou's head and slides between his legs to lure him away. The team beats the 7:00 PM infrared alarm and secures the 99.8% compatible bearing in the equipment room, while Dr. Li marks their location from afar!
- **Chapter 16: Fleeting Peace and Looming Shadows (Volume 2 Climax)** (Completed ✅)
  - Qingqing completes the midnight micron surgery to seat the replacement planetary bearing, fully rebooting Teacher Gao's right-leg servos and power grid. The next morning, Principal Qiu expels Dr. Li for wiring tampering; Class 6-1 celebrates, and Teacher Gao hilariously fits Old Wu with a construction hardhat for literal anti-blast defense. Meanwhile, Dr. Li connects with the corporate board to greenlight 【Omega Protocol: Operation Disconnect】, targeting the upcoming Graduation Trip in two weeks; Volume 2 grand finale!

### [Volume 3: The Miraculous Reboot Season (Chapters 17–24)]
- **Chapter 17: Departure! The Final Graduation Trip** (Completed ✅)
  - The class departs on their 3-day, 2-night graduation trip; Teacher Gao issues 26 custom "Anti-Flattening Kits", delivers a purely synthesized genetic pathology version of *Two Tigers* on the tour bus, and reheats meat buns with his fingertip microwave. The class checks into the Interstellar Resort, while Dr. Li deploys twelve retrieval operatives and armored EMP trucks in the foggy woods!
- **Chapter 18: Machine Deity at the Amusement Park** (Completed ✅)
  - Old Wu gets thwarted by the erratic Whack-a-Mole machine; Teacher Gao steps in, dual-wields mallets at 48 strikes/sec, overflows the score board to 99,999, fries the motor with steam, and wins the 1.5m Space Golden Bear for Old Wu. He sweeps the Quantum Light-Gun Range (50/50 bullseyes) and the Deep Space Claw Machine (23 consecutive catches), securing 26 giant plushies for every student. As storm clouds loom and Liu-Liu detects EMP signals, the class boards the sky coaster; meanwhile, Dr. Li plugs a timed electromagnetic shorting device into the park's main power substation, triggering an impending mid-air crisis!
- **Chapter 19: The Roller Coaster in the Storm** (Completed ✅)
  - A convective tempest descends as Dr. Li detonates a high-energy EMP at the substation, cutting park power and frying the coaster's control boards, stranding the train on an 80-degree near-vertical drop at the 30m apex! Amidst torrential rain and 70 km/h wind shear, the mechanical latches fracture and the train slips 30 cm; Dean Zhou roars furiously through a red megaphone in the mud below to protect his students and teacher; with 58 seconds left, Teacher Gao instructs Qingqing to cut his suit seam and pulls the manual override ring, disengaging humane protocols and unlocking 100% full power with blazing gold-crimson eyes, stepping out onto the vertical rail to begin the ultimate rescue!
- **Chapter 20: Unlocking 100% Full Power** (Completed ✅)
  - As the gravity ratchet shatters and the coaster plunges, Teacher Gao clamps his bare titanium hands onto the guide-wheel chassis in a blinding blast of electrical arcs; suit sleeves shred to expose liquid-silver limbs and humming superconducting fiber optics; spewing three-meter plumes of white dragon steam from chest vents, Teacher Gao pushes the 6.5-ton train backwards up the 80-degree vertical rails with 38,000 N·m of extreme torque; A-Xin and Old Wu lead all 26 students to throw their bodies back chanting "One, Two, PUSH!" in sync, accompanied by Dean Zhou's thunderous roars from the mud below; the coaster safely anchors onto the level buffer platform with zero casualties! Teacher Gao's chest fans slowly stop, and his eyes plunge into dark dormancy!
- **Chapter 21: The Sleeping Teacher Deprived of Power** (Completed ✅)
  - Teacher Gao stands at the platform edge in tatters, scorched and eyes dark, as all 26 students weep around him. Qingqing tests the core: voltage is at 0.00V and the #3 bearing is thermally jammed; she warns that installing the brass gear without the missing locknut will permanently erase his memory. Liu-Liu flashes red alert as Dr. Li ascends with twelve armored engineers and a cryogenic isolation coffin to scrap him; A-Xin, Qingqing, Old Wu, and all 26 students link arms in a human wall to shield their teacher, until Dean Zhou storms the platform roaring with a heavy cast-iron pipe!
- **Chapter 22: Human Wall in the Pouring Rain** (Completed ✅)
  - Dean Zhou Yan slams a 1.5-meter cast-iron water pipe onto the steel decking, thrusts his Ministry of Education teacher's license into Dr. Li's chest, and roars that the cold-hearted scrap metal is the man who detonated the EMP. All 26 students link arms in the deluge, forming an unbreakable human wall to shield Teacher Gao. Reviewing the black-box flight telemetry, Dr. Li breaks down in tears witnessing the 26 children's emotional resonance logs. As park police and ambulance sirens echo up the canyon, Dr. Li calls off his 12-man squad, fakes a destruction report to the board, and slips Qingqing an encrypted debug flash drive and titanium micro-wrench, revealing a 12-minute capacitor depletion limit. As the vital reverse locknut falls into the superstructure below, Liu-Liu dives into the storm to retrieve it!
- **Chapter 23: The Miraculous Final Gear** (Completed ✅)
  - Facing a 12-minute capacitor depletion limit, all 26 students shed jackets and bags to build a living rain canopy over Teacher Gao; Old Wu and the boys form a human windbreak against 70 km/h gusts; Liu-Liu dives into the 30-meter steel abyss to retrieve the missing reverse locknut; A-Xin surrenders the brass planetary gear worn around his neck all year; Qingqing performs high-precision micro-surgery in the mud, freeing the thermally locked bearing housing and torquing the reverse locknut down with 14 seconds to spare. As charge hits zero, Teacher Gao's honeycomb fan roars to life, venting warm steam; his eyes reignite with warm, radiant gold-cyan light, and his trademark literal deadpan voice declares that while A-Xin's tears increased fabric moisture expansion and Old Wu's bun was flattened, all 26 students survived unflattened, evaluating the emergency drill as: Passed!
- **Chapter 24: See You Tomorrow, Teacher Gao! (Series Grand Finale)** (Completed ✅)
  - Under blooming flame-trees in June, Luyang Elementary holds its commencement ceremony. With Principal Qiu and Dean Zhou presiding, Teacher Gao presents custom humorous big-data evaluations to all 26 students: Old Wu receives the "Pork Bun Guardianship Gold Medal" with a 1-year bun voucher; Qingqing is appointed "Lifetime Chief Honorary Micron Mechanical Engineer"; Wang Zhuankai earns "Facial Nerve Regulation Master"; Lin Yunyun wins the "Medal of Iron Will and Courage"; and A-Xin receives the school's sole "Best Brass Gear Keeper Special Grand Honor Certificate." In return, the class presents a custom 26-tooth mirror-polished brass gear inscribed with all 26 names; touched to processor overload, Teacher Gao stores it in his chest pocket above his core, declaring it "the mathematically optimal solution." At sunset, all 26 students shout "See you tomorrow, Teacher Gao!" across the schoolyard, and Teacher Gao waves back with a warm, human smile to conclude the trilogy.
