# Chapter 12: Class 6-1 Campus Guerrilla War

The warning bell for morning self-study had not yet rung, yet inside the classroom of Class 6-1, an austere tension permeated the air, resembling a military command post.

Twenty-six desks and chairs had been quietly pushed back, clearing a large open expanse right in the center of the room.

Above the teacher's podium, the projector screen had been pulled down, displaying high-density holographic schematics that Qingqing had deciphered overnight.

In the exact center pulsed a glaring, blood-red digital countdown: [066:42:19]—less than sixty-seven hours remained before the high-frequency electromagnetic pulse (EMP) scrap sequence set by tech headquarters would detonate.

Directly beneath the timer, the secondary planetary bearing inside Teacher Gao—labeled [4mm-Titanium-Bearing]—flashed a dangerous crimson alarm at eighty pulses per minute.

"Everyone has seen the intel clearly," A-Xin said, gripping a makeshift baton rolled from corrugated cardboard, his expression as grave as a tactical academy graduate. "Dr. Li, the plumber, lost his confidential data capsule in the basement last night, and he's bound to be going out of his mind."

"He knows the capsule is on campus, and Liu-Liu left a scent trail in the vents last night," Qingqing said, adjusting her glasses and tapping the campus floor plan drawn on the blackboard. "According to Liu-Liu's dawn reconnaissance in the faculty parking lot, Dr. Li brought three sets of portable wideband spectrum trackers today, intending to sweep every floor during recess."

"He only has one objective—" Old Wu added murderously around a mouthful of red bean bun, "to track down Teacher Gao, or find the ones who stole his capsule last night—us!"

"Can we let him get away with it?" A-Xin rallied, raising an arm.

"Never!" all twenty-six classmates roared in unison, keeping their voices low like the rumble of heavy subwoofers.

Sitting by the window, Wang Zhuankai immediately raised his hand: "A-Xin, I raided my older brother's stash yesterday for eight rolls of ultra-strength double-sided tape and twelve heavy-duty water guns. We can deploy them on the staircases anytime!"

Representing the girls' squad, Lin Yunyun also stepped forward: "We borrowed all sixty remaining rolls of reflective cellophane from the art rooms. We can cover the corridor windows to create refraction blind spots and blind his infrared lenses!"

"Excellent!" A-Xin nodded approvingly. "We must stall for time until school lets out and night falls. Only under the cover of midnight can we escort Qingqing into the Natural Science Lab and Craft Workshop to extract the 4mm bearings from the centrifuge and the CNC engraving machine!"

"So what's our daytime battle doctrine?" Old Wu asked, gulping down his bun and wiping his mouth.

"Operation Code: 'Campus Sparrow Guerrilla War to Protect Our Robot Teacher'!" A-Xin tapped his paper baton heavily against the blackboard. "All twenty-six of us will split into four tactical squads!"

"Squad One: 'Squeaky-Clean Low-Friction Commando', led by Old Wu and six boys! Your sector is the western stairwell. Using dish soap, soapy water, and marble bearings, completely obliterate the enemy's footing!"

"Copy that! Guarantee that fake plumber experiences a zero-gravity moonwalk!" Old Wu patted his round chest stoutly.

"Squad Two: 'Suspended Particle Optical Smokescreen Unit', led by Lin Yunyun and eight girls! Your sector is the second-floor central corridor. Armed with thirty dust-saturated blackboard erasers, unleash a high-density chalk dust flak barrage the instant an alarm sounds!"

"We'll turn his high-tech goggles completely blind, guaranteed!" Lin Yunyun clenched her small fists.

"Squad Three: 'Multi-Band Decoy Jamming Unit', helmed by Qingqing and Wang Zhuankai! Using five modified transistor radios and a hand-crank dynamo, broadcast fake high-frequency electromagnetic static along the eastern corridor to lure him straight into Dean Zhou's office!"

"Frequencies calibrated. We're ready to arrange a touching encounter between him and Dean Zhou at any moment," Qingqing smiled with icy confidence.

"Squad Four: 'Core Defense & Literal Damage Control Unit', commanded personally by me!" A-Xin declared firmly. "The remaining students will stick close to Teacher Gao, responding to any hyper-literal calculation surges and packaging any supernatural glitches as Class 6-1's model classroom activities!"

"Gu-jee! Gu-jee!" Perched at the corner of the podium, Liu-Liu gave sharp, crisp squeaks, as if applauding the tactical layout.

"Liu-Liu serves as Supreme Airspace Recon Commander, darting through the vent network to relay his latest coordinates!" A-Xin gave Liu-Liu a thumbs-up.

Right on cue, the hallway echoed with a pair of leather shoes stepping in strict, immutable cadence—exactly seventy-five centimeters per stride, pulsing at a precise one hundred and twenty beats per minute.

"Everyone to battle stations!" A-Xin commanded.

In less than three seconds, the projector screen whipped upward with a snap, the tactical map on the blackboard was wiped clean by the daily monitor, twenty-six desks and chairs snapped into perfect alignment, and the class sat upright like bronze bells, hands neatly folded on desks, chanting their morning reading in unison.

Tucking his lesson plan under his arm, Teacher Gao stepped into the classroom with microsecond precision, his black-rimmed glasses gleaming coldly in the morning sun.

"Good morning, everyone." Teacher Gao scanned the room, a stream of self-diagnostic logs flashing across his lenses. "According to ambient sensors, suspended particulate density is abnormally elevated, and floor friction coefficients exhibit non-uniform distribution. Does Class 6-1 have specialized thermodynamic plans scheduled today?"

"Reporting to Teacher! None at all!" A-Xin stood up, chest puffed out. "We are simply greeting a wonderful day full of physical mysteries with high spirits!"

The faint blue glint in the depths of Teacher Gao's pupils flickered briefly as if querying the semantic database for the definition of "full of physical mysteries," before he nodded: "Logically consistent. Please open your Chinese textbooks to page fifty-six."

The first period passed uneventfully, but the second the dismissal bell chimed, tension clamped down upon the campus once again.

"Beep-beep—" The micro-buzzer beneath A-Xin's desk mat sounded two urgent, clipped chirps.

That was Liu-Liu's highest-tier red alert: the enemy had breached the ground-floor western breezeway!

At that very moment, around the corner of the first-floor western staircase, Dr. Li crept forward in his rumpled blue plumber's uniform, clutching a metal detector disguised as a toolbox.

Dark circles bruised his eyes and stubble shadowed his jaw; he had clearly spent the entire night without sleep hunting for the missing data capsule.

"Curse that blasted rodent... where did it stash the chip?" Dr. Li muttered through gritted teeth, glaring at his detector's display. "But as long as GS-X01 is operating, the high-frequency vibration harmonic of its secondary bearing cannot be masked... All I need is to reach the second floor..."

He took broad, aggressive strides, surging toward the western stairs leading up to Class 6-1.

Yet the very instant his rubber work boots landed on the first tread, chaos erupted!

"Oh no! My mop bucket tipped over!" Old Wu let out an extravagantly theatrical wail from the landing above.

The oversized bucket in Old Wu's hands "accidentally" tumbled, releasing three full bottles of concentrated mint dishwashing soap blended with two hundred giant glass marbles in a roaring flash flood down the terrazzo stairs!

Before Dr. Li could even register the rolling barrage before his eyes, the friction coefficient beneath his boots plummeted from 0.7 to an instantaneous 0.01!

"Waaaaaah—!"

A curdling shriek split the air as Dr. Li's legs whipped forward as if snagged by invisible steel cables; he spun a full one-hundred-and-eighty degrees in mid-air, slamming flat on his back against the soap-soaked steps!

Nor was that the worst of it. Lubricated by foaming dish soap, the two hundred marbles formed a kinetic roller bearing matrix, turning Dr. Li into an uncontrollable high-speed toboggan rattling down the staircase!

With a thunderous crash, Dr. Li slammed feet-first into the recycling bin at the base of the stairs, a yellow plastic basket labeled "Aluminum Cans Only" clamped upside-down over his head.

"Reporting to Janitor Uncle! Friction experiment successful!" Old Wu leaned over the handrail, waving with boundless warmth. "Per Newton's First Law of Motion, an object in motion stays in motion! Your sliding posture was textbook perfect—I'd award it ninety-five points!"

"You... you insolent brats!" Dr. Li ripped the plastic basket off his head, trembling with fury, green soap suds dripping from every seam of his clothes.

He scrambled up, preparing to unleash hell, when a pebble dropped from the ceiling vent grating above, cracking neatly against his detector antenna.

Snapping his head up, Dr. Li saw a snow-white little beast crouching on the lip of the exhaust grille, sticking out a pink tongue at him before darting like a white streak down the central hallway.

"It's that mechanical pet! The chip must be on it!" Eyes bloodshot and reason completely shattered, Dr. Li swiped suds from his brow and bolted into the central corridor.

But the moment he breached the second-floor central corridor, he charged straight into a pitch-white, visibility-zero abyss.

"Release—the—smoke!" Lin Yunyun's crisp order echoed from both ends of the hallway.

Eight girls wielding oversized double-sided blackboard erasers emerged like elite ninja assassins from behind classroom doors, launching a furious "Synchronized Clapping Barrage" into thin air!

Thirty erasers saturated with a full week's worth of fine calcium carbonate dust discharged simultaneously, raising an impenetrable white tempest that swallowed the corridor!

"Cough, cough, hack! What kind of sick trick is this!" Dr. Li plunged into the smog, coughing violently as tears streamed down his cheeks.

More lethally, a thick glaze of chalk dust coated his multifunctional smart visor, flashing frantic error codes across his display: [Optical Sensors Severely Obstructed. Visibility: 0.05 Meters].

"I can't see! You little devils!" Dr. Li flailed his arms like a headless fly in the blinding fog.

Just then, a remarkably authentic synthesized vocal chimed from the far end of the hallway: "Beep—beep—abnormal frequency detected, initiating thermal self-destruct countdown—"

"To the east!" Dr. Li's heart jolted; he immediately groped his way toward the audio feed in a desperate dash.

What he did not know was that the voice did not belong to Teacher Gao at all, but came from Wang Zhuankai, who was sprinting backwards with a grin while clutching a cassette recorder hooked to a bullhorn.

Guided by the audio lure, Dr. Li sprinted forward and threw his weight against the door at the end of the hall, roaring: "GS-X01! Nowhere left to run!"

Yet as he wiped the chalk crust from his lenses, what met his eyes was not Teacher Gao, but a human face darker than charcoal, radiating gaze cold enough to commit homicide.

Inside the Discipline Office, Dean Zhou Yan was unscrewing his thermos to steep tea when a plumber dripping in green foam, coated in chalk flour, and shrieking hysterically slammed into his office—his dark complexion darkening to an ominous sheen.

"Who did you say has nowhere left to run?" Dean Zhou set his thermos down, his voice chilling like a Siberian gale.

Dr. Li froze stiff, his mouth twitching: "Uh... Dean... I was scanning... scanning for ultrasonic rodents in the walls..."

"Rodents? Look at yourself, what kind of disgrace is this!" Dean Zhou slammed his desk so hard the window blinds rattled. "First thing in the morning you're ice-skating in the halls, kicking up dust storms, and now you barge into the Discipline Office! What maintenance firm are you with? Where is your industrial safety license!"

"I... I really have an emergency repair protocol..." Cold sweat poured down Dr. Li's neck just as his spectrum tracker shrieked an ear-splitting tone, indicating the target high-frequency harmonic was migrating from the third floor toward the central courtyard!

Ignoring Dean Zhou's volcanic wrath, Dr. Li spat out "Excuse me" through gritted teeth and bolted straight out the door!

"Halt! You stop right there! Insanity! Utter anarchy!" Dean Zhou's outraged roars and stomping feet echoed behind him.

Dr. Li pounded up to the third-floor connecting concourse, heart thumping. There he saw it—in the middle of the corridor, Teacher Gao Zhixian was leading the students of Class 6-1 toward the sports field for an outdoor perimeter survey lesson!

"GS-X01! Let's see you dodge this time!" Dr. Li sneered, whipping out a high-voltage electromagnetic shorting gun as he closed in from behind.

The shorting gun's effective range was barely three meters; a discharge from behind would instantly fry Teacher Gao's motherboard without rupturing the synthetic skin!

Ten meters, seven meters, five meters...

Glancing back, A-Xin spotted the charging Dr. Li, his heart leaping into his throat; every trap had been spent, and the open concourse offered nowhere to hide!

"Teacher Gao, watch your back!" A-Xin yelled involuntarily.

At the head of the formation, Teacher Gao stopped in his tracks and turned.

Dr. Li's eyes flared with ecstasy, his finger curling around the shorting gun's trigger: "Die, you defective junk!"

Yet in the fraction of a millisecond before the trigger clicked, Teacher Gao's spectacles blazed with an eerie, amber-yellow luminescence.

[Detected: High-velocity metallic polymer construct approaching from rear vector.]

[Literal interpretation protocol engaged: Student alert 'Watch your back'.]

[Executing primary defense sequence: Even distribution of dorsal impact profile.]

Teacher Gao did not retreat, nor did he adopt a defensive stance; instead, defying all human kinesiology with an absolute right angle, he whipped his torso backward one hundred and eighty degrees while his right arm thrust forward like a hydraulic crane jib—precise, elegant, and thunderous!

Dr. Li saw only a blur before agonizing pain racked his wrist, threatening to splinter bone.

Before electrical current could even energize the coils, the shorting gun was pinned effortlessly within Teacher Gao's slender fingers.

"Valued construction personnel," Teacher Gao's voice was placid as glass, facial muscles held in an exact 0.0-degree smile. "Per Campus Safety Code Article 28, running in student thoroughfares while bearing pointed metallic implements yields a kinetic index 420% above the safe threshold."

"L-let me go!" Dr. Li bared his teeth in agony, straining with all his might to wrench his arm free, but Teacher Gao's grip remained unyielding as a hydraulic clamp welded to steel rail.

Teacher Gao tilted his head slightly, gaze settling upon the shorting gun's electrode prongs: "Optical scan indicates this apparatus lacks BSMI insulation certification, presenting severe leakage hazards at the grip. As an educator, this instructor has a duty to eliminate electrical perils from educational premises."

Teacher Gao's right index finger tapped lightly against the core capacitor of the shorting gun.

"Zzzzt—crackle!"

A faint, pale-blue electromagnetic arc scarcely visible to the naked eye blossomed from Teacher Gao's fingertip.

The next second, muffled pops resembling popcorn crackled inside the casing as thin wisps of black smoke curled from between the screws; within a single second, the million-dollar tech weapon was reverse-fried by high-frequency microwaves into a steaming lump of scrap!

Releasing his fingers, the charred shorting gun clattered to the tiles, snapping cleanly in two.

"Hazard neutralized. It is recommended that upon returning home, you review Chapter Three of the Electrical Safety Code." Teacher Gao offered a polite, measured bow.

Dr. Li stared blankly at the smoking wreckage on the floor, then looked up at Teacher Gao's immaculate, composed face, his mind completely shattering.

Right on cue, a wheezing Dean Zhou finally charged onto the third-floor concourse accompanied by two campus security guards.

"Seize him! That's the man!" Dean Zhou roared, hair bristling with rage as he pointed at Dr. Li. "Smearing soap on stairs, throwing marbles, hurling chalk dust, and now threatening our model trainee teacher with a dangerous toy weapon! March him to the guardhouse at once and have his union supervisor come retrieve him!"

The two burly security guards wasted no words, flanking the dazed Dr. Li and hauling him down the stairs like a sack of damp sand.

"Unhand me! He's a robot! He's really a machine, aaaagh—!" Dr. Li's frantic, desperate wails faded down the echoing corridors, eventually vanishing entirely toward the campus front gates.

Total silence fell across the concourse.

All twenty-six students of Class 6-1 stared dumbfounded at Teacher Gao before someone broke the quiet, unleashing an ear-splitting cascade of cheers and applause that nearly blew off the roof!

"Teacher Gao is an absolute god!" Old Wu bounded into the air, shaking the very concrete beneath their feet.

"That 'Absolute Defense Bare-Handed Blade Catch' was insanely cool!" Wang Zhuankai's face flushed scarlet with delight.

Teacher Gao adjusted his glasses, the amber glow fading into placid obsidian.

"Silence," Teacher Gao remarked evenly. "This was merely routine disposal of hazardous items on campus grounds; excessive emotional feedback is unwarranted. Seven minutes remain in recess; please reassemble into formation and resume our campus perimeter survey."

A-Xin and Qingqing exchanged a look; their palms were slick with cold sweat, yet their eyes danced with an unspoken thrill and camaraderie.

The guerrilla operation was an unqualified triumph! Dr. Li had been expelled by security, ensuring he could not set foot in Luyang Elementary for the rest of the day.

Dusk descended, bathing the campus in molten gold and crimson hues.

The dismissal chimes chimed melodiously; students departed in scattered clusters with backpacks slung over shoulders as the noisy day subsided into peaceful dusk.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, A-Xin, Qingqing, Old Wu, and Liu-Liu gathered by the sill, watching the fiery evening sky.

Qingqing withdrew the data capsule from her pocket and clicked the side sensor.

Hovering in the air, the digital readout reappeared: [059:18:04]—less than sixty hours remained until EMP detonation.

Beneath the counter, the 3D schematic revealed that Teacher Gao's internal planetary bearing wear had quietly crept up to 84.1% due to the day's intense processing spikes.

"We're running out of time," Qingqing said softly, her eyes steel-firm.

"Are the tools all set?" A-Xin whispered.

"Micro-screwdrivers, insulated pliers, grease, and two bags of brown sugar steamed buns Old Wu snuck in—they're all in my kit," Qingqing patted her backpack.

"Gu-jee!" Liu-Liu bounded onto A-Xin's shoulder, fluffy tail perked up in high spirits.

A-Xin clenched the cool, heavy brass gear resting in his pocket, gazing toward the shadow-draped corners of the darkened school buildings.

"Tonight at midnight: Natural Science Lab and Craft Workshop." A-Xin took a deep breath, an adventurer's grin touching his lips. "Class 6-1 Component Retrieval Squad—prepare for deployment!"
