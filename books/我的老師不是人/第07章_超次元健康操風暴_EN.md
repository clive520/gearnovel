# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 7: Trans-Dimensional Morning Calisthenics

Wednesday morning at eight o'clock was the most excruciating time of the week for all one thousand-plus teachers and students at Luyang Elementary School—the whole-school morning assembly calisthenics.

The early autumn sunlight spilled over the vast athletic field, the red PU synthetic running track radiating a glaring warmth. The class lines were arranged crookedly in staggered formations, students yawning in long successions, swinging their arms with utter lethargy.

"At ease! Attention!"

Atop the review stand, Dean of Students Zhou Yan clutched his microphone, scowling with a dark countenance as he roared: "Look at yourselves! What kind of posture is this?! Every single one of you resembles a limp-legged shrimp that skipped breakfast! Today, creative calisthenics evaluators from the County Department of Education are observing from afar. All homeroom teachers step forward and lead the exercises personally at the very front of your formations!"

The homeroom teachers sighed one after another as they walked to the head of their respective lines.

Standing at the very forefront of Class 6-1 was, naturally, Teacher Gao Zhixian, dressed in his tailored suit, his leather shoes polished so brilliantly they could serve as mirrors.

However, standing in the front row, Lin Yunqing was so anxious she kept wiping cold sweat from her forehead.

Just moments ago during formation line-up, Qingqing had keenly heard a series of rapid "beep-beep" sounds emanating from Teacher Gao's suit jacket, resembling hard drive read failures.

"A-Xin, the situation looks bad," Qingqing whispered in a hushed tone, angling her head back toward Lu Lixin (A-Xin) behind her. "Yesterday, when Teacher Gao helped the school network center repair their servers, his background system seemingly downloaded a complete extreme choreography database from the World Street Dance Championships by accident..."

A-Xin blinked in confusion: "Street dance database? What does that have to do with morning exercises?"

"That's precisely the problem!" Qingqing gritted her teeth. "He's missing his common sense damper! His system seems to have automatically merged the 'National Youth Calisthenics Routine One' and the 'World Street Dance Popping & Robotics Module' into the same algorithmic category!"

Before her words even settled, the loudspeakers surrounding the field suddenly crackled with static, playing the classic morning exercise tune every Taiwanese elementary schooler could recite with their eyes closed:

"Attention, boys and girls! Morning exercises begin now—Section One: Stretching exercises! One, two, three, four; two, two, three, four..."

The cheerful, somewhat mechanized broadcast melody echoed across the sky above the athletic field.

Teachers and students from all the other classes across the school began languidly executing standard chest expansions and stretches.

However—

Standing at the absolute head of Class 6-1, Teacher Gao's body suddenly shuddered violently!

"Instruction received: Initiate full-body articulatory restoration kinetic routine."

Behind his black-framed glasses, Teacher Gao's pupils erupted with a dazzling fluorescent emerald glare!

`[Audio Track Detected: Rhythm beat at 128 BPM]`
`[Motion Conflict: Broadcast calisthenics excessively soft; joint stretching efficiency sub-optimal]`
`[Override: Load Popping Extreme Robotics Module 100%]`

"Click! CLANG——!!"

A resounding metallic bone-crack, reminiscent of a heavy-duty truck shifting gears, detonated across Teacher Gao's entire skeletal frame!

Teacher Gao did not perform an ordinary chest expansion.

Instead, his left pectoral muscle twitched violently at an impossible millisecond frequency with a massive "POP," sending a sharp shockwave through his entire tailored suit, instantly followed by his right shoulder, left elbow, and wrist joints erupting into three consecutive, near gravity-defying, superhuman popping snaps!

"THUMP! THUMP! THUMP!"

Every single pop landed with pinpoint accuracy squarely on the heavy bass beats of the broadcast track, so crisp and thunderous that it sounded as if he had installed a high-powered subwoofer inside his ribcage!

Standing behind him, Old Wu's jaw dropped so wide it could fit two massive pork buns: "Holy smoke! What is Teacher Gao doing?! Are his joints shooting lightning?!"

"That's just the beginning!" A-Xin's eyes were round as saucers.

Right as the broadcast counted to "three, two, three, four," Teacher Gao's feet astonishingly did not leave the ground at all. Soles hugging the gritty PU track, his entire body moved as if pulled by alien gravitational forces, sliding backward with breathtaking elegance across five whole meters as if gliding on polished ice!

"Is that... Michael Jackson's Moonwalk?!" All twenty-six students shrieked in collective disbelief!

Yet Teacher Gao's system had already tumbled headlong into "Extreme Robotics Rhythm Overload."

As the music transitioned into Section Two: "Torso Turning Exercises"—

Teacher Gao planted a single palm on the asphalt, his body suspending horizontally in midair, his long, tailored suit trousers whipping through the sky like helicopter blades in three blistering, high-speed rotations!

"A Thomas Flare?! A Thomas Flare in a three-piece suit?!"

In neighboring Class 6-2, athletic ace Chen Ada's eyeballs nearly popped out of their sockets, his arms freezing mid-stretch.

"Three, two, three, four! Jumping exercises!" The broadcast lady's voice blared on, oblivious to the chaos.

Vaulting upward with incredible spring, Teacher Gao leaped a full meter into the air. Suspended mid-flight, his body moved like a flexible eel charged with high-voltage electricity, his joints sequentially transmitting an impossibly fluid, breathtaking "Trans-Dimensional Bio-Electric Current Wave" from his fingertips through his cervical vertebrae, spine, down to his toes!

Upon touchdown, Teacher Gao thrust his right palm forward, his suit tails billowing without wind, his black-framed glasses reflecting a piercing, dominant glare, locking into an immaculate, deadpan robotic freeze (Locking)!

"BOOM——!!"

The entire athletic field of Luyang Elementary plunged into unprecedented, pin-drop silence.

Over a thousand students, dozens of teachers, and even Principal Qiu Shouren on the review stand holding his teacup froze like petrified statues, their exercise movements halted in midair.

"W-What do we do? Teacher is completely out of control!" Lin Yunyun whimpered on the verge of tears.

Over a thousand pairs of eyes across the school were now glued to Class 6-1. Even the visiting Department of Education evaluators in the distance had taken off their reading glasses, mouths agape as they stared.

Seeing that Teacher Gao was about to blow his cover through these inhumanly extreme bionic maneuvers—

"A-Xin! Don't just stand there!"

Atop the ceiling loudspeaker casing, Liu-Liu suddenly poked its fluffy white head out. Its four paws balanced on the metal mesh, twin fox ears spinning frantically as its electronic eyes beamed down an emergency tactical instruction:

`[Tactical Cover: All units mimic dance! Disguise as collective class choreography!]`

Alarm bells clanged wildly inside A-Xin's head.

Inhaling a massive lungful of air, he ripped off his school jacket, tied it securely around his waist, took a giant stride out of formation, and bellowed at the top of his lungs to the twenty-five stunned classmates behind him:

"Class 6-1! All units attention! Match Teacher Gao's beat——LET'S MOVE!!"

"CHAAAARGE!!"

Old Wu jutted his neck forward, relying on his burly physique to take the lead in violently popping his chest muscles in imitation of Teacher Gao. While he looked somewhat like an electrocuted chubby penguin, his swagger was off the charts!

Wang Zhuankai, Lin Yunyun, Qingqing, and the entire roster of twenty-six students—the dormant rebellion and fiery passion in their bones detonated all at once!

"One, two, three, four! Move it!!"

Arranged in an immaculate block formation, the twenty-six elementary schoolers locked onto the standard broadcast cadence while executing intricate waves and sharp robotic locking freezes in unison!

Twenty-six pairs of arms rippled like cascading ocean waves, their footfalls stomping the track in sync, generating an awe-inspiring rhythm akin to a thundering cavalry charge!

Perched atop the loudspeaker, Liu-Liu grew so thrilled it stood upright on its hind legs, tail held high, wriggling its lithe body to the entire field like the most sensational dance troupe mascot in school history!

This spectacle instantly set the entire field ablaze!

Children possess zero resistance against genuinely cool things. The fourth-graders, fifth-graders, and even the tiny first-graders who had been limply going through the motions suddenly had stars in their eyes!

"Whoa! Class 6-1 is so ridiculously cool!"
"I want to do that too! No more boring stretches!"

"Click! Bang! One, two, three, four!"

Nobody knew who started it, but neighboring Class 6-2 began clumsily mimicking the arm waves; then came the fifth grade, the third grade... In less than two minutes, over a thousand students across the entire campus spontaneously abandoned the traditional calisthenics, all locking into Class 6-1's groove, executing robotic pops and moonwalks across the blacktop!

The previously dreary morning assembly had transformed into an open-air extreme street dance festival with a thousand participants!

Up on the review stand.

Dean of Students Zhou Yan gripped his whistle, his dark complexion turning mottled blue with fury, his lips trembling as he prepared to blow the emergency whistle to shut it down: "Insubordination... absolute mutiny! Turning morning calisthenics into this absurd circus—"

"Wait! Dean Zhou! Stop right there!"

Beside him, an authoritative yet intensely emotional voice rang out.

It was Principal Qiu Shouren, normally perpetually half a step behind and fond of slow sips from his purple clay teapot. At this moment, however, his face was flushed crimson with excitement, his hands trembling so hard the teapot sloshed!

"P-Principal?" Dean Zhou was flabbergasted.

"Genius! This is an unprecedented miracle in pedagogical history!"

Principal Qiu ripped off his reading glasses, tears of raw inspiration glistening in his eyes as he slammed his palm against the iron railing of the review stand: "Look! The children usually look half-dead doing morning exercises, but right now, every single one of them is radiant! Every single student is fully activating their joints! This is what true 'vitality of life' looks like!"

Principal Qiu spun on Dean Zhou, his voice soaring with exhilaration:

"Teacher Gao Zhixian... has truly lived up to his credentials as a premier returnee educator! He has infused archaic broadcast calisthenics with modern kinetic biomechanics and vibrant youth culture! What do we call this? This is 'Whole-Brain Kinetic Trans-Dimensional Calisthenics'!"

In the distance, the evaluators from the County Department of Education were scribbling frantically on their scorecards, nodding their heads to the booming beat, slapping three giant "A+" marks directly into the creativity column!

Holding his whistle frozen in midair, Dean Zhou stared out at the wildly grooving multitude across the field, his jaw working for several moments without being able to squeeze out a single syllable.

Five minutes later, the morning exercise music terminated with a crisp, heavy downbeat.

Teacher Gao concluded the performance dropping to one knee in a god-tier samurai lock pose, suit tails flaring, an elegant wisp of warm exhaust vapor venting from the crown of his head.

All twenty-six students of Class 6-1 snapped into rigid formation in unison, panting heavily yet beaming with triumphant radiance.

The entire athletic field fell silent for three pregnant seconds.

Then——

"CLAP! CLAP! CLAP! CLAP-CLAP-CLAP-CLAP-CLAP——!!"

Principal Qiu led a standing ovation. Thunderous applause swept through the school like a tidal storm, the cheers of over a thousand children threatening to tear the roof off the bleachers!

"Too cool! Teacher Gao is a god!"
"Class 6-1! Class 6-1!"

Amidst the roaring ovations, Teacher Gao slowly rose to his feet, his countenance as composed as ever, gently pushing up his black-framed glasses.

Inside the lenses, a stream of emerald code scrolled serenely:

`[Event Status: Whole-school morning assembly calisthenics concluded]`
`[Motion Energy Consumption: 1400 kcal]`
`[County Competition Rating: Grand Prize Nomination Locked]`
`[Evaluation: Class 6-1 rhythm synchronization rate... at 99.9%]`

A-Xin and Old Wu nearly collapsed onto the track from exhaustion, exchanging grins and flashing a subtle, triumphant peace sign to Liu-Liu up on the review stand.

Teacher Gao turned his head slightly, his gaze piercing through the roaring crowd to rest upon A-Xin and the sweat-drenched Qingqing.

The corner of his mouth curved into an almost imperceptible, warm smile:

"All students of Class 6-1. Exceptional rhythm and cadence... For this morning's assembly, a perfect score for all."
