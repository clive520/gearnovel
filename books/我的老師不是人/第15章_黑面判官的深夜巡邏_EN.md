# Chapter 15: Black-Faced Judge's Midnight Patrol

The blinding, snow-white beam of the halogen flashlight pierced through the frosted glass above the door of Science Lab No. 1 like a solid blade.

The patch of light darted frantically across the ceiling and inner walls, while the heavy, rhythmic tread of leather shoes outside abruptly halted just one meter from the brass doorknob.

"Clack, clack... clink..."

The crisp, metallic jangle of a massive cluster of master keys echoed through the thin wooden door, followed by a gruff, authoritative clearing of the throat.

Inside the dark laboratory, A-Xin and Qingqing—who had just tightened the very last casing screw on the centrifuge—froze as if struck by a lightning bolt, every hair on their bodies standing on end!

"Retreat! Under the lab counters!" A-Xin mouthed silently in a frantic, airless whisper, his hands gesticulating wildly toward opposite sides of the room.

Displaying the inhuman poise and agility of a veteran mechanic, Qingqing dropped her knees and slid soundlessly across the floor like an eel, ducking into the deepest recess behind the acid-resistant reagent cabinet under the central counter.

A-Xin executed a fluid side-roll, tucking himself tightly into the kneehole beneath the teacher's demonstration podium, pressing the sealed plastic tube containing the 4mm titanium planetary bearing against his racing heart.

"Guji!"

Perched on the counter, Liu-Liu instantly cut all his glowing LEDs and launched himself like a grey lightning bolt, landing soundlessly inside the wooden chalk trough beneath the blackboard and curling his tiny paws inward to disguise himself as a dusty felt eraser.

At the exact millisecond all three slipped into their respective hiding spots—

"Clack."

A heavy, cold brass master key was inserted cleanly into the laboratory door's cylinder lock.

With a dry, harsh mechanical screech, the tumbler pins aligned and the spring latch snapped open.

The carved wooden door was slowly pushed inward by a thick, powerful hand, letting a gust of late-autumn evening air sweep into the stifling classroom that smelled faintly of chemical reagents.

The blazing, snow-white halogen beam sliced into the room like a searchlight, sweeping back and forth between the blackboard, the anatomical skeleton model, and the chemical fume hoods.

A size-43 heavy-soled black leather shoe—polished to an impeccable mirror shine without a single crease on the toe cap—stepped heavily across the threshold with an aura of absolute disciplinary authority.

Clutching a riot-grade high-lumen halogen flashlight in his left hand and tucking a three-centimeter-thick Discipline and Infraction Log embossed with the golden school crest under his right arm, the man wore a face as dark as cast iron: Dean of Student Affairs Zhou Yan, the dreaded "Black-Faced Judge"!

"Strange... I could have sworn I saw shadows shifting up here when I was down at the stairwell..." Dean Zhou muttered under his breath, his voice rumbling like low thunder.

He stepped slowly into the classroom, pausing the flashlight beam on the ceiling ventilation duct for two full seconds before drifting downward to scan the neatly aligned ranks of glass beakers and titration tubes.

Crouched inside the podium recess, A-Xin pressed his spine flush against the icy wooden paneling, not daring to exhale a single molecule of air as if his lungs had frozen into solid dry ice.

His heart slammed violently against his ribs: Thump! Thump! Thump! Each resonant beat sounded as loud as a war drum in the deathly stillness of the midnight laboratory.

Dean Zhou took three deliberate strides down the central aisle, the heavy rubber soles of his shoes producing a microscopic "creak" against the polished terrazzo floor.

"Hm?"

Dean Zhou's footsteps stopped dead without warning, his thick, bushy black eyebrows knotting instantly into a severe furrow.

He tilted his chin upward like a seasoned bloodhound, his nostrils flaring twice with intense, discerning sniffs.

"This smell..." Dean Zhou's pupils constricted sharply, a trace of dangerous vigilance creeping into his muttered words.

Mingling with the stale scent of aged formalin was a faint yet unmistakably fresh trace of aerospace-grade cryogenic synthetic grease, accompanied by the sharp ozone tang of a high-speed micro-motor that had only just cooled down!

It was the undeniable physical residue left behind from Qingqing's high-speed reverse disassembly of the centrifuge casing moments before!

"Something is wrong... Teacher Li from the science faculty had no low-temperature micro-centrifugation experiments scheduled for this afternoon. Why would a three-hundred-thousand-dollar piece of precision machinery have residual heat from running?"

The glint in Dean Zhou's eyes turned razor-sharp, his seasoned disciplinarian instincts instantly spiking to red alert!

With a sharp *whoosh*, the flashlight beam snapped downward from the ceiling, locking dead-center onto the sleek, rounded white German refrigerated centrifuge at the far end of the counter!

The heavy, tower-like footsteps sounded again, moving step by step directly toward the demonstration station!

With every stride Dean Zhou took, the distance between him and Qingqing's hiding spot behind the reagent cabinet shrank by half a meter.

If Dean Zhou walked up to the centrifuge and inspected it closely, he would not only spot microscopic metal friction shavings on the security screws—he would instantly see the neon-pink shoelace of Qingqing's sneaker protruding from the cabinet baseboard!

Beneath the podium, cold sweat poured down A-Xin's forehead as he bit down hard on his lower lip, his heart threatening to leap out of his throat.

Meanwhile, two floors below in the deep shadows of the central staircase landing—

Wu Chenkai, the burly, bear-like physical education representative whose mind was normally occupied by steaming pork buns, was pacing in circles while clutching an ice-frosted bottle of mineral water, sweat dripping from his chin.

Under the original battle plan, Old Wu was supposed to stand guard beside the paralyzed Teacher Gao in the first-floor equipment room; but peering through the wooden slats earlier, he had spotted Dean Zhou's halogen beam heading straight for the third floor of the Science Wing, realizing immediately that the infiltration squad was cornered!

Armed with his intimate knowledge of campus patrol routines, Old Wu had risked exposure to creep up to the landing between the second and third floors.

He glanced down at the digital watch on his wrist—【18:56:15】!

Only three minutes and forty-five seconds remained before the campus-wide infrared perimeter security system armed at exactly seven o'clock!

If A-Xin and Qingqing failed to break out of the building before seven, the perimeter sensors would trigger deafening sirens across the school, the automated metal roll-down gates would lock shut, and everyone would be caught red-handed!

"We're finished... the Black-Faced Judge is going to bottle them up inside!" Old Wu clenched his fists, the sweat on his palms making the plastic water bottle crackle under pressure.

He had to lure Zhou Yan away! He had to create a tier-one diversion within ten seconds that would force the dean to sprint out of that lab!

Yet what kind of noise could divert Zhou Yan without blowing their cover as students trespassing after hours?

In a flash of lightning, Old Wu's brain—normally reserved for fried chicken drumsticks and braised beef noodles—sparked with an epiphany, recalling a legendary piece of campus lore—

The iron-fisted, rule-obsessed Black-Faced Judge was secretly an incurable animal lover! Just last semester, when a three-legged calico kitten was cornered by stray dogs near the school gates, Dean Zhou had personally peeled off his suit jacket to rescue it, even delivering a tearful five-minute speech on animal welfare during morning assembly!

"A cat! That's it... if I mimic a stray cat, the Black-Faced Judge will drop his guard and investigate!"

Old Wu swallowed hard, cleared his raspy adolescent vocal cords that were in the throes of a middle-school voice change, cupped his beefy hands into a megaphone around his mouth, and took a massive lungful of air facing the resonant concrete stairwell.

"Meoowww... wuuu..."

A mournful, eerie cry detonated inside the hollow, pitch-black stairwell shaft of the Science Wing.

In Old Wu's imagination, this was intended to sound like a tiny, helpless, adorable kitten shivering in the cold night breeze.

However, because Old Wu weighed over seventy kilograms, possessed the chest resonance of a siege cannon, and suffered from cracking vocal breaks, the sound bounced between the concrete walls and warped into the ungodly bellow of a prehistoric monster!

"Meooww... ROOOAAAR—! Woof... wait no! Meow-cough-cough—AWOOO—!"

Inside the third-floor lab, Dean Zhou—who had been stalking toward the centrifuge—froze mid-stride as though hit by a petrification ray.

The heavy halogen flashlight trembled violently in his thick grip.

"What on earth was that?!" Dean Zhou spun around, his hawk-like gaze stabbing toward the open corridor doorway.

"Was that... a cat?" The corners of Dean Zhou's mouth twitched as sheer bewilderment washed over his darkened face.

"Where under the heavens exists a cat with a roar so coarse, booming, and thunderous? That acoustic signature... sounded like a Siberian wild boar in heat that had just choked on two hundred pounds of carrots!"

Dean Zhou took two cautious steps backward, clutching his infraction log tightly as he edged toward the corridor door.

Beneath the podium, A-Xin's face turned bright purple as he clamped both hands over his mouth, nearly suffocating on his own saliva to stifle a roar of laughter.

Old Wu, you absolute buffoon! That wasn't a cat—that sounded like a Tyrannosaurus rex belching in Jurassic Park!

Yet Zhou Yan was a seasoned veteran who had presided as disciplinary dean for twelve years; his wariness far exceeded ordinary expectations.

The moment his leather shoes reached the doorframe, he stopped short once more, glancing back over his shoulder with deep suspicion and sweeping his beam into the dark corners of the lab.

"No, something is wrong. In the forty-year history of Luyang Elementary, no matter how lush our campus ecology is, it could never spontaneously breed a mutated stray cat weighing over a hundred pounds..."

Dean Zhou let out a cold scoff, his eyes narrowing into slits of pure cunning: "This is a diversion! There is definitely a rat hiding inside this room!"

With that, Dean Zhou reversed his grip on the flashlight, spun on his heel, and marched back toward the inner row of counters with thunderous strides!

Behind the reagent cabinet, Qingqing held her breath, her fingernails digging into her palms until they turned white, beads of cold sweat trickling down the tip of her nose.

In just two more seconds, Dean Zhou's blazing beam would cross the cabinet partition and catch her red-handed!

In this final second of absolute peril, the unsung guardian angel of Class 6-1 finally bared its teeth.

Resting inside the chalk trough disguised as an eraser, Liu-Liu's twin sapphire optical sensors suddenly flared with blinding high-frequency blue laser arcs in the dark!

【Fatal Threat Detected. Initiating Ultimate Diversion Protocol: Phantom Acrobatics Blitz!】

Liu-Liu's marshmallow-soft bionic limbs contracted with coiled spring force, launching the tiny creature upward like an armor-piercing silver micro-missile!

His trajectory was far from random; his onboard quantum coprocessor had calculated a three-dimensional trick-shot bank in less than a ten-thousandth of a second.

First rebound: Liu-Liu's tiny paws touched down lightly upon the crown of the full-body human anatomical skeleton model standing in the corner.

The skeleton's jaw dropped with a sharp *clack*, its dangling plastic ribs swinging violently as the white skull spun eerily left and right in the shadows!

Second rebound: Banking off the skull, Liu-Liu traced an impossible parabolic arc through the air, landing on the very top tier of the two-meter-tall biology specimen cabinet.

With a flick of his bushy, mechanized tail like a precision robotic arm, he smacked a specimen box holding an oversized green synthetic rubber bullfrog used for biology demonstrations!

"Splat!"

The palm-sized, slimy rubber bullfrog plummeted straight down from the three-meter-high shelf, landing with squelching, dead-center perfection smack on top of Dean Zhou's neatly parted black hair!

"WAAAUUUGH—!"

Dean Zhou—the most terrifying authority figure at Luyang Elementary, whose single scowl could bring six hundred unruly brats to absolute attention—let out a piercing, glass-shattering soprano shriek!

The dean hopped frantically as if his trousers were on fire, his arms flailing like windmills as he swatted frantically at the slimy "unidentified organism" clinging to his scalp.

The halogen flashlight whipped wild, chaotic spirals across the ceiling, transforming the laboratory into a demonic disco hall!

"Hostile ambush! High-altitude airdrop of unknown toxic slime fauna!" Dean Zhou roared, finally slapping the rubber frog off his head and sending it flying across the floor.

Before Dean Zhou could even catch his breath, a whirlwind of silver-grey fur was already sweeping toward his face!

Third rebound: Diving down from the top shelf, Liu-Liu tapped Dean Zhou's broad shoulder with his paws, then executed an impossibly smooth ice-skate glide straight through Dean Zhou's parted trouser legs!

"Guji!"

A tiny, mockingly chirped sound effect rang out at the doorway as Liu-Liu blurred into a streak of grey lightning, rocketing out into the corridor at eight meters per second!

"Halt! Where did that oversized, long-tailed tactical super-rat come from?!"

In that split second, all of Dean Zhou's attention was incinerated by righteous fury—his supreme dignity as disciplinary dean had been violated like never before!

"You dare drop a frog on my head?! If I don't drag you down to the office and make you copy the school charter three hundred times, my name isn't Zhou!"

Like an enraged adult rhinoceros, Dean Zhou hoisted his flashlight and charged out of the laboratory with earth-shattering thuds, roaring in hot pursuit toward the eastern wing!

"Don't you run! Come out from behind that fire extinguisher if you have the guts!" Dean Zhou's deafening bellows rapidly receded down the eastern stairwell.

Inside the laboratory, the raging storm vanished instantly, plunging the room back into dead silence.

"Move! Now!" A-Xin shot out from beneath the podium like an uncoiled spring.

Qingqing tumbled out from behind the reagent cabinet with equal haste, dusting off her knees and snatching her tool backpack in one fluid motion.

"Do you have the bearing secure?" Qingqing whispered urgently.

"Safely against my skin, not a single micron scratched!" A-Xin patted his inner chest pocket firmly.

Moving like seasoned cat burglars, the pair bolted to the laboratory doorway, eased the wooden door shut, and snapped the spring latch back into place with a muffled *click*.

Slipping into the western emergency fire stairs, they took the steps three at a time, their sneakers making virtually no sound against the concrete treads.

The moment they burst through the ground-floor exit door and slipped into the holly hedge, a tall, burly silhouette tumbled out of the bushes, gasping for breath.

"Huff... huff... A-Xin! Qingqing! You... you actually made it out alive?!" Old Wu wheezed with his hands on his knees, his face beet-red and his voice as raspy as coarse sandpaper.

"Old Wu, that cat roar of yours almost summoned every stray mutt in the entire township!" A-Xin laughed breathlessly, planting an affectionate fist onto Old Wu's broad shoulder.

"Be grateful! My throat is on fire from trying to hit the mating resonance of an alley cat!" Old Wu sniffled defensively.

"Save the chatter, look at the time!" Qingqing looked down at her luminous watch.

【18:58:40】!

Before her words could settle, a faint rustling sounded from the rainwater downspout overhead, and a sleek silver-grey figure drifted lightly downward, landing squarely atop A-Xin's head—it was Liu-Liu, who had successfully ditched his pursuer, twitching his fuzzy ears and swishing his tail proudly without a single bent whisker!

"Incredible job, top-gun ace of the universe!" A-Xin praised softly, rubbing Liu-Liu's velvety ears.

The trio wasted not a single second, staying low and darting like shadows past the pull-up bars on the edge of the dark sports field, sprinting toward the rear window of the physical education equipment room.

Just as their sneakers crossed the window ledge into the room—

From the distant security booth speaker, a crisp electronic chime sounded: 【BEEP—Campus night-time infrared perimeter security system armed.】 A web of invisible infrared alarm beams flared to life across every perimeter wall of Luyang Elementary!

The time was exactly 7:00 PM on the dot!

Deep inside the stuffy, shadowed sports equipment room, Teacher Gao Zhixian remained propped rigidly against a mountain of stacked high-jump landing mats.

His right leg was still locked at a dead ninety-degree angle, while the faint blue glow in his left optical socket had degraded into an amber emergency warning: 【Remaining Emergency Battery: 1.8%. Total Servo Seizure Countdown: 01:52:10】.

"Teacher Gao! We brought your new heart back!" A-Xin slid to his knees before him, reverently presenting the crystal-clear sealed plastic tube.

Teacher Gao turned his head stiffly, a razor-thin red laser scanning beam emitting from his glasses frame to bathe the metallic component in light.

"Optical geometry scanning in progress..." Teacher Gao's synthesized voice crackled with static, yet remained remarkably steady and precise. "Outer diameter 4.00mm, inner diameter 1.50mm, miniature titanium alloy planetary balls... compatibility with right lower-limb servo assembly... 99.8%..."

"Awesome!" Old Wu collapsed onto a gymnastics mat in sheer, breathless relief, letting out a long sigh.

"Don't celebrate just yet," Qingqing unrolled her canvas toolkit filled with micro-screwdrivers, fiber-optic endoscopes, and thermal paste, her eyes locking in like a master surgeon's. "We have less than an hour and a half to perform this micron-level surgery in total darkness."

"Reporting to homeroom teacher!" A-Xin snapped to attention and gave a comically crooked salute. "Class 6-1 covert behind-the-lines parts retrieval operation: zero casualties, mission accomplished!"

Teacher Gao gazed at the three disheveled, sweat-soaked elementary pupils with soil and grass clinging to their knees; the heat sink on his chest motherboard hummed softly as an unnamed emotional algorithm began rapidly rewriting system priority queues.

"Gratitude expressed to all comrades... pursuant to 《Class 6-1 Campus Conduct Code》... this unit evaluates... this operation's grade as: Exemplary."

Meanwhile, across the sports field beneath a dim, amber streetlamp, Dean Zhou stood with hands on his hips, panting heavily while clutching the flattened green rubber bullfrog, staring with deep suspicion at the silent, darkened Science Wing and gymnasium.

And even further away beyond the school perimeter fence, perched high in the crown of a dense banyan tree across the road, a pair of camouflage-painted high-magnification night-vision binoculars was slowly lowered.

Cloaked in shadow, Dr. Li glanced at the faint electromagnetic pulse briefly flickering on his handheld terminal, a cold and ruthless smirk curling across his face: "So you're holed up in the first-floor equipment room... Enjoy your very last night as a human teacher, GS-X01. When the sun rises tomorrow, your countdown to the scrapyard begins."
