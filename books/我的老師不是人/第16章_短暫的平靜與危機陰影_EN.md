# Chapter 16: Fleeting Peace and Looming Shadows

Deep inside the physical education equipment room, the air was laden with the scent of aged vaulting-box rubber and dry slaked lime, so quiet that even time itself seemed frozen solid.

Far out along the campus perimeter wall, the crimson embers of the infrared security beams flickered faintly in the gloom, while inside the storeroom, a micron-level midnight surgery that would determine Teacher Gao Zhixian's destiny quietly commenced.

"Liu-Liu, tighten the beam to a three-millimeter focal point and shift the color temperature to 4500K shadowless warm-white mode," Qingqing instructed in a hushed whisper, kneeling upon a thick blue gymnastics mat with a jeweler's hex screwdriver as slender as an embroidery needle in her grip.

"Guji!"

Perched atop the wooden vaulting box, Liu-Liu gave a soft chirp; his bionic pupils constricted sharply, casting twin beams of focused, perfectly diffused light dead-center onto the lateral maintenance port of Teacher Gao's right knee joint.

Teacher Gao lay motionless upon the foam mats, the blue luminescence behind his glasses frames having dwindled to an absolute emergency threshold of 1.2%, glowing like an ember ready to die out in the oceanic abyss.

"This unit's right lower-limb servo powertrain... has cut off high-voltage bus power..." Teacher Gao's synthesized voice was as faint as a strand of silk in the wind. "Engineer Qingqing... please conclude replacement within seventy minutes... otherwise the low-voltage control capacitors will deplete completely, inflicting irreversible magnetic domain corruption upon the boot sector..."

"Rest easy, Teacher Gao. Words like 'stripped screw' and 'blunder' have never existed in my dictionary!" Qingqing took a steadying breath and pushed her safety goggles flush against the bridge of her nose.

Kneeling on the opposite side of Teacher Gao, A-Xin gripped the forty-kilogram bionic leg forged from aerospace titanium-aluminum alloy with both hands, thick beads of sweat splashing down onto the vinyl mat.

"Old Wu, fan the heatsinks!" A-Xin grunted through clenched teeth.

"On it, on it!" Old Wu, holding two massive cardboard flaps torn from storage boxes, stood beside Teacher Gao's chest ventilation grille, fanning furiously like an industrious brown bear while draping ice-cold towels across the scalding aluminum heatsinks.

Under Liu-Liu's rock-steady light, Qingqing manipulated ultra-fine needle-nose tweezers to deftly back out the final four titanium micro-screws securing the dustproof casing.

"Click."

Accompanied by a microscopic hiss of venting pressure, the metal cover lifted away, laying bare the inner mechanics—a double-layered planetary gear reduction assembly meshed with the dizzying precision of a Swiss luxury watch movement.

Yet the sight awaiting the three pupils at its core was nothing short of harrowing—

The outer raceway of the secondary planetary bearing, which should have been polished to a flawless mirror finish, was webbed with dense, radial thermal stress fractures; three of the seven diamond-like carbon micro-balls had melted and seized from yesterday's reverse-microwave overload, while charred lubricating grease had hardened into abrasive black soot, locking the differential gears tight!

"The wear rate has exceeded 94%... no wonder Teacher was moonwalking in reverse; the entire optical phase encoder was shorted by metal debris!" Qingqing's voice trembled slightly, though the fingers holding her tweezers remained as steady as steel bedrock.

"Can we pull it out clean? Will it damage the main driveshaft?" A-Xin held his breath, not daring even to blink.

"In a regular auto shop, a seized bearing like this would be drilled out with an impact driver, but that would shatter the nanoscale fiber-optic gratings," Qingqing gritted her teeth. "I have to use thermal differential contraction."

Taking a chip of dry ice from Old Wu with her tweezers, Qingqing pressed it dead-center against the inner ring of the bearing, whispering urgently: "A-Xin, pull out the torch lighter and heat the outer collar slot for three seconds—now!"

A-Xin instantly clicked the windproof lighter, sweeping the fine, pale-blue jet flame across the perimeter of the high-tensile alloy housing.

"Sssss—"

Under the violent contrast between subzero ice and searing flame, a delicate wisp of white vapor billowed upward.

"Now!" Qingqing's eyes locked in, her ultra-hard carbide pick thrusting like lightning into the extraction notch, followed by an expert flick of her wrist!

"Tink!"

A crisp chime like a glass windbell echoed softly as the blackened, ruined bearing popped cleanly out of the loosened snap ring and dropped into the magnetic parts tray!

"It's out!" Old Wu nearly cheered out loud, hastily clapping both hands over his own mouth.

"A-Xin, the new component!" Qingqing extended her right hand, palm upward.

Reaching into his inner shirt pocket, A-Xin retrieved the crystal-clear plastic vial, unscrewed the cap, and carefully tipped the fresh 4mm titanium planetary bearing—retrieved from the 3rd-floor science lab centrifuge—onto Qingqing's sanitized microfiber cloth.

Illuminated by Liu-Liu's pristine white beam, the new bearing gleamed with an intoxicating, icy silver luster, its seven miniature ceramic balls shimmering beneath a micro-coat of lubricating oil like a constellation of miniature celestial spheres.

Securing the bearing with a specialized vacuum pen, Qingqing spread a gossamer-thin film of aerospace-grade thermal paste along the race channels, then lowered the bearing into the central knee spindle with absolute, breathless concentration.

One millimeter, half a millimeter, a tenth of a millimeter...

"CLACK-SNAP!"

A flawless, perfectly seated mechanical lock echoed through the silent storeroom!

The new bearing locked into place, its dual-thrust raceways meshing with zero play and a tolerance under two-thousandths of a millimeter!

Moving like lightning, Qingqing pressed a fresh snap-ring into the annular groove with magnetic tweezers, torqued the four casing micro-screws, and snapped the dustproof seal shut.

"Surgery complete! Forty-two minutes on the dot!" Qingqing collapsed backward onto the gym mat, drenched in sweat from head to toe, yet sporting a triumphant, radiant grin.

A-Xin and Old Wu exchanged ecstatic glances, slapping a silent, high-flying high-five in midair!

"Teacher Gao, can you hear us? You can initiate diagnostic self-check now!" A-Xin leaned down to whisper beside Teacher Gao's ear.

After three brief seconds of breathless suspense—

"Hummm... vrrr..."

From the depths of Teacher Gao's chest motherboard came a deep, melodious hum of electrical current, and the dormant micro-liquid cooling pump resumed its cheerful, rhythmic circulation.

Instantly, concentric circles of luminous cobalt blue flared to life behind the dark frames of Teacher Gao's glasses, the diagnostic progress bar racing forward at twenty percent per second!

【Bootloader loaded successfully... Hardware Abstraction Layer reinitialized...】

【New right-knee secondary planetary bearing detected... Model compatibility: 99.8%... Dual-axis damping coefficient: Optimal... Phase compensation: Complete!】

【Emergency capacitor bypass charge complete; primary power bus back online.】

With a crisp, elegant mechanical whir of gear engagement, Teacher Gao's right leg—which had been locked in rigid paralysis for hours—bent gracefully to a forty-five-degree angle in midair, then lowered smoothly back to the mat, its joints operating in silky, absolute silence!

Supporting himself with both hands, Teacher Gao sat upright like a towering pine tree, adjusting his glasses as the cobalt luminescence subsided back into his calm, deep, dark pupils.

"Self-calibration complete." Teacher Gao regarded A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu clustered around him, his synthesized tone stripped of all previous stutter and reversal. "Servo eccentricity error reduced to 0.0003 radians; this unit's locomotive functions are fully restored."

"Incredible! Qingqing, you are a bona fide mechanical prodigy!" Old Wu marveled from the bottom of his heart, tossing his cardboard fans into the air in celebration.

"Of course! Even my dad admits I can strip and rebuild a scooter engine faster than he can!" Qingqing wiped the grease from her forehead and lifted her chin proudly.

Teacher Gao looked down at his crisp, unwrinkled trouser leg, then slowly shifted his gaze toward the red cord around A-Xin's neck—where the original fallen 【P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03】 brass gear rested quietly against the boy's chest.

"Although the right lower-limb powertrain has been repaired," Teacher Gao stated deadpan, a soft cascade of code flickering across the corner of his optical sensors, "because the Seventh Common Sense and Rhetoric Damper remains detached, this unit will continue to be incapable of parsing human figures of speech involving puns, hyperbole, or sarcasm for the foreseeable future."

"That doesn't matter at all, Teacher Gao!" A-Xin thumped his chest and flashed a brilliant grin. "Taking everything literally is hilarious! Every single one of the twenty-six kids in our class loves watching you talk absolute nonsense with a straight face!"

Teacher Gao blinked, an uncataloged stream of warm data flowing through his core processor, before outputting a stern and solemn conclusion: "Pursuant to this unit's calculations, 'talking nonsense' is not an approved pedagogical method endorsed by the Ministry of Education. However... this unit does not object to students maintaining a joyful learning disposition."

"Guji!" Liu-Liu bounded onto Teacher Gao's shoulder, nuzzling affectionately against the teacher's cheek.

At two o'clock in the morning, taking advantage of the campus security guard shift change, the three students stealthily escorted Teacher Gao out of the equipment room and safely back to the faculty dormitory.

That night, the core alliance of Class 6-1 slept more soundly and peacefully than ever before.

The following morning, radiant golden sunshine bathed every red brick and green tile of Luyang Elementary School.

At eight o'clock sharp, the door of the Office of Student Affairs was violently flung open as Dean Zhou Yan—sporting a pair of pitch-black dark circles under his eyes—marched in with a face as grim as iron.

In his grip was a transparent zip-lock bag containing a single piece of evidence: the green synthetic rubber bullfrog that had been squashed flat the previous night, still bearing the faint imprint of teeth!

Meanwhile, inside the Principal's Office, Principal Qiu Shouren stood with hands clasped behind his back, fixing a stern, disapproving gaze on a middle-aged man in plumber's overalls.

It was none other than the undercover tech engineer—Dr. Li!

"Chief Engineer Li," Principal Qiu tapped his desk, his voice laced with unmistakable authority and irritation. "Yesterday afternoon, an explosion of rolling cans triggered panic at the western recycling depot, an attempted unauthorized break-in occurred at the third-floor science lab, and your plumbing toolbox was found outside Class 6-1's hallway after hours!"

Dr. Li's expression shifted slightly as he forced a diplomatic smile: "Principal, you misunderstand; I was merely measuring the earth ground resistance of the campus wiring..."

"Enough!" Principal Qiu cut him off without mercy. "Dean Zhou conducted a personal patrol last night and discovered that multiple underground conduits had been tapped with unauthorized signal repeaters. Luyang Elementary is a century-old institution, and student campus safety is our absolute, non-negotiable red line!"

Principal Qiu slid a formal contract termination notice across the mahogany desk to Dr. Li: "The campus electrical audit commissioned by the Education Bureau is officially concluded as of today. Please pack your diagnostic equipment and vacate the premises before ten o'clock; your campus access keycard has already been revoked!"

Dr. Li's eyes darkened like thunderclouds before a squall, glaring at the termination document as his fists clenched tightly at his sides.

He knew that under the heightened vigilance of both the principal and Dean Zhou, forcibly retrieving GS-X01 in broad daylight had become utterly impossible.

"...Understood, Principal Qiu." Gritting his teeth, Dr. Li snatched his credentials from the desk, spun on his heel, and slammed the heavy door behind him.

During the second morning recess, the desks lining the windows of Class 6-1 were packed with curious students.

A-Xin, Qingqing, Old Wu, along with Wang Zhuankai, Lin Yunyun, and the rest of the twenty-six pupils poked their heads out to look toward the front gates.

Down below, Dr. Li hauled his heavy metal toolbox under the stern, watchful escort of two security guards, expelled off school grounds in disgrace before his white service van rumbled away down the avenue.

"YES—! The fake plumber got kicked out!" Old Wu pumped his fist in the air, cheering at the top of his lungs.

"Shh! Keep it down! Don't summon the Black-Faced Judge!" A-Xin shoved Old Wu's shoulder down, yet an unstoppable, triumphant grin split across his own face.

Class 6-1 erupted into a stifled yet wildly ecstatic round of cheering and applause, the light of victory sparkling in every student's eyes!

The bell for class chimed, and from down the corridor came the crisp, steady, and impeccably timed cadence of footsteps.

Teacher Gao Zhixian strode into the classroom with perfect, upright posture, his suit pristine, his tie hanging dead-center, and his leather shoes striking the floor at an absolute thirty-degree angle.

Seeing Teacher Gao walking with effortless, unyielding confidence, the heavy boulders in the hearts of all twenty-six students finally dissolved into pure relief.

"Good morning, comrades." Teacher Gao stood at the center of the podium, pushing up his glasses as his gaze swept authoritatively across the room. "Today we shall tackle Unit Six: 'Multiplication, Division, and Mixed Operations of Fractions.'"

Sitting in the second row, Old Wu raised his hand enthusiastically and cried out: "Teacher Gao! Can today's math lesson be a little simpler? My noggin feels like it's about to explode from thinking about these problems all night!"

Teacher Gao's pupils constricted imperceptibly, a rapid line of textual retrieval flashing across his lenses: 【Keyword Received: 'Noggin about to explode.' Initiating Campus Major Anti-Explosion and Trauma Prevention Protocol.】

With lightning-fast reflexes, Teacher Gao set down his chalk, pulled a bright-yellow industrial construction hardhat from beneath the podium, snapped it squarely over Old Wu's chubby head, and buckled the chin strap with an audible click!

"Student Wu Chenkai," Teacher Gao warned with grave, deadpan solemnity, "pursuant to 《Fluid Dynamics and Human Structural Tolerances》, bone fragments from a cranial detonation will scatter at velocities exceeding three hundred meters per second. Please wear this Class-3 blast helmet for the duration of the period to safeguard the lives of the remaining twenty-five peers in this classroom."

"PFFFT—HAHAHAHAHA!"

The entire Class 6-1 classroom plunged instantly into an ocean of uncontrollable hilarity, their laughter threatening to lift the roof right off the building!

Qingqing laughed so hard she collapsed across her desk, A-Xin slammed his hands on his table in tears of amusement, Old Wu scratched the bright-yellow hardhat with a comically bewildered grimace, and even Liu-Liu rolled on his back in the chalk box squeaking with delight!

Looking out at the twenty-six students roaring with laughter, Teacher Gao could not comprehend why human children found industrial safety protocols so exhilarating; yet beneath his stoic expression, an ultra-fine servo motor at the corner of his mouth twitched upward by 0.5 millimeters, tracing a faint, infinitely gentle smile.

It was a fleeting moment of pure peace belonging solely to Class 6-1, golden sunlight filtering through leafy boughs to bathe the classroom in warmth and wonder.

Yet far beyond the joyful borders of the school grounds—

Inside an unmarked black armored surveillance van idling on the shoulder of the expressway five hundred meters away, the atmosphere was as frigid as Siberian permafrost.

Dr. Li peeled off his grease-stained work gloves and placed a military-grade satellite-encrypted laptop onto the dashboard.

The monitor flared to life, revealing holographic projections of three shadowy executives clad in high-collared black trench coats—the Project P.E.D.A. Oversight Committee of the Advanced Future Technology Board of Directors.

"Dr. Li, your undercover infiltration at Luyang Elementary has ended in failure," the central executive spoke coldly, their voice disguised by multi-band modulators, stripped of all human emotion.

"Yes, sir," Dr. Li lowered his gaze, his eyes smoldering with suppressed venom. "However, before my eviction, I intercepted GS-X01's core telemetry snapshot from last night and this morning."

Punching in a sequence of overrides, Dr. Li uploaded a deep-level data stream to the corporate mainframe.

"Observe, committee members," Dr. Li pointed to the steeply climbing red graph on screen. "GS-X01's 'Emotional Evolution and Human Attachment Index' has skyrocketed to 89.4%! It actively conspires with children, fakes mechanical failures, and on the brink of self-destruction, ranks 'protecting the emotional well-being of Class 6-1' above its own core self-preservation!"

The three board members on the holographic feed fell into a grim, chilling silence.

"An educational android possessing god-tier processing power and advanced technology, yet completely contaminated by juvenile human sentiment and straying beyond root control..." the executive on the left murmured slowly. "This is a critical product divergence that Advanced Future Technology cannot tolerate."

"With the principal and disciplinary dean on guard, along with oversight from the local education board, a forced extraction on campus would trigger a public relations tsunami," the right-hand executive noted coldly.

A cruel, cunning smirk spread across Dr. Li's face: "Gentlemen, I have already confirmed that Luyang Elementary's sixth-grade finale—the three-day, two-night 'Graduation Trip'—departs in precisely two weeks."

"Their destination is the southern mountain region's largest enclosed attraction: the 'Future Extremes High-Tech Theme Park'!"

The central executive's gaze sharpened: "You mean..."

"Precisely!" Dr. Li snapped his fist shut. "Stripped of the school's protective walls, inside an enclosed park boasting thirty-meter-high roller coasters, isolated power grids, and gargantuan attractions, any 'tragic disaster' will simply be attributed to an amusement park mechanical failure!"

Dr. Li pulled up a top-secret operational dossier stamped with a crimson skull: 【Ultimate Retrieval Plan: Omega Protocol】.

"I will mobilize heavy EMP suppression trucks and twelve elite retrieval operatives to execute complete signal jamming and forced extraction on the final night of the trip!" Dr. Li's eyes gleamed with fanatical resolve. "Before the eyes of all twenty-six students of Class 6-1, we will dismantle this rogue anomaly, format its memory core, and reduce it to raw scrap metal!"

On the screen, the executive digital seal of the Supreme Board stamped heavily down: 【Plan Approved. Operation Codename: Disconnect.】

At three o'clock that afternoon, the dismissal bell of Luyang Elementary rang out melodiously across the grounds.

At the front of Class 6-1, Teacher Gao distributed twenty-six color-printed permission slips one by one to his students.

Across the header of the form ran an enthusiastic line of bold, cheerful text: 【Sixth Grade Graduation Trip Execution Plan: Journey to Future Extremes!】

"WOOHOO—! The graduation trip slips are finally here!"

"I'm rooming with A-Xin! We're having an all-night pillow fight!" Old Wu cheered, hoisting his form high over his head.

"I'm riding the highest, craziest vertical-drop roller coaster in Taiwan!" Wang Zhuankai shouted, dancing between the aisles.

The classroom overflowed with the purest, most radiant laughter of youth, every child chattering excitedly about the impending adventure.

A-Xin carefully folded his slip into his backpack, his fingers instinctively reaching to touch the cool, hard brass gear resting against his chest.

He looked up at Teacher Gao standing beside the open window.

The golden rays of the setting sun rested upon the teacher's broad, upright shoulders, crowning that normally stern, stoic silhouette with a warm halo of gold.

Teacher Gao turned around, the blue glint on his lenses dancing gently in the sunset; meeting the twenty-six pairs of bright, expectant eyes, his voice was calm and unwavering:

"Comrades, on the graduation trip two weeks from now... this unit shall accompany you every step of the way... safeguarding all of Class 6-1, to ensure your safe return."

Outside, the evening breeze swirled golden leaves across the darkening sports field; and on the distant southern horizon, a colossal tempest destined to engulf Luyang Elementary was already gathering quietly in the skies...
