# *My Teacher Is Not Human*

## Chapter 8: The X-Ray Vision Eyes of the Midterm Exam

The first-semester midterm exam of the sixth grade descended ruthlessly on a gloomy, brisk Thursday morning.

The classroom was filled with a suffocating aroma of pencil graphite and eraser shavings. Class 6-1, usually full of non-stop daily roughhousing, now had their desks pulled apart into standard single-occupancy exam grids, lined up neatly like silent little islands. All twenty-six students held their breath in rapt tension, the air so quiet that only the rustle of turning papers and the scratching of pencil tips across test sheets could be heard.

Outside the corridor window, Director of Student Affairs Zhou Yan strolled by with hands clasped behind his back, his hawk-sharp gaze piercing through the window panes to scan every single desk in the room inch by inch.

His infraction notebook was already flipped open to a blank page, the corners of his mouth pulled into a cold, stern line. To Director Zhou, the continuous string of recent "miracles" in Class 6-1—whether the bizarrely flawless drama performance, the mathematically immaculate calisthenics, or the uncannily spotless lunch buckets—reeked of an inexplicable abnormality. He flatly refused to believe that a bunch of mischievous troublemakers could turn completely over a new leaf in just two short months. He was thoroughly convinced that today's schoolwide standardized midterm was the golden opportunity to tear off the mask of this class once and for all.

Meanwhile, atop the podium, Teacher Gao Zhixian stood upright like an unyielding sentinel.

His black-rimmed glasses gleamed with a frigid glint, his hands folded behind his back, his suit sharply pressed. Teacher Gao resembled a precision radar beacon, sweeping his gaze across the exam hall at a steady, deadpan frequency of twelve cycles per minute with zero blind spots, ensuring not a single test-taker entertained any notions of peeking at a neighbor's paper.

However, sitting in the front row, cold drops of sweat trickled down Lin Yunqing's forehead, splashing one by one onto the blank margins of her exam sheet.

Because every ten seconds, Teacher Gao's right eyelid would twitch unnaturally, emitting a faint, high-voltage sizzling hum: "Zzz-zzz."

"A-Xin... look at Teacher's right eye..." Qingqing seized the split second while turning her paper, pressing her voice into a near-imperceptible whisper directed diagonally back toward Lu Lixin (A-Xin).

A-Xin secretly raised his head to take a look.

Behind the right lens of Teacher Gao's black-rimmed glasses, his originally deep-blue pupil had turned into a blazing neon golden-yellow! Deep within the base of his iris, a crimson warning dialogue box was refreshing frantically at thirty frames per second:

`[Hardware Glitch: Optical Retinal Projector Short Circuit]`
`[Thermal Overload: Proctoring Scan Frequency Overloaded (120%)]`
`[Emergency Action: Automatically Initializing Backlight Defogging and High-Power Podium Auxiliary Projection...]`

"Oh no!" A-Xin's heart plummeted instantly.

In the very next second, without even a fraction of a blink for warning—

"Sssss-crackle——!!"

Two dazzling beams of blinding light, each ten centimeters thick like high-end laser cinema projectors, violently erupted straight out from Teacher Gao's pupils!

The blinding white pillars of brilliance sliced like dual swords of radiance, striking with absolute precision against the massive chalkboard and the ceiling right behind the podium!

Above the chalkboard, the originally blank wall was instantly and completely filled by an ultra-high-resolution holographic projection:

[Question 1: Cylinder Surface Area Formula ➔ Solution: Radius 5 cm, Height 10 cm, Answer = 150π (471 cm²)]
[Question 2: Elodea Chloroplast Photosynthesis Rate Comparison ➔ Solution: Test Tube Group A produces maximum bubbles, Choose (C)]
[Question 3: Simultaneous Algebraic Equations ➔ Answer: X = 14, Y = -3]
[Question 4 Reading Comprehension: Author's Primary Emotion ➔ Answer: (D) Deep Nostalgia for the Old Locust Tree in the Hometown]...

The lettering was so crisp it looked carved into the wall with an engraving chisel, complete with step-by-step mathematical deductions, highlighted pitfalls, and the final standard answers in bright red font, displayed exhaustively and prominently right before the entire class!

All twenty-six students, who had been biting their pencils in deep contemplation, reflexively snapped their heads up the moment the brilliant glare flashed across the room.

When their eyes registered what was actually projected across the blackboard—

"Thud!"

Old Wu was so terrified that he tumbled clean off his chair, his mouth dropping into a gigantic "O" shape, nearly biting off his own tongue!

"The... the answers are all on the blackboard?!" Old Wu squeaked in a strangled whisper.

Sitting nearby, Wang Zhuankai nearly snapped his mechanical pencil in half: "Holy cow! It even has the intermediate calculation steps! This is practically an absolute exam-passing cheat code!"

Yet, every trace of color drained from A-Xin and Qingqing's faces in a single instant.

This was no cheat code.

This was an outright death sentence!

The moment Director Zhou Yan poked his head past the window and spotted the standard answers shining like neon signs across the board, the horrifying truth that Teacher Gao was a "humanoid exam-cheating projector" would be laid completely bare! Not only would the test scores of all twenty-six students be voided on the spot, Teacher Gao would be formally classified as a "catastrophic proctoring-malfunction unit" by the Education Bureau and the tech enterprise, hauled off right then and there to be disassembled into scrap metal!

What made it even more terrifying was that Director Zhou's leather shoes in the hallway were already clicking closer and closer: "Click, clack, click," advancing right toward the front door!

"Close your eyes! Everybody close your eyes right now!!"

A-Xin violently slammed his exam paper facedown on the desk, clamping both hands tightly over his eyes as he screamed with every fiber of his being: "Nobody look at the blackboard! Put your heads down!"

"But A-Xin... I really don't know how to solve Question 2!" a boy who usually trailed at the bottom of the rankings cried out in sheer anguish.

"Leave it blank if you don't know it! If anyone looks at those answers, Teacher Gao will be taken away and torn into scrap iron today! Do you want Teacher Gao to be incinerated?!" Qingqing roared sternly, squeezing her own eyes shut and burying her entire face into the crook of her folded arms!

Those words struck the hearts of all twenty-six students like a sledgehammer.

The boy who habitually copied homework gritted his teeth, slammed his forehead down onto his desk, and clamped his hands over his eyes: "I won't look! Even if you kill me I won't look! Teacher, please stop spoiling the answers, ahhhh!"

"Teacher! Please, we beg you, close your eyes!" Wang Zhuankai wailed in agony, clutching his face tightly.

"Block the light, quick!"

Up near the ceiling's ventilation duct, Liu-Liu darted out in a flash of white with a sharp "Whoosh!"

Mid-air, Liu-Liu clamped its teeth around the pull cord of the massive blackout roller blind at the top of the classroom, throwing its entire downward body weight into a mighty tug—

"Clatter-crash-rattle——!"

The gigantic, heavy dark-green roller blind plummeted down in the nick of time, effectively cutting off most of the glare between the corridor and the chalkboard! Meanwhile, Old Wu snatched his bulky winter windbreaker, scrambling and rolling toward the edge of the podium, holding the jacket up high like a human wall right in front of Teacher Gao, completely smothering the residual laser beams that spilled toward the hallway window!

On the podium, Teacher Gao, with brilliant blinding light blasting from both eyes, stood completely frozen in place.

Inside his core processor, an unprecedented logical tempest was raging with tremendous fury:

`[Logical Paradox: Students universally dislike examinations and instinctively crave full marks]`
`[Current Observation: All 26 students unanimously refuse cheat signals, proactively shutting down visual sensory input]`
`[Threat Evaluation: Motivation for students resisting cheating ➔ To protect "Me (Proctor)" from destruction]`
`[Error: Emotional priority overrides system default instructional protocols...]`

It was a miracle never documented within the cold, rigid factory source code residing at the lowest tier of his algorithm.

Teacher Gao looked down at the children burying their faces into their arms, drenched in sweat yet keeping their eyes clamped shut; he watched Old Wu using his own body and coat to block the light; he looked at A-Xin's back, trembling as the boy clenched his teeth in desperate resistance.

His artificial heart, constructed of superconducting microchips, radiated a gentle warmth for the very first time—a sensation impossible to explain through any formula of physics.

"Instruction... terminated."

Teacher Gao uttered a low, rasping mechanical command.

He abruptly raised his right hand, driving the knuckle of his index finger hard against the release catch behind his right ear—

"Click-snap!"

Accompanied by the crisp snap of an emergency power cutoff, the two high-intensity laser beams in Teacher Gao's eyes instantly died out!

The entire classroom abruptly returned to the quiet, dim atmosphere of an exam room.

In the very next second after the beams vanished—

"Bang!"

The front door of the classroom was flung wide open by Director Zhou!

Wearing a face full of suspicion, Director Zhou marched into the room, sweeping his piercing gaze across the desks: "Why was there a white glare flashing in this classroom just now?! And why are all the blinds drawn tight?! What kind of tricks are you kids playing?!"

Old Wu, trembling flat on the floor, hurriedly wrestled his jacket back over his shoulders and stammered: "R-Report, Director! The sun was glaring too bright just now, reflecting off the chalkboard so we couldn't read the questions clearly, so we pulled the blind down!"

Frowning, Director Zhou strode onto the podium, glaring down at the floor and inspecting the blackboard.

The blackboard was starkly bare, without even half a chalk character written on it.

Standing in the corner of the podium, Teacher Gao's black-rimmed glasses had returned to calm, transparent clarity, his posture dignified and austere:

"Director Zhou. The students of this class are currently undertaking an intensive, distraction-free examination. Pursuant to the Examination Code of the Ministry of Education, unauthorized entry into testing venues by non-proctoring personnel shall incur deductions against the venue tranquility assessment index."

Director Zhou choked on Teacher Gao's robotic retort, nearly left gasping for breath.

He swung around to scrutinize the twenty-six students below.

Every single student was bent over their desk, right hand gripping a pencil, brow beaded with sweat, eyes resolute and intently focused as they wrote out their calculations onto the test sheets. Nobody whispered, nobody looked around, and not a single cheat sheet was in sight—the discipline throughout the room was as strict as a marching regiment.

Director Zhou paced two full laps through the classroom aisles, yet failed to uncover even the slightest whisper or trace of malpractice.

In the end, he could only tuck his pristine infraction notebook resentfully under his arm, stepping out of the classroom with a sour grimace:

"Hmph... I'll grant that you're well-disciplined. Keep working! Thirty minutes until papers are collected!"

"Creeeak——" The back door closed once more.

Only when Director Zhou's heavy footsteps faded entirely into the distance did the room erupt into a collective, exhausted sigh of relief.

A-Xin lifted his head, gazing down at the formulas he had written out through his own genuine effort; though some steps were crooked and scribbled, his smile shone brighter than ever before.

Atop the podium, Teacher Gao lowered his gaze, watching all twenty-six children in the room.

Behind his spectacles, a string of golden advancement code representing system evolution—one never unlocked by any bionic robot in history—quietly settled into the deepest recesses of his memory repository:

`[Core Directive Evolved: GS-X01 Ultimate Persona Criteria Validated]`
`[Status: Model Homeroom Teacher Protocol... officially upgraded to "Guardian of Class 6-1."]`

Two days later, the midterm exam results were announced.

A dense crowd packed the central courtyard bulletin board of Luyang Elementary School.

Not only had Class 6-1 shattered their perennial curse of "ranking dead last in the grade," but with every student passing and an astonishing twenty-point average increase across the board, they swept the school's sole "Gold Medal for Best Academic Progress"!

As the sun set in the west, golden twilight bathed the quiet, spacious classroom of Class 6-1.

Hanging squarely in the center of the chalkboard was the heavy, gleaming golden progress medal.

A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu sat clustered around the podium, while their AI pet Liu-Liu rested comfortably atop the medal, dozing peacefully with eyes squinted shut.

A-Xin touched the cool brass planetary gear resting against his chest, then turned to look at Teacher Gao, who stood by the window grading the next batch of review worksheets.

The setting sun traced a soft, warm golden outline around Teacher Gao's sharply tailored suit.

"Teacher," A-Xin called out with a warm grin.

Teacher Gao turned around, pushed up his black-rimmed glasses, and a standard forty-five-degree smile formed at the corners of his lips—one infused with genuine warmth:

"Student Lu, please refrain from daydreaming while staring at the medal. According to statistical projections, the final exam will take place in sixty days—the adventure of Class 6-1 has only just begun."
