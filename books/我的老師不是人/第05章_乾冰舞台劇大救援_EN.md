# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 5: Dry-Ice Drama Rescue

Although Lin Yunqing had miraculously hammered the number-three brass planetary gear back into Teacher Gao's nape yesterday evening in the sports equipment room using a rubber mallet and precision screwdrivers, "unofficial brute-force maintenance" inevitably left lingering aftereffects.

During the morning integrated activities period, Teacher Gao Zhixian stood tall at the podium, his suit as impeccably pressed as ever and his tie as ruler-straight as ever.

However, sitting in the front row, Lin Yunqing was so nervous that cold sweat slicked her palms.

For every time Teacher Gao blinked, a tiny string of yellow warning notices popped up in the lower-right corner of his black-framed glasses:

`[Thermal Warning: Liquid Cooling Circulation Resistance High]`
`[Core Temperature: 68°C... 72°C... Rising]`

"Dear students of Class 6-1," Teacher Gao began in an even tone, though deep within his voice there hummed an almost imperceptible, low whistle akin to a kettle on the verge of boiling, "today's topic for integrated activities is 'Multitasking Logic and Problem Solving.' This teacher shall simultaneously accept twenty-six difficult questions across twenty-six different subjects from all twenty-six students, delivering optimal solutions for all concurrently within five seconds."

"Teacher, don't push yourself!" A-Xin shouted in panic from his desk in a hushed voice.

Yet the rest of the class had clearly not realized the impending danger, excitedly raising their hands one after another to fire off queries:

"Teacher! How do you convert light-years into catties?!"
"Teacher! Why don't penguins get frostbite on their feet?!"
"Teacher! If Old Wu eats eight pork buns a meal, how many pig farms will he devour in a year?!"

"Question data intake complete. Computational core running at full throttle—"

The moment Teacher Gao's words landed, a blinding burst of azure light erupted from his eyes!

His superconductive bionic processor began redlining at the extreme rate of tens of billions of operations per second. Unfortunately, the gear that had been violently hammered into place seemed to have jammed the exhaust valve—

"Sizzle—sizzle—sizzle—!!"

A piercing, shrieking sound of a pressure valve venting tore through the front of the classroom!

Atop Teacher Gao's slick, neatly parted black-haired head, there was a sudden "poof," and a dense plume of pure white steam as thick as a bowl shot straight into the air!

"Waaah! Smoke! Teacher's head is spewing steam!" Old Wu was so terrified he squished the pork bun in his hand into mush.

Massive billows of scalding, snow-white water vapor poured out like an erupting volcano, endlessly gushing from the nape of Teacher Gao's neck and hairline. Within mere seconds, the entire podium was completely engulfed in a swirling white fog, which began rapidly expanding toward the front row of desks!

"Calculations... complete..."

Standing dead center in the dense fog, Teacher Gao looked entirely like an immortal sage from a celestial realm. As he spoke, two long wisps of white smoke puffed from his mouth: "Penguin feet possess a countercurrent heat exchange system... Old Wu will consume 1.2 pig farms per annum... however... the local thermal dissipation system has reached maximum threshold... emergency pressure discharge imminent..."

"Psssshhhhh————!!"

The white smoke from Teacher Gao's head billowed even more violently, resembling nothing less than a professional-grade theatrical dry-ice smoke grenade detonating inside the classroom!

Just as all twenty-six students in the room began coughing, rubbing their watering eyes, and the situation threatened to spiral into total chaos—

A shrill, urgent electronic siren suddenly sounded from the ceiling ventilation pipe!

"Beep-beep-beep! Priority alert! Code Red Alert!"

A snow-white silhouette darted out from the vent grille like an electric bolt. The AI pet "Liu-Liu" tumbled and scrambled onto A-Xin's desk, its little fox ears swiveling backward frantically as its obsidian electronic eyes projected real-time surveillance footage of the hallway:

"Black-Faced Judge! Dean of Students Zhou Yan holding the red violation clipboard! Distance to back door: only fifteen meters! Ten meters! He smelled something burning and is sprinting toward our classroom!!"

All twenty-six hearts leaped into their throats in an instant!

If the Black-Faced Dean caught Teacher Gao spewing white smoke like a steam locomotive, the secret that Teacher Gao was a robot would be blown wide open right then and there! The tech corporation's retrieval truck would arrive as early as this afternoon to drag Teacher Gao away and tear him apart for scrap!

"What do we do?! The windows won't budge! The smoke is way too thick to clear out in time!" Old Wu stomped his feet in a frenzy.

"If we can't clear it—then we don't clear it!"

At this critical juncture, A-Xin's brain flashed with a stroke of genius that surpassed ordinary human wit.

Stepping onto his chair, he pumped his fist and bellowed at the top of his lungs, his voice overpowering the roar of the venting steam: "Attention, Class 6-1! Initiate emergency protocol—All-Class Gala Performance of *Snow White and the Phantom Fog*! Qingqing, pull the curtains! Old Wu, grab brooms for spears! Wang Zhuankai, you play Snow White!"

"Huh? Me play Snow White? But I'm a boy!" Wang Zhuankai protested.

"Cut the chatter! You've got the fairest skin in the whole class! Put the tablecloth on!" A-Xin yanked the red dust-cloth from the podium and tossed it over Wang Zhuankai's head.

The entire classroom mobilized in a flash!

The twenty-six students unleashed an unprecedented display of collective teamwork. Qingqing snapped the navy-blue curtains shut with a swish, plunging the room into dramatic gloom; two boys seized mops, striking medieval knight dueling poses in the thick mist; Old Wu inverted a black wastebasket over his head to act as the magic mirror; and A-Xin grabbed a bamboo broom, striking a profoundly solemn stance atop the front desk shrouded in mist.

At the very last second—

"BAM!!"

The back door was kicked violently open by sheer force!

The pitch-black, austere face of Dean Zhou Yan, resembling a judge from the underworld, appeared in the doorway. Clutching his red violation clipboard tightly, his eyes sharp as scalpels, he was prepared to catch them red-handed:

"Class 6-1! What in broad daylight are you fools causing a racket about—"

However, Dean Zhou's roar was choked halfway down his throat by the awe-inspiring sight before his eyes.

The moment the door swung open, a chilly, dense wave of white mist rolled out of the classroom like a tidal wave, washing straight over his face.

Inside the deep, shadowed classroom, billowing smoke swirled in layers, evoking an enchanted fairy tale deep within the Black Forest.

At the highest point of the podium, Teacher Gao Zhixian stood with hands clasped behind his back in his tailored suit, his head continuously emitting a sacred, noble aura of white mist. His black-framed glasses reflected the crisp chill of the vapor, radiating an authority as grand as an Olympian deity from Greek myth.

Beside Teacher Gao, Wang Zhuankai, draped in the red tablecloth, was delicately biting half an apple with a helpless expression, while A-Xin gripped his bamboo broom, roaring with Shakespearean operatic agony:

"Ah! Mist envelopes the forest! The poisoned apple pierces the soul! O great Forest Sage, Teacher Gao, bestow upon this land the final glimmer of light—!"

Old Wu, head encased in the wastebasket, banged the metal tin with a boom-boom: "Magic mirror, magic mirror on the wall! Who is the most handsome teacher of them all—!"

In unison, all twenty-six students dropped to one knee amidst the thick fog, chanting at the top of their lungs: "Teacher Gao! Teacher Gao! Teacher Gao!"

Dean Zhou stood completely petrified in the doorway.

His perpetually scowling black face now resembled a shattered artist's palette of conflicting expressions.

"What... what is this..." Dean Zhou sniffed the air hard, frowning as he surveyed the sky-high fog. "Where did this smoke come from? Where did you get the budget for dry ice?!"

Every student's heart skipped a beat.

Right at the razor's edge when cold sweat soaked A-Xin's palms—

Standing at the epicenter of the mist, Teacher Gao slowly pushed up his glasses.

His computational core had finally cooled down to the safe threshold. The white steam from his head abruptly receded, dispersing into two elegant, fine wisps through the exhaust ports behind his ears.

Turning around, he looked down at Dean Zhou with an expressionless gaze, his tone dignified, composed, and utterly without flaw:

"Reporting, Dean Zhou. This class is currently conducting an immersive rehearsal for next month's county-wide student drama competition. This is my proprietary 'Nano-Ultrasonic Ambient Water-Mist Atmospheric Module,' one hundred percent eco-friendly, non-toxic, and harmless. Its objective is to stimulate students' artistic empathy receptors with maximal realism."

Dean Zhou was dumbfounded.

He looked down at the violation clipboard in his hand, glanced up at the twenty-six students thoroughly immersed in their convincing acting, and finally fixed his gaze upon the enigmatic Teacher Gao, who seemed to radiate celestial grace.

As the most old-fashioned disciplinarian in school, though he sensed something was oddly peculiar, he had to admit—this theatrical smoke effect was rivaled only by the National Theater!

"Ahem! Hrmph!"

Dean Zhou awkwardly cleared his throat, tucked away his violation clipboard, and said with a stern face:

"Absurd! Rehearsals are fine... but as sixth-graders, academic preparation and exams still come first! Still... this stage fog effect is undeniably top-tier. Keep it up, but open the windows to ventilate before recess! Do you hear me?!"

"Loud and clear—thank you, Dean—!!" The entire class yelled in unison at the top of their lungs.

Dean Zhou nodded in satisfaction, turned on his leather heels, and strode down the hallway.

The moment his footsteps vanished around the stairwell corner—

"PHEW——————!!!"

The air seemed to be sucked out of the classroom all at once as all twenty-six students collapsed across their desks like noodles.

Wang Zhuankai tore off the red tablecloth, drenched in sweat: "Holy smokes... I thought I was getting a major demerit for sure!"

Old Wu removed the wastebasket from his head, exhaled a long breath of relief, and wiped moisture from his face.

On the podium, Teacher Gao looked down at the sprawling, sweat-soaked children.

Deep within his pupils, where the blue radiance was softening gently, the core log silently updated with a piece of unprecedented data:

`[System Log: Non-logical collective deception behavior detected]`
`[Event Description: Dry-Ice Drama Rescue]`
`[Teamwork Cohesion: 100% (Maximum Cap reached)]`
`[Conclusion: Students of Class 6-1... truly possess extraordinary vitality]`

The corners of Teacher Gao's mouth curled upward ever so slightly, puffing a final microscopic wisp of white steam:

"Attention, all personnel. The theatrical performance is concluded—now, open your textbooks to page forty-five, and we shall resume our lesson."
