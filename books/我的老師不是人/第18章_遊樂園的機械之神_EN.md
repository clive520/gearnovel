# Chapter 18: Machine Deity at the Amusement Park

At eight-thirty the following morning, the colossal silver alloy gates of the Future Extremes High-Tech Theme Park swung slowly open amidst a magnificent symphonic fanfare.

Morning sunlight bathed the transparent transit tubes and geodesic domes suspended in mid-air like scenes from a science fiction epic, transforming the entire park into an interstellar spaceport that had just touched down on Earth.

"CHARGE—!"

Wearing matching navy-blue school windbreakers and bearing the "Emergency Anti-Flattening Kits" distributed by Teacher Gao the previous day flush against their chests, the twenty-six students of Class 6-1 surged through the turnstiles like twenty-six joyful cannonballs.

Teacher Gao Zhixian strode in the center of the formation with the rigid, towering poise of a pine tree; his dark-rimmed glasses refracted a calm, brilliant sheen in the dawn light, holding a high-precision 3D topological park map tablet while maintaining millisecond-precise strides.

"Pursuant to this unit's 《Theme Park Optimal Transit Topological Algorithm》," Teacher Gao's synthesized voice carried evenly across the morning breeze, "between 09:00 and 11:00, pedestrian density within the central 'Interstellar Cyber Carnival Pavilion' stands at a mere 18.4% of peak capacity. Prioritizing exploration of this zone will reduce mean attraction queuing latency by 74.2%."

"AWESOME! The arcade! The arcade!" Old Wu leaped in place with sheer exuberance, the soft padding of his tummy jiggling like jelly.

Passing through a glass skyway shimmering with neon hues, a gargantuan indoor cyber carnival pavilion spanning the footprint of three full football fields opened up before their eyes.

The cavernous hall was a blaze of dazzling lights, where hundreds of cutting-edge holographic arcade cabinets, haptic light-gun ranges, dual taiko drums, and claw machines hummed with alluring electronic soundscapes.

And dead-center upon the Grand Prize exhibition dais of the pavilion stood a monolithic plushie standing a full 1.5 meters tall, swathed in silky, golden-dusted fleece—the "Interstellar Limited Edition Ultra-Giant Space Golden Bear"!

"Sweet mother of dumplings..." Old Wu's sneakers froze to the floor as if pinned by industrial electromagnets, drool nearly escaping the corner of his lips. "The Golden Bear! That's the exact same bear I was hugging in my dreams last night!"

A crowd of dejected, defeated tourists milled around the booth, while a carnival vendor wearing a black cowboy hat and chewing on a toothpick tossed game tokens casually in his palm with a smug grin.

"Step right up, step right up, don't miss your chance of a lifetime!" the vendor hollered at the top of his lungs. "The toughest challenge in the entire park, precision-engineered with German craftsmanship—the 'Faster-Than-Light Reactionary Whack-a-Mole Terminator'! Strike all ninety-nine randomly springing mechanical moles in succession without missing a single one, and this twelve-thousand-dollar limited-edition Golden Bear is yours to take home!"

"Miss even a single mole, or register an impact latency exceeding 0.1 seconds in thirty seconds, and you're instantly disqualified!"

"Let me at it!" Old Wu volunteered eagerly, digging out his pocket money to purchase ten tokens and stepping onto the platform with chest puffed out.

"BEEP—Three-second countdown, challenge commence!"

The alloy apertures of the cabinet shrieked with piercing chimes as four mechanical moles erupted simultaneously along erratic, curved trajectories!

"HAAA-YAH!" Old Wu hoisted the hefty foam-cushioned rubber mallet, slamming it down with all the muscle in his body!

"WHACK!" The first mole was struck dead-on.

Yet in the very next millisecond, three other apertures ejected decoy moles with deceptive counter-rotational spins, rocketing upward with the velocity of discharged bullets!

Caught in utter disarray, Old Wu flailed the mallet in a whirlwind of black shadows until—*THUD!*—the mallet bounced off the metal bezel, nearly sending him tumbling face-first into the holes from overextended momentum.

【Time Expired! Accuracy: 4/99. Challenge Failed! Thank you for playing!】 The machine spat out a patronizing digital chuckle.

"How on earth can they be that fast?! Did those moles have rocket boosters strapped to their backs?!" Old Wu slumped onto the floor, staring with tearful longing at the glorious, unreachable Golden Bear.

Wang Zhuankai and A-Xin took turns stepping up to challenge the machine, but the best run topped out at the eleventh mole before the erratic, non-linear springing patterns blurred their vision, forcing them to concede defeat.

The vendor chortled with hands on his hips, wearing an insufferable smirk: "Give it up, kids! The springs in this baby are driven by aerospace micro-motors running chaotic anti-cheat algorithms. Even a national table tennis champion only managed forty moles here; human synaptic reaction speed simply cannot breach that threshold!"

Just as all twenty-six pupils were hanging their heads in collective disappointment, a white-gloved hand with slender, steel-like fingers rested gently atop the icy start button of the control console.

"You asserted moments ago... that the spring actuation of this mechanism is driven by aerospace micro-motors?"

Teacher Gao Zhixian had materialized before the cabinet, his black-rimmed lenses scanning the internal circuit topology and spring fatigue coefficients at a rate of one thousand hertz.

"That's right, pal. What, you fancy a go yourself, Teacher?" The vendor arched an eyebrow, his face dripping with condescension. "Same rules apply to grown-ups: miss one in thirty seconds and you're out!"

Teacher Gao remained as calm as an iceberg, retrieving a metal token from his breast pocket with practiced grace and feeding it into the slot.

"Correcting your erroneous assertion," Teacher Gao responded deadpan, his synthesized voice devoid of inflection. "Human synaptic transmission velocity is indeed constrained by the physiological threshold of one hundred and twenty meters per second. However... this unit is not human."

"Huh? Say what?" The vendor blinked in confusion.

Teacher Gao did not deign to reply; instead, he reached out with both hands, simultaneously gripping the twin rubber mallets resting on either side of the console!

"Akimbo dual-wielding mode?!" A-Xin sucked in a sharp breath of disbelief.

"Guji!" Liu-Liu bounded up onto the neon signage above the machine, his sapphire optical sensors locked squarely onto the score display.

【Challenge Commenced! Countdown: Thirty Seconds!】

A shrill siren sounded as all nine metallic apertures flared with blinding crimson strobes!

WHOOSH! WHOOSH! WHOOSH! WHOOSH!

Wave one: four mechanical moles erupted from their burrows with near-transonic acceleration!

Teacher Gao's arms moved.

It was a motion entirely beyond the resolving power of the human retina—to the twenty-six students and dozens of onlookers, Teacher Gao's arms dissolved into a seamless, kaleidoscopic blur of cobalt light!

"CRACK! CRACK! CRACK! CRACK!"

Four crushing impacts overlapped into a single, earth-shattering boom within the same microsecond! The four moles had barely poked their noses out before twin mallets hammered them back into the abyss at a perfectly perpendicular trajectory!

【Hits: 10... 20... 30...】 The red LED counter raced upward like a runaway stopwatch!

The central motherboard of the cabinet seemed enraged by this non-biological input rate; its internal cooling fans screamed at maximum rpm as it forced the software into anti-cheat overdrive frenzy!

Moles rained out of all nine apertures in chaotic, non-repeating barrages, the scent of burning electrical coils wafting through the vents!

Yet standing before the console, Teacher Gao did not sway by so much as a single millimeter.

Behind his lenses, twin blazing concentric rings of cobalt blue burned at full processing capacity, cascades of textual telemetry pouring through his HUD:

【Target Vector Acquisition: Complete.】

【Air Resistance Compensation: 0.0012 Newtons.】

【Alternating Mallet Cadence: 48 strikes per second.】

"THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD-THUD—!"

That was no longer a game of whack-a-mole; it was an absolute, micron-precision carpet bombing! The twin mallets wove an impenetrable canopy of kinetic force, decimating every emerging component the exact instant it broke the surface!

The vendor's jaw unhinged into an 'O' shape, the toothpick slipping from his lips and clicking onto the floor, his eyeballs nearly popping from their sockets!

"Wh-what number is he on?!" Old Wu shook A-Xin's sleeve, bellowing in manic euphoria.

"Eighty! Ninety! Ninety-five... NINETY-NINE!!" the entire class roared in synchronized unison!

In the final second, as the ninety-ninth gold-plated boss mole breached the surface, Teacher Gao's right wrist whipped horizontally at an absolute ninety-degree angle—

"KABOOM!"

The entire cabinet shuddered violently as the ninety-ninth mole was crushed flush into the steel plating!

And the digital score display, upon hitting 【99】, did not stop; accompanied by a shower of sparking shorts, it rolled over frantically into 【99999】!

【System Overflow! Error: Processing Capacity Exceeded! Historic Apex Unlocked: Mole Terminator!】

In the dead silence that gripped the pavilion, the coin return chute belched a thick plume of white steam, before all display lamps extinguished simultaneously as the circuitry fried completely!

"OH MY SWEET GOSH—!"

After an agonizing beat of stunned silence, the entire pavilion erupted into a deafening tidal wave of cheers that nearly lifted the steel rafters!

"LONG LIVE TEACHER GAO! TEACHER GAO IS A GOD!!" Old Wu wrapped his arms around a structural pillar, screaming with joyous abandon, while A-Xin and Wang Zhuankai hurled their caps into the rafters!

The vendor dropped to his knees before the charred console, cold sweat pouring down his face as he trembling hands lifted the 1.5-meter Golden Bear from the dais, presenting it reverently to Teacher Gao.

"M-Master! It's yours! Just please don't touch any more of my machines—there are only three of these units in all of Taiwan!"

Teacher Gao did not take the bear; instead, he pointed toward Old Wu, whose eyes were glowing emerald with drool cascading from his mouth: "Transfer this item to student Wu Chenkai. Pursuant to this unit's calculations, holding plush material of this mass will reduce his probability of distraction in gym class by 38.6%."

"BWAHAHAHA! MY GOLDEN BEAR!!" Old Wu plunged face-first into the fluffy belly of the golden beast, nearly fainting from culinary and plush bliss.

Yet this legendary conquest was only the opening movement of Class 6-1's arcade sweep.

"Teacher! The top prize at the Quantum Light-Gun Range next door is a full-scale motorized Cyber T-Rex!" Wang Zhuankai yelled, pointing toward the shooting gallery.

"And the Deep Space Claw Machine over there! It has the official Laser Kitty Lin Yunyun has wanted forever!" Qingqing pointed with sparkling eyes.

Teacher Gao adjusted his glasses, his calm gaze sweeping across the entire avenue of glowing grand-prize showcases.

"Class 6-1 wish-list query compiled," Teacher Gao stated placidly. "Pursuant to 《Class 6-1 Graduation Trip Incentive Protocol》, this unit shall achieve total prize coverage for all comrades within thirty minutes."

Over the next half-hour, the Cyber Carnival Pavilion endured the darkest, most miraculous onslaught in its decade of operation—

At the Quantum Light-Gun Range, Teacher Gao fired one-handed on maximum gale-force crosswind simulation, pulling the trigger every 0.02 seconds to drill all fifty laser bolts dead-center into the 10.01 bullseye, posting a 100% perfect score that pulverized the record set by an army special forces instructor!

Before the Deep Space Claw Machine, Teacher Gao's optical lasers mapped a 3D gravity matrix, computing claw swing harmonics and plush fabric friction; with twenty-three consecutive drops, the claw snagged every prize without a single miss, operating until the gantry winch motor burned out from sheer fatigue!

One plushie, two, five, ten, twenty...

When Teacher Gao led the class out of the flagship pavilion, an awe-inspiring spectacle paraded onto the sunlit concourse—

All twenty-six students of Class 6-1 were clutching colossal, limited-edition plush toys previously seen only on promotional posters!

Old Wu hugged the Golden Bear, Qingqing carried the Cyber Kitty, A-Xin was wrapped in a two-meter celestial dragon, and even Liu-Liu wore a miniature space-squirrel plushie around his neck!

Twenty-six children paraded down the central concourse in triumphant glory, prompting hundreds of onlookers to halt, snap photos, and gasp in utter disbelief!

"Look at that! Which school is that class from? They cleaned out every grand prize in the carnival!"

"That's insane! That golden bear has sat in that booth for three years without anyone winning it!"

A-Xin walked beside Teacher Gao holding the celestial dragon, feeling as though he were walking on air: "Teacher Gao, you were incredible back there! You truly are the Machine Deity of the Theme Park!"

Teacher Gao marched forward with his silver briefcase in hand, a faint line of code scrolling across his lenses: "Correction: This unit's designation is GS-X01, occupational title is Trainee Homeroom Teacher, not a theological deity. However, as class emotional indices have reached peak saturation, this praise is logged into the positive pedagogical feedback registry."

Gathering before the colossal mechanical fountain in the central plaza, the tour guide excitedly hoisted her camera as twenty-six students hoisted their giant plushies around their stoic yet softly smiling teacher:

"One, two, three—Class 6-1, legendary strike!"

"CLICK!"

The shutter snapped, capturing twenty-six radiant youthful smiles, twenty-six gargantuan plushies, and one extraordinary mechanical teacher in a photograph drenched in golden sunlight.

Yet at the exact summit of their jubilation—

Across the cloudless azure sky, an unnatural, bone-chilling gust of mountain wind swept over the park.

Perched on A-Xin's shoulder, Liu-Liu's triangular ears twitched alertly, his internal RF spectrum analyzer detecting a sharp, turbulent burst of directional electromagnetic pulses!

"Guji...!" Liu-Liu emitted a low, distressed whimper, his tiny paws clutching A-Xin's collar tightly.

Teacher Gao's strides halted momentarily, his optical sensors shifting into high-altitude atmospheric scanning mode.

Over the southern peaks where blue skies had reigned moments before, a colossal bank of ink-black storm clouds crackling with violet lightning was barreling toward the park with terrifying speed!

Barometric pressure plummeted; howling winds sent loose balloons spiraling into the sky as the ambient temperature plunged eight degrees in under two minutes!

And dead-center in the park, the thirty-meter-tall steel leviathan plunging vertically at ninety degrees—the 【Sky Quantum Coaster】—stood menacing and stark against the bruised lead-gray heavens.

"Teacher Gao! The rain is coming, and the coaster will close for weather soon!" Wang Zhuankai pleaded frantically. "Can we catch the very last train? Just once!"

"Yes, Teacher Gao! We came all this way; skipping the craziest coaster in Taiwan means our graduation trip isn't complete!" All twenty-six students turned pleading, hopeful eyes upon him.

Looking at the eager faces of his pupils, Teacher Gao's processor analyzed the incoming Doppler radar wind vectors; the red abort flag that should have prohibited boarding in severe weather was miraculously delayed by five minutes under the warmth of his emerging human algorithms.

"This unit calculates: twelve minutes remain before the primary squall line hits," Teacher Gao stowed his terminal, issuing a calm directive. "Boarding permitted. All units embark onto Train Consist Number One in orderly sequence; this unit shall accompany you aboard to maintain operational security."

"HOORAY—!" Twenty-six students cheered as they stowed their plushies in the lockers, racing toward the towering launch platform of the coaster.

And in that very same second, deep in the shadows of the park's isolated main electrical sub-station—

Wearing a tactical helmet, Dr. Li sneered as he disconnected the emergency earth ground cables, jamming a black electromagnetic timed shorting unit straight into the primary busbars!

"Board the train, little lambs..." Dr. Li murmured, watching the converging radar storm fronts and the coaster telemetry on his wrist display with murderous glee.

"A thirty-meter precipice, severe convective squalls, and a total park-wide blackout..."

"Let us see how you save twenty-six lives this time, GS-X01!"
