# Chapter 24: See You Tomorrow, Teacher Gao! (Series Grand Finale)

The June breeze carried the scorching heat of early summer and the fiery crimson of blooming flame-tree blossoms, gently rustling through the crowns of the ancient royal palms at Luyang Elementary School.

Strains of melodious, faintly wistful brass music drifted from the auditorium, where the annual commencement ceremony was underway.

Yet today, seated right in the center of the auditorium's front row, the twenty-six students of Class 6-1 presented a sight unlike any other, dressed in crisp navy graduation gowns with vivid red flame-tree corsages pinned to their chests.

Over the course of this unforgettable past year, they had experienced the school lunch miracle of heating meat buns with microwave fingertips, soap bubble surfing across hallway floors during spring cleaning, an all-class eyes-shut solidarity strike against exam x-ray projection, and had even linked arms thirty meters high in a raging storm over roller coaster tracks to wrest their teacher back from the clutches of a multinational tech corporation's armored tactical retrieval unit.

Now, all the chaos and danger had finally settled into peace.

In the wake of the breathtaking rescue at Future Extremes Theme Park, Principal Qiu Shouren and Dean of Student Affairs Zhou Yan had co-authored an eighty-page "Emergency Incident Response Report on Field Trip Amusement Equipment Malfunction," officially attributing the heroic train-arresting miracle to "a scientific self-rescue operation executed by faculty and students amidst extreme weather anomalies."

Meanwhile, having covertly switched sides, Dr. Li submitted a formal "GS-X01 Complete Structural Meltdown & Total Loss Report" to the tech corporation's executive board, permanently decommissioning Gao Zhixian's prototype experimental designation.

From that day forward, the world was short one nine-figure, cold-blooded educational bionic android; but on Luyang Elementary School's official faculty roster, there was forever one more fully certified homeroom teacher backed by the Ministry of Education——Gao Zhixian.

"Next, let us give our warmest round of applause to welcome the homeroom teacher of Class 6-1——Teacher Gao Zhixian, to the stage to present the graduation diplomas and personalized big-data commentary!" the master of ceremonies announced over the auditorium loudspeakers.

Hundreds of parents, teachers, and students instantly erupted into a thunderous tempest of applause and cheers.

Teacher Gao rose from his seat among the faculty.

He was clad as ever in his dark-gray suit, pressed so sharply that not a fold deviated by zero point one millimeters, his leather shoes striking the polished wooden floorboards in a steady, measured cadence.

Upon his face sat a brand-new pair of black-framed glasses whose lenses refracted the chandelier light with gentle warmth; only from mere inches away could one detect the faintest glimmer of blue system code sweeping across the edges.

And perched upon his left shoulder was none other than the AI pet, Liu-Liu, sporting a custom miniature mortarboard crafted from black velvet and happily flicking the tip of its antenna tail!

Striding onto the stage with his trademark sixty-centimeter paces, Teacher Gao took his place beside Principal Qiu and Dean Zhou, offering a slight, dignified bow to the assembly.

Clutching his vacuum flask of tea, Principal Qiu beamed warmly at Teacher Gao, the wrinkles around his eyes crinkling in profound gratification.

Beside him stood Dean Zhou Yan, who had miraculously donned a pristine dark suit and a deep red tie for today's occasion, carrying neither his fearsome cast-iron water pipe nor his dreaded rule-violation ledger.

Looking down at the band of legendary rascals who had once driven him to the brink of madness on a daily basis, this normally stony-faced "Black-Faced Judge" had faint rims of red around his eyes, clearing his throat repeatedly to mask his surge of emotion.

Accepting the microphone, Teacher Gao's deep, metallic-timbered baritone carried cleanly across the auditorium via the speakers:

"Good morning, respected parents, colleagues, and all twenty-six departing graduates of Class 6-1."

"According to analysis conducted across forty-eight million daily behavioral logs compiled by this unit... ahem, by this homeroom teacher over the preceding three hundred and sixty-five days, Class 6-1 exhibits the highest deviance metrics in Luyang Elementary School's sixty-eight-year history, yet simultaneously displays the strongest dynamic emotional resonance."

A ripple of affectionate chuckles drifted through the audience, while down in the front row, all twenty-six children puffed out their chests, their eyes shining with pride.

"At this juncture marking the conclusion of your elementary school phase, in accordance with our class's unique educational protocol, this teacher shall present your personalized big-data graduation commentary and honorary credentials."

Opening the golden folder upon the lectern, Teacher Gao directed his calm, laser-accurate gaze toward the first row:

"First: Student Wu Chenkai."

Leaping up in excitement, Old Wu bounded up the stage steps three at a time, his chubby face glowing beet-red with glee.

"Student Wu Chenkai. Over the past academic year, you have unlawfully stored and consumed a cumulative total of one thousand two hundred and forty-eight fresh steamed pork buns inside your classroom desk, generating three hundred and eighty-two grams of flour crumbs."

The audience burst into a roaring wave of laughter, while Old Wu sheepishly scratched the back of his head.

"However, upon the evacuation platform thirty meters above ground, utilizing your personal mass as mechanical damping, you generated three hundred and twenty newtons of reverse thrust, successfully shielding your classmates from severe crosswinds; furthermore, under conditions of extreme resource scarcity, you steadfastly maintained a flattened pork bun as your psychological anchor."

Teacher Gao presented a heavy, gold-embossed certificate along with a custom voucher:

"You are hereby awarded: The Grand Gold Medal for All-Weather Perpetual Muscular Endurance and Pork Bun Guardianship, alongside a one-year voucher for daily steamed buns at Luyang Old Street Bun Bakery, personally sponsored by this teacher. Congratulations, Student Wu Chenkai. You were not flattened."

Clutching the certificate, tears welled up in Old Wu's eyes as he wrapped both arms around Teacher Gao's waist, bawling: "Thank you, Teacher Gao! From now on, whenever I eat buns, I'll split every single one in half with you!"

A faint hum of servo rotation purred softly within Teacher Gao's chest as he gently patted Old Wu's broad shoulder: "It is recommended that the sharing proportion be adjusted to zero percent; this teacher's internal power core possesses zero metabolic mechanism for carbohydrates."

The auditorium erupted once more into delighted laughter at the familiar literal humor.

"Next: Student Lin Yunqing."

Qingqing bounded up the stage with brisk, graceful steps, her neat ponytail swinging jauntily, a miniature metal screwdriver still peeking discreetly from her uniform pocket.

"Student Lin Yunqing. During your tenure at this school, you executed forty-seven unauthorized disassemblies of campus instructional equipment, including the science laboratory centrifuge, the gymnasium volleyball pump, and this teacher's posterior cranial maintenance hatch."

Qingqing poked her tongue out playfully, while down below, Dean Zhou shook his head with an exasperated chuckle.

"Nonetheless, during the crucial twelve-minute emergency rescue, operating at a precision tolerance of zero point zero two millimeters, you successfully achieved the reverse-torque locking of an aerospace-grade planetary gear, thereby safeguarding this teacher's core dynamic memory banks."

Teacher Gao presented an exquisite certificate engraved onto a polished alloy plate:

"You are hereby appointed: Lifetime Chief Honorary Micron Mechanical Engineer of Luyang Elementary School. Student Lin Yunqing, should any mechanical malfunction arise in the future, this teacher's cranial maintenance diagnostic port shall permanently remain accessible under your administrative credentials."

Holding the cool, weighty alloy commission, tears brimmed in Qingqing's eyes as she bowed deeply to Teacher Gao: "Thank you, Teacher Gao! No matter where you are in the future, if your head ever starts smoking, I'll grab my toolkit and rush to your side immediately!"

"Next: Student Wang Zhuankai."

Wang Zhuankai, whose threshold for laughter was famously non-existent, stepped onto the stage with his facial muscles twitching uncontrollably.

"Student Wang Zhuankai. Your average latency threshold for laughter is zero point one two seconds, the lowest recorded in the school's historical database; yet during the storm along the human wall perimeter, you maintained a stern, unmoving countenance for forty-two consecutive minutes, successfully suppressing facial nerve reflex anomalies."

Handing him his certificate, Teacher Gao stated: "You are hereby awarded: The Campus Master of Extreme Facial Nerve Regulation Award. May your laughter remain vibrant throughout your junior high school years, though you are advised to avoid inducing resonance during formal examinations."

"Next: Student Lin Yunyun."

Normally the timidest girl in school, who would scream at the sight of a caterpillar, Lin Yunyun walked forward with reddened eyes.

"Student Lin Yunyun. Historically, your heart rate routinely spiked to one hundred and forty-five beats per minute when encountering insects or surprise pop quizzes; yet within the deluge of the human wall, holding up rain gear to build a shelter, your feet retreated by zero point zero millimeters."

Teacher Gao looked upon her with gentle eyes: "You are hereby awarded: The Medal of Iron Will and Courage. Data proves that your bravery index substantially exceeds your self-perception."

Weeping tears of gratitude, Lin Yunyun accepted the diploma, nodding vigorously.

One by one, every student of Class 6-1 stepped up to the stage.

With every name announced, Teacher Gao cited their most hilarious, absurd mishaps over the past year with pinpoint precision, yet without exception illuminated the shining, deeply moving courage and kindness hidden behind those cold metrics.

Waves of applause, cheerful laughter, and emotional sniffling echoed through the grand hall.

"And lastly..."

Teacher Gao raised his head, his gaze settling upon the boy sitting empty-chested yet bright-eyed in the middle of the front row:

"Student Lu Lixin."

A-Xin rose to his feet.

Drawing a deep breath under the warm, attentive eyes of the entire assembly, he climbed the stage steps one deliberate stride at a time.

Standing before Teacher Gao, their gazes met in mid-air.

One year ago, it was this mischievous boy who had scooped up that fallen brass gear beneath the podium; and it was this boy who, wearing the gear around his neck, had led the entire class through one hilarious, heart-stopping defense operation after another.

"Student Lu Lixin. Over the past three hundred and sixty-five days, you committed a cumulative total of one hundred and eighty-four school rule violations, including rigging eraser gravity traps, setting hallway marble slides, and repeatedly attempting to probe your homeroom teacher's physiological composition."

Rubbing his nose, A-Xin grinned through tears that had already soaked his cheeks.

"However..." Teacher Gao's voice slowed slightly, his vocal synthesizer chiming with an unprecedented, gentle harmonic resonance:

"Throughout an entire year, you faithfully safeguarded the number three core gear governing this teacher's common-sense computing against your chest, allowing not even zero point one micrometers of oxidation or surface scratching; in moments of crisis, your tactical decision efficiency and class cohesion index reached ninety-nine point nine percent."

Holding up the largest award of the day—a supreme honorary certificate framed in solid, gleaming brass—Teacher Gao placed it solemnly into A-Xin's hands:

"By unanimous consensus of Luyang Elementary School's Class 6-1, you are conferred the sole distinction in school history: The Special Grand Honorary Certificate for Best Brass Gear Keeper of Class 6-1."

"A-Xin, you performed exceptionally well."

This was no longer cold algorithmic data, but a teacher's heartfelt, unreserved pride in his finest student.

Unable to hold back any longer, A-Xin let out a sob as warm tears streamed down his face.

Holding the heavy certificate in both hands, he took a step back and gave a crisp, familiar hand signal toward his classmates below.

"Class, ATTENTION!" A-Xin shouted through his tears.

SWOOSH——!

All remaining twenty-five students of Class 6-1 rose as one from their seats.

Carrying an exquisite navy-blue velvet box in both hands, Qingqing stepped forward and approached Teacher Gao.

"Teacher Gao," Qingqing said, tears glistening in her eyes as a radiant smile broke across her face, "the real 【Core-Gear #03】 has already returned to your chest to beat alongside your heart; but all twenty-six of us in Class 6-1 want to give you a gear that will never, ever fade!"

Qingqing opened the velvet box.

Resting at its center was a brand-new brass gear, machined and polished on the lathe in Qingqing's garage with pocket money pooled by all twenty-six students!

Polished to a mirror-like gleam, the brass gear possessed exactly twenty-six teeth.

Meticulously carved onto the outer rim of each tooth was the name of a student: Lu Lixin, Lin Yunqing, Wu Chenkai, Wang Zhuankai, Lin Yunyun... twenty-six names in all, interlinked, seamless, and unbreakable.

And upon the central hub of the gear was engraved an affectionate, playful inscription:

【To the most incredible Teacher Gao Zhixian: You are not human, but you are our best teacher. —— Respectfully presented by the twenty-six students of Class 6-1, Luyang Elementary School】

"You all..."

Staring at the gear inscribed with their names, the gold-cyan light behind Teacher Gao's glasses flickered and danced with profound intensity.

With a gentle hum, the honeycomb cooling fan over his chest suddenly surged in speed, and a faint, warm wisp of white steam curled softly from beneath the collar of his white dress shirt.

Down beside the podium, Dean Zhou flinched, instinctively reaching to swat the smoke away, but froze midway, letting out a laugh and shaking his head with fondness.

Teacher Gao made no attempt to conceal the plume of steam signaling that his processor had hit emotional computation overload.

Extending his slender fingers, he lifted the brass gear bearing twenty-six names with infinite gentleness.

"Mass: one hundred and twenty-eight point five grams. Diameter: forty-eight millimeters. Tolerances: ergonomically flawless," Teacher Gao murmured softly.

Then, with the utmost reverence, he tucked the brass gear into his left breast pocket, positioned directly above his internal core processor.

Nestled against the superconducting chips, the metal gear settled with a crisp, reassuring click.

"Gear in position," Teacher Gao whispered, pressing a hand over his pocket before looking up at the children before him. "By my calculations, this additional payload increases left-torso mass by zero point one two eight kilograms, but simultaneously... elevates this teacher's emotional logic unit stability by one hundred percent. Mathematically speaking, this is the optimal solution."

Perched upon Teacher Gao's shoulder, Liu-Liu let out an ecstatic "Squeak!", rubbing its tiny mortarboard against his cheek.

"WAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH——!!!"

Disregarding auditorium decorum entirely, all twenty-six children cheered and swarmed the podium, wrapping Teacher Gao in a massive, tearful, laughing group hug!

Camera flashes strobed continuously, freezing this moment forever into Luyang Elementary School's historic archives.

By late afternoon, the golden sun of high summer washed the front gates of Luyang Elementary School in warm amber and crimson.

Cicadas hummed tirelessly amidst the branches of the flame-trees, and the breeze scattered bright crimson petals across the asphalt road leading toward the future.

The graduation ceremony had concluded, and a serene calm settled over the campus.

Holding their diplomas and colorful awards, the twenty-six children clustered along the wrought-iron gates in small groups, lingering to exchange farewells, laughter, and commemorative photographs.

Impeccably suited, carrying that name-engraved brass gear over his heart, Teacher Gao stood quietly beneath the flame-tree by the school gate, watching each child step out into the world.

With his backpack slung over one shoulder and clutching his brass-framed "Best Gear Keeper" certificate to his chest, A-Xin reached the gate.

He paused, turned around, and looked back at Teacher Gao standing tall and straight beneath the canopy.

The setting sun cast Teacher Gao's long shadow across the courtyard, his black frames glinting with the warm, molten gold of the twilight.

On ordinary school days, the words students dreaded most from a teacher were "See you tomorrow," for they usually portended stacks of homework, spelling quizzes, or standing in detention.

But at this moment, on this golden dusk that marked the official end of their elementary school years, those three words had become the sweetest, most precious promise in the entire universe.

Drawing in a deep breath, A-Xin cupped his hands around his mouth and shouted toward Teacher Gao with all the strength in his lungs:

"Teacher Gao——! See you tomorrow——!!"

Turning around at the school gates, the other twenty-five students halted in their tracks and shouted together through their joyful tears:

"Teacher Gao! See you tomorrow——!!"

The unified cry echoed high above the warm, spacious campus, startling a pair of white egrets into graceful flight from the treetops.

Bathed in the golden rays of the setting sun, Teacher Gao gazed at the children rushing forward into their futures.

Raising his right arm slowly and with exquisite precision, he traced a perfect ninety-degree angle against the sky, waving back to them with quiet, unwavering strength.

And across that face once so stern and mechanical, the corners of his lips softened completely, blossoming into an unprecedented, warmly human smile:

"See you tomorrow... Class 6-1."

(The End)
