# Chapter 22: Human Wall in the Pouring Rain

"CLANG——!!"

A heavy, solid cast-iron water pipe, as thick as a bowl and a meter and a half long, was slammed violently onto the anti-slip steel deck of the evacuation platform by Dean Zhou Yan, sending a spray of blinding orange-red sparks dancing into the storm!

Amidst the tempest, drenched from head to toe, with his suit torn and necktie crooked, Dean Zhou Yan stood with his chest thrown out, like a towering black monolith rising from the earth, planting himself immovably between twelve fully armed tactical engineers and twenty-six children.

That "Black-Faced Judge" visage that usually struck terror into every mischievous student back at Luyang Elementary was now grimly ashen with towering fury; his hawk-like eyes glared unflinchingly at Dr. Li, his chest heaving violently as he drew heavy, ragged breaths.

"Zhou Yan?!" Rattled by the thunderous crash, Dr. Li instinctively recoiled half a step, and upon recognizing the newcomer, his brow knotted into a tight scowl: "How did you get up here?! This area has been placed under full jurisdiction by Advanced Future Technology HQ; bystanders, withdraw immediately!"

"Bystanders?!" Zhou Yan let out a laugh of pure fury, a terrifying, savage grin curling at his lips. "Li Chongming, open your damn dog eyes and take a good look! I am the Dean of Students of Luyang Elementary School!!"

Zhou Yan tore open his shirt pocket, soaked and plastered to his chest, pulling out a dark blue credential swollen with rainwater yet still pristinely sealed in plastic, and slammed it squarely against Dr. Li's chest!

It was a Senior Teacher's License issued by the Ministry of Education!

"On this three-day, two-night graduation trip, every single strand of hair on these twenty-six students of Class 6-1, along with the safety of this model intern teacher standing behind me, is under my absolute, sole responsibility!" Brandishing the cast-iron pipe right at the tip of Dr. Li's nose through the rain, Zhou Yan's voice boomed like a bronze bell, every word falling like a crack of thunder. "Who the hell do you think you are?! Don't think for a second that just because you're wearing a black raincoat and brought a pack of lackeys in gas masks, you can barge up to a thirty-meter summit and kidnap my people?!"

Catching the license, Dr. Li's face flushed from green to pale white, and humiliated, he tossed the credential into the muddy puddles, sneering coldly: "Dean Zhou, drop the theatrical farce about teacherly sanctity! The thing you're protecting behind you isn't even human! He is our corporation's expensive bionic prototype, designation GS-X01! He has no heartbeat, no soul, and certainly no teaching certification! He is merely a piece of experimental scrap metal harboring severe logical defects, remaining here illegally!"

"Experimental scrap metal?!"

Hearing those three words, Zhou Yan's eyes flared with terrifying, bloodshot rage; stepping forward like an ignited powder keg, he swung the cast-iron pipe with a fierce whoosh past Dr. Li's ear, smashing it heavily against the adjacent steel railing and making the entire structure screech in agonizing resonance!

"Just minutes ago, on an eighty-degree vertical cliff thirty meters in the air, it was this very 'scrap metal' you sneer at that bore the deadweight of a six-point-five-ton train and dragged twenty-six young lives back from the jaws of death!"

Zhou Yan grabbed Dr. Li by the collar of his tactical raincoat, hauling him brutally before his face, rain pelting against Zhou Yan's contorted features as his furious roar nearly shattered Dr. Li's eardrums: "And five minutes ago, it was you—a 'true human' with a biological heart pumping warm blood—who detonated an EMP at the substation, nearly sending twenty-six innocent children plunging to be crushed into paste!!"

"Li Chongming! Put your hand on your conscience and tell me!" Zhou Yan shoved Dr. Li back violently, eyes blazing with towering flames of righteous indignation. "Just who is the scrap metal here?! Who is the damn cold-blooded monster devoid of humanity?!"

Zhou Yan's thunderous denunciation detonated across the platform like a bolt of lightning, plunging the entire summit into stunned silence, so fierce that even the howling tempest seemed to recoil in deference.

Staggering backward against the frigid cryogenic container, his glasses askew and rain streaming down his chin, Dr. Li turned pale as a sheet; his mouth parted, but not a single syllable of rebuttal could escape his lips.

The twelve burly tactical engineers, clutching hydraulic spreaders and hoisting cables, froze mid-stride under the terrifying, predatory aura radiating from Zhou Yan, none daring to advance another inch.

Yet behind Zhou Yan, the true, heart-stopping revelation had only just begun.

"Dean Zhou is right! Teacher Gao is the best teacher in the whole universe!"

A-Xin wiped the tears from the corner of his eyes, tightly gripping the wrists of Qingqing and Old Wu beside him as he took a resolute step forward.

"We won't let you stuff him into that black box!" Qingqing's school uniform was soaked through, mud splattered across her cheeks, but her back remained straight as a ramrod, the miniature screwdriver in her fist catching the rain with unyielding glints.

"If you want to touch Teacher Gao, you'll have to step over my dead body first!" Old Wu hollered with his raspy, booming voice, using his stout, heavy frame to plant himself at the very vanguard of the human barrier, his chest heaving against the cold gale.

Immediately, twenty-six youthful yet unwavering voices merged through the downpour like surging waves, swelling into an unstoppable tsunami!

"Don't touch Teacher Gao!!"

"We'll never let him go!!"

The timid Lin Yunyun's eyes were red and swollen, yet she clung tightly to the hem of her classmate's uniform, crying out through sobs; Wang Zhuankai's glasses had been washed away by the deluge, yet he shook his fists in defiant roar; every single one of these twenty-six notorious pranksters stood their ground, not a single one fleeing, not a single one letting go of the hands beside them!

Twenty-six pairs of muddy, small hands clasped tightly together amidst the freezing tempest, forging a seamless, unbreakable wall of flesh and blood!

Rain lashed mercilessly against their trembling eyelashes and winds tore at their thin uniforms, but the blazing light radiating from those twenty-six pairs of eyes outshone both the darkness and the storm.

With their own heartbeats and body warmth, they fiercely sheltered that charred, dormant robot teacher at the deepest, most protected sanctuary of their circle.

Dr. Li fell back onto the deck, fumbling with trembling hands into his waterproof pocket to retrieve the tactical tablet that was still actively running.

On the screen, the black-box flight telemetry recorded during the coaster's reverse-propulsion rescue was scrolling in an endless waterfall of data.

[Timestamp: 13:42:15]

[Warning: Servo torque overloaded by 280%; primary heat dissipation coils completely incinerated.]

[Error Interruption: External neural network pulses from 26 unnamed emotional sources detected; self-preservation fuse protocol forcibly terminated.]

[Terminal Override Command: Executed—At full structural loss, ensure 100% survival of Class 6-1.]

[Command Signature: Homeroom Teacher of Class 6-1, Luyang Elementary—Gao Zhixian (GS-X01).]

Staring at that forcibly overridden code signature, Dr. Li's eyelid twitched violently, his heart struck as if by a sledgehammer.

As the chief R&D director of this project, he had poured half a lifetime of effort into trying to simulate the ultimate "educational model" through algorithms, only to be constantly dismissed by the corporate board as a "cold, rigid, failed prototype devoid of human resonance."

Yet never in his wildest dreams could he have imagined that the technical barrier he had failed to bridge across decades of research would be shattered wide open here—in this legendary prankster class of Luyang Elementary, through a dropped brass gear and countless absurd literal interpretations, blasted open into a living "soul" by the pure tears and absolute trust of these children!

This was no logical malfunction.

This was a genuine miracle of "humanity" evolved by a mechanical lifeform under the companionship of twenty-six children!

"WEE-WOO——WEE-WOO——!!"

Right at that moment, down on the horizon below the theme park, countless blinding red and white revolving emergency lights pierced the sheet of rain!

Piercing sirens from police cruisers and ambulances cut through the howling gale as at least a dozen squad cars and fire rescue aerial ladders blasted through the park gates, roaring toward the base of the coaster tower!

Zhou Yan stared down icily at Dr. Li, the cast-iron pipe tapping lightly against his palm: "Did you hear that, Dr. Li? Police, firefighters, and the Luyang Elementary Parent Association—they're all here. If you want to publicly abduct the hero teacher who just saved twenty-six children right in front of every television camera in Taiwan, I'm more than happy to entertain you to the bitter end!"

Ashen-faced, Dr. Li looked upon the unbroken wall of children linking arms against the storm at the platform's edge, then down at the telemetry log chronicling this "miracle."

The icy arrogance and scientific fanaticism in his eyes finally splintered in that instant, giving way to a complex expression of bitterness, release, and profound awe.

Drawing a deep, shuddering breath, Dr. Li slowly rose to his feet in the deluge.

He issued no order to apprehend; instead, he raised a hand toward the twelve tactical engineers behind him, flashing the tactical signal to stand down and abort.

"Tactical squad, stow the containment container." Dr. Li's voice was hoarse, yet laden with unprecedented calm.

"Doctor?! But the Omega Protocol..." the squad leader pressed anxiously.

"I said, abort the operation!" Dr. Li barked sharply, then turned to walk toward Lin Yunqing at the edge of the platform.

A-Xin and Old Wu tensed and stepped forward warily, but Zhou Yan reached out to hold them steady.

Rummaging through his tactical tool kit for a few seconds, Dr. Li retrieved a micro-calibration wrench forged entirely of titanium and etched with precision markings, alongside an encrypted high-grade engineering debug flash drive.

Bending down, he placed both items gently beside Qingqing's tool pouch on the deck.

"Little girl, your mechanical intuition is spot on," Dr. Li murmured, rain coursing down his spectacles. "The thermal expansion jammed his #3 planetary bearing, causing severe micro-arcing in the primary stator. If you apply power directly or force the gear into place, the residual capacitance will flash-format his core memory into a blank slate."

Qingqing's pupils shrank, her head snapping up to look at Dr. Li.

"This flash drive contains GS-X01's complete underlying schematics and bearing tolerance tables; that micro-wrench will allow you to pry open the jammed coils without severing the fiber optics." Dr. Li closed his eyes, his voice as soft as a whisper. "However, you must complete the reboot within twelve minutes. In twelve minutes, the residual micro-voltage in the backup capacitors will decay to absolute zero, and after that... not even God will be able to recover his memories."

"And... the most critical component of all..." Dr. Li pointed downward toward the pitch-black, gale-lashed maintenance catwalk beneath the track, "when he hoisted the train over the ridge earlier, the anti-vibration locking nut securing the primary spindle was dislodged by high-pressure exhaust. That is a reverse-threaded locknut made of specialized aerospace alloy; nowhere in the world is there a standard nut that can replace it. Without it securing the spindle, the gear cannot mesh even if you seat it."

With that, Dr. Li turned around, pulling up the hood of his raincoat to shield his face from view.

"Li Chongming, what are you playing at?" Zhou Yan asked in a deep voice.

Pausing at the spiral stairwell, Dr. Li did not look back, merely letting out a self-deprecating chuckle into the rain: "Dean Zhou, when I return, I will report to the board that GS-X01 suffered a catastrophic core breach during the roller coaster accident, and its wreckage plummeted into the deep sea, unrecoverable."

"The remaining twelve minutes... belong to your Class 6-1."

With the fading footsteps of tactical combat boots descending the stairs, Dr. Li and his twelve operatives swiftly vanished into the curtain of rain below.

Upon the platform, the tempest raged on unabated.

"Qingqing! Twelve minutes!" A-Xin spun around, an unprecedented frenzy of urgency blazing in his eyes. "We only have twelve minutes to save Teacher Gao!"

"B-But... the anti-vibration locknut fell onto the tracks below!" Qingqing's face went deathly pale. "This steel tower is thirty meters high, engulfed in wind and rain—how could we ever find a thumbnail-sized nut among thousands of steel girders?!"

Just as everyone stared into the abyss in utter despair, the silver-gray ball of fur perched upon Teacher Gao's scorched shoulder suddenly erupted with an ecstatic, high-pitched chirp!

"SQUEAK-SQUEAK——!!"

Liu-Liu's tiny nose twitched frantically against the air, and before anyone could blink, it transformed into a streak of silver lightning, diving headfirst into the raging, pitch-black chasm of the storm!
