# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 1: The New Savior of Class 6-1

Along the hallways of Luyang Elementary School, terrifying legends about "Class 6-1" were constantly passed down.

Rumor had it that just last semester, this class drove their third trainee teacher to the brink of insanity—that poor Teacher Li, after grading a hundred consecutive essays titled "My Dream Is to Invent a Pencil That Writes Homework by Itself," handed in his resignation with hollow eyes and fled to a secluded mountain to cultivate Taoist immortality.

And today was the first day of the new semester.

Morning sunlight streamed through the slightly chalk-dusted windows of Class 6-1. In the back of the classroom, the class's most mischievous "King of Ideas," Lu Lixin (nicknamed A-Xin), was crouched stealthily behind the podium.

"A-Xin, are you sure this will actually work?"

The speaker was Wu Chenkai. Everyone usually called him "Old Wu." Old Wu's nickname sounded mysterious... well, truth be told, he looked nothing like a sorcerer; rather, he was plump, fair, and good-natured. He perpetually held half a freshly steamed pork bun in his hand, chewing as he stared nervously at the classroom door. "The last teacher ran away after being spooked by your 'Jumping Candy Waterweed Spider in the Desk.' This time, Black-Faced Director from the Student Affairs Office will definitely keep a close eye on us."

"Relax! This time it's a high-tech upgraded edition!" A-Xin proudly displayed the blackboard eraser in his hand.

That was no ordinary blackboard eraser. A-Xin had fastened an ultra-fine fishing line to the top of it. As soon as the new teacher pushed open the classroom door, the box of chalk dust piled high above the podium would descend like a "blizzard blizzard," slamming squarely and accurately onto their forehead.

Sitting in the front row, Class President Lin Yunqing adjusted her glasses, holding a miniature screwdriver in her hand, and spoke coldly:
"From a physics perspective, the coefficient of friction on your fishing line is too large. If the door opens with an acceleration of less than 0.5 meters per second, the chalk dust will only drop right at the center of the podium—which happens to be the exact spot where you'll be standing in detention later."

"Lin Yunqing, stop pouring cold water on me!" A-Xin protested.

Right at that moment, an agile silver-white figure flashed across the vent with a whoosh!

It was the AI smart pet secretly co-funded and raised by the entire class—"Liu-Liu."

Liu-Liu looked like a fluffy white mouse yet possessed a pair of sharp, alert ears like a little fox, trailing a bushy tail with a metallic sheen behind it. Moving as fast as lightning, it was the ultimate gossip king regarding all school rumors, confidential intel, and the patrolling patrol routes of the Dean of Students!

Liu-Liu dangled gracefully upside-down from the ceiling wire conduit, its obsidian-like electronic eyes blinking furiously with red light, as its tiny mouth issued a rapid electronic warning:

"Beep-beep! Alert! Alert! New target spotted at the corridor corner! Distance to back door: thirty meters... twenty meters... ten meters! Aura intensity: five stars! Repeat, five stars!"

Having completed its broadcast, Liu-Liu vanished like a white afterimage with a swish, darting straight into its dedicated little charging nook behind the blackboard.

The classroom instantly grew so quiet that one could hear a pin drop on the floor. Twenty-six pairs of eyes stared in unison at the ajar wooden classroom door.

"Creak—"

The door was pushed open.

The fishing line snapped taut in an instant! Perched atop the podium, the box of chalk dust—accumulated over an entire summer vacation, thick and dense—plunged straight toward the doorway with a howling gust of wind!

A-Xin nearly jumped for joy, about to yell "Bingo!"—

However, what happened in the very next second caused the air in Class 6-1 to freeze completely.

The man who walked in did not scream, nor did he frantically clutch his head.

In fact, he didn't even knit his brows.

He wore a tailored dark gray suit with a red-and-blue diagonal striped tie, as pristine as if it had just been peeled from dry-cleaner plastic wrap. A stylish pair of black-framed glasses rested on his nose, and his hair was combed meticulously, every strand angled as though precisely calibrated by a protractor.

At the hairsbreadth moment when the box of chalk dust was a mere five centimeters above his head—

"Swish!"

The man's right hand rose upward at an impossible, blur-like speed. His five fingers carved a smooth arc in midair, and the chalk box spun elegantly twice in the air without spilling a single speck of dust, coming to rest steadily in the palm of his hand!

"Initial free-fall velocity: 2.4 meters per second. Target mass: 180 grams. Composition: 92 percent calcium carbonate powder; aerodynamic drag coefficient: 0.47."

The man's voice was steady and crisp, imbued with a peculiar metallic resonance, sounding both like a professional radio announcer and the voice assistant of the latest flagship smartphone:

"Good morning, students of Class 6-1. I am your new homeroom teacher for this semester—Gao Zhixian."

The entire class dropped their jaws, their chins smacking against their desks in unison.

The pork bun in Old Wu's mouth fell onto his desk with a plop, and he completely forgot to pick it up.

Teacher Gao Zhixian strode onto the podium with strides of perfectly identical rhythm. Each step measured exactly seventy-five centimeters, the sound of his soles striking the floor as rhythmic as a metronome: "Tap, tap, tap, tap."

He gently placed the chalk box on the corner of the desk and pushed up his black-framed glasses.

At the very instant he pushed his glasses, Lin Yunqing, sitting in the front row, keenly caught sight of it: deep within Teacher Gao's pupils behind the lenses, a faint, almost imperceptible pale blue ring contracted and expanded slightly, just like a camera lens focusing.

"According to this school's student database," Teacher Gao's gaze swept over the entire room, "the expected attendance for this class is twenty-six students, with twenty-five present. The absentee is Student Number 14, Li Xiaoming—currently located on the second floor of Teaching Building Two in the restroom. Due to consuming expired winter melon lemon tea on his way to school, he is projected to arrive in three minutes and forty-two seconds."

"Huh? Seriously? He even knows about the winter melon lemon tea?!" A-Xin couldn't help muttering.

Teacher Gao's gaze locked onto A-Xin in the back row instantly.

"Student Lu Lixin: height 142 centimeters, weight 34.5 kilograms. Put on his left sock inside-out before leaving home this morning. Carrying a packet of Pop Rocks, two yo-yos in his backpack side pocket, and three unfinished math test sheets tucked inside his workbook."

A-Xin almost bounced right out of his seat. He instinctively felt his backpack's side pocket—not an error in sight! Even the fact that he secretly shoved his math test papers into the very bottom this morning had been seen through!

Behind the blackboard, Liu-Liu poked its pointy fox ears through the crack, its electronic eyes frantically calculating Teacher Gao's threat level.

"Finally," Teacher Gao turned toward the dazed Old Wu, "Student Wu Chenkai, the pork bun on your desk has fifty-seven seconds remaining in its optimal tasting window. Its internal temperature has dropped to forty-two degrees Celsius. If not consumed immediately, the fats will coagulate, reducing nutrient absorption by thirteen percent."

Old Wu reflexively shoved the bun into his mouth and nodded vigorously: "Y-Yes, Teacher! I'll eat it right away!"

This was no teacher.

This was definitely no ordinary trainee teacher!

The same thought flashed simultaneously through the minds of all the rascals in Class 6-1—they had encountered an ultimate final boss. A supreme final boss capable of computing gravitational acceleration, seeing through backpacks, and even monitoring intestinal digestion with pinpoint precision!

The morning sun bathed Teacher Gao Zhixian's flawless, handsome face. The corners of his mouth curved upward slightly, forming a textbook forty-five-degree smile:

"Now, please turn to page one of your language textbook. Our sixth-grade semester begins now."
