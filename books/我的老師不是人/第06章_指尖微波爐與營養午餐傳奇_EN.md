# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 6: Fingertip Microwave & School Lunch Legends

At Luyang Elementary School, at 11:50 every morning, a subtle chemical change took place in the air across the entire campus.

That was the aroma drifting from the basement kitchen of Teaching Building One—infused with the savory glaze of braised pork ribs, the sweetness of sweet corn chowder, and the steaming warmth of colossal steel vats of freshly cooked white rice.

And for the twenty-six students of Class 6-1, this was the most sacred, and most cutthroat, battlefield of the day.

"Chime—chime—chime—chime—"

At the very first note of the fourth-period dismissal bell, Athletic Representative Old Wu (Wu Chenkai) had already launched from his seat like an arrow released from a taut bowstring. With an agility that seemed utterly incongruous with his round, portly frame, he snatched up two gleaming metal food-serving tongs, his gaze as razor-sharp as a stalking jaguar.

"Today's main entree is Crispy Golden Giant Fried Chicken Drumsticks!" Old Wu wiped away a suspicious trace of drool from the corner of his mouth, turning to yell to A-Xin, "According to the kitchen intel Liu-Liu and I just intercepted, the quota of drumsticks allocated to each class today is strictly rationed! We cannot let duty students from other classes swap our insulated food carriers!"

Perched in the ceiling vent, Liu-Liu immediately gave two spirited "squeak-squeaks," its twin fox ears perking up alertly as its electronic eyes projected a three-dimensional green hologram of a drumstick in midair, labeled "Target Calories: 380 kcal."

On the podium, Teacher Gao Zhixian was snapping shut the class's language arts workbooks at a blazing speed of thirty pages per second.

"School Regulation 32, posted on the blackboard: Running and shoving in the hallways during lunchtime is strictly prohibited."

Teacher Gao rose with poise, gently adjusting his black-framed glasses. His suit was as pristine as ever; after the morning's massive exhaust venting, his head now maintained only a mild, soothing warmth: "As the homeroom teacher of Class 6-1, I shall personally accompany the duty students to the kitchen to inspect and receive the school lunch, ensuring that the caloric, protein, and trace mineral intake of our twenty-six students satisfies the optimal growth curve."

"Whoa! Teacher is personally coming with us to serve food?!" The entire class broke into cheers.

Five minutes later, four massive stainless steel thermal insulated vats were neatly lugged and arranged in front of Class 6-1's podium.

However, as Old Wu pried open the first large vat, the broad smile across his face collapsed in an instant.

"Waaah... how did this happen..." Old Wu stared down at an ice-cold bun wrapped in aluminum foil in his hands, weeping without tears. "I left home in such a rush this morning that I grabbed the extra-large mushroom scallion black pork bun grandma steamed yesterday and chilled in the fridge, planning to enjoy it with the chicken drumsticks at noon... now it's as hard as an iron shot put!"

Qingqing strolled over to take a look, rapping her knuckles against the rock-hard bun, producing dull, heavy "thud-thud" noises: "Surface hardness: 3.5 on the Mohs scale. Core temperature: eight degrees Celsius. Old Wu, if you bite straight into that, I guarantee your front teeth will snap."

Sitting nearby, Wang Zhuankai instinctively covered his front teeth, shivering involuntarily.

Cradling his freezing pork bun, Old Wu looked tearfully at the vat of piping-hot corn chowder, sinking into profound despair.

Right at that moment, a tall shadow fell across Old Wu's desk.

Unbeknownst to anyone, Teacher Gao had soundlessly appeared beside him. A gentle pale-blue scanning wave glinted across his black-framed glasses:

`[Target: Mushroom Scallion Black Pork Bun]`
`[Mass: 220g; Moisture: 42%]`
`[Status: Starch retrogradation, fat solidification; culinary satisfaction index degraded to 12%]`

"Significant student food temperature anxiety detected."

Teacher Gao extended his right index and middle fingers with serene grace, a posture as poised as an ethereal martial arts master from a kung fu epic. Fingers curling slightly, he tapped the tip of his fingers against the frozen, rock-solid bun cradled in Old Wu's trembling hands.

"Teacher? What are you doing?" Old Wu was baffled.

"High-frequency electromagnetic molecular resonance heating protocol engaged. Power output: 700 watts. Three-second countdown."

Before his words even finished, the tips of Teacher Gao's fair, slender fingers began emitting an ultra-faint "hummm—" buzz at a frequency nearly imperceptible to the naked eye!

Then, an unbelievable miracle took place—

The chilly bun, previously hard as granite, began expanding and softening at a speed visible to the human eye! A tantalizing puff of pure white steam hissed out with a "pssst" from the bun's pleated crust, releasing an explosion of rich, savory mushroom and black pork aroma that had the entire row of seated students drooling uncontrollably!

"Ding!"

Teacher Gao drew his fingers back elegantly, his suit cuffs falling into neat, sharp lines: "Heating procedure complete. Internal core temperature: sixty-five degrees Celsius; crust fluffiness restored by ninety-eight percent. Please consume while warm."

"A... a celestial deity!!"

Holding the piping-hot, fluffy, aromatic bun in both hands, Old Wu was so moved he nearly dropped to his knees right there: "Teacher Gao! Your fingers are a walking 7-Eleven microwave!"

"That's not a microwave," A-Xin lunged forward to clamp his hand over Old Wu's mouth, shouting loudly to several dumbstruck students from the neighboring class standing outside the door, "That's Shaolin martial arts! Teacher Gao practices the Pure Yang Boy Skill... wait, no, the One Finger Sun Meridian Qigong!"

"Qigong?!" The passing students' eyes widened like brass bells, and they spun around, sprinting down the hallway in a panic: "Heavens! The new teacher of Class 6-1 knows Shaolin One Finger Qigong! He poked a cold bun with one finger and it instantly cooked!!"

Within three minutes, legendary rumors of "Class 6-1's teacher possessing peerless internal martial arts" spread like wildfire throughout the school hallways.

However, the real chaos had only just begun to unfold.

At 12:30 in the afternoon, lunch concluded, and the entire campus transitioned into routine campus cleaning time.

The area assigned to Class 6-1 happened to be the fifty-meter-long marble corridor on the second floor of Teaching Building Two.

"Students," Teacher Gao stood in the dead center of the corridor holding a cleaning rag, his gaze deadpan and solemn, "according to the school sanitation assessment metrics, residual surface dust directly impacts our overall class honor score. To achieve one hundred percent sterile dust eradication, surfactant agents and lubricants must be added to the mopping water to reduce ground friction to zero, maximizing cleaning displacement velocity."

Washing her mop nearby, Qingqing froze completely upon hearing this: "Wait, Teacher Gao! What on earth do you mean by 'reducing ground friction to zero'?!"

Too late.

Having lost his common sense damper, Teacher Gao had no grasp whatsoever of the human concept of "moderate cleaning detergent."

With one hand, Teacher Gao hoisted a giant ten-liter jug of concentrated heavy-duty dish soap left by the handyman Old Lin at the corner of the corridor. Without even blinking, he glug-glug-glugged the entire jug into three large mop buckets brimming with water!

"Formulation ratio complete. Initiating surface spray."

Teacher Gao swung the buckets with his long arms, launching the soapy water in an arc so perfect it looked plotted by a robotic arm, splashing all three buckets of ultra-concentrated sudsy water—whoosh!—evenly across the fifty-meter polished marble hallway!

"Sizzle-fizz-fizz-fizz——!!"

The moment the concentrated soap met the marble floor, a sea of snow-white, dense bubbles bloomed across the corridor, sparkling like a rainbow prism under the midday sun—so slick that even a housefly landing on it would break its legs!

"Waaah! I'm slipping!"

Old Wu took a single step onto the floor with his mop and instantly lost all balance, turning into a speeding bowling ball as he whooshed down the sudsy hallway at thirty kilometers per hour!

"Hold on, Old Wu!" A-Xin yelled, instinctively reaching out to grab him, only for his feet to slip out from under him, launching him flying into the foam with a slick "whizz!"

Yet, the moment he went flying, A-Xin felt no pain whatsoever.

The dense cushion of bubbles was so thick it felt like an air mattress; gliding across it was just like riding a high-speed flume slide at a water park!

"Holy cow! This is ridiculously fun!" A-Xin threw his arms wide open in pure ecstasy, carving graceful curves across the hallway, drifting smoothly around corners!

Witnessing this spectacle, the latent rascal genes within the rest of the class ignited into a full-blown blaze!

"Charge!! Class 6-1 Water Park is officially open!"

Wang Zhuankai kicked off his sneakers, sliding into the foam sea in his rubber slippers with uproarious laughter;
Lin Yunyun grabbed a plastic washbasin, treating it like a bumper car as she spun merrily in the center of the hall;
Perched on the vent, Liu-Liu became so thrilled that it leaped straight down, all four paws balancing atop a felt blackboard eraser, bushy tail arched high, cruising nimbly through the frothy waves like an extreme professional surfing mouse!

Twenty-six elementary schoolers plus one AI pet turned a mundane campus cleaning duty into the most raucous indoor bubble surfing rave the school had ever witnessed!

Just as everyone was partying in absolute delirium, the entire building echoing with joyful shrieks at the fever pitch of the chaos—

A heavy, familiar, and breath-stealing cadence of leather shoes sounded abruptly from the stairwell!

"Tap, tap, tap, tap."

Every single step struck a chord of sheer panic in everyone's hearts!

Liu-Liu's pointy ears twitched violently. Its electronic eyes flared crimson instantly as it stood atop the eraser, issuing an urgent broadcast:

"Beep-beep! Black-Faced Judge! Dean of Students Zhou Yan is coming upstairs! Estimated arrival at the second-floor landing in three seconds! Repeat, three seconds!"

"We're doomed! If the Dean catches us turning the hallway into a slip-and-slide, the whole class will be put on probation!" Qingqing shrieked.

At that moment, Teacher Gao stood at the very head of the sudsy raceway, surveying the twenty-six foam-covered students before him, his black-framed glasses computing frantically:

`[Hazard Level: Extreme]`
`[Violation: School Rule 18 (Campus Horsing Around) + Rule 50 (Waste of Public Property)]`
`[Solution: Engage Supersonic Synchronized Mopping Module]`

"All students, brace yourselves against solid fixtures."

Teacher Gao suddenly barked in a low command.

Grasping two thick wooden-handled heavy mops with both hands, the anti-skid bionic micro-suction cups on his shoe soles engaged at maximum power!

"SWOOSH————!!"

Teacher Gao transformed into a supersonic snowplow running at full throttle, blurring into a streak of motion down the corridor! Sweeping in reverse at an incredible velocity of ten meters per second, the two mops spun like twin whirlwinds in his hands. Everywhere he passed, all the dish soap suds, puddles, and stains were swept up and sucked dry within 0.5 seconds by his powerful air currents and mops!

When Dean Zhou Yan stepped onto the second-floor hallway with a stormy countenance—

Across the entire fifty-meter marble corridor, not a single soap bubble remained.

The floor was so spotlessly clean it bordered on criminal; the polished marble mirrored Dean Zhou's dark, scowling face with immaculate clarity, reflecting a dazzling gleam of light.

Along both sides of the corridor, the twenty-six students of Class 6-1 stood in two neat, rigid ranks, each clutching a cleaning rag, backs straight as arrows, drenched in sweat yet radiating absolute determination.

In the dead center of the hallway, Teacher Gao stood tall with mop in hand, suit immaculate, his black-framed glasses glinting with serene cool against the blinding reflection of the floor:

"Reporting, Dean Zhou. Class 6-1's campus cleaning progress is at one hundred percent. Floor sanitation rating: sterile mirror polish. You are cordially invited to inspect."

Dean Zhou stood utterly stunned at the entrance of the hallway.

He looked down at the marble floor beneath his feet, clean enough to perform open-heart surgery upon, then looked at the panting, seemingly "model and diligent" students and their flawless homeroom teacher.

He could even see the reflection of the mole on the bridge of his own nose on the gleaming tiles.

"This... did the most rambunctious Class 6-1 in the entire school really scrub this?" Dean Zhou muttered in disbelief.

He had initially rushed upstairs after hearing screams, intending to catch violators red-handed.

Yet now... this hallway was so pristine he felt almost guilty sullying it with his leather shoes.

Dean Zhou held his breath for a long while before finally coughing with an immensely conflicted expression:

"Ahem... Teacher Gao, outstanding work. In my twenty years of teaching at Luyang Elementary, this is the cleanest corridor I have ever seen! For sanitation inspection... your class gets a perfect score!"

"THANK YOU, DEAN——!!" All twenty-six students belted out at the top of their lungs, unleashing a cheer that shook the ceiling.

Dean Zhou nodded in satisfaction, turning to tip-toe down the stairs carefully, as if petrified of leaving a smudge on the immaculate floor.

Only after Dean Zhou's figure completely vanished did A-Xin turn to look at Teacher Gao beside him.

Down at the cuff of Teacher Gao's suit trousers, a tiny soap bubble quietly surfaced, popping into the air with a soft "snap."

Teacher Gao looked down at A-Xin, the deep blue glow behind his lenses blinking gently:

"Inspection passed. Today's lunch and campus cleaning missions... successfully concluded."
