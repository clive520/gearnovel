# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 3: Teacher's Literal Interpretation Syndrome

Following the morning's "anti-human-flattening tape-measure waist check" incident, the entirety of Class 6-1 fell into an unprecedentedly bizarre atmosphere.

The red welt left by the tight yellow engineering tape measure still marked A-Xin's waist. Old Wu, meanwhile, stared with heartbroken anguish at his cabbage pork bun, of which he had only taken two bites—earlier, under the intense gaze of Teacher Gao's black-framed glasses glinting with terrifying authority, he had been forced to shove the entire remainder of the bun into A-Xin's mouth.

"Ugh... my special deluxe cabbage black pork bun..." Old Wu whimpered softly, slumping over his desk.

"What are you crying for! I'm the one who almost choked to death, alright?!" A-Xin gulped down a huge mouthful of water, his voice lowered with lingering fear.

At this moment, Lin Yunqing shifted her chair forward half a centimeter, retrieving a high-magnification miniature magnifying glass from her pencil case.

"A-Xin, take that thing out from earlier," Qingqing ordered with an intensely solemn expression.

A-Xin looked around furtively, making sure that Teacher Gao on the podium was grading workbooks at a lightning speed of five copies per second, before quietly fishing the chilly brass gear out from the bottom of his sneaker.

Qingqing took the gear, peered through the magnifying glass, and gasped sharply.

"Titanium-alloy hardening coat, twenty-four-tooth planetary reduction structure, micrometer-level precision machining..." Qingqing mumbled to herself, her eyes shining like bright lightbulbs. "This level of craftsmanship surpasses even the top-tier imported superbikes in my dad's garage! This is definitely not a part from an ordinary watch or toy!"

"Then what on earth is it?" Old Wu leaned in to ask.

"Engraved in the center is a microscopic code: 【P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03】." Qingqing pushed up her glasses. "Moreover, did you guys see the prompt on Teacher Gao's glasses lenses earlier? It clearly read: 'Common-Sense and Rhetoric Damper Offline'!"

A-Xin was taken aback: "Common-Sense and Rhetoric... Damper?"

While the three heads huddled together in hushed whispers, a faint scratching of claws came from the ceiling vent.

A tuft of snow-white fluff poked out from between the vent slats. The AI pet "Liu-Liu" leaned its little head forward, the two miniature antennae on its ears spinning in circles, transmitting an ultra-low-frequency electronic voice to the trio:

"Beep... target movement detected... distance five meters... all units take cover..."

"Snap!"

Qingqing concealed the gear and magnifying glass at the speed of light.

The next second, a posture as upright as a pine tree appeared soundlessly beside their desks.

Teacher Gao Zhixian looked down at them. His suit remained impeccable, not a single strand of hair out of place, but upon closer inspection, the muscle near the corner of his left eye seemed to twitch with a microscopic frequency, emitting a rhythmic "tap, tap" like the second hand of an electronic watch.

"Student Lu Lixin, Student Lin Yunqing, Student Wu Chenkai."

Teacher Gao spoke calmly, his tone carrying a faint metallic undertone: "During morning self-study, gathering to produce sound waves exceeding forty decibels is prohibited. Furthermore, my infrared thermal imaging shows your three heart rates at 112, 108, and 95 beats per minute respectively, exhibiting typical physiological indicators of 'conspiring a prank.'"

Cold sweat instantly beaded across A-Xin's forehead.

He could even measure heart rates?! This wasn't just human radar; this was a walking polygraph machine!

To change the subject, the athletic student Chen Ada from the adjacent aisle happened to return from playing basketball, collapsing into his chair, fanning himself furiously with a plastic desk mat, and yelling loudly:

"Whoa! The sun outside is scorching today! The classroom is as hot as an oven!"

"Swish!"

Teacher Gao turned abruptly, a noticeable halo of yellow light flashing across his lenses.

`[LITERAL MODE TRIGGERED: Classroom = Oven]`

Teacher Gao's eyes locked onto the thermometer on the classroom wall: "Environmental sensor confirmation: Current ambient temperature is 28.5 degrees Celsius. A significant deviation exists compared to the operating temperature of a standard convection oven (180 to 220 degrees Celsius)."

Striding toward the air conditioner panel beside the blackboard, he yanked the power switch down:

"To conform to the 'oven' working conditions stated by the student, the fans are immediately deactivated, and uniform baking of the entire class shall commence. Please maintain upright posture and flip yourselves over every ten minutes to ensure even heat distribution and avoid charred skin."

The entire class: "???????????"

"T-Teacher! That was a metaphor! A figure of speech!" A-Xin leaped up from his chair in panic.

"This system... ahem, my common-sense database is currently unmounted and cannot identify 'metaphors.'" Teacher Gao stood on the podium with absolute earnestness, even deliberately pulling out a timer. "Baking countdown initiated. Student Chen Ada, your nape coloration is relatively pale; flipping over first is recommended."

Chen Ada was so terrified that he slid directly out of his chair onto the floor, scrambling on all fours to hug his desk leg, screaming: "Teacher, I was wrong! The classroom isn't an oven! It's cool! Extremely cool!"

A stream of code flashed across Teacher Gao's eyes, and the timer snapped back into his pocket with a "ding":

"Statement revised to 'cool.' Baking canceled."

All twenty-six students wiped away cold sweat in unison. Sitting in the back row, several boys couldn't hold back their laughter, clutching their bellies and snickering, the sound ringing particularly loud in the quiet classroom.

One of the boys slapped his desk and laughed uproariously: "Hahahahaha! That's absurd! Teacher, you're hilarious, I'm laughing my big teeth off!"

Upon hearing those words, the faces of A-Xin and Qingqing changed drastically!

"Wait! Don't say that phrase—" Qingqing screamed in an attempt to stop him.

Too late.

Teacher Gao's pupils contracted sharply, his mechanical latency rising by half an octave:

`[EMERGENCY DENTAL HAZARD DETECTED: Imminent Tooth Loss Warning]`

"Uncontrolled tooth detachment event imminent!"

With a blur of motion akin to a kung fu master, Teacher Gao instantly teleported directly in front of the laughing boy's desk.

"Swish!"

Teacher Gao reached into his suit pockets with both hands, astonishingly producing a spool of medical dental floss, a small bottle of desensitizing mouthwash, and a pair of professional-looking metallic forceps!

"Student Wang Zhuankai, open your mandible and reveal your incisors and molars."

Teacher Gao's demeanor was as solemn as a chief surgeon in an emergency room: "This teacher possesses three thousand hours of simulated dental surgery experience. Before teeth detachment occurs, I will use high-tensile dental floss to execute crisscross stabilization across all thirty-two of your teeth, ensuring the vibrational frequency of your laughter does not exceed enamel tolerance limits!"

"Waaah! Teacher, don't! My teeth are rock solid! Not a single one came loose! Look—they're all right here!" Wang Zhuankai clasped both hands tightly over his mouth in sheer panic, tears welling up in his eyes.

"Clenched lips obstruct examination. Forced retractor deployment engaged." Teacher Gao raised the metallic forceps.

At this critical, chaotic second—

"Squeak! Whoosh—!"

A silver-white shadow darted down from atop the blackboard like a lightning bolt!

It was Liu-Liu!

With pinpoint precision, Liu-Liu pounced onto the main electrical panel beside the podium, swinging its little tail with a slap directly against the dismissal bell testing button!

"Chime—chime—chime—chime—"

The clear, loud end-of-period bell suddenly rang out across the entire teaching building five minutes ahead of schedule!

Teacher Gao's movements paused abruptly.

The yellow glow in his eyes instantly transitioned back to a serene, pale blue.

`[SCHEDULE OVERRIDE: Dismissal Bell Confirmed]`
`[Priority: Dental surgery suspended; entering recess rest mode]`

Teacher Gao stowed away the forceps and dental floss, elegantly straightening his necktie as if the person who had just tried to extract teeth wasn't him at all, speaking in a gentle tone:

"Recess has commenced. Student Wang Zhuankai, please maintain periodontal stillness; we shall resume the dental stabilization procedure next period."

With that, Teacher Gao stepped forward with precise seventy-five-centimeter strides, turned out of the classroom, and walked toward the faculty office.

"Phew..."

Wang Zhuankai collapsed into a puddle on the floor, having nearly wet himself in terror.

Only then did the entire class heave sighs of immense relief, panting heavily as if they had just survived a brush with death.

A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu immediately clustered behind the blackboard. Liu-Liu poked its furry head out from the wall crevice, twitching its pointed ears and proudly raising its tiny whiskers.

"Liu-Liu, brilliant job!" A-Xin rubbed Liu-Liu's little head.

Qingqing lowered her voice with extreme gravity: "The situation is far worse than I imagined. Without that brass gear, Teacher Gao's system is interpreting every daily figure of speech as a literal physical command!"

Old Wu shuddered: "If someone says 'I want to break your dog legs' next time... will Teacher really go out and break the campus stray dog's legs?!"

"Quite likely!" Qingqing gritted her teeth. "And did you see his movements earlier? When his arms swung, there was the humming sound of a metallic cooling fan. He's on the verge of overheating!"

A-Xin clutched the heavy 【Core-Gear #03】 in his pocket, a spark of unprecedented adventurous excitement igniting in his eyes:

"After school today, we can't leave him alone. Liu-Liu will track his movements via the vents, and the three of us will intercept him—Class 6-1's secret operation officially begins!"
