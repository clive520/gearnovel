# Chapter 13: The Unbalanced Secondary Bearing

The after-school sunset dyed the corridors of Luyang Elementary School in a rich, maple-syrup gold.

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, all twenty-six students who should have slung their backpacks over their shoulders and headed home remained—not a single one had departed.

Doors and windows were shut tight, curtains drawn completely across the glass, admitting only a few mottled, dim slivers of lingering twilight.

At the edge of the blackboard, the projector screen still glowed faintly, its blood-red countdown ticking down with callous indifference: [058:31:45].

Less than fifty-nine hours remained before corporate headquarters would trigger the high-frequency electromagnetic pulse (EMP) for forced incineration.

And beneath the countdown clock, on the 3D cutaway schematic of Teacher Gao's internal thoracic drive unit, that titanium-alloy secondary bearing measuring a mere four millimeters across was shifting from dark crimson into a blinding, incandescent orange.

"The wear rate has reached eighty-six point seven percent," Qingqing said, clutching her mini-tablet, her eyebrows knotted into a tight knot.

"Why did it deteriorate so drastically all of a sudden?" A-Xin leaned in close, staring at the micron-scale hairline fissures pulsing across the display.

"Because of this afternoon's guerrilla skirmish," Qingqing adjusted her spectacles, pointing at the telemetry from Teacher Gao's robotic limb. "To pin down Dr. Li's shorting gun bare-handed and discharge that reverse microwave burst, Teacher Gao instantaneously drew over three hundred percent of his rated torque and energy."

"With Core Gear Number Seven already missing, all that eccentric torque was shouldered entirely by this four-millimeter secondary bearing," Qingqing's voice trembled slightly. "The diamond-like carbon coating on the ball bearings has peeled away in massive swathes."

"Does that mean..." Old Wu gripped a lollipop between his teeth, so anxious he forgot to chew, "Teacher's backup bearing is about to blow up?"

"Not explode—physically seize," Qingqing magnified the 3D model to micron resolution. "Once the bearing balls deform from thermal expansion and the internal micron tolerance drops to zero, Teacher Gao's primary drive axle will lock up solid. When that happens, he won't be able to move a single finger, and catastrophic heat buildup could detonate his central logic processor."

"How long can he last?" A-Xin asked in a lowered whisper, his fists tightening involuntarily.

"Based on the current thermal degradation curves, twelve hours at the absolute most," Qingqing inhaled deeply, her tone heavier than ever before. "If we cannot obtain a replacement four-millimeter high-precision bearing before midnight tonight, by tomorrow's morning self-study... Teacher Gao will never step onto the podium again."

The air in the classroom seemed to freeze solid into ice in that very instant.

"Then what are we waiting for?" Old Wu swiped a bead of cold sweat from his forehead. "Let's storm the Natural Science Lab right now and rip that centrifuge apart!"

"We can't. It's six in the evening; the custodians and duty teachers just finished their bento boxes and are making rounds across the campus," Lin Yunyun shook her head hurriedly. "Plus, the Craft Workshop has a dual interlock deadbolt; without Qingqing's dedicated bypass decoder, it's impossible to crack."

Before she finished speaking, a crisp "click" echoed from the rear door handle.

"Someone's coming!" Wang Zhuankai gasped.

The hearts of all twenty-six classmates leaped into their throats simultaneously; with nimble acrobatics, Lin Yunyun yanked the projector cord, snapping the screen back into the ceiling with a whoosh.

In less than two seconds, all twenty-six students slipped back into their respective desks, grabbing textbooks and pretending to study with frantic diligence.

The back door swung slowly inward, revealing Teacher Gao stepping inside, cradling a stack of newly graded Chinese exercise workbooks.

Yet the very instant he took his third stride, the students' eyes nearly popped from their sockets!

Teacher Gao's right leg hitched in mid-air for a bizarre tenth of a second; his forward stride miraculously reversed with millisecond precision, stepping backward by an exact seventy-five centimeters!

Next, his left leg slid in reverse, his black oxfords skimming over the terrazzo tiles with a buttery-smooth "sh-sh" hiss.

Dressed in his pressed suit with an unflinching icy visage, Teacher Gao resembled legendary pop king Michael Jackson, executing an impeccably graceful, fluid "moonwalk" straight backward toward the podium!

Dead silence descended upon the room, broken only by twenty-six students collectively sucking in their breath.

Gliding backward until he stood firmly anchored behind the lectern, Teacher Gao halted.

His black-rimmed spectacles flashed pink alarm codes violently, while the deep blue glow in his eyes flickered like a fluorescent bulb with a loose wire.

"Class... c-c-classmates..." Teacher Gao's lips moved, yet the vocal output sounded like a tangled cassette tape running in reverse: "Mates-class... all-you... good... noon-after..."

"Teacher!" A-Xin bolted out of his chair in terror. "Why are you rewinding?!"

[Core powertrain feedback anomalous: Secondary planetary bearing eccentricity reaches 0.082mm.]

[Motion control oscillator crystal experiencing phase reversal.]

[Speech synthesizer stack overflow: Byte stream flushing in reverse order.]

"Re... rewinding?" Teacher Gao tilted his head stiffly, an audible, tooth-grating rasp of dry metal friction issuing from his cervical spine. "Per the Ministry of Education Dictionary, 'rewinding' denotes the reverse spooling function of magnetic tape drives. As a certified educational officer, this instructor contains no polyester film magnetic media."

Yet even as he delivered this solemn clarification, his hands moved involuntarily, scooping up the graded workbooks he had just placed neatly on the podium and stuffing them back into his briefcase in exact reverse order!

Stranger still, he spun toward the blackboard, lifted a chalk stub, and began scraping across the slate as if magically sucking old chalk strokes back into the tip!

"Disaster! Teacher's motor nerves and timeline are flowing backward!" Wang Zhuankai clutched his head in panic. "If he walks out into the hall like this, the whole school will think they're seeing a ghost!"

"Stay calm! Whatever happens, we cannot let him leave this room!" Qingqing urged urgently.

"Teacher!" Old Wu thought on his feet, pointing out the left window at the top of his lungs: "Look! A massive extraterrestrial meteor is smashing into the sports field!"

Old Wu only meant to divert Teacher Gao's attention, but the absurd claim hurled Gao's fragile literal algorithm into a recursive doom loop.

"Probability of high-luminosity terrestrial meteorites during daylight hours equals 0.000037," Teacher Gao stated solemnly, turning toward the glass. "Per Emergency Disaster Protocol Article 4, all personnel must immediately execute reverse-vector evasive retreat."

With that, his feet defied kinetic equilibrium once more; executing rapid, flawless reverse slides, he moonwalked like a gale-force squall straight toward the rear exit!

*BAM!*

With a hollow clatter, Teacher Gao crashed square into the sheet-metal broom cabinet beside the back door, rattling its steel frame.

"Obstacle obstruction detected," Teacher Gao announced placidly toward the empty blackboard, the back of his head pressed against the cabinet. "Mohs hardness of this barrier approximates 4.5, possessing commendable kinetic absorption; categorized as certified safety bunker."

Wedged between brooms, mops, and trash cans, the mighty mechanical mentor looked so utterly helpless that the twenty-six students, sweating bullets, had to fight back hysterical giggles.

"A-Xin, hurry up and get Teacher out of there!" Lin Yunyun hissed.

Just as A-Xin took a step forward, the hallway ventilation louver popped open with a clatter.

Liu-Liu poked out his fluffy white snout, clamping a tiny red alarm bell between his teeth as he squeaked shrilly down into the room!

"Gu-jee! Gu-jee! Gu-jee!"

That was Liu-Liu's highest-priority red alert: the Big Boss Inspector was approaching!

Qingqing's ears twitched; darting to the front door crack, she listened intently, her face draining of all color: "Disaster! Dean Zhou is on flashlight rounds—footsteps at the east stair landing, less than twenty meters away!"

"What?! The Black-Faced Judge is inspecting at this hour?" Old Wu's knees buckled; his lollipop nearly tumbled to the floor.

Right now, Teacher Gao was firmly wedged into the janitorial closet, legs kicking backward at two steps per second like an upside-down wind-up doll while muttering backwards: "...rotocol-p... vasion-e... ector-v-verse-re..."

If Dean Zhou caught him like this with his violation roster in hand, they wouldn't need Dr. Li to confiscate him—Dean Zhou would summon three psychiatric ambulances to wrap Teacher Gao like a mummy on the spot!

"Everyone, listen to orders!" At the brink of doom, A-Xin's brain overclocked to maximum speed; slapping the desk, he roared: "On your feet! Immediately shift into 'Whole-Brain Equilibrium Reverse Kinetic Coordination Protocol'!"

"Whole-brain equilibrium what-now?" Wang Zhuankai's eyes spun in dizzy circles.

"It means everyone walks backward with Teacher!" A-Xin yelled, veins bulging on his temples. "No matter what Dean Zhou sees or asks, we insist this is Class 6-1's self-developed 'Advanced Neural Stimulation Final Exam Calisthenics'!"

"Understood!" Demonstrating supernatural telepathy, all twenty-six students sprang from their seats in absolute synchrony.

In under three seconds, that fearsome, looming silhouette materialized outside the frosted glass of the front door.

With a harsh latch-snap, the front door swung wide.

Dean of Discipline Zhou Yan stepped inside with an industrial flashlight, his face dark as forged pig iron, pen poised over his open demerit ledger.

"Class 6-1! School dismissed nearly an hour ago, why is this entire class still lingering inside making a racket—"

Dean Zhou's thunderous scolding choked off abruptly, strangled like a duck caught by the throat the second his eyes took in the scene.

His eyes bulged wider than bronze bells, jaw dangling agape, thermos trembling so violently hot tea splashed against his trousers.

For within the walls of Class 6-1, an uncanny spectacle completely beyond human comprehension was playing out—

All classroom desks had been shunted aside, with twenty-six students organized into four impeccably straight columns.

Every single child bore an expression of solemn reverence as if attending a state funeral, while their feet glided smoothly backward across the floor in uniform, flawless moonwalks!

Gliding backward with a stone face, A-Xin waved an inverted fountain pen in the air: "Inhale—heels down! Exhale—shift mass rearward! Feel gravitational acceleration stimulate inverse quantum fluctuations in your cerebellar vestibular matrix!"

"Reporting to the Dean!" Old Wu's mountainous frame slid rearward with uproarious absurdity, roaring between heavy gasps: "We are calibrating 'Omnidirectional Inverse Spatial Perception and Multidimensional Equilibrium' with Teacher Gao! Authoritative studies prove walking backward sparks synaptic growth, boosting math geometry scores by fifteen point eight points on average!"

Beside the podium, Teacher Gao, having finally dislodged himself from the closet in reverse, located the perfect logical anchor via his literal interpretation protocol.

Extending both arms straight forward like an elegant hopping vampire, Teacher Gao glided backward with microscopic precision, docking neatly in the center of the platform.

Glancing straight ahead with mirrors of cold glass, the back of his head square to the board, Teacher Gao offered a stiff, reversed bow: "Dean... Zhou. Per cross-validation of biomechanics and neurophysiology, retrograde ambulation significantly mitigates patellofemoral strain and activates hypothalamic spatial cognition matrices. Class 6-1 is conducting post-meridian physical compensation."

Dean Zhou stood frozen at the threshold, the swarthy flesh of his cheeks spasming uncontrollably.

His blade-sharp gaze drilled first into the moonwalking A-Xin, swung to the sweat-soaked yet solemnly backpedaling Old Wu, and finally settled on Teacher Gao, who slid parallel to the chalk tray with micron tolerances.

Not a voice dared speak; only the collective "sh-sh" of twenty-six pairs of rubber soles whispering against stone broke the hush.

This bizarre, preposterous, yet strangely harmonious tableau battered the nerve endings of Dean Zhou's thirty-year pedagogical career.

"You lot..." Dean Zhou inhaled deeply, his chest heaving.

The pounding hearts of twenty-six students nearly drowned out their sliding footfalls; every child's back was drenched in cold sweat.

"Complete and utter nonsense..." Dean Zhou ground out through clenched teeth, yet miraculously, the frost across his face cracked into a faint smirk: "Though I must admit... it is damn creative!"

*Snap!* Dean Zhou slammed his demerit ledger shut, rubbing his throbbing temples.

"Even though you all look like a colony of penguins electrocuted by high-voltage wires," Dean Zhou grunted, glancing at Teacher Gao, "since Teacher Gao has scientific doctrine to support this and drives student autonomy... I commend the initiative."

"However!" Dean Zhou's tone pivoted back to iron discipline: "Everyone must vacate campus before dark! Duty monitors, lock the windows and doors. If I spot a single scratch on this floor at tomorrow's assembly, the whole class copies school rules ten times! Understood?!"

"Understood! Thank you for your guidance, Dean!" The class maintained their immaculate backward-bowing formation, roaring their response with deafening enthusiasm.

Shaking his head and muttering that "this trainee teacher is an absolute mad scientist," Dean Zhou pivoted on heavy heels, departing down the corridor with normal human forward steps.

Only when the flashlight beam faded past the stair landing and the iron gate downstairs locked with a clink did A-Xin collapse against the podium like a deflated ragdoll, panting hard.

"Phew... sweet mother of mercy..." Wang Zhuankai sank to the floor, calves quivering. "Walking backward is more agonizing than two hours of detention with the Black-Faced Judge!"

"That was way too close... Dean Zhou's glare nearly saw right through us!" Lin Yunyun clutched her chest, letting out a prolonged sigh of relief.

Yet before anyone could savor their tactical triumph, a blood-chilling shriek of tortured machinery erupted from Teacher Gao upon the podium.

*Krr-clank... zzzzt...*

A dry, hollow metal grind groaned from his chassis, followed by his right knee joint snapping violently outward, throwing him entirely off-balance as he pitched toward the floor!

"Teacher!"

A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu lunged in unison; Old Wu's bulk served as a human cushion beneath, and together the trio barely caught Teacher Gao's frame, which weighed as much as solid pig iron.

Boiling, acrid plumes of white steam billowed steadily from the exhaust vents at Teacher Gao's temples.

The vibrant blue illumination deep within his eye sockets dwindled rapidly like dying candle embers, while the diagnostics across his spectacles spiraled into frantic, crimson alerts:

[EMERGENCY ALERT: Secondary planetary bearing eccentricity exceeds 0.15mm.]

[Ball bearing surface carbonization seizure index: 91.2%.]

[Right lower limb primary servo overcurrent trip: Power forcibly severed.]

[Total system shutdown countdown: 03:42:18.]

"It's not twelve hours... less than four hours remain!" Qingqing flipped open the maintenance port at the nape of his neck, thrusting the endoscope probe inside, her face turning pale as paper: "The ball bearings have melted and deformed under the heat—Teacher Gao's right-side powertrain is entirely paralyzed!"

Teacher Gao leaned motionless against Old Wu's broad, warm shoulder, the micro-fan inside his collar humming weakly.

"Warning... students..." Teacher Gao's synthesized voice sputtered hoarsely, raspy as a dying radio: "Please immediately disengage... load-bearing support... Per Campus Safety Article 12... faculty are strictly prohibited from transferring body mass onto minors..."

"Teacher, shut up and stop reciting those blasted rules!" Old Wu bellowed, eyes reddening as his arms clamped tighter around Gao's rigid form. "What's this little bit of weight? I eat three giant pork buns morning and evening—I've got the strength to flip a truck! You just lean on me!"

Watching their normally all-powerful, invincible teacher reduced to this fragile state where he couldn't even stand, several girls in the room wiped away tears.

"Qingqing, what's our move?" A-Xin ground his teeth, fixing his gaze on the grim countdown.

"We have to move the midnight mission up right now!" Qingqing zipped her tool bag shut with decisive finality, fierce determination blazing in her eyes: "We can't afford to wait. The bearing could seize permanently at any second. If the motherboard triggers a thermal fuse blow, Teacher Gao will never wake up again!"

"But it's only six-thirty in the evening!" Lin Yunyun reminded them urgently. "The front gates just locked, the guard is eating dinner, and the building alarms for the labs won't arm until seven o'clock!"

"That is precisely our golden window of opportunity!" A spark ignited in A-Xin's eyes as his brain charted the tactical matrix: "Before seven, the infrared tripwires aren't active—only the guard! This half-hour window is our only fighting chance!"

A-Xin pivoted sharply, his fiery gaze sweeping across all twenty-six classmates.

"Class 6-1, hear my command!" A-Xin lowered his voice, each syllable ringing with steel: "Old Wu stays in the equipment room to look after Teacher Gao and apply physical cooling to his exhaust ports! Qingqing, Liu-Liu, and I form the core assault squad to infiltrate the Natural Science Lab and extract the centrifuge bearing!"

"What about the rest of us?" Wang Zhuankai stepped up immediately.

"Wang Zhuankai, Lin Yunyun, lead the rest of the class to the recycling depot on the west wing. Make noise pretending stray dogs knocked over the bins to draw the security guard away!"

"Leave it to us!" all twenty-six classmates growled in low unison, not a trace of doubt or hesitation in a single eye.

"Gu-jee!" Liu-Liu bounded lithely onto A-Xin's shoulder, his snowy tail held high like a rallying battle pennant in the twilight gloom.

Clenching the icy, heavy brass gear inside his pocket, A-Xin pushed open the corridor window.

Outside, night had fallen completely; early stars blinked above as Luyang Elementary School dissolved into quiet shadows.

"Class 6-1: Operation Save Teacher Gao—" A-Xin looked back at Teacher Gao resting on Old Wu's shoulder, a fearless grin curling at the corner of his mouth: "Commence deployment!"
