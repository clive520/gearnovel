# Chapter 21: The Sleeping Teacher Deprived of Power

On the evacuation platform thirty meters high, torrential rain washed relentlessly over the cold, slick metal deck.

Teacher Gao Zhixian still stood ramrod straight at the platform's edge, his posture as erect as an obsidian monument battered by the fury of war.

Yet the black suit upon his frame had been shredded to tatters by the blistering heat and violent airflow, charred ribbons fluttering limply in the gale; across the silver-white titanium alloy skeleton exposed along his arms and chest, a dark layer of oxidized scorch marks spread, with several micro-circuit terminals still weakly sputtering with hissing, pale electrical sparks.

Most heart-wrenching of all, those eyes that had always been calm and inscrutable, that once flickered with faint light during calculations, and that had burned with gold-crimson fire in the final hour, were now completely dark, like two frigid, hollow abysses.

From the honeycomb cooling vents along his chest that had once roared with plumes of scalding white dragon steam, not a single whisper of breath emerged; the internal micro-cooling fans had frozen entirely in place, locked in deathly silence.

"Teacher Gao... Teacher Gao?!"

Wiping the grime of rainwater and tears from his face, A-Xin was the first to stumble forward before Teacher Gao, trembling as he reached out with both hands to clutch Teacher Gao's cold, rigid alloy wrist.

A palm that in the past would provide precise thermal feedback and faint servo tremors at the lightest touch was now as deathly cold as perennial glacier ice, devoid of any pulse, devoid of any response.

"Teacher! Wake up! Didn't you say our class is the most complex dynamic equation in the whole school?!" Tears gushed from A-Xin's eyes as he screamed with a heart-wrenching cry, "The roller coaster has been pushed all the way up! All twenty-six of us are here, not one missing! Open your eyes and look at us!"

"Teacher Gao..."

Behind him, all twenty-five drenched students surged forward in tears, surrounding Teacher Gao in a tight circle.

Clutching that massive Space Golden Bear, Old Wu slammed his head against the metal exoskeleton of Teacher Gao's thigh, bawling at the top of his lungs: "Teacher! Don't scare me! Didn't you say my pork buns had an optimal freshness rating of ninety-eight point four percent?! I still have two cold ones in my pocket! Please reheat them with your fingertip microwave, okay?! As long as you wake up, I promise I'll never drop meat stuffing into my desk drawer again while eating buns! Waaah..."

The timid Lin Yunyun and several other girls squatted on the ground, burying their faces in their hands, weeping uncontrollably.

Wang Zhuankai bit his lip hard, tears streaming off his chin, trying to crack a joke, but when he opened his mouth, only desperate sobs came out: "T-Teacher... didn't you hate it most when we were late for morning study hall... You're closing your eyes right now, so if I break into a street dance on this platform... w-why aren't you throwing chalk at me anymore..."

"Everyone stop crying for a second! Give him some room!"

Qingqing wiped away the rain blurring her eyes, forcibly suppressing the overwhelming ache surging in her chest, and swiftly retrieved a miniature Phillips screwdriver and a digital multimeter from her waterproof pouch.

"Qingqing, what's happened to Teacher?!" A-Xin asked with bloodshot eyes.

"The extreme load caused a core blowout... I have to inspect his main control board immediately!"

Gritting her teeth, her trembling fingertips gripping the screwdriver, Qingqing inserted it precisely into the emergency mechanical release slot beneath the second scorched button on Teacher Gao's chest.

A crisp "click" sounded.

The alloy maintenance hatch on Teacher Gao's chest popped open by a finger's width, and a pungent odor of scorched circuit coils and melted polymer immediately hit their nostrils.

Carefully inserting the multimeter's red and black metal probes, Qingqing tapped into the primary power bus terminals inside.

The digits on the LCD screen flickered wildly twice, then settled brutally at—`0.00 V`.

"Voltage... zero volts." Qingqing's voice trembled violently in the chilly wind. "The micro-capacitors in the auxiliary nuclear fusion simulated battery have completely burned through; the primary power core has dropped entirely to zero..."

"Then... what if we replace it with a new battery?!" A-Xin pressed urgently.

Her eyes brimming with tears, Qingqing shook her head, using her probe to point toward a twisted, scorched cluster of metallic components deep within the chassis: "It's not just the power... to cancel out six point five tons of gravitational pull just now, the planetary gearbox inside Teacher was forced to operate under temperatures exceeding two hundred degrees Celsius; bearing number three has suffered severe thermal expansion deformation, jamming the entire core module solid!"

Lowering his head, A-Xin reached into his collar with trembling fingers and pulled out the [P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03] brass planetary gear hanging from a red cord.

The gear gleamed with a gentle, faint golden luster amidst the tempest—the very first catalyst that had dropped before the blackboard at the beginning of everything.

"Use this! Qingqing!" A-Xin held the brass gear before Qingqing's eyes, the final spark of hope burning in his gaze. "This is the gear Teacher dropped at the very start! If we put it back in, will Teacher reboot?!"

"No... absolutely not right now!" Qingqing's scalding tears splashed against the charred metal casing. "The bearing seat has warped, and the anti-vibration locking nut securing the primary spindle was shaken loose during that overclocked thrust! Without the correct torque and locking nut, forcing the gear in will cause an instantaneous short-circuit in the primary computing chip, permanently erasing and formatting all memory data!"

"Permanently formatted..." A-Xin felt as if struck by lightning, staggering backward a step. "You mean... Teacher will completely forget us? Forget everyone in Class 6-1?!"

The hearts of all twenty-six students plummeted into an icy abyss, their weeping sounding profoundly desolate and despairing amidst the howling wind and rain.

Right at that moment, a frantic rustling sound suddenly echoed from the ventilation ducts above the platform!

"Squeak! Squeak-squeak——!"

A drenched ball of silver-gray fur darted like lightning through the exhaust grille, leaping squarely onto Teacher Gao's scorched shoulder.

It was Liu-Liu!

Liu-Liu's fluffy coat was soaked through, clinging to its tiny frame. The tip of its tail, gleaming with metallic luster, was flashing a blinding, crimson emergency alarm at a frantic frequency!

"Liu-Liu?!" A-Xin gasped in surprise.

Liu-Liu anxiously scratched at Teacher Gao's cold spectacles with its tiny paws, emitting sharp, rapid synthesized alert chirps, its little head constantly jerking toward the spiral steel maintenance staircase leading to the ground on the right!

"Clank, clank, clank——"

The heavy, rhythmic, cold thud of tactical combat boots ascending the iron steps cut through the roaring curtain of rain, rapidly approaching this thirty-meter-high evacuation platform!

A-Xin and Qingqing jerked their heads up.

Rounding the corner of the rain-drenched staircase, twelve strapping men clad in black anti-static tactical rain ponchos and reflective gas masks filed up one after another.

In their hands, they carried heavy hydraulic spreaders, insulated carbon-fiber securing cables, and a two-meter-long, pitch-black, hermetically sealed cryogenic isolation container radiating a cold metallic sheen!

And right at the forefront of these twelve fully armed tactical engineers walked none other than Dr. Li!

Dr. Li wore no mask, letting the icy rain wash over his thin metal-framed spectacles. There was not a shred of relief on his face for the rescue's success—only a chilling, fanatic arrogance.

"Splendid. Truly splendid."

Dr. Li halted at the platform entrance five meters away from the students, looking down upon the charred, rigid figure of Teacher Gao Zhixian, a cruel, triumphant smirk curving at his lips.

"A bionic prototype deemed to possess severe logical flaws and scheduled for decommission and scrapping actually unleashed fifty thousand Newtons of unauthorized humane rescue thrust without instructions from headquarters." Dr. Li pushed up his glasses, his voice cutting through the storm with grating harshness. "Yet that is all it amounted to. His superconducting coils are incinerated, his primary logic gates permanently destroyed, his backup battery completely drained to zero. Right now, he is nothing more than a hundred-and-eighty-kilogram heap of decommissioned high-grade scrap metal."

"Shut your mouth!!" A-Xin's eyes were bloodshot as he took a ferocious step forward, glaring at Dr. Li. "Teacher Gao is not scrap metal! He is our homeroom teacher!!"

"Teacher?" Dr. Li let out a derisive scoff. "Ignorant brats. He is merely experimental consumable property under Advanced Future Technology's [Project P.E.D.A.], designation GS-X01. Every single screw, every line of underlying code—ownership belongs entirely to the corporate board."

Waving his hand, Dr. Li issued his order in cold tones: "Tactical squad, execute [Omega Protocol: Final Retrieval]. Secure the chassis with carbon-fiber blast-proof hoists, seal it in the cryogenic isolation container, and transport it immediately to the heavy transport truck at the rear mountain. Upon return to headquarters, proceed with on-site non-hazardous dismantling and smelting."

"Understood!"

Four burly tactical engineers immediately stepped forward with alloy crowbars and heavy insulated chains, advancing impassively toward Teacher Gao!

"Let's see who dares touch him!!"

A-Xin threw his arms wide open, like a wolf pup guarding its pack, planting himself fiercely before Teacher Gao's battered chassis!

"A-Xin is right! Nobody lays a finger on Teacher Gao!"

Qingqing stepped up beside A-Xin without a second's hesitation, the miniature screwdriver gripped tight in her hand, her gaze as sharp as cold steel!

"Get lost! You bad guys in black raincoats!"

Old Wu hurled the one-and-a-half-meter Space Golden Bear hard at one of the tactical operatives, then threw his own stout, hefty frame forward like a human shield!

"Protect Teacher Gao!!"

"You're not taking our teacher!!"

In the very next second, an awe-inspiring scene erupted thirty meters high amidst the howling storm.

Among the remaining twenty-three students of Class 6-1, not a single one backed down; not a single one ran!

Even Lin Yunyun, usually the most timid girl who would shriek at the sight of a caterpillar, wiped her tears and charged forward with gritted teeth; Wang Zhuankai rolled up his sleeves, his eyes blazing; every child linked arms, shoulder to shoulder, forming a youthful yet impregnable human wall across the storm-battered platform!

Twenty-six sixth-grade children, using their slender bodies, fiercely shielded their cold, dormant robot teacher at the very core of their circle!

The frigid rain soaked everyone's hair and clothes, but the fury burning in those twenty-six pairs of eyes made the four battle-hardened operatives hesitate in their tracks.

"Doctor... these kids are blocking us; we can't deploy the mechanical hoists," an operative turned back to report.

Dr. Li's expression darkened completely, a vicious glint flashing in his eyes: "A bunch of foolish, insolent brats! Move in and pull them away by force! Anyone who resists gets bound with riot zip-ties!"

Just as the operatives took another stride forward, extending gloved hands to seize A-Xin's shoulder—

From the direction of the spiral iron staircase, an enraged roar suddenly exploded like a thunderclap splitting the earth!

"LI CHONGMING!! DON'T YOU DARE LAY A DAMN FINGER ON MY STUDENTS?!!"

BOOM!!

A burly dark figure coated in yellow mud and rainwater, dragging a heavy solid cast-iron water pipe torn from a maintenance shed, burst through the iron gate like an enraged lion consumed by towering fury, storming onto the evacuation platform!

It was Dean of Students—Zhou Yan!
