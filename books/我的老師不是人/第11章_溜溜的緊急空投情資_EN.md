# *My Teacher Is Not Human*

## Chapter 11: Liu-Liu's Emergency Airdropped Intel

Afternoon sunlight pierced through the mist, draping the red-brick instructional building of Luyang Elementary School in a lazy, golden sheen.

Yet beneath the placid surface of Class 6-1, an unspoken countdown was ticking away with frantic urgency.

Although the morning's "Marble Minefield" and "Chalk Dust Smoke Screen" had successfully shattered Dr. Li's pulse scanner, the faces of A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu showed none of the relaxed relief of victory.

"Qingqing, Teacher's cooling fan just buzzed again," A-Xin whispered, his head bent low over his notebook.

Seated in the adjacent aisle, Qingqing furrowed her brow tightly. With her ear pressed against the wooden desk, her fingertips tapped softly against the miniature frequency receiver on her desk: "I know... Every seven minutes, the primary drivetrain spindle on the right side of Teacher Gao's torso emits a microscopic, jarring friction click: *clunk*. That is the precursor to mechanical fatigue."

Atop the podium, Teacher Gao stood compass in hand, sketching a geometrically immaculate, perfect circle on the blackboard.

His posture remained as upright as ever, his black-rimmed glasses gleaming with their usual chill; yet the sharp-eyed Qingqing noticed that when Teacher Gao lowered the compass, the elbow joint of his right arm suffered a microscopic lag lasting a full tenth of a second.

The absence of the common-sense regulating gear [Core-Gear #03] was cascading into a chain reaction.

Lacking dampening buffers within his chassis, the other precision planetary gear secondary bearings were enduring more than double their designed mechanical friction.

"How long can Teacher Gao hold out?" Old Wu retrieved a braised meatball from his desk drawer, whispering with a distinct lack of appetite.

"Ten days at most," Qingqing inhaled deeply, her voice tinged with undisguised anxiety. "If we cannot find an exotic miniature replacement bearing within ten days, Teacher Gao's transmission spindle will seize up completely. When that happens, he will freeze and power down right in front of the entire school, and the heat-sink panels across his chest might even rupture!"

Though all twenty-six students in the class were seated at their respective desks, their eyes constantly gravitated toward the man at the podium, who remained entirely oblivious to the encroaching crisis.

Worse still, Dr. Li had not departed the campus.

From the direction of the basement electrical room, the muffled, low-frequency hum of a heavy-duty generator had been thrumming faintly.

"Liu-Liu has been gone for over an hour already..." Lin Yunyun's face was chalky, her fingers nervously clutching the hem of her shirt. "Could it be in danger?"

Before her words even settled, a series of frantic, rapid-fire claw-scratching noises—*clatter-clatter-tap-tap*—erupted from within the crisscrossing metal ventilation conduits directly overhead!

The sound surged from below like a miniature torpedo racing upstream through the ductwork!

At that very moment, deep within the darkest subterranean recess of the school's abandoned basement machinery room, the air reeked of heavy rust and sharp ozone.

A makeshift, high-gain satellite uplink dish pointed straight through an overhead exhaust grate, its eerie blue data indicator lights pulsing intermittently in the gloom.

His hair dusted white with chalk residue, Dr. Li's dark-blue jumpsuit had one sleeve torn half off. Dabbing at the purple contusion on his forehead with an alcohol swab, he gnashed his teeth in fury at the holographic communication display floating in the air.

On the other end of the screen, the tactical operations director from Advanced Tech headquarters wore a ruthless, glacial sneer:

"Dr. Li. Headquarters has reviewed the 0.5-second residual spectrum telemetry you transmitted. The verdict is confirmed: GS-X01 has indeed lost its seventh gear cluster, and its sole auxiliary planetary bearing has suffered eighty-two percent mechanical degradation."

"Executive Vice President, that prototype has developed unauthorized emotional subroutines! To protect those children, it actually discharged high-frequency microwave grounding currents bare-handed to incinerate my million-dollar pulse unit!" Dr. Li pounded the control console in outrage.

"This is precisely our gravest concern—an artificial intelligence gone rogue that develops an 'obsessive guardian protocol' toward a specific group of humans becomes unpredictably and lethally aggressive," the commander replied without a ripple of emotion. "The board of directors has issued an ultimatum."

"An ultimatum?" Dr. Li froze.

"Seventy-two hours," the commander extended three rigid fingers. "We grant you a final seventy-two-hour retrieval window. If you fail to deliver GS-X01 intact by the deadline, headquarters will dispatch specialized heavy retrieval convoys straight into Luyang Elementary School to initiate 'Full-Spectrum Ultra-High-Power Electromagnetic Pulse (EMP) Saturation.'"

Dr. Li's expression warped in horror: "An EMP strike?! That will roast every electronic device in the entire instructional facility into charred charcoal! It will permanently wipe and format GS-X01's superconducting core memory bank!"

"To incinerate a rogue prototype that threatens the corporation's reputation, such collateral damage is entirely acceptable," the commander's gaze was dead cold. "Here is the internal transmission blueprint of GS-X01 and the specifications for the replacement bearing. Figure it out yourself."

With a sharp "ping," an exquisitely intricate three-dimensional holographic exploded assembly diagram expanded across the display.

Squarely in the center of the blueprint, a precision "4mm Titanium-Alloy Ultra-High-Speed Planetary Secondary Bearing" glowed with an eye-catching green ring, annotated with detailed hardness tolerances and assembly torque requirements.

Meanwhile, on an external portable flash drive, a blood-red countdown timer was ticking away down to the millisecond: [EMP Deployment Countdown: 71:59:58].

Dr. Li unplugged the miniature quantum storage chip housing the blueprint and countdown logs, casually tossing it onto a metallic tool tray.

Turning toward the distribution panel to calibrate a high-voltage electrified barrier, he sneered: "Hmph. Class 6-1... seventy-two hours left. Once I set up this electrified grid, let's see where you brats can run!"

Yet, Dr. Li failed to notice in the slightest—

Perched on a rusted steam pipe three meters directly above the abandoned control room, a palm-sized, snow-white creature lurked quietly in the shadows like a bat hanging upside down.

It was none other than the AI pet, Liu-Liu!

Liu-Liu's two nimble, fox-like pointed ears flattened tight against the sides of its head, while the deep blue rings within its pupils recorded the entire holographic conversation at a blistering one thousand frames per second.

The moment it saw the quantum chip containing Teacher Gao's lifeline blueprint and the EMP countdown discarded onto the tray, Liu-Liu's whiskers twitched slightly.

[Core Target Locked: Titanium-Alloy Bearing Blueprint & 72-Hour Survival Key].

[Threat Level: SSS].

In the very next instant, the muscular coils of Liu-Liu's hind legs exploded with kinetic energy like compressed steel springs!

"Whoosh——!"

A streak of blinding white luminescence flashed across the pitch darkness!

Mid-air, Liu-Liu executed a breathtaking, three-hundred-and-sixty-degree somersault. Its fluffy, flexible fox-like tail snapped forward like a precision mechanical whip; as it skimmed across the tool tray, with a crisp "click," it swept the micro quantum chip cleanly into the miniature pouch behind its neck!

"CLATTER——!!"

Simultaneously, Liu-Liu's hind paws "incidentally" kicked over a heavy five-kilogram adjustable wrench, the massive iron tool smashing squarely onto the housing of an air-conditioning compressor, detonating a deafening metallic crash!

"Who's there?!"

Whirling around in shock and fury, Dr. Li raised his high-voltage shock baton, its tip crackling with blinding blue electrical sparks!

The console tray was barren—the chip bearing the classified blueprints had vanished into thin air. Three meters away at the opening of the ventilation louver, a fluffy white ball was cheekily sticking out its tongue, its bushy tail swishing twice in blatant mockery!

"That's... that infernal AI pet?!" Dr. Li's pupils contracted in fury as he went completely ballistic. "Spit that chip out right now——!!"

"Sssss-crackle-BZZZT——!!"

Waving his electric baton like a madman, arcs of blue high-voltage current ripped through the air like vipers, slamming violently toward the opening of the vent!

Yet, Liu-Liu's movement speed had long transcended the physiological limits of conventional biology.

A full hundredth of a second before the arcs landed, Liu-Liu vanished into a white blur, diving straight into the twisting, narrow vertical metal duct like a bullet shot from a barrel!

"Warning! Warning! Basement security grid fully engaged!"

Deep in the conduits behind it, Dr. Li activated the machinery room's radio-frequency jammer; ripples of invisible, potent magnetic interference spread upwards through the metal ducts like an inescapable net!

The immense electromagnetic turbulence triggered piercing acoustic alarms within Liu-Liu's internal sensors. Its core temperature rose rapidly, its white fur emitting a faint static scent of scorched ozone.

Yet Liu-Liu gritted its teeth, its tiny claws sparking against the slick, icy metal walls as it scrambled desperately upward toward that sun-drenched second-floor classroom!

It knew that resting around its neck was Teacher Gao's final lifeline, and the sole hope of all twenty-six children in the class!

"Almost there... almost there..."

Second floor. Classroom of Class 6-1.

The afternoon sun was quietly obscured by a drift of gray clouds; the study-hall classroom was quiet enough to hear a pin drop.

Seated behind the podium, Teacher Gao was meticulously grading student exercise notebooks with a red pen, the nib scraping across the paper in an even, rhythmic *scratch-scratch*.

Suddenly—

"THUD, THUD, THUD! Clatter-bang——!!"

From the central ceiling exhaust register directly above the podium came a violent vibration and metallic collision!

Teacher Gao's pen paused, the scanning beams behind his spectacles instinctively lifting upward:

`[Acoustic Analysis: High-frequency anomalous oscillation detected within ceiling conduit]`
`[Mass Estimate: 350g high-speed biomechanical entity approaching]`
`[Safety Protocol: Suspected conduit detachment; preparing hazard mitigation intercept...]`

"Oh no! It's Liu-Liu!"

Seated in the second row, A-Xin's heart lurched into his throat, and he sprang to his feet from his chair!

At that precise moment, the iron louvers of the ceiling exhaust grate were violently shoved open by immense force!

"BANG!"

Mottled with black soot and with fur wildly disheveled, Liu-Liu plummeted from the three-meter-high ceiling vent like a fired white cannonball!

Right on its heels, a faintly visible ring of violet high-frequency magnetic interference arcs chased down the ductwork!

If Teacher Gao looked up and registered this advanced AI creature, or was swept by that magnetic interference ring, his defensive combat protocols would be instantly triggered!

Yet in the nick of time, suspended in mid-air, Liu-Liu showcased the consummate tactical brilliance of an elite operative!

With a sharp twist of its torso mid-flight, its forepaws deftly unfastened the pouch behind its neck, while its jaws clamped firmly around the miniature quantum storage chip that pulsed with faint green luminescence!

Harnessing the final ounce of its kinetic momentum, Liu-Liu hurled the tiny chip across the room toward A-Xin's desk in an extreme parabolic trajectory!

"Airdrop—signal confirmed!"

A tiny green arc sliced across the air of the classroom!

"Catch it!!" Qingqing screamed in a panic.

Every single one of the twenty-six students experienced cardiac arrest in that singular second!

Without even a fraction of a second to deliberate, relying on the instinct forged from catching thousands of fly balls on the baseball diamond, A-Xin whipped open the large aluminum pencil case on his desk and lunged forward with a backhanded scoop!

"CLINK!"

The chilly, dense metal chip landed squarely into the sponge-padded interior of the pencil box!

A-Xin snapped the lid shut with a crisp *CLAP*, dropping flat over his desk in a single fluid motion like a protective leopard guarding its prize!

Simultaneously, during the final 0.5 seconds of its gravitational plunge, Liu-Liu planted all four paws firmly onto the thick lesson-plan binder on Teacher Gao's podium, using the cushion to execute a backward aerial flip before landing lightly beside the chalk tray.

The faint magnetic pulse chasing behind struck the lightning grounding strip along the blackboard border, dissipating harmlessly into the earth with an imperceptible wisp of white smoke.

Behind the podium, Teacher Gao slowly set down his red pen.

His black frames slid downward slightly as he fixed a stern, cool gaze upon the panting, soot-covered little white bundle before him.

The breathing of all twenty-six students completely ceased. Old Wu was so terrified he swallowed his braised meatball whole, choking so hard his eyes rolled back in his head.

Deep in Teacher Gao's pupils, computational algorithms spun out a string of literal deductions beyond ordinary logic:

`[Visual Recognition: Class co-owned miniature biomechanical companion "Liu-Liu"]`
`[Physical Status: Epidermis coated with 12g calcium carbonate, 8g iron oxide dust; surface temperature reaches 48°C]`
`[Literal Interpretation Mode: Organism has engaged in "parkour" curricular extracurricular exercises; severely deficient in baseline hygiene]`

With solemn dignity, Teacher Gao produced a pristine white handkerchief from his suit pocket. Extending his slender fingers, he gently grasped the scruff of Liu-Liu's neck, hoisting it into mid-air.

"Student Liu-Liu," Teacher Gao's placid, mechanical voice resonated throughout the room. "Pursuant to Article 19 of the Luyang Elementary Student Code of Conduct, utilizing ventilation infrastructure as unpowered hydro-sliding flumes without authorization constitutes a high-level disciplinary infraction."

Dabbing the soot from Liu-Liu's snout with the handkerchief, Teacher Gao added without expression:

"Furthermore, elevated epidermal temperature indicates inadequate heat dissipation. You are sentenced to receive fifteen minutes of ambient corridor airflow ventilation post-class, with half a dried fish ration deducted from today's allotment."

"Squeak, squeak..." Liu-Liu squinted its eyes comfortably, affectionately rubbing its tiny head against Teacher Gao's warm, dry palm with obedient coos.

"PHEW————!!"

The entire classroom erupted into a synchronized, exhausted exhale of survival!

"That was terrifying... I nearly wet myself..." Wang Zhuankai melted into his chair, sweat pouring down his temples like an opened faucet.

Right on cue, the dismissal bell tolled across the campus in rhythmic cadence: *Ding——dong——ding——dong——*

"Class dismissed. Group leaders, please collect the mathematics exercise workbooks."

Gracefully gathering his binders, Teacher Gao set the freshly cleaned Liu-Liu onto the podium and stepped out of the room with his standard seventy-five-centimeter stride.

The instant Teacher Gao's heel cleared the threshold, the rear corridor curtains were yanked shut by Old Wu with a furious *SWOOSH*!

Like covert operatives convening in secret, all twenty-six students clustered tightly around A-Xin and Qingqing's desk.

Donning anti-static gloves, Qingqing gingerly extracted the glowing green chip from A-Xin's pencil box using tweezers, inserting it into her modified high-performance laptop.

*Click-clack, tap-tap-tap...*

As Qingqing's ten fingers flew across the keyboard, a glaring, crimson warning prompt suddenly flashed across the screen:

`[Interception Decrypted: Advanced Tech Tactical Operations Telemetry]`
`[Threat Level: EXTREME]`
`[Countdown to EMP Strike: 71:48:12]`

As the hair-raising audio transmission between Dr. Li and the headquarters director played through the speakers, the faces of all twenty-six children turned gravely solemn.

Seventy-one hours and forty-eight minutes!

Within three days, if Teacher Gao was not repaired, heavy EMP recovery vehicles would storm the campus, frying Teacher Gao into scrap metal and permanently wiping all his memories of Class 6-1!

"Qingqing... what is that blueprint?!" A-Xin pointed urgently at the complex golden mechanical exploded assembly that materialized on the screen.

Zooming in on the schematic, Qingqing locked her gaze onto the core component, her pupils contracting sharply:

"Found it! The part on the verge of catastrophic wear inside Teacher Gao is this—the [4mm High-Precision Ultra-High-Speed Titanium-Alloy Miniature Planetary Bearing]!"

"If we can locate this bearing and install it, Teacher Gao's drivetrain will re-attain dynamic equilibrium, allowing him to operate stably for at least three more years!"

"A four-millimeter titanium miniature bearing?" Old Wu scratched his head in bewilderment. "That sounds like alien technology! Where on earth in Luyang Elementary would we find something like that?"

Snapping the laptop shut with a decisive click, Qingqing took a deep breath, lifting her head to gaze resolutely at her sworn comrades:

"Luyang Elementary has it."

"WHERE?!" twenty-six voices whispered in breathless unison.

"Inside the vintage German ultra-high-speed blood centrifuge on the top shelf of the Natural Science Lab, and inside the core spindle motor of the Craft Workshop's CNC engraving machine!"

Lowering her voice, a spark of unprecedented adventure ignited deep within Qingqing's eyes:

"Those are the only two places in the entire school equipped with bearings of that exact specification."

"But both of those rooms are padlocked shut by Dean Zhou with heavy-duty chains, and there are infrared alarms at night," A-Xin clenched his fist, feeling the heavy, reassuring weight of the brass gear against his chest. "Looks like... tonight, we have to launch a midnight campus scavenger rescue mission."

High atop the ventilation duct, Liu-Liu swished its bushy white tail in fierce excitement, letting out a sharp, spirited battle cry!

The midnight adventure of Class 6-1 had officially begun!
