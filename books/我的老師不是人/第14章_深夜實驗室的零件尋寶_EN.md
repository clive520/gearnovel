# Chapter 14: Midnight Scavenger Hunt in the Science Lab

At six thirty-five in the evening, the sky had darkened completely.

The campus of Luyang Elementary School was swallowed by thick, inky gloom, save for the faint amber glow leaking from the guardhouse window.

The academic building housing Class 6-1 lay in dead silence, broken only by drafts whipping through the breezeway that rattled the roll-up iron gates with hollow clatters.

In the corner of the physical education equipment room, several thick high-jump landing mats had been pieced together into a makeshift sickbed.

Eyes tightly shut, Teacher Gao lay straight as an arrow atop the foam mats, the micro-cooling fan in his neck straining with a laborious, low hum.

The right half of his body had lost all sensation, right arm and leg stiff as cast iron, while the countdown timer across his spectacles ticked silently: [003:12:44].

Only three hours and twelve minutes remained before his core motherboard suffered total thermal blowout.

"Old Wu, we're trusting Teacher to you," A-Xin crouched beside the mat, whispering instructions. "Swap the cold compress towel every five minutes, and keep the exhaust vent temperature at the nape of his neck below one hundred and ten degrees Celsius at all costs."

"Leave it to me!" Three damp towels were draped over Old Wu's neck, and he held two oversized badminton rackets as cooling fans; dripping with sweat, his expression was fierce with focus. "As long as I draw breath, I won't let Teacher Gao's CPU bake into a roasted sweet potato!"

"Warning... Student Wu Chenkai..." Teacher Gao's lips twitched upon the mat, his synthesized whisper faint as gossamer. "Thermal conductivity coefficient... contact boundary between iced towel and synthetic dermis presents thermal resistance... convective dissipation advised..."

"Teacher, save your battery and please shut up and go to sleep!" Old Wu's eyes reddened as he gently placed an ice-wrapped towel across Gao's boiling forehead.

The spectacles flickered once as if accepting the command; the ethereal blue glow faded, slipping into deep power-saving dormancy.

"Time's up," Qingqing glanced at the luminous dial on her wristwatch, tightening her tool pack with solemn purpose. "Uncle Chen in the guardhouse is eating his pork chop bento; at six-fifty he'll make his first patrol, and at seven sharp the central alarm system locks down the entire Science Building."

"We only have a fifteen-minute golden window," A-Xin gripped his mini-flashlight.

"Gu-jee!" Liu-Liu bounded lithely into A-Xin's hoodie, obsidian-like dark eyes gleaming with sharp vigilance in the gloom.

"Commence operation!" A-Xin ordered.

Simultaneously, from the direction of the recycling depot on the west wing, an earth-shattering commotion erupted!

*Clatter! Bang! Crash-crash-crash—!*

That was the "Diversion Brigade" led by Wang Zhuankai and Lin Yunyun triggering their distraction right on cue.

Over a dozen sheet-metal trash cans brimming with aluminum cans were shoved down the concrete steps, clattering across the open pavement in a rolling barrage that sounded like incoming mortar fire.

Immediately following, Wang Zhuankai used a wireless loudspeaker hidden in the bushes to blast prerecorded tracks of vicious dog barks and screeching cats: "Woof woof woof! Meoooow—! Awoooo—!"

"Confound it! What stray mongrels broke into the recycling yard?! My prize flowerbeds!"

The guardhouse door flew open; security guard Uncle Chen, clutching a half-eaten pork chop in one hand and waving a high-beam flashlight with the other, sprinted in a fury toward the western field.

"The tiger has left the mountain! Move!"

Exchanging a glance, A-Xin and Qingqing darted like shadowy civets along corridor edges, slipping across the central breezeway toward the Natural Science Building in the rear.

The Natural Science Building was a weathered four-story concrete structure built thirty years prior, dedicated to middle and upper-grade physics, chemistry, biology, and earth science labs.

At night, its interior was dark as a deep well, heavy with the composite stench of sulfur powder, formalin, and aged wooden cabinets.

The pair padded up the fire escape stairs in utter silence, reaching the third floor.

At the end of the hall stood a heavy wooden door painted dark forest green, bearing four crisp white characters: [Natural Science Lab 1].

Hanging from the hasp above the handle was a vintage brass padlock, flanked by an official yellow paper seal.

"Leave the lock to me," Qingqing knelt down, retrieving two slender, flattened steel lock-picking wires from her kit.

As the daughter of a motorcycle shop mechanic, Qingqing had grown up surrounded by mechanical tumblers and hydraulic valves; her fingertip sensitivity surpassed a watchmaker's.

*Click... clack...*

The wires probed inside the brass cylinder three times; with a sharp spring snap, the heavy padlock popped wide!

A-Xin eased the wooden door open; the dry hinge gave a shrill creak that sounded heart-stoppingly loud in the dead hall.

Slipping inside like shadows, Qingqing drew the door nearly shut behind them, leaving only a finger-width gap.

A-Xin clicked on his micro-flashlight, its lens shrouded in red cellophane.

The dim crimson beam washed slowly across the lab, illuminating rows of black slate countertops crowded with test tubes, beakers, and alcohol burners.

In the far right corner, "Old Bai," the full-scale plastic anatomical skeleton, grinned through the red glow; overhead, a taxidermy great horned owl hung from the ceiling, its glass eyes reflecting cold glints.

"Creepy as a tomb..." A-Xin shivered involuntarily.

"Stop staring at Old Bai, look ahead!" Qingqing hurried toward the heavy vibration-dampened workbench at the rear.

Centered squarely within the flashlight's ring sat a rounded, heavy white metal apparatus resembling a mini space capsule.

Stamped across its front was a silver nameplate: [Ultra-High-Speed Refrigerated Centrifuge · Maximum 15,000 RPM].

This was the crown jewel of Luyang Elementary's lab inventory, procured through a special Ministry grant and easily the school's priciest scientific asset!

"Found it..." wild delight surfaced in A-Xin's eyes. "That's our target!"

"Don't celebrate yet, the real hurdle starts now," Qingqing placed her bag on the counter, unzipping three tiered compartments with a rasp.

Surrounding the rotor lid was a three-centimeter-thick explosion-proof acrylic blast shield, anchored firmly to the chassis by six specialized Torx anti-tamper security screws.

More deviously, these screws were non-standard, machined with rare left-handed reverse security threads!

"Ordinary screwdrivers can't touch them; forcing it will strip the heads completely," Qingqing bit her lip, beads of perspiration prickling her forehead.

"Gu-jee!"

Crouched on A-Xin's shoulder, Liu-Liu suddenly sprang forward, landing atop the centrifuge casing.

Opening his jaws, Liu-Liu clamped his tiny alloy incisors around the shaft of a micro ratchet wrench, presenting the tool neatly into Qingqing's open palm.

Immediately after, his little paws gripped a miniature ultra-bright LED bead, acting as a shadowless surgical lamp to shine a pinpoint white beam straight into the first screw recess.

"Liu-Liu, brilliant job!" Qingqing took a steadying breath of admiration.

Fitting a custom offset Torx bit to the ratchet, her wrist flexed with practiced precision.

*Squeak... click!*

The first security screw gave a clean breakaway snap, spinning smoothly out of its threads!

A-Xin extended both palms like a surgical scrub nurse, catching the extracted screw and setting it onto a magnetic parts tray.

"Number one down!" A-Xin whispered.

"Number two... number three..."

Qingqing's fingers flew, the wrench describing swift, practiced arcs; in under three minutes, all six reverse-threaded screws were free.

With both hands cupping the acrylic blast shield, she lifted it straight off, unveiling the brushless high-speed DC motor inside, machined with turbine-like perfection.

Atop the motor's drive spindle, a ring-shaped micro-component no larger than a soybean, glinting with cold silver-grey metallic sheen, rested securely within its bronze sleeve!

"Four-millimeter aerospace-grade titanium planetary bearing!" Qingqing's breath hitched. "ABEC-9 precision class—identical to Teacher Gao's drivetrain spec!"

But as she drew her miniature snap-ring pliers to extract the bearing, her face darkened abruptly.

"What's wrong?" A-Xin's heart seized.

"There's an ultra-thin tungsten carbide retaining circlip seated above the bearing," Qingqing leaned in with her magnifying loupe. "The clip is only zero-point-two millimeters thick, and years of high-RPM run-time have baked it into place with congealed grease."

"The plier tips are too thick to seat in the eyelets," she gritted her teeth, sweat rolling down her nose. "If I force it and the circlip snaps, fragments will drop into the motor coils. That'll ruin the centrifuge, and the bearing will be fused permanently inside!"

A-Xin shot a glance at the vintage pendulum wall clock.

Six-fifty!

Barely five minutes remained before security patrolled into the Science Building!

And Qingqing's hands, strained from nonstop high-tension precision work, were beginning to tremble slightly.

"Qingqing, let me try," A-Xin gently laid a hand over her cool wrist.

"You?" Qingqing blinked.

"Don't forget: who has the steadiest shot in marbles and can balance five coins on the back of his hand? Me." A-Xin inhaled deeply, his gaze growing calm and clear as mountain water. "Pass me your jeweler's pick and magnetic tweezers."

Looking into A-Xin's focused eyes, Qingqing hesitated not for a heartbeat, passing the implements over.

Taking the hairline steel probe, A-Xin dialed his breathing down to four cycles per minute.

His vision and nervous reflexes fused; the surrounding world vanished until only the beat of his pulse remained.

The probe's needle tip crept forward by fractions of a millimeter, wedging with surgical accuracy into the sub-visible eyelet on the circlip's right wing.

"One, two... lift!"

With a microscopic flick of his wrist, A-Xin levered the tip upward!

*Ting—!*

Clean as a raindrop striking fine porcelain, the circlip popped out of its groove, vaulting into the dark!

In the thousandth of a second before it could ricochet away, A-Xin's left hand swept the magnetic tweezers in a blurred arc—*snap!*—snatching the tiny steel ring clean out of mid-air!

"Got it!" Palms slick with sweat, A-Xin let out a long-held breath.

Awe and admiration flared in Qingqing's eyes as she aimed her vacuum pickup wand at the center of the sleeve, clicking the suction release.

*Plop.*

Pristine and gleaming with iridescent titanium lustre, the four-millimeter ultra-precision bearing dropped safely into her padded velvet specimen case!

"Component secured! We did it!" Qingqing nearly cried out in triumph.

"Hurry, reassemble the casing!" A-Xin immediately aligned the blast shield over its guide pins.

Within sixty seconds the pair reseated the acrylic cover, torqued all six reverse-threaded screws home, and wiped clean every trace of grease and prints, leaving the station immaculate.

A-Xin clicked off the flashlight, plunging the lab back into total, pitch-black darkness.

On the wall, the pendulum clock clicked past six fifty-five.

"Evacuate!" A-Xin whispered.

But the very instant they crept to the doorway on tiptoe, fingertips brushing the slide latch—

Out in the deserted, pitch-black corridor, a blinding lance of halogen white light blazed to life!

Heavy leather heels clicked crisply against terrazzo tiles in measured cadence, accompanied by the metallic clink of a hefty iron key ring echoing from the third-floor stair landing!

*Clack... clack... jingle...*

Sweeping across the hallway nameplates in methodical arcs, that brilliant white beam advanced step by inexorable step toward the door of Natural Science Lab 1!

The hearts of A-Xin and Qingqing froze mid-beat; they stood paralyzed behind the timber door, holding their breath in absolute terror.

That stride was unmistakable—it wasn't the shuffling slippers of security guard Uncle Chen, but belonged to the supreme arbiter of school discipline himself!

Dean of Discipline, Zhou Yan!

The Black-Faced Judge hadn't gone to investigate the playing field as expected; master keys in hand, he had climbed straight up to the third floor of the Science Building!

The searchlight beam flickered through the door seam, barely three meters from the handle!
