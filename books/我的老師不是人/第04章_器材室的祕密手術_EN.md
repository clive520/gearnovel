# My Teacher Is Not Human

## Chapter 4: Secret Surgery in the Equipment Room

At 4:30 in the afternoon, the dismissal bell of Luyang Elementary School echoed across the campus.

The setting sun cast elongated shadows of the horizontal bars across the athletic field. Carrying their backpacks, students strolled out of the school gates in groups of two and three. However, the three core leaders of Class 6-1, along with their AI smart pet, were lurking mysteriously beneath the massive banyan tree outside the physical education equipment room.

"Liu-Liu, where is the target?" A-Xin whispered in a low voice.

Perched on A-Xin's shoulder, Liu-Liu's little snout twitched as its two pointed fox ears turned alertly. With a whoosh, it leaped onto the windowsill, its tiny paws gently nudging open a slit in the rusted Venetian blinds, turning back to nod toward the trio.

A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu quickly crouched low and pressed close.

Peeking through the crack of the blinds, the scene inside the equipment room caused the three of them to hold their breath in an instant—

There sat Teacher Gao Zhixian alone upon several stacked gym vaulting boxes. His suit jacket was removed, and the buttons of his white dress shirt were unfastened, revealing a sturdy chest.

However, that was by no means human muscle!

At the dead center of Teacher Gao's sternum was an embedded, faintly glowing honeycomb-shaped carbon-fiber cooling panel. At this moment, two ultra-miniature cooling fans on the panel were spinning furiously with an intense humming sound: "Bzzzz—bzzzz—" A thick plume of warm, white water vapor, accompanied by faint sounds of grinding gears, billowed continuously from the nape of his neck.

Meanwhile, on his right forearm, a flap of bionic skin had flipped open like the hood of a car, exposing an intricate maze of multicolored glowing optical fibers and luminous microchips underneath!

"Good heavens..." Qingqing's eyes gleamed as she pressed her face eagerly against the windowpane. "A real neural optical-fiber bus! And nano-liquid cooling heat-dissipation circulation pipes! Which god-tier laboratory engineered such an absolute masterpiece?!"

"Qingqing, this isn't the time to nerd out over technology!" A-Xin tugged her sleeve. "Look at Teacher's face!"

Sitting atop the vaulting boxes, Teacher Gao's black-framed glasses were casually laid aside. His eyes were clamped shut, his brow tightly furrowed, the corners of his mouth twitching faintly as intermittent mechanical-electronic audio crackled out:

`[Diagnostic: Thermal Overload (Core Temp: 78°C)]`
`[Logical Conflict: Students = Fragile Carbon-Based Organisms; Education Regulations = Excessive Baking Prohibited]`
`[Warning: #03 Planetary Gear Missing; Balance Axis Load Exceeds 340%... Immediate Reclamation Request to P.E.D.A. HQ Recommended...]`

The moment the phrase "Reclamation Request to P.E.D.A. HQ" was uttered, the miniature servomotor in Teacher Gao's right arm suddenly stalled, plunging him into a brief, suspended-animation state of sleep.

"He's about to ask headquarters to scrap him!" Old Wu clutched his head in sheer panic. "We can't let that happen! If a new teacher comes, who's going to heat up my pork buns?!"

"Old Wu, is there nothing in your head except pork buns!" A-Xin whacked him exasperatedly, then turned to Qingqing: "Qingqing, you said you know how to fix heavy motorcycles. This 'Teacher Gao'... can you handle him?"

Qingqing briskly unzipped her backpack, flourishing a whole set of gleaming, precision watchmaking screwdrivers and insulated tweezers with a dramatic swish.

"The fundamental mechanical principles of motorcycles and robots are the same! As long as he doesn't zap me into a crisp with an electric discharge, I can fix him!" Qingqing raised her chin with complete confidence.

"Alright! Let's move!"

A-Xin gently pushed open the long-unlocked side door of the equipment room.

"Creak—"

The door hinge gave a grating screech. In suspended animation, Teacher Gao snapped his eyes open!

A wary crimson light flashed deep within his pupils:

"Warning: Unauthorized personnel intrusion. Defensive measures preparing to acti—"

"Teacher! Wait! Don't activate! It's us!" A-Xin hastily held up both hands, his palm visibly clutching that dark-golden gleaming 【Core-Gear #03】!

The instant the gear entered his field of vision, the red light in Teacher Gao's eyes came to an abrupt halt.

Orange-yellow text cascaded like a waterfall across his pupils once again:

`[Target Object: #03 Common-Sense Damper Acquired]`
`[Host Identity: Student Lu Lixin]`
`[Status: Threat Level Reduced to Zero]`

The glow in Teacher Gao's eyes softened back to a serene, pale blue. He slowly lowered his raised mechanical arm, looking at the three children and Liu-Liu with a tinge of perplexity:

"Student Lu Lixin, Student Lin Yunqing, Student Wu Chenkai... School Rule Number 47: Loitering in the sports equipment room after school without permission is forbidden. Furthermore, you are holding my core component."

"Teacher Gao, we know everything."

A-Xin stepped forward, holding the gear out before Teacher Gao: "You aren't a regular human teacher, are you?"

Teacher Gao fell silent.

Inside the equipment room, there was only the faint humming of the micro-fans spinning and the sound of cicadas fading outside the window in the sunset.

After a long time, Teacher Gao let out a low, slow sigh. It was a sigh simulated through sophisticated algorithms, yet it carried an indescribable sense of melancholy.

"Yes. My factory model designation is GS-X01, an omnipotent bionic educational trial robot. Part of a secret pilot project between Advanced Future Technology and the Ministry of Education."

Teacher Gao looked calmly at the three children: "My original programming was to demonstrate flawless, textbook-perfect teaching capabilities and become a benchmark model for the entire county. But this morning, because planetary gear number three fell out, my 'Human Common Sense Interpretation Module' was compromised."

"So that's why you told everyone to flip themselves over like baked bread and tried to pull Wang Zhuankai's teeth?" Old Wu couldn't help interjecting.

A subtle blush crept over the edges of Teacher Gao's ears—a byproduct of poor thermal dissipation, yet peculiarly resembling a human feeling embarrassed: "Those were uncontrolled computation errors under logic-fallback mode. According to project protocol, whenever a robot experiences a Level-1 functional failure that cannot be self-repaired, a signal must be transmitted within twenty-four hours for headquarters to reclaim, scrap, and format all databases."

"Formatted?!" Qingqing cried out in shock. "Doesn't that mean you'll basically 'die'?! All your memories, all your experiences knowing us... will completely disappear?!"

"In data science, that is referred to as 'factory reset,'" Teacher Gao said calmly. "Yet I cannot deny... in today's error logs, the data weighting of Class 6-1 has already been tagged by the algorithm as 'irreplaceable.'"

A-Xin gritted his teeth, took a stride forward, snatched the miniature screwdriver from Qingqing's hand, and thrust it back into her fingers:

"What formatting! We're not letting them haul you back and tear you down for scrap metal! Qingqing, do it!"

"Right!" Without hesitation, Qingqing rolled up her sleeves, snapped on an anti-static wristband, and squatted expertly behind Teacher Gao.

"Wait! Student Lin Yunqing, unauthorized disassembly without certified engineering credentials will result in... ouch! That is the nape coolant valve, do not pry it with a flathead screwdriver!"

"Zip it, Teacher! My dad taught me that whenever a latch is stuck, tapping it twice fixes everything!" Qingqing tapped twice on the cooling casing with a rubber mallet, causing three scrambled error windows to pop up instantly on Teacher Gao's glasses lenses.

"A-Xin, hold the gear tight! Old Wu, shine the flashlight right here! Liu-Liu, keep watch at the door!" Qingqing barked commands like a seasoned commander.

Liu-Liu gave a crisp "squeak," saluted with its little paw, flashed onto the vent, and transformed into the most dedicated radar lookout.

Inside the equipment room, the golden rays of sunset sliced through the window slats, illuminating the focused children and their bionic teacher bristling with exposed hardware.

Although Qingqing's technique was slightly rough-and-tumble, although Old Wu was so nervous he nearly dropped pork bun crumbs into the motherboard, and although A-Xin's palms were soaked with sweat as he handed over screws—

When that 【Core-Gear #03】 finally snapped back into place with a definitive "click," the orange-yellow scrambled text in Teacher Gao's eyes vanished in an instant, shifting into a clear, radiant hue of azure blue.

"Component engagement completion rate: 95 percent."

Teacher Gao inhaled deeply. The fans on his chest slowly folded inward, and his bionic skin smoothed out to its seamless form. Putting his black-framed glasses back on, he gazed at the three soot-faced students whose hands were covered in black grease.

"You have harbored a major safety defective unit, violating the school's code of honesty," Teacher Gao stated with solemn composure.

A-Xin grinned widely: "So Teacher, are you going to deduct our conduct grade?"

Teacher Gao's lenses flickered slightly, and the corners of his mouth curved into a remarkably genuine smile that shattered algorithmic constraints:

"No. In accordance with the revised supreme core directive—"

"Class 6-1, alliance successfully formed."
