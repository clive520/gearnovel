# Chapter 17: Departure! The Final Graduation Trip

At six-ten in the morning, beneath the century-old banyan tree fringing the sports field of Luyang Elementary School, a vibrant mountain of twenty-six oversized hiking backpacks was already piled high.

The dawn mist had yet to fully dissipate, leaving the autumn air laced with a crisp, cool dew, yet the gathering point of Class 6-1 was already bubbling like a kettle on full boil.

"A-Xin! Check out my luggage!" Old Wu, sporting a pair of ecstatic panda-like dark circles under his eyes, zipped open his extra-large duffel bag with a flourish, revealing twelve packs of spiced beef jerky, eight bags of seaweed potato chips, six vacuum-sealed mushroom-pork buns, and two squashed inflatable pillows.

"Old Wu, we're going on a three-day, two-night graduation trip to the south, not embarking on a thirty-day wilderness survival show on a deserted island!" A-Xin, hoisting his daypack, playfully knocked on Old Wu's round belly with a wide grin.

"What do you know? According to my digestive thermodynamics calculations, for every thousand meters traversed on a tour bus, two hundred kilocalories must be replenished to maintain peak cerebral alertness!" Old Wu retorted with solemn conviction.

"Stop making excuses for your insatiable appetite!" Qingqing, wearing her high ponytail and carrying a tactical gear pack loaded with multi-pliers, first-aid bandages, and backup power banks, walked over and rolled her eyes at him.

"Guji!"

Liu-Liu poked his tiny head out from the hood of Qingqing's windbreaker; around his neck was tied a ceremonial miniature red scarf, and clutching a bite-sized piece of hard candy in his fuzzy paws, he blinked cheekily at everyone.

"Reporting to Class President! All twenty-six students are present and accounted for—zero tardiness!" Class Activities Coordinator Lin Yunyun trotted over happily, clutching her attendance clipboard.

Wang Zhuankai, brandishing twin high-pressure electric water blasters, was locked in heated tactical discourse with several boys over how to storm the theme park's "Pirate Splashdown Rapids."

Just then, a rhythmic and formidable cadence of footsteps echoed from the covered walkway.

"Click, click, click..."

All twenty-six pranksters instantly snapped to silent attention like well-trained soldiers, turning their heads in unison.

There strode their homeroom teacher, Gao Zhixian, clad in an impeccably pressed dark-blue technical outdoor jacket; his dress trousers were as sharp as razor blades, and his black-rimmed glasses refracted a calm, profound sheen in the morning light.

Fastened around his left wrist was a rugged multi-functional engineering chronograph; in his right hand, he effortlessly carried a sleek, silver-white blast-proof alloy briefcase.

Two paces behind Teacher Gao walked the Dean of Student Affairs, Zhou Yan—the dreaded "Black-Faced Judge"—with his infraction register tucked beneath his arm, his expression as dark as weathered iron.

Dean Zhou's piercing gaze lingered on A-Xin, Qingqing, and Old Wu for two full seconds, his nostrils flaring faintly, as though still pondering the rubber bullfrog that had plummeted from the heavens the night before, along with that world-shattering "wild boar cat roar."

Yet he merely let out a heavy, guttural scoff and turned toward Teacher Gao: "Teacher Gao, Class 6-1 is the most hyperactive cohort in this school. Away from campus, school discipline must be rigidly enforced. If there is any rowdiness or misconduct on the tour bus..."

"Pray rest assured, Dean Zhou." Teacher Gao pushed up his glasses, a microsecond glimmer flaring across the lenses. "This unit has pre-loaded all four hundred and twenty-eight clauses of the 《National Elementary Outdoor Education Safety Protocols》 and the 《Off-Campus Public Order Maintenance Regulations》 into temporary cache; operational error tolerance is zero."

The corner of Dean Zhou's mouth twitched imperceptibly; having no rebuttal, he gave a curt nod, clapped Teacher Gao's shoulder, and turned toward the school gate to inspect the remaining buses.

Watching the Black-Faced Judge march away, all twenty-six students let out a collective, breathless sigh of immense relief.

"All units, quiet. Commencing pre-departure material distribution and safety verification routine." Teacher Gao placed the silver-white briefcase flat atop the stone planter and popped the dual combination latches with a crisp snap.

The briefcase swung open, revealing twenty-six silver vacuum-sealed foil pouches arranged in immaculate grid formation.

Each pouch was neatly stamped with a roster number and student name, emblazoned with a prominent warning label: 【P.E.D.A. Emergency Human Anti-Flattening & Micro-Maintenance Multi-Kit・V3.2】.

"Teacher Gao... what is this?" A-Xin received the pouch bearing his name, squeezing it curiously; it felt surprisingly dense and heavy.

"This is the dedicated 'Emergency Anti-Flattening Kit' for Class 6-1," Teacher Gao explained with absolute, deadpan literal solemnity. "In light of student Lu Lixin and student Wu Chenkai frequently asserting in class and daily activities that 'my belly is flattened from starvation' or 'I am crushed completely flat by homework', to prevent irreversible physical geometric collapse of human anatomy during our three-day itinerary, this unit has custom-engineered these survival kits."

"Each pack contains four bars of ultra-compressed high-energy carbohydrate gel, one high-elasticity anti-thoracic compression lumbar brace, three nano-hemostatic dressings, and an active dual-mode Beidou-GPS emergency transponder beacon."

Teacher Gao's gaze swept over the class: "Please keep these affixed to your person at all times. Should you encounter a bodily flattening crisis, bite open the blue gel immediately and activate the distress beacon."

"PFFFT—!"

The entire class could no longer contain themselves, erupting into a deafening roar of uncontrollable laughter!

"Incredible! Teacher even engineered an anti-flattening survival kit for us!" Old Wu laughed so hard tears streamed down his cheeks, reverently stuffing the pouch into his inner jacket pocket like a sacred talisman.

A-Xin grinned as he stowed his pack, his fingertips brushing against the brass gear hanging from the red cord beneath his shirt, feeling an overwhelming sense of warmth and security.

"HONK-HONK—!"

From the campus gate came the deep, resonant horn of the tour bus; a brand-new, luxurious three-axle double-decker motorcoach rolled slowly into the courtyard, a brilliant scarlet banner across its windshield: 【Luyang Elementary Graduation Tour・Bus No. 1 (Class 6-1)】.

"All aboard, everyone!" the cheerful tour guide lady waved enthusiastically from the steps.

"Let's GOOO—!" Twenty-six sixth-graders cheered like birds released from a cage, surging toward the bus door.

Yet at the boarding threshold, Teacher Gao transformed into a walking security scanner, extending his arm to halt the rush.

"Please proceed through inspection in single file." A nearly invisible grid of laser scan lines flickered from Teacher Gao's pupils, scanning each piece of hand luggage in turn.

"Student Wang Zhuankai: fluid within water blasters confirmed as municipal tap water, non-corrosive, cleared."

"Student Lin Yunyun: instant camera film reserve verified sufficient, recommended aperture set to F4, cleared."

When it came to Old Wu, Teacher Gao's gaze locked onto his bulging duffel bag, an amber alert popping across his HUD: 【Excessive Mass Load Detected.】

"Student Wu Chenkai, your hand-carried baggage mass has reached 18.6 kilograms, exceeding the static structural load limit of the overhead luggage rack." Teacher Gao issued a solemn directive. "To prevent centroid displacement leading to centrifugal rollover, transfer six packages of beef jerky and four bags of meat buns to the undercarriage ballast zone."

"Teacher! That's my mid-journey emergency ration!" Old Wu wailed in despair.

"Pursuant to vehicular dynamic stability algorithms, vehicular safety takes priority over snack intake queues." Teacher Gao confiscated half of Old Wu's snack supply with a single sweep, stowing it into the lower cargo hold and positioning it dead-center over the left-axle balance point.

The entire class erupted in fresh gales of laughter as they jostled their way into the cabin.

At seven o'clock sharp, the motorcoach engine gave a throaty rumble; amidst the farewell waves of Principal Qiu Shouren and the faculty at the gate, the bus—brimming with unbridled merriment—eased onto the expressway, barreling southward toward the sunlit horizon.

Inside the cabin, the air conditioning blew crisp and cool; plush high-back seats and panoramic windows kept the eager graduates buzzing with boundless energy.

The bus had barely cleared the first expressway interchange before the tour guide took the microphone: "Alright, Class 6-1! To kick off our three-hour ride, the cabin karaoke showdown officially begins now! Who's taking the opening song?"

"Old Wu! Old Wu goes first!" the whole bus chanted in unison.

Old Wu seized the mic, puffed out his barrel chest, and belted out the pop classic *Love You Ten Thousand Years* with his raspy, booming voice.

Alas, having strained his vocal cords mimicking that feral cat the previous night, his high notes cracked like chainsaws and his low notes sputtered like rusty tractors, sending the entire bus into convulsions of helpless laughter.

"Too destructive on the eardrums! Switch singers, switch!" A-Xin snatched the microphone, spinning around to face Teacher Gao, who sat bolt upright in the center of the first row like a ceremonial spear.

"Teacher Gao! All twenty-six of us have sung; it's time for the homeroom teacher to deliver the grand finale!" A-Xin thrust the microphone into Teacher Gao's hands, mischievous sparks dancing in his eyes.

The bus erupted into thunderous claps and chants: "One song from Teacher Gao! One song from Teacher Gao!"

Teacher Gao gazed at the metallic microphone, a torrential deluge of data cascading across his lenses: 【Received collective cultural-entertainment engagement request... Querying Ministry of Education Primary School Music Curriculum Database...】

Teacher Gao stood up, his demeanor as grave as a statesman addressing the United Nations General Assembly, and raised the microphone to his lips.

"Since comrades insist, this unit shall, pursuant to 《Acoustic Physics and Harmonic Resonance Principles》, perform a standard frequency calibration rendition of *Two Tigers*."

Instantly, from the overhead stereo speakers came Teacher Gao's completely unmodulated, chillingly precise, purely synthesized robotic voice:

"Two tigers... two tigers... Frequency: 261.63 Hertz (C4 pitch)... Running fast... running fast... Relative velocity: 45.2 kilometers per hour..."

"One has no ears... one has no tail... Pursuant to genetic and phenotypic mutation analyses... this represents severe physiological impairment induced by autosomal recessive allele deletion... So bizarre... so bizarre..."

A dead silence fell across the bus for two full seconds.

And then—

"BWAAAHAHAHAHAHAHA—!"

The entire coach nearly tipped over from the thunderous explosion of mirth!

Even the bus driver was clutching the steering wheel with tears streaming down his face; Qingqing slapped her knees in stitches, A-Xin slid out of his seat onto the aisle carpet laughing, and Old Wu choked on his lollipop, sending it flying across the row!

To sing a nursery rhyme like a forensic pathology report on genetic mutations—on this green earth, only Teacher Gao Zhixian was capable of such a feat!

Perched on the overhead luggage rack, Liu-Liu rolled on his back in sync with the beat, his electronic pupils flashing a dazzling kaleidoscope of rainbow lights.

At eleven-thirty, the bus pulled into the largest expressway rest area in the south for a lunch break.

The food court was packed to the gills; Old Wu queued up and bought ten frozen microwave pork buns, only to discover to his horror that all four public microwaves had tripped the circuit breaker from overload!

"We're doomed! The filling inside is still rock-hard ice! My lunch is ruined!" Old Wu cradled the icy paper box, pacing in utter despair.

Right then, a white-gloved hand with slender fingers reached over, resting gently atop the box of buns.

"Organic starches and animal lipids detected in subzero crystalline state," Teacher Gao's calm synthesized voice chimed. "Initiating ten-finger high-frequency microwave oscillation heating protocol."

Bzzzz—!

A faint, mesmerizing shimmer of pale-blue electromagnetic light swirled discreetly across Teacher Gao's fingertips, high-frequency waves slicing through the cardboard to uniformly agitate the water molecules within.

A mere ten seconds later, a rich, mouthwatering aroma of braised pork and fresh dough billowed forth, plumes of piping-hot steam bursting from the seams of the box!

"They're cooked! And heated ten times more evenly than a microwave, with all the juices sealed right inside!" Old Wu took a massive bite, tears of pure culinary bliss welling in his eyes. "Teacher Gao! You truly are the greatest mobile microwave in the whole universe!"

Teacher Gao sternly corrected him: "This unit is a Ministry-certified advanced omnipotent educational bionic robot, not a culinary home appliance. Do not conflate the two categories."

Surrounding classmates swarmed forward, thrusting their lunchboxes out: "Teacher! My rice ball needs five seconds of heating!" "My cheese croissant too!"

Though Teacher Gao protested that "domestication into home appliances denigrates this unit's high-tier artificial intelligence", his fingers deftly tapped each student's meal in turn, filling the entire seating pavilion with delicious, sizzling aromas within seconds.

At one-forty in the afternoon, the motorcoach descended the mountain interchange, gliding down a highway flanked by lush, emerald tropical rainforest boughs.

As the vehicle rounded the final bend in the mountain pass, a breathtaking sci-fi metropolis suddenly materialized before their eyes!

"WHOA—! We're here! Look out the window!"

All twenty-six students pressed their faces against the glass, letting out an earth-shattering roar of awe!

Sprawling across several square kilometers was a colossal theme park: gleaming silver geodesic domes reflecting the golden sun, while suspended maglev trains roared overhead through transparent tubes.

And towering dead-center over the park was a thirty-meter-tall steel leviathan plunging vertically into the earth at ninety degrees—the 【Sky Quantum Coaster】!

Its obsidian tracks twisted and corkscrewed through the clouds, sending adrenaline surging through the veins of anyone who dared look upon it.

The tour bus pulled to a smooth halt before the park's flagship resort: the "Extremes Interstellar Resort Hotel."

"Class 6-1, disembark and collect room keys!" Teacher Gao stood in the center of the grand lobby, hoisting the class banner as he methodically assigned rooms.

A-Xin, Old Wu, and Wang Zhuankai were assigned to Room 302; Qingqing, Lin Yunyun, and the girls were in Room 305 across the hall; while Teacher Gao's single chaperone room was Room 301, situated dead-center with an unobstructed vantage over the hallway.

By four o'clock, having unpacked their gear, the children were frolicking freely across the hotel lawns—squirt-gun battles, group snapshots, and shared treats echoing under the mountain twilight.

A-Xin stood on the corridor balcony, leaning into the humid mountain breeze as he watched technicians run trial runs on the roller coaster in the distance, his spirit soaring with pure joy.

Teacher Gao walked over holding a glass of pure water, gazing outward across the theme park's intricate mechanical architecture.

"Teacher Gao, can we really ride whatever we want tomorrow?" A-Xin asked with a cheeky grin.

"Provided parameters remain within safety specifications and temporal limits, this unit shall accommodate all exploratory requests from comrades," Teacher Gao replied evenly, the blue glint on his lenses softening under the sunset.

"Thank you, Teacher Gao," A-Xin said earnestly, patting the brass gear resting against his chest. "Having you as our teacher is truly the best thing ever."

Deep within Teacher Gao's core processor, the primary bus hummed softly as an unnamed humanized algorithm generated another cascade of warm, golden code.

"Pursuant to this unit's database evaluation... serving as instructor for Class 6-1... has proven to be the sole divergent, yet mathematically optimal decision in this unit's entire operational lifecycle."

It was a tranquil, golden twilight, the children basking in the eve of their long-awaited graduation revelry, completely unaware of the dark shadow closing in.

At six in the evening, night blanketed the southern mountain range, and dense mountain mist and drizzle began creeping through the pine groves.

In a shadowy pine thicket a mere eight hundred meters from the theme park's rear logistics access road—

Two heavy, three-axle tactical transport trucks, their hulls armored with lead-alloy plating and electromagnetic shielding, extinguished their headlamps and melted into the foggy gloom.

The passenger door swung open, and Dr. Li—clad in an anti-static tactical jumpsuit and communications headset—hopped down onto the muddy gravel.

Behind him stepped twelve heavily equipped tactical retrieval engineers bearing the corporate insignia of Advanced Future Technology, carrying directional high-energy microwave cannons and hydraulic jaws.

Dr. Li pulled up a 3D holographic radar display on his wrist terminal, where a faint yet distinct quantum signature in Room 301 of the Interstellar Resort flashed steadily: 【Target Codename: GS-X01・Distance: 820 meters】.

"Reporting to Board: Retrieval Squads One and Two have reached target ambush coordinates," Dr. Li murmured into his throat mic, his voice icy and venomous.

From the earpiece came the emotionless synthesized reply of the Board: 【Excellent. Meteorological radar indicates severe convective squall lines and thunderstorms will hit the park tomorrow afternoon.】

【When the theme park power grid trips, Operation Disconnect shall commence. Neutralize the target's power reserves, execute full core formatting, and retrieve the prototype intact.】

"Understood!" A ruthless, fanatical glint burned in Dr. Li's eyes as he cut the feed and stared toward the brightly lit resort pavilion through the misty night.

"Enjoy your very last night as a human teacher, GS-X01..."

"Tomorrow's roller coaster will be the execution ground where Class 6-1 watches you turn into scrap metal!"

The mountain wind howled across the darkened iron rails, as the fatal opening salvo of an ultimate battle of life, death, and salvation was officially unleashed!
