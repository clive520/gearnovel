# Chapter 20: Unlocking 100% Full Power

The frigid steel rails tilted at an eighty-degree incline were as slick as if coated in butter in the torrential downpour, while howling gales sliced laterally thirty meters above the ground, whipping Teacher Gao's black suit into a furious flutter.

Yet those black leather shoes clung like rooted electromagnets, biting tenaciously into the slick flange of the I-beam, sending a muffled resonance humming through the steel rail with every step he took.

"CRACK——!!"

A final, heart-wrenching metallic detonation suddenly tore through everyone's ears!

The gravity self-locking ratchet on the right side of the coaster, already riddled with hairline fractures, finally succumbed under the combined assault of its six-point-five-ton deadweight and the tearing winds, disintegrating into dozens of scorched steel fragments glowing with sparks that whistled into the bottomless abyss below!

Deprived of its final mechanical latch, the entire train carrying the twenty-six students of Class 6-1 lurched violently downward, its guide wheels shrieking in agonizing friction against the track as it began a runaway plunge straight toward the yawning chasm!

"AAAAAAAH——!!" The collective shrieks of all twenty-six children dissolved into desperate wails amidst the storm.

In that hair-thin instant between life and death, Teacher Gao Zhixian, stationed upon the vertical steel rail, moved!

His velocity far exceeded the physical limits of human dynamic vision; his silhouette blurred through the air, knees flexing slightly, both arms lunging forward like the heavy blades of an unholstered hydraulic shear straight into the descending forward chassis of the runaway car!

"BOOOOM!!"

Two palms enveloped within the cuffs of his black suit locked relentlessly onto the coaster's leading traction crossbeam and guide-wheel assembly!

High-tensile alloy slammed violently into the coarse steel beam, unleashing a blinding halo of searing white electrical arcs and explosive sparks that flashed like a miniature supernova detonating within the blackened tempest!

"SKRRRRRCH——SCREEEEECH——!!"

The entire six-point-five-ton iron beast, descending at terrifying speed, was forcefully halted by the colossal counter-tractive torque surging from Teacher Gao's arms!

Under the violent neutralization of kinetic forces, the wheelsets and slick rails shrieked like a dying behemoth; the immense inertia dragged Teacher Gao's feet forty centimeters down the steel rail, his alloy soles carving two deep, scorched grooves through the track's anti-slip teeth!

"I-It stopped...?!" Poking his head out from the Golden Bear's stomach, tears and snot streaming down his round face, Old Wu stared dumbfoundedly at the impossible spectacle before him.

The entire roller coaster had been arrested single-handedly by Teacher Gao, suspended upon the razor-thin boundary of life and death less than thirty centimeters from the vertical drop!

At that very moment, the sleeves of Teacher Gao's black suit ruptured into ribbons under the extreme thermal load and tensile forces, exposing the titanium alloy mechanical arms shifting beneath like liquid silver.

The synthetic bionic skin covering his forearms had been utterly abraded by the furious friction, baring the dense thickets of whining hydraulic lines and micro-superconducting fiber optic bundles pulsing with dazzling gold-crimson light!

"Full power unlock completion: one hundred percent."

"Servo motor torque output: thirty-eight thousand Newton-meters."

"Core nuclear fusion simulated battery temperature: one hundred and forty-five degrees Celsius... Alarm: climbing at a continuous rate of two degrees per second."

Teacher Gao's cold, flat synthesized voice filtered cleanly through the storm into the ears of A-Xin and Qingqing in the front row.

Those eyes, usually calm and inscrutable, had transformed completely into twin nuclear beams of blazing gold-crimson fire, burning two transparent tunnels through the blackened curtain of rain!

"Teacher Gao..." Clutching the safety bar with white knuckles, his eyes bloodshot, A-Xin found his throat so parched he could barely make a sound.

This was not the playful "steam venting from the head" or "fingertip microwave" they had witnessed in secret back in the classroom; this was true, unbridled mechanical operation running at absolute maximum capacity!

To arrest this falling roller coaster, every single gear, bearing, and superconducting cable within Teacher Gao's chassis was bearing a horrific overload more than double its physical design tolerances!

"Status report to all students," Teacher Gao turned his face impassively, his gold-crimson light pupils reflecting twenty-six pale, tear-streaked faces, "this unit is currently maintaining train suspension via reverse-traction protocol. However, according to material fatigue limit models, the chassis crossbeam gripped by this unit will experience plastic deformation fracture in seventy-five seconds."

"Then... what happens to us?!" Wang Zhuankai cried out tremulously, all traces of his usual prankster humor drained from his voice.

"To guarantee a ninety-nine point nine-nine percent survival rate for all personnel," Teacher Gao slammed both feet down onto the steel rail once more with a resounding metallic clang, "this unit must push the train backwards up the eighty-degree vertical track to the upper platform before structural fracture occurs."

Qingqing screamed in horror: "Push it backwards?! Teacher! That's six point five tons! And the track is tilted at eighty degrees! Your cooling system can't endure that sustained power consumption—your main processing core will be incinerated!!"

"Executing the student safety retrieval protocol is this unit's primary core directive; it is non-negotiable." Teacher Gao's voice was unwavering.

Before his words even faded, the entire row of honeycomb cooling vents across Teacher Gao's chest erupted with a high-pitched pneumatic shriek!

"FSSSSSSHHHH——!!"

Two plumes of scalding, incandescent white steam extending three meters long blasted out into the night sky like enraged dragons, instantly flash-boiling the torrential rain within a three-meter radius into a swirling cloud of superheated mist!

"Reverse traction propulsion——engaged!"

Accompanied by a low, metallic roar from Teacher Gao, glowing crimson heat blooms rippled beneath the muscular contours of his arms as the internal micro-superconducting motors screamed past their maximum limits!

"Clank... clank!"

Upon the eighty-degree vertical cliff, that colossal twelve-car roller coaster actually climbed backward along the rain-slicked steel rails, inch by agonizing inch, ascending a full centimeter!

"It moved! The roller coaster is moving backwards!" Several boys in the rear rows shouted in wild excitement.

Yet with every single centimeter pushed upward, the steel rails groaned beneath Teacher Gao's feet with an agonizing metallic wail.

Teacher Gao's alloy soles crushed deep indentations into the steel, rainwater sizzling into steam the instant it struck his burning mechanical arms, sending a pungent stench of scorched insulation expanding through the air.

"Core battery level: twenty-eight percent... twenty-four percent... twenty percent..."

Along the fractured rim of his spectacles, terrifying blood-red numbers plummeted in freefall.

Thirty meters below beside the tactical command van, Dr. Li stared petrified at the wildly spiking energy telemetry on his tablet, his entire body frozen like a stone statue.

"Madness... this robot has completely lost its mind!" Dr. Li's eyelid twitched violently, his complexion turning as white as a corpse. "He is burning through his primary superconducting coils! For twenty-six human children who share not a single drop of blood with him... he actually chooses to reduce himself to a heap of scrap metal?!"

"Doctor! His reverse thrust has exceeded fifty thousand Newtons! The coaster is being hoisted back toward the platform!" the tactical engineer reported in a trembling voice.

Down on the muddy ground, Dean of Students Zhou Yan, notorious for his volcanic temper, had long since hurled aside the broken red megaphone.

Craning his neck, gazing thirty meters up at that figure bathed in white-hot steam and gold-crimson fire, defying heaven itself with the indomitable will of flesh and blood, hot tears suddenly spilled from the dean's eyes.

"TEACHER GAO——!!" Zhou Yan roared into the tempest with every shred of strength in his lungs. "Hold on! You are the finest teacher our Luyang Elementary has ever seen!! You little rascals of Class 6-1! Put your backs into it! Lighten the load for your teacher!!"

Hearing Dean Zhou's thunderous roar piercing the storm, A-Xin in the front row fiercely wiped the tears from his cheeks.

"Did you all hear that?!" A-Xin spun around, standing upon his seat, shouting at the top of his lungs to the twenty-five terrified classmates behind him. "Teacher Gao is putting his life on the line to save us! We can't just sit here like helpless cowards waiting to die!"

"He's right! We're not dead weight!" Old Wu hurled the Golden Bear behind him. "Everyone, follow my lead! Whenever the coaster pushes back, lean your bodies all the way back! Use our body weight to help Teacher balance the center of gravity!"

"Qingqing! Old Wu! Everyone chant together!" A-Xin's eyes burned with wild resolution.

"One! Two——PUSH!!"

"One! Two——PUSH!!"

The twenty-six children of Class 6-1, suspended thirty meters up in the screaming storm, threw their bodies backward in perfect unison with Teacher Gao's pushing rhythm, unleashing a chorus of youthful roars that reverberated across the entire theme park!

This was a miracle forged jointly by human children and a divine bionic android.

Every single roar from the children seemed to inject an unfathomable neural network pulse into Teacher Gao's teetering core algorithms!

Within Teacher Gao's cold, calculating processor, the wildly proliferating error codes were forcibly overridden by the synchronized heart rate telemetry of all twenty-six students.

"Unidentified external emotional data injection detected..."

"Self-preservation fuse protocol... permanently overridden by Teacher Authority!"

"GET——UP THERE!!"

Teacher Gao unleashed an earth-shattering roar that transcended mechanical synthesizer tones, ringing with the fury of a living human soul!

His legs planted like twin adamantine pillars anchoring the earth, the micrometric alloy gears at his waist showering brilliant sparks as his arms erupted with unprecedented, titanic force, driving the entire six-point-five-ton train into a final, ferocious ten-meter sprint up the eighty-degree vertical rail!

"CLANK, CLANK, CLANK——BOOOOOM!!"

With a tremendous crash that sounded as though thunder had cleaved the world in two, the leading guide wheels of the roller coaster finally crested the summit ridge of the vertical track, slamming heavily onto the horizontal evacuation buffer platform above!

"CLANG——RUMBLE!"

The massive, purely mechanical anti-slip gravity anchors on both sides of the platform instantly engaged, locking down the train's wheel bearings with unyielding grip!

Safety lock achieved!

The Sky Quantum Coaster, carrying the twenty-six students of Class 6-1, had returned entirely to the absolute security of level track!

The torrential rain continued to cascade, but the coaster no longer trembled; the terrifying abyss had been left behind.

"We survived... we really survived!!"

Old Wu collapsed back into his seat, and a moment later, the entire train erupted into deafening cheers and weeping embraces.

The children threw open their safety bars, scrambling out of the cars like mad, desperate to throw themselves into the arms of their hero teacher.

Yet as A-Xin and Qingqing burst through the car doors first, they froze dead in their tracks, tears instantly bursting forth unchecked.

There, at the edge of the storm-swept evacuation platform, Teacher Gao Zhixian still stood ramrod straight.

But his black suit had been shredded into scorched tatters by the blistering heat and steam; upon the silver-white mechanical skeleton exposed across his arms and chest, dark scorch marks and sputtering blue electrical arcs danced fitfully.

Those eyes that had once shone so brilliantly, ablaze with gold-crimson fire, were now dimming like a candle down to its final thread of wick.

Looking upon the safe, unharmed faces of A-Xin, Qingqing, and all the children gathering behind them, Teacher Gao's lips twitched slightly, as if attempting to simulate a standard, gentle human smile.

"Twenty-six students... all safely arrived... zero casualties..."

Intermittent electronic static sputtered from Teacher Gao's vocal synthesizer module.

Then, accompanied by a faint mechanical wheeze, the honeycomb cooling fans in his chest slowly ground to a halt.

That final glimmer of light dancing in the depths of Teacher Gao's eyes went "click," plunging completely into boundless darkness.
