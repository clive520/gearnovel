# Chapter 23: The Miraculous Final Gear

The ferocious night rain fell like lashes of silver, whipping relentlessly against the evacuation platform thirty meters above Future Extremes Theme Park.

The retreating footsteps of Dr. Li and his twelve tactical operatives had long vanished down the metal staircase, but the air on the platform hung colder and heavier than the tempest itself.

Kneeling on the mud-streaked, water-logged diamond-plate steel, Qingqing had long lost feeling in both knees from the freezing deluge, and her hands trembled uncontrollably.

Before her eyes, Teacher Gao's scorched, shredded suit jacket lay open, and the alloy inspection hatch over his chest—once sleek as a mirror—had been forced ajar, exposing the tangled labyrinth of fiber optics and cold, dormant superconducting microchips within.

The master debugging flash drive left behind by Dr. Li was already plugged into the maintenance diagnostic port, its miniature holographic projection shivering wildly in the storm as it cast a line of crimson, high-priority emergency alerts——

【Supercapacitor Residual Charge: 7.4%】
【Countdown: 11 minutes 42 seconds】
【Warning: Upon complete charge depletion, core logic units will execute permanent formatting routine; all dynamic memory banks will suffer irreversible erasure.】

"Eleven minutes... only eleven minutes left..." Qingqing choked out, wiping rainwater from her face as her lips turned a chilling shade of purple.

"Qingqing, don't panic! We're all right here!" A-Xin roared at the top of his lungs, violently wiping away the tears in his eyes.

Whirling around, he bellowed toward his shivering classmates who refused to retreat even a single step across the gale-swept platform: "Class 6-1, hear my order! Everyone take off your jackets, raincoats, and backpacks! Move! Fast!"

"We'll block the rain! Not a single drop of water can touch Teacher Gao's chest!" Old Wu wiped away the mud on his face, stripping off his heavy, water-soaked windbreaker without hesitation.

All twenty-six drenched children sprang into coordinated action amidst the clattering thunder and flashing lightning.

Old Wu, Wang Zhuankai, and several of the sturdier boys formed a human windbreak with their broad backs, digging their heels into the wet steel against the shrieking seventy-kilometer-per-hour crosswinds.

Lin Yunyun and the other girls hoisted everyone's raincoats, jackets, and schoolbags overhead, erecting a makeshift yet completely watertight canopy directly over Qingqing and Teacher Gao.

Dropping to one knee beside Qingqing, A-Xin held Dr. Li's high-lumen tactical flashlight in a death grip, locking its brilliant beam directly onto the open core of Teacher Gao's chest cavity.

Still gripping that heavy, ice-cold cast-iron water pipe, Dean Zhou Yan stood planted like an unyielding guardian statue at the head of the iron stairs, his hawk-like gaze glaring into the darkness below to guard the children's final perimeter.

"Qingqing, look here! How's the number three bearing housing?" A-Xin asked with bated breath.

Sucking in a sharp lungful of wet air, Qingqing forced her numb, stiff fingers to clamp firmly around Dr. Li's specialized titanium left-handed reverse micro-wrench.

Guided by the flashlight's intense beam, she saw the full catastrophe inside Teacher Gao's primary power transmission chamber.

This was the heart of all Teacher Gao's superhuman strength, where the core planetary gear train was supposed to mesh in seamless harmony; right at its center, the crucial third planetary gear was conspicuously absent.

Because it had endured the extreme thirty-eight-thousand-newton-meter torque of pushing the coaster train, the planetary bearing seat that secured the third gear had suffered micron-level thermal warping under the dual shocks of incandescent friction and instant chilling, snapping shut like a stubborn iron clam and pinching the central drive spindle tight.

"The bearing housing suffered thermal binding..." Qingqing gritted her teeth, sliding the thin titanium wrench into the cramped chassis slit. "If we don't back off this warped alloy locking tab two point five millimeters counter-clockwise, the gear won't even fit in!"

"Then turn it, fast!" Old Wu bellowed over the howl of the storm.

"I can't just wrench it! Dr. Li warned that this is an aerospace-grade left-handed reverse thread!" Qingqing's eyes rimmed with red, veins bulging along the backs of her trembling hands from intense strain. "Ordinary tools can't bite into the reverse groove! And... without that dedicated reverse locknut, even if I pry the bearing seat open and shove the gear in, the moment he reboots at thirty thousand revolutions per minute, centrifugal force will instantly fling the gear loose and completely blow up the core motherboard!"

On the holographic projection, the countdown digits ticked relentlessly downward: 【08 minutes 15 seconds】.

"The locknut... where did that dropped locknut go?!" A-Xin's heart hammered against his ribs as he whipped around toward the pitch-black, terrifying steel abyss beyond the platform edge.

Beneath the coaster tracks lay thirty meters of tangled steel girder trusses, where the ferocious storm transformed crisscrossing pipes into countless plunging waterfalls, opening a yawning chasm like a beast waiting to swallow everything whole.

On such an ink-black, stormy night, trying to locate an all-black nut the size of a fingernail among thousands of slick, rusted girders was far more hopeless than searching for a needle in the open sea.

"Squeak-squeak——!!"

Right then, a sharp, fiercely resolute chirp sliced through the roar of the tempest.

Out on a sloping tubular rail at the outermost edge of the platform, a pinpoint of gold darted through the blinding deluge.

It was Liu-Liu!

The palm-sized AI pet was drenched until its golden fur clung in skinny, soaked ribbons, but its slender bionic antenna tail blazed with an unprecedented, high-frequency sapphire beacon against the storm!

Its four miniature electromagnetic suction paws alternated with blistering speed along the slick, vertical steel pipes, its tiny nose twitching madly against the wind to lock onto the unique, trace chemical scent of aviation-grade synthetic ester lubricant.

"Liu-Liu! Be careful!" Lin Yunyun gasped, covering her mouth in terror.

A blinding bolt of lightning tore open the night sky, followed instantly by a deafening clap of thunder!

A violent gust sent Liu-Liu tumbling through mid-air, nearly plunging the tiny creature into the bottomless chasm below.

But with a lightning-fast flick of its tail against a steel beam, its titanium claw tips scraped a bright shower of sparks off the pipe, locking its suction grip back onto the sheer vertical drop thirty meters above ground!

The countdown display flashed an ominous crimson: 【05 minutes 29 seconds】.

"Did you find it yet... Liu-Liu?!" Sweat and rainwater mingled in Old Wu's eyes, burning bitterly as he strained forward in agony.

Suddenly, twenty meters down along the recessed rim of a horizontal girder, a faint metallic glint winked in the dark!

There it was—the custom octagonal left-handed reverse locknut, wedged precariously into the rubber vibration-damper seam between two intersecting beams, barely five millimeters from tumbling into oblivion!

Liu-Liu's golden optical lenses locked instantly onto the prize; digging all four paws into the metal, it dashed down the vertical guide-rail through the pouring rain!

It snapped its tiny jaws firmly around the cold, rigid titanium nut, whipped its tail upward and transformed into a golden streak of lightning, launching into a heart-stopping reverse ascent through the dense forest of girders!

"It got it! Liu-Liu has the locknut!" A-Xin yelled in pure ecstasy, pointing toward the superstructure.

A long-suppressed cheer erupted from the twenty-six children on the platform, tears glistening in every pair of eyes under the flashlight's beam.

Rocketing out of the dark chasm like an artillery shell, Liu-Liu executed a clean mid-air flip and landed square in Qingqing's outstretched palm!

Shivering from head to tail, the tiny creature parted its jaws with a sharp "clink", dropping the oil-smeared, mud-spattered black reverse locknut right onto her palm.

Liu-Liu nuzzled Qingqing's finger with its soaked head, then collapsed onto the steel deck, panting in total exhaustion as the sapphire glow on its tail dwindled into a faint breathing pulse.

"Good boy, Liu-Liu! Class 6-1 awards you a major medal of honor!" Old Wu blubbered, wiping snot and tears across his sleeve.

"Three and a half minutes left! Qingqing, it's all up to you!" A-Xin roared.

The holographic alert had escalated from a steady whine to an urgent, ear-splitting high-frequency screech: Beep, beep, beep—【03 minutes 18 seconds】!

Wiping the water from her fingers, Qingqing's gaze sharpened with an intensity nobody had ever seen in her before.

As the daughter of an auto repair shop owner, she had grown up surrounded by grease, wrenches, and gearboxes, but never in her life had she undertaken an operation where so much was on the line.

"A-Xin, give me the necklace!" Qingqing extended her right hand.

Without a moment's hesitation, A-Xin snapped the paracord necklace that had hung around his neck for an entire year.

Inscribed with 【P.E.D.A. Core-Gear #03】, the heavy, exquisite brass planetary gear gleamed with a warm, eternal golden luster amidst the storm.

It was this very gear that had tumbled across the classroom floor a year ago, kicking off their year with this eccentric teacher who heated meat buns with his fingertips and measured waistlines to prevent students from starving flat.

"Old friend, time to go home," A-Xin whispered, placing the brass gear reverently into Qingqing's palm.

Holding the titanium reverse micro-wrench in her left hand and precision magnetic tweezers in her right, Qingqing lined the brass gear up with the primary transmission slot deep inside Teacher Gao's chest.

"Reverse torque calibration... two point five millimeters counter-clockwise..." Qingqing held her breath, every muscle coiled tight.

Snap! A faint metallic click echoed as the jammed third bearing seat finally gave way under the titanium lever, springing cleanly back into its factory tolerance alignment!

"Gear seating!" Qingqing smoothly guided the brass gear onto the primary drive shaft.

Inside the frozen gearbox, the brass planetary gear meshed seamlessly with the surrounding alloy cogs, chiming with an exquisite, crystalline "clink——!"

"Reverse locknut, mounting!"

Clamping the nut with magnetic tweezers, Qingqing aligned it with the spindle threads and spun the wrench counter-clockwise three and a half rapid turns!

One final, heavy, micro-calibrated torque stroke——

"Click!"

The self-locking mechanism snapped into full engagement, torqued to perfection!

On the holographic screen, the countdown froze at: 【00 minutes 14 seconds】!

A dead, absolute silence fell over the evacuation platform.

The savage storm seemed to have been muted in an instant; none of the twenty-six children dared draw breath, the veins on Dean Zhou's hands bulged white against his iron pipe, and every gaze fixed squarely upon Teacher Gao's shut eyes.

One second... two seconds... three seconds...

Nothing happened.

Teacher Gao remained motionless and cold in the rain, his eyes like two bottomless, dark wells, stripped of all luminescence.

"Why... how could this be..." A-Xin's tears burst loose as he dropped heavily to his knees at Teacher Gao's feet, clutching the teacher's rigid legs and shaking them frantically. "Teacher Gao! The gear is back in! The nut is tightened! Open your eyes and look at us!"

"Teacher... please wake up..." Lin Yunyun sobbed into her hands.

Hurling his giant golden bear plushie onto the wet deck, Old Wu threw his arms around Teacher Gao's waist and wailed: "Teacher Gao! Didn't you say you were saving this bear for when I got a hundred on my test?! Wake up and yell at me! Order me to run laps around the track!"

The digits on the holographic screen ticked over to 【00 minutes 00 seconds】.

Residual capacitor charge: zero.

Slumping limply into the muddy puddle, the wrench slipped from Qingqing's grasp as tears tracked silently down her wet cheeks.

Was it... truly too late?

Just as despair was about to swallow everyone like an incoming tide——

"HUMMMMMMMMM——"

An intensely deep, rich mechanical resonance suddenly thrummed from deep within Teacher Gao's tattered suit!

Immediately following, the rain-slicked honeycomb cooling fan on his chest produced a faint rustle of friction, then began spinning furiously, accelerating from a slow hum to a blistering blur!

Whoosh——!

A plume of clean, white steam laced with the warm scent of light machine oil billowed from Teacher Gao's chest cavity, instantly dispelling the biting chill of the rain around them!

"BEEP————"

A crystalline, ethereal chime—like a golden dawn beam piercing through storm clouds—rang out melodiously against the thunderous sky!

Behind those spiderweb-cracked glasses perched upon Teacher Gao's nose——

Deep within his dark, vacant optical sockets, two faint amber pinpricks suddenly ignited!

The light flickered, calibrated, and rearranged...

Within zero point five seconds, two beams of pure, warm, dazzling golden-cyan superconducting light blazed into radiant bloom across Teacher Gao's eyes!

The rigid neck joint beneath his collar released with a series of precision gear clicks; his chin tilted up, and his head inclined slowly at a standard rate of fifteen degrees per second.

Those newly rekindled eyes swept gently across Qingqing kneeling in the mud, across A-Xin weeping at his knees, across Old Wu whose face was covered in tears and snot, and across every single flushed, ecstatic, awe-struck face gathered around him.

Rainwater trickled down Teacher Gao's chiseled cheekbones; across that austere face that rarely betrayed emotion, his vocal synthesizer hummed with that familiar, deadpan, slightly metallic baritone:

"System self-diagnostic complete. Primary power grid restored to one hundred percent safe standby status."

Nudging his fractured black frames up his nose, Teacher Gao locked his gaze precisely onto A-Xin, who was hiccuping through his tears, and spoke with calm, unruffled serenity:

"Student Lu Lixin. According to meteorological database analysis, current precipitation at this site is sixty-five millimeters per hour, relative humidity ninety-eight point seven percent, and sensory temperature twelve point three degrees Celsius."

"Under conditions lacking certified hooded rain gear, your act of secreting high concentrations of sodium chloride fluid from your ocular glands and pressing your cranium directly against your homeroom teacher's lower garment fabric will, by my calculations, increase the moisture expansion rate of said textile fibers by forty-two percent."

Pausing slightly, Teacher Gao turned his gaze slowly toward Old Wu and Qingqing, the corner of his lips twitching imperceptibly upward by zero point zero zero one millimeters:

"Furthermore, Student Wu Chenkai, the spare fresh pork bun stowed inside your trouser pocket has been compressed into a flat solid measuring one point two centimeters in thickness during your recent physical impact."

"However, taking into consideration that all twenty-six students of this class have survived without being physically flattened in any manner..."

Raising that liquid-silver-clad right hand—scorched and exposed, yet still poised, long, and strong—Teacher Gao rested his palm gently atop A-Xin's drenched head, radiating a deeply comforting warmth through his fingertips:

"This field-trip emergency drill... for all members of Class 6-1, is hereby evaluated as: Passed."

"WAAAAAAAAAAAHHHHH——!!!"

After a stunned heartbeat of silence, the entire thirty-meter evacuation platform erupted in thunderous roars of laughter, cheers, and joyous weeping!

Unable to hold back for another second, all twenty-six children swarmed forward like fledglings returning to the nest, throwing themselves into Teacher Gao's warm, steam-scented embrace!

With his back turned to the celebrating class, Dean Zhou Yan dragged his sleeve across his face—wiping away what might have been rain or might have been tears—slammed the heavy iron pipe down onto the deck, and broke into a grin that looked uglier than crying, but shone with unutterable relief.

High above in the night sky, the thunderclouds quietly parted, letting a beam of pure, gentle moonlight filter through the clearing storm to bathe every single soul of Luyang Elementary School's Class 6-1.
