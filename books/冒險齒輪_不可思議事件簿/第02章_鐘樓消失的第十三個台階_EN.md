# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 2: The Vanishing Thirteenth Step of the Clock Tower

On a rainy night, stormwater streamed down the stone channels of Luyang Elementary's old clock tower like miniature waterfalls, as thunder cracked among dark clouds.

Patrol Officer Qin Weiguo wiped rainwater from his face. His flashlight beam suddenly picked up a small, cloaked figure darting stealthily into the spiral stone staircase.

"Freeze! Who's there!" Officer Qin drew his baton and gave chase in large strides.

Built during the early 1900s, this ancient clock tower had exactly twelve stone steps leading to a sealed second-floor storage vault.

However, just as Officer Qin counted to the twelfth step and lunged forward to grab the shadow, his foot plunged into empty air—a "thirteenth step" that had never existed before had materialized in the dark!

"Crash!" The stone slab flipped downwards like a trapdoor. Losing his balance, Officer Qin tumbled three meters down into a damp underground cellar with a yell.

By the time he scrambled up and shone his light overhead, the trapdoor had closed flush with the floor, and the mysterious figure had vanished into thin air.

The following morning, limping and bruised, Officer Qin arrived at Class 6-2 to seek out Ian, Aoi, and Sparky, who had gained fame from the music room affair.

"There truly was a thirteenth step!" Officer Qin rubbed his bruised knee with conviction. "I've patrolled here for eight years and walked those stairs every day. I could count twelve steps with my eyes closed!"

"The physical count of stairs does not multiply out of nowhere unless space folds, or..." Ian remarked while updating his notebook, "a hidden mechanism is triggered under specific conditions."

The trio accompanied Officer Qin to the staircase. In broad daylight, the stone was dry and clean. Everyone counted again and again—precisely twelve steps, no more, no less.

Sparky crouched before the twelfth step, using his vernier caliper to gauge the gaps: "Curious! The gap around step twelve is a full three centimeters wide, stuffed with black waterproof rubber gaskets!"

Aoi struck her resonance tuning fork against the stone wall: "Hummm—"

"The wall behind this is hollow, and I hear rushing water churning within," Aoi listened intently.

Ian pulled down his brass monocle, switching to polarized micro-magnification: "Look at the base of the wall. There are distinct metallic scrape marks worn down by high-pressure gears."

"Officer Qin, was it pouring torrential rain at the time of the incident last night?" Ian asked.

"Indeed! It rained buckets, and the drainage gutters on the tower roof were overflowing!" Qin recalled.

Ian's eyes lit up: "Sparky, check the exterior rainwater pipes on the clock tower."

Sparky complied immediately, deploying Spiky the clockwork hedgehog up the facade drainpipe. Real-time footage soon displayed a large brass collection funnel fitted with a weighted balance valve inside the conduit!

"The mystery is resolved," Ian pointed to a cross-sectional sketch of the foundation. "This is an ingenious 'hydraulic gravity rainwater trapdoor'!"

During dry weather, counterweights lock the hidden step flush into the stone masonry, leaving only the normal twelve steps visible.

During torrential rain, however, excess water funnels into the cistern. Once critical mass is reached, it trips the gear levers, thrusting out the thirteenth step while simultaneously arming the trip-plate to collapse upon foot pressure!

"Then how did the intruder know the mechanism and escape unharmed?" Aoi asked.

"Because the intruder knew the threshold trigger weight," Ian bent down, collecting a strand of silver filament and peppermint scent residue from the edge.

Just then, a burglar hiding deep in the cellar attempting to smuggle antique clockwork artifacts realized his trap had been exposed and dashed for a window, only to be tackled by Officer Qin!

The legend of the 'Thirteenth Step' was conquered, and the agency notched its second triumphant case in their logbook.

[Detective Case Notes]
- Impossible Phenomenon: A twelve-step stone staircase miraculously gains a "thirteenth step" in heavy rain; stepping on it causes the floor to collapse into a cellar while the suspect vanishes.
- Key Clues: Three-centimeter gap with rubber seals, sound of running water behind stone wall, exterior collection funnel with counterweight valves.
- STEM Science Revealed: Gravitational accumulation of rainwater converted into mechanical force (Archimedes buoyancy & lever counterweight), actuating a hydraulic spring-loaded trapdoor.
