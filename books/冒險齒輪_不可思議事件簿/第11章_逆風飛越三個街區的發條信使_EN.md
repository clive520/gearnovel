# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 11: The Clockwork Messenger Flying Three Blocks Upwind

At sunset in whistling gusts, Luyang Port's grandest monument—an eighty-meter gantry crane—stood stark against the breakwater.

As dusk descended, dockworkers and residents strolling along the seaside halted in their tracks.

Perched atop the crane's dizzying framework was the dark-dusted silhouette—the Clockwork Magician had returned!

"Look up there! The thief is on the crane!" Crowds shouted as sirens wailed along the quay.

From his duster, the Magician produced a silver clockwork albatross, casting it into the breeze. Spreading metallic wings, it took flight into the sky!

An impossible spectacle unfolded—a force-seven maritime headwind was blowing fiercely onshore, yet this metal-and-spring albatross did not plunge. Instead, it banked gracefully against the gale, climbing smoothly into the slipstream!

Beating upwind over three full blocks and spanning eight hundred meters across the docks, the clockwork bird sailed like an avian myth, homing straight toward the distant clock tower before spiraling down to alight neatly upon a stone bollard right before Ian!

Folding its wings with a musical gear chime, the bird released an embossed brass cylinder from its beak.

"Flying eight hundred meters upwind against gale-force gusts?! That completely defies basic aerodynamics!" Officer Qin gasped as he caught up.

Ian lifted the mechanical bird with a handkerchief, allowing Sparky to unscrew the dorsal housing with miniature jewelers' drivers.

"Incredible... the craftsmanship inside is astonishing!" Sparky breathed.

Aoi tapped the wing spars with her tuning fork: "The skeletal frame is forged of shape-memory nitinol, coated with micro-piezoelectric ceramic film!"

Ian pointed into the fuselage: "This is no crude glider; it is a bio-mimetic dynamic soarer equipped with geomagnetic micro-thrust guidance!"

"First, the wing camber reproduces the Bernoulli airfoil of pelagic albatrosses, turning strong head-on winds into massive vertical lift!"

"Second, a miniature liquefied CO2 micro-thruster delivers controlled bursts into the teeth of the gale, while piezoelectric wingtips adjust their angle of attack to execute 'dynamic soaring'—harnessing wind shear gradients for sustained energy!"

"The exact aerodynamic secret that allows real albatrosses to sail for thousands of leagues across stormy oceans without flapping!" Ian concluded.

Unscrewing the brass capsule, a punched parchment starmap slid out, bearing twelve precise gear apertures and a cursive note on the reverse:

"To My Worthy Opponents:
Your intellect meshes as cleanly as hardened clockwork.
Tomorrow at midnight, within Luyang's beating heart—the Golden Clock Tower.
There, I shall cause the time of this entire town to rewind by three full hours.
This is no mere trick; this is the wheel of destiny.
I await your arrival."

"Rewinding the entire town's time... by three hours?!" Sparky and Aoi cried in unison.

Ian clenched the parchment, gazing toward the towering silhouette of the Golden Clock Tower: "It seems the stage for our final showdown is set!"
