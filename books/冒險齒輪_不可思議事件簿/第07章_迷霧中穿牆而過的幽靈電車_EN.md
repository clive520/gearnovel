# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 7: The Ghost Tram That Passed Through Walls in the Fog

Under a heavy sea fog at midnight, the abandoned steam rail terminal of Luyang's old port loomed like a slumbering iron leviathan.

Dockwatch Old Chen strolled alongside the rusted rail tracks carrying a hurricane lantern. The line had been retired thirty years prior, ending at a massive, double-layered, heavily rusted security roller shutter door.

Without warning, an ear-splitting, muffled steam locomotive whistle tore violently through the fog!

Chen shuddered with terror as a vintage rail carriage gleaming with ghostly green luminescence burst through the gloom, its steel flanges spraying sparks against the rails!

"Good heavens! There is no electricity on this line, and no locomotive!" Chen shrieked, throwing himself into the roadside ballast gravel.

Without slowing down, the green-lit carriage barreled straight toward the locked iron roller shutter!

Yet the anticipated catastrophic impact never came—the carriage sliced cleanly "through" the solid iron door like a phantom apparition, vanishing entirely into the concrete cul-de-sac beyond!

By dawn, Officer Qin and his unit had secured the terminal. The massive shutter remained padlocked, its rusted chains undisturbed.

"A phantom train passing through solid walls... Could this be another trick of that 'Clockwork Magician'?" Officer Qin wiped cold sweat, looking to Ian, Aoi, and Sparky.

Ian crouched near the steel rails, donning his brass monocle. Swapping in the polarized spectrum filter, he revealed a microscopic film of transparent paraffin-wax gel coating the rail heads.

"Sparky, dismantle the roller drive housing above the shutter," Ian instructed calmly.

Wielding his heavy-duty ratchet wrench, Sparky stripped the dust shield away in moments. Behind it hung a newly installed high-pressure pneumatic-hydraulic winch and counterweight assembly!

Aoi tapped the shutter with her resonance tuning fork, eyes closed: "The echo is sharp and rapid! This shutter is not solid antique iron, but weathered lightweight aluminum rapid-coiling slats!"

"The ghost tram illusion is solved," Ian declared. "The carriage was no ghost, but an unpowered vintage rolling-stock chassis fitted with a kinetic gravity flywheel."

"The culprit rolled it down the harbor incline under gravitational acceleration. The body was painted with luminescent zinc sulfide powder, creating an eerie green glow in the maritime fog."

"The instant the carriage approached the barrier, a trip-lever beneath the cowcatcher struck a concealed pneumatic valve on the sleepers, triggering high-pressure air winches to shoot the shutter skyward in under 0.5 seconds!"

"The moment the carriage cleared, counterweights snapped the shutter back down so swiftly that amidst the fog and optical persistence of vision, it appeared to pass through solid metal!" Ian charted the velocity timeline.

"Where did the tram end up?" Officer Qin pressed.

Ian gestured to the mountain of wooden cargo crates in the cul-de-sac: "Behind those stacks sits a 30-degree turntable leading into an underground coal transfer shaft!"

Qin ordered a raid into the tunnel, recovering the modified carriage. Inside lay an embossed card bearing a gear mark—the Magician's first maritime illusion had fallen before the detectives!
