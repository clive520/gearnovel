# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 5: Trackless Snow and the Locked-Room Footprints

If the preceding mysteries occurred under sunlight or in bustling halls, the fifth case presented an absolute masterclass in the classic "trackless snow locked-room."

A rare December blizzard blanketed Luyang in pure white. Following the tempest, the open lawn surrounding the elementary school gymnasium was covered in fifteen centimeters of untouched snow, pristine as a blank sheet of rice paper.

At seven in the morning, physical education teacher Mr. Ma unlocked the gym's main doors, only to discover that the district's sole "Titanium-Alloy High-Precision Chronometer Chest" had vanished from the central equipment cage!

In a panic, Mr. Ma dialed the police. But when Officer Qin arrived with his squad, the vista before them made everyone draw a sharp breath.

Every door and window of the gymnasium had been locked securely from the inside without a trace of jimmying. More baffling still, the deep snow surrounding the facility for fifty paces was completely unbroken—not even a sparrow's claw mark marred the surface!

The sole marking was a razor-thin, dead-straight single wheel furrow extending five meters from the gym's exterior wall before abruptly ending in the open snow—no footprints, no return tracks, vanishing clean into empty space!

"Did this thief sprout wings, fly inside, and take off into the sky?" Officer Qin pushed his cap back, dumbfounded.

"If humans could fly, we wouldn't have needed airplanes," Ian knelt beside the terminus of the wheel track, wearing protective snow boot covers as he inspected the packed crystals.

Aoi tilted her head back toward the gym's twelve-meter wavy steel ceiling, her charcoal pencil sketching the three-dimensional geometry onto her art pad in swift strokes.

"Look at the skylight exhaust vent!" Aoi pointed to a narrow louvre near the roof peak. "One corner of the shutter slat is bent, and a thread of fiber is caught on the edge!"

Sparky directed Spiky up the external downspout, training the lens on the skylight frame. The monitor clearly revealed shiny silver friction scars worn into the overhead girder by taut steel cables!

Ian gave a curt nod: "The culprit never used ground access. This was an ingenious 'elevated zipline and self-retracting clockwork shuttle system.'"

Pointing to the lone furrow in the snow, Ian explained: "Last night, as the blizzard waned, the perpetrator rigged a high-tension carbon-fiber zipline from the boughs of the century-old banyan tree behind the gym straight to the skylight."

"Sliding through the vent to steal the chronometer case, he loaded it into a miniature radio-guided clockwork unicycle cart," Ian pointed to the abrupt dead end in the snow. "The cart rolled outward five meters, aligning directly below the vertical drop of the skylight cable!"

"Then why are there no returning tracks?" Officer Qin pressed urgently.

"Because it never turned back!" Sparky yelled in revelation. "A power winch mounted on the rafters hoisted the cart and chest straight into the air, pulling them up through the skylight!"

"As for the perpetrator himself," Ian pointed to severed cable remnants between the banyan branches and the roof, "he hauled himself back across the zipline to the tree, descending onto the exterior wall without ever touching a single snowflake on the ground!"

Following Ian's trajectory, Officer Qin dispatched squad cars to cordon off the alleyway behind the banyan. Sure enough, inside an abandoned van, they caught a sports equipment smuggler red-handed with the chronometer case!

"An immaculate snow-covered locked room, when confronted with physics and pulley mechanics, reduces to a straightforward geometry problem," Ian penned his final case clearance in his ledger.

[Detective Case Notes]
- Impossible Phenomenon: A locked gymnasium surrounded by 15cm of undisturbed snow is burglarized without human footprints, leaving only a single wheel line that ends mid-air.
- Key Clues: Bent skylight louvre slat, overhead cable friction abrasions, single wheel track ending directly beneath the skylight's vertical plumb line.
- STEM Science Revealed: High-tension aerial zipline + clockwork single-wheel ground transport + vertical winch hoist recovery mechanism (leaving zero ground footprints).
