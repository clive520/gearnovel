# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 13: The Non-Existent Fifth-Floor Space of the Clock Tower

The Golden Clock Tower of Luyang stood monolithic in the town square, its weathered brick spire having weathered two centuries of shifting fortunes.

Tonight, the township was draped in festival bunting. To celebrate the impending Bicentennial Jubilee, golden floodlights bathed the four immense dial faces in warm radiance.

According to municipal architectural blueprints, however, the structure comprised exactly four tiers: ground foyer, weight drop chamber, central clockwork engine room, and bell loft.

At nine in the evening, Ian, Aoi, and Sparky climbed the cast-iron spiral staircase by flashlight.

Passing through the gargantuan gearwork of the third-tier movement, they continued their ascent up the spiral steps, where the unthinkable transpired—

After winding through a 180-degree rotation, they emerged not into the open bell platform, but before a massive bronze portal set with thirteen interlocking clockwork escapements!

Pushing the heavy valves aside, a six-meter-tall circular vault opened before them, filled with antique armillary spheres, brass star-charts, and heavy hydraulic linkages!

"Where... where are we? The blueprints clearly indicate only four floors! How can there be an entire 'Fifth Floor' out of thin air?!" Sparky shone his beam about, eyes round as saucers.

Aoi struck her resonance tuning fork against the bronze flagstones, listening intently: "The resonance is deep and echoing... Beneath our soles, subterranean rapids churn fiercely, as if the tower were anchored above a roaring underground river!"

Ian donned his brass monocle, switching to polarized rangefinding prisms to survey the eight stone columns and mirrored embrasures.

"Space has not dilated; this is a classical architectural 'Möbius intertwined spatial fold'," Ian explained with quiet composure.

"A Möbius spatial fold?" Sparky scratched his temple.

Ian pointed down the spiral shaft: "The staircase we climbed is not a single spiral, but an interlocking double-helix flight (Chambord Double-Helix Principle)! Step pressure on specific treads secretly tilted the descent angle by thirty degrees, funneling us into the sealed hollow mezzanine between tiers three and four—originally engineered as the weight-drop shaft!"

"In other words, this chamber has slumbered inside the tower's heart for two centuries!" Aoi marvelled, brushing the console at the chamber's core.

Dominating the hall stood a three-meter-tall mechanical command console bearing twelve circular receptacles surrounded by engraved sluice gate relief maps.

"Look at the gear teeth module specs," Ian cross-checked his field notes. "They match the exact hub tooth counts of all twelve antiquities collected by the Magician across the school, auction, and docks!"

"So... Oliver orchestrated those breathtaking spectacles solely to gather the twelve key gears for this very console!" Sparky realized with a gasp.

At that moment, a heavy subterranean metallic clack shuddered through the floorboards—the final countdown had begun!
