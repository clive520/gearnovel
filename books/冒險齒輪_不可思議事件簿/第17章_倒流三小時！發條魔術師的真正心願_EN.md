# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 17: Rewinding Three Hours! The Clockwork Magician's True Wish

At 11:59 PM, the entire Golden Clock Tower shuddered violently, as though an earthquake were tearing through the bedrock!

Amidst rain and wind, the Clockwork Magician stood upon the revolving gantry and unclasped his mask, revealing a determined young face—the fourth-generation descendant of Master Oliver, who co-engineered the tower two centuries ago!

"Mr. Oliver, spending months recovering the twelve historic gears and staging impossible wonders was never about theft," Ian shouted over the roaring wind.

"Ian, look at the manometer on the bedrock casing!" Aoi cried, pointing to the redlining gauge. "Subterranean hydrostatic pressure has eclipsed the two-hundred-year flood threshold! In three minutes, the surging dark river will liquefy the town's foundations, drowning Luyang beneath a deluge!"

"Precisely," Oliver spoke with fierce conviction. "Two centuries ago, my ancestor built a gravitational floodgate deep beneath the foundation. But to trip those hundred-ton flood gates requires a counter-torque of 100,000 Newton-meters delivered from the main arbor!"

"In other words, the ten-ton planetary movement must run in reverse, rewinding time by three full hours!" Oliver slammed the Golden Prime Drive Gear into the final console receptacle!

"RUMBLE—!"

As all twelve gears meshed, brilliant golden flashes radiated across the dials! As midnight chimed, the immense bronze clock hands on the exterior faces began spinning furiously to the left before the gaze of tens of thousands in the plaza!

Eleven o'clock... ten o'clock... nine o'clock!

"Time... time is rewinding by three hours!" The township erupted in deafening roars of amazement.

Not chronos-bending magic, but pure, breathtaking mechanical physics!

The epicyclic differential cluster spun in reverse, channeling gargantuan mechanical torque straight down the core shaft into the abyss!

"It's jammed!" Sparky shrieked. "The final reduction gear is bound by two centuries of corrosion at the nine-o'clock index; torque is insufficient to breach the seal!"

"All together!" Ian cast aside his notes, throwing his entire weight onto the manual emergency lever!

Aoi and Sparky slammed onto the bar beside him. Oliver gripped the iron lever with clenched teeth, their four hands locked together with straining muscles!

"There is no puzzle in this world that cannot be cracked, and no gear that cannot be turned—!" Ian bellowed into the tempest!

"CLACK—SNAP!"

With a thunderous, triumphant metallic clench, the seized differential broke free!

Deep beneath the stones, a primeval rumble shook the earth as the hundred-ton sluice gates opened wide! Roaring floodwaters cascaded into the storm drain network, surging harmlessly into the open sea, and street flood levels plummeted in minutes!

The town was saved! Across three rewound hours of miraculous mechanics, Luyang had cheated catastrophe!
