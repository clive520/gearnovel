# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 8: The Auction Pendulum Frozen for Three Seconds

Inside the gilded dome hall of Luyang Maritime Auction House, prominent guests mingled amid stringent security.

Within the bulletproof glass display pedestal stood the evening's crowning treasure—the 'Aquamarine Clockwork Escapement Fork.' The beating heart of an 18th-century marine chronometer, it was crowned with a mesmerizing deep-blue sapphire.

Beside the case stood a two-meter-tall antique grandfather clock, its hefty brass pendulum swinging faithfully once per second.

At eight sharp, just as the auctioneer raised his gavel, the crystal chandeliers overhead began pulsing violently with dazzling white bursts!

The intense stroboscopic strobe created severe visual vertigo. In that precise second, an impossible wonder occurred—

The grandfather clock's pendulum, swinging to its rightmost crest, locked stock-still in mid-air with a sharp snap!

Time seemed to freeze. Defying gravity, the pendulum hung suspended motionless in empty air for three full seconds!

"Heavens above! The pendulum is frozen in space!" Guests shrieked in bewilderment.

Three seconds later, the chandeliers settled, and the pendulum resumed its rhythm, but the display glass had been silently breached by a circular cut—the aquamarine escapement fork was gone!

"The Clockwork Magician struck again!" The auction director collapsed in dismay.

Ian, serving as special security advisor, cordoned off the dais. Aoi tested the clock case with her tuning fork.

"Intense electromagnetic excitation residue!" Aoi exclaimed. "Fierce thermal radiation radiates from beneath the flagstones!"

Ian adjusted his brass monocle, switching to infrared thermal imaging. Sure enough, beneath the clock, a massive circular thermal bloom stained the granite flooring!

"Sparky, lift this tile," Ian directed.

Using vacuum suction cups and a pry bar, Sparky raised the granite slab to reveal a heavy copper coil solenoid beneath!

"A 'Superconducting Electromagnetic Pulse Lock Mechanism'!" Ian examined the coil. "The clock's brass bob was secretly bored out and fitted with a high-permeability neodymium magnetic core."

"At the peak of the pendulum's swing, where kinetic energy shifts to potential energy and instantaneous velocity reaches zero, a sudden high-voltage jolt surged through the coil, arresting the magnetic bob in mid-air!"

"And the flickering chandeliers?" Aoi asked.

"A tailor-made 'stroboscopic visual illusion'," Ian indicated the light fixtures. "Flashing at thirty hertz, it disrupts the regeneration of rhodopsin in human retinas, imposing three to five seconds of optical afterimage persistence!"

"Under cover of that collective visual freeze, the thief sliced the glass with an ultrasonic diamond wheel and extracted the jewel!"

"A coil this large requires enormous capacitor storage—he cannot be far!" Sparky traced the heavy gauge power feeder.

Following the cables down into the subterranean electrical vault, they discovered a mobile diesel generator with its manifold still piping hot.

The Magician's second grand illusion was cracked wide open, leaving behind a brass cog engraved with a lighthouse motif—pointing directly toward their next showdown!
