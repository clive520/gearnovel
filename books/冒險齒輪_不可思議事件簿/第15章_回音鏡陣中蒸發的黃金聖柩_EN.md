# Chapter 15: The Golden Sarcophagus That Vanished in the Hall of Echoing Mirrors

Beyond the threshold of the Celestial Pivot Gate, at a depth of forty-five meters beneath the ancient bedrock, the atmosphere was dense with an icy, sanctified aura of oxidized bell-metal. Piercing the petrified subterranean mists, the incisive beams of their polarizing torches illuminated a breathtaking architectural wonder: The Whispering Basilica of Mirrors. Stretching sixty meters in length and forty meters across, its vaulted ceiling was paved with interlocking blocks of polished white marble forming a continuous prolate ellipsoid. Embedded along the perimeter walls were hundreds of mirror-polished concave bronze reflectors rising three meters high, scattering the electric beams into thousands of intersecting golden rays like a subterranean celestial palace of light and metal.

Yet when their eyes converged upon the central altar—a three-meter-high dais of black polished basalt ascended by nine ceremonial tiers—their collective breath locked in their throats. According to imperial archives and the confiscated manual, this dais was the sanctum sanctorum of the Ming Dynasty's imperial mechanicians, designed to enshrine the very soul of the underground astronomical clock: the four-ton sarcophagus of Master He, forged from solid gold and interlocked alloy steels. Yet resting atop the altar now was nothing—the platform was starkly, impossibly empty!

The white marble surface of the altar was immaculate, bearing not a scratch of dragging friction nor the indentations of hauling cables. The beam of Leo's torch passed unimpeded through the empty air directly above the dais, striking the concave bronze mirrors on the far wall without casting a shadow or suffering refractive dispersion. A four-ton monument of solid gold and bronze, the size of a steam coach, had evaporated into thin air within a hermetically sealed chamber deep in the earth!

"Evaporated... Four tons of solid gold truly evaporated?!" Toby bounded up the nine basalt steps, clutching Piqiu. He waved his arms frantically across the empty altar, grasping at thin air, his hands encountering only chill, damp emptiness. "Leo! Emma! There is literally nothing here! It's not a glass coat—the space itself is vacant! Could the Counter-Clock Council have chanted an incantation and teleported four tons of gold into the fourth dimension?!"

The instant Toby shouted, an uncanny phantom manifested! Ten meters ahead of the dais, the air rippled like disturbed water in the electric beam, and there, suspended in mid-air, a shimmering mirage of the colossal golden sarcophagus—etched with the twenty-four zodiac constellations—appeared out of nothingness! Yet when Toby lunged forward to embrace the golden metal, his body passed cleanly through the apparition like a ghost drifting through fog, grasping only bitter drafts of subterranean air!

"Look at the ceiling! Another one! And in the alcove!" Emma swept her beam across the chamber. Defying all perception, within the focal planes of the hundreds of concave bronze mirrors, seven or eight identical golden sarcophagi hovered simultaneously in different orientations! One hung inverted from the marble dome; another leaned within the colonnade shadows; and from their depths echoed the metronomic ticking of precision escapements: 'Tick... tick... tick...'

Simultaneously, from the depths of the bronze mirrors whispered a disembodied, icy voice, shifting unpredictably from left to right: "Intruders... you have stumbled into the dead end of chronological eternity... The master's reliquary has dissolved into the void, and your mortal frames shall feed the ocean of mercury..." The voice hissed directly against their ears, yet when they spun around, only cold, barren stone greeted them!

"Gurgle... gurgle..." As the whisper faded, subterranean sluice gates around the perimeter grated open, disgorging torrents of dense, viscous, mirror-bright liquid metal! Tons of pure elemental mercury surged along the annular trenches, sending plumes of sweet, lethal metallic vapors curling into the air!

"Catastrophe! A mercury siphon inundation mechanism!" Emma shouted through the gloom, snapping activated-carbon respirators onto Leo and Toby. "Mercury displaces 13.6 times the mass of water, and its vapors attack the central nervous system! This chamber forms a closed depression; within fifteen minutes, forty tons of liquid mercury will drown the floor, transforming this vault into an inescapable chamber of toxic death!"

"The master shut-off valve for the mercury siphon is located within the plinth of the true golden sarcophagus!" Leo secured his respirator, his eyes razor-sharp through the glass lenses. "Locate the true physical monument, and we can throw the emergency bypass! Toby, stay focused! Every illusion and whisper within this basilica obeys classical optics and geometrical acoustics!"

Leo directed his beam toward the white marble canopy: "Emma, calculate the major and minor semiaxes of this dome! This ceiling is no spherical cupola—it is an exact prolate ellipsoid of revolution!"

Emma fired laser rangefinder pulses across both axes. Reading the digital telemetry, she declared: "Major axis: 60.0 meters; minor axis: 40.0 meters! By the canonical ellipse formula x-squared over a-squared plus y-squared over b-squared equals one, this prolate ellipsoid possesses twin geometrical acoustic foci, F1 and F2, hovering exactly 1.5 meters above the floor!"

"That is the physical key to the ghostly whispers: The Whispering Gallery Elliptical Effect!" Leo pointed toward an ornamental basalt dragon pillar at the eastern perimeter. "By the fundamental reflective geometry of the ellipse: any acoustic wave emitted from focus F1, regardless of its departure angle across the marble dome, reflects along paths of identical distance and **converges with zero dispersion at focus F2!**"

Leo indicated the flags beneath their boots: "Where we stood moments ago is precisely focus F2! The two council sentries are not invisible phantoms—they are crouching sixty meters away behind the pillar at focus F1! They need only whisper against the polished stone, and the dome gathers and focuses the acoustic energy directly into our eardrums!"

"Two cowards hiding in the shadows playing ghosts!" Toby snarled, whipping a high-tension wrist-rocket slingshot from his kit and loading a steel marble filled with fluorescent dye. "Piqiu, zero in on focus F1 with acoustic triangulation! Let's paint those phantoms bright neon!"

Piqiu's copper whiskers locked onto the faint respiratory signature sixty meters east; its dorsal laser pointer aligned instantly! Toby released the sling: the steel pellet hurtled across the vault like a shooting star, detonating against the shadow behind the pillar! "CRACK!" A brilliant splash of luminous neon paint erupted across the stone, followed by two yelps of startled agony!

Two sentries in black hooded smocks and gas masks, coated in neon green dye, scrambled out from the shadows coughing and fumbling for service revolvers. But Toby's follow-up smoke pellets burst at their feet, blinding and disorienting the guards until they collapsed gasping onto the flagstones, thoroughly neutralized!

"Acoustic haunting solved!" Toby dusted his hands, but then looked down: the mercury tide had already submerged the first tier of the basalt dais, the liquid metal shimmering with deadly chill. "Yet Leo, where is the true golden sarcophagus? The altar is vacant, the floating illusions are intangible, and the hall has solid walls—four tons of physical alloy cannot simply vanish from existence!"

"It never vanished—it has stood directly before us from the start!" Leo's gaze dropped from the empty altar to the ring of flagstones encircling its base, where bizarre optical caustic fringes flickered. "Emma, uncase the six hyperbolic quartz lenses we confiscated in the previous chamber!"

Emma withdrew the six teardrop quartz hyperbolic lenses. Raising the first lens into the electric beam, the transmitted light warped into an extraordinary hyperbolic caustic curve, twisting like a Möbius ribbon across the basalt!

"This is a Confocal Catoptric Cloaking Array!" Emma gasped, her optical intellect cutting through the illusion. "Those hundreds of concave bronze mirrors around the walls are not ornamental—they form an optically coupled confocal hyperbolic-parabolic ray-folding system aligned with this central dais!"

Emma sketched ray trajectories at lightning speed: "By the principles of transformation optics: light rays penetrating this array do not strike the central altar; they are smoothly bent by the hyperbolic curvature of the mirrors around the altar's volume! The rays flow around the monument like water around an island, and recombine seamlessly behind it!"

"Which means," Leo declared, "when we stand here looking at the altar, the 'empty space' we perceive is not air, but the image of the rear white marble wall folded seamlessly around the altar by the catoptric cloaking array! The rays bypass the monument entirely, rendering it completely invisible to sight!"

"Yet Leo!" Toby cried in confusion. "I ran onto the dais and swung my arms through the air! I felt nothing but empty draft! If the sarcophagus is physically sitting there, shouldn't I have smashed my skull into four tons of gold?!"

"Because this engineering feat couples catoptric cloaking with a subterranean hydraulic elevator!" Leo struck the edge of the marble slab with the butt of his torch. "Toby, what you tread upon was not the true stone dais! It was a transparent diaphragm of high-tensile natural mica sheets! The monument never vanished—it rests two meters directly beneath your boots!"

"The mercury tide has reached the third tier! Barely five minutes remain!" Emma warned, watching the silver torrent surge toward the dais. "Mica diaphragms cannot withstand the hydrostatic head of liquid mercury! If the shaft floods, the master gearing inside the sarcophagus will be destroyed!"

"Toby, insert the six hyperbolic lenses into the hexagonal brass sockets around the altar rim!" Leo commanded. "Those six lenses were no casual spares—they are the Phase-Reversal Optical Key engineered by the Ming artificers to neutralize the cloaking array!"

Toby and Piqiu moved in seamless coordination around the hexagonal perimeter! Toby slotted the crystal lenses into the six concealed brass sockets; while Piqiu used its miniature claws to trip the spring detents beneath each lens, unleashing six crystalline clicks in rapid succession!

The instant the sixth lens locked home, six brilliant golden beams erupted from their facets, weaving a three-dimensional hexagram of coherent light above the altar! By optical interference, the exact pi phase shift introduced by the lenses cancelled the hyperbolic deflection field in a microsecond!

The optical mirage across the dais shattered like brittle ice! The transparent mica diaphragm retracted along bronze tracks, unveiling the vertical stone shaft supported by four colossal hydraulic rams!

"The shaft is open! The elevation lever is on the third bronze pylon!" Emma shouted, spotting a heavy lever carved with imperial taotie reliefs.

"Toby, haul with me!" Leo lunged forward, gripping the freezing bronze lever. Toby threw his entire weight onto the bar beside him; planting their boots against the granite curb, they threw their combined strength into the pull: "Heave!"

"ROARRRRR—!" Ancient counterweighted hydraulic rams thundered to life within the bedrock! Driven by falling lead weights, four bronze pistons rose steadily from the deep, elevating a monument of imperial majesty—The Golden Sarcophagus of Master He—surging upward into the light!

Measuring four meters long and nearly two meters tall, the sarcophagus was forged from hammered gold and case-hardened alloy steels! Its surface was chiseled with 3,600 astrological deities, the twenty-eight lunar mansions, and micrometer-scale gear teeth profiles. At its four corners, four golden vermilion birds held half-meter rotating differential starwheels in their beaks, radiating breathtaking mechanical harmony!

The mercury flood, barely ten centimeters from overtopping the platform, ground to a halt with a shuddering hydraulic slam, before draining harmlessly into subterranean reservoirs!

"The mercury is draining! It's gone!" Toby collapsed onto the basalt steps, tearing off his respirator to gulp fresh air, his coat drenched in cold sweat. "Two inches more... and we would have been canned like gilded sardines!"

Piqiu let out a long mechanical sigh, its gears spinning at a leisurely cadence as it waddled to Toby's knees, resting its brass forepaws on his trousers, prompting an affectionate laugh from the exhausted detectives.

Leo stepped to the front of the sarcophagus, resting his palm upon the gold taiji cipher plate. At its center was a recessed socket comprising three concentric brass pinions. Beware!'

The flagstones heaved; geysers of superheated white vapor hissed from the mortar seams, accompanied by the shriek of tens of thousands of tons of pressurized water crashing against iron turbine blades!

Behind their flying footsteps, the hundreds of bronze mirrors within the Basilica scattered the silhouettes of the young detectives into thousands of blazing golden rays, advancing like an invincible vanguard of science toward the titan raging in the deep!
