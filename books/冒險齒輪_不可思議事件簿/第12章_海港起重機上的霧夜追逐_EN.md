# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 12: The Foggy Night Pursuit on the Harbor Cranes

A hundred meters above the crashing surf, atop the gantry crane's aerial catwalk, maritime gusts howled through the steel lattice.

Luyang Port was illuminated bright as noon by sweeping searchlights. Ian, Aoi, and Sparky climbed the steel service ladders, confronting the Clockwork Magician standing at the jib's outer precipice.

Still cloaked in his charcoal duster and silver gear-patterned mask, the Magician balanced lightly over the abyss.

"You pursued me all the way to the heavens, young detectives," the Magician spoke, his voice melodious and calm.

"Mr. Oliver, the curtain falls here," Ian stepped forward, his multi-spectral brass monocle gleaming cold and clear. "You took the aquamarine escapement fork, cast the holographic mirage at the lighthouse, and took the ancient chart. Yet you never damaged an artifact, and you steered your messenger directly to our feet."

"Because you are no common felon; your true horizon is tomorrow's Bicentennial Jubilee at the Golden Clock Tower!" Aoi took a pace forward, her tuning fork shivering in the headwind. "I can hear the chronometers ticking beneath your coat—not the beat of avarice, but the heavy toll of a desperate countdown!"

A soft sigh breathed behind the silver mask: "Perceptive girl... you hear the weeping of time."

"Sparky, now!" Ian barked.

Sparky slammed the chest launcher on his harness. Spiky shot forward like a bolt, its magnetic tether snagging the velvet pouch containing the aquamarine escapement from the Magician's hip!

"Clack!" Spiky reeled backward, snapping the pouch into Sparky's hands!

The Magician made no move to resist. Instead, he tipped his brim with polished elegance: "The sapphire rightfully belongs to the world; its purpose is fulfilled—I have duplicated its microscopic tooth geometry to the sub-micron."

"As for tomorrow at midnight," the Magician leaned back into the void, stepping off the edge of the sky, "the hands of Luyang Clock Tower will rewind. You shall bear witness to the true heart of this township."

"Wait! Don't jump!" Sparky shrieked.

With a crisp snap, the Magician's umbrella blossomed into a delta-wing ram-air paraglider. Boosted by micro-thermal thrust canisters, he banked through the updrafts, gliding into the rolling sea fog like a nocturnal falcon!

His voice echoed through the maritime gloom: "Gather the twelve gears before time reverses, Clockwork Detectives!"

Ian secured the returned aquamarine jewel, turning his gaze toward the monolithic silhouette of the clock tower inland.

The second act's harbor mysteries drew to a breathless close, as the ultimate trial awaited within the great bronze gears of the tower!
