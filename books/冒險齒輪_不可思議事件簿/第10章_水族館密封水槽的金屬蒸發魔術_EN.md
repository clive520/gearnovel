# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 10: The Metal Evaporation Magic in the Sealed Aquarium Tank

In the main hall of Luyang Ocean Aquarium, crowds pressed against a five-meter-tall, acrylic-walled tank containing hundreds of tons of seawater.

Suspended in the tank center by four stainless steel chains was a titanium security cage housing a 300-year-old "Abyssal Marine Navigational Gear Sphere."

Cast of solid alloy and weighing five kilograms, this historic antiquity supposedly charted ancient oceanic trench currents.

At ten in the morning, as the docent lectured visiting schoolchildren, a steady trail of microscopic bubbles rose behind a submerged driftwood ornament.

Under hundreds of watchful eyes, the gleaming bronze surface inside the locked cage began to shimmer with an uncanny liquid luster!

Within thirty seconds, the solid metal sphere softened like ice cream under the sun, collapsing into a puddle of shimmering silver liquid that dripped through the mesh into the sand below, vanishing entirely!

"The metal melted into water! The sphere evaporated before our eyes!" Screams erupted as aquarium guards locked down the exits.

"Metals have melting points of hundreds or thousands of degrees! The tank water is twenty degrees Celsius; how could metal melt in room temperature water?" The director paced in distress.

Arriving at the scene, Ian donned his monocle, switching to microscopic magnification to inspect residual silvery droplets clinging to the cage bottom.

Sparky lowered a sampling syringe into the tank, retrieving droplets of the silver liquid to run through his portable centrifuge analyzer.

"Reporting, Ian!" Sparky checked the test strips. "This is not bronze or steel, but a 'Gallium-Indium-Tin Eutectic Alloy (Galinstan)'!"

Ian nodded in approval: "Precisely! Pure gallium melts at 29.76°C, liquefying in a human palm; when combined with indium and tin as a eutectic alloy, its melting point plunges to -19°C!"

"The culprit infiltrated earlier, swapping the genuine artifact with an alloy replica stabilized by an exterior low-temperature paraffin wax seal!"

"Then how did it melt right on cue in twenty-degree water?" Aoi struck the acrylic wall with her tuning fork, sensing acoustic vibrations.

"High-frequency ultrasonic oscillation behind the driftwood!" Aoi pointed to the sunken log. "A micro-heating element is active!"

Ian pointed to the log: "A submersible ultrasonic induction heater was hidden inside. When triggered by timer, it rapidly warmed local water past 30°C, melting the wax seal and liquefying the alloy sphere instantly!"

"Where did the genuine five-kilogram bronze sphere go?" The director pleaded.

Ian mounted the gantry overhead, pointing to one of the four suspension tethers: "Of the four chains, this cable sags two centimeters lower than the others under tensile strain!"

Sparky operated the hoist to retrieve the cable. To everyone's astonishment, the true bronze sphere, coated in matte black enamel, was masquerading as an anti-sway ballast weight directly overhead!

The miraculous melting illusion fell completely before thermodynamics, prompting joyous cheers across the gallery!

- Impossible Phenomenon: A 5kg solid metal sphere inside a locked underwater cage melts into silver liquid and vanishes in room-temperature seawater.
- Key Clues: Silvery liquid residue, ultrasonic high-frequency vibrations behind submerged driftwood, one chain sagging two centimeters lower under excess load.
- STEM Science Revealed: Gallium-indium-tin low-melting eutectic alloy + ultrasonic localized induction heating + structural ballast counterweight concealment.
