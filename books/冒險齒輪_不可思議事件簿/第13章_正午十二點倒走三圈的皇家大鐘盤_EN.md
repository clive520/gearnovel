# Chapter 13: The Royal Clock Face That Ran Backwards Three Times at Noon

During the autumn equinox, the skies over the ancient capital of Luyang arched crystalline and serene, golden ginkgo leaves fluttering like delicate clockwork pinions across the cobblestone imperial avenues of the historic metropolis. Having returned triumphant from the maritime perils of Sea-Mist Harbor, the trio of the Gear Detective Agency—Leo, Emma, Toby, and Piqiu the indefatigable clockwork hedgehog perched upon a shoulder—now stood before the city's most majestic landmark. Towering for six centuries at the civic heart, marrying eastern mortise-and-tenon masonry with western horological precision, rose the octagonal seven-tiered monument: The Millennium Astronomical Clock Tower.

The crowning jewel of the edifice was the colossal Twenty-Four Solar Terms Astrolabe Dial, measuring ten meters across and embedded into the fourth-tier facade. Forged of eight tons of gilded bronze and driven by 3,600 precision alloy gearwheels revolving around a three-meter central differential sun gear, it tracked hours, minutes, and seconds while planetary gear trains modeled the celestial motions of the sun, moon, and twenty-eight lunar mansions across the zodiac. For six centuries, through the rise and fall of dynasties and centuries of storm, its gilded hands had never halted for a single second.

Yet on this noon, tens of thousands of citizens, scholars, and international travelers packed the sprawling cobblestone plaza. For one week prior, an archaic gilded proclamation had appeared mysteriously pinned to the belfry's bronze doors. An ancient, esoteric order calling itself 'The Counter-Clock Council' had audaciously prophesied that precisely at the stroke of high noon, before the gaze of one hundred thousand witnesses, they would 'reverse the celestial flow of time, commanding the six-hundred-year-old royal dial to spin backwards three full revolutions!'

"Utter lunacy! Unscientific claptrap!" Academician Zhou, Director of the Municipal Antiquities Bureau and Chief Horological Conservator, paced frantically before the observation desk, sweat beading at his temples. "The motive power of this clockwork originates from a twelve-ton lead gravity driving weight anchored in the foundations, governed by triple anti-reverse ratchet pawls and a dual-pendulum escapement. Mechanically speaking, the gear train can only engage in a clockwise progression! Unless someone can haul twelve tons of lead skyward in a millisecond, the hands cannot run backwards!"

"Physical impossibility is invariably the fertile soil where illusion and applied physics take root," Leo said calmly, peering through his brass surveyor's transit to inspect the cloud-scroll bronze bezels framing the dial. "Academician Zhou, examine the ornamental cold-cathode illumination ribbon ringing the perimeter. Those luminaires appear to be routine civic floodlights, yet their ballasts and coaxial feeds exceed the electrical specifications of ordinary xenon tubes by an order of magnitude."

Emma crouched beside the main power distribution cabinet on the ground tier, holding a portable high-frequency oscilloscope with dual high-voltage probes tapped into the shielded conduit. "Leo has hit the mark! The potential across that decorative lighting circuit spikes to eight thousand volts—not continuous AC, but a train of microsecond square-wave pulses precisely tuned to 59.5 hertz! This rapid pulsed discharge is no municipal illumination; it is a colossal stroboscopic optical field bathing the entire plaza!"

Toby leaned over the velvet-lined crystal vitrine in the third-tier public gallery, Piqiu sniffing the ornamental bronze plinth with curious twitches of its brass whiskers. "Leo, Emma, look at this! The sacred centerpiece of today's equinox gala—the legendary Wanli Double-Helix Differential Astrolabe, inherited from the ancient imperial master artificers—is secured inside this bulletproof vitrine! Rumor has it the astrolabe conceals the master cipher key to the clock tower's deepest underground vaults!"

The beryllium-copper whiskers across Piqiu's carapace snapped upright, vibrating with a sharp acoustic warning. Toby leaned close, discovering a faint trace of volatile, sweet almond-scented fluid seeping from the hairline mortar seam beneath the plinth. "What is this aroma? It smells sweet like crushed almonds, yet with an acrid, metallic tang underneath..."

Emma dashed over, catching the faint scent before her eyes narrowed with alarm: "Do not inhale! That is a cryogenic cyanoacrylate depolymerization solvent! Someone dissolved the high-tensile security adhesives anchoring this plinth minutes ago! The entire vitrine pedestal is decoupled—a minimal lateral shear could pivot the housing like a revolving door!"

"Their ultimate prize is indeed the Wanli Differential Astrolabe!" Leo's mind assembled the tactical pieces. "Weaponizing the collective cognitive paralysis and visual blindness of an impossible temporal reversal at noon to plunder the relic! It is their hallmark grand misdirection! Yet... how can they force an eight-ton mechanical dial to appear to spin backwards three full turns under the unblinking gaze of a hundred thousand spectators?"

At that exact heartbeat, the preliminary noon chimes began their countdown from the belfry's perimeter bells! "DONG—! DONG—! DONG—!" The solemn, bronze tolling echoed over the roofs of the ancient city. A breathless hush swept across the vast sea of one hundred thousand spectators; thousands of binoculars, camera lenses, and eyes converged upon the ten-meter astrolabe dial!

Eleven fifty-nine and fifty-eight seconds... fifty-nine seconds... Twelve o'clock sharp!

The microsecond the hour and minute hands fused at the top apex, the impossible spectacle erupted! The massive gilded bronze hands shuddered with a piercing metallic resonance, ground to a halt, and then—violently, impossibly—**began to whirl counter-clockwise at blinding speed!**

"Heavens above! Look at the dial! The hands are running backwards! Time is reversing!!" Shrieks of pure pandemonium ripped through the plaza! The half-ton bronze minute hand swept counter-clockwise at the dizzying rate of one revolution every twenty seconds, hurtling backwards past the Solar Terms—Winter Solstice, Frost Descent, End of Heat, Summer Solstice—spinning three full cycles in reverse! Thirty-six hours of temporal progression undone within sixty seconds!

Even more terrifying, alongside the frantic reverse rotation, an unearthly, nauseating sub-audible drone vibrated through the air! Thousands in the front rows clutched their temples in vertigo: water droplets from the civic fountain seemed to freeze and retreat into their spouts, pigeons in the sky appeared to glide in reverse, and the tolling bells sounded like an ominous phonograph recording played backwards!

"Time has truly reversed... The heavens are wrathful!" several elderly scholars stammered, their knees giving way onto the granite paving. Every municipal official, constable, and engineer on the dais stood paralyzed in existential horror, Academician Zhou slumping into his seat, his mind overwhelmed by absolute cognitive paralysis!

And in that exact instant of global stupor, a faint mechanical whisper clicked within the third-tier gallery! The bulletproof pedestal securing the Wanli Double-Helix Differential Astrolabe pivoted silently through one hundred and eighty degrees! A hand in a black velvet glove darted through a recessed wall fissure, swapping the ancient national treasure for an empty brass counterfeit in the blink of an eye!

"Hold it right there! Not on our watch!" Toby roared, having remained vigilant in the shadows. He hurled Piqiu forward like a missile: "Piqiu, alloy jaw clamps! Seize his sleeve!"

Piqiu snapped twin titanium grapples with spring-loaded ferocity, locking firmly into the hem of the intruder's midnight cape! Caught off guard by the clockwork guardian, the thief uttered a cold hiss, deploying an ultrasonic miniature blade to sever the pinned velvet, before hurling a dense magnesium flash-smoke grenade across the gallery floor!

"BANG—!" Blinding optical incandescence and acrid smoke choked the chamber. By the time Toby and the constables cleared the haze, the secret fissure had sealed flush with the masonry, the operative vanished into the labyrinthine service ducts with the true Wanli Astrolabe! Clamped within Piqiu's jaws was a torn scrap of black velvet embroidered with a luminescent silver Möbius strip: the heraldic crest of the Counter-Clock Council!

Outside, having completed three full reverse cycles, the ten-meter dial came to an abrupt halt with a deep clatter of teeth, freezing ominously at the midnight position of the previous evening. The crowd remained gripped by metaphysical terror, rumors of temporal collapse spreading like wildfire through the streets.

"The dial ran backwards... and the astrolabe is stolen..." Academician Zhou cradled his head, his voice cracking with despair. "Six centuries of horological majesty, bowed before sorcery... Can time truly be unspun?"

"Time never unspun, and those bronze hands never traveled backwards by a single millimeter!" Leo's voice rang out like iron on anvil, cutting through the panic. "Academician Zhou, lift your eyes and examine this high-speed industrial footage captured at one thousand frames per second!"

Leo patched the camera feed into the master cathode-ray tube monitor, engaging ultra-slow playback. Frame by frame, the staggering physical reality behind the miracle unveiled itself before their stunned eyes!

In the ultra-slow playback, the half-ton bronze minute hand was not spinning leftward in reverse at all—it was hurtling clockwise at the staggering velocity of fifty-nine revolutions per second (59.0 Hz) in an **extreme forward rotation**!

"H-How can this be?!" Academician Zhou gasped, clutching his glasses. "It is racing forward clockwise at blinding speed, yet to our naked eyes, it appeared to crawl counter-clockwise at twenty seconds per turn?!"

"This is one of the most celebrated phenomena in optical physics and visual neuroscience: The Stroboscopic Effect, universally known as the Wagon-Wheel Optical Illusion!" Emma declared, tracing her laser pointer along the flashing bezel lamps, her explanation razor-sharp.

Emma sketched the governing equations across the ledger: "The human eye's retinal persistence lasts roughly 0.1 seconds, giving our visual cortex an effective sampling rate. Surrounding this dial, the culprits installed xenon pulse arrays flashing at precisely 59.5 hertz (f_pulse = 59.5 Hz)!"

"When the hidden electric planetary servo accelerates the hand clockwise to 59.0 revolutions per second (f_motion = 59.0 Hz), the hand's rotational frequency is slightly lower than the flash rate!" Emma explained with brilliant clarity. "At each microsecond flash of illumination, the hand has completed just under a full 360-degree cycle! Thus, in the sequential retinal frames stitched together by the human brain, the hand appears to have moved slightly BACKWARD!"

"By the stroboscopic aliasing formula: Delta f = f_motion minus f_pulse = 59.0 minus 59.5 = -0.5 Hz!" Leo concluded with triumphant conviction. "The negative sign denotes apparent reverse motion, and an aliasing frequency of 0.5 hertz translates into precisely one reverse revolution every twenty seconds! In objective reality, the hand was hurtling clockwise; but human cognition was hijacked by stroboscopic aliasing into witnessing an unmistakable backward flight of time!"

"Merciful heavens..." Academician Zhou murmured, the scales falling from his eyes. "Stroboscopic aliasing! Exactly like observing the spokes of a speeding carriage or locomotive—the faster the wheel turns forward, the more convincingly it appears to roll backward!"

"Yet Leo, why did the thousands in the plaza experience violent vertigo, swearing the fountain water and pigeons were drifting backward too?" Toby asked, rubbing his temple. "Even up in the third tier, my chest tightened, my heart raced, and my spatial orientation scrambled!"

"Because alongside optical deception, they unleashed a covert acoustic weapon: 18.9-hertz resonant infrasound!" Emma redirected her sensors toward the pneumatic organ flues flanking the belfry. "Eighteen point nine hertz is the natural mechanical resonant frequency of the human eyeball and thoracic cavity! Infrasound at this threshold induces microscopic oscillations within the ocular vitreous humor, triggering retinal motion blur, phantom illusions of floating or reversing objects, while overstimulating the inner ear's vestibular system into severe nausea, panic, and temporal disorientation!"

"Stroboscopic optics paired with infrasonic resonance, striking the sensory nervous systems of one hundred thousand souls to orchestrate mass temporal hypnosis in broad daylight!" Leo said coldly. "And that entire grand illusion was constructed solely to shield the hand reaching for the Wanli Astrolabe!"

"Yet... what does this Counter-Clock Council intend to achieve with the Wanli Astrolabe?" Academician Zhou asked in anguish. "It has been preserved in our vaults for six hundred years! Beyond its historical value, does it possess actual mechanical power?"

Leo crouched, taking the scrap of black velvet from Piqiu's jaws. Adhering to the reverse side were grains of dark-green metallic dust. Rolling the powder between his fingertips, catching the distinct scent, Leo's gaze turned razor-sharp: "Mercury-amalgamated phosphor bronze powder. Across this ancient city, only one subterranean vault utilizes this archaic anti-wear alloy: the Water-Powered Armillary Sphere catacombs thirty meters beneath this tower!"

"The Water-Powered Armillary Sphere catacombs?!" Academician Zhou turned deathly pale. "Merciful heavens... The ancient scrolls were true?! During the Yongle reign of the Ming Dynasty, imperial artificers engineered a subterranean hydro-differential colossus deep within the bedrock—The Yongle Celestial Pivot! Legend says it is an underground mechanical heart capable of reversing the city's aqueducts and fortress locks! But its master key was lost three centuries ago... Could the Wanli Astrolabe be...?!"

"The astrolabe is no mere key—it is the topological decoder for the planetary differential gearbox!" Emma pulled up the ultra-high-resolution macro photography taken before the theft. "Look closely at the dual-helical Möbius scales along its rim: they encode three-dimensional gear transmission ratios! If slotted into the subterranean drive spindle, the entire imperial mechanism will be thrown into reverse, unleashing pressurized artesian aquifers to rupture the city's foundations!"

"This is the true endgame of the Counter-Clock Council!" Leo stood, stowing the velvet scrap with resolute defiance. "They seek no philosophical reversal of time—they seek catastrophic mechanical inversion, harnessing fluidic destruction to level six centuries of civic order!"

Toby cradled Piqiu, punching the air with defiant fury: "Clockwise, counter-clockwise, who cares what council they call themselves! We tamed the tidal colossus of Sea-Mist Harbor—we aren't backing down from sewer rats skulking in the dark! Give the word, Leo! We storm the catacombs and take back our astrolabe!"

Piqiu's gears chimed in high-frequency agreement, its copper whiskers pointing toward the heavy cast-iron portal leading into the subterranean crypts, locked onto the volatile chemical scent and magnetic signature of the fleeing saboteur.

Academician Zhou seized Leo's hands in gratitude and dread: "President Leo, the catacombs are fraught with lethal traps—gravity pitfalls, counterweighted deadfalls, and quicksand flumes sealed for six centuries! No expedition has navigated that labyrinth alive! Are you certain you wish to descend?"

"Because it is a domain of ancient mechanisms, the Gear Detective Agency is uniquely equipped to tread there," Leo smiled, unlimbering multi-gauge gear wrenches, polarizing torches, and high-tensile carbon lines from his field case. "To our eyes, there exist no fatal curses—only unanalyzed pulleys, levers, and escapements."

Emma distributed three pairs of polarized night-vision goggles: "These lenses filter stroboscopic flare, while the integrated acoustic baffles neutralize infrasonic resonance traps below ten hertz."

Toby fitted Piqiu with twin micro-tungsten rotary drill bits and high-intensity xenon headlamps: "Piqiu subterranean sapper module—fully locked and loaded!"

With a heavy rumble of brass tumblers aligning, the cast-iron portal to the crypts groaned like distant thunder, parting along iron tracks. A gust of Stygian air, laden with six centuries of petrified humidity, damp stone, and ancient tallow lubricants, rushed upward from the fathomless black stairwell like the breath of a subterranean leviathan.

Leo clicked on his high-output polarizing torch, the incisive white beam slicing through centuries of dust to reveal an octagonal double-helix stone staircase descending into the bedrock. "Move out, Gear Detective Agency! The opening campaign of Volume Three has begun!"

With calm, unyielding strides, the trio crossed the threshold into the subterranean depths. Piqiu rolled ahead, its brass claws clattering against the flagstones in an energetic rhythm like a battle hymn of deductive science.

Behind them, the ancient city of Luyang gradually regained its composure under the autumn sun. Yet in alley shadows and behind watchmakers' windows, unseen eyes watched the towers—the acolytes of the Counter-Clock Council knew that the ultimate war for the temporal order had begun in the abyss.

From the depths of the shaft resonated a rhythmic, titanic hydraulic thrum like the heartbeat of the earth itself: the respiration of the six-hundred-year-old Yongle Celestial Pivot, stirring in the darkness, awaiting the true keys of science to awaken from the dust of centuries.

"Next destination: thirty meters below—The Silent Gallery of the Inverted Gravity Pendulum!" Leo gripped his brass wrench, his eyes blazing like stars in the subterranean dark. "No matter how deep your labyrinths go, every mechanism shall bow before the sovereignty of deductive physics!"
