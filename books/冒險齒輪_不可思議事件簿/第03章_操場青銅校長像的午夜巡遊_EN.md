# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 3: The Midnight Stroll of the Bronze Principal Statue

If the first two cases merely rattled nerves, the third event that unfolded on Monday morning sent the entire school into absolute astonishment.

In the very center of the schoolyard, the eight-hundred-kilogram solid bronze statue of the founding principal, normally anchored firmly to a concrete pedestal, had "walked" twenty meters across the grounds to the entrance of the Heritage Archives!

Both hands resting upon his walking cane, facing the archive doors with solemn dignity, it looked as though the old principal had personally arisen at midnight to inspect his campus.

Even more bizarrely, along the dirt running track from the original pedestal to the archive steps lay two parallel rows of crisp bronze shoeprints, pressed half a centimeter deep into the earth at exact intervals!

"The statue came alive! The old principal's spirit has returned!" Students formed a dense ring around the scene, gasping in disbelief.

Disciplinary Director Zhou Yan rushed to the scene with his violation ledger, his face grim. Touching the ice-cold bronze forearm, he was struck momentarily speechless.

"Eight hundred kilograms of solid bronze cannot be picked up and hauled twenty meters in total silence, even by ten burly adults," Ian knelt beside the first footprint, inspecting the soil through a magnifying lens.

Aoi and Sparky pushed through the cordon. Sparky dug up a pinch of soil from the rim of a print and gasped: "Wait! There is white frost coating the earth, and it is freezing to the touch!"

"It is early summer; how could schoolyard soil be frosty?" Aoi wondered aloud, striking her resonance tuning fork against the bronze base.

"The frequency is dull, but the metal around the outer base shows an extremely uniform ring of circular abrasion," Aoi promptly relayed to Ian.

Ian pushed his brass monocle, a sharp glint illuminating his pupil: "The frost is no supernatural wonder, but an intense physical endothermic reaction—sublimating dry ice (solid carbon dioxide) absorbs immense thermal energy, condensing moisture from ambient air into frost!"

"Dry ice? Like the hovercraft principle?" Sparky realized with a jolt.

"Precisely," Ian stood and gestured along the level track. "The perpetrator attached a sealed, flexible metal skirt around the statue base, packed tightly with crushed high-purity dry ice."

"As dry ice warms, it sublimates violently into carbon dioxide gas, producing a thin yet immensely powerful high-pressure cushion within the skirt—industrial 'air bearing levitation'!" Ian explained. "The friction coefficient drops virtually to zero, allowing one or two individuals to slide an 800kg mass across smooth ground with ease!"

"Then what about these neatly stamped footprints?" Director Zhou interjected hurriedly.

Sparky chuckled, pointing beneath the rear edge of the base: "Look underneath! There is a pair of clockwork ratchet stamping dies! As the statue floated forward, wheels drove the gears, stamping the bronze shoe molds down hard into the earth once every meter!"

Everyone peered under the hem, spotting the remaining micro-clockwork mechanisms and stamping levers tucked away.

"But why go through such madness just to move the statue to the archives?" Aoi pondered.

Ian walked to the archive entrance, glancing up toward the second-story window where an ultra-thin steel cable remained lashed to the sill, running directly to the statue's nape.

"This was no prank; it was a heavy-duty counterweight crane!" Ian concluded. "The second floor houses the Ming Dynasty Brass Celestial Armillary Sphere for the upcoming jubilee. The culprit planned to use the statue's massive weight as a ground ballast to rip open the vault window grates using lever physics!"

Before Ian even finished, Archive Custodian Uncle Chen, ashen-faced in the rear of the crowd, attempted to slip away, only to be pinpointed by Ian's gaze.

"Custodian Chen, does your car trunk still contain three unopened crates of dry ice?" Ian asked mildly.

Trembling all over, Chen slumped to the paving stones, confessing that gambling debts had driven him to use the statue to pry open the vault grates unseen.

The truth laid bare, the eight-hundred-kilogram bronze enigma yielded completely to pneumatic physics, cementing the reputation of the Clockwork Detectives across Luyang!
