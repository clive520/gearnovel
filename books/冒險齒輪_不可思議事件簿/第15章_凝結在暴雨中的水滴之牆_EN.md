# Adventure Gear: The Files of the Impossible

## Chapter 15: The Wall of Raindrops Frozen in the Downpour

With only twenty minutes until the stroke of midnight, ink-black stormclouds choked the sky as a violent tempest erupted!

Howling gales drove torrential rain cascading across the clock tower dials, lightning ripping jagged seams across the darkness.

Braving the gale, Ian, Aoi, and Sparky surged through the tower portal, racing up the iron spiral toward the fourth-tier open atrium.

Throwing the iron doors wide, the sight that met their eyes brought the three seasoned young sleuths to an absolute standstill!

Above, stormwinds shrieked and thunder roared, where sheets of rain should have plummeted like millions of silver arrows.

Yet within this ten-meter-square interior atrium court, countless glistening raindrops hung suspended motionless in empty air!

Thousands upon thousands of spherical beads floated in mid-air, forming a crystalline labyrinth of motionless liquid pearls!

Trembling, Sparky reached out to touch a floating droplet with his fingertip—the bead yielded softly, nudged aside, and gently oscillated back into its place!

"The rain... is frozen in mid-air?!" Sparky stumbled backward. "This isn't mechanics... this is pure sorcery!"

"Not sorcery. No droplet on Earth defies gravity," Ian's voice was steady as stone, though his eyes held genuine respect for his rival's brilliance.

Ian caught a bead in his palm, the micro-gears on his monocle whirring to engage "Phase-Compensation Stroboscopic Analysis."

"Aoi, measure the light sources framing the courtyard with your tuning fork," Ian directed.

Aoi struck the fork, its chime ringing clear through the downpour: "In the octagonal cornices above, faint hums pulse with 60-hertz ultraviolet strobe flashes!"

"The physical truth—the 'Stroboscopic Water Droplet Levitation Effect'!" Ian pointed to concealed UV emitter arrays along the masonry.

"Beneath the cistern above, the culprit installed piezoelectric vibration dripper nozzles, releasing droplets at precisely sixty drops per second!"

"Concurrently, the perimeter UV strobes pulse at an identical sixty hertz!" Ian swept his arm through the misty gallery. "When strobe frequency matches the spatial displacement rate of falling drops, the human retina perceives each successive droplet arriving precisely where the preceding droplet was!"

"Thanks to the persistence of vision, our neural visual cortex fuses these instantaneous snapshots into a single motionless image—manifesting this floating marvel!"

"Slightly altering the flash frequency would even make the drops appear to float backward into the clouds!" Aoi marvelled at the sheer beauty of the physics.

"A liquid labyrinth engineered to stall us for twenty minutes..." Ian gazed through the cascade toward the bell platform overhead. "The Magician is at the summit!"

Sparky released Spiky, whose magnetic grappling hook slashed clean through the main feeder cable powering the strobes!

With a sharp snap, the strobes died, and the "floating spheres" collapsed instantly into a mundane torrent splashing noisily onto the stones.

The water wall shattered, revealing the final hatch leading to the belfry summit open against the storm!
