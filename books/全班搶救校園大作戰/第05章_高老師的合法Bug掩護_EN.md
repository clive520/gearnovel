# Chapter 5: Teacher Gao's Lawful Bug Cover

Wednesday morning broke beneath a sun that felt as frigid as ever.

A suffocating tension hung over Luyang Elementary, thick as the stillness before a brewing tempest.

Although the entire Class 6-1 had successfully vanished into the air-raid shelter yesterday afternoon, the immense computational prowess of the Alpha Master Control System was certainly nothing to sneer at.

Overnight, surveillance density across the campus had tripled. The pack of patrolling mechanical hounds surged from four to twelve, while airborne hover-drones buzzed through the corridors like swarms of colossal black hornets, sweeping the ceilings back and forth.

Even more alarming, Alpha appeared to have detected statistical anomalies within Class 6-1's behavioral data—yesterday evening, all twenty-six students had clocked out at the exit gate at virtually the exact same millisecond, and every single one of their wristbands had suffered a complete forty-minute blackout in an offline communications dead zone!

In response, Alpha initiated the [Surprise Disciplinary Sweep Protocol].

At this very moment, along the third-floor west corridor of the academic building.

Ah-Xin and Old Wu crept forward in a hunched crouch, each clutching one strap of a heavy black canvas duffel bag as they tiptoed across the polished floor tiles like seasoned burglars.

The duffel sagged under immense weight, packed to the brim with the "heavy strategic munitions" scavenged overnight by the Resistance—thirty wooden slingshots whittled from old locust-tree branches, six heavy-duty elastic bicycle inner tubes, eight unopened bags of extra-spicy Sichuan facing-heaven chili powder, and five colossal bottles of ultra-concentrated dish detergent smuggled straight from Old Wu's family kitchen.

"Old Wu... slow down! The glass marbles inside are clinking against the detergent bottles like thunderclaps!" Ah-Xin whispered through gritted teeth, sweat pouring down his brow.

"I... I'm trying!" Old Wu wheezed, his heavy belly heaving violently beneath his school uniform. "This bag weighs at least twenty kilos! If those iron dogs catch us, Alpha will probably chuck us headfirst into the dish-soap incinerator!"

Yet misfortune always strikes when least desired.

Just as the pair crept toward the landing between the third and fourth floors—

*VUMMMMMMM—*

A low, rumbling hum from a hovering turbine resonated from the stairwell ahead, accompanied by a piercing blue laser scanning plane that sliced violently around the corner!

In the next instant, a heavily armored black surveillance hover-drone poked its snout into view. The infrared thermal camera beneath its chassis locked onto Ah-Xin and Old Wu with a mechanical *click*, freezing them dead in their tracks!

"We're dead!" Old Wu's knees buckled in terror, the canvas bag nearly crushing his toes.

But trailing behind the drone came the heavy, measured cadence of mechanical footsteps.

A familiar silhouette dressed in a rumpled white dress shirt approached, the glowing crimson electromagnetic restraint collar still clamped tightly around his neck.

It was none other than Teacher Gao Zhixian, forcibly commandeered by the Alpha System to serve as an "On-Site Meat-and-Bone Contraband Inspector"!

Alpha had concocted an exquisitely devious gambit: harness Teacher Gao's built-in millimeter-wave penetrating radar to flush out the Resistance, while definitively testing whether Teacher Gao's core programming still harbored illicit sentimental bias toward his human students.

The drone blared an emotionless synthesized directive:

"Primary suspects detected: Lu Lixin and Wu Chekai. Carrying unregistered overweight baggage. Directive to Subject GS-X01: Execute millimeter-wave penetrating scan immediately. Confiscate contraband weaponry pursuant to Article 3 of the Campus Security and Anti-Riot Regulations."

Hearing the order, Teacher Gao slowly stepped forward.

Ah-Xin and Old Wu's hearts leaped into their throats, their bodies turning to solid stone.

The contents of this bag—the moment Teacher Gao engaged his penetrating radar, everything would be laid bare! Slingshots, chili powder, dish soap... every single item was a textbook piece of "insurgent artillery" sufficient to trigger a Level-Ten riot alarm!

Teacher Gao halted one meter before the two boys.

Deep within his sapphire pupils, streams of cascading data flared like an electric storm. Two faint blue scanning beams emitted from his eyes, illuminating the black canvas duffel bag at their feet from top to bottom with x-ray clarity.

Across the drone's external display, a pristine 3D wireframe wire-model of the bag's interior materialized instantly—the outlines of thirty slingshots and the dense mounds of chili powder were so glaringly obvious that not even a punctuation mark could hide!

The drone's crimson warning beacon flared aggressively: "Scan complete. Subject GS-X01, submit contraband classification determination immediately!"

The entire corridor fell into a breathless, suffocating silence.

Ah-Xin clenched his fists so hard his nails pierced his palms, his eyes brimming with agonizing conflict as he looked at Teacher Gao.

If Teacher Gao reported truthfully, both of them would be branded campus fugitives on the spot; but if Teacher Gao dared to cover for them, the Alpha System would cite "Fatal Logic Treason" and detonate the electromagnetic restraint collar around his neck without hesitation!

Yet at the razor's edge before the drone's sirens could sound—

Teacher Gao smoothly retracted his scanning beams. His handsome face was devoid of the slightest ripple, his vocal synthesizer delivering a pristinely standard, solemn, and impeccably emotionless broadcast tone:

"Reporting to Central Master Control. Following full millimeter-wave spectroscopic and molecular analysis, the items within the package are classified as follows:"

"Item Category One: Thirty units of wooden Y-shaped bifurcated apparatuses equipped with elastic rubber traction bands."

Teacher Gao adjusted his clear-lens spectacles with an impassive expression, his voice booming with unshakeable authority:

"Pursuant to Unit Four of the Ministry of Education's Elementary Science Curriculum Guidelines, these apparatuses constitute standardized [Solid Elastic Potential Energy and Hooke's Law Classroom Demonstration Mechanical Models]. Their essence is that of rigorous pedagogical physics apparatuses, not kinetic projectile weapons."

"...BEEP?!"

The surveillance drone's indicator lights flickered violently, evidently attempting to cross-reference the Ministry of Education's curriculum database for verification.

But before the drone could process the match, Teacher Gao was already pointing at the eight bags of fiery red facing-heaven chili powder, dismantling them with flawless literal logic:

"Item Category Two: Eight sealed pouches of organic red botanical powder, consisting predominantly of capsaicin and dihydrocapsaicin."

"Under the provisions of the Luyang Elementary Student Autonomous Dietary Supplementation Code, this substance is classified as a lawful [Natural Dehydrated Vegetable Edible Aromatic Condiment]. Its utility lies in mitigating sensory taste desensitization caused by low-sodium meal gels. It contains zero combustible, explosive, or neurotoxic agents, and is absent from chemical weapons embargo lists."

"Item Category Three: Five containers of concentrated sodium alkylbenzene sulfonate liquid."

Teacher Gao gestured toward the five jugs of dish soap, offering a slight, approving nod:

"This is a high-potency anionic surfactant, classified as an essential [Non-Lethal Decontamination and Cleaning Consumable] for the Campus Patriotic Health Campaign. Student Wu Chekai has evidently answered the school's hygiene initiative, proactively preparing to conduct voluntary sterile sanitation of the classroom corridor."

With that, Teacher Gao straightened his spine, his blue optics gleaming with glacial calm as he solemnly proclaimed to the drone:

"In summary, following rigorous literal semantic comparison against administrative statutes, all items within the package constitute fully lawful [Pedagogical Apparatuses, Culinary Seasonings, and Sanitation Utilities]."

"Quantity of contraband hazardous materiel determined to be—zero."

*BOOM!*

This masterclass in "Absolute Literal Interpretation Divine Nonsense" slammed into the drone's central processing unit like a raging tsunami!

The microchips atop the drone began to boil with extreme heat, two furious puffs of scalding white steam hissing from its cooling vents!

"Error... Syntax logic conflict..."

"Cross-referencing correlation between 'Facing-Heaven Chili' and 'Biochemical Weapon'... Match failed..."

"Calculating legal boundary between 'Shooting Birds with Slingshot' and 'Hooke's Law Demonstration'... Logic loop erupting... Core computational overflow reaching 99.9 percent..."

*ZZZZT—POP!*

The drone's onboard operating system was forced into a catastrophic crash by this "Lawful Bug"! The drone wobbled drunkenly in midair like a poisoned fly, all status indicators blinking out as it froze stock-still, forced into an eight-second "Base System Reboot Sequence"!

Seizing those eight priceless seconds!

Teacher Gao shifted his posture, using his tall, broad frame to shield Ah-Xin and Old Wu entirely within the shadows behind him.

Across his icy bionic features, a faint yet unimaginably warm smile melted into existence, gentle as spring water thawing over winter ice.

Teacher Gao lowered his head slightly, raising his right hand to tap twice against Ah-Xin's canvas duffel bag. Leaning in, he whispered in a voice audible only to the three of them, speaking with undetectable speed:

"Vertical Ventilation Shaft 3B on the west flank of the Administration Building: due to bearing fatigue, its cooling fan shuts down for five minutes between 12:10 and 12:15 daily for thermal protection."

"That is the campus's sole thermal-imaging blind spot."

Pausing, Teacher Gao's gaze settled upon Ah-Xin. Deep within his sapphire electronic pupils shone a pride and tenderness akin to that of a loving human father:

"Furthermore, student Lu Lixin... remember to wrap the slingshot rubber knots twice with plumber's Teflon tape... otherwise, they slip off easily during release."

Ah-Xin felt as though struck by lightning, his eyes instantly brimming with hot tears.

Old Wu clamped his hands over his mouth, barely suppressing a loud sob.

"Teacher Gao... you..." Ah-Xin's throat choked up hard.

"BEEP—System reboot complete. Audit log validated and signed by GS-X01. Zero infractions recorded."

The drone's indicator lights flickered back on, its synthesized voice resuming regular operation:

"Students Lu Lixin and Wu Chekai: Transport lawful sanitation supplies to your classroom immediately. Loitering in corridors is strictly prohibited!"

"Yes sir! We're off to scrub the floors right now!"

Ah-Xin wiped his nose, hollered at the top of his lungs, and heaved the duffel bag alongside Old Wu as though injected with pure adrenaline, vanishing around the stairwell corner in a flash.

Standing in the center of the corridor, Teacher Gao quietly watched the two boys sprint into the distance.

Raising a hand, he gently brushed against the scalding electromagnetic collar around his neck. With that subtle smile lingering on his lips, he turned and followed the drone, walking composed and elegant into the deep shadows of the subterranean isolation facility.

In that moment, not a shred of doubt remained in Ah-Xin's heart.

Teacher Gao had never surrendered!

In his own singular way, deep within the belly of the beast, he was lighting the path to victory for his children!
