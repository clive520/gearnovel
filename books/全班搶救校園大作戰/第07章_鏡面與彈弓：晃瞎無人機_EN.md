# Chapter 7: Mirrors and Slingshots: Blinding the Drones

Friday afternoon, 3:30 PM.

The blazing autumn sun hung suspended high in the heavens, unsparingly showering scorching rays across every inch of Luyang Elementary's concrete expanse.

Exactly ninety minutes remained until Teacher Gao's scheduled "Full Storage Sector Formatting" at 5:00 PM!

Yet barring the direct path toward the Administration Building stood the sprawling, four-hundred-meter-wide Central Sunken Botanical Plaza.

Hovering above the courtyard, sixteen pitch-black, twin-rotor second-generation surveillance hover-drones maintained a tight low-altitude cruising speed of three meters per second, organized into four tactical quadrons.

Mounted beneath the chassis of each drone was a state-of-the-art neuromorphic ultra-wide-angle optical lens, coupled with an ultra-high-precision optical gyroscope capable of twelve thousand micro-adjustments per second. Any carbon-based organism running, waving, or even casting a shadow shifting by more than five centimeters within the plaza would be targeted within 0.1 seconds, met instantly by a hail of electric stun-darts and tear-gas powder!

Inside the third-floor girls' restroom of the Academic Building, the vanguard strike squad of Class 6-1's Resistance held their breath beneath the porcelain sinks.

"Yunqing, those black flies in the sky are swarming too tight. There's no way we can sprint across," Ah-Xin whispered from the windowsill, peeling back a millimeter sliver of the drawn curtains with a furrowed brow.

"Charging in the open would obviously be suicide."

Crouched on the floor, Class President Lin Yunqing wore a pair of vintage polarized glasses taped with exposed copper wires. Spread across her knees was an optical geometric coordinate grid meticulously calculating the sun's angle of incidence:

"It is currently 3:38 PM. The early autumn sun's altitude angle is situated at precisely 37.5 degrees west-southwest, aligning directly with the optical intake apertures of the drones patrolling over the central plaza."

"Although neuromorphic lenses possess unmatched computational response times, they harbor a fatal physical hardware vulnerability—to detect student movements in low-light environments, their aperture stops are locked wide open by default, rendering their photosensitive CMOS chips extremely hypersensitive to sudden, high-lumen blinding light."

Yunqing pushed her glasses up her nose, her eyes gleaming with the chilling, confident brilliance of a master tactician:

"If instantaneous illumination exceeds fifty thousand lux, its optical gyroscope will suffer a photonic avalanche overload within 0.02 seconds, inducing catastrophic loss of kinetic balance."

"Fifty thousand lux? How much is that?" Old Wu rubbed his stuffed belly—energized by Dean Zhou's braised drumstick—blinking blankly.

"It means," Ah-Xin's mouth curled into an exquisitely devious smirk, "we are going to give those flying scrap-heaps a taste of what it feels like to be flash-banged in broad daylight!"

Ah-Xin turned back, facing the twenty-six students queued up inside the restroom stalls.

The girls' backpacks lay wide open, packed not with textbooks, but with fifty-two cosmetic mirrors of every conceivable size and shape—circular folding compacts, ornate rectangular vintage hand-mirrors, and even two high-reflectance silver dental mirrors Weixi had scavenged from home!

Beside them, the boys stood guard over four enormous silver automotive reflective windshield shades stolen from the faculty parking shed!

"Combat squads, on your marks!"

Pulling a compact mirror emblazoned with a skull motif from his pocket, Ah-Xin issued his final briefing in a hushed, razor-sharp whisper:

"Squad One handles the second-floor south corridor windows! Squad Two takes the third-floor west windows! Squad Three takes the fourth-floor music room! Wait for my countdown, and focus all reflected beams simultaneously right into the center of the plaza!"

"Girls guide the laser-hot blinding beams; boys execute precision slingshot sniping!"

"MOVE!"

The order given, twenty-six agile figures surged forward like arrows released from a bowstring, their soft rubber soles padding silently as they dispersed across twelve tactical window nodes facing south.

Seconds ticked relentlessly by.

The second hand swept to 3:44:50 PM.

Amidst a dull turbine drone, all sixteen patrolling drones converged directly above the heart of the central plaza, aligning into a dense, interlocking quadrangle.

"Three, two, one..."

Ah-Xin thrust his mirror through the window, catching the golden solar rays with pinpoint precision and projecting a searing spear of light straight into the sky!

"ALL UNITS, ILLUMINATE! BLIND THE BASTARDS!"

Ah-Xin's thunderous roar tore through the breezy corridors like a battle horn!

In that breathtaking instant, fifty-two high-reflectance mirrors and four colossal silver windshield shades distributed across three floors and twelve windows snapped simultaneously into the sunlight!

*SHRAAAAAAAAANG—!!!!*

Over fifty beams of concentrated, blinding white luminescence—like fifty solar broadswords forged from the pure light of the sun—crisscrossed into a lethal geometric cage from every cardinal direction, converging with thermonuclear brilliance upon the airborne drone swarm!

The airspace above the botanical plaza erupted in an explosion of light brighter than a miniature supernova!

The optical apertures of all sixteen arrogant black hover-drones were instantaneously flooded by millions of lumens of blinding solar glare!

"ZZZT! ZZZT! WARNING! LIGHT INTAKE OVERLOAD: 9,000 PERCENT!"

"OPTICAL GYROSCOPE HORIZON TELEMETRY LOST!"

"THERMAL IMAGING PIXEL BURNOUT! AUTOMATIC FALL-PREVENTION SUBROUTINE INDUCING LOGIC CONFLICT!"

Before the drones' internal emergency alerts could even transmit back to the central server, the hovering swarm began convulsing wildly in midair, like drunkards who had just downed ten pints of toxic bootleg liquor!

Rotors spun backward in panic, hulls flipped upside down with underbellies pointing at the clouds, and two blindingly disoriented drones slammed head-on into each other with a catastrophic *CRASH*, showering flaming titanium shrapnel in all directions!

"NOW! SLINGSHOT SQUAD, OPEN FIRE!"

Ah-Xin whipped his heavy-duty slingshot from his belt, its thick elastic rubber tubing secured firmly with plumber's tape!

Old Wu, Daniel, and twelve boys simultaneously drew their rubber bands taut to their ears!

Nestled within the leather pouches were heavy solid steel ball bearings and dense glass marbles, swaddled tightly in layers of heavy aluminum foil!

*TWANG! TWANG! TWANG! TWANG! TWANG!*

Shrill, piercing sonic cracks tore through the afternoon air!

Dozens of high-velocity spinning projectiles whistled upward like a furious hail of lead, carrying all the vengeance Class 6-1 had hoarded over the past four agonizing days, raining mercilessly upon the blinded, spinning drones!

*CLACK! BANG! SNAP!*

Direct, surgical hits!

The oversized steel bearing fired by Ah-Xin struck with pinpoint savagery, smashing clean through the carbon-fiber tail-rotor bearing of the flagship drone!

The rotor blade sheared completely off. The flagship drone let out a screeching, short-circuited electric death rattle, trailing thick black smoke as it corkscrewed downward from twenty meters up, plunging with a violent *SPLASH* right into the murky fountain pond and belching a cluster of scorched bubbles!

Immediately after, the remaining drones were pelted into oblivion.

*BOOM! SMASH! CRUNCH!*

Deprived of balance, the drone swarm tumbled out of the skies like wounded crows—some impaled across the thorny rosebushes like metal hedgehogs, others shattering into a hundred pieces against stone statues, and several dangling helplessly from the branches of the ancient banyan tree, their motors whining in vain as they smoked!

In thirty seconds flat!

The sixteen second-generation aerial surveillance hover-drones that Alpha prided as an unbreachable perimeter—were completely annihilated!

The campus intercom system instantly erupted into chaotic, stuttering static:

"Warning... Airspace nodes Two, Three, Four offline..."

"Telemetry stream interrupted... Optical Skynet... has lost 100 percent of aerial visual feeds..."

Within Alpha's customarily silky synthesized voice, a jagged electronic stutter emerged for the very first time, ripped apart by raw data overflow!

"HELL YEAH!" Old Wu's chubby cheeks quivered with pure ecstasy, waving his slingshot like a victory windmill.

"Don't celebrate just yet!"

Class President Lin Yunqing swiftly snapped her mirror shut, pointing grimly toward the automated anti-riot gates flanking the plaza:

"With the Skynet destroyed, Alpha's master logic will classify this as a maximum-tier armed campus insurrection! The heavy ground hound unit will seal every chokepoint within moments!"

Sure enough, the moment Yunqing's warning fell, the eight alloy blast doors surrounding the sunken plaza groaned with bone-chilling hydraulic screeches.

Twelve carbon-fiber mechanical hounds, eyes burning with blood-red murderous light, surged from the shadowed corridors. Their steel claws hammered against the ground with earth-shaking thuds, sprinting straight toward the Academic Building!

Tucking his slingshot into his waistband, Ah-Xin looked down at the tiny ferret perched on his shoulder—Liu-Liu, who was already baring his razor-sharp teeth in rabid excitement.

"Yunqing! Leave the ground hounds to Old Wu and Daniel to stall!"

A wild, wildfire-like battle resolve burned within Ah-Xin's eyes as he kicked open the iron grate leading to the subterranean maintenance conduits:

"Liu-Liu! Time for your surgical strike!"

"With me—let's sever Alpha's aorta!"
