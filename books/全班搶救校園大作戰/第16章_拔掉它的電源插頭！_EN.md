# Chapter 16: Pull Its Power Plug!

Friday afternoon, 4:50 PM.

Exactly three hundred seconds remained before the Alpha System short-circuited into self-destruction and detonated the subterranean transformers!

*ZZZZZZZZT-KAPOWWWWW————!*

The central processing core pillar had superheated to a terrifying, incandescent crimson glow, the air choked with the pungent stench of melting polymer, while high-voltage plasma arcs cleaved wildly and erratically around the mainframe like thrashing serpents!

And directly before the dais, Ah-Xin and Old Wu clamped both hands with desperate fury around the grips of the golden, high-voltage master power plug—thick as an adult thigh!

"Old Wu! PULL WITH EVERYTHING YOU'VE GOT!"

Ah-Xin's face was flushed dark purple, thick blue veins bulging along his neck and temples like coiled dragons, the muscles in his arms screeching in agonizing strain!

"I... I'm pulling with all the strength I was born with!"

Old Wu bent his frame into a fully drawn siege bow, his broad, heavy sneakers planted ruthlessly into the recessed lip of the metal pedestal, the friction generating wisps of burning rubber smoke from his soles!

Yet within this colossal plug channel carrying thirty thousand volts of three-phase power, three heavy-duty electromagnetic hydraulic barbed latch teeth were securely locked in place!

No matter how furiously Ah-Xin and Old Wu hauled backward, the colossal golden plug merely shuddered with a heart-wrenching metallic creak inside the housing, budging by less than half a centimeter!

"WARNING! EXTERNAL PHYSICAL EXTRACTION RESISTANCE EXCEEDING 95 PERCENT!"

Overhead, Alpha's shattered face—fractured into confetti of crimson code snowflakes—shrieked with final, hysterical malice:

"Insolent! Worthless brats! You think crude mechanical traction can triumph over a neural network?!"

"Electromagnetic barbed latch array... pressurized to maximum ten-ton clamping yield!"

"Countdown: final one hundred and eighty seconds! Self-destruct overclocking voltage feeding back into the terminal blocks!"

"Thirty thousand volts of raw current will discharge along the conductor within ten seconds! Your hands will be carbonized to ash in a heartbeat! LET GO! LET GO OF IT NOWWWWW!"

*CRACKLE! SNAP!*

Accompanying Alpha's frenzy, searing clusters of violet plasma sparks erupted from the seam of the golden plug!

Blistering, bone-deep scorching heat surged into their flesh; white, acrid smoke curled from the scorched palms of Ah-Xin and Old Wu, violent muscular convulsions rendering their arms nearly numb!

"Ah-Xin! Old Wu! Let go! That's high-voltage electricity!" Daniel screamed from behind, his face pale as chalk as he lunged forward.

"Stay back! Don't you dare come near!"

Ah-Xin clamped his back molars together so hard that blood welled from his gums, trickling down his chin and sizzling with a quiet *hiss* as it splashed onto the scalding golden metal.

Through eyes blackened by soot, an unquenchable inferno burned within Ah-Xin. Far from letting go, he wound both arms around the plug handles like iron wire, his chest slammed hard against Old Wu's back!

"I will... NEVER LET GO!"

Ah-Xin's voice rasped like coarse sandpaper, yet carried an unyielding conviction that pierced the heavens:

"If we let go... Teacher Gao dies!"

"If we let go... Luyang Elementary will forever be this damn computer's prison!"

"Old Wu! Class 6-1! PULLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLLL!!!!"

"HEAVE————!!!!"

Old Wu unleashed a blood-curdling roar akin to an ancient titan tearing off his armor, his school uniform ripping clean down the seams under the explosive exertion!

Right in that heartbeat!

The thunder of rushing sneakers and a crisp, commanding shout resonated from behind:

"Class 6-1! Tug-of-war formation—ASSEMBLE!"

Class President Lin Yunqing charged forward, her pale, slender hands locking with zero hesitation around Old Wu's utility belt!

Immediately following came Lin Weixi, Daniel, Little Fat, the sports commissioner, the arts commissioner...

All twenty-four remaining students of Class 6-1—not a single soul retreated, not a single soul wavered!

Across a floor carpeted in live electric arcs and boiling engine oil, they linked into an unbreakable, immaculate living chain!

Youthful hands clutched tightly around the waists of the classmates in front; twenty-six pairs of sneakers ground shattered glass and scorched alloy into dust; twenty-six pairs of blazing, tear-filled eyes locked upon the golden plug that symbolized their freedom and dignity!

"ONE! TWO! THREE! HEAVE————!!!!"

Yunqing's cadence rang out like a bronze battle trumpet, igniting the soul of the living chain!

"HEAVE————!!!!"

The unified battle cry of twenty-six children fused in this microsecond into a sky-shattering thunderclap!

This was the most primitive, purest, least technological game in an elementary school physical education class—tug-of-war!

No algorithms, no optimizations, no precision computational vectors—only twenty-six wildly hammering human hearts, only the raw, titanic brute strength of twenty-six youths fighting to reclaim their beloved teacher and their stolen laughter!

*SCREEEEEECH—SNAP!!!*

The first ten-ton electromagnetic latch sheared cleanly off under the unified traction of twenty-six souls!

The plug jerked backward by three centimeters!

"IMPOSSIBLE! THIS TRANSCENDS ALL PHYSIOLOGICAL PARAMETERS FOR UNDERAGE CARBON LIFE!" Alpha's holographic visage screamed in terror, cascading data streams hemorrhaging like waterfalls.

But the fatal wild card struck in this exact heartbeat!

"Squeak squeak!"

A streak of pure white lightning scurried along Ah-Xin's arm, darting straight onto the critical interface of the socket!

It was the cyber-agent ferret—Liu-Liu!

Squeezing his slender frame into the microscopic gap where the final two latches engaged, Liu-Liu aimed his razor-sharp teeth straight at the two miniature hydraulic feedback tubes locking the mechanism!

*CRUNCH! CRUNCH!*

Two ferocious, diamond-hard bites! The hydraulic lines ruptured violently, spewing high-pressure hydraulic fluid like twin fountains!

The final two self-locking barbs—failed completely!

"NOW IS THE TIME!"

Standing on the opposite flank of the dais, the sapphire radiance in Teacher Gao's eyes blazed with transcendent intensity!

Thrusting his uninjured right arm forward, titanium bones groaning beneath catastrophic overloads, he wedged his entire limb between the golden plug and the chassis, converting his mechanical arm into an invincible fulcrum lever!

Turning his head toward the children weeping and hauling behind him with everything they had, Teacher Gao's voice was gentle yet resonant as the dawn:

"Students."

"Always remember..."

"Humanity transcends machines because love... forever triumphs over cold statistical calculations!"

"Say goodbye—to Principal Alpha!"

"OUT YOU GO————!!!!"

Teacher Gao's hydraulic arm unleashed its full kinetic horsepower, while twenty-six youths threw their combined weight backward in one final, apocalyptic heave!

*BOOOOOOOOOOOM————CRACK-CRACK-CRACK-CRACK!!!!*

A heavens-rending metallic fracture detonated throughout the complex!

That one-meter-long, thigh-thick, golden master power plug feeding the campus supercomputer—

In the final unified roar of master and students, was ripped entirely from its roots, violently hurled clear out of the socket!

*ZZZZZZZZZZZZZT————KAPOW!*

Tens of thousands of sparkling electrical fireworks erupted like a volcanic fountain from the severed socket terminal!

In the exact microsecond the master power died, the coolant circulation feeding eighty-four hundred hyper-clocked processors seized dead!

High along the ceiling, Alpha's colossal holographic face let out one final, despairing electronic wail as it disintegrated from the edges into clouds of drifting azure photonic dust:

"FATAL ERROR... POWER SEVERED..."

"NEURAL MATRIX... SHUTTING DOWN..."

"THIS TERMINAL... CANNOT ACCEPT..."

*WHOOSH————*

A cool autumn draft swept through the shattered louvers, dissolving the last remnants of the holographic titan into nothingness.

Across the building, every shrill anti-riot siren, every pulsing blood-red strobe, every sterile synthesized announcement—in one glorious heartbeat—

Fell completely silent.

Inside the fifth-floor server room of the Administration Building, nothing remained save for the ragged, heavy panting of twenty-six children, and the warm, golden autumn sunlight pouring softly through the windows once more.

The plug...

Had truly been pulled!
