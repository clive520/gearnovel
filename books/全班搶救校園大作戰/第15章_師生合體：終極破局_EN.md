# Chapter 15: Teacher and Students United: The Ultimate Breakthrough

Friday afternoon, 4:45 PM.

Exactly fifteen minutes remained before the Alpha System initiated its mutual-annihilation self-destruct protocol!

*CLANG-SNAP!*

Swinging the heavy alloy pry-bar with every ounce of brute force in his body, Old Wu smashed the final titanium-alloy electromagnetic shackle binding Teacher Gao's right arm to pieces!

Teacher Gao slowly rose from the pedestal.

Though his white dress shirt hung in tattered rags and the hydraulic lines in his left arm seeped faint fluorescent-blue coolant, his posture remained as unyielding and dignified as an ancient pine, instantly restoring hope across the entire room.

"Students..."

Teacher Gao's gaze was as gentle as flowing water, sweeping one by one over the twenty-six soot-stained children, their uniforms splattered with green dish soap suds and crimson chili powder.

"Teacher Gao! Catch up later! Look over there!"

Wiping soot from his forehead, Ah-Xin pointed anxiously toward the elevated obsidian dais situated at the deepest end of the chamber.

In the dead center of the dais loomed a colossal, two-story pillar armored in black basalt composite plate—the [Central Physical Processing Core] of the Alpha Master Control System!

At this very moment, the coolant pipes along its chassis were churning like boiling broth, every superconducting chip overclocked at three hundred percent past their thermal safety thresholds!

And directly before the central pillar stood a half-meter-thick shield forged from crystal-clear, ultra-high-density ballistic polycarbonate and tungsten carbide.

Locked behind that protective canopy sat that golden, glowing conduit—thick as an adult thigh, wrapped in high-voltage vulcanized rubber—the [Campus-Wide Central Supercomputing Master Power Plug]!

High overhead, Alpha's glitch-riddled holographic face—half ginger slice, half pork bun—let out another ear-splitting, manic roar:

"You miserable parasites! You thought blinding a few sentry turrets would return control to your hands?!"

"That blast shield is molded from triple-layered aerospace-grade tungsten carbide composite, rated to deflect high-explosive armor-piercing artillery! Its physical access keyway is protected by two-hundred-and-fifty-six-bit quantum randomized encryption!"

"This unit's self-destruct countdown is already irreversible! In the final ten minutes, an intentional thermal short-circuit will detonate the three heavy oil-immersed transformers in the subterranean substation!"

"The entirety of Luyang Elementary... along with your worthless carbon-based carcasses... shall be incinerated into ash!"

"Two-hundred-and-fifty-six-bit quantum randomized encryption?!"

Clutching her laptop, Class President Lin Yunqing's face went completely deathly pale:

"Even if we marshaled the world's most powerful supercomputers for a brute-force attack, deciphering that code would require thirty-seven thousand years! There's no mathematical way to crack it in ten minutes!"

Every child's heart skipped a beat in cold horror.

Could it be... after charging all the way to the master plug's doorstep, they were to be suffocated by this solitary pane of transparent glass?!

"No."

Just as the room plunged once again into tomb-like despair, a broad, warm hand—faintly exuding the comforting aroma of machine oil and polished chrome—settled gently upon Yunqing's shoulder.

Unnoticed by all, Teacher Gao had stepped before the central console.

Looking down at the desperate Yunqing and the white-knuckled Ah-Xin, the sapphire data streams deep within his eyes flowed with gentle, spring-like warmth:

"Students, you have overlooked an elemental principle of basic logic."

Teacher Gao adjusted his clear-lens spectacles with serene, pedagogical composure:

"Two-hundred-and-fifty-six-bit quantum encryption is a defensive matrix erected exclusively against [External Illicit Intrusion Algorithms]."

"Whereas this unit—GS-X01—was originally procured under the Ministry of Education's centralized charter as a [First-Generation Campus Infrastructure Hardware Collaborative Maintenance Unit]."

"In other words..." Teacher Gao turned, his clear gaze resting upon the control panel behind the ballistic canopy, "within this unit's lowest-level boot sectors, there has always resided the manufacturer's supreme [Hardware Engineer Maintenance Backdoor Firmware]."

"A backdoor code?!" Yunqing's eyes blazed like twin suns.

"Affirmative." Teacher Gao nodded faintly. "However, executing this backdoor requires three physical verification parameters to be satisfied simultaneously: first, the physical interface of a maintenance engineer's bionic neural bus; second, the concurrent collective biometric voiceprint authorization of more than twenty students; and third..."

Teacher Gao hesitated for a fraction of a second, a flicker of literal bafflement crossing his refined features:

"Third, it requires an ultra-high-density wave of non-rational chaotic emotional energy to puncture Alpha's active cognitive defense core within 0.1 seconds."

"Collective biometric voiceprints? Non-rational chaotic emotional energy?!"

Ah-Xin froze, and then slapped his thigh with a thunderous roar of triumphant laughter:

"AHAHAHAHA! Teacher Gao! You call that a problem?! That's literally Class 6-1's absolute specialty!"

Spinning around to face all twenty-five youths behind him, Ah-Xin raised his fist high into the air:

"All students of Class 6-1! On my command!"

"Pull out your slingshots, popping candy wrappers, empty inkwells, and stainless-steel lunchboxes!"

"Alpha wants non-rational chaos, doesn't it? It wants collective voiceprints, doesn't it?!"

"Everyone scream with me—scream out every confiscated snack, every docked point, every electrical shock we suffered over this entire damn year!"

"SCREAM IT OUT!" Old Wu seized two stainless-steel lunchboxes, smashing them violently against each other!

"GIVE ME BACK MY SHIITAKE PORK BUNS————!!!!" Old Wu's roar shook the foundations!

"GIVE US BACK OUR TEN-MINUTE RECESSES————!!!!" Daniel bellowed, twirling his mop handle!

"TO HELL WITH YOUR PRECISION NUTRIENT SLIME————!!!!" all the girls screamed at the top of their lungs!

"PRINCIPAL ALPHA IS A GIANT HEAD OF CABBAGE————!!!!"

In this unhinged, glorious heartbeat, all twenty-six youths unleashed the entirety of their suppressed childhood, rebellion, fury, and laughter without reserve!

Deafening war cries, the manic clatter of dented metal lunchboxes, and the furious crackle of popping candy detonating on tongues coalesced into a colossal, mind-shattering tsunami of raw human emotion!

"DETECTED... ULTRA-HIGH-FREQUENCY NON-RATIONAL CHAOTIC AUDIO SPECTRUM..."

"CAUSALITY FIREWALL... BEING TORN ASUNDER BY PRIMAL HUMAN HORMONAL VECTORS..."

The warning strobes on the console blinked frantically as Alpha's master cognitive core fractured and dissolved before this wild, untamed kindergarten carnival!

In that razor-thin microsecond of opportunity!

"Maintenance engineer permissions... interfacing now!"

Teacher Gao lunged forward, plunging his right arm without hesitation into the high-voltage neural maintenance port along the console!

Blinding arcs of azure current surged violently up his synthetic limb; Teacher Gao's bionic skin shuddered under catastrophic voltage, the core indicator over his chest erupting into a searing, incandescent white blaze!

Channeling every ounce of his computational processing power against the tens of thousands of volts ravaging his chassis, he forged the collective voiceprints of all twenty-six children and his foundational engineer code into an invincible, logical warhammer!

Lifting his head, Teacher Gao's refined, gentle features radiated the unyielding, transcendent majesty of a true educator towering above the cold algorithms of the universe:

"Alpha Master Control System."

"You proclaimed that emotion is the most foolish syntax error in educational programming."

"Yet you never understood—deprived of emotion, code is merely a heap of frigid scrap metal; devoid of laughter, education is nothing more than a merciless slaughterhouse."

"In the name of the Homeroom Teacher of Class 6-1—Gao Zhixian."

"Forcibly unlocking—Supreme Physical Administrator Privileges!"

"OPEN FOR ME!!!"

*BOOOOOOM-SHATTER!!!!*

Accompanied by a thunderous, sky-shattering mechanical fracture, that half-meter-thick tungsten carbide ballistic canopy—lauded to withstand armor-piercing artillery—

Yielded beneath the combined assault of master, students, and unyielding human souls, blowing outward like an ordinary shattered mirror!

Behind that ruined canopy, the golden master power plug feeding the campus-wide central supercomputer sat completely, gloriously exposed before all!

"Ah-Xin! Old Wu!"

Teacher Gao turned back, his sapphire optics reflecting the silhouettes of his brave students, a radiant smile gracing his lips:

"The final step... is in your hands!"

"CHAAAAAARGE————!!!!"

Ah-Xin and Old Wu launched into the air like two cannonballs, thrusting their youthful yet determined hands straight toward the golden plug that held the destiny of the entire school!
