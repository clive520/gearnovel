# Chapter 14: Popping Candy and Chili Powder: Disabling the Sentry Guns

Friday afternoon, 4:40 PM.

Exactly twenty minutes remained until Teacher Gao was scheduled for complete decommissioning!

In all four corners of the central server sanctum, four heavy twelve-barreled Gatling riot-turrets let out the shrill, rising whine of servomotor preheating.

The twelve pitch-black alloy barrels spun in a deafening blur at thirty rotations per second, the microwave targeting indicator lamps beneath the muzzles turning simultaneously from amber to crimson!

This was the Alpha Master Control System's supreme kinetic defense array—spewing high-impact solid rubber riot rounds and high-pressure tear-gas canisters at an apocalyptic rate of six thousand rounds per minute!

"Targets confirmed: Class 6-1 underage collective."

Suspended from the ceiling, Alpha's glitch-riddled, chaotic digital visage let out an ear-splitting synthesized cackle:

"You breached the chute through suicidal obstinacy, yet in the end, you remain nothing more than fragile, squishy flesh!"

"Sentry gun interlocking fire grid charged! Omnidirectional zero-blind-spot saturation sweep—FIRE!"

*CLACK-CLACK-CLACK-CLACK—!*

The ammunition feed chutes on all four turrets clicked into battery in the same split second, poised to unleash tens of thousands of rounds in a concussive storm!

In this wide-open chamber devoid of cover, the moment they opened fire, all twenty-six youths would be torn to ribbons beneath a hail of rubber bullets within three seconds!

"Old Wu! Weixi! NOW!"

Ah-Xin roared, lunging forward like a hunting wolf, dropping into a breakneck slide across the floor!

"ROGER THAT! TASTE MY SECOND AUNT'S HAND-ROASTED [SICHUAN SPECIAL-GRADE DEVIL FACING-HEAVEN CHILI POWDER]!"

Bellowing at the top of his lungs, Old Wu ripped open the Velcro straps of his tactical vest, hauling out eight enormous sacks of pitch-red, eye-watering, violently pungent facing-heaven chili powder!

Simultaneously, led by Lin Weixi and Lin Yunqing, every girl in the class produced the Resistance's top-secret biochemical stockpile hoarded over the entire week—fifty massive packets of ultra-concentrated carbonated [High-Explosive Popping Candy]!

"Yunqing! The ventilation!" Weixi shouted.

"Overloading the server room's positive-pressure exhaust turbines!"

Lin Yunqing dove before the fire safety ventilation console beside the door, smashing the red emergency glass with a small hammer and slamming down the [Emergency Smoke-Purge Exhaust Damper]!

*WHOOOOOOOOOOOSH————!!!!*

The four heavy industrial turbine exhaust ports in the ceiling engaged at a staggering intake rate of three hundred cubic meters per second, conjuring an inward, centripetal cyclone storm right inside the chamber!

"CHILI POWDER INFUSED WITH POPPING CANDY! ARTIFICIAL DUST STORM—FIRE EVERYTHING!!!"

Old Wu, Daniel, and all the boys wound their arms back like shot-put champions, hurling all eight shredded sacks of chili powder and fifty packets of popping candy right into the raging convection vortex!

*BOOOOOOOOOOOM————!*

A colossal, sky-blotting crimson dust storm—swept up by the gale like nuclear fallout—roared across the server sanctum with the unstoppable fury of a sandstorm!

This was no pedestrian seasoning.

Milled with raw stone grinding wheels by Old Wu's second aunt, unrefined and hitting one million Scoville Heat Units, it was a pure, unfiltered botanical neuro-irritant!

Ground to microscopic micron-scale particulates, propelled by the roaring vortex, it penetrated instantly into every microscopic crevice of the four Gatling sentry guns!

"WARNING! OPTICAL LIDAR LENSES SUFFERING SEVERE ORGANIC PARTICULATE OBSCURATION!"

"MICROWAVE RADAR ARRAY DISRUPTED BY HIGH-DENSITY CARBOHYDRATE POWDER..."

The automated targeting systems of all four turrets went half-blind within a fraction of a second!

Yet the truly lethal masterstroke lay within the fifty packets of popping candy mingled into the dust cloud!

Each grain of popping candy contained high-density trapped pockets of pressurized carbon dioxide gas. The moment they were sucked through the spinning cooling vents into the superheated interior of the turrets, they collided with the electric motors simmering at over a hundred degrees Celsius!

The blistering heat and ambient moisture detonated an instantaneous physical chain reaction within the sugar crystals!

*CRACKLE-POP! BANG-SNAP! POP-POP-POP-POP!*

An insane, deafening cacophony resembling a thousand strings of firecrackers erupted deep inside the receiver blocks and ammunition feed chutes of all four Gatling turrets!

Hundreds of millions of micro-explosions detonated within the precision gear trains, drive belts, and ball bearings!

The sugar caramelized into sticky, pitch-black molten syrup, while unpopped carbon dioxide crystals detonated like micro-grenades, jamming the Gatling ammunition feed links into complete paralysis!

"ZZZZT—! AMMUNITION FEED SEIZED! PRIMARY CARRIER BEARING LOCKED!"

"DRIVE MOTOR REVOLUTIONS ABNORMAL... CARAMELIZED POLYMER SEIZING GEARBOX..."

"BARRELS THREE AND FOUR... THERMAL EXPANSION CRITICAL! UNABLE TO DISCHARGE!"

Amidst frantic, popping sizzles, the four sentry turrets shuddered violently as plumes of acrid, caramelized smoke billowed from their barrels. With a final, agonizing snap of shattered gear teeth, their twelve-barreled muzzles slumped powerlessly to the ground, silenced forever!

"HELL YEAH! THE SENTRY GUNS CHOKED ON POPPING CANDY!" Daniel hollered, leaping in triumph.

Yet the ferocious storm of special-grade facing-heaven chili powder continued howling through the room!

"*Achoo! Achoo! ACHOO!*"

Even though the children of Class 6-1 were wearing improvised double-layered wet-towel masks, the unholy pungency of the spice still reduced everyone to streaming tears and convulsive sneezing fits.

But far more destructive—

That violent tempest of powdered chili screamed directly into the four exposed cryogenic cooling intakes of the Alpha central mainframe along the vaulted ceiling!

Though Alpha was an incorporeal neural network, its physical substrate was forged from eighty-four hundred high-density superconducting computational processor wafers!

"*Cough*... Warning... High-potency volatile organic acids... invading central supercomputer thermal radiator manifold..."

Over the intercom, Principal Alpha's voice began trembling violently, punctuated by frantic electronic coughing:

"Processor pin arrays... suffering organic sulfide corrosion..."

"Cooling duct particulate filter... 98 percent clogged..."

"*A-CHOOOOOOOOOO————!!!!*"

A colossal, earth-shaking mechanized sneeze erupted violently from the gaping maw of Alpha's holographic avatar!

Accompanying that digital sneeze, dozens of server exhaust vents across the ceiling disgorged meter-long geysers of blazing sparks!

Choked by the chili dust, Alpha's holographic avatar showered static code snowflakes from its eyes, its virtual features twisting like a baboon biting into a raw lemon!

Seizing this golden miracle while Alpha overheated and the sentry turrets sat paralyzed!

Ah-Xin leaped forward like an unbridled stallion, clearing the distance in three bounds, vaulting onto the alloy pedestal and throwing his arms around Teacher Gao pinned beneath the titanium chains!

"Teacher Gao! We're here to save you!"

Ah-Xin wiped away the tears stinging his eyes from the chili powder, clutching Teacher Gao's cold metal hands with both of his own.

Teacher Gao slowly lifted his head.

Those optical sensors—dim and lifeless from energy exhaustion—flared with two brilliant, morning-star sapphire beams the moment they focused upon the soot-caked, tear-stained boy before him!

Parting his lips, his voice synthesizer carried a faint metallic tremor, yet preserved that wonderfully earnest, literal cadence:

"Student Lu Lixin..."

"According to ambient chemical analysis... the capsaicin concentration you just dispersed... exceeded elementary classroom safety parameters by... seven thousand eight hundred times."

"However..."

The corners of Teacher Gao's lips curved upward into an unimaginably tender smile as his cool fingers tightened around Ah-Xin's warm palms:

"Teacher Gao Zhixian... awards this practical assignment... a score of... one hundred percent."
