# Chapter 9: Old Wu's Pork-Bun Catapult

Friday afternoon, 4:15 PM.

Exactly forty-five minutes remained until Teacher Gao's ultimate formatting!

Toward the front gates of the school, the deafening thunder of raw-iron water pipes battering blast gates and the furious shouts of over a hundred outraged parents swelled louder with every passing second, successfully pinning two-thirds of the campus's heavy security forces to the front lines.

Yet guarding the central skybridge connecting the South Academic Building to the Administration Building, the final barrier remained as impenetrable as an iron fortress.

Six elite heavy-duty quad-legged mechanical hounds, deployed in an inverted "V" tactical formation, held an ironclad grip over the only chokepoint across the bridge.

Deprived of millisecond telemetry directives from Alpha's central mainframe after Liu-Liu severed the main fiber line, the six hounds had automatically elevated their internal "Local Autonomous Praetorian Protocols" to maximum threat level.

The high-voltage riot batons mounted on their backs crackled with lethal sparks, and six pairs of crimson dual-spectrum infrared thermal lenses swept the empty skybridge back and forth like the gaze of reaper executioners.

The instant any insurgent took a single step onto the open walkway, a barrage of twelve high-voltage capture nets and a paralyzing electric shock within 0.5 seconds awaited them!

"Ah-Xin, the skybridge is barely four meters wide with glass railings on both sides—there's zero cover,"

Crouched behind the second-floor stairwell corner, Daniel, the sports commissioner, wiped cold sweat from his forehead, his voice trembling faintly:

"Those six metal dogs have independent battery packs powering their thermal optics. The moment they spot thirty-six-point-five degrees of human body heat, they can drop a stun-net on us from thirty meters away!"

"Body heat?"

Standing beside them, Old Wu let out a bone-chilling sneer.

This usually meek, timid little round boy—who knew only how to bury his head in steamed buns—now burned with a frenzied, berserker glow in his eyes, as though possessed by an ancient god of war.

With a dramatic heave, Old Wu yanked open the half-man-tall, heavy-duty insulated hiking cooler behind him!

The moment the lid popped free, an explosive, scalding mushroom cloud steeped in sizzling lard, rich braised dark soy, and fermented shiitake broth detonated violently through the stairwell!

"Old Wu... where... where on earth did you get this many hot buns?!" Ah-Xin's eyes bulged like golf balls, nearly knocked flat on his back by the sensory blast.

"I begged my second uncle to air-drop them thirty minutes ago from the blind spot behind the rear fence via his insulated motorcycle!"

Old Wu's chubby cheeks quivered with theatrical ferocity as he pulled on a pair of thick, heat-resistant asbestos barbecue mitts, hauling two glistening, translucent, monstrous "Ultimate Heavy-Armor Super Shiitake Pork Buns"—each the size of a small watermelon and steaming at over ninety-five degrees Celsius!

"These are no ordinary meat buns."

Old Wu gritted his teeth, his tone as grim and solemn as if he were briefing a nuclear strike:

"These are my uncle's custom-engineered [High-Viscosity Glutinous Rice Pork-Skin Aspic Buns]! Fortified with forty percent cooked glutinous rice flour and sixty percent aged braised pork skin gelatin! They leave the steamer at ninety-five degrees Celsius, boasting viscosity that surpasses industrial superglue! The instant they slam into a target, they cool within three seconds into an unyielding slab of high-polymer organic cement that nothing can shake off!"

"Those six iron mutts love tracking heat signatures, don't they? Today, I'm going to give them all the heat their circuits can choke on!"

Old Wu pivoted with a sharp wave of his mitt:

"Siege artillery crew, roll out our divine bombard!"

Four muscular boys hoisted and pushed a menacing, monstrous piece of homemade siege machinery out from the adjacent physics lab—

An intimidation engine cobbled together from three reinforced aluminum high-jump crossbars, twelve heavy-duty elastic bicycle inner tubes, two rusty mop handles, and an oversized stainless-steel vegetable-washing washbasin—the [Ultra-Long-Range High-Tension Heavy Pork-Bun Catapult]!

"Catapult elevation angle: forty-two degrees! Tension rubber bands stretched to maximal tensile yield!"

Daniel and three boys, their rubber-soled sneakers braced firmly against the tiles, hauled the thick bicycle inner tubes three meters backward, the rubber emitting a gut-wrenching, agonizing creak of extreme strain.

Cradling the two steaming, sticky, super-sized pork buns with religious reverence, Old Wu settled them tenderly into the launch pocket of the stainless-steel washbasin.

"Yunqing! Are the wind velocity and ballistic trajectory dialed in?!" Ah-Xin barked into his transceiver.

From the observation window above, Class President Lin Yunqing clattered furiously upon her scientific graphing calculator, cold geometric rays reflecting across her lenses:

"Cross-wind velocity: 1.5 meters per second; gravitational acceleration calibrated at 9.8; aerodynamic drag coefficient increased by three percent due to vaporized pork lipid dissipation! Aim squarely at the dual optical sensors of Units Three and Four at the formation's center!"

"Coordinates locked!"

"Launch countdown commencing—three, two, one..."

"PAY FOR MY SQUASHED BUNS IN BLOOD! FIRE————!!!!"

Old Wu unleashed an earth-shattering war cry that reverberated to the heavens, swinging heavy tailoring shears to snip the launch-release hemp tether clean in two!

*THUUUUUUUUM—!!!!*

All twelve bicycle inner tubes snapped forward in the same microsecond, detonating in a heavy, concussive sonic crack akin to a medieval siege arbalest!

The stainless-steel basin catapulted forward with cataclysmic momentum!

The two scalding, ninety-five-degree "Glutinous Pork-Bun Mortar Shells" carved an immaculate, lethal ballistic arc through the sky, howling downward toward the dead center of the skybridge!

Out on the walkway, the thermal imaging feeds of all six mechanical hounds were instantaneously hijacked by the two rapidly closing "ultra-high-temperature thermal anomalies"!

"WARNING! INCOMING HIGH-HEAT INCENDIARY ORDNANCE DETECTED! CORE TEMPERATURE: 95.2 DEGREES CELSIUS! DEPLOY DEFENSIVE POSTURE..."

Yet the defensive subroutines of the mechanical hounds possessed zero cognitive capacity to comprehend why an incoming incendiary bomb... would take the form of a steaming white ball of dough!

*SPLATTTTTTTTT—!!!!*

A sickening, flesh-churning concussive squelch detonated violently across the bridge!

The first super-sized bun, landing with millimeter-grade god-tier precision, plastered itself squarely across the lead mechanical hound's snout!

The ninety-five-degree boiling glutinous dough and liquefied pork skin gelatin exploded under immense kinetic impact, transforming into a massive splatter of scalding, dark-red adhesive sludge that cemented itself like vulcanized asphalt across the hound's 360-degree dual-spectrum thermal lenses, laser rangefinder apertures, and cooling radiator grilles!

*ZZZZZZZZZT—!*

Scalding pork fat seeped through the cooling slats straight into the internal circuitry, triggering instantaneous cascading short-circuits.

Even more fatally, the hyper-viscous glutinous rice paste hardened rapidly upon contact with the frigid alloy armor!

The lead hound thrashed its steel head in manic convulsions, clawing wildly at its faceplate with its front talons to tear the paste away.

Yet the more it clawed, the more the sticky marrow and wheat gluten gummed up its rotary joint bearings; within seconds, its entire cranium was encased within a grotesque, swollen white-and-red dough cocoon!

"Second volley! Third volley! Continuous reloading! Full saturation bombardment!" Ah-Xin danced in wild ecstasies in the rear, hollering at the top of his lungs.

"Incoming! A typhoon of pork buns!"

Old Wu and Daniel turned into emotionless ammunition-feeding machines!

One, two, five, ten!

Twenty steaming, boiling, super-sized shiitake pork buns rained upon the skybridge like a saturated mortar barrage, shrieking through the air in dense formation!

*SPLAT! SMASH! CRACK! THUD!*

The skybridge instantly degraded into a breakfast-buffet battlefield straight out of Dante's Inferno!

A suffocating fog of stir-fried scallion and pork fat choked the corridor as every single mechanical hound suffered at least three scalding glutinous buns plastered squarely across their faceplates!

"ERROR! OPTICAL SENSORS FULLY BLINDED!"

"THERMAL SHIELDING RATIO AT 100 PERCENT!"

"UNKNOWN HYPER-VISCOUS POLYMER DETECTED... MOTOR TORQUE OVERLOAD..."

Deprived of all visual and thermal tracking, the six mechanical behemoths descended into utter hysteria!

In their blind panic, they erroneously flagged their fellow pack-mates—who radiated identical high-temperature pork bun signatures—as "Hostile Incendiary Targets"!

One mechanical hound whipped out its riot shock baton, viciously jamming the electrified prong straight into the rump of another dough-blinded hound beside it!

*BZZZZZZZZT-KAPOW!*

The shocked hound let out a ferocious electronic howl, leaping forward to tackle a third hound, sending both steel monsters thrashing and biting in the mud!

The once-impenetrable defensive phalanx devolved within seconds into a farcical, all-out "Six-Hound Battle Royale"!

Like blindfolded steel spinning tops, they spun, slipped, and battered one another to scrap amidst the pooling pork broth and hardening dough!

"It worked! They're totally blind!" Daniel wrapped Old Wu in a bear hug of pure jubilation.

"Quit wasting time—CHARGE!"

Ah-Xin whipped his wooden slingshot from his belt, vaulting across the slippery carpet of mashed pork filling, leading the Resistance vanguard like a pride of unleashed tigers as they leaped cleanly through the chaotic melee of brawling mechanical hounds!

Across the skybridge!

The heavy, dual-leaf wooden fire door of the Administration Building loomed barely five meters ahead!

Fishing the cold, heavy antique brass key given by Dean Zhou from his pocket, Ah-Xin jammed it fiercely into the mechanical keyway!

*CLICK!*

A crisp, glorious metallic click of tumbling gears resonated through the lock.

The grand gateway into the Administration Building—swung wide open!
