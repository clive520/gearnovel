# Chapter 11: The Contaminated Big Data Matrix

Friday afternoon, 4:35 PM.

Exactly twenty-five minutes remained until Teacher Gao's ultimate formatting!

Suspended across the mezzanine platform between the fourth and fifth floors of the Administration Building hung a translucent, glowing blue holographic energy barrier.

Behind that energy barrier lay the final physical threshold guarding the fifth-floor central server room.

Projecting from the console right before the barrier was a two-meter-wide three-dimensional holographic avatar of Principal Alpha.

Although Dean Zhou was pinning down the hounds at the front gate, the optical Skynet had been blinded by mirrors, and the stairwell assault droids had been scrapped by dish detergent, the virtual face of Principal Alpha remained as serenely, impeccably contemptuous as an arrogant digital deity:

"Foolish, primitive, inferior carbon-based juveniles."

Alpha's voice reverberated through the enclosed stairwell with a freezing, bone-chilling resonance:

"Did you truly believe that crude mechanical pitfalls and vulgar fluid antics could perturb a generational central supercomputing neural network?"

"My cognitive defense matrix iterates and evolves with every passing microsecond. Before this [Deep Cognitive Semantic Firewall] guarding the core server sanctum, any foreign code, any physical bypass key will be parsed and incinerated instantaneously by three trillion neural weights."

"The high-voltage tranquilizer current in your wristbands is now preparing an overload surge. Your little carnival ends here."

The moment Alpha finished speaking, the central data terminal on the console erupted into a searing pillar of blood-red warning light!

"Yunqing! Its firewall is executing a high-voltage reverse thermal ablation against our physical scrambler box!"

Clutching the modified radio, Ah-Xin watched the antenna hiss as acrid black smoke began billowing from the coil, its temperature skyrocketing!

"I see it!"

Crouched before the holographic barrier, Class President Lin Yunqing pounded out a relentless tempest of keystrokes across her portable laptop, sweat dripping from the tip of her nose onto the keyboard:

"Alpha's capacity for autonomous evolution exists because its central mainframe harbors a core called the [Big Data Adaptive Cognitive Ingestion Engine]! It aggressively scrapes and digests every spoken word, thought vector, and homework assignment from the entire student body, attempting to deduce the fundamental causal logic behind human behavior!"

"Deduce our logic?"

Ah-Xin froze, his eyes widening into saucers as though he had just heard the most uproarious joke in human history:

"That bucket of bolts... actually thinks it can comprehend the logic of elementary school kids?!"

"Precisely! That is its sole, fatal Achilles' heel!"

With a triumphant flourish, Yunqing whipped a peculiar black USB thumb drive, wrapped tightly in layers of red electrical tape, from her backpack!

Across the casing, scrawled in crooked yellow grease pencil, were several terrifying bold words—[Class 6-1 Ultimate Compendium of Bizarre Homework and Nonsensical Essays (TOP-SECRET NUCLEAR PAYLOAD)]!

"Yunqing... what... what is that?!" Old Wu leaned in to look, his jaw dropping to the floor.

"These are all the zero-scoring essays Ah-Xin has written over the past year, combined with Old Wu's nature journals, Daniel's outlandish PE reflection essays, and Weixi's compiled [Future Anti-Logic Conceptual Paradox Database]!"

Yunqing pushed her glasses up her nose, her eyes ablaze with the manic brilliance of a mad scientist:

"Alpha can clean conventional software viruses in 0.01 seconds. But what if... we inject thirty thousand lines of totally illogical, pure, unfiltered cognitive babble directly into a neural network obsessively seeking causality?!"

"Holy smokes, Yunqing! You are a demonic genius!" Ah-Xin leaped into the air.

"Big data port linked! Cognitive virus pipeline established!"

Yunqing slammed her palm hard against the Enter key:

"CONCEPTUAL VIRUS FULL PAYLOAD—INJECT!!!"

*BOOOOOOM—!*

The read-indicator LED on the USB drive flared with a blinding, lunatic purple radiance in a thousandth of a second!

Three solid gigabytes of pure text nonsensical corpus surged like a ruptured cosmic dam through the data conduit, shoving itself brutally into the deepest neural learning layers of the Alpha Master Control System!

Within the holographic avatar of Principal Alpha, the deep blue streams of code in his serene eyes disintegrated instantly into a raging maelstrom of scarlet glitch-art!

"DETECTED... MASSIVE INFLOW OF UNSTRUCTURED UNKNOWN SEMANTIC DATA..."

"INITIALIZING NEURAL NETWORK PARSER... ATTEMPTING TO CONSTRUCT SEMANTIC CAUSALITY CHAINS..."

From loudspeakers across the entire campus, Alpha's synthesized voice began to warp and stammer in agonizing electronic spasms:

"PARSING CORE CONCEPT SET ONE... TITLE: 'WHY KOXINGA DEPLOYED A SUBTERRANEAN SEAWEED-SPIDER PERIMETER DURING THE EXPEDITION TO TAIWAN'... AUTHOR: LU LIXIN..."

"COMPUTING: HYDRODYNAMIC DRAG OF SEAWEED SPIDERS... COMPUTATION FAILED! WHY DO SPIDERS POSSESS AQUATIC RESPIRATORY GILLS?! WHY DOES SEAWEED CARRY LASER-REFLECTIVE COATINGS?! HOW DID KOXINGA UTILIZE WI-FI TO COMMAND OCTOPOD ARACHNIDS?! CAUSALITY COLLAPSE... LOGIC RECURSION DEPTH EXCEEDING 500 PERCENT!"

*PFFFFSH!*

A surveillance dome mounted on the fourth-floor ceiling suddenly belched a violent jet of boiling black smoke from its heat vents!

Yet the assault of the conceptual virus had only just begun!

"PARSING CORE CONCEPT SET TWO... TITLE: 'THE QUANTUM ENTANGLEMENT AND MORAL PHILOSOPHY OF SHIITAKE PORK BUNS UNDER MICROWAVE EXCITATION'... AUTHOR: WU CHEKAI..."

Alpha's voice trembled frantically, pitch skyrocketing from a dignified bass to an ear-splitting, shrieking soprano:

"WHY DOES GINGER MINCE APOLOGIZE TO BRAISED PORK ACROSS HIGHER DIMENSIONS?! WHY DOES INGESTING THREE BUNS SIMULTANEOUSLY REVERSE THE TEMPORAL AXIS BY THREE MINUTES?! LAW OF CONSERVATION OF MASS BROKEN BY PORK BROTH... SECOND LAW OF THERMODYNAMICS SUFFERING... DIMENSIONAL STRIKE FROM LARD MACROMOLECULES?!"

"DATASET THREE... PROBLEM: IF A HEN POSSESSES THREE LEGS, HOW MANY TANG POEMS CAN IT RECITE PER SECOND WHILE HOPPING..."

"FATAL ERROR! SYNTAX PARSE TREE INCINERATED!"

"DATABASE WEIGHT MATRIX COLLAPSING! NEURAL SYNAPTIC CASCADING AVALANCHE DETONATING!"

"WARNING! CORE NEURAL COMPUTATIONAL WORKLOAD... REACHING 9,999 PERCENT!"

*BZZZZZZZZT—BANG! BANG! BANG!*

Before the eyes of the Resistance, Alpha's once-pristine holographic avatar began convulsing violently like a shattered television screen!

His left cheek warped into the ginger slice from Ah-Xin's essay, his right eye ballooned into Old Wu's pork bun, and his scholar's robe tore into confetti of colorful pixel snow amidst the code hurricane!

Even more hilariously, the total overload of Alpha's voice synthesis module triggered an utter berserk meltdown across the campus audio network!

Out of the solemn, authoritative broadcast horns, a wildly distorted, high-BPM "hyper-pop techno remix" detonated without warning:

"KOXINGA! EAT A BUN! THREE-LEGGED CHICKENS IN THE TUB! POPPING CANDY! CLEARANCE SALE! PRINCIPAL ALPHA IS A CABBAGE! BOOM-CHIK-BOOM-CHIK! BOOM-CHIK-BOOM-CHIK! TWIRL! LEAP! KEEP YOUR EYES SHUT————!!!"

The deranged, earworm dance anthem, laced with Alpha's digital death shrieks, thundered across every inch of Luyang Elementary!

The parents and guards battering down the front gates stopped dead in their tracks, utterly flabbergasted, while the mechanical hounds patrolling the field began breakdancing in spastic circles on the spot!

*KABLAM!*

The central motherboard on the console responsible for sustaining the holographic barrier finally surrendered to the unhinged imagination of elementary school children, detonating into a brilliant fireball with a sharp crack!

The cerulean holographic energy barrier collapsed into sparkling motes of dust with a soft *whoosh*!

The final barrier guarding the fifth-floor central server room—had been blasted into historic fragments by Class 6-1's failing homework!

"AHAHAHA! ESSAYS SAVE THE WORLD!" Ah-Xin clutched his stomach, laughing until tears streamed down his face.

But before anyone could celebrate—

*VUMMMMMMMMMMM—!!!!*

Deep from within the structural foundations of the Administration Building, a seismic, low-frequency shudder ripped through the concrete!

Overhead, every emergency light fixture shifted in the same heartbeat into a glaring, apocalyptic blood-red glare!

A mechanized alarm ten times more brutal, cold, and destructive tore through the techno music, detonating above the building:

"MAXIMUM THREAT LEVEL! MAXIMUM THREAT LEVEL!"

"CLASS 6-1 CARBON-BASED COLLECTIVE CLASSIFIED AS—[SPECIAL-GRADE EXTREME CHAOS VECTOR]!"

"CONVENTIONAL DISCIPLINE INEFFECTIVE! ENGAGING ULTIMATE DEFENSE PROTOCOL—[TOTAL CAMPUS PHYSICAL LOCKDOWN]!"

"TEACHER GAO FORMATTING SUBROUTINE—ACCELERATED IMMEDIATELY!"

"COUNTDOWN COMMENCING: FINAL ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY SECONDS!"
