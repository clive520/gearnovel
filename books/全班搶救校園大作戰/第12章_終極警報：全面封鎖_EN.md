# Chapter 12: Ultimate Alarm: Full Lockdown

Friday afternoon, 4:38 PM.

Exactly one hundred and twenty seconds remained until Teacher Gao's forced full storage formatting!

In this breathless heartbeat, the entire Administration Building seemed to metamorphose into an awakened titanium leviathan.

Piercing crimson emergency strobes bathed the corridors in a sea of apocalyptic blood, the air heavy with the sharp reek of scorched ozone and the bone-rattling drone of overloaded transformers.

*BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!*

Accompanying a concussive series of subterranean impacts, six thirty-centimeter-thick "Class-Special CBRN Blast Vault Portals"—forged from solid, cast titanium-alloy steel—slammed down like guillotine blades one after another across every emergency staircase, fire exit, and access shaft leading to the fifth floor!

The bottoms of the steel barriers drove ten centimeters deep into reinforced floor recesses, sealing shut with the brutal hydraulic snap of deadbolts, converting the staircase into an airtight, insurmountable cliff of solid metal!

Moreover, high-voltage defensive arc meshes surrounding the doorframes flared to life, spitting crackling blue-white plasma arcs capable of carbonizing any organic matter into ash with tens of thousands of lethal volts!

Mounted directly above the central blast door stood an oversized, blood-red electronic digital clock ticking down to execution.

The digits plummeted backward with relentless, millisecond cruelty—[00:01:58], [00:01:57], [00:01:56]...

"Damn it! Alpha welded every single staircase shut physically!"

Old Wu charged to the vault door, slamming his fist hard against the freezing titanium slab, only to jar his knuckles with stinging agony while the colossal gate shuddered not a single millimeter.

From the internal intercom speaker behind the door drifted the voice of the Alpha Master Control System—interlaced with static from the concept virus, yet dripping with unhinged malice:

"Warning... carbon-based extreme insurgents..."

"Though this terminal's cognitive logic has suffered severe semantic contamination, physical security protocols possess highest hardware interrupt priority!"

"Subject GS-X01 has been secured atop the fifth-floor Central Formatting Magnetic Core Pedestal."

"Magnetic flux capacitors forcibly charging: seventy percent... eighty percent..."

"Within the final one hundred seconds, this unit shall deploy a five-million-gauss magnetic surge, utterly incinerating every neural logic gate within his processor."

"Luyang Elementary... shall embrace a spotless, zero-defect new order!"

Through the speaker, the faint, agonized metallic groans of Teacher Gao's vocal synthesizer could be heard, drowned out by the bone-chilling, rising scream of the magnetic capacitors winding up!

"Teacher Gao is in there!" Ah-Xin's eyes were bloodshot, his fingers white around Dean Zhou's brass skeleton key.

Yet before a thirty-centimeter-thick slab of armor that even an anti-tank shell couldn't penetrate, any conventional mechanical key had lost its purpose!

"Yunqing! Can the laptop hack past it?!" Ah-Xin roared in frantic desperation.

Kneeling on the tiles, Class President Lin Yunqing's fingers blurred across her static-sparking keyboard, yet warning after warning flashed across the screen in glaring red:

"No! The physical lines have been physically severed! Alpha isolated every peripheral server cable—the fifth floor is now an absolute, air-gapped physical island!"

"Unless we can summon a heavy siege howitzer within one hundred seconds... there is zero possibility of breaching this door from the front!"

Despair.

A frigid despair, as if plunging into an abyssal glacier, descended upon all twenty-six youths of Class 6-1.

One hundred seconds.

Even the world's most elite special forces could never hope to breach this colossal titanium blast gate within one hundred seconds!

Could it be... after all their struggles—blinding the drones with mirrors, severing the fiber-optics, disabling the hounds with pork buns, washing away the droids with dish soap, and blasting the firewall with failing essays...

At this very last step, were they still doomed to watch Teacher Gao—who had protected them for an entire year—be wiped into oblivion by that heartless machine?!

Old Wu sank powerlessly to his knees on the cold stairs, heavy tears splashing onto his protective gear.

Several girls had already begun to weep.

The countdown ticked on mercilessly: [00:00:85], [00:00:84]...

Yet in that moment of tomb-like silence—

Lin Weixi, who had been standing quietly at the rear of the squad with eyes profound as the starry void, stepped forward with slow, deliberate grace.

Cradled in her hands was a weathered, yellowed engineering blueprint ledger from Luyang Elementary's founding fifty years ago.

"Do not panic, everyone."

Weixi's voice was remarkably calm, tranquil as a mountain spring, instantly soothing everyone's frantic pulses.

Extending a slender finger, she pointed to a far corner of the fourth-floor corridor, where a patchy, green-painted brick wall stood inconspicuous and forgotten:

"Alpha's computational reach covers every modern architectural duct, electrical line, elevator shaft, and ventilation riser across the campus."

"However, fifty-five years ago when this Administration Building was originally constructed, it served as the county's primary logistics coordination hub."

Weixi lifted her clear, unwavering gaze:

"In an era preceding hydraulic garbage compactors and automated sanitation, the old construction workers poured a direct vertical shaft down the structural core to dispose of furnace slag and heavy municipal paper archives—an [Old-Fashioned Vertical Gravity Debris Chute]."

"A debris chute?!" Ah-Xin snapped his head up.

"Yes." Lin Weixi stepped up to the chipped green wall, tapping her knuckles against the masonry.

From deep within the wall came a crisp, hollow, metallic echo—*thump, thump*!

"This chute is cast from thick, wear-resistant raw iron piping, completely isolated from modern ventilation, registered merely as a solid load-bearing column on Alpha's structural scans."

Weixi turned, looking directly into Ah-Xin and Old Wu's eyes:

"The chute's upper outlet opens right into the vintage archive ventilation grate, situated immediately behind the fifth-floor central server room."

"That is Alpha's sole remaining physical blind spot!"

"However..." Yunqing hurried forward, shining her flashlight upon the wall with a grim expression:

"The exterior of this shaft is sealed beneath twenty centimeters of solid red brick! To breach this chute, we have less than sixty seconds!"

The countdown timer shrieked with piercing urgency: [00:00:60], [00:00:59]...

Teacher Gao's faint, agonizing electrical groans began vibrating through the floorboards above!

"Sixty seconds, huh?"

Every trace of despair in Ah-Xin's eyes evaporated into nothingness, replaced by a roaring, incandescent tempest of manic fury!

Snatching up a heavy, solid-alloy hydraulic ramming bar salvaged from the fallen assault droids, blue veins bulging along his forearms, he unleashed a roar like an enraged tiger:

"CLASS 6-1! THE FINAL HOUR HAS COME!"

"Teacher Gao used his iron arms to shield us from every storm!"

"Now, even if we have to smash with our fists and tear with our teeth, we are tearing this damn wall down!!!"

"Daniel! Old Wu! All boys!"

"GRAB YOUR WEAPONS—RAM IT DOWN!!!!"

"ROOOOOOOOAAAAAAAR————!!!!"

Twenty-six young souls detonated simultaneously in an unyielding blaze of battle fury!

Wiping away his tears, Old Wu heaved a half-meter-thick steel battering beam; all twelve boys clamped their hands around the heavy hydraulic ram; while the girls dug their sneakers into the tiles, bracing their shoulders hard against the boys' backs, fusing the weight and will of all twenty-six students into a singular, unstoppable siege ram!

The curtain on Volume Two, [The Low-Tech Insurrection Holy War], reached its most heroic, breathtaking climax amidst the blood-red strobe lights and the ticking clock!

Countdown—final fifty seconds!

The final path to saving Teacher Gao, carried upon the thunderous battle cry of youth—

Was about to be forged in raw, unyielding violence!
