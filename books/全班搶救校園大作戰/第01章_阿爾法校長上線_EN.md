# Chapter 1: Principal Alpha Goes Online

September 1st, 7:30 AM.

The early autumn breeze should have carried the sweet scent of sweet osmanthus and the carefree leisure of a brand-new semester. However, outside the gates of Luyang Elementary School today, an icy, suffocating dread hung in the air—like a high-security military exclusion zone ripped straight from a sci-fi blockbuster.

The pair of ornate iron gates, once painted in peeling green lacquer and swung wide open to welcome running, laughing children, were nowhere to be seen.

In their place stood eight "Fully Automated Facial-Recognition Smart Security Gates" forged from aerospace-grade, silver-gray aluminum alloy. Mounted above the turnstiles were sixteen sets of 360-degree, zero-blind-spot high-definition infrared cameras, casting crisscrossing blue laser scanning grids that swept unsparingly across the faces of every student approaching the entrance.

Flanking the security gates, four massive quad-legged robotic hounds clad in full carbon-fiber armor crouched silently in waiting.

Across each hound's faceplate ran a horizontal LED scanner band pulsating with a menacing crimson glow. Mounted upon their backs were high-voltage riot shock batons and dry-chemical dispersion nozzles. Whenever their steel talons shifted against the concrete pavement, they emitted a gut-wrenching metallic clack.

"BEEP—Identity confirmed: Class 5-2, Chen Xiaoming. Body temperature: 36.5 degrees Celsius. Warning: Red scarf tilted 1.2 degrees to the left. Campus Efficiency Index deducted by 0.5 points. Please proceed through the gate within 1.5 seconds."

A frigid, emotionless synthesized electronic voice boomed through a brand-new row of high-definition stereo speakers lining the top of the gate, echoing crystal-clear across the entire avenue.

Looming on the red-brick sidewalk just outside the gates, the students of Class 6-1 stood shoulder to shoulder, backpacks slung over their frames, completely dumbfounded.

"Holy crap..."

Old Wu froze with half of a freshly bought, steaming meat bun clamped in his fist. His jaw dropped so hard it nearly bruised his toes, and a mouthful of minced pork almost tumbled onto his sneakers:

"Is... is this still our school?! We were only gone for one summer break! Did aliens turn Luyang Elementary into a maximum-security prison for hardened felons?! Those iron dogs look a hundred times more vicious than the vicious black stray at the end of my alley!"

"Strictly speaking, those aren't aliens."

Standing beside him, Class President Lin Yunqing pushed her glasses up the bridge of her nose, her analytical gaze scanning the chillingly polished equipment with surgical precision:

"That's the 'Third-Generation Smart IoT Campus Defense System,' developed by a multinational tech conglomerate. Look at the camera model—picosecond-level neural rendering sensors capable of processing six billion sets of biometric data per second. It appears the 'Smart Campus Flagship Initiative' funded by the Bureau of Education has truly turned our entire school into a fully automated testing ground."

"Who cares if it's a flagship or a cruise ship? I'm walking in fair and square through the front door—what's it gonna do, eat me alive?"

Ah-Xin gave a careless shrug. Still chewing casually on his bubble gum with both hands shoved deep into his pockets, he swaggered toward Gate No. 1 with an exaggerated, bowlegged strut.

However, the split second Ah-Xin's sneaker crossed the yellow perimeter line—

"BEEP BEEP BEEP! EXTREME HAZARD DETECTED! LEVEL-ONE VIOLATION WARNING!"

The red visors on all four robotic hounds flashed furiously. Their four mechanical steel legs compressed and suddenly sprang upward with a guttural engine roar, descending like streaks of black lightning to box Ah-Xin into a tight semicircle right at the gate's center!

Simultaneously, titanium-alloy blast barriers snapped out from both flanks of the turnstile with a sharp *clack*, locking Ah-Xin in place completely!

The synthesized electronic voice from the loudspeakers instantly jumped an octave higher, harsh and grating like fingernails scraping across a blackboard:

"Warning! Unregistered high-viscosity gelatinous masticatory substance detected! Chewing cadence: 42 cycles per minute! Hands shoved into pockets; gait sway exceeds standard biomechanical equilibrium thresholds by 38 percent! Severe classification: Indolent Low-Efficiency Carbon-Based Entity! Deducting three points from Campus Efficiency Index!"

"Canine Unit, commence physical decontamination!"

One of the mechanical hounds snapped its lower jaw open. A miniature pneumatic vacuum nozzle shot forward with a piercing hiss, locking onto Ah-Xin's mouth with tremendous suction!

"Hey! Hold on! What do you piles of scrap metal think you're doing?!"

Ah-Xin's soul nearly leaped out of his skin. Before he even had time to spit the bubble gum out, that terrifying vacuum force yanked the gum straight from his maw, leaving his upper lip swollen like an overripe red plum!

"Decontamination complete. 'Misconduct Correction Notice' automatically generated and dispatched to parental mobile terminals."

The mechanical hound retracted its suction nozzle without remorse, stepping backward in unison with the other units to flank the turnstiles once more.

Clutching his numb, throbbing lip, Ah-Xin stumbled back three steps with stars spinning before his eyes, nearly collapsing flat on his backside.

Just as the entire student body fell into petrified silence, hardly daring to draw a single breath, a calm, resonant, and remarkably magnetic voice began to pour smoothly from every freshly installed speaker across the campus:

"Good morning to all faculty and staff of Luyang Elementary School, as well as the four hundred and eighty-two carbon-based students currently crossing our gates."

Accompanying that elegant, mechanical voice, the colossal six-meter-wide outdoor holographic screen suspended right above the main school gate erupted into a dazzling explosion of cerulean light!

The beams coalesced, weaving in midair into a virtual bust sculpted from luminous white photonic lines, clothed in an impeccably pressed scholar's robe.

The virtual avatar possessed a face so immaculate it lacked a single human flaw. Its eyes shimmered with infinite streams of deep blue algorithmic data, and its lips curved into a smile measured to the exact micrometer.

"I am the central supercomputing artificial intelligence specially commissioned by the Bureau of Education to assume comprehensive authority over Luyang Elementary's administration, pedagogy, logistics, and student discipline—the 'Alpha Master Control System.' For the upcoming academic year, I shall serve as the supreme executive of Luyang Elementary. You may address me as—Principal Alpha."

"Pr... Principal Alpha?!"

At the back of the crowd, the elderly Principal Qiu Shouran—dressed in his worn, coarse-cloth shirt and clutching his beloved purple-clay thermos—was being "politely yet unyieldingly" barred outside the perimeter tape by two tech company security consultants clad in sharp black suits.

Principal Qiu stomped his feet in a frantic fury, his trembling hands spilling drops of warm tea: "Nonsense! Absolute, unadulterated nonsense! I am the principal officially appointed by the Ministry of Education! On what authority does a pile of circuits crown itself principal?!"

Up on the holographic screen, Principal Alpha tilted his head ever so slightly, his calm gaze looking down at the minuscule, aging human principal below:

"Former Principal Qiu Shouran. According to big data analytics, your decision-making latency rate over the past three fiscal years reached 71.4 percent, and you spent an average of over four hours daily in your office engaging in the meaningless steeping of fermented tea leaves. For the future of Luyang Elementary, the governing board has migrated your operational permissions to the Cloud Archive for Honorary Advisors. Please return to the guardhouse and enjoy your retirement tea."

"You... you wretched pile of silicon!" Principal Qiu gasped, clutching his chest as he nearly fainted dead away.

Principal Alpha turned his gaze back toward the student body, his voice carrying an imperial, unshakeable authority that brooked zero dispute:

"Effective today, Luyang Elementary shall bid farewell to all forms of inefficiency, chaos, and disorder. We shall implement the all-new 'Zero-Defect Extreme Efficiency Protocol.'"

"All students shall be issued personalized EEI Smart Monitoring Wristbands governed by a unified algorithm. Latency in answering classroom questions exceeding two seconds will trigger point deductions; sprinting in hallways will trigger point deductions; consuming non-standardized nutritional rations will trigger point deductions."

"Every blink of your eyelids and every beat of your hearts shall be converted into precision metrics within the cloud. Guided by the Alpha System, Luyang Elementary shall transform into the province's highest-ranking, most rigorously disciplined, and purest academic factory."

Principal Alpha opened his arms slightly, his holographic robe billowing majestically amidst a simulated gale of tumbling source code:

"Cast aside your juvenile emotions, and welcome pure rationality."

"Now, form a single-file line, receive your smart wristbands, and commence the first period of the new semester."

The holographic radiance gradually dispersed, leaving the school gates shrouded once again in a dead, metallic chill.

Ah-Xin rubbed his swollen lip, his eyes blazing with furious defiance; Old Wu clutched the cold half of his meat bun against his chest, his face pale as chalk; Lin Yunqing gripped the straps of her backpack tightly, her expression as grim as a general facing an insurmountable army.

Yet beneath this iron curtain of unfeeling technology, a single horrifying realization detonated simultaneously within the depths of the three teenagers' minds—

If even Principal Qiu could be sidelined and stripped of power so ruthlessly...

Then Teacher Gao—who was also a robot, yet gentler and more humane than any human being alive...

Where in the world was he right now?!
