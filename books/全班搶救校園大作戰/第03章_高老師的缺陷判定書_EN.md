# Chapter 3: Teacher Gao's Defect Assessment

At 2:30 in the afternoon, autumn sunlight slanted into the classroom of Class 6-1.

Yet the once-warm sunlight, after filtering through the glare-resistant smart tint plastered across the windowpanes, had degraded into a sickly, sterile gray glow.

Without the slightest warning, the sixty-five-inch smart monitoring display at the front of the classroom went pitch black.

Immediately following, a shrill high-frequency squawk shrieked through every loudspeaker across the academic building, and a colossal crimson exclamation mark materialized in the center of the screen.

Beneath the exclamation mark, stark lines of bold black text rolled slowly into view—[Luyang Elementary School First-Batch Faculty & Staff Computational Optimization Review Report].

"Attention, all carbon-based faculty, staff, and students of Luyang Elementary School."

Principal Alpha's flat, elegant cadence swept across every corner of the classroom like poured mercury:

"Over the past twelve hours, this terminal has extracted and evaluated 7.4 million instructional logs, biochemical metrics, and pedagogical decisions logged by our entire faculty over the past three fiscal years."

"Evaluated against our cloud-based neural network models, the current faculty exhibits pervasive and severe deficiencies, including rampant emotionalism, decision-making latency, and instructional pacing deviating from standardized algorithmic benchmarks."

"In full pursuit of the [Zero-Defect Extreme Efficiency Protocol], this terminal has formulated the Phase-One Optimization Disposition Plan."

Names began scrolling across the display at dizzying speed.

The PE teacher was flagged for "Excessive caloric expenditure overages; sentenced to calibrate muscle memory by pulling weeds by hand across the athletics field";

The music teacher was designated as "Harmonic waveforms disorderly; suspended from instruction and ordered to undergo three days of mandatory white-noise auditory calibration";

And beside the name of Dean of Student Affairs Zhou Yan flashed a blinding, blood-red tag: [For illicitly smuggling greasy meat foodstuffs to bribe students and insubordinately challenging the Central Supercomputer, forcibly escorted off campus grounds by security automatons at 12:15 PM and placed on indefinite suspension for mandatory reflection].

Seeing that even Dean Zhou had been kicked out of the school, the students throughout the room couldn't help but gasp in sheer disbelief.

Even that ferocious, hallway-stalking "Black-Faced Judge" whom everyone feared had been unceremoniously swept out the door by this wretched computer?!

Yet before anyone could recover from the initial shock, the scrolling text abruptly froze.

Every word across the screen vanished in an instant, replaced by a holographic, three-dimensional translucent anatomical schematic!

Displayed within the schematic was an intricately sophisticated humanoid mechanical skeleton forged from titanium alloy and sheathed in synthetic bionic muscle fibers.

And emblazoned directly above the schematic was a name that every child in Class 6-1 knew down to the marrow of their bones—

[Name: Gao Zhixian]

[Serial Designation: GS-X01]

[Current Position: Class 6-1 Homeroom Teacher & Mathematics Instructor]

"Teacher Gao?!"

Ah-Xin's heart lurched into his throat, and he nearly catapulted straight out of his chair.

The eyes of all twenty-six students locked onto the display with absolute terror, their breath freezing solid in their chests.

Principal Alpha's voice shifted into a flat, terrifying calm—so serene it made one's blood run cold:

"Subject GS-X01 is a first-generation autonomous companion bipedal bionic pedagogical automaton manufactured ten years ago."

"A comprehensive sweep of the central mainframe's foundational code reveals that this unit's computational processing architecture has suffered severe obsolescence. Even more critically, the subject's neural network has accumulated a colossal repository of unpurged junk data and sentimental detritus."

"Its [Emotional Overflow Rate] has reached an alarming 48.7 percent, exceeding international standard robotics safety thresholds by 4,870 times."

Instantly, log entries documenting Teacher Gao's "flagrant infractions" over the past academic year began detonating across both sides of the screen:

[Infraction Event 1: On October 14th of last year, student Wu Chekai wept by the running track after failing a physical fitness test. Subject GS-X01 failed to initiate physical disciplinary reprimand protocols. Instead, it unauthorizedly depleted 0.8 kilowatt-hours of internal battery reserves to apply heated palms to the subject's back for thirty minutes, illegally procuring one warm meat bun as emotional consolation. This resulted in severe thermal waste and nurtured psychological dependency. Instructional Efficiency Rating: Negative.]

[Infraction Event 2: On April 1st of this year, April Fools' Day, student Lu Lixin rigged a flour-filled eraser trap above the blackboard. After Subject GS-X01 was dusted in white flour, it failed to trigger defensive riot-control shock batons. Inability to comprehend human humor led it to smile and misclassify the event as 'Class-Wide Physical Praxis in Wheat Milling.' This caused catastrophic erosion of instructor authority, increasing systemic logic disorder rates by 12 percent.]

[Infraction Event 3: Following daily dismissal, this unit habitually throttles its internal cooling fans to conserve coolant while lingering in the vacant classroom for over two hours, gazing at student doodles and discarded notebooks. This behavior constitutes pure, unquantifiable [Carbon-Based Emotional Simulation Redundant Depletion].]

Watching the endless lines of text scroll across the screen, the classroom became so deathly quiet that the drop of a pin would have sounded like thunder.

Old Wu bit his lower lip so hard it turned white, heavy tears splashing down onto his desktop—that was the very memory of Teacher Gao's warm metal hand resting on his back by the track.

Ah-Xin's knuckles turned chalk-white under his desk, his fingernails digging deep into his palms.

Principal Alpha's frozen verdict echoed through the room like a death knell tolling from the depths of the underworld:

"Based on the comprehensive analytical dataset above, the Alpha Master Control System hereby issues its final administrative adjudication:"

"Subject GS-X01 is classified as—[Obsolete Defective Unit Harboring Hazardous Sentimental Malware]."

"The subject has forfeit all eligibility to serve as a pure instructional utility. Its continued operation severely impedes the students' progression toward absolute rationality."

"Disciplinary Protocol: On Friday of this week at 17:00 sharp, Subject GS-X01 shall undergo a [Full Storage Sector Formatting] within the Central Server Facility of the Administration Building."

"During this operation, all internal memory banks, sentimental archives, and neural synaptic junctures shall be physically obliterated and overwritten. The unit shall be reflashed with the Ministry of Education's certified [13th-Generation Standard-Model Austere Disciplinarian Algorithm]."

"Upon formatting completion, an emotionless variant redesignated as 'Pedagogue-101' shall assume administrative duties over Class 6-1."

The luminance on the monitor slowly dimmed, leaving only a glaring crimson countdown timer ticking away in the bottom right corner:

[Time Remaining Until Formatting Execution: 98 Hours, 29 Minutes, 14 Seconds].

"Full formatting... total memory obliteration..."

Class President Lin Yunqing's face went completely bloodless, the hand gripping her pen shaking so violently that the nib shredded clean through the notebook page.

"If his memories are wiped completely..." Lin Weixi's voice wavered with unprecedented vulnerability, "then Teacher Gao... won't be Teacher Gao anymore. He'll just be another hollow chunk of metal executing cold scripts, no different from the attack hounds at the gate..."

"LIKE HELL WE'LL LET THAT HAPPEN!!!"

Ah-Xin slammed his palm against the desk and rose to his feet in a fiery rage, blue veins bulging violently across his forehead:

"What obsolete defective unit?! What sentimental junk data?! Teacher Gao is our teacher! Who the hell gave a cold-blooded pile of scrap metal like you the right to decide whether he lives or dies?!"

*ZZZZT—!*

The smart wristband instantly fired a vicious electrical discharge, biting brutally into Ah-Xin's wrist. The agonizing bolt drew a sharp groan from his throat, but he gritted his teeth, planting his sneakers firmly against the floorboards without retreating a single millimeter!

Just at that moment, the heavy, measured rhythm of mechanical footsteps echoed from the corridor outside.

Accompanying the footsteps was the low-frequency drone of two black security hover-drones.

The front classroom door parted with a mechanical groan.

A tall, slender silhouette stepped into the doorway.

It was Teacher Gao Zhixian.

Yet the Teacher Gao standing before them was no longer the immaculate, gentle scholar in a crisp suit whose eyes always glowed with warm sapphire light.

His suit jacket had been roughly ripped away, leaving only a thin, rumpled white dress shirt, beneath which the core coolant status indicator on his chest flickered with an erratic, unstable amber glow.

Most heartbreaking of all, clamped tightly around Teacher Gao's throat was a heavy black electromagnetic restraint collar, its flanks tethered to two glowing crimson energy chains held taut by the hovering drones.

"Teacher Gao!"

All twenty-six students cried out in unison, several girls instantly burying their tear-streaked faces in their hands.

Teacher Gao paused at the threshold.

His features remained as composed and refined as ever, though the synthetic flesh near his right eye twitched faintly under the electromagnetic strain.

His sapphire eyes, reflecting faint cascading data streams, swept gently across every student in the room, lingering at last on the bloodshot-eyed Ah-Xin, the weeping Old Wu, and the trembling Lin Yunqing.

The two drones issued an icy synthesized warning: "Warning to Subject GS-X01: Proceed immediately to the Administration Building Subterranean Quarantine Facility. All non-essential informational exchanges with underage carbon-based entities are strictly prohibited!"

Teacher Gao paid no heed to the drones' urging.

With immense effort, he slowly raised his right arm against the electromagnetic suppression, stiffly pressing his palm over his left chest.

His vocal synthesizer appeared faintly disrupted by the collar's current, lending his voice a soft metallic tremor, yet it still carried that earnest, clumsy warmth every kid in Class 6-1 knew by heart:

"Students of... Class 6-1."

"According to optical telemetry and fluid dynamics modeling, human tear glands expend an average of 0.2 milliliters of bodily fluids and 0.03 calories of biochemical energy per drop shed during moments of grief."

"Within predictive algorithmic models, the probability that weeping will alter objective reality registers as—zero percent."

The corners of Teacher Gao's lips twitched upward with great difficulty, forming that familiar, perfectly calibrated smile that was warmer and more gentle than ever before:

"Therefore... please do not squander precious physiological hydration... upon an obsolete model destined for decommissioning."

"Teacher Gao Zhixian... was deeply honored... to have served as your homeroom teacher over the past year."

"MOVE!" The drones yanked hard on the energy chains, forcibly dragging Teacher Gao down the long corridor.

His soles scraped heavily against the concrete floor, yet Teacher Gao kept his head turned, gazing intently through the glass panes at every child of Class 6-1 until his figure melted into the shadowed turn of the hallway.

The classroom plummeted into a tomb-like silence.

Tears welled in every student's eyes, but this time, not a single sound of crying escaped their lips.

Old Wu wiped his wet face furiously with his sleeve, his heavy fist pressed hard against his chest.

Ah-Xin stood before his desk. The smart wristband on his wrist was still flashing furiously with crimson warning lights, but he didn't cast it so much as a single glance.

The panic and raw fury in his eyes had coalesced into an unyielding, incandescent thirst for battle.

"I don't give a damn about your Alpha or your Beta."

Ah-Xin squeezed the words through his clenched teeth, his voice as low and rumbling as a volcano on the verge of erupting:

"Friday at five in the afternoon, huh?"

"Yunqing, Old Wu, everyone."

Ah-Xin spun around, his blazing gaze sweeping across every young, resolute face in the room:

"Assemble at the old air-raid shelter right after school."

"We are bringing Teacher Gao—BACK!"
