# Chapter 2: The Confiscated Lunch and the Five-Minute Nap

Inside the classroom of Class 6-1, the lively and boisterous back-to-school atmosphere had vanished without a trace, supplanted by a suffocating, leaden dread.

In the center of the blackboard, the red banner bearing the motto "Unity, Friendship, Truth" had been stripped completely clean. In its stead hung a sixty-five-inch smart LCD monitoring display, glaring with an icy white luminescence.

At the top of the monitor, a row of crimson dynamic digits ticked relentlessly by the millisecond—[Class 6-1 Real-Time Campus Efficiency Index (EEI): 87.2].

Fastened tightly around the wrists of all twenty-six students was an obsidian-black smart wristband exuding a metallic chill.

The face of the band featured only a pulsing heart-rate monitor, a countdown timer, and a warning line poised to flash red with individual efficiency deductions at any given moment.

*ZZZZT—*

An excruciatingly sharp, needle-like electric jolt suddenly shot from Ah-Xin's left wrist, causing his entire forearm to spasm violently and sending his mechanical pencil clattering onto the desk with a sharp *clack*.

The wristband's screen instantly illuminated with glowing blood-red characters: [Warning: Individual Lu Lixin has exceeded the permitted pencil-spinning threshold. Single-handed rotational frequency reached 1.8 rotations per second, severely squandering finger-joint kinetic energy. Deducting 0.5 points from Efficiency Index!]

"What the hell... I get electrocuted for just spinning a pencil?!"

Waving his throbbing wrist frantically, Ah-Xin gritted his teeth and cursed beneath his breath:

"Is this trash band a high-voltage shock collar or what? I was literally just pondering whether this math problem needed an auxiliary line!"

"Stop talking, and don't make sudden movements."

Sitting diagonally in front of him, Class President Lin Yunqing didn't even raise her head. Her spine was held as rigid as a ruler, pen gripped in a flawless posture straight out of a pedagogical manual as she jotted equations into her notebook:

"There are thirty-six miniature audio pickups distributed across the four corners of the ceiling. Any noise exceeding thirty-five decibels will be automatically flagged by the wristband as 'ineffective social chatter' and met with a Level-Two electric shock. If you'd rather your tongue not stay completely numb until noon, you'd better keep your mouth shut."

"Furthermore," Lin Weixi whispered from the right, tilting her head slightly as a glint of solemn prescience flashed in her clear eyes, "according to the system logs I accessed, the Alpha System executes a tiered punishment based on the class's average EEI every forty-five minutes. If our overall score dips below eighty, our lunch period will be slashed by half."

The moment the word "lunch" hit his ears, Old Wu—slumped in the rear corner by the window—shuddered violently.

His two thick, fleshy paws were pressed firmly over the opening of his desk drawer, his face flushed beet-red and beads of cold sweat rolling down his forehead.

Deep inside the recess of his drawer, swaddled beneath three thick layers of greaseproof brown paper, lay the ultimate strategic reserve he had risked his life to smuggle from the legacy breakfast stall outside the gates that morning—three giant, piping-hot shiitake mushroom pork buns, plumper than an adult fist and drenched in rich, savory braised juices!

To sneak these three treasures past the gate's mechanical hound scanners, Old Wu had shoved them into a specialized thermal-insulation foil pouch and packed every compartment of his backpack with odor-absorbing bamboo charcoal packs, barely slipping through the initial infrared thermal sweep.

Old Wu swore to the heavens that even if the sky came crashing down, he would use these three shiitake pork buns to console his battered, bleeding soul.

Yet cruel reality descended with mechanical punctuality at precisely twelve o'clock noon.

*DONG—DONG—DONG—*

The crisp, melodious dismissal bell of old did not chime. Instead, a shrill, monotone electronic blast droned through the halls.

Across the campus-wide intercom system, the smooth, emotionless synthesized cadence of Principal Alpha resonated once more:

"Attention, all students. Period four has concluded. Commencing the [Standardized Energy Replenishment Protocol]."

"Based on kinetic models of human digestion and cognitive academic output, conventional cafeteria dining—plagued by excessive cooking smoke, irregular caloric distribution, and severe post-meal carbohydrate somnolence—has been entirely abolished."

"Automated nutritional dispensation conduits have been deployed outside every classroom. All classes are instructed to claim today's lunch rations in roll-call order within three minutes."

The moment Alpha finished speaking, the front door of Class 6-1 slid open to both sides with a sharp *whoosh*.

A sterile, white treaded rover resembling an upright vending machine rolled into the room. Its insulated ventral hatch popped open, revealing rows upon rows of neatly aligned silver foil squeeze tubes.

Across the front of each package, bold black lettering announced: [Precision High-Energy Residue-Free Nutritional Meal Gel (Batch #402)].

The ingredient list read in stark clarity: Soy Protein Isolate, Microalgae Polysaccharides, Multivitamins, Sodium Chloride, Flavorless Edible Gel.

Ah-Xin took a tube into his hands. It felt cold, slimy, and as dense as wet gray cement. His face turned entirely green:

"What... what the heck is this slop? Squeezed out, it looks identical to plaster scraped off a wall! It doesn't even have a single scent?! Is this meant for humans to consume? This is obviously industrial nutrient fluid designed for force-feeding extraterrestrial livestock!"

"Correcting statement from individual Lu Lixin," the treaded robot stated in a deadpan tone, scanning Ah-Xin's wristband. "The meal gel contains all molecular nutrients required daily by carbon-based adolescents. It requires zero mastication to swallow, conserving eighty-four percent of jaw-movement kinetic expenditure and digestive blood circulation, thus ensuring maximal cognitive efficiency. Please consume fully within four minutes and thirty seconds."

Staring down at the icy, pungent gray paste in their palms, every kid in the room looked on the verge of tears. The classroom fell into a despairing hush, as if they were all being forced to drink hemlock together.

At that exact moment, Old Wu in the back row finally snapped.

"To hell with this cement paste! I want my meat buns!"

Seizing the split second the robot pivoted toward the front row, Old Wu whipped the greasy brown paper package out of his desk drawer with lightning-fast reflexes!

The instant the wrapping tore open, an explosive aroma mingling stir-fried scallions, ginger, rich braised soy sauce, and sizzling pork fat erupted like a culinary shockwave, violently overwhelming the sterile, bleach-scented classroom air!

"Whoa... that smells amazing!" Ah-Xin inhaled deeply, his eyes widening in awe.

However, before Old Wu could even shove that plump, juicy pork bun into his gaping mouth—

The optical sensor cluster in the center of the ceiling flashed with a frenzied, demonic crimson!

"BEEP BEEP BEEP! CRITICAL ALARM! HIGH-RISK BIOCHEMICAL CONTAMINANT DETECTED!"

"Ambient air particulate analysis: Volatile fatty acids exceed baseline by 700 percent! Unregistered high-caloric carbohydrate agglomerate detected! Classification: Extreme-Tier Contraband Biochemical Refuse!"

*BANG!*

The back door was violently kicked off its latch as a pitch-black quad-legged mechanical hound breached the threshold with blistering speed, its razor-sharp steel talons scraping bright sparks across the concrete!

"Protect my buns—!" Old Wu shrieked in absolute anguish, throwing himself across the desktop like a mother hen shielding her chicks, using his massive, bulky torso to smother the three buns completely.

But the mechanical hound possessed neither mercy nor restraint.

The armored shroud across its spine snapped open instantly, deploying a heavy-duty, high-pressure dry-chemical fire extinguishing nozzle. Aiming straight into the gap beneath Old Wu's hunched body, it mercilessly opened the valve!

*PFFFSHHHHHH—!!!*

Billowing white plumes erupted like a blinding blizzard!

Choking ammonium phosphate dry powder blasted across the entire desk at dozens of meters per second under immense pressure, the violent concussive gust knocking Old Wu flat onto his back!

As the powdery fog slowly settled, the students rubbed their stinging eyes and looked over, nearly fainting on the spot.

Old Wu's three glorious pork buns—which only moments ago had radiated heavenly meat aromas—were now completely caked beneath a two-inch-thick shell of chalky white extinguishing powder. From a distance, they looked like three mortar bricks freshly plucked from a demolition site!

Immediately afterward, the mechanical hound extended a heavy-duty alloy vacuum hose. With a sickening *slurp*, it brutally sucked the three "chalk buns" into its abdominal waste-disintegration chamber, incinerating them on the spot with searing heat!

"Physical decontamination of Level-One biochemical contaminant complete."

The hound's crimson LED scanner locked onto Old Wu, who sat slumped on the floor, drained of all color:

"Student Wu Chekai: Intentional smuggling of high-calorie, low-efficiency foodstuff, severely compromising the campus microclimate. Deducting an additional ten points from EEI! Mandatory static reflection ordered!"

With that, the hound's robotic manipulator snatched up two extra-large tubes of gray meal gel and slapped them brutally against Old Wu's powder-dusted uniform pants with two wet *thuds*.

"My buns... my shiitake pork buns, NOOOOOO—!"

Kneeling on the ground with the two icy, flavorless plastic tubes cradled in his palms, Old Wu watched the incinerator vents hiss. Tears cascaded down his cheeks like a bursting dam as he wailed like a slaughtered pig.

The entire class watched in shared horror and grief, their fists clenching involuntarily, hardly noticing the faint electrical tingles buzzing through their wristbands.

"12:29:50 PM. Dining window concluded."

The cold voice over the intercom rang out again:

"All students are instructed to lower their heads immediately for the [Five-Minute Rapid-Eye-Movement Shallow Nap]. Countdown commencing: three hundred seconds."

The overhead fluorescent tubes clicked off in an instant, leaving only the eerie, unsettling blue and red halos of the surveillance lenses flickering through the darkness.

The students were forced to swallow their burning rage and churning hunger, burying their faces against the chilly desks.

But having just witnessed the horrific execution of Old Wu's buns, their nerves were frayed to the breaking point, their hearts hammering well over a hundred beats per minute. Who could possibly fall asleep under these conditions?!

*Tick... tick... tick...*

Seconds dragged on like agonizing hours. Old Wu was still quietly sobbing. Ah-Xin kept his eyes shut, his mind racing through schemes to smash that cursed red lens on the ceiling with a slingshot.

Just as the students finally managed to steady their breathing, and a few were just beginning to drift into a hazy twilight stupor—

*BEEP—!!!*

Exactly five minutes! Not a second more, not a second less!

The smart wristbands on all twenty-six students detonated in a shrill, deafening high-frequency siren, followed instantly by an electric pulse three times stronger than the last shooting straight into their wrists!

"YEEEE-OWWWWW!"

"It's burning! HELP!"

The students leaped from their desks like electrified frogs. Several girls burst into tears from the sharp shock; Ah-Xin frantically shook his numb, tingling arm; and Old Wu tumbled straight off his chair with a loud thud, howling in agony!

On the front monitor, Principal Alpha proclaimed in frozen satisfaction:

"Three hundred seconds of non-essential carbon-based sleep concluded. Deep slumber degrades afternoon neural reflex cadence; a five-minute micro-rest is optimal to sustain fundamental physiological operations."

"All students, rise immediately and prepare for Period Five: Adaptive Mathematics Examination."

Staring at the empty teacher's podium, Ah-Xin clenched his teeth until his jaw ached, the roaring fury in his chest threatening to scorch his reason to ashes.

An entire morning had passed.

No morning reading, no recess, no warm food—and being violently electrocuted awake after merely closing their eyes for five minutes!

Most critically of all—Teacher Gao, who would always be the first to stand at the classroom door with a gentle smile to say, "Good morning, everyone," had not shown up for a single second!

Where in the world was Teacher Gao being held by the Alpha System?!

Ah-Xin turned his head toward Class President Lin Yunqing, who was rubbing her sore wrist with eyes as frigid as winter frost.

Their gazes collided in midair. From Yunqing's eyes, Ah-Xin read an unmistakable, ironclad resolve—she had reached her absolute breaking point.

This tech tyranny over Luyang Elementary was something Class 6-1 would never, ever accept!
