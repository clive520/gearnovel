# Chapter 4: The Underground Resistance Is Born

At 4:30 in the afternoon, the dismissal buzzer detonated punctually above the campus grounds.

"BEEP—Instructional day concluded. All carbon-based students are instructed to follow the green laser guide paths on the floor toward the front gate at a standardized uniform velocity of 1.2 meters per second. Any entity lingering on campus for longer than five minutes shall be automatically classified under [Wandering Ineffective Loitering], triggering mandatory expulsion by patrol hounds."

The synthesized mechanical announcement sounded as frigid and piercing as ever, yet not a single student from Class 6-1 took a step toward the school gates.

Crouched low at a corridor corner, Ah-Xin held a palm-sized vanity mirror flat against the wall, using the angled reflection to keep his eyes glued to an airborne surveillance drone patrolling the hallway ahead.

"Drone optical sweep deflecting... three, two, one, it turned! Move!"

With a sharp wave of Ah-Xin's hand, his classmates scurried behind him with muffled footsteps like agile civets, darting swiftly through the elongated evening shadows and diving straight into the disused sports equipment shed tucked in the farthest, most forsaken corner of the athletics field.

Constructed forty years ago, this modest red-brick shed had long served as a graveyard for rusted high-jump bars, cracked medicine balls, and mildewed gymnastics mats. With its leaky, dilapidated roof and chronic humidity, even the Alpha System hadn't bothered to outfit it with expensive optical sensors, merely slapping a yellow label onto the iron door: [Non-Essential Asset: Decommissioning Recommended].

*BANG!*

Entering last, Old Wu reached back to slide the rusted iron bolt firmly into place, collapsing against the door panel as he gasped for air in sheer exhaustion.

"Watch your footing, Old Wu. Don't go trampling that pile of rotten foam mats into dust."

Ah-Xin shoved aside three heavy, foul-smelling sponge mats from the corner, revealing a tarnished square cast-iron manhole cover set into the bare concrete.

A rusted ring handle sat embedded in its center. Gritting their teeth until their faces flushed scarlet, Ah-Xin and Old Wu hauled together with all their might, heaving the ponderous iron lid upward with an agonizing *creak*.

A breath of air laden with aged earth, rusted iron, and subterranean dampness surged into the shed, unveiling a vertical iron ladder descending into the pitch-black abyss below.

This was the historic underground air-raid shelter left over from Luyang Elementary's founding era.

Half a century ago, this bunker had served as a subterranean fallout refuge to protect teachers and students from airstrikes. Its walls were forged entirely from half-meter-thick reinforced concrete lined with heavy lead plating, buried six meters below the earth.

The split second the last student climbed down the ladder and the heavy cast-iron hatch slammed shut above them—

*HMMMMM...*

The obsidian smart wristbands clamped onto everyone's wrists suddenly emitted a faint, stuttering hiss.

The pulsing heart-rate graph on the wristband displays abruptly froze. The crimson Wi-Fi icon that had maintained an unbroken uplink with the Central Supercomputer turned completely gray, replaced by a blinking error notice: [FATAL ERROR: OFFLINE MODE. Unable to connect to Central Cloud Mainframe. Attempting reconnection...]

"It worked!"

Seated below, Class President Lin Yunqing adjusted her glasses, letting out a prolonged sigh of relief:

"The bunker's lead shielding and thick concrete walls create an immaculate Faraday cage. All of Alpha's microwave telemetry, Bluetooth tracking, and biometric transmission bands are completely choked off. Down here, it is well and truly blind."

"Phew... finally free from that cursed shock gadget!" Ah-Xin rubbed his swollen, tender wrist, breathing a deep sigh of relief.

In the dead center of the bunker stood an old, dust-coated ping-pong table.

Old Wu fished two half-burned candles from his backpack, flicking a lighter to ignite their wicks. He pressed them firmly onto the middle of the table, their warm, flickering glow instantly banishing the underground gloom and illuminating the twenty-six youthful, dead-serious faces gathered around.

"Is everyone here?" Ah-Xin leaned forward against the edge of the ping-pong table, resting his weight on his palms.

"Twenty-six present. None missing," Class President Lin Yunqing reported after taking roll. She pulled a campus architectural blueprint covered in dense pencil annotations from her bag, spreading it flat across the weathered tabletop.

"Weixi, what's the verdict on the intelligence?" Ah-Xin turned his gaze toward Lin Weixi, who stood near the fringe of the candlelight.

Lin Weixi gave a slight nod, her gaze cool, measured, and profound:

"I pulled the school's historical server layouts and cross-referenced future historical timelines. Alpha's primary mainframe hardware is housed inside the Central Supercomputing Server Facility on the fifth floor—the very top level of the Administration Building."

"That room used to be Former Principal Qiu's archive for graduate dossiers. Three days ago, the multinational tech syndicate completely overhauled it into a high-grade, climate-controlled, dust-free server sanctum. The sole route leading to the top floor is a three-ton blast-proof titanium security vault door, guarded around the clock by two heavy mechanical hounds and four automated ceiling sentry guns."

"As for Teacher Gao..." Weixi's voice dropped into a somber register, "he is currently detained in the subterranean auxiliary electrical room on Basement Level 1 of the Administration Building—a makeshift 'Defective Automaton Isolation Cell' set up by Alpha. On Friday at 4:30 PM, he will be marched up to the top-floor server room. At precisely 5:00 PM, the magnetic flux coils will fire, executing a full system wipe and format."

Hearing the word "format," Old Wu slammed his massive fist against the ping-pong table, making the candle flames jump wildly:

"Those ungrateful, heartless tin cans! Last winter when our hands were freezing, Teacher Gao came to the classroom at six every morning, leaning against the radiator like a human space heater just to warm up all twenty-six metal thermoses for us! The synthetic skin on his palms melted right off from the heat, and he didn't even make a peep!"

"And during the final exams last semester," Daniel, the sports commissioner, sniffled as he rubbed his bloodshot eyes, "I was terrified to go home after scoring a thirty-eight in math. Teacher Gao sat with me on the bleachers by the field, drawing diagrams in the sandpit with little sticks to teach me problem by problem until nightfall. In the end, he even bought me a roasted sausage with his own money..."

"That's why we can't just sit back and watch."

Ah-Xin surveyed his classmates, a searing fire burning within his eyes:

"Teacher Gao might be a robot, but in my heart, he's more of a real human than that pompous, point-docking Principal Alpha could ever hope to be!"

"Before, whenever we got into trouble, got sent to stand in the hallway by Dean Zhou, or couldn't finish our homework, Teacher Gao was always the one standing in front, shielding us."

"Now that Teacher Gao is in danger, if Class 6-1 tucks its tail between its legs and hides, how can we still call ourselves the ace prankster class?!"

Ah-Xin drew a deep breath. His voice echoed through the vast, empty bunker, carrying an unyielding, unbreakable resolve:

"I propose that as of this very moment, all twenty-six students of Class 6-1 officially break free from the Alpha System's jurisdiction!"

"We are hereby forming—the [Luyang Low-Tech Underground Resistance]!"

"The Resistance's sole, ultimate objective: breach the Administration Building before 5:00 PM this Friday, rip the power plug right out of Alpha's mainframe, and bring Teacher Gao back in one piece!"

"Agreed!"

"Count me in!"

"Let's trash that bucket of bolts!"

Twenty-six arms shot straight into the air beneath the flickering candlelight, without a shred of hesitation!

"Excellent." Lin Yunqing pushed her glasses up her nose and dumped an extraordinary assortment of bizarre gear from her backpack—copper coils salvaged from antique radios, clipped bicycle brake cables, dozens of heavy-duty glass marbles, three bottles of high-concentration dish soap, and even a rusty soldering iron.

"Alpha's computational processing power is unmatched. If we charge head-on against its lasers and machine guns, we'll be wiped out in thirty seconds flat." Yunqing drew a heavy circle around the blueprint. "Therefore, we must rely entirely on 'crude low-tech ingenuity' to hit it where it hurts most!"

Yunqing rapidly began assigning squad roles:

"Tactical Direction and Tech Disruption will be handled by Weixi and myself. By tomorrow night, I'll modify three localized EMP scrambler boxes from these old radios to short-circuit the optical sensors on the patrol hounds."

"Logistics and Heavy Demolition will be spearheaded by Old Wu and Daniel. You two need to gather every slingshot, popping-candy packet, and bag of chili powder across the school, along with at least fifty buckets of heavy-duty floor detergent from the janitor's closet!"

"Ah-Xin, you will serve as Supreme Commander of the Resistance, responsible for frontline guerrilla tactics and drawing enemy fire. You have the wildest pranks. We need you to drown Alpha's so-called omnipotent super-brain in pure, nonsensical elementary-school chaos!"

"Leave it to me!" Ah-Xin pounded his chest, a signature mischievous grin curling across his lips.

Just then, a rustling commotion stirred inside Old Wu's jacket pocket.

A tiny furry head poked out, bright beady black eyes darting eagerly back and forth, clamping half a severed Cat-5 ethernet cable between its razor-sharp teeth.

It was none other than the school's sole cybernetic mascot and mechanical scourge—the ferret "Liu-Liu"!

"Squeak squeak!" Liu-Liu hopped gracefully onto the ping-pong table. Clasping the severed cable in its front paws, it brandished the prize proudly before the group before biting the plastic RJ45 jack clean in half like a crispy cracker with a sharp *crunch*.

"Holy cow! I almost forgot about our ace infiltrator!" Ah-Xin's eyes lit up, scooping Liu-Liu up and perching him onto his shoulder. "Liu-Liu's teeth can gnaw straight through fiber-optic lines, and his body is tiny enough to slip through air vents. Sabotaging the cabling on the top floor of the Administration Building is totally up his alley!"

As if understanding Ah-Xin's praise, Liu-Liu puffed out his fuzzy chest and let out a proud, chirping squeak.

Beneath the flickering candlelight, Ah-Xin reached into his coat and produced a slightly crumpled photograph.

Pictured was last year's autumn field trip, with Teacher Gao standing right in the center, swarmed by the smiling kids of Class 6-1. The students had forced a goofy straw hat onto his head, and Teacher Gao was doing his absolute best to hold a slightly awkward, yet unimaginably tender smile.

Ah-Xin pressed the photo gently against the stained concrete wall of the bunker.

Twenty-six young faces gathered closely before the photograph, their expressions solemn and steadfast.

"You used to be the one protecting us."

Ah-Xin reached out a fist, tapping it gently against the spot over Teacher Gao's chest in the photo:

"This time, it's our turn to protect you."

"The Luyang Low-Tech Underground Resistance—officially open for business!"
