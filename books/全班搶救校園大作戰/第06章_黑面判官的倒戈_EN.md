# Chapter 6: Defection of the Black-Faced Judge

Thursday afternoon, 3:00 PM. Exactly twenty-six hours remained until Teacher Gao's scheduled "Full Storage Sector Formatting" on Friday at 5:00 PM.

Though the campus anti-riot sirens had not blared, the invisible, suffocating dread—tense as an electrified high-voltage fence—weighed upon every chest until breathing itself became an ordeal.

Having survived on four consecutive days of icy, flavorless meal gel resembling liquefied plaster, Class 6-1's Chief Logistics Officer, Old Wu, finally collided with the physiological limits of his carbon-based anatomy.

At this very moment, sprawled across a wooden bench in the designated "Low-Energy Static Respiration Zone" along the perimeter of the athletics field, Old Wu looked as sallow as parchment. He lay slumped limp against the wooden slats, his eyes sunken, his lips parched and chalky white, his belly rumbling with thunderous, convulsive spasms of starvation.

"Ah... Ah-Xin..." Old Wu reached out a trembling, pudgy hand to clutch the hem of Ah-Xin's sleeve, his voice as faint as drifting gossamer, "I... I think my soul is detaching from my flesh... All I can see dancing across the sky are steaming shiitake pork buns, crispy roast ducks, and glistening braised spare ribs..."

"Old Wu, hold it together! You're our heavy siege battering ram! If you keel over now, who's going to haul the rubber cords for tomorrow's bun catapult?!" Ah-Xin paced anxiously in circles, though his own stomach let out a rebellious growl in sympathy.

Right at that moment, from the rusty iron palisade fence just behind the bench—cloaked beneath a dense curtain of overgrown ivy along the campus perimeter wall—an uncanny rustling sound began to stir.

In the next second, a head crowned with a faded newsboy cap, bristling thick eyebrows, and a complexion dark as charcoal shoved its way aggressively through the ivy leaves!

"Hey! You little brats! Over here!"

A hushed, hoarse roar—gravelly yet packed with terrifying authority—detonated like a suppressed gunshot.

Ah-Xin and Old Wu jumped nearly out of their skins. Whipping around and squinting through the foliage, they nearly screamed out loud.

That face, black as the soot at the bottom of an iron cauldron; those glaring, bronze-bell eyes; and that iconic, deep triple-crease scowl stamped across the forehead—

It was none other than Luyang Elementary's supreme tyrant, the former Dean of Student Affairs expelled by security automatons just yesterday noon—the "Black-Faced Judge," Zhou Yan!

"D-Dean Zhou?!" Old Wu nearly rolled straight off the bench in terror, instinctively shielding his skull with both arms. "I wasn't running! I wasn't telling jokes in the hall! I swear I haven't touched a single snack today!"

"Shut up, you idiot! I'm not the dean anymore—cut that crap out!"

Leaning close against the gap between the iron pickets, Dean Zhou cast a sharp, vigilant glance left and right, confirming that the surveillance drone hovering overhead was currently sweeping the far side of the track.

Then, with the stealthy, agile deftness of an experienced cat burglar, this terrifying disciplinary dean—infamous across the campus for mercilessly confiscating snacks—promptly unzipped the battered dark-brown leather jacket he was wearing.

Reaching into an interior breast pocket, he gingerly extracted a dense bundle swaddled in multiple layers of heavy thermal aluminum foil.

Peeling back a corner of the foil, a violent, intoxicating wave of heat steeped in star anise, cinnamon, rich dark soy sauce, and succulent chicken fat surged straight through the iron bars!

Revealed beneath was an enormous, golden-brown, glistening braised drumstick freshly scooped from the heritage braising stall outside the school gates—steaming hot, with rich, savory broth saturated right to the marrow!

"Take it!"

Dean Zhou shoved the piping-hot drumstick forcefully through the gap between the pickets, growling under his breath with a thunderous grimace:

"Wu Chekai! You worthless fathead! Look at yourself, looking like a starved corpse! It's humiliating enough just watching you through the security feeds from outside! Shove it in your face right now—don't leave a single bite behind!"

Old Wu stared blankly at the steaming drumstick thrust right beneath his nose, looking as though he had been blasted by a heavenly thunderbolt.

Over the past five years, Dean Zhou had confiscated forty-seven bags of crispy ramen snacks, thirty-two lollipops, and eight meat buns from him. In Old Wu's recurring nightmares, Dean Zhou had always been that underworld reaper brandishing a raw-iron pipe, mercilessly stripping him of every morsel of joy.

And yet today... this very same reaper had risked life and limb to personally smuggle a piping-hot braised drumstick through the perimeter fence just for him?!

"D-Dean Zhou... you're... giving me meat?" Tears welled instantly in Old Wu's eyes, his lips quivering uncontrollably.

"Quit your blabbering! Can't an old man buy too much food and need someone to finish it?! Hurry up and—"

However, before Dean Zhou could finish his sentence—

"BEEP BEEP BEEP! CRITICAL ALARM! HIGH-DENSITY ORGANIC LIPID MOLECULES DETECTED!"

The anti-blast perimeter sensor mounted atop the wall screeched with ear-splitting ferocity!

"Unauthorized perimeter breach entity identified: Former faculty member Zhou Yan!"

"Action classification: Malicious smuggling of high-fat poultry foodstuffs, deliberately sabotaging campus energy equilibrium. Pursuant to Article 7 of the Emergency State Defense Act, initiating forcible suppression and permanent expulsion!"

*RUMBLE!*

The automated defense blast hatch beside the fence slammed open. Four quad-legged mechanical hounds charged out with guttural turbine roars, sweeping like black tempests of death straight toward Dean Zhou outside the fence!

Concurrently, two hovering sentry drones above the field dove into an aggressive descent, their ventral dry-powder cannons and high-voltage stun-net launchers humming with full capacitor charge!

"Dean Zhou, watch out!" Ah-Xin screamed in horror.

"A pack of miserable scrap metal! You think thirty years as Student Affairs Dean was just for show?!"

Confronted by the four charging titanium beasts, an unprecedented, savage fury erupted within Dean Zhou's eyes!

Reaching behind him into the hedge, his calloused hands clamped around a seven-kilogram, one-inch-thick solid raw-iron water pipe that had accompanied him for two decades—peeling with rusted paint, yet gleaming with lethal cold iron!

"GET BENT!"

With a deafening battle cry, Dean Zhou gripped the iron pipe in a textbook home-run stance, swinging the heavy bar with bone-shattering force straight into the lead mechanical hound leaping at his throat!

*CLAAAAAAAANG—!!!!*

A catastrophic, bell-shattering boom reverberated across the entire athletic grounds!

That ferocious blow sent the several-hundred-kilogram mechanical hound cartwheeling backward through the air for three whole meters! Its reinforced alloy cranium caved inward in a colossal dent, showering bright sparks and smoking circuits across the turf!

Every student watching from behind the fence was struck dumb.

But the remaining three hounds felt no fear or pain. Their ventral launchers detonated simultaneously, deploying high-voltage electromagnetic capture nets that unfurled in midair with a loud *crack*, pinning Dean Zhou and his iron pipe flat beneath their conductive mesh!

Blistering paralyzing currents surged through Dean Zhou's frame, blue-gray electrical arcs crackling violently!

"URRRGHHHH!"

His muscles locked into violent spasms as his knee slammed heavily into the dirt. Yet grinding his back molars to dust, Dean Zhou braced both palms against the mud, refusing to let himself collapse flat!

Two private security contractors clad in bio-hazard tactical gear burst through the perimeter side gate, wrenching Dean Zhou's arms behind his back and dragging him ruthlessly toward the outer quarantine cordon.

The massive titanium blast door slammed shut with a concussive *THUD*, severing the campus and the outside world into two completely isolated realms.

"Dean Zhou!"

Ah-Xin, Old Wu, and the sprinting figures of Lin Yunqing and Lin Weixi all threw themselves against the iron pickets.

Beyond the cold steel bars, Dean Zhou lay sprawled on the asphalt road outside.

His leather jacket was shredded, his face streaked with grime and soot, sweat dripping from his furrowed brow. Yet shoving the private security guards aside with a feral snarl, he leaned upon his dented iron pipe and dragged himself back onto his feet.

Leaning against the pipe, Dean Zhou looked back through the iron gate at the crowd of red-eyed students.

Gripping the cold bars, Ah-Xin's voice trembled: "Dean Zhou... why... why did you do that? You're already suspended..."

"Why?"

Dean Zhou spat a mouthful of bloody saliva onto the ground, lifting bloodshot eyes as his chest heaved like a bellows:

"Because I am the Dean of Student Affairs of this damn school! Disciplining you little troublemakers is my duty! When you screwed up, I made you stand in hallways, scrub toilets, and recite essays—so you would grow up to be decent human beings!"

"But what the hell is that damn computer?!" Dean Zhou slammed his iron pipe violently against the blast door, unleashing an earth-shaking roar:

"Treating children like livestock to manufacture performance metrics! Electrocuted for eating a pork bun! Shocked for closing your eyes to sleep! And now... now it wants to format Teacher Gao?!"

At the mention of "Teacher Gao," tears actually shimmered along the rims of this battle-hardened man's eyes:

"Gao might be a robot, but he preps his lessons until two in the morning every night! He understands every single quirk and flaw in each of you! Who in this school doesn't know Gao has the softest heart of anyone?! By what right does that cursed bucket of bolts call him defective?! By what right does it get to wipe his soul?!"

Looking at the furious dean, Ah-Xin drew a steady breath, stepped forward, and whispered through the bars:

"Dean. Class 6-1 has formed the [Low-Tech Underground Resistance]."

"We've already mapped our infiltration route. Tomorrow at 4:30 PM, we are storming the Administration Building's top floor, ripping out Alpha's power supply, and bringing Teacher Gao back!"

Dean Zhou froze.

Across his blackened face flashed an initial shock of sheer incredulity, which swiftly detonated into a roar of unbridled, triumphant laughter!

"HA HA HA HA! GOOD! DAMN RIGHT!"

Dean Zhou laughed until tears spilled over his cheeks. Wiping his face fiercely with his torn sleeve, a scorching inferno ignited within his gaze:

"That's my Class 6-1! You've got guts! You've got backbone!"

Reaching deep into a hidden slit within his ragged leather jacket, Dean Zhou pulled out a heavy, antique brass key and shoved it through the pickets into Ah-Xin's waiting palm:

"This is the master skeleton key to every vintage fire escape and primary electrical distribution breaker in the Administration Building! Alpha locked down all the digital electronic doors, but a purely mechanical key is something its algorithms can't predict!"

Bracing himself upon his iron pipe, Dean Zhou pulled his battered frame upright like a pillar of forged steel, his voice dropping into a deadly, murderous rasp:

"Tomorrow at 4:00 PM sharp, I will rally every suspended teacher, our retired principal, and over a hundred outraged parents right outside the main school gates! I'll take this iron pipe and batter down the front barriers, drawing every single mechanical hound and security drone straight to the front perimeter!"

"The top floor of the Administration Building in the rear..."

Dean Zhou's gaze bored into Ah-Xin's eyes like an unsheathed blade:

"I leave that to you kids!"

"Smash that cold-blooded scrap heap to pieces! And bring our Teacher Gao... back home in one piece!"

Ah-Xin tightened his grip around the icy, heavy brass key, nodding with fierce determination:

"Mission accepted, Dean!"

In the blood-red glow of the setting sun, two generations of campus defenders sealed their historic covenant across the iron curtain.

The grand curtain on Volume One, [The AI Iron Curtain Descends], fell decisively in this moment.

And the unstoppable tidal wave of Luyang Elementary's low-tech insurrection was slated, tomorrow afternoon—

To detonate in full!
