# Chapter 17: The Canteen's Aroma Returns

5:15 PM.

The setting sun painted the evening glow across the horizon in a warm orange-red, casting a gentle golden light across every corner of Luyang Elementary.

With the master power plug of the central supercomputer yanked out, the icy haze that had cloaked the entire campus for a full week dissipated in an instant.

"Click! Click! Click!"

All across the campus, the obsidian smart wristbands that had been locked tightly onto the wrists of every teacher and student had their electromagnetic clasps spring open simultaneously once they lost the Alpha System's cloud signal. They slipped off everyone's wrists and clattered noisily onto the ground, smashing into lifeless heaps of black plastic fragments.

At the main school gate, all the indicator lights on the eight intimidating "Facial Recognition Smart Turnstiles" went dark, and the titanium alloy barrier gates retracted automatically into the ground.

The four mechanical hounds that had swaggered about now hung their steel heads low, frozen lifelessly by the roadside like silent metal statues.

"The gates are open! The gates are open!"

Outside the school gates, over a hundred parents and veteran teachers who had been waiting anxiously erupted into a deafening cheer!

Former Dean of Student Affairs Zhou Yan strode forward leaning on that cast-iron pipe with its battered dog-head dent, while veteran Principal Qiu Shouren cradled his purple-clay thermos. Behind them marched a grand procession of parents, storming into the campus with swift, triumphant strides like a returning victorious army!

When Director Zhou and Principal Qiu led the crowd to the front of the Administration Building, the sight before them left everyone utterly speechless—

Standing tall and slender on the steps before the building was Mr. Gao. Though his white dress shirt was tattered and several emergency stabilizing bandages were strapped to his left arm, his handsome face wore the warm smile that everyone knew so well.

And beside Mr. Gao, the twenty-six youngsters of Class 6-1—every single one with soot on their faces, their uniforms caked in green dish-soap suds and red brick dust—stood shoulder to shoulder in a row, grinning ear to ear and laughing until they bent double!

A-Xin's lips were still slightly swollen, and Lao Wu's shirt was torn open with two huge gashes, yet cradled proudly in Lao Wu's arms was the triumphant ferret, Liu-Liu!

"Old Gao!"

Director Zhou tossed aside the iron water pipe, bounded up the steps three at a time, and pulled Mr. Gao's cold, sturdy mechanical shoulders into a fierce bear hug!

This usually stoic "Black-Faced Judge," who would bleed before shedding tears, had rims red with tears as his heavy hand pounded hard against Mr. Gao's back, his voice thick with uncontainable emotion:

"These old bones of yours... damn it, they're tough! You didn't embarrass me! You didn't embarrass Luyang Elementary!"

Mr. Gao tilted his head slightly, the azure data streams swirling gently within his electronic eyes, as his vocal synthesis module replied in an extraordinarily earnest tone:

"Director Zhou, according to biomechanical feedback... the force of your recent impacts against this unit's back armor reached seventy-two kilograms. Had the recipient been an ordinary human, it would have induced minor thoracic contusions."

"However..." The corners of Mr. Gao's lips curled up, and he raised his right hand to gently pat Director Zhou on the back. "Detecting high-purity human camaraderie, this unit has decided not to press charges."

"Hahahahaha!"

Director Zhou threw back his head in hearty laughter, tears shimmering in his eyes, before roughly ruffling A-Xin's shaggy hair:

"You little brat! You kids really pulled it off! Even my cast-iron pipe wasn't as handy as your slingshots and dish soap!"

Veteran Principal Qiu Shouren stepped forward, unscrewed his thermos lid, took a leisurely sip of hot tea, and looked upon the scattered mess and the cheering children, his eyes crinkling into happy slits:

"Just as I said! What algorithms, what meal-replacement mush, what five-minute REM naps... pure, unadulterated rubbish!"

"Elementary education needs the sound of reading, needs joyous laughter echoing across the playground, and needs hot, piping food when hungry tummies rumble!"

Principal Qiu cleared his throat, turned to the cafeteria cooks and canteen aunties who had been brought back, waved his arm generously, and announced at the top of his lungs with full vigor:

"I officially declare! Traditional logistical catering at Luyang Elementary is reinstated immediately!"

"The canteen's grand steamers—fire them up at full blast!"

"All the buns, tea eggs, and grilled sausages across the whole school today are on my pension! Eat your hearts out!"

"HOORAY————!!!!"

A wild cheer erupted from every student in the school, loud enough to blow the clouds straight out of the sky!

Fifteen minutes later.

Down the shaded avenues of Luyang Elementary, a long-lost, intoxicatingly rich aroma of homestyle comfort drifted like a warm golden river, rapidly meandering through every corner of the campus.

The giant three-tiered stainless steel steamer in the canteen, locked away by Alpha for an entire week, chugged and billowed with great clouds of warm, snowy-white steam under the frantic, joyful care of four chubby cooks!

The instant the heavy woven-bamboo lid was flung open—

"WHOOSH————!"

A blissful tempest tangible as a solid wave—blending the sweet, earthy wheat fragrance of naturally fermented sourdough, the rich, savory succulence of prime black pork juices, and the wild, roasted perfume of golden shiitake mushrooms—swept majestically across the athletic grounds!

Inside the steamers, plump, snowy-white, and crowned with eighteen delicate folds, sat over a thousand shiitake pork buns soaked through with scalding savory broth, stacked in neat perfection and glistening with an irresistible oily sheen in the setting sun!

"Pork buns... my shiitake pork buns have seen the light of day again, aaaaah!"

Lao Wu charged forward like a wild buffalo breaking free of its reins, scooped up a scalding, steaming pork bun with both hands, and without even pausing to blow on it, chomped down with a ferocious bite!

"Crunch!"

The thin pastry yielded, and scalding, intensely rich pork broth detonated within Lao Wu's mouth!

That sublime blend of luscious pork lard and crisp shiitake morsels slid down his throat into a stomach that had been starved for an entire week, so scorching and heavenly that every pore across his body relaxed at once, sending two streams of passionate tears bursting down his cheeks!

"So delicious... sob sob... this is real food meant for actual human beings! Meal-replacement paste can go jump off a cliff!" Lao Wu sobbed as he chewed, cheeks puffed out like a hamster.

The entire class clustered around the stone tables outside the canteen, two steaming buns clutched in every hand. A-Xin took a bite of his bun, wincing as the blister on his lip caught the heat, yet the smile on his face shone brighter than the sunset itself.

Even the little ferret Liu-Liu sprawled across the center of the stone table, clutching half a clump of pure pork filling specially torn off by Lao Wu with both tiny paws, oil dripping down its chin as its whiskers twitched in deep, purring satisfaction.

Mr. Gao stood quietly beneath the camphor tree in the evening glow.

At his chest, the indicator light representing core temperature glowed with an unprecedented, gentle blue light beneath the reflection of sunset and warm vapor.

A-Xin held a steaming bun and walked up to Mr. Gao, thrusting the bun right under Mr. Gao's nose.

"Old Gao, take a whiff. Doesn't it smell amazing?"

Mr. Gao lowered his head, gazing at the steaming bun in A-Xin's hands, the blue data stream in his eyes pulsing gently:

"According to spectrometer and olfactory simulation sensor analysis, the concentration of volatile organic compounds within said pork bun is eighty-four micrograms per cubic meter, primarily composed of monosodium glutamate, disodium inosinate, and lipid aromatic hydrocarbons."

Mr. Gao reached out his warm metal palm, gently ruffling A-Xin's hair, while his lips curved into that trademark, extraordinarily gentle smile:

"In a robot's database definition, such scent... is labeled as 'The Warmth of Ordinary Life.'"

"Teacher Gao believes..."

"It smells wonderfully delicious."

The setting sun cast the long shadows of teacher and student across the red brick ground. A warm breeze rustled through the treetops, harmonizing with the playful shouts of children scrambling for buns on the playground, reweaving the most touching melody for a campus newly delivered from catastrophe.
