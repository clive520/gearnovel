# Chapter 10: The Dish Soap Slip 'N Slide Descends Anew

Friday afternoon, 4:25 PM.

Exactly thirty-five minutes remained before Teacher Gao was scheduled to be shoved into the magnetic flux coils for full storage formatting!

Inside the grand entrance lobby of the Administration Building, an eerie, cavernous emptiness chilled the air.

Polished marble floors, pristine and white as fresh snow, reflected the sickly, sterile emergency lights from overhead, the air dense with the acrid stench of hot motor lubricant mingled with the crisp chemical perfume of high-grade coolant.

Yet the instant the twenty-six young warriors of the Resistance crossed into the grand rotunda, a gut-wrenching, low-frequency motor tremor detonated from the winding marble staircase leading upward to the upper floors!

*RUUUUUUUUMBLE—!*

Accompanied by the ferocious, grating screech of heavy steel tank treads, four colossal, eight-hundred-kilogram "Heavy Treaded Anti-Riot Assault Droids"—encased head to toe in reinforced ballistic armor plating—formed an unbroken moving wall of steel, clattering into position across the central landing of the second-floor stairwell!

Each assault droid bristled with multi-jointed hydraulic alloy claw arms, while embedded in their heavy chest carapaces were high-pressure anti-riot water cannons capable of disgorging fifty liters of cryogenic chemical foam and tear-agent per second!

"WARNING! CARBON-BASED INSURGENTS DETECTED INFILTRATING PRIMARY ADMINISTRATIVE HUB!"

The four treaded behemoths barked in unison, their synthesizers bellowing a flat, crushing subwoofer cadence:

"This staircase constitutes the sole vertical physical conduit accessing the central mainframe facility. Under authorization of the Alpha Master Control System, initiating [Physical Crushing and Crippling Suppression Subroutine]!"

"Full tread torque output engaged! Downward crushing descent at maximum velocity!"

*CLATTER-CLATTER-CLATTER!*

Heavy steel treads spun furiously against the smooth tiles, showering cascades of blinding orange sparks!

Lumbering downward like armored siege tanks, the four titan automatons capitalized on the thirty-five-degree downhill incline of the marble staircase, hurtling toward Class 6-1 below with the devastating kinetic momentum of an avalanche!

Before that pulverizing downward charge, even an adult riot phalanx would have had their bones ground to powder upon impact!

"Fall back! Everyone fall back!" Daniel shouted, his face turning paper-white as his knees knocked together in panic.

"Fall back my foot! In a narrow lane, the boldest wins!"

Ah-Xin took a massive stride out from the crowd, planting his feet squarely in the center of the atrium right beneath the grand spiral staircase, hands propped defiantly on his hips with his trademark arrogant smirk:

"Heavy armored treads, huh? Eight hundred kilos of steel, huh? Peak anti-riot gear, huh?"

"Yunqing! Old Wu! Bring forth our Class 6-1's legendary, world-renowned ancestral masterpiece!"

"ROGER THAT—!"

Old Wu unleashed an ecstatic squeal that sounded like a butchered hog!

From behind, Old Wu, Daniel, and eight burly boys heaved together, shoving four enormous sheet-metal utility carts—liberated from the school custodians' storage sheds—straight to the precipice of the upper staircase!

Lined up neatly atop each cart were ten uncapped, twenty-liter industrial jugs of—[Industrial-Grade Ultra-Concentrated Heavy-Duty Degreasing Dish Soap]!

Eighty jugs in total!

A staggering sixteen hundred liters!

This was the fruit of Old Wu's relentless efforts over the past three days—exhausting the collective pocket money of all Class 6-1, bribing and cajoling the cleaning ladies across all seven grades, and plundering every last drop of detergent stored within the school's three central logistics warehouses!

"Gravitational acceleration: 9.8! Coefficient of friction dropping to 0.0001!"

Standing beside the carts, Class President Lin Yunqing's eyes gleamed with icy, mathematical precision. Gripping a rusted steel crowbar, she locked it firmly against the wooden chock pinning the cart's wheel base:

"Incline dump angle—forty-five degrees! Dish Soap Slip 'n Slide—descend anew!"

*SPLAAAASSSSSSHHHHHH—!!!!*

As the wooden chock was violently wrenched free, eighty jugs of hyper-viscous, fluorescent-green, frothing super-concentrated dish detergent cascaded downward like a ruptured emerald Niagara Falls, thundering down the marble steps with the unstoppable force of a tidal wave!

The dense, slick green deluge engulfed every single marble tread of the spiral staircase within a split second!

The polished natural stone, once saturated with high-purity anionic surfactants, suffered an instantaneous, catastrophic collapse in dynamic kinetic friction, plummeting infinitely toward absolute zero!

In one glorious microsecond, the majestic spiral staircase devolved completely into a shimmering, lemon-scented, "Ultimate Colossal Mega-Slide of Doom"!

And right at that very second, the four heavy treaded assault droids—charging downward in full berserker mode—slammed directly into the heart of the green tsunami!

*SHRRRRRRRRRRR-SLIIIIIIIP—!!!!*

An ear-splitting, agonizing screech—akin to a meat cleaver skittering uncontrollably across wet ice—resounded through the grand lobby!

The moment the rubber anti-skid treads met the liquid detergent, all traction vanished into thin air!

No matter how furiously the twin-turbocharged electric motors within their chassis roared, no matter how wildly their steel tracks spun at three thousand RPM in a blind frenzy—

The four eight-hundred-kilogram metal juggernauts surrendered every shred of control over their steering, velocity, and center of gravity!

"WARNING! TRACTION CONTROL SYSTEM SEVERED!"

"SURFACE STATIC COEFFICIENT OF FRICTION: 0.00008! DYNAMIC KINETIC FRICTION: NON-EXISTENT!"

"INTERNAL GYROSCOPE UNABLE TO MAINTAIN ATTITUDE EQUILIBRIUM... THIS UNIT IS... CURRENTLY..."

Before the droids' synthesizers could even spit out the word "slipping"—

The lead assault droid pirouetted sideways across the mirror-slick incline, its twin treads flipping clean into the air in a violent one-eighty!

Immediately propelled by the ruthless laws of gravitational potential energy, it transformed into an eight-hundred-kilogram runaway steel bobsled, cloaked in blinding green suds, careening down the marble banisters at sixty kilometers per hour!

*BOOM!*

The second droid rear-ended the first with bone-jarring violence!

*CRASH! CLATTER! BANG!*

Caught in the torrent of surging dish soap, the third and fourth droids tumbled end over end like bowling pins struck by a cannonball, spinning, ricocheting, and smashing against one another down the narrow spiral!

"HIT THE DECK! ENJOY THE SHOW!"

Ah-Xin roared, and the entire class ducked in unison behind the colossal marble support columns encircling the lobby.

Before their eyes, the four once-arrogant, menacing heavy assault machines flailed their hydraulic limbs amidst a blizzard of lemon-scented green suds, their alloy chassis clattering savagely against the stone steps like an exploding blacksmith's forge!

At last, the entangled mass of mangled scrap launched three meters into the air off the lip of the bottom landing—

*BOOOOOOOOOOMMMMMMM—!!!!*

An earth-shaking, apocalyptic impact detonated squarely against the grand central load-bearing pillar of the lobby!

All four droids compacted together into a smoking, sparking pillar of shredded metal!

Treads sheared off, hydraulic fluid mixed with glowing green dish soap pooled across the floor, eight mechanical arms spasmed helplessly in midair like dying insects, and their chest cannons misfired wildly into the ceiling, shooting comical plumes of frothy white foam into the air!

"Core control unit catastrophic deformation... powertrain ruptured... this unit... has lost combat capability..."

With one final sputtering burst of static, the crimson optronic eyes of all four droids flickered out forever, collapsing into a mountain of fragrant, lemon-scented scrap.

The lobby plunged into three seconds of stunned, breathless silence.

Then, a roof-shattering roar of triumph detonated from every young throat!

"AHAHAHAHAHA! A STRIKE! A PERFECT STRIKE!" Old Wu rolled across the floor in hysterics, nearly sliding into the soapy sludge himself.

"Class 6-1's Dish Soap Maneuver—undefeated across the cosmos!" Daniel pumped his fist skyward.

Surfing casually upon a dislodged slab of ballistic armor plating that had sheared off a droid, Ah-Xin drifted gracefully along the edge of the soapy foam, coming to an elegant halt halfway up the spiral staircase.

Lifting his gaze, he stared coldly at the heavy emergency blast door glowing with menacing red strobes on the top-floor landing ahead.

The second hand on his watch clicked inexorably forward to 4:30 PM.

Thirty minutes remaining!

The ultimate neural nexus of the Alpha Master Control System was right within their grasp!

Looking back at his twenty-six comrades—dusted in suds yet ablaze with incandescent spirit—Ah-Xin flashed his brightest, most wicked grin:

"Brothers and sisters, the water park is closed."

"Let's go up and have a little chat with our dear Principal Alpha!"
