# Chapter 8: Liu-Liu's Physical Network Disconnect

Friday afternoon, 3:55 PM.

Exactly sixty-five minutes remained until Teacher Gao's ultimate formatting.

*CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!*

Inside the ground-floor lobby of the Academic Building echoed the dense, thunderous cadence of mechanical footfalls.

Twelve mechanical hounds, their ocular visors flaring with crimson bloodlust, scraped their steel talons across the chilly floor tiles, frantically hunting for the "low-tech armed insurgents" who had just flash-blinded the drone fleet from the windows above.

Every loudspeaker along the ceiling had shifted into its highest emergency tier, screeching with deafening sirens:

"WARNING! MASS EXTREME SUBVERSIVE ACTIVITY DETECTED IN THE ACADEMIC BUILDING! CENTRAL SUPERCOMPUTER HAS SEIZED FULL CONTROL OF THIS SECTOR'S FIRE SUPPRESSION SYSTEM!"

"IN THIRTY SECONDS, ALL TITANIUM-ALLOY FIRE SHUTTERS ACROSS THIS FACILITY WILL UNDERGO MANDATORY LOCKDOWN! RIOT-CONTROL TEAR GAS CANNONS CHARGING!"

"Yunqing, if those blast shutters drop, we'll be boxed in like trapped dumplings!"

Cornered in a cramped stairwell landing leading down toward the utility conduit hatch, Ah-Xin braced his shoulders firmly against the heavy fire door behind them, sweat rolling constantly off the tip of his nose.

"I know! That's why I had zero intention of wrestling control from it through software!"

Crouched beside the utility access well, Class President Lin Yunqing deftly unscrewed a yellow-and-black hazard-striped sheet-metal hatch plate with a screwdriver.

Beneath the plate lay a pitch-black, subterranean low-voltage conduit measuring a meager twenty-five centimeters in diameter.

"All of Alpha's real-time operational commands, telemetry video streams, and electromagnetic shutter locks for the South Academic Building rely entirely on the [100-Gigabit Core Single-Mode Fiber-Optic Cable] threaded through this very pipe."

Yunqing adjusted her glasses, pointing into the murky depths of the conduit:

"This conduit leads directly into the subterranean foundation beneath the Administration Building. It is fortified with high-voltage arc-mesh screens and ultrasonic rodent-repellent oscillators. A human couldn't possibly crawl inside. However, while it keeps out rats and repels digital hackers..."

Here, the corners of Yunqing's mouth curled into an exquisitely sly smile, turning her gaze toward the ferret perched upon Ah-Xin's shoulder:

"It has zero defense against Luyang Elementary's supreme god of destruction!"

"Squeak squeak!"

The moment his name was evoked, Liu-Liu reared up on his hindquarters in sheer exuberance, rubbing his tiny furry paws eagerly before his chest as his beady black eyes glistened with mischievous excitement beside the dark pipe opening.

"Special Agent Liu-Liu, suit up!"

Ah-Xin swiftly fished a red elastic hairband from his pocket, strapping a tiny, ultra-bright micro-LED spotlight securely onto the top of Liu-Liu's skull.

Yunqing then gingerly nestled a modified miniature earpiece into Liu-Liu's fuzzy left ear, securing three crispy dried fish snacks—hand-roasted by Old Wu—onto his tiny harness:

"Liu-Liu, this earpiece is patched directly into my transceiver. Follow my voice once you're inside. Mission success means these three dried fish are your personal victory feast!"

Liu-Liu twitched his pink nose. Catching the mouthwatering aroma of savory dried fish, the little ferret bristled with boundless energy, darting his pink tongue across his whiskers before shooting forward like a bolt of silver lightning, diving headfirst into the pitch-black conduit!

"Micro-camera feed live and synced!"

Across the modified radio screen resting on Yunqing's lap, a narrow, violently bobbing first-person perspective sprang to life.

The micro-spotlight atop Liu-Liu's head sliced clean through the subterranean darkness.

The interior of the narrow pipe was choked with an intricate web of thick black insulated cables.

Two massive sewer rats sheltering in the conduit suddenly came face-to-face with Liu-Liu; the formidable mustelid apex predator bared his lethal fangs with a terrifying hiss, sending the oversized rodents squealing in sheer terror as they scrambled head over heels toward the drainage vents!

"Liu-Liu, maintain a straight bearing! There is a central communications terminal junction box fifteen meters ahead!" Yunqing whispered commands through her transceiver.

Within the slick plastic conduit, Liu-Liu scampered as smoothly as across open terrain, his long, serpentine spine flexing into aerodynamic curves as he tore through the cable jungle!

Within a mere ten seconds, Liu-Liu's head popped out from the conduit's egress, emerging into a subterranean cable chamber bristling with metal equipment racks and cooling vents.

In the dead center of the chamber stood a meter-wide, silver-gray waterproof junction box, emblazoned with stark serial lettering—[South Wing Primary Network Node: Junction-Alpha-09].

"Found it!" Yunqing's breath caught in her throat. "Liu-Liu! Climb up! See that black cable wrapped in yellow spiral protective tubing on the lower left of the chassis? The one two centimeters thick!"

Hearing Yunqing's voice through his earpiece, Liu-Liu tilted his head with keen intelligence.

With a lithe leap, his nimble claws hooked onto the metal rim, hoisting himself atop the junction box.

Illuminated beneath the beam of his headlight, a thick, black-sheathed optical cable greeted the camera feed, stamped with embossed gold lettering: [Primary Fiber-Optic 100G Trunk].

"That's it! That's the carotid artery linking Alpha to the entire academic wing!" Yunqing shouted in excitement. "Liu-Liu, chew it to pieces!"

Liu-Liu leaned close to sniff it, his eyes narrowing into cold, lethal slits.

Snapping his jaw open, he unveiled two rows of pristine white incisors sharper than surgical scalpels, hard enough to bite through solid coins!

*CRUNCH! CRUNCH! CRUNCH!*

Crisp, violent gnawing sounds relayed crystal-clear through the earpiece back up to the maintenance shaft!

This military-grade high-density optical cable—lauded for its tensile resistance, crush resistance, and armored with triple-layered Kevlar ballistic mesh and braided stainless-steel wiring—was as brittle as overcooked noodles before Liu-Liu's terrifying rodent jaw pressure!

Steel strands were violently sheared apart; Kevlar weave was shredded into fuzz; and finally—

*SNAP! SNAP! SNAP!*

The ninety-six cores of ultra-pure silica glass fiber bundles sealed within were shattered into a shower of sparkling crystalline powder with one savage jerk of Liu-Liu's skull!

*BOOM—!*

The entire digital infrastructure of the South Academic Building plunged into catastrophic collapse in that very microsecond!

The eight heavy titanium-alloy fire shutters that had been plummeting toward the floor ground to a violent halt with ear-splitting screeches merely thirty centimeters above the tiles, their brakes spewing glowing sparks as they seized dead in midair!

Out in the hallways, all twelve mechanical hounds erupted into frantic synthesized alarms:

"WARNING! MILLISECOND TELEMETRY SYNCHRONIZATION WITH CENTRAL SUPERCOMPUTER SEVERED!"

"PHYSICAL FIBER CHANNEL COMPLETELY OFFLINE! SHIFTING TO LOCAL AUTONOMOUS PROTOCOLS..."

"Awesome! The main trunk is completely fried!" Ah-Xin pumped his fist in wild triumph.

Yet Yunqing's expression remained razor-sharp: "Hold on! Alpha's auxiliary failover grid is initializing! There is a 380-volt secondary substation relay right beside the terminal box. If auxiliary power engages, it will drop the gravity counterweights on the shutters within ten seconds!"

"What do we do?! Liu-Liu can't just swallow a transformer whole!" Ah-Xin cried out.

A ruthless glint flashed behind Yunqing's lenses as she barked into the transceiver:

"Liu-Liu! See the exposed cooling vent and exposed copper busbars atop the transformer?!"

"EXECUTE SPECIALIZED BIOLOGICAL FLUID SHORT-CIRCUIT STRIKE!!!"

Down in the subterranean chamber, Liu-Liu seemed to grasp the assignment instinctively.

The brilliant little critter spun around, wiggling his fluffy bottom, and elegantly cocked his left hind leg in the air—

A steaming, crystal-clear stream of electrolyte-rich biological fluid arched through the air in an exquisite ballistic trajectory, splashing with pinpoint precision across the live, high-voltage exposed copper busbars awaiting secondary power engagement!

*BZZZZZZZZT-KAPOWWWWWWW!!!!*

The moment natural electrolytic salts met 380 volts of raw alternating current, a catastrophic electrical flashover detonated!

Blinding azure arcs of plasma burst like fireworks inside the transformer chassis!

Plumes of thick, blue-white smoke billowed violently as the entire substation's main fuses melted instantly into a puddle of molten iron with a violent *POP*!

*CLICK! CLACK! SNAP!*

Every smart monitoring screen, electronic speaker, and corridor emergency strobe throughout the South Academic Building blinked out simultaneously!

Pursuant to fire-safety failsafe protocols, the moment absolute power loss occurred, every electronically locked classroom door throughout the building clicked open, reverting automatically to mechanical release!

"Squeak squeak!"

A cheerful chirp echoed from the mouth of the conduit.

Covered in cobwebs and exuding a faint, scorched scent of ozone, the little ferret Liu-Liu emerged like a victorious conquering warlord, proudly clamping a shredded chunk of golden optical fiber in his jaws as he leaped straight into Ah-Xin's waiting palm!

"You magnificent little general!" Ah-Xin scooped Liu-Liu up, planting a huge kiss on his fuzzy forehead before stuffing three crispy dried fish into his eager paws.

Right at that very second, from the distant direction of the main school entrance, a thunderous uproar akin to a raging tsunami detonated through the air!

*CLANG! CLANG! CLANG!*

The deafening cacophony of raw-iron water pipes battering furiously against the titanium gates, mingled with the furious war cries of over a hundred outraged parents and veteran teachers, rumbled through the school buildings!

Ah-Xin lifted his wrist and checked his watch.

4:00 PM on the dot!

The solemn promise made by the Black-Faced Judge, Dean Zhou—had been fulfilled to the letter!

The grand diversion at the main gate had officially commenced!
