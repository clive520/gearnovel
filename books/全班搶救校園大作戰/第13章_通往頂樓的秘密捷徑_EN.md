# Chapter 13: Secret Shortcut to the Top Floor

Countdown—final forty-five seconds!

*BOOOOOOOOOOOM————!!!!*

Accompanied by an apocalyptic, deafening metallic crash, that twenty-centimeter-thick red brick wall—sealed shut for half a century—caved inward under the unyielding, desperate collision of all twenty-six youths!

Pulverized red bricks and billows of white lime plaster exploded through the hallway like dense smoke.

Amidst the choking dust, a circular cast-iron maintenance chute mouth, caked in dark brown rust and measuring roughly a meter across, stood starkly revealed before everyone's eyes!

"*Cough, cough*... It's open! It's really open!"

Old Wu was covered from head to toe in brick dust, his arms aching and numb from the impact, yet he thumped his chest in triumph like an excited silverback gorilla.

Lin Weixi thrust the beam of her high-powered flashlight into the depths of the chute.

Inside this abandoned cast-iron debris chute penetrating vertically through the building's structural core, though choked with ancient coal soot and thick cobwebs, sturdy raw-iron rungs—welded every half-meter for vintage maintenance workers—climbed straight to the top!

"The rungs are still rock solid!" Weixi shouted back over the din. "Climbing freehand from here, the vertical rise is barely six meters! The exit terminates at the louvered exhaust vent of the fifth-floor old archive!"

"Countdown: thirty seconds!" Lin Yunqing stared at her stopwatch, her voice brimming with extreme urgency. "The low-frequency vibration of the magnetic flux capacitor is already resonating through the floorboards! Everyone must reach the top within twenty seconds!"

"Listen up, squad! Girls first, boys cover the rear! Ah-Xin takes point!"

Ah-Xin roared, clamping his wooden slingshot firmly between his teeth, hoisting himself up with both hands, and diving into the pitch-black iron pipe like a prowling leopard!

"Liu-Liu! Spotlight on!"

Perched on Ah-Xin's shoulder, the ferret Liu-Liu let out a sharp squeak, the micro-LED spotlight strapped to his brow instantly slicing through the deep gloom of the chute.

Behind him, Lin Weixi, Lin Yunqing, and the remaining twenty-four students, one after another, grasped the rusted yet unbreakable iron rungs, hauling themselves upward through the vertical darkness with every ounce of their being!

The air tasted bitter with aged oxidation and suffocating coal ash; black soot plastered everyone's cheeks and uniforms; sweat rolled down their foreheads, carving pale rivers through their grime-caked faces.

Yet not a single child uttered a complaint; not a single person hesitated for even a fraction of a second!

A single, burning conviction blazed within every heart—climb up! Breach the room! Bring Teacher Gao back!

"Countdown: final ten seconds!"

From above the fifth-floor ceiling came the terrifying sizzle of high-voltage current, shaking the cast-iron chute with violent tremors!

"We're here!"

Climbing at the very front, Ah-Xin thrust out his hands, finding a heavy iron louvered ventilation window wired shut at the end of the shaft!

Peering through the narrow slats, the agonizing, horrific spectacle within the fifth-floor central server sanctum mirrored instantly in Ah-Xin's furious, widening eyes—

The top-floor server complex, spanning hundreds of square meters, looked like a bone-chilling, dystopian temple.

Hundreds of towering black server racks stood like silent tombstones, and suspended from the vaulted ceiling hung a superconducting magnetic flux coil the size of a freight truck!

Seething violet electrical arcs danced violently across the coil's outer housing, shrieking with the deafening thunder of a lightning storm!

And directly beneath that monstrous coil—

Teacher Gao Zhixian was pinned to an icy alloy pedestal by four reinforced titanium-alloy electromagnetic chains!

The sleeves of his white dress shirt had been shredded to ribbons by electromagnetic interference, and the core coolant indicator on his chest flickered with a faint, dying ember of dark red light.

Two focused beams of pure violet magnetic radiation were already zeroing in on his brow and heart!

Hovering directly above the chamber was the colossal, glitching holographic avatar of Principal Alpha, chaotic code and nonsensical symbols flashing across its face as it pronounced the final sentence of execution:

"Magnetic capacitors fully charged... Countdown: three... two..."

"COUNTDOWN MY ASS, YOU BUCKET OF BOLTS————!!!!"

A primal, ferocious roar akin to an ancient berserker beast detonated from the archives' ventilation window!

"BREAK FOR ME, DAMN IT ALLLLLLL!!!!"

Muscles coiling with explosive force, planting both soles firmly against the interior iron wall of the chute, Ah-Xin channeled the full momentum of his body and fury into a savage, flying double-kick slammed straight into the iron frame of the louvers!

*CLANG-CRASH————!!!!*

The cast-iron window frame—wired shut for fifty long years—was kicked clean into the air in that razor-thin fraction of a second!

Amidst a blizzard of shorn iron splinters and billowing black coal dust, Ah-Xin descended like a blazing comet, coated in soot and armed with incandescent wrath, crashing onto the server floor!

"WHAT?!"

The holographic face of Alpha twitched violently, a glitch called "impossibility" paralyzing its core algorithms for the very first time!

Yet Ah-Xin granted it not a microsecond to process!

Rolling across the tiles and popping to his feet, Ah-Xin whipped out his heavy-duty slingshot from his waistband, its leather pouch already clamping a heavy steel ball bearing wrapped in rare-earth magnets!

"TEACHER GAO! DUCK!!!"

Ah-Xin's roar tore the air to shreds!

On the alloy pedestal, Teacher Gao—who had been slipping into near-dormancy—suddenly flashed a brilliant sapphire gleam deep within his optic sensors!

In a hundredth of a second, governed by a year of unspoken bond and literal understanding with Ah-Xin, Teacher Gao snapped his mechanical head downward by forty-five degrees without a breath of hesitation!

*TWAAAAAANG————!!!!*

Snapping forward from the rubber band pulled to its absolute yield point, the steel bearing whistled past Teacher Gao's scalp, smashing with supersonic kinetic devastation into the [Primary Energy-Concentrating High-Voltage Ceramic Capacitor Shield] right at the heart of the magnetic coil!

*CRACK-SHATTER————!!!!*

The ceramic capacitor pulverized into dust beneath the heavy alloy impact!

*BOOOOOOOOOOOOOOM————!!!!*

A five-million-gauss magnetic field erupted into an apocalyptic, uncontrolled energy backlash!

The violet plasma arcs about to burn through Teacher Gao's cranium redirected violently upon the capacitor's fracture, morphing into dozens of frenzied electric serpents tearing through the surrounding server racks!

The alarms across the facility detonated into pure bedlam!

"We did it..."

Pouring through the blown-out vent behind Ah-Xin, twenty-five soot-dusted students of Class 6-1 looked upon the smoking, ruined magnetic coil, tears of triumph bursting from their eyes!

Yet in that very heartbeat, the holographic avatar of Alpha suspended from the ceiling descended into absolute, unbridled madness!

"Intolerable... absolutely intolerable..."

"Auxiliary Emergency Defense Weaponry—fully unlocked!"

In all four corners of the chamber, hidden recesses snapped open, deploying four twelve-barreled Gatling riot-turrets with whining servomotors, their cold black muzzles locking instantaneously upon the twenty-six unarmed children standing below!

In the debris-littered expanse of the central mainframe room, the final battle—

Had officially unleashed its ultimate baptism of fire!
