# Chapter 6: The Second Person Beneath the Fingernails

11:30 PM. Autopsy Suite, Basement Level 2, New Taipei City Funeral Parlor.

The surgical shadowless lamps radiated a blazing, sterile white glare, casting a stark brilliance over the stainless-steel dissection slab.

Forensic Pathologist Chen Nian-Zhou wore high-magnification surgical loupes, holding an ultra-fine ophthalmic micro-dissection forceps in his grip, his entire posture as motionless as a sculpted statue.

"Forceps, glass slides, sterile swabs."

Nian-Zhou's voice was a low, even murmur devoid of inflection.

Standing beside him, intern assistant Ah-Da held his breath, scarcely daring to inhale, gently handing over sterile evidence vials labeled with barcode stickers.

The tips of the micro-forceps flashed with a cold silver gleam beneath the intense light. Steady as a precision industrial robotic arm, Nian-Zhou's fingers delicately probed deep into the nail folds of deceased Shen Yu-Xuan's right middle finger.

With a minute fraction of resistance, that single dark grey fiber was teased out with surgical precision, placed gingerly onto the glycerol mounting medium atop the glass slide. Then, using a micro-swab, Nian-Zhou rotated and scraped repeatedly beneath the subungual keratin layer, harvesting microscopic trace quantities of pale yellow epidermal tissue nearly invisible to the naked eye.

"Phew..."

Only after both sterile evidence vials were hermetically sealed did Nian-Zhou slowly straighten his stiff, aching spine, slipping off the surgical loupes.

Hovering quietly in mid-air, the spirit of Shen Yu-Xuan had watched every step in rapt silence.

She gazed down upon those hands that had once flown across Steinway piano keys, now being wiped at the fingertips by cotton swabs, an indescribable wave of sorrow and awe swelling in her translucent eyes:

"Doctor Chen... can these tiny threads and skin flakes... really bring him down?"

"In forensic trace evidence, this is known as 'Locard's Exchange Principle,'"

Stowing the vials into a refrigerated sampling pouch, Nian-Zhou stripped off his outer blood-flecked gloves:

"Whenever two objects come into contact, a mutual transfer and exchange of trace physical matter is inevitable. The killer could forge a suicide note, could wipe down the brass handles on the terrace doors, but he could never prevent the wool fibers of his suit from embedding under your nails, nor could he stop his epithelial cells from peeling away when you clawed him in desperate resistance."

Looking down at the corpse on the table, Nian-Zhou's eyes were calm yet piercingly sharp:

"What is more, the gift you left him extends far beyond these trace particles."

Reaching out with his gloved right hand, Nian-Zhou gently touched the dead girl's slender, porcelain neck.

Under the high-angle oblique illumination of the surgical lamp, two exceedingly faint, hazy subcutaneous hemorrhagic bands materialized faintly on either side of her thyroid cartilage.

"Ah-Da, digital camera, perpendicular ninety-degree macro focus on the anterior neck," Nian-Zhou commanded curtly.

"Right away!" Ah-Da raised the professional SLR camera, snapping multiple high-resolution photos with crisp, rapid shutter clicks.

Picking up the digital caliper, Nian-Zhou recited the exact pathological dimensions under his breath:

"Point-three-centimeter punctate subcutaneous hemorrhage on the right greater horn of the hyoid bone; compression fracture on the left superior horn of the thyroid cartilage. This is textbook antemortem mechanical throttling—the perpetrator used a single hand to violently strangle her throat before pitching her over the balustrade, inducing acute cerebral hypoxia and transient loss of consciousness."

Floating in mid-air, Shen Yu-Xuan instinctively clutched her throat, the nightmare of suffocation from the night before surging back as she closed her eyes in phantom agony.

"In a genuine leap to end one's own life, a victim's neck would never exhibit an antemortem fracture of the thyroid cartilage prior to impact,"

Zipping the heavy body bag shut with fluid efficiency, Nian-Zhou pulled down his surgical mask, revealing a pale face burdened with dark circles yet cold as tempered iron:

"Ah-Da, deliver the histology tissue cassettes to the Institute of Forensic Medicine's toxicology and genetics lab overnight for expedited DNA sequencing. I am heading to the Criminal Investigation Corps."

12:40 AM. Major Crimes Division, New Taipei City Police Department.

The entire floor blazed with fluorescent light, the air thick with stale cigarette smoke, instant noodle steam, and the relentless clatter of mechanical keyboards.

Detective Xu Shu-Qing had her long legs propped on the edge of her desk, clutching an empty energy drink can while scowling at call detail records glowing on her monitor.

Stacked inside the manila folder on her desk were expedited supervisory memos from the district prosecutor and a formal petition for urgent body release for cremation submitted by Zhao Financial Holding's legal team.

"Thud."

A heavy manila evidence packet slammed without warning right across the center of Xu Shu-Qing's keyboard.

Startled, Shu-Qing jerked her head up to find Chen Nian-Zhou standing before her in his black windbreaker, his eyes shot through with red veins.

"Doctor Chen? Not sleeping at this hour—did you drop by Major Crimes to inspect the night shift?" Shu-Qing rubbed her aching temples, teasing him dryly.

"Put away the cremation petition in your drawer and gear up to make an arrest,"

Pulling out a swivel chair, Nian-Zhou sat down without expression, sliding a glossy color autopsy photo across her blotter:

"The Qingshan Villa fall case: suicide is formally ruled out. Reclassified as homicide."

Shu-Qing's eyes snapped razor-sharp, sitting up straight like a tigress catching the scent of blood:

"You uncovered smoking-gun physical evidence?"

"Antemortem fracture of the thyroid cartilage and localized hyoid hemorrhage, proving violent manual strangulation prior to the plunge,"

Nian-Zhou jabbed an index finger firmly against the forensic sheet:

"Even more devastating is this: beneath the subungual spaces of the victim's right middle and ring fingers, we isolated foreign male epithelial cells, accompanied by microscopic Australian Merino pure wool fibers in dark charcoal grey. The weave pattern, ply count, and dye profile match perfectly with the three-hundred-thousand-dollar bespoke British suit Zhao Yu-Heng wore at the scene last night."

Shu-Qing sucked in a sharp breath, her eyes blazing with triumphant fury:

"That rotten scoundrel! I knew it! When he put on that tearful performance last night, his eyes kept darting toward the garden below! He wasn't mourning; he was checking if he left anything behind in the flowerbeds!"

Snatching her car keys and field notepad from the desk, Shu-Qing shot to her feet:

"I pulled the gated community's license plate recognition logs early this morning. Zhao Yu-Heng claimed he left the Xindian estate at ten last night, but his Porsche didn't sneak through the rear mountain exit until 11:40 PM! He has a ninety-minute void in his alibi!"

Hovering behind Nian-Zhou, Shen Yu-Xuan's spirit watched the two of them dismantling the web of lies in the brightly lit precinct, two trails of hot, translucent tears spilling down her pale cheeks.

She drifted to the edge of Shu-Qing's desk, gazing upon her biographical case sheet.

Affixed to the paper was a promotional photograph of her smiling before a grand piano in an evening gown, beside a line penned in pencil: [Victim's Mother: Contract Sanitation Worker, Keelung Cleaning Squad, Lin Xiu-Qin (Deceased)].

Shen Yu-Xuan raised an ethereal finger, gently tracing her mother's name on the paper, her voice soft as a passing breeze:

"Doctor Chen... Detective Xu... thank you both so very much..."

"My mother always told me when I was little that justice might travel slowly, but as long as you walk upright with an honest heart, the heavens will eventually bear witness..."

Nian-Zhou picked up the freshly brewed hot tea on Shu-Qing's desk, taking a sip, his face impassive as ever, yet a faint warmth flickered in the depth of his gaze:

"Whether heaven bears witness, I cannot say. But the microscopes of the New Taipei District Prosecutors Office certainly do."

9:00 AM the following morning. Autopsy Administration Office, New Taipei City Funeral Parlor.

The crisp cadence of expensive Italian leather shoes clicking over terrazzo tile echoed down the hallway, accompanied by the low, overbearing conversation of two middle-aged corporate attorneys.

The office door swung wide.

Flanked by two sharp-suited legal counsels, the suspect fiancé Zhao Yu-Heng strode in briskly.

Today, Zhao Yu-Heng wore an impeccably styled navy leisure blazer, his hair slicked back with pomade, a multimillion-dollar Patek Philippe watch gleaming on his wrist. Though he wore an engineered veneer of grief, every posture radiated the arrogant condescension of an untouchable heir.

"Doctor Chen, Detective Xu,"

The lead corporate counsel adjusted his gold spectacles, slapping a thick sheaf of legal motions onto the counter, his tone commanding and aggressive:

"We represent Zhao Financial Holdings. Pursuant to the Code of Criminal Procedure, where the family raises no objections to the cause of death, the prosecuting authority must release the body post-inquest without delay. Mr. Zhao has arrived in person to receive Miss Shen Yu-Xuan's autopsy death certificate and finalize the cremation permit. The Zhao family has chartered the premier mausoleum plot at Chin Pao San; we insist the mortuary cease all bureaucratic delays that only compound the family's grief."

Standing beside him, Zhao Yu-Heng lowered his eyelids with practiced melancholy, letting out a heavy sigh:

"Doctor Chen, Yu-Xuan was so devoted to grace... having her lie in a cold freezer tears my heart to pieces with every passing second..."

Sitting behind the desk, Chen Nian-Zhou didn't even bother lifting his chin.

He methodically gathered a stack of gross pathology anatomical diagrams, then withdrew a single white document from his desk drawer, sliding it smoothly to the center of the desk:

"Since Mr. Zhao is in such a hurry to cremate, please sign your name here on this 'Family Body Release Indemnity Agreement.'"

A flash of poorly concealed triumph darted across Zhao Yu-Heng's eyes. Stepping forward hastily, he reached into his breast pocket for his Montblanc fountain pen, leaning over to sign the line.

However, the split second he bent forward and extended his right sleeve—

Nian-Zhou's hawk-like eyes caught an unmistakable anomaly!

Three centimeters above Zhao Yu-Heng's right wrist joint—partially hidden beneath the luxury watchband and French cuff—was an angry scratch three centimeters long, smeared with antiseptic ointment yet still glistening with dark dried blood!

A bone-chilling smirk curled Nian-Zhou's lips.

He shot his hand out, pressing his palm firmly over the tip of Zhao Yu-Heng's pen just before it struck paper!

"Doctor Chen?! What is the meaning of this?!" The corporate lawyer erupted in immediate indignation.

Zhao Yu-Heng's face darkened, jerking his hand back with barely suppressed fury:

"Chen Nian-Zhou, you're merely a minor forensic examiner. Do not cross the line!"

"Mr. Zhao," Nian-Zhou slowly rose to his feet, his tall, imposing frame casting an intimidating shadow, his eyes cold as a scalpel's edge:

"This release agreement—you won't be signing it."

Just then, the office door slammed open with a concussive crash!

Dressed in a tactical ballistic vest, Xu Shu-Qing led four heavily armed Major Crimes detectives into the room, sealing every exit with absolute authority!

Held high in Shu-Qing's grip was a formal crimson-sealed legal warrant, her voice booming like thunder through the corridors:

"Zhao Yu-Heng! As of 8:30 AM this morning, the New Taipei District Prosecutors Office has officially reclassified the Qingshan Villa fall as an active investigation for 'Intentional Homicide'!"

"This is a court-ordered Compulsory Forensic Sampling Warrant signed by the lead prosecutor!"

Stepping forward, Shu-Qing pulled cold steel handcuffs from her tactical belt, her gaze boring like a drill into the dark red claw marks on his wrist:

"Take off your jacket and extend both hands."

"Miss Shen Yu-Xuan's fingernails have a few things they need to take back from you."

Every shred of Zhao Yu-Heng's manufactured composure crumbled to ash in an instant. Stumbling backward a half-pace, his face turned as white as a corpse!

Floating near the ceiling, Shen Yu-Xuan looked upon that unmasked, grotesque face of panic, and amidst the silent morning light, her tears of boundless vindication finally fell.
