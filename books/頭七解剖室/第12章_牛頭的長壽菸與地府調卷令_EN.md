# Chapter 12: Ox-Head's Long Life Cigarette and the Underworld Case Warrant

As night descended upon Qitang Old Street in Jinguashi, the stillness grew so profound that only the distant, muted roar of waves crashing against the rocky shores of the Yin-Yang Sea remained.

The northeast monsoon swept bone-chilling mountain drafts and thick maritime fog across the slick green flagstone steps. In the distance, the Japanese colonial-era Thirteen Levels Remains loomed beneath the bleak moonlight like a slumbering Pompeii, while the Golden Waterfall, tinted with rust-red mineral runoff, wound its way into the deep azure ocean, churning with yellowish-cyan foam where the two waters met.

Built against the steep mountain slopes, this antique mining town radiated a dreamlike, desolate beauty beneath the sparse, amber glow of streetlamps.

In the turnout of the narrow alley outside the stone house, a black unmarked police cruiser sat quietly in park. In the back seat, forensic intern Ah-Da delicately wiped down the yellowed, dented "Taiwan Metal Mining Corp" plastic helmet with a paper tissue, while beside him, little A-Mei had fallen into a deep sleep in the female officer's embrace, a faint, peaceful smile lingering upon her lips.

A few meters outside the cruiser, beside the abandoned Taiwan Metal Mining Corp narrow-gauge railway, Chen Nianzhou stood alone on the cliff's edge facing the biting sea wind, his faded black trench coat fluttering in the nocturnal gale.

The luminescent hands of his wristwatch quietly pointed to eleven fifteen in the evening.

Only forty-five minutes remained until the stroke of midnight—the seventh day after old miner Lin Fude was murdered, marking the absolute deadline of the Touqi crossing between the living and the dead.

"Nianzhou, Prosecutor Lin just called from the District Prosecutors Office," Captain Zhang said, stubbing out the half-burnt Long Life cigarette between his fingers and crunching over the ballast stones, his voice filled with hard-earned relief. "The team of heavyweight criminal defense attorneys hired by Chen Jinbiao petitioned the Keelung District Court this afternoon to revoke his pretrial detention, even falsely claiming that the skull fracture on Lin Fude was natural trauma from a mine rockfall, and attempting to paint those thirty taels of gold as 'untraceable smuggled contraband.'"

Nianzhou's gaze turned frosty, without looking back: "What did Prosecutor Lin say?"

"Prosecutor Lin slapped the forensic pathology report you drafted right onto the judge's bench!" Captain Zhang grinned broadly, his eyes gleaming with satisfaction. "The gold dust isotope analysis from the micro-spectrometer, the thumb skin DNA on the figure-eight winch knot insulation, plus the registered master ledger bearing the thumbprints of forty-one retired miners before a notary thirty years ago. The judge dismissed the defense petition on the spot, ruling that Chen Jinbiao's brutal premeditated murder was established by overwhelming evidence—detention without bail, no appeals permitted! Even the mob lawyer who orchestrated the stunt was immediately placed under criminal investigation by the prosecutor for tampering with evidence!"

"The legal battle has reached its conclusion," Nianzhou said, gazing toward Keelung Mountain sinking into the dark night, his tone calm as still water. "The mortal case file is closed, but the underworld docket has only just arrived at the bench."

Before the words had settled, the heavy maritime fog swirled violently like boiling water.

The night wind, previously scented with sea salt, abruptly died down, and without warning, the air was saturated with the peculiar scent of aged sulfur and ancient burnt parchment.

At the far terminus of the abandoned railway tracks, two tall, imposing figures tread upon ethereal flagstone paving, emerging silently from the depths of the foggy sea.

Walking on the left, Ox-Head remained wrapped in his iron-heavy black trench coat, his towering horns concealed in the hood's shadow, his countenance as majestic and severe as a cliff hewn by an axe; beside him, lean and sharp, Horse-Face held a finely carved obsidian digital tablet that glowed with a piercing, eerie green light resembling antique bamboo scrolls.

"Old Zhang, go back to the car and watch over A-Mei," Nianzhou ordered softly.

Though Captain Zhang could not perceive spirits, the sixth sense forged over decades in homicide caused the hairs on his neck to stand straight on end, a bone-chilling frost shooting from his soles straight to the crown of his skull. Swallowing hard, he wisely slipped back inside the police cruiser.

Ox-Head halted beside the railroad ties three paces away from Nianzhou.

Rather than brandishing his clanking wrought-iron soul chains as in the past, he quietly produced a slightly crushed yellow pack of Long Life cigarettes from the inner pocket of his trench coat.

Ox-Head placed a cigarette between his lips, then raised eyes as deep as an ancient mountain spring, looking silently at Chen Nianzhou.

Understanding the silent gesture, Nianzhou produced his metal lighter, flicked the flint with a sharp *clink*, cupped the modest orange flame with both hands, and held it to the tip of Ox-Head's cigarette.

*Sizzle—*

The flame licked the tobacco leaves, and a pure, familiar scent of Long Life tobacco smoke drifted into the freezing night air.

"The Long Life cigarettes of the mortal realm are as harsh as ever," Ox-Head took a deep drag, exhaling a pale blue smoke ring. His voice was deep and sonorous, echoing like a bronze bell tolling in an ancient temple. "Forensic Pathologist Chen, you have labored hard."

"It is a forensic doctor's duty," Nianzhou pocketed the lighter, speaking evenly. "Old Ox, the appointed hour has not yet arrived. You are half an hour early."

Beside him, Horse-Face adjusted the holographic optical visor hovering over his face, his fingers dancing across the obsidian tablet with practiced swiftness, his synthesized voice tinged with rare solemnity:

"Forensic Pathologist Chen, this is no ordinary soul-claiming patrol. Half an hour ago, the Ten Halls of the Underworld jointly issued a top-tier 'Special Pardon and Case Review Warrant.'"

Horse-Face pivoted the tablet screen toward Nianzhou.

Floating in the center of the dark jade screen was a formal decree inscribed in ancient seal script, stamped with a bold vermilion cinnabar seal. Below it were forty-one names and their years of death, topped with the heading:

[Special Judicial Underworld Docket No. 1042: Case Review Warrant on the Karmic Merits of the Brotherhood and Mutual Aid of the Forty-Two Miners of Jinguashi Benshan Mine].

"Back when mining accidents plagued Taiwan Metal Mining Corp, forty-odd miners pooled their meager savings to care for widowed families, entrusting their lives to one another," Horse-Face pointed to the shimmering golden merit values on screen. "For thirty years, Elder Lin Fude administered this gold, providing for twenty-seven fallen comrades in old age and arranging their final rites, without taking a single cent for himself. This profound righteousness may be just a few yellowed sheets in the human world, but before the Underworld Karma Mirror, it has congealed into towering golden radiance."

Ox-Head tapped the ash from his cigarette, gazing up toward the stone house on the old street: "Tonight at midnight, this Officer comes on the command of the Underworld Sovereign not to bind a soul for interrogation, but to escort him to reunite with the brothers who have waited for him in the underworld for thirty long years."

Before the words had finished echoing, gentle, steady footsteps sounded from the direction of Number 64 Qitang Road.

The soul of retired miner Lin Fude, dressed in clean, tidy blue cotton work clothes, walked peacefully along the abandoned rail tracks toward the seaside cliff.

At this moment, the old man no longer coughed; his once-hollowed, deformed chest was whole and tranquil, and the indelible black quartz dust specks on his skin had softened into a warm, luminous spiritual glow.

Seeing Ox-Head and Horse-Face, the elderly man paused instinctively with bashful hesitation.

Snuffing out the cigarette butt in his palm, Ox-Head clasped his fists together and bowed toward Lin Fude in a solemn, ancient underworld greeting:

"Mining Team Leader Lin Fude of Jinguashi Benshan Shaft No. 5, your earthly span has ended, but your merits are fulfilled," Ox-Head's voice was solemn and warm. "Old brother, fall in."

"Fall in...?" Lin Fude murmured in astonishment.

Just then, along both flanks of the abandoned narrow-gauge tracks, a crisp, familiar metallic clinking rang out—

*Clang! Clang! Clang—!*

It was the traditional signal struck by Jinguashi miners using steel picks against iron rails during shift changes deep underground!

From the churning sea fog, dozens of dim yet warm carbide safety lamps flared to life one after another across the void.

With steady, synchronized footsteps, forty figures of varying stature emerged through the mist from the mouth of the abandoned drift!

Every one of them wore rough canvas miner's overalls and plastic hard hats, carrying steel picks and haulage cables over their shoulders, their faces beaming with rustic, joyful smiles.

They were the forty-one fellow miners who had died in past mining disasters or had passed away from silicosis over the past thirty years!

Walking at the very front was a burly middle-aged man—A-Mei's biological grandfather, Chen Maosen, who had used his own body to shield Lin Fude from falling rocks!

"Brother Maosen...? Ashui... Wangfa...? Why... why are you all here..." Lin Fude rubbed his eyes, tears streaming instantly down his cheeks, his lips trembling violently.

Chen Maosen strode forward, extending calloused, sturdy hands, and pulled Lin Fude into a crushing embrace!

"Ah-Fu, you stubborn fool of a brother!" Chen Maosen patted Lin Fude's back with reddened eyes. "For thirty years, you guarded those thirty taels of gold, living alone in a leaking stone shack eating sweet potatoes, seeing all our old folks off and raising my A-Mei to twelve years old... Every hardship you endured, we watched from below with our own eyes!"

"Brother Maosen... I didn't touch a single penny... I looked after A-Mei..." Lin Fude wept like an overwhelmed child, clutching Chen Maosen's sleeve.

"We know! We know it all!" Chen Maosen turned and gestured toward the forty brothers standing in neat formation behind him. "Thirty years ago, the brothers swore a pact—no one would reincarnate ahead of the others; we all waited by the Naihe Bridge. Today, all forty-two members of Shaft No. 6 are finally present and accounted for!"

Behind him, the forty retired miners removed their helmets in unison and burst into hearty, thunderous laughter that echoed across the Jiufen valley, dispelling all chill and gloom.

Just then, the door of the police cruiser pushed open a crack.

Rubbing her sleepy eyes, little A-Mei peeked out, looking toward the warm golden figures glowing in the mist.

The little girl spoke no words, but she saw her grandfather encircled by uncles and elders wearing the same miner's uniform, everyone smiling with pure, radiant joy. Sniffling, A-Mei raised both small hands outside the car window, forming a gentle gesture of "peace and safety."

Chen Maosen and Lin Fude turned together, gazing fondly at the obedient little girl from afar.

"A-Mei... grow up strong and happy," Chen Maosen whispered with tears glistening in his eyes.

Then, Chen Maosen led all the miner brothers to turn toward Chen Nianzhou and Ah-Da standing on the stone steps. Over forty men straightened their backs in unison and bowed deeply:

"Forensic Pathologist Chen, young brother Ah-Da, thank you for clearing Ah-Fu's name and for returning a future to A-Mei. We old bones underground owe you a debt as vast as the heavens."

Nianzhou stood tall and straight like a pine, while Ah-Da hastily wiped his tears and snapped to attention; both bowed back in the highest professional salute:

"Have a safe journey, elders. We will guard justice in the human realm; please rest assured regarding A-Mei's life."

The midnight bell of the Zi hour tolled distantly across the ethereal plane.

Ox-Head waved his long coat, and an antique ore cart glowing with soft azure light materialized from the void.

"Team Leader Lin, board the cart. The brothers are waiting for you to lead the shift," Ox-Head said with a gentle smile.

Lin Fude wiped his tears, donned the brand-new safety helmet handed to him by Chen Maosen, and stepped vigorously onto the ore cart.

The cart started forward, accompanied by the rhythmic clatter of iron rails and a rousing miner's cadence; the silhouettes of the forty-two miners, together with Ox-Head and Horse-Face, dissolved into the starry sky and the deep mist, turning into a rain of golden light that dusted the ridges of Keelung Mountain.

The sea wind gradually calmed, and a pale sliver of dawn broke across the eastern horizon.

Captain Zhang stepped out from the car and walked up to Nianzhou, looking at the empty tracks and exhaling a long sigh: "Nianzhou, are they gone?"

"They're gone," Nianzhou lit a Long Life cigarette, the spark glowing in the dawn light. "Forty-two men, not one missing—all reported for duty."

From his coat, Nianzhou pulled out his black leather *Morgue Notebook*, turning to the page inscribed with Lin Fude's name.

As the morning breeze rustled the yellowed page, Nianzhou uncapped his fountain pen and neatly inscribed the final entry beside the "Status" column:

"Case Type: Blunt force skull fracture resulting from physical assault followed by post-fall arson. Cause of Death: Smoke inhalation asphyxiation with thermal respiratory tract injury. Dying Wish: Safeguard thirty-year miner mutual aid fund; protect deaf-mute granddaughter A-Mei. Status: Fully completed; all brothers reunited."

Closing the notebook, Nianzhou looked up as the morning sun pierced the clouds, spilling ten thousand beams of golden light across the storied slopes of Jinguashi.

Deep wrongs in dark chasms are finally avenged; no inferno can consume a pure, selfless heart.

And at the boundary between life and death in New Taipei, the scalpel of Forensic Pathologist Chen Nianzhou still waits to give voice to the next departed soul.
